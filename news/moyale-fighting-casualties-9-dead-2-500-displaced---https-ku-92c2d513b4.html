Moyale Fighting Casualties: 9 Dead, 2,500 Displaced - https://kulu-media.com/

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 20:32:47 GMT

Fighting in the Ethiopian border town of Moyale has resulted in at least nine deaths and the displacement of over 2,500 Ethiopians into Kenya, as reported on October 23, 2026. The violence, which erupted on October 21, is attributed to tensions between communities from Ethiopia's Oromia and Somali regions. A hospital official indicated that around 30 individuals were treated for injuries, many from heavy weaponry, with fatalities occurring over two days. The influx of displaced persons has strained resources in the Kenyan town of Moyale, where many have sought shelter with relatives and local communities. The Kenya Red Cross has initiated an emergency response, targeting 1,000 households with essential supplies. The clashes have also caused property damage, with reports of destruction at commercial facilities. Additionally, stray gunfire has injured civilians on the Kenyan side, prompting increased security measures. The situation remains fluid, with ongoing concerns about further violence and humanitarian needs.
