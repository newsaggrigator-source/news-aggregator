Al-Shabaab fighters capture village near Mataban in central Somalia - Somali Guardian

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 18:14:29 GMT

Al-Shabaab fighters have captured the village of Coomaad, located approximately 30 kilometers from Mataban in Somalia's Hiiraan region. This takeover occurred after Somali National Army troops withdrew following brief clashes, allowing the militants to gain control of the strategically significant settlement, which connects central Somali towns. The capture of Coomaad is part of a broader trend, as Al-Shabaab has been reclaiming rural areas and military positions in the region over recent months, raising concerns for nearby towns that have remained largely free from major attacks for four years. This escalation coincides with political tensions involving the Hawadle clan, which has been in conflict with the federal government over leadership disputes in Hirshabelle state. Additionally, Al-Shabaab's recent seizure of Warxoolo near Guriel poses further threats to central Somalia, as Somali forces are already stretched thin due to redeployments and ongoing military commitments in other regions.
