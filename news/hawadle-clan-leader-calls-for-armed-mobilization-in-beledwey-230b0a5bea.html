Hawadle Clan Leader Calls for Armed Mobilization in Beledweyne as Federal Forces Face Defections - FTL Somalia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 22:07:08 GMT

In Beledweyne, the leader of the Hawadle clan has urged armed mobilization among his community as federal forces experience significant defections. This call to action comes amid growing concerns over the security situation in the region, where the presence of federal troops has been increasingly challenged. The Hawadle clan leader emphasized the need for local defense to protect their interests and maintain stability in the face of potential threats. Reports indicate that the defections have weakened the federal forces' effectiveness, prompting local leaders to take matters into their own hands. The situation reflects broader tensions within Somalia, where clan dynamics and security challenges continue to complicate governance and peace efforts. The mobilization call highlights the urgent need for a cohesive strategy to address both local and national security concerns, as communities seek to safeguard their safety and autonomy amidst ongoing instability.
