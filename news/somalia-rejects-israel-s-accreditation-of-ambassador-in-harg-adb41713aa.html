Somalia Rejects Israel’s Accreditation of Ambassador in Hargeisa - Dawan Africa

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 19:27:11 GMT

Somalia has officially rejected Israel's accreditation of an ambassador in Hargeisa and the establishment of an embassy in Somaliland, deeming these actions a violation of its sovereignty. The condemnation follows a ceremony where Somaliland President Abdirahman Mohamed Abdullahi Irro received the credentials of Israel's ambassador, Michael Lotem. Somalia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs stated that these actions were taken without the federal government's consent, labeling them as a "flagrant violation" of Somalia's territorial integrity. The ministry emphasized that Hargeisa and Somaliland are integral parts of Somalia and that diplomatic relations require the consent of the sovereign state, as outlined in the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations. Somalia has called for Israel to reverse its actions and urged other nations and international organizations to reject them to avoid legitimizing the situation. The federal government reserves the right to pursue diplomatic, political, and legal measures in response. (159 words)
