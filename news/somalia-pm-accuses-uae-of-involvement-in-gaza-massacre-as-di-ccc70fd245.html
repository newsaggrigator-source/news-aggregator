Somalia PM Accuses UAE of Involvement in Gaza Massacre as Diplomatic Rift Deepens - Dawan Africa

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 09:57:33 GMT

Somalia's Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre has accused the United Arab Emirates (UAE) of being involved in the recent massacre in Gaza, escalating diplomatic tensions between the two nations. Speaking at a ceremony in Mogadishu, Hamza claimed that the UAE played a direct or indirect role in the violence, asserting that the Somali public has long been aware of the UAE's detrimental influence in the region. He linked the UAE to conflicts in Libya and Yemen, and alleged that it has armed militias responsible for significant casualties in Sudan. Furthermore, he suggested that the UAE is collaborating with Israel to undermine Somalia, highlighting the presence of the Israeli flag in the country. This accusation comes amid a backdrop of deteriorating relations, marked by Somalia's decision to expel the UAE ambassador and annul agreements with the UAE earlier this year. The UAE has denied these allegations, stating it has provided humanitarian aid to Gaza.
