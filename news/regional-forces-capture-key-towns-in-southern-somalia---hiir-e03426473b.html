Regional forces capture key towns in southern Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 21:45:21 GMT

Forces loyal to ousted South West State leader Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen have captured several key towns in southern Somalia, significantly undermining the federally-backed local government. Following the government's military intervention in March to remove Laftagareen, he has sought to regain control of the region, including its capital, Baidoa. On Saturday, his forces seized Berdale, Dinsoor, Buurhakaba, Qansaxdheere, and Awdiinle, which is located about 30 kilometers from Baidoa. This rapid territorial gain, mostly achieved without conflict except for minor clashes in Buurhakaba, has raised concerns among local residents. Eyewitnesses reported that Laftagareen's forces took control of Berdale without resistance, prompting local officials to flee to a nearby African Union base. The South West Information Minister stated that efforts would be made to reclaim the districts now under Laftagareen’s control. Residents expressed confusion over the shifting power dynamics, as Laftagareen's forces appear to be focusing on surrounding towns near Baidoa.
