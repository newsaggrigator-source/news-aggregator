Qatar Charity Concludes Campaign to Treat Heart Defects in Children in Somalia - Qatar news agency

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 17:09:29 GMT

Qatar Charity has successfully concluded a campaign aimed at treating heart defects in children in Somalia, in collaboration with the Ministry of Health and local hospitals. The initiative, part of the "Dafa'Al-Bala" campaign and held during the "27th Night of Ramadan," provided medical examinations for 144 children and performed 40 cardiac catheterization procedures, surpassing the initial target of 35 cases. The closing ceremony was attended by Qatar's Ambassador to Somalia, Dr. Abdullah bin Salem Al Nuaimi. The campaign has been hailed as a significant medical achievement, giving many children a new lease on life. Plans are underway for a specialized campaign focused on pediatric open-heart surgeries to address more complex cases. This initiative builds on Qatar Charity's previous efforts, which have collectively benefited 137 children in Somalia with heart conditions. Medical professionals involved praised the dedication of Somali healthcare workers and the trust placed in them by families. (159 words)
