More than 20 children from Somalia feared trafficked to Ireland - Irish Examiner

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 20:03:00 GMT

More than 20 children from Somalia are feared to have been trafficked to Ireland, according to reports. The children, some as young as 12, are believed to have arrived in the country unaccompanied and are at significant risk of exploitation. Authorities are concerned about their safety and well-being, as the children may be vulnerable to various forms of abuse. The Irish government and relevant agencies are working to investigate the situation and provide support to the affected children. This alarming situation highlights ongoing issues related to human trafficking and the need for enhanced protective measures for vulnerable minors.
