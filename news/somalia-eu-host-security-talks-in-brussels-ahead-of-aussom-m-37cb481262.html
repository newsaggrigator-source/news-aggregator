Somalia, EU host security talks in Brussels ahead of AUSSOM mandate decision - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 20:04:08 GMT

Somalia and the European Union convened a high-level security meeting in Brussels on November 19, 2026, involving representatives from 50 countries and international organizations. The discussions centered on Somalia's plan to take full responsibility for its national security as the mandate of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) is set for review. Somali Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi highlighted the country's security transition roadmap and the need for continued international support. The meeting, co-chaired by Somali and EU officials, emphasized the importance of a sustainable security framework that requires political consensus among Somali stakeholders. With AUSSOM's current UN authorization expiring on December 31, 2026, concerns about funding and troop reductions were raised, pressuring Somalia to enhance its military capabilities against al-Shabab. While the EU reaffirmed its commitment to Somalia's stability, no final agreements on future security arrangements or financial commitments were reached during the talks.
