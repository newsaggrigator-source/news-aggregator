Ethiopia Delivers Over 1 Million Digital Business Registration, Licensing Services

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 05:08:08 GMT

Ethiopia has successfully delivered over 1.08 million digital business registration and licensing services within a three-month period, as reported by the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration. This achievement reflects the country's commitment to enhancing business procedures and expanding online public services. The report indicates that 1,088,787 services were provided across various federal institutions, regional states, and city administrations, with Oromia Regional State leading at 441,550 services. Addis Ababa and Amhara followed with 204,512 and 156,193 services, respectively. The most common service was trade name clearance, accounting for 579,785 services, while business license renewals and amendments also contributed significantly. The digital system aims to alleviate bureaucratic challenges for businesses, improving efficiency, accessibility, and transparency in public service delivery. This initiative is part of Ethiopia's broader digital transformation agenda, which seeks to modernize public administration and enhance access to government services through technology.
