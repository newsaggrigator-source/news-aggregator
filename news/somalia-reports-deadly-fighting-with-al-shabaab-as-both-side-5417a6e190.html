Somalia reports deadly fighting with Al-Shabaab as both sides claim heavy losses - AOL.co.uk

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 16:10:43 GMT

Fighting between Somali federal troops and the Al-Shabaab militant group has resulted in significant casualties, with both sides claiming heavy losses. The Somali defense ministry reported that 75 militants were killed and 56 wounded during an offensive in the Lower Shabelle region, while Al-Shabaab claimed to have killed 321 government soldiers in a three-hour ambush. The exact toll remains unverified. The clashes occurred as part of Operation Banooda, targeting Al-Shabaab positions, and involved the use of explosive-laden vehicles by the militants. Local media indicated that the Somali government convened an emergency cabinet meeting to address the reported heavy casualties and military equipment losses. The conflict highlights the ongoing struggle against Al-Shabaab, which continues to control significant territory in central Somalia. Additionally, the Somali government faces challenges in securing international support, particularly as the US has ceased funding for crucial logistics operations, further complicating efforts against the insurgency.
