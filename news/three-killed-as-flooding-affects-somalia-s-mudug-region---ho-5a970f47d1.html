Three killed as flooding affects Somalia’s Mudug region - Horseed Media

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 17:56:48 GMT

Heavy flooding in Somalia's Mudug region has resulted in the deaths of three individuals. The flooding, caused by persistent heavy rains, has led to significant damage to infrastructure and displacement of residents. Local authorities are struggling to provide adequate assistance to those affected, as many homes have been submerged and access to essential services has been disrupted. The situation remains critical, with ongoing concerns about health risks and food security for the displaced populations. Efforts are being made to assess the full extent of the damage and to mobilize resources for relief operations.
