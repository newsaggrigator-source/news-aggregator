Radio Ergo Weekly Feedback Report, Issued: 27 Nov 2026 - ReliefWeb

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 13:55:46 GMT

The Radio Ergo Weekly Feedback Report, issued on November 27, 2026, highlights the ongoing impact of rainfall on communities in Somalia, particularly in Jubbaland, South West, and Hirshabelle. While many callers reported positive effects from recent rains, some areas experienced reduced rainfall and rising temperatures, leading to water shortages, especially in Gedo and Bay regions. Health concerns emerged as callers noted increased mosquito populations and malaria cases, prompting requests for mosquito control. Internally displaced persons (IDPs) expressed hardships, including lack of aid and crop failures due to pests. Additionally, callers raised issues related to food security, livestock health, and the effects of climate change, with some regions reporting drought conditions. The report underscores the need for continued humanitarian assistance and effective agricultural support to address these challenges. Overall, the feedback reflects a mix of optimism due to rainfall and urgent calls for aid amid ongoing difficulties. (159 words)
