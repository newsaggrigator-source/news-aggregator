Security Council Meets on Situation Concerning Western Sahara - UN Media

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 21:21:27 GMT

The United Nations Security Council convened on October 30, 2026, to discuss the situation in Western Sahara and vote on a draft resolution aimed at renewing the mandate of the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO). The resolution, proposed by the United States, garnered 11 votes in favor, with one vote against from the Russian Federation and three abstentions from China, Pakistan, and Somalia. However, the resolution was ultimately not adopted due to the Russian veto. This meeting highlights the ongoing complexities and geopolitical tensions surrounding the Western Sahara issue, which remains unresolved despite international efforts to facilitate a referendum for self-determination. The situation continues to be a focal point of discussion within the Security Council, reflecting the broader challenges of peacekeeping and conflict resolution in the region.
