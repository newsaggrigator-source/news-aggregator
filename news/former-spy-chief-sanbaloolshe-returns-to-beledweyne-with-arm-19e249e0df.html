Former Spy Chief Sanbaloolshe Returns to Beledweyne with Armed Convoy, Backing Breakaway Hiiraan Administration - FTL Somalia

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 15:03:45 GMT

Former Somali intelligence chief Abdullahi Mohamed Ali Sanbaloolshe has returned to Beledweyne with an armed convoy, signaling his support for a breakaway administration in Hiiraan amid escalating political tensions. His arrival, confirmed by multiple sources, comes as local fighters have taken control of regional administration offices, reflecting a growing demand for an independent Hiiraan administration. Sanbaloolshe's backing of this movement places him in direct opposition to the Hirshabelle administration and the federal government in Mogadishu. His return is not merely a political visit; it involves significant military presence, raising concerns about external influences, particularly from Ethiopia and the UAE. The political backdrop includes the recent re-election of Hirshabelle's president, which Hiiraan leaders rejected, citing a lack of inclusive representation. The federal government is responding with meetings to address the crisis, but the underlying grievances of the Hawadle clan remain unaddressed, complicating the situation further.
