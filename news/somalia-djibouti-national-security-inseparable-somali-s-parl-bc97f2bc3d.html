Somalia, Djibouti national security inseparable: Somali's parliament speaker - TRT Afrika

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 21:37:36 GMT

During his first official visit abroad, Somalia's parliament speaker, Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur, emphasized the inseparable nature of national security between Somalia and Djibouti. Speaking to Djibouti's parliament, he highlighted Djibouti's critical role in Somalia's rebuilding efforts, particularly through the historic Arta peace conference and military support against terrorism. Nur stated, "Djibouti’s security is our security, and Somalia’s security is Djibouti’s security," underscoring their shared responsibility for peace and stability. He praised Djibouti's contributions to Somalia's state-building since the collapse of its government in 1991 and acknowledged the sacrifices of Djiboutian troops in the fight against terrorism. Nur called for enhanced cooperation between the two nations, particularly in parliamentary diplomacy, to strengthen their ties and address regional security challenges. He concluded by expressing gratitude for Djibouti's longstanding support, reinforcing the notion that the two countries are "twin nations" bound by history and solidarity.
