Indian Navy rescues hijacked fishing vessel with 20-member Iranian crew off Somalia coast - DT Next

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 10:14:40 GMT

The Indian Navy successfully rescued the fishing vessel Azini, which had been hijacked by Somali pirates with a 20-member Iranian crew onboard. The operation, lasting over 48 hours, was initiated after the vessel was seized earlier last month. The Navy reported that all crew members were safely rescued without any casualties. The Iranian embassy in New Delhi expressed gratitude to the Indian government and armed forces for their effective response. The Navy emphasized that the hijacking posed a significant maritime security threat, as the vessel could have been used for further attacks on merchant shipping. The operation involved a coordinated effort between naval and air forces, including the deployment of marine commandos via airdrop. This successful mission highlights India's commitment to maritime security and humanitarian assistance in the Indian Ocean Region, reinforcing its role as a key security partner. The Iranian embassy commended the professionalism and coordination of the Indian armed forces in this humanitarian effort.
