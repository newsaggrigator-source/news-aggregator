Ethiopian Americans Urge African Union to Include Amhara People and All Major Stakeholders in Ethiopia’s Peace Process - Borkena

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 02:24:18 GMT

Ethiopian Americans, represented by the Coalition of Ethiopian Americans Civic Organizations, have urged the African Union (AU) to include the Amhara people and other key stakeholders in Ethiopia's peace process. In a letter dated October 19, 2026, the coalition emphasized that lasting peace cannot be achieved without the meaningful participation of all affected communities. They highlighted the need for a comprehensive approach that addresses the root causes of Ethiopia's ongoing conflicts, including political instability and humanitarian crises. The coalition expressed concerns over human rights violations against the Amhara population and called for credible investigations into these issues. They proposed seven recommendations for sustainable peace, including broadening stakeholder participation, addressing underlying grievances, and ensuring civilian protection. The coalition also stressed the importance of the AU's leadership in facilitating an inclusive dialogue that respects human rights and promotes national reconciliation. They believe that without the inclusion of diverse communities, any peace agreement risks being temporary and ineffective.
