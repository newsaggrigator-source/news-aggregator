UAE joins Israel at centre of Somalia’s escalating dispute with breakaway African territory as China, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye resist recognition - Business Insider Africa

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 15:00:07 GMT

Somalia's diplomatic tensions with the United Arab Emirates (UAE) have intensified, as the Somali government accuses Abu Dhabi of undermining its sovereignty through unauthorized agreements with regional administrations like Somaliland, Puntland, and Jubaland. Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud claims the UAE has established military bases and supported armed groups without federal approval. In response, the UAE has dismissed these allegations, suggesting that Somalia is manipulating the situation for domestic political gain. The conflict escalated following Israel's recognition of Somaliland and the appointment of a diplomatic envoy, which Somalia protested. The UAE has not recognized Somaliland but is implicated in the controversy. Meanwhile, China, Saudi Arabia, and Türkiye have expressed opposition to Israel's recognition of Somaliland, reaffirming their support for Somalia's territorial integrity. Somalia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs has called for the UAE to engage solely with the Federal Government, rejecting any parallel security arrangements.
