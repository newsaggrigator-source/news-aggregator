Seven key players return to Black Stars for Somalia double-header - 3News

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 17:32:29 GMT

Seven key players have rejoined the Black Stars squad ahead of Ghana's 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying double-header against Somalia. Interim coach Kim Lars Björkegren welcomes back Mohammed Salisu, Mohammed Kudus, Thomas Partey, Jordan Ayew, Daniel Kofi Kyereh, Alidu Seidu, and Prince Adu Kwabena. Notably, Kudus returns after recovering from an injury that sidelined him during the 2026 FIFA World Cup, while captain Ayew is back after missing previous qualifiers due to a groin injury. Kyereh's inclusion marks his return to the national team after nearly four years away due to injury. The squad also features five players from the Ghana Premier League, showcasing a mix of international experience and local talent. Ghana will host Somalia on November 12 and again on November 16, aiming to strengthen their qualifying campaign for the upcoming tournament.
