Africans Must Reclaim Cultural Narratives - Nebiyou

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 17:50:31 GMT

Ethiopia's State Minister for Culture and Tourism, Nebiyou Baye, has called for Africans to reclaim their cultural narratives by taking ownership of the study and promotion of their heritage. In an interview, he emphasized the need for African scholars, anthropologists, and sociologists to lead in documenting and interpreting the continent's rich cultural wealth, which has often been viewed through foreign lenses that misrepresent its traditions. Nebiyou highlighted the importance of cultural exchanges among African nations, advocating for greater familiarity with each other's traditions before seeking global recognition. He pointed to Ethiopian cuisine, music, and festivals as cultural assets that should be shared across Africa. Additionally, he noted the role of music and cinema as tools for cultural diplomacy, citing influential figures like Mulatu Astatke and Haile Gerima. By promoting their unique cultural expressions, Nebiyou believes Africans can reshape global perceptions and showcase their identity effectively. (160 words)
