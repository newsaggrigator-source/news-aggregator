Ethiopia Faces Contrasting Drought, Flood Risks As Potentially Record-Strength Super El Ni&#xF1;o Looms

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 11:07:18 GMT

Ethiopia is facing significant climate challenges as a potentially record-strength Super El Niño develops for 2026-27. An analysis by WaterAid indicates that while northern and central regions may experience worsening drought conditions, southern Ethiopia is expected to see above-normal rainfall, raising the risk of flooding. This contrasting weather pattern highlights an east-west divide in Ethiopia, with Addis Ababa having previously faced severe dryness during past El Niño events. The anticipated drought could strain water supplies and agricultural production, exacerbating existing water-security issues. Conversely, the increased rainfall in the south poses risks of flooding, infrastructure damage, and waterborne diseases. WaterAid emphasizes the need for proactive measures to enhance water security and climate-resilient systems, particularly ahead of the COP31 climate summit. The analysis warns that without intervention, the impacts of these extreme weather patterns could threaten the livelihoods and stability of vulnerable communities across the region. (159 words)
