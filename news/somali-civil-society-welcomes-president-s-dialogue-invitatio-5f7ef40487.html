Somali Civil Society Welcomes President’s Dialogue Invitation as Opposition Weighs Its Response - FTL Somalia

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 09:56:57 GMT

The Somali Non-State Actors (SONSA) coalition has expressed support for President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's invitation to political stakeholders for dialogue aimed at resolving disputes over federal elections. They view this initiative as a crucial step towards achieving tangible results and emphasize the need for genuine discussions that address the core issues surrounding the electoral process. However, SONSA's endorsement comes with conditions, stressing that dialogue must lead to meaningful compromise rather than merely convening a forum. The coalition previously criticized the government's announcement of a 2027 election timetable as premature, arguing it undermined trust and dialogue efforts. The upcoming forum, scheduled for November 14-18 in Mogadishu, will involve various political groups, including Nabad iyo Nolol and the Somali Future Council. While SONSA's participation is seen as beneficial, the success of the dialogue ultimately hinges on the willingness of the opposition and regional administrations to engage seriously and reach a consensus.
