Abiy Ahmed Calls Ethiopia’s Sea Access Existential, Offers Dialogue at Tana Forum - https://kulu-media.com/

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 18:47:53 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed emphasized the critical importance of maritime access for Ethiopia during his address at the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, held in Bahir Dar. He described reliable sea access as an existential issue for the nation, which has a population exceeding 130 million, linking it to national security and economic development. Abiy reiterated his government's commitment to dialogue in resolving political and security disputes, inviting groups willing to abandon violence to engage in peaceful discussions. He highlighted Ethiopia's landlocked status as a vulnerability, advocating for negotiations to secure dependable trade routes. Abiy also discussed the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam's role in enhancing regional cooperation and energy exports. He called for stronger African institutions and emphasized the need for economic sovereignty and regional security collaboration. His remarks underscored Ethiopia's strategic focus on maritime access, regional integration, and the importance of addressing environmental challenges as part of a broader peace and security strategy.
