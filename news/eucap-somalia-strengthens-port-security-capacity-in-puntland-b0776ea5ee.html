EUCAP Somalia Strengthens Port Security Capacity in Puntland Through ISPS Code Training - Horseed Media

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 13:08:47 GMT

The European Union Capacity Building Mission in Somalia (EUCAP Somalia) has conducted a five-day training program on the International Ship and Port Facility Security (ISPS) Code in Puntland, aimed at enhancing maritime security and port safety. Held from November 15 to 19, 2026, the training involved participants from various maritime and government institutions, focusing on international port security standards and their practical applications. Key topics included government responsibilities, Port Facility Security Assessments, and operational security measures. A tabletop exercise allowed participants to apply their knowledge to simulated scenarios, improving decision-making and inter-agency coordination. The initiative aims to bolster security at Bossaso Port and foster cooperation among maritime stakeholders. Participants expressed that the training significantly enhanced their understanding of security threats and risk assessment. EUCAP Somalia continues to support Somalia's maritime security development through specialized training and technical assistance, contributing to safer and more resilient port operations in Puntland.
