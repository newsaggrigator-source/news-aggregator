From Postal Union to Digital Integration: Somalia’s Communications Ambitions and the Question of Government Control - www.ftlsomalia.com

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 17:34:08 GMT

Somalia's Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre recently inspected the renovated headquarters of the Ministry of Communications and Technology, highlighting the government's ambitions to restore its membership in the Universal Postal Union (UPU) and participate in a regional digital integration project with East African nations. The ministry aims to modernize Somalia's communication systems and improve broadband access. While the restoration of UPU membership is a significant milestone, the push for a government-managed internet raises concerns about potential censorship and the state's capacity to manage a sector that has thrived with minimal government interference. The Prime Minister emphasized the need for a unified communications framework to enhance cybersecurity and service reliability. However, the effectiveness of increased government control remains uncertain, as the telecommunications sector has flourished independently. The ministry's plans for digital integration and regulatory improvements are promising, but the implications of greater government oversight warrant careful examination.
