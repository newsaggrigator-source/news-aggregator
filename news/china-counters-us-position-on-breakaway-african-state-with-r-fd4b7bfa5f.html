China counters US position on breakaway African state with Red Sea navigation pledge days after Trump’s Somaliland remarks - Business Insider Africa

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 19:08:02 GMT

China and Djibouti have reaffirmed their support for Somalia's territorial integrity, opposing the international recognition of Somaliland's independence. This statement follows remarks by U.S. President Donald Trump, who described Somaliland as a distinct entity from Somalia, leading to celebrations in Somaliland. The joint declaration, issued after a meeting between the foreign ministers of China and Djibouti, emphasized the importance of freedom of navigation in the Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb Strait, as well as regional sovereignty. China’s Foreign Ministry confirmed the discussions, which included a memorandum on security cooperation under China's Global Security Initiative. The communiqué also addressed peace and stability in the Horn of Africa and the security of vital shipping routes. While the U.S. seeks closer ties with Somaliland for strategic access to the Berbera port, China aims to counter this influence, reinforcing its commitment to Somalia's unity and its own interests in maintaining open trade routes in the region.
