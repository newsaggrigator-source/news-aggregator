ICRC Reaffirms Commitment to Partnership with Ethiopia - ENA English

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 16:06:28 GMT

The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has reaffirmed its commitment to enhancing its partnership with Ethiopia, recognizing the significant humanitarian contributions of the Ethiopian Red Cross Society (ERCS). In a meeting with President Taye Atske Selassie, ICRC Regional Director for Africa Mamadou Sow praised the ERCS for its extensive humanitarian efforts throughout the country. The discussions highlighted the importance of international humanitarian law and effective leadership in delivering essential assistance to those in need. Sow commended President Taye for fostering collaboration among various Ethiopian government ministries, which has facilitated ICRC's operations. He emphasized the critical role of the ERCS in implementing programs related to water, health, and food security, describing it as a vital partner. Additionally, Sow acknowledged the invaluable contributions of ERCS volunteers, calling them the backbone of the organization and the broader International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement. The partnership is seen as a source of pride for both the ICRC and Ethiopia. (159 words)
