Today's top news: Yemen, Occupied Palestinian Territory, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, Somalia - OCHA

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 18:08:58 GMT

The Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) highlights critical humanitarian crises in Yemen, the Occupied Palestinian Territory, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, and Somalia. In Yemen, ongoing conflict has displaced nearly 310,000 people since August, with Ta’iz city facing severe shortages of essential goods and rising prices. In Gaza, aid efforts continue amid persistent violence, while the West Bank sees disruptions in olive harvesting due to Israeli settler violence. The Democratic Republic of the Congo faces escalating violence, with significant civilian casualties and a rising Ebola outbreak. In Sudan, drone strikes on civilian infrastructure are exacerbating humanitarian needs, disrupting essential services like healthcare. Meanwhile, Somalia grapples with a convergence of crises, including famine risks and climate shocks, affecting over 1.7 million people. OCHA emphasizes the urgent need for humanitarian access and funding to address these escalating crises and protect civilians in affected regions.
