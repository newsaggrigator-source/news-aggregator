US authorities seize Ilhan Omar's DNA sample, Trump says she can be jailed, deported - India Today

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 16:06:02 GMT

US authorities have seized Congresswoman Ilhan Omar's DNA sample and passport as part of an investigation into her citizenship. This probe, which has intensified ahead of the November 3 midterm elections, examines allegations that Omar provided inaccurate information about her age on immigration documents decades ago. President Donald Trump has threatened Omar with jail or deportation, calling her "a disgrace to our country." The investigation reportedly includes claims that she may have committed immigration fraud by marrying her brother, although these allegations remain unproven. Omar has described the federal actions as political intimidation and has appealed for financial support for her campaign. The investigation's timing has raised concerns about its political motivations, with former President Barack Obama defending Omar and criticizing the inquiry as an alarming use of government power against an elected official.
