At Least 35 Christians Reportedly Killed in Fresh Attack in Ethiopia’s Arsi Zone - DNE Africa

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 18:03:22 GMT

At least 35 Christians were reportedly killed in a recent attack in the Arsi Zone of Ethiopia, highlighting the ongoing violence in the region. The assault occurred in the early hours, with armed assailants targeting a village during a religious gathering. Eyewitnesses described a chaotic scene as gunfire erupted, leading to widespread panic among the local population. This incident is part of a troubling trend of escalating violence against Christians in Ethiopia, particularly in areas where ethnic and religious tensions are high. Local authorities have condemned the attack and called for increased security measures to protect vulnerable communities. The Ethiopian government has faced criticism for its inability to prevent such violence, raising concerns about the safety of religious minorities in the country. As the situation develops, humanitarian organizations are calling for urgent assistance to support those affected by the violence and to address the underlying issues contributing to the unrest.
