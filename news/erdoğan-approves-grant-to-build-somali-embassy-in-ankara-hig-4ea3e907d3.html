Erdoğan Approves Grant to Build Somali Embassy in Ankara, Highlighting Asymmetric Ties and Sovereignty Questions - FTL Somalia

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 19:38:55 GMT

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan has approved a grant for the construction of the Somali Embassy in Ankara, as outlined in a recent presidential decree. This agreement, originally signed in 2022, allows Türkiye to design and build the embassy on a plot of land previously transferred to Somalia under a diplomatic protocol. The embassy, which will cover 3,000 square meters, is part of a reciprocal arrangement where Somalia provided land for Türkiye's embassy in Mogadishu. However, the deal has raised concerns about the asymmetry in the relationship, as Türkiye retains significant control over the construction and future use of the property. Somalia is required to use the premises solely for diplomatic purposes, and if the embassy is closed, ownership reverts to Türkiye. Critics argue that this dependency on Turkish support could undermine Somalia's sovereignty and limit its foreign policy autonomy, reflecting broader issues of diplomatic capture and financial oversight challenges.
