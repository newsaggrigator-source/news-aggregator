Ethiopian Passport Ranks Among World's Lowest, Limiting Travel to 17 Visa-Free Destinations

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 13:33:43 GMT

Ethiopian passport holders face significant travel limitations, ranking 90th globally on the 2026 Global Passport Power Index, alongside countries like Somalia and Yemen. They can only visit 17 countries without a visa and access an additional 33 destinations through visas on arrival. Among African Union member states, only five—Kenya, Benin, The Gambia, Rwanda, and Togo—offer visa-free entry to Ethiopians. The report highlights that stringent visa requirements, extensive documentation, and processing delays hinder international travel for Ethiopian citizens, impacting business, education, and cultural exchanges. Factors contributing to this low ranking include economic conditions, security concerns, and diplomatic relations, with foreign governments often considering income levels and security risks when setting entry requirements. The lack of reciprocal visa-waiver arrangements further complicates mobility, creating challenges for Ethiopian businesses and individuals seeking opportunities abroad. These restrictions not only affect commerce but also academic cooperation and international engagement.
