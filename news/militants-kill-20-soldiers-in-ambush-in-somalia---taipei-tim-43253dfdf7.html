Militants kill 20 soldiers in ambush in Somalia - Taipei Times

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 16:00:00 GMT

Militants from the al-Shabaab group ambushed a convoy of Somalian soldiers near Jameeca village, resulting in the deaths of at least 20 soldiers, according to a military officer who survived the attack. The assault occurred as government troops advanced into the area, leading to intense fighting. While the Somalian Ministry of Defence acknowledged damage to military vehicles, it did not confirm troop casualties. Al-Shabaab claimed responsibility for the ambush and asserted that it killed 321 soldiers, capturing additional military equipment and personnel, although these claims remain unverified. The Ministry reported that its forces killed approximately 75 militants during the confrontation. This incident highlights the ongoing conflict in Somalia, where al-Shabaab has been engaged in a protracted struggle against the government, utilizing guerrilla tactics and suicide bombings. The situation is further complicated by internal strife within the South West state, where government troops recently clashed with local militias.
