Somali man loses refugee status after fraudulent certificate and three-month visit to home country - IOL

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 03:26:02 GMT

A Somali man, Ahmed Mohammed Ali, has lost his refugee status after a High Court ruling upheld the withdrawal of his status due to a fraudulent certificate and a three-month visit to Somalia. The South Gauteng High Court found that Ali had voluntarily re-availed himself of the protection of his home country, Hargeisa, which he visited in 2017 to see his terminally ill father. The court dismissed his claims that the visit was motivated by humanitarian reasons, noting he failed to mention this in his initial representations against the withdrawal. Judge Mokate Victor Noko ruled that Ali's actions constituted re-availment under the Refugees Act, and his arguments regarding potential persecution upon deportation were rejected, as South Africa does not recognize Somaliland as independent. The court concluded that Ali's application to restore his refugee status was bound to fail due to his failure to challenge the fraudulent certificate basis for the withdrawal.
