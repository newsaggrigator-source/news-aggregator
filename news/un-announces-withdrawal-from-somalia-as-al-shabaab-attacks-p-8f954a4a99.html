UN Announces Withdrawal from Somalia as Al-Shabaab Attacks Persist in Kenya - Kenyans.co.ke

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 07:12:04 GMT

The United Nations (UN) has announced the conclusion of its transitional mission in Somalia, set to officially end on October 31, 2026, after over 30 years of involvement in peacebuilding efforts. This decision follows requests from Somalia's federal government to assume greater responsibility for its political and security matters, as well as resolutions from the UN Security Council in 2024 and 2025. Despite the mission's closure, UN Secretary-General António Guterres emphasized ongoing security challenges, particularly from the militant group Al-Shabaab, which continues to pose a threat along the Kenya-Somalia border. Kenya has faced multiple Al-Shabaab attacks, notably in Mandera County, complicating plans to reopen the border, which has been closed for over 13 years. While the UN mission ends, the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), which includes Kenyan troops, remains active, with its mandate set to expire on December 31, 2026, unless extended.
