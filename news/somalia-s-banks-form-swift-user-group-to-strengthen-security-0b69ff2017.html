Somalia’s Banks Form SWIFT User Group to Strengthen Security and Compliance - FTL Somalia

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 12:52:30 GMT

Somalia's Central Bank has established a SWIFT User Group, bringing together representatives from the country's banks to enhance security and compliance within the banking sector. This initiative aims to strengthen cooperation among banks and align with international standards, particularly in light of the increasing cyber threats targeting SWIFT's global messaging network. The user group will facilitate coordinated efforts to meet security standards, which is crucial for rebuilding correspondent banking relationships that have been strained due to de-risking practices by foreign banks. Currently, only a few Somali banks possess SWIFT codes, and the lack of direct dollar-clearing access results in an estimated annual loss of $210 million. The Central Bank has been working on broader reforms, including joining international financial organizations and preparing to reintroduce the Somali shilling. While the SWIFT User Group is a significant step toward improving security and compliance, it is not a standalone solution to the challenges facing Somalia's banking sector.
