Leaked Intelligence: Eritrean-Sudanese Coordination of Drone Attacks on Ethiopia's Mekelle & Assosa - horn review

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 17:00:25 GMT

Leaked intelligence suggests a troubling coordination between Eritrea and Sudan to conduct drone attacks on Ethiopian cities, specifically Mekelle and Assosa. This alleged collaboration indicates a significant escalation in hostilities, moving beyond proxy warfare to direct assaults on civilian targets. The Eritrean regime, led by Isaias Afwerki, has historically portrayed Ethiopia's maritime ambitions as a threat, while simultaneously supporting armed opposition groups against Ethiopia. Recent incursions by Eritrean forces into Ethiopian territory further complicate the already strained relations. Sudan's military leadership under General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan has also adopted a hostile stance towards Ethiopia, despite Ethiopia's efforts to maintain neutrality and humanitarian support amid Sudan's internal conflict. The invocation of self-defense by Sudan against Ethiopia raises concerns about potential military confrontations. The convergence of interests among Eritrea, Sudan, and Egypt poses a significant risk to regional stability, with the potential for direct aggression against Ethiopia threatening broader conflict in the Horn of Africa.
