Somali Military Court Sentences Police General’s Bodyguard to Death for Murder - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:16:35 GMT

The Somali Military Court has sentenced former police Corporal Jamaal Hassan Abdulle to death by firing squad for the murder of Brigadier General Salad Mohamed Guleid, a senior police commander. The court's ruling, delivered by Colonel Hassan Ali Nuur Shuute, revealed a case marked by betrayal and theft. Jamaal, who had been the general's bodyguard, killed him in June 2026 after selling a government-issued AK-47 rifle he was entrusted with to fund a betting account. Following the murder, he attempted to destroy evidence and misled investigators. A second defendant, Mustaf Hussein Guure, received a one-year sentence for his role in concealing the crime. This case highlights the challenges within Somalia's security forces, where personal indiscipline and weapon accessibility can lead to tragic outcomes. The military court's swift verdicts, while addressing public demands for accountability, have raised concerns about transparency and fairness in the justice system. The execution is seen as a significant step towards accountability for security personnel.
