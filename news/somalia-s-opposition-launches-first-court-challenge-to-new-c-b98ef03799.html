Somalia’s opposition launches first court challenge to new constitution - Somali Guardian

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 08:05:35 GMT

Somalia's opposition, led by Sharif Sheikh Ahmed, has initiated a court challenge against the newly approved constitution, which they claim was unilaterally drafted by the current government without opposition input. The Supreme Court has ordered the federal government to respond by Thursday. Regional states like Puntland and Jubaland, along with the opposition, reject the new charter, asserting that it lacks the broad consensus that characterized the 2012 constitution. Critics argue that the case will test the independence of Somalia's judiciary, which has long been perceived as influenced by political pressures. The federal government is exploring strategies to contest the opposition's claims and potentially delay court proceedings.
