Somalia Stands With Kenya Following Eastleigh Building Collapse - Dawan Africa

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 14:50:28 GMT

Somalia has expressed solidarity with Kenya following the tragic collapse of a multi-storey building in Eastleigh, Nairobi, on October 23, 2026. Somalia's Ambassador to Kenya, Jabril Ibrahim Abdulle, praised the Kenyan government and rescue teams for their swift and coordinated response during the crisis. As of Saturday, the Kenya Red Cross reported that 66 out of 67 construction workers had been accounted for, with one individual rescued from the rubble and receiving treatment at Kenyatta National Hospital. Ongoing rescue efforts are focused on locating several women who were conducting business near the site and remain unaccounted for. The incident has raised serious concerns regarding the building's structural integrity, prompting inquiries into the quality of materials used in its construction. Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja confirmed that the building had proper documentation but noted that the owner was still being sought. The situation remains critical as rescuers continue their search for any potential survivors.
