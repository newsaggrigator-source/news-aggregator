Somalia Offers Condolences to Qatar Following Martyrdom of Amiri Naval Forces Member - Qatar news agency

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 11:40:16 GMT

The Federal Republic of Somalia has expressed its heartfelt condolences to Qatar following the martyrdom of a member of the Amiri Naval Forces while on duty. In a statement issued by the Somali Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Somalia reaffirmed its solidarity with the Qatari government and its people during this tragic time. The statement conveyed prayers for God's mercy upon the deceased and extended sympathies to the bereaved family, wishing them patience and strength as they navigate their loss. This gesture underscores the strong diplomatic ties and mutual respect between Somalia and Qatar, highlighting their commitment to supporting each other in times of grief. The incident reflects the sacrifices made by military personnel in service to their nations, and Somalia's response illustrates its recognition of such sacrifices.
