EUCAP Somalia: Strengthening the Somali Coast Guard’s maintenance capabilities - EEAS

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 08:27:54 GMT

EUCAP Somalia has successfully concluded a nine-day training program aimed at enhancing the maintenance capabilities of the Somali Police Force's Coast Guard Department. The training, which ended on November 6, 2026, included three days of theoretical instruction on preventive maintenance planning, workshop safety, and resource management, attended by 11 participants, including Coast Guard Command officers. The subsequent six days focused on practical skills at the Coast Guard Boat Workshop, covering essential areas such as welding, mechanical maintenance, and surface treatment of materials. This initiative is designed to improve the Coast Guard's efficiency and safety in maintaining its fleet and infrastructure, ultimately supporting the long-term sustainability and operational readiness of Somalia's maritime assets. The program reflects EUCAP Somalia's commitment to bolstering maritime security and operational capabilities in the region.
