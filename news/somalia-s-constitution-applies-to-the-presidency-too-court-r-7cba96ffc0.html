Somalia’s Constitution Applies To The Presidency Too, Court Rules – OpEd - Eurasia Review

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 00:34:51 GMT

The Somali Supreme Court has ruled that the country's constitution applies to the presidency, affirming that the president must adhere to constitutional provisions. This landmark decision comes amid ongoing political tensions and debates regarding the interpretation of the constitution. The ruling emphasizes that the president is not above the law and must operate within the legal framework established by the constitution. This clarification is expected to have significant implications for the governance of Somalia, reinforcing the principle of accountability at the highest levels of power. The court's decision is seen as a step towards strengthening democratic institutions in Somalia and ensuring that all leaders are subject to the same legal standards. Observers believe this ruling could help mitigate political disputes and enhance the rule of law in the country, which has faced challenges in governance and stability. The ruling is a crucial development in Somalia's ongoing efforts to establish a robust democratic framework. (150 words)
