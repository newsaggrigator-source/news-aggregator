Somalia, India discuss expanding cooperation in trade, healthcare and education - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 28 Nov 2026 07:20:17 GMT

Somalia and India have engaged in discussions to enhance their bilateral cooperation in key areas such as trade, healthcare, education, and consular services. The talks were held between Somalia's State Minister for Foreign Affairs, Ali Mohamed Omar, and India's Minister of State for External Affairs, Pabitra Margherita. Both officials reviewed the current state of bilateral relations and explored strategies to strengthen cooperation. A significant focus was placed on establishing joint technical committees aimed at facilitating the implementation of existing agreements. The officials stressed the importance of moving beyond mere agreements to ensure practical benefits for their citizens. The proposed committees are intended to coordinate technical discussions and monitor progress in the identified sectors. Somalia and India have maintained diplomatic relations for decades, with ongoing collaboration in education, healthcare, trade, and capacity-building. Both nations expressed a commitment to ensuring that their agreements yield tangible outcomes for their populations. (158 words)
