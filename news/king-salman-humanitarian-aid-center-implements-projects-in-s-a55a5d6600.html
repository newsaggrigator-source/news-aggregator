King Salman Humanitarian Aid Center Implements Projects in Somalia, Yemen, and Gaza - صحيفة سبق الإلكترونية

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 21:15:40 GMT

The King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Center (KSRelief) has initiated several humanitarian projects in Somalia, Yemen, and Gaza to address urgent needs in these regions. In Somalia, the center is focusing on providing food assistance to vulnerable populations affected by drought and conflict. In Yemen, KSRelief is delivering medical supplies and healthcare services to support those impacted by the ongoing humanitarian crisis. Additionally, in Gaza, the center is working to provide essential supplies and support to families affected by recent conflicts. These initiatives reflect Saudi Arabia's commitment to alleviating suffering and improving living conditions in these areas. KSRelief continues to collaborate with various international organizations to ensure effective delivery of aid and to reach those most in need. The projects aim to provide immediate relief while also fostering long-term recovery and development in the affected regions.
