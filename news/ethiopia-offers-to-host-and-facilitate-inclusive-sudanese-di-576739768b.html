Ethiopia offers to host and facilitate inclusive Sudanese dialogue - Radio Dabanga

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 17:51:39 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has proposed to host and facilitate an inclusive dialogue among all military, political, and civilian factions in Sudan to end the ongoing conflict and achieve national consensus. He emphasized Ethiopia's readiness to leverage its diplomatic relations and regional influence to foster conditions conducive to negotiations and lasting peace. Despite accusations from Sudan regarding Ethiopia's support for the Rapid Support Forces, Ethiopia denies these claims and instead points to Sudan's alleged backing of the Tigray People's Liberation Front. Abiy's initiative aims to create a neutral platform for Sudanese parties to resolve their differences independently, focusing on restoring state institutions and ensuring national unity. He called for international support to aid the Sudanese people and emphasized that Sudan's stability is crucial for Ethiopia and the broader Horn of Africa region. Abiy expressed hope that all parties would prioritize Sudan's interests and engage in dialogue to establish a new social contract for peace.
