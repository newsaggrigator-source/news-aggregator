Somalia Hosts IGAD Regional Conflict Early Warning Meeting for First Time - Horseed Media

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 15:25:00 GMT

Somalia recently hosted the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD) Committee of Permanent Secretaries on Conflict Early Warning and Response in Mogadishu, marking the first time the country has taken on this role. The two-day meeting, which concluded on November 12, brought together representatives from IGAD member states, the organization’s secretariat, and experts from the Conflict Early Warning and Response Mechanism (CEWARN). The discussions focused on enhancing regional cooperation in conflict prevention and improving the identification of potential conflicts. Participants underscored the necessity of timely information-sharing and coordinated responses to security threats in the Horn of Africa. CEWARN aims to monitor instability indicators and support interventions to prevent conflicts from escalating. Somalia's hosting of the meeting reflects its increasing involvement in regional peace and security initiatives, particularly as the Horn of Africa grapples with various security challenges, including armed conflicts and political instability. The event highlights the importance of preventive diplomacy in addressing these shared challenges. (158 words)
