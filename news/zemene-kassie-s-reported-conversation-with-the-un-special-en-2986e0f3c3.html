Zemene Kassie’s Reported Conversation with the UN Special Envoy ... - Borkena

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 02:33:01 GMT

The reported conversation between Zemene Kassie, Chairman of the Amhara Fano National Movement, and Ambassador Guang Cong, the UN Special Envoy for the Horn of Africa, highlights shifting political dynamics in Ethiopia. This engagement suggests a potential re-evaluation of how the Ethiopian government addresses its internal conflicts, particularly regarding the Amhara movement, which has been framed as a security threat. The conversation underscores the importance of recognizing political grievances and the necessity of including various actors in conflict resolution efforts. It challenges the narrative that armed movements can be solely treated as security issues, advocating for a more nuanced understanding of Ethiopia's political landscape. The Alliance for Survival, which Kassie represents, aims to unify various Ethiopian forces with shared interests, emphasizing the need for a credible political program. This development is significant not only for Ethiopia but also for regional stability in the Horn of Africa, where interconnected security challenges persist.
