IG Kanja Orders Additional Police Deployment to Moyale Amid Ethiopia Border Crisis

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 06:12:30 GMT

Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja has ordered the deployment of additional police officers to Moyale, Marsabit County, due to escalating violence in neighboring Ethiopia, which has led to a significant influx of refugees into Kenya. This decision was made during a security assessment meeting on November 5, where Kanja emphasized the need for heightened vigilance among security personnel to protect residents and those seeking refuge. The violence in Ethiopia, linked to territorial disputes between communities, has resulted in numerous casualties and widespread displacement, with reports indicating that up to 50,000 people have crossed into Kenya. The humanitarian situation is dire, with many refugees lacking adequate shelter and basic necessities. Kanja assured residents of the National Police Service's commitment to maintaining stability and urged community cooperation in reporting security threats. The ongoing conflict has also disrupted cross-border trade, raising concerns about safety in the region.
