Somali Prime Minister Arrives in Jeddah - وكالة الأنباء السعودية

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 13:56:45 GMT

Somali Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre has arrived in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, as part of an official visit aimed at strengthening bilateral relations between Somalia and Saudi Arabia. During his visit, Prime Minister Barre is expected to engage in discussions with Saudi officials on various issues, including economic cooperation, security, and humanitarian assistance. This visit comes at a crucial time as Somalia faces significant challenges, including ongoing security threats from extremist groups and a humanitarian crisis exacerbated by drought and food insecurity. The Prime Minister's trip underscores Somalia's commitment to fostering partnerships with regional allies to address these pressing issues. Additionally, the visit is anticipated to enhance collaboration in areas such as trade and investment, which are vital for Somalia's recovery and development. The Somali government has expressed optimism that this visit will lead to tangible outcomes that benefit both nations and contribute to regional stability. The Prime Minister's delegation includes key ministers and advisors who will support discussions during the visit.
