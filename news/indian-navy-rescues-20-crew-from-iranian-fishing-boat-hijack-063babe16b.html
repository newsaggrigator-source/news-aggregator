Indian navy rescues 20 crew from Iranian fishing boat hijacked off Somalia - NPR

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 14:13:58 GMT

The Indian Navy successfully rescued 20 crew members from the Iranian fishing boat Azini, which had been hijacked off the coast of Somalia. The operation, lasting over 48 hours, involved a warship, a maritime surveillance aircraft, and marine commandos. The hijacking occurred on October 29, amid a resurgence of Somali piracy in the Gulf of Aden and the western Indian Ocean. Indian forces disarmed 12 suspected pirates during the operation and confiscated their mobile phones, with the suspects later brought ashore in northeastern Somalia. Fortunately, the operation concluded without any casualties. The Indian Defense Ministry emphasized the importance of the mission, as there were concerns that the hijacked vessel could be used as a base for further attacks on commercial shipping. This incident highlights the ongoing risks of piracy in the region, which has seen a troubling increase in hijackings despite previous improvements in maritime security.
