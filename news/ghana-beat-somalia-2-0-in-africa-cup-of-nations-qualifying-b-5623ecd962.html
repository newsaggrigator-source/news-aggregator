Ghana beat Somalia 2-0 in Africa Cup of Nations qualifying — BBC Sport - UA.NEWS

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 21:52:19 GMT

Ghana's national football team secured a 2-0 victory over Somalia in Sekondi-Takoradi during the qualification round for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON). This win marked Ghana's first points in Group C after suffering defeats in their initial matches against Côte d'Ivoire and Gambia. The match saw a missed penalty in the 14th minute when Jordan Ayew's shot was saved by Somalia's goalkeeper, Ibrahim Hussein. However, Ghana opened the scoring in first-half stoppage time with Jonas Adjetey netting a goal following a corner from Mohammed Kudus. Felix Afena-Gyan extended the lead shortly after the hour mark with a shot that slipped past Hussein. Following this victory, Ghana trails Côte d'Ivoire and Gambia by three points in the group standings. The match was overseen by Kim Björkegren, the interim coach, after the departure of Carlos Queiroz. In another qualifying match, DR Congo defeated Sierra Leone 3-1.
