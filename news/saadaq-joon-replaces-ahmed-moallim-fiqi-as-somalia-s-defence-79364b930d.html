Saadaq Joon replaces Ahmed Moallim Fiqi as Somalia's defence minister - Hiiraan Online

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 18:50:49 GMT

Somali Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre appointed Saadaq Omar Mohamed Hassan, known as Saadaq Joon, as the new defence minister on November 23, 2026, replacing Ahmed Moallim Fiqi. This change was part of a limited Cabinet reshuffle that also included two deputy ministerial appointments. The reasons for Fiqi's departure were not disclosed, nor was it confirmed if he would take on another role. Saadaq Joon, a member of the House of the People and former commander of the Banadir Regional Police Force, steps into the role amid ongoing military operations against al-Shabab and discussions regarding the future of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia. His appointment is believed to be linked to a power-sharing arrangement following recent political changes in Galmudug. The reshuffle also saw Ali Ifiye Ali and Maryan Ali Abukar appointed as deputy ministers for ports and fisheries, respectively, with the Prime Minister urging all officials to perform their duties with integrity.
