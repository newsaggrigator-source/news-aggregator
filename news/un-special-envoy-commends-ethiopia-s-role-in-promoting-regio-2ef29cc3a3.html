UN Special Envoy Commends Ethiopia’s Role in Promoting Regional Peace, Security - ENA English

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 13:29:59 GMT

United Nations Special Envoy for the Horn of Africa, Guang Cong, has praised Ethiopia for its significant contributions to regional peace and security. In an interview with the Ethiopian News Agency, he highlighted Ethiopia's role in peacekeeping and its hosting of platforms for dialogue, notably the Tana Forum in Bahir Dar, which facilitates discussions among national, regional, and international stakeholders on security challenges. Guang emphasized the importance of respecting sovereignty and territorial integrity among countries in the region and called for strengthening regional institutions to address security issues effectively. He noted Ethiopia's strategic importance, given its large population and growing economy, and its status as a diplomatic hub. Additionally, he acknowledged Ethiopia's efforts in peace initiatives, particularly in South Sudan. Regarding Ethiopia's quest for sea access, Guang advocated for resolution through dialogue and negotiations, reiterating the UN's commitment to support initiatives aimed at reducing regional tensions.
