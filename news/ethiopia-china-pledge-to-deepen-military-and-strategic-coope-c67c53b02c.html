Ethiopia, China Pledge to Deepen Military and Strategic Cooperation

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 04:53:53 GMT

Ethiopia and China have committed to enhancing their military and strategic cooperation, as reaffirmed by Ethiopia's Minister of Defense, Mohamed Idris, during discussions with Chinese Ambassador to Ethiopia, Chen Hai, in Addis Ababa. The talks focused on expanding collaboration in the defense industry, technology transfer, and military training. Minister Idris highlighted the significance of maintaining their historic military partnership while seeking new areas for cooperation, particularly in defense industrial development and capacity-building through training opportunities. He emphasized that Ethiopia's efforts to protect its national interests contribute to regional and international peace and stability, urging China to continue its support. Ambassador Chen Hai echoed this sentiment, expressing China's readiness to deepen military ties and expand cooperation in the defense sector, noting the positive outcomes from their ongoing investment and bilateral engagement. This partnership aims to elevate their strategic cooperation to a higher level, reinforcing their longstanding military relationship.
