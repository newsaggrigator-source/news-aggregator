El Niño threatens food security despite wheat production gains - Capital Newspaper

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 22:00:00 GMT

El Niño is posing a significant threat to global food security, despite recent gains in wheat production. The phenomenon, characterized by warmer ocean temperatures in the Pacific, is expected to disrupt weather patterns, leading to adverse effects on crop yields. While wheat production has seen improvements in some regions, the overall impact of El Niño could negate these gains, particularly in vulnerable areas. Experts warn that the increased likelihood of droughts and floods could severely affect agricultural output, exacerbating food shortages and inflation. Countries heavily reliant on wheat imports may face heightened risks, leading to potential social unrest. As nations prepare for the upcoming El Niño event, there is an urgent need for strategic planning and investment in agricultural resilience to mitigate the impending challenges to food security.
