Somalia condemns Israeli envoy’s accreditation in breakaway Somaliland - Anadolu Ajansı

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 20:27:49 GMT

Somalia has condemned Israel's recent accreditation of an ambassador to the breakaway region of Somaliland, declaring the move null and void. The Somali Foreign Ministry stated that this action, taken without the consent of the Federal Government, violates Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity. It emphasized that Somaliland is an inseparable part of Somalia and that diplomatic relations require the approval of the sovereign state, as outlined in the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations. The ministry criticized Israel's actions as exacerbating tensions in a fragile region, especially amid ongoing insecurity in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden. Somalia called for Israel to reverse its decision and urged other nations and international organizations to reject any engagement that might legitimize this accreditation. The government affirmed its commitment to defending Somalia's sovereignty and unity through all appropriate diplomatic and legal means. (151 words)
