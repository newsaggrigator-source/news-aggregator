AUSSOM commander meets Hirshabelle leader amid tensions with Hiiraan region authorities - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 17:54:05 GMT

Brig. Gen. Michel Ndenzako, commander of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) Sector 5, met with Hirshabelle State President Ali Abdullahi Hussein Guudlawe in Jowhar to discuss security coordination amid rising tensions with Hiiraan region authorities. The meeting, held at Villa Hirshabelle, emphasized collaboration between AUSSOM, Somali security forces, and local authorities to enhance security and protect critical infrastructure. Guudlawe praised the AUSSOM Burundi National Defence Force for their ongoing support. This dialogue occurs against the backdrop of a dispute between the Hirshabelle administration and Hiiraan authorities, particularly following Hiiraan Governor Mohamed Hussein Mohamed's rejection of a dismissal by Hirshabelle. The conflict has led to disruptions in customs operations, including the closure of a customs checkpoint in Matabaan and the establishment of a new one in El-Gal. Both sides expressed a commitment to work together for peace and stability in the region. (159 words)
