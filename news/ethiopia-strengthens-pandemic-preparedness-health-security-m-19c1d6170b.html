Ethiopia Strengthens Pandemic Preparedness, Health Security, Minister Says

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 05:20:59 GMT

Ethiopia has made notable advancements in its health security and pandemic preparedness, as announced by Health Minister Dr. Mekdes Daba. The country has raised its International Health Regulations (IHR) capacity score from 62 to 70, largely due to a $50 million grant from the Pandemic Fund, supplemented by $63 million in national funding. Key improvements include enhanced laboratory capabilities, with more facilities achieving international accreditation, and an increase in antimicrobial resistance surveillance sites from 26 to 41. Ethiopia has also expanded its specimen transportation services and operationalized an electronic emergency management platform across eight regions. The country successfully contained the Marburg virus outbreak and is preparing for potential Ebola threats. Additionally, the Ministry of Agriculture has improved animal disease detection and surveillance through expanded training programs. Dr. Mekdes emphasized the need for sustained investment and alignment of international funding with national priorities to ensure lasting results in health security. (159 words)
