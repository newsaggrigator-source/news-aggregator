Puntland President Warns Somalia Faces Threat to Statehood Amid Election Dispute - Garowe Online

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 12:35:44 GMT

Puntland President Said Abdullahi Deni has issued a stark warning regarding the threat to Somalia's statehood amid ongoing election disputes. Speaking at the opening of Puntland's parliamentary session, Deni accused the federal government, led by President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, of undermining the federal system and failing to uphold political agreements, including the provisional constitution. He expressed concern that the country is entering a perilous phase, with political instability escalating to violence, including drone attacks targeting officials. Deni emphasized that Puntland will not accept an election process dictated solely by the federal government and called for a national conference to address these issues. He urged Somalis to unite in safeguarding state institutions and stressed the need for broad political consensus on constitutional and electoral matters. The Puntland leader's remarks highlight the ongoing tensions between regional administrations and the federal government, complicating efforts to establish a stable political framework in Somalia.
