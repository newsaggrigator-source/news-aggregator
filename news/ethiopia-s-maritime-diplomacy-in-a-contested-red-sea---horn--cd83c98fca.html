Ethiopia’s Maritime Diplomacy in a Contested Red Sea - horn review

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 17:49:42 GMT

Ethiopia is enhancing its maritime diplomacy amid the complexities of the Red Sea region, as evidenced by a recent workshop held in Addis Ababa from October 26 to 29, 2026. This event brought together naval commanders and maritime representatives from Yemen, Oman, Djibouti, Somaliland, and Puntland to discuss maritime search and rescue, information sharing, and regional security. Ethiopia's geographical landlocked status has historically limited its access to maritime routes, making cooperation with neighboring countries essential for its economic interests. The workshop highlights Ethiopia's efforts to engage with coastal nations despite being excluded from regional security forums like the Council of Arab and African States Bordering the Red Sea. While Ethiopia's relationships with Somaliland and Puntland are crucial for diversifying transport corridors, they also come with political complexities. The meeting underscores Ethiopia's commitment to building maritime governance and security partnerships, which are vital for its economic future and regional stability.
