Jarma Mohamed: How Jersey Bulls midfielder became a Somalia international - BBC

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 07:13:52 GMT

Jarma Mohamed, a 21-year-old midfielder for Jersey Bulls, has made a remarkable journey from youth football with prestigious clubs like Liverpool and Manchester City to representing Somalia internationally. After being released by Wigan Athletic, he found himself at a crossroads until he was discovered through a mobile app designed for footballers. Mohamed's Somali heritage, stemming from his grandfather, allowed him to join the national team, which is ranked 200th globally. He made his debut in March against Mauritius, helping Somalia reach the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers for the first time in history. Currently, he balances his role as a player with coaching youth football on Jersey. Despite facing tough competition in the qualifiers, including matches against Ghana and Ivory Coast, Mohamed remains optimistic, emphasizing teamwork and a competitive spirit. His journey reflects resilience and the importance of community in pursuing sporting dreams.
