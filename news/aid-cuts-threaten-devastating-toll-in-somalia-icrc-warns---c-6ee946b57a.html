Aid cuts threaten devastating toll in Somalia, ICRC warns - China Daily

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 13:27:01 GMT

The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has issued a stark warning regarding the severe consequences of declining humanitarian aid in Somalia, where funding has plummeted from approximately $2.3 billion in 2022 to around $300-$350 million. This drastic reduction threatens vulnerable communities already grappling with the dual crises of drought and conflict, compounded by the impending El Niño-induced floods. ICRC head Antoine Grand emphasized the urgent need for international support, as closures of nutrition centers and health facilities leave families with limited options for assistance. The UN's humanitarian appeal for Somalia, aimed at helping 2.4 million people, remains only 30% funded. With 3.3 million people displaced and access to clean water and healthcare severely restricted, the situation is dire. Grand highlighted the importance of anticipatory action and investment in infrastructure to bolster resilience against future crises. He urged donors not to overlook Somalia amid competing global priorities, stressing that reduced aid leads to increased suffering.
