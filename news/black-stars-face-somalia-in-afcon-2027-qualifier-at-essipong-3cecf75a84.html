Black Stars face Somalia in AFCON 2027 qualifier at Essipong today - GBC Ghana Online

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 08:00:54 GMT

The Black Stars of Ghana are set to face Somalia today in a crucial qualifier for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) at the Essipong Sports Stadium, with kick-off scheduled for 3:00 PM. This Matchday 3 encounter sees Somalia playing as the home side, despite the match taking place in Ghana. The Black Stars are eager to recover from two consecutive defeats against Côte d’Ivoire and The Gambia, leaving them without any points in the qualifying campaign thus far. Conversely, Somalia aims to leverage their home advantage, even while competing on Ghanaian soil. This match marks the first of two meetings between the teams, with a return fixture planned for November 16 at the same venue, providing both sides with an opportunity to improve their standings in the AFCON qualifiers. The stakes are high as both teams seek to secure vital points in their quest for qualification.
