DP Kithure Kindiki Arrives in Ethiopia for 12th Tana Forum on African security - the-star.co.ke

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 06:24:17 GMT

Deputy President Kithure Kindiki has arrived in Ethiopia to participate in the 12th Tana Forum on African security, a significant event focusing on security challenges and solutions across the continent. The forum, which gathers leaders, policymakers, and experts, aims to foster dialogue and collaboration on pressing security issues affecting African nations. This year’s discussions are expected to cover a range of topics, including conflict resolution, peacebuilding, and the impact of climate change on security. Kindiki's attendance underscores Kenya's commitment to regional stability and its role in addressing security concerns in East Africa. The Tana Forum serves as a platform for sharing best practices and strategies to enhance security cooperation among African states. As the forum progresses, participants will engage in discussions that aim to strengthen partnerships and develop actionable plans to tackle the myriad of security challenges facing the continent today. The outcomes of this forum could significantly influence future security policies in the region.
