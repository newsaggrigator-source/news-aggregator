Ethiopian Airlines and Air Peace Sign Bilateral Interline Agreement to Enhance Connectivity Across Africa and Beyond

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 14:59:37 GMT

Ethiopian Airlines and Air Peace have signed a bilateral interline agreement aimed at enhancing air transport connectivity across Africa and beyond. This partnership allows both airlines to issue tickets for itineraries that include flights operated by either carrier, facilitating a more integrated travel experience for passengers. Travelers can book qualifying journeys on a single ticket and enjoy through-checked baggage, eliminating the need for separate bookings and re-checks. The agreement connects Air Peace's domestic and regional network with Ethiopian Airlines' extensive international routes, providing seamless access for travelers from Nigeria and West Africa to global destinations via Addis Ababa. Ethiopian Airlines Group CEO Mesfin Tasew and Air Peace Chairman Dr. Allen Onyema emphasized the collaboration's potential to strengthen economic ties and support tourism by simplifying travel arrangements. This partnership is expected to contribute significantly to regional integration and economic development, aligning with the goals of the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA). (159 words)
