More Than 60 Killed in Oromia as Ethiopia’s Instability Deepens, Raising Alarm for Mogadishu - FTL Somalia

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 10:10:52 GMT

More than 60 people were killed in Ethiopia's Oromia region, highlighting the escalating instability in the country and raising concerns for neighboring Somalia. The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church reported that the victims were targeted in attacks attributed to a newly formed rebel coalition, the Alliance for Survival, which includes the Oromo Liberation Army (OLA). Eyewitness accounts revealed that children were among the dead and numerous homes were destroyed. This incident is part of a broader pattern of sectarian violence in Oromia, where over 458 people have died in similar attacks in recent years. The violence also affects ethnic Somali communities, evidenced by clashes in the border town of Moyale. The ongoing turmoil in Ethiopia is diverting military resources away from Somalia, creating a security gap as the UN political mission concludes. The situation underscores the interconnectedness of regional security, with Ethiopia's instability posing direct threats to Somalia's safety and political landscape.
