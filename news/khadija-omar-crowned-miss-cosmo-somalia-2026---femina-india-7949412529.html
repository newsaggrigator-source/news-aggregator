Khadija Omar Crowned Miss Cosmo Somalia 2026 - Femina India

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 14:44:26 GMT

Khadija Omar has been crowned Miss Cosmo Somalia 2026, making history as the first representative from Somalia to compete in the international pageant. The 25-year-old Somali-Canadian model, entrepreneur, and humanitarian was born in a refugee camp in Kenya and raised in Canada. Her diverse interests, including basketball, makeup, and photography, have led her to a successful career in entrepreneurship and pageantry. Khadija has previously made history as Somalia's first contestant in both Miss World and Miss Universe. Beyond pageantry, she serves as a Smile Train ambassador, advocating for cleft care, and founded A Smile from Somalia to support children's access to treatment. Through her fashion brand, House of Hooyo, she promotes Somali heritage and empowers African women. At Miss Cosmo 2026 in Vietnam, Khadija aims to inspire others by demonstrating that dreams should not be limited by circumstances and that embracing one's identity can be a source of strength.
