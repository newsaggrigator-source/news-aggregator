Egypt discusses ties, regional developments with Somalia, Djibouti - Egyptian Gazette

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 14:56:12 GMT

Egypt's Minister of Foreign Affairs, Badr Abdelatty, met with Somali counterpart Abdisalam Abdi Ali on November 7, 2026, during the 22nd Ordinary Session of the Executive Council of the Community of Sahel-Saharan States in Tripoli, Libya. The discussions aimed to enhance bilateral relations and coordinate on regional issues, focusing on economic, development, and investment cooperation. Abdelatty emphasized Egypt's commitment to supporting Somalia's state institutions and reaffirmed Cairo's stance on Somalia's unity and sovereignty. The ministers also addressed developments in the Horn of Africa, highlighting the importance of safeguarding national institutions. In a separate meeting, Abdelatty engaged with Djiboutian Foreign Minister Abdoulkader Houssein Omar to discuss strengthening ties and regional matters, particularly concerning Sudan and the Red Sea. Both meetings underscored the need for collaboration among Red Sea littoral states to ensure maritime security and freedom of navigation.
