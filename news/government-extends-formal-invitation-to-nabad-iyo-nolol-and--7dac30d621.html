Government Extends Formal Invitation to Nabad iyo Nolol and Other Rivals for November Dialogue - FTL Somalia

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 09:19:35 GMT

President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has formally invited four political and civic groups, including former President Mohamed Abdullahi Farmaajo’s Nabad iyo Nolol party, to a National Consultative Forum in Mogadishu from November 14 to 18, 2026. The forum aims to address federal election matters and seek a political consensus. This invitation marks a significant shift in the government's approach, as Mohamud has previously maintained a unilateral electoral timetable that has faced opposition. The Somali Future Council, which includes leaders from Puntland and Jubaland, has rejected the government's timeline and demands independent mediation. The invitation's political implications are complex, as it may be a strategy to engage opposition figures separately, potentially isolating the more hardline factions. The response from the invited parties remains uncertain, with concerns about the legitimacy of the forum and whether it will genuinely address the opposition's core demands. The outcome could either facilitate dialogue or exacerbate the ongoing political crisis in Somalia.
