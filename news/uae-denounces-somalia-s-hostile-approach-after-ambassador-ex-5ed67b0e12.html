UAE denounces Somalia’s ‘hostile approach’ after ambassador expelled - Somalia Today

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 20:01:02 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has condemned Somalia's "escalatory and hostile approach" following the expulsion of its ambassador, Ahmed Jumaa Alremeithi, who was given 72 hours to leave the country. The UAE's Ministry of Foreign Affairs criticized Somalia's actions as detrimental to bilateral relations and cooperation. This diplomatic rift follows Somalia's earlier decision to cancel all agreements with the UAE, citing violations of national sovereignty. Somalia's government accused the UAE of establishing unauthorized military bases and undermining federal authority by engaging with regional administrations. In response, the UAE emphasized its commitment to the Somali people and its ongoing support for Somali military forces. Tensions have escalated amid Somalia's internal political struggles, with President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud alleging that the UAE is interfering in Somalia's affairs. The UAE's embassy in Mogadishu was closed earlier this year due to what it described as hostile practices by the Somali government.
