Somalia president term extension ‘unlawful’: Supreme Court - Arab News

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 13:57:04 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court has ruled that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's one-year term extension is "unlawful." Mohamud's term officially ended in May, but his government declared a "transition period" to prepare for upcoming democratic elections scheduled for April and May 2027. The president argued that this extension was legal based on constitutional amendments passed by a parliament vote in March, which was boycotted by opposition parties and regional leaders. The Supreme Court determined that these amendments did not apply to his current term, insisting he should have vacated office in May. In response to the ruling, Mohamud dismissed Chief Justice Bashe Yusuf Ahmed, citing concerns about judicial efficiency. However, the court rejected this dismissal, labeling it an attempt to obstruct justice regarding the constitutional case. The situation has led to tensions, with unconfirmed reports suggesting that the chief justice may have been arrested following his dismissal.
