What Did Ilhan Omar’s Father Do in Somalia? - National Review

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 13:43:36 GMT

Ilhan Omar, a prominent U.S. Congresswoman, has often spoken about her family's background and experiences as Somali refugees. Her father, Nur Omar Mohamed, played a significant role in their family's history in Somalia. Before fleeing the country due to civil war, he was a teacher and a community leader. His educational background and commitment to social issues shaped the values he instilled in his children. After relocating to the United States, he continued to emphasize the importance of education and civic engagement. Omar's upbringing in a politically active family has influenced her own political career, as she advocates for immigrant rights and social justice. The article explores the complexities of her father's past and how it has impacted her identity and political views, highlighting the broader narrative of Somali immigrants in America and their contributions to society. Omar's story reflects the resilience of immigrant families and the challenges they face in adapting to a new life.
