Ethiopia Establishes Abbay and Red Sea Forum - Borkena

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 19:54:58 GMT

Ethiopia has launched the Abbay and Red Sea Forum, aimed at promoting the country's water and maritime interests amid regional diplomatic challenges. This initiative, spearheaded by civil society leaders and policy experts in Addis Ababa, seeks to advance Ethiopia's strategic, historical, legal, and economic claims concerning the Nile Basin and access to the Red Sea. Ethiopian Minister of Water and Energy, Dr. Habtamu Itefa, emphasized the importance of securing direct access to the Red Sea as a national priority, especially following developments related to the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD). The forum has been legally recognized and plans to mobilize scholars and professionals to provide evidence-based policy recommendations. Key objectives include raising public awareness and collaborating with regional organizations like the African Union and IGAD. Meanwhile, neighboring littoral states, including Egypt, Somalia, and Djibouti, have reaffirmed their commitment to regional maritime governance, emphasizing the importance of maintaining security in the Red Sea.
