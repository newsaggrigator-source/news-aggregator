Mogadishu tourism: The young Somali woman changing how the world sees her city - BBC

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 01:19:49 GMT

In Mogadishu, Sucdi Abdul Fattah Abdul Khader, a 23-year-old tour guide, is reshaping perceptions of her city, which is often associated with violence and insecurity. She aims to showcase Mogadishu's beauty, culture, and welcoming atmosphere through guided tours that highlight its scenic spots, such as Jazeera beach and local markets. Sucdi, who began guiding tours as a hobby, now works with her father and uses social media to attract visitors. Despite the challenges posed by the city's tumultuous history and ongoing security concerns, tourism is slowly growing, with a reported 50% increase in visitors in 2024. Many tourists express surprise at the city's charm and the friendliness of its people, contrasting sharply with negative media portrayals. Sucdi hopes to continue promoting a more positive image of Mogadishu, emphasizing its rich history and vibrant culture while encouraging others to explore the country beyond the headlines.
