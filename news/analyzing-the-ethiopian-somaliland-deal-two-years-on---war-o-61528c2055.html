Analyzing the Ethiopian-Somaliland Deal, Two Years On - War on the Rocks

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 19:04:03 GMT

Two years after the Ethiopian-Somaliland deal, the threat of al-Shabaab's expansion remains a concern, although it has not yet materialized as predicted. In a 2024 analysis, James Barnett warned that the agreement could exacerbate tensions in the Horn of Africa, potentially leading to a broader conflict. While al-Shabaab has not launched significant attacks in Ethiopia or Somaliland, it continues to maintain sophisticated networks in the region. New dynamics, such as al-Shabaab's growing connections with the Houthis, could pose additional risks for both countries. As the U.S. focuses on other global issues, the situation remains precarious, and experts caution against complacency. The evolving landscape underscores the need for vigilance and strategic partnerships, particularly with Mogadishu, to counter the persistent threat of al-Shabaab. The analysis highlights the complexities of regional security and the potential for future instability if not addressed.
