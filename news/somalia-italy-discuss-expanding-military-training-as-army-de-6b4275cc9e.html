Somalia, Italy Discuss Expanding Military Training as Army Delegation Visits Turin - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 12:20:46 GMT

A delegation from the Somali National Army, led by Major General Ahmed Isse Ahmed, visited Italy to discuss enhancing military training cooperation and strengthening defense ties between Somalia and Italy. The delegation toured Italy’s Training and Doctrine Command headquarters in Turin and engaged with Italian military officials to explore ways to improve the skills and capabilities of Somali forces. This visit is part of ongoing high-level discussions between Somali and Italian defense leaders, including previous meetings focused on advanced training for special operations units and tactical support for ground troops. Italy has been a key security partner for Somalia, contributing significantly to the EU Training Mission in Somalia (EUTM) and leading initiatives to bolster police forces. Despite these efforts, questions remain about the long-term effectiveness of the training, as the Somali National Army continues to rely on external support. The visit underscores Italy's commitment to Somalia's security while highlighting the need for a cohesive national military strategy.
