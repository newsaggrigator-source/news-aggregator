More Than 64, 000 Ethiopian Refugees, Asylum-Seekers Remain in War-Hit Sudan, UN Data Shows

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 15:23:07 GMT

As of October 31, 2026, there are 64,902 Ethiopian refugees and asylum-seekers in Sudan, primarily due to the ongoing conflict that began in April 2023. The United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR) reports that over two-thirds of these individuals are concentrated in Gedaref State, which hosts 43,177 Ethiopians, accounting for 67% of the total. The overall refugee population in Sudan stands at 921,476, with Ethiopians representing about 7%. The UNHCR also highlights significant internal displacement, with 273,817 refugees relocating within Sudan. Among the Ethiopian refugees, 70% reside in camps, while 30% live outside them. The demographic breakdown shows that women make up 45% and children aged 17 and below represent 35% of the population. Additionally, there are 1,326 individuals with disabilities and 712 unaccompanied or separated children among the registered Ethiopian refugees.
