Ethiopia Mobilizes Global Partnerships, Climate Finance Ahead of COP32

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 05:33:40 GMT

Ethiopia is ramping up its efforts to attract global partnerships and climate finance as it prepares to host the 32nd United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP32) in November 2027. Finance Minister Fitsum Assefa emphasized the importance of COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, as a platform for Ethiopia to showcase its readiness and influence in climate negotiations. The country is presenting its national pavilion, featuring around 60 programs that highlight its climate initiatives and cultural identity, aiming to engage international stakeholders and secure investment opportunities. Ethiopia's domestic initiatives, such as the Green Legacy Initiative, which promotes environmental protection through massive tree planting, and its reliance on renewable energy sources, underscore its commitment to climate resilience. Fitsum noted that Ethiopia's ambition for COP32 extends beyond participation; it aims to advance substantive climate negotiations and mobilize necessary investments to address global climate challenges, thereby enhancing Africa's role in the global climate agenda.
