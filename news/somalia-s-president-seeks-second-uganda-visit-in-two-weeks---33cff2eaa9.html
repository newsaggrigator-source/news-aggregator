Somalia’s president seeks second Uganda visit in two weeks - Somali Guardian

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 14:23:30 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has requested a one-day visit to Uganda to meet with President Yoweri Museveni, marking his second trip to the country in two weeks. This visit underscores Somalia's ongoing efforts to secure security and diplomatic support from Uganda, particularly regarding the presence of Ugandan troops in Somalia. During his previous visit in late October, Mohamud sought to delay the withdrawal of these troops, who are crucial in maintaining control over strategic towns in the Lower Shabelle region, but his request was denied, and the troop drawdown has commenced. Additionally, Mohamud's government is facing challenges, including a Supreme Court ruling declaring the extension of his presidential term unlawful. In light of these issues, he has dispatched advisers to seek financial support from African Union and European Union partners to strengthen his administration amid ongoing political and security difficulties in Somalia.
