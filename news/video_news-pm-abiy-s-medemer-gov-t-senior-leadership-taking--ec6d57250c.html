A video has surfaced showing senior leaders of Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's Medemer government participating in military training at the Hurso center. This initiative appears to be part of a broader effort to enhance the capabilities of government officials in response to ongoing security challenges in Ethiopia. The training is likely aimed at equipping leaders with skills to better manage crises and ensure national stability. The footage has sparked discussions on social media regarding the implications of government officials undergoing military training, especially in a country facing various internal conflicts. Observers are questioning the necessity and potential impact of such training on governance and civil-military relations in Ethiopia. The government has not provided detailed information about the objectives or duration of the training, leaving many to speculate on its significance in the current political climate. The video has garnered significant attention, reflecting public interest in the government's approach to security and leadership preparedness.
