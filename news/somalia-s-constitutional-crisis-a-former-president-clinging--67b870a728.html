Somalia’s Constitutional Crisis: A Former President Clinging to Power and a Chief Justice Who Found His Voice After Dismissal - Garowe Online

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 18:32:13 GMT

Somalia is facing a significant constitutional crisis following the dismissal of Supreme Court Chief Justice Bashe Yusuf Ahmed by former President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. This action has raised serious concerns regarding the legitimacy of presidential authority and judicial independence. The Supreme Court ruled the dismissal unlawful, asserting that both the president's and parliament's terms had expired, thus necessitating elections within 90 days. The controversy stems from amendments that extended presidential terms, which have been contested by opposition leaders. The Supreme Court's late intervention has prompted scrutiny over its previous silence during the political turmoil surrounding these amendments. The situation highlights the fragility of Somalia's constitutional order, as both the presidency and judiciary grapple with their roles and responsibilities. Ultimately, the crisis underscores the need for a constitutional framework that upholds the law consistently, rather than selectively, to ensure the integrity of Somalia's governance and judicial processes.
