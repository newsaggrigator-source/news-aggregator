Ethiopia Wins Seat On Expanded UN Aviation Council With 133 Votes

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 15:21:40 GMT

Ethiopia has secured a seat on the governing council of the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) after receiving 133 votes during a recent election held on November 20, 2026, in Montreal, Canada. This election was part of an extraordinary session of the ICAO Assembly, which expanded the council from 36 to 40 members by adding four new seats. Alongside Ethiopia, Türkiye, Oman, and Indonesia were also elected to the council. The Ethiopian Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced the achievement on social media, highlighting the significant support received from member states. Ethiopia now joins eight other African nations on the council, including Egypt, Nigeria, South Africa, and Uganda. The newly elected council members will serve until the next elections at the ICAO's 44th Assembly in 2028, marking a notable moment for Ethiopia in international aviation governance.
