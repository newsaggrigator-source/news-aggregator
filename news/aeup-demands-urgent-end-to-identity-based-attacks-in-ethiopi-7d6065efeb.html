AEUP Demands Urgent End to Identity-Based Attacks in Ethiopia - Borkena

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 01:25:53 GMT

The All Ethiopian Unity Party (AEUP) has urgently called for an end to identity-based violence and human rights abuses in Ethiopia. In a statement released on October 22, 2026, the opposition party condemned the ongoing mass killings and arbitrary detentions, particularly in the Arsi and Wollega zones, highlighting the grief and fear that have affected communities due to security failures. AEUP emphasized the need for an inclusive political system that prioritizes shared citizenship and human dignity over divisive structures. The party demanded independent investigations into the alleged atrocities and accountability for those responsible, asserting that the government's legitimacy hinges on its ability to protect citizens' lives and rights. AEUP also called for immediate protection for peaceful residents and the safe return of internally displaced persons to their homes. The party concluded by stressing the importance of establishing a legal framework based on equal citizenship and the rule of law to ensure lasting peace and security in Ethiopia.
