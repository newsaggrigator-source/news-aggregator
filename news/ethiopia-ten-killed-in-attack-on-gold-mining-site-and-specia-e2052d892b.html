Ethiopia : Ten Killed in Attack on Gold Mining Site and Special Forces Patrol in Bero Woreda - Borkena

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 04:25:03 GMT

In an armed attack on November 18, 2026, in Bero woreda, Southwest Ethiopia Peoples’ Region, ten individuals were killed, including nine members of regional special forces and a security guard at a gold mining site. Eyewitnesses reported that the assailants targeted the Chilema Shet area, setting fire to company staff quarters and vehicles, including heavy machinery. The special forces were ambushed while responding to the attack, resulting in the deaths of nine out of ten personnel in their patrol vehicle, with one member sustaining injuries. Families of the deceased received the bodies for burial shortly after the incident. The region has a history of insecurity, with previous attacks affecting both locals and foreign visitors. Following his appointment in September 2026, Regional Chief Administrator Fikre Aman vowed to tackle security challenges in the pastoralist areas of the region. Local officials have not commented on the incident.
