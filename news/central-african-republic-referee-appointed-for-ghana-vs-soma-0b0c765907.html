Central African Republic referee appointed for Ghana vs Somalia AFCON qualifier - GhanaWeb

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 18:21:04 GMT

The Central African Republic has appointed a referee for the upcoming Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifier between Ghana and Somalia. The match is set to take place on November 13, 2023, and is crucial for both teams as they aim to secure a spot in the prestigious tournament. The referee's selection is part of the Confederation of African Football's (CAF) efforts to ensure that matches are officiated by qualified individuals. This appointment highlights the importance of maintaining high standards in officiating during international competitions. Ghana, a historically strong team in African football, will be looking to capitalize on their home advantage, while Somalia aims to make a significant impact in the qualifier. The match is expected to draw attention from fans and analysts alike, as both teams prepare for this pivotal encounter in their AFCON qualifying campaign.
