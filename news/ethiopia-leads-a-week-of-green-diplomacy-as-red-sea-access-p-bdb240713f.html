Ethiopia Leads a Week of Green Diplomacy As Red Sea Access Push Gains International Momentum

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 05:53:46 GMT

In November 2026, Ethiopia showcased its diplomatic efforts at COP31 in Türkiye, emphasizing its commitment to climate leadership and its aspiration for sovereign access to the Red Sea. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed highlighted the need for accessible climate finance and practical solutions to address climate vulnerabilities, particularly given Ethiopia's reliance on hydropower and the impacts of climate change on agriculture and livelihoods. The country is preparing to host COP32 in 2027, aiming to transition from pledges to actionable outcomes. Concurrently, Ethiopia's quest for Red Sea access gained international recognition, with support from various political figures who acknowledged its legitimacy and potential for regional stability. Ethiopia's Water and Energy Minister reiterated the importance of equitable utilization of Nile waters, advocating for cooperation among riparian countries. Overall, Ethiopia's diplomatic engagements reflect its ambitions for sustainable development, regional cooperation, and a more significant role in global climate discussions. (160 words)
