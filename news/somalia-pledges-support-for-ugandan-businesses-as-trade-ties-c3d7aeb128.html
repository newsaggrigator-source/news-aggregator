Somalia pledges support for Ugandan businesses as trade ties deepen - Horseed Media

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 17:15:31 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has expressed strong support for Ugandan businesses operating in Somalia, aiming to enhance economic ties alongside their existing security partnership. This commitment was made during Uganda's 64th independence anniversary celebrations in Mogadishu, where President Mohamud assured Ugandan private sector representatives of cooperation from Somali institutions. The pledge follows a three-day economic diplomacy event in Mogadishu, which included the mid-term review of the Uganda-Somalia Joint Permanent Commission and a business forum featuring over 100 Ugandan delegates and 146 Somali representatives. Discussions focused on collaboration in various sectors, including trade, investment, and tourism. The two countries have been working together since 2022 to strengthen their economic relationship, with Uganda Airlines increasing flights to Mogadishu to facilitate trade and travel. President Mohamud also acknowledged Uganda's significant role in promoting peace and stability in Somalia, highlighting the historical ties and sacrifices made by Ugandan troops in the region.
