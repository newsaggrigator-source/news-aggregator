US forces accused of ‘weaponising’ Somalia’s floods by trapping women and children in the path of El Niño - The Guardian

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 11:01:00 GMT

US forces in Somalia are facing accusations of "weaponising" the country's floods amid a military campaign against the Islamist group al-Shabaab. Reports indicate that coordinated airstrikes are forcing civilians to seek refuge near the flood-prone River Juba, exacerbating their vulnerability as El Niño intensifies the rainy season. Eyewitness accounts describe a relentless barrage of airstrikes, leading to significant civilian casualties and destruction in towns like Jilib. Critics argue that the US military strategy appears to exploit the flooding to weaken al-Shabaab, raising ethical concerns about the use of humanitarian data for military purposes. While US officials deny targeting civilians or using weather as a weapon, the situation remains dire, with insufficient flood defenses and a lack of humanitarian aid. The International Committee of the Red Cross warns of severe flooding, and local authorities express concern over the potential for large-scale displacement and loss of life as conditions worsen.
