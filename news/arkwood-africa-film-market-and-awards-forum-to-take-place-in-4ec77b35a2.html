Arkwood Africa Film Market and Awards Forum to Take Place in Addis Ababa

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 05:24:25 GMT

The second edition of the Arkwood Africa Film Market and Awards Forum is set to take place at the Adwa Victory Memorial Museum in Addis Ababa from November 27-28, 2026. Organized by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, this event aims to advance Africa's audiovisual industry by connecting filmmakers, investors, and distributors. Over 500 professionals from various African nations are expected to attend, with the theme "Building the African Audiovisual Industry Ecosystem: From Plan to Action." State Minister Nebiyu Baye highlighted the government's efforts to create a supportive environment for artistic works that reflect Pan-African ideals. The forum will feature four major panel discussions on topics such as policy frameworks, women's participation in film, and the direction of film education in Ethiopia. Additionally, a four-day film costume design training is currently being held at the Addis Ababa Cinema Complex Art Gallery, leading up to the main event.
