Somalia’s WTO Bid Nears Completion After Technical Mission in Mogadishu - FTL Somalia

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 18:28:52 GMT

Somalia's bid to join the World Trade Organization (WTO) is progressing, nearing completion following a technical mission in Mogadishu from October 10 to 15. Led by the WTO's Head of Accessions Division, the mission focused on refining Somalia's accession documents and preparing market access offers. This effort, funded by the UK’s Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office, aims to address questions regarding customs, licensing, and health standards. Somalia's leaders view WTO membership as a pathway to economic growth and investment, particularly for its livestock sector, which constitutes 75% of exports. However, challenges remain, including the need to meet international standards and manage commitments to regional trade agreements like the East African Community and the African Continental Free Trade Area. While the technical mission marks significant progress, Somalia must still finalize its offers and demonstrate its capacity to implement WTO rules effectively before full accession can be achieved. The next working party meeting is anticipated in 2027. (158 words)
