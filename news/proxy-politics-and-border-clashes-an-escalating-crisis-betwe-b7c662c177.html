Proxy politics and border clashes: an escalating crisis between Sudan and Ethiopia – Ayin network - شبكة عاين

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 19:39:15 GMT

The diplomatic tensions between Sudan and Ethiopia have escalated, moving beyond mere border disputes to encompass broader geopolitical interests. Recently, Ethiopia closed the Sudanese consulate in Assosa, prompting Sudan to reciprocate by shutting down the Ethiopian consulate in Gedaref. This diplomatic fallout followed Sudan's complaint to the UN and African Union, accusing Ethiopia of allowing the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) to launch drone strikes from its territory. Ethiopia denied these allegations and countered with accusations against Sudan, Cairo, and Asmara for supporting Tigrayan fighters. The situation is further complicated by regional rivalries involving Egypt and Eritrea, particularly concerning the management of the Nile waters and the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam. Analysts warn that the conflict is evolving into a proxy war, with both nations leveraging non-state actors to exert influence. This indirect warfare threatens regional stability and could have catastrophic consequences, drawing in neighboring countries and undermining state sovereignty across the Horn of Africa.
