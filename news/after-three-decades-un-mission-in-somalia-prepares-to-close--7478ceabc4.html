After three decades, UN mission in Somalia prepares to close operations - Capital Newspaper

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 11:44:53 GMT

After three decades of operations, the United Nations is set to close its mission in Somalia, marking the end of a significant chapter in the country's history. Established in 1992, the mission aimed to address the humanitarian crisis and support peace efforts amid ongoing conflict. Over the years, the UN has played a crucial role in providing aid, facilitating political dialogue, and promoting stability in a nation plagued by civil war and instability. The decision to withdraw comes as Somalia has made strides towards self-governance and security, although challenges remain, including threats from extremist groups. The UN's departure is seen as a pivotal moment for Somalia, as it transitions towards greater autonomy and responsibility for its own governance. The mission's closure reflects both achievements and ongoing struggles, highlighting the complex dynamics of international intervention in conflict zones.
