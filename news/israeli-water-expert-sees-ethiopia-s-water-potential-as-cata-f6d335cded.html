Israeli Water Expert Sees Ethiopia's Water Potential As Catalyst for Agricultural Superpower Ambition

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 21:33:44 GMT

Israeli water expert Ravid Levy has highlighted Ethiopia's significant potential to become an agricultural superpower through the effective harnessing of its water resources. Speaking at the Third Ethiopian Water and Energy Week in Addis Ababa, Levy emphasized that investments in hydropower and infrastructure could transform Ethiopia's agricultural sector, enhance energy production, and stimulate broader economic growth. He noted the interconnectedness of water and energy, advocating for coordinated development in both sectors to improve water management and support sustainable economic advancement. Levy also pointed out the importance of modern water-management technologies and suggested that collaboration between Ethiopian and Israeli institutions could lead to innovative solutions for water-related challenges. He stressed that strong government commitment and active participation from universities and private companies are essential for translating shared development interests into tangible results, ultimately benefiting both nations in addressing their water challenges and advancing technological innovation.
