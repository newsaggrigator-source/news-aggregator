India, Somalia review security cooperation during first bilateral visit in years - The Times of India

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 15:08:00 GMT

India and Somalia have engaged in discussions to enhance their security cooperation during a significant bilateral visit, marking the first high-level Indian delegation to Somalia in years. The meeting took place between India's MEA Secretary Sripriya Ranganathan and Somali Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi, focusing on key areas such as maritime security, counter-terrorism, and capacity building. Ranganathan's visit aims to strengthen bilateral defense ties and address the regional security landscape. The High Commission of India in Nairobi, which oversees relations with Somalia, emphasized the importance of this visit in reinforcing the longstanding friendship between the two nations. India and Somalia have shared maritime and commercial links for centuries, with formal diplomatic relations established in 1960. Despite closing its resident mission in Mogadishu in 1991 due to civil unrest, India has continued to support Somalia through development assistance and security cooperation. Ranganathan's visit is expected to explore new avenues for diplomatic and developmental partnerships. (159 words)
