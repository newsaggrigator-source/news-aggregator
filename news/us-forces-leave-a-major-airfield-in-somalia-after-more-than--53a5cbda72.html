US forces leave a major airfield in Somalia after more than a decade - Task & Purpose

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 22:01:09 GMT

U.S. forces have vacated Baledogle Airfield in Somalia after 14 years of operations, although they will continue their presence in the country. The airfield, located approximately 60 miles from Mogadishu, has been a strategic site for U.S. counterterrorism efforts, particularly against the militant group al-Shabaab. The exit was first reported by Horn Observer, and a military official confirmed that troops have relocated to other areas within Somalia, with the possibility of returning to Baledogle in the future. While the exact number of troops stationed at the airfield prior to their departure remains unclear, the U.S. military has historically maintained a significant presence there. The airfield has been pivotal in training Somali commandos and conducting operations against militants. Despite the withdrawal from Baledogle, U.S. forces have continued airstrikes against al-Shabaab, indicating an ongoing commitment to counterterrorism in the region.
