Somalia aviation authority, IATA sign agreement to strengthen safety oversight - Hiiraan Online

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 22:17:10 GMT

The Somalia Civil Aviation Authority (SCAA) has signed a significant agreement with the International Air Transport Association (IATA) aimed at enhancing safety oversight in the country's aviation sector. This partnership is expected to improve regulatory frameworks and operational standards, ultimately fostering a safer flying environment in Somalia. The agreement includes provisions for training and capacity-building initiatives for local aviation personnel, ensuring compliance with international safety standards. The collaboration reflects Somalia's commitment to developing its aviation industry and enhancing air travel safety, which is crucial for economic growth and connectivity in the region.
