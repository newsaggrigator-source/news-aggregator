Mekelle Residents Call for Sustained Federal Govt Efforts to Advance Reforms in Tigray

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 17:05:32 GMT

Residents of Mekelle, Ethiopia, have called on the Federal Government to enhance and expedite reform and development efforts in the Tigray region. This appeal was made during a consultative forum led by Field Marshal Birhanu Jula, Chief of Staff of the Ethiopian National Defense Force. Participants expressed a strong desire for lasting peace, security, and sustainable development, emphasizing the urgent need to rebuild damaged infrastructure and create job opportunities for youth. They welcomed the resumption of salary payments for civil servants and ongoing rehabilitation projects as positive steps toward restoring normalcy. The residents urged the government to maintain its commitment to Tigray and to accelerate initiatives aimed at fostering peace and improving livelihoods. They highlighted that their primary concerns include the reconstruction of critical infrastructure and the restoration of security, allowing citizens to live without fear. The forum concluded with a collective call for sustained federal efforts to ensure stability and prosperity in the region.
