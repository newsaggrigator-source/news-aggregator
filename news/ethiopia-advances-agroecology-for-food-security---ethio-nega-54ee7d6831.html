Ethiopia Advances Agroecology for Food Security - Ethio Negari

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 17:26:35 GMT

Ethiopia is implementing an agroecology-based strategy to enhance food security, restore degraded land, and bolster farmers' resilience to climate change. Developed by the Ministry of Agriculture in collaboration with various stakeholders, this comprehensive strategy addresses the entire food system, focusing on sustainable farming practices, crop production, and natural resource management. At the Third National Agroecology Conference in Addis Ababa, officials emphasized the need for agricultural approaches tailored to Ethiopia's diverse ecological conditions, particularly soil fertility improvement. The conference gathered policymakers, researchers, and farmers to share successful agroecological practices and promote sustainable food production. Challenges such as climate variability, soil erosion, and land degradation were highlighted, particularly affecting smallholder farmers reliant on rain-fed agriculture. Agroecology practices, including crop rotation and composting, are seen as vital for improving soil health and biodiversity. This initiative aligns with Ethiopia's broader efforts in environmental protection and sustainable land management, underscoring the importance of integrating ecological farming into national policies.
