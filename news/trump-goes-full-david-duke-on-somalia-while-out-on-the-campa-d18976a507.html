During a recent campaign event, former President Donald Trump made controversial remarks about Somali immigrants, claiming that "98% of the people that we have here now in Somalia don't work" and suggesting they live lavish lifestyles funded by theft. He further asserted that they drive luxury cars like Mercedes-Benzes and implied they possess "low IQ." Trump's comments have drawn comparisons to the rhetoric of David Duke, a known white supremacist, raising concerns about the implications of such statements on public perception of Somali immigrants. The remarks sparked backlash on social media, with critics highlighting the unfair generalizations and stereotypes perpetuated by Trump. Supporters of the former president argue that he is addressing issues related to immigration and taxpayer burdens. The discourse surrounding Trump's comments reflects broader tensions in American society regarding immigration, economic disparity, and racial stereotypes, with many calling for a more nuanced understanding of immigrant contributions to the economy.
