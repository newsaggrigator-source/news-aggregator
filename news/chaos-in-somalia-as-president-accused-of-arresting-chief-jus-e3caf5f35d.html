Chaos in Somalia as president accused of arresting chief justice - The Japan Times

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 03:10:00 GMT

Somalia is experiencing a deepening political crisis as President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud faces accusations of unlawfully arresting the Supreme Court's chief justice. This turmoil follows the court's declaration that Mohamud's extension of his presidential term, which he announced during a "transition period," was illegal. The situation has escalated tensions between the government and opposition factions, leading to violent clashes amid ongoing challenges from the Islamist group Al-Shabaab. Mohamud has aimed to transition Somalia towards democratic elections, moving away from a clan-based political system. However, the recent actions against the judiciary have raised concerns about the rule of law and the government's legitimacy, further complicating the country's efforts to stabilize and gain international support. The unfolding events highlight the fragile state of Somalia's political landscape and the potential for increased unrest.
