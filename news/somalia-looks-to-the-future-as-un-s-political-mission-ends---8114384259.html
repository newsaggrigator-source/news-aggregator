Somalia Looks To The Future As UN's Political Mission Ends - Genocide Watch

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 06:00:23 GMT

The United Nations' political mission in Somalia, known as UNTMIS, officially concluded on October 31, marking the end of three decades of international engagement aimed at stabilizing the country. Raisedon Zenenga, the UN Secretary-General's Deputy Special Representative for Somalia, highlighted the progress made in governance and state-building since the early 1990s, despite ongoing challenges such as insecurity, contested elections, and humanitarian crises exacerbated by climate shocks. With the mission's closure, Somalia's institutions are now tasked with leading the recovery efforts, supported by various UN agencies still active in the region. These agencies continue to address pressing humanitarian needs, with millions of Somalis requiring assistance. The UN emphasizes the importance of ongoing international support to sustain progress and tackle issues like poverty and security threats from groups like Al-Shabaab. Zenenga noted that this transition signifies not an end, but a new chapter in the partnership between the UN and Somalia.
