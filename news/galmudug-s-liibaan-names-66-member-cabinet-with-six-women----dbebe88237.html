Galmudug’s Liibaan Names 66-Member Cabinet With Six Women - FTL Somalia

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 22:06:49 GMT

Galmudug's President Liibaan Ahmed Hassan has appointed a 66-member cabinet, which includes 24 full ministers, 18 state ministers, and 24 deputy ministers, as per Presidential Decree No. 007 issued on November 22, 2026. Notably, only six women are included in this cabinet, with one serving as a full minister, Haweya Isma’il Ahmed, who heads the Ministry of Family, Children and Human Rights. The remaining key positions, particularly in security and finance, are held exclusively by men. This cabinet formation follows Liibaan's election on October 22, which was marked by federal intervention and a controversial electoral process. Critics argue that the limited representation of women reflects broader issues of gender inclusion in Somali politics. Additionally, the large size of the cabinet raises concerns about financial sustainability in a region facing significant developmental challenges. Liibaan's administration is now tasked with addressing pressing security and humanitarian issues while proving its governance extends beyond political patronage.
