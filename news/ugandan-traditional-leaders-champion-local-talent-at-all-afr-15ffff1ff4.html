Ugandan Traditional Leaders Champion Local Talent At All Africa Leather Fair in Addis Ababa

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 11:22:33 GMT

A delegation of Ugandan traditional leaders recently visited the Ugandan Embassy in Addis Ababa during the 6th Continental African Traditional Leaders Conference. They emphasized the need for collaboration between cultural institutions and diplomats to promote Uganda's heritage and foster growth across Africa. The delegation, which included notable cultural leaders, discussed strategies to unite communities and enhance Uganda's cultural representation. They later attended the 15th All African Leather Fair, where Ugandan designer Yoram Atuhamize was recognized as one of the Top 15 African Leather Exhibitors for his socially conscious footwear initiative. The conference, which took place from November 10-15, aimed to strengthen the role of traditional leaders in resolving local disputes and preserving cultural values, while also connecting them with initiatives like the African Continental Free Trade Area to support local businesses and youth opportunities. The event culminated in the signing of the Addis Ababa Declaration, establishing the African Traditional Leaders Council. (160 words)
