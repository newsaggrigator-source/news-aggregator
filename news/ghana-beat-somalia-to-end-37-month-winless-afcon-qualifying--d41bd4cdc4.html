Ghana beat Somalia to end 37-month winless AFCON qualifying run as Ivory Coast lead Group C - Arab News

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 02:43:15 GMT

Ghana ended a 37-month winless streak in Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers by defeating Somalia 2-0 in Essipong. This victory marked Ghana's first win in Group C after previous losses to Ivory Coast and Gambia. Defender Adjei Adjetey scored the opening goal just before halftime, followed by Felix Afena-Gyan, who secured the win with a second goal in the 62nd minute. Despite the win, Ghana's performance raised concerns, as both goals resulted from defensive errors by Somalia, ranked 200th globally. Meanwhile, Ivory Coast maintained their lead in Group C by beating Gambia 2-1, accumulating nine points. Ghana now has three points, while Somalia remains without any. The Black Stars are set to face Somalia again, with hopes of securing another win to improve their chances of qualifying for the tournament. The upcoming matches against Ivory Coast and Gambia will be crucial for Ghana's campaign.
