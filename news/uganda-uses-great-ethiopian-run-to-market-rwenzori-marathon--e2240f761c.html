Uganda Uses Great Ethiopian Run to Market Rwenzori Marathon, Boost Tourism

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 01:54:23 GMT

Uganda is leveraging the Great Ethiopian Run to promote the upcoming 2027 Tusker Lite Mt Rwenzori Marathon, aiming to boost tourism and strengthen ties with Ethiopia. The Uganda Embassy in Addis Ababa participated in the 26th edition of the run on November 22, 2026, which attracted over 60,000 participants. The Rwenzori Marathon, scheduled for September 4, 2027, in Kasese District, is designed to showcase Uganda as a premier adventure tourism destination. Ambassador Rebecca Amuge Otengo emphasized the importance of sports diplomacy in fostering relations between the two nations. The marathon will feature various race categories, starting at the Equator and ending in Queen Elizabeth National Park, with a total prize purse of Shs290 million. This initiative aligns with Uganda's broader Economic and Commercial Diplomacy agenda, which aims to significantly expand the economy by 2040, with tourism playing a crucial role in achieving these goals.
