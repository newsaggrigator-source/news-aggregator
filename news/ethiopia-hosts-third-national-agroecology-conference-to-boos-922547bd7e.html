Ethiopia Hosts Third National Agroecology Conference to Boost Climate Resilience and Food Security - Fidel Post

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 15:34:27 GMT

Ethiopia recently hosted its Third National Agroecology Conference at the Ministry of Agriculture Conference Center, focusing on the importance of ecological farming in combating climate change and food insecurity. Organized by PELUM Ethiopia alongside civil society and development partners, the conference gathered researchers, policymakers, and farming communities under the theme “Agroecology for Environmental Health, Sustainable Food Systems and Climate Resilience.” Key speakers emphasized the need to incorporate ecological principles into national agricultural policies, advocating for the scaling up of agroecological practices to restore degraded soils and protect smallholder farmers from extreme weather. The event featured an exhibition of indigenous seeds and local farming tools, showcasing farmer-led adaptation techniques. The conference concluded with awards recognizing outstanding contributions to seed conservation and sustainable farming. Stakeholders underscored the necessity of ongoing collaboration to implement the conference's recommendations effectively. This initiative reflects Ethiopia's commitment to enhancing climate resilience and food security through sustainable agricultural practices. (160 words)
