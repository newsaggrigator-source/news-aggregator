Somalia will remain neutral in disputes between Ethiopia and Eritrea, president says - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 11:56:25 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has declared that Somalia will maintain a neutral stance in the ongoing disputes between Ethiopia and Eritrea. In an interview with Sky News on October 30, 2026, he emphasized that Somalia has established good relations with both nations and does not intend to intervene in their conflicts. The president highlighted that Somalia's foreign policy is centered on peaceful coexistence, mutual understanding, and cooperation with neighboring countries. He reiterated that the government’s priority is to ensure stability within Somalia while fostering constructive relationships externally. As tensions rise between Ethiopia and Eritrea over regional security and political interests, Somalia aims to avoid involvement in these disputes. Mohamud's statements reflect Somalia's commitment to promoting stability in the Horn of Africa through dialogue and cooperation, reinforcing the country's position of non-interference in the affairs of its neighbors.
