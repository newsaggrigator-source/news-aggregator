Black Stars must deliver in crucial Somalia test as AFCON 2027 qualifiers resume at Essipong on Thursday - Business & Financial Times

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 03:54:32 GMT

The Black Stars of Ghana are set to face Somalia in a pivotal match for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers at the Essipong Sports Stadium in Sekondi-Takoradi on November 12, 2026. This encounter is crucial for the Black Stars as they aim to rejuvenate their qualifying campaign in Group C. The team will be looking to secure a vital win to enhance their chances of advancing in the tournament. The match comes at a critical juncture, and the players are under pressure to deliver a strong performance against Somalia. Fans and analysts alike are eager to see if the Black Stars can rise to the occasion and improve their standing in the qualifiers. The outcome of this match could significantly impact Ghana's aspirations for the upcoming AFCON tournament.
