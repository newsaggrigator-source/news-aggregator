Is Somalia’s President Looking for an External Enemy to Distract from His Failures? - Horseed Media

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 13:32:13 GMT

Somalia is facing a deepening political crisis under President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, who has been accused of extending his presidential term and consolidating power through controversial constitutional amendments. These changes, which have sparked fierce opposition and violent clashes in cities like Mogadishu and Baidoa, have led to allegations of corruption and nepotism within his administration. The Supreme Court recently ruled that Mohamud's mandate expired in May 2026, ordering elections within 90 days, further complicating his political standing. Amid these challenges, Mohamud has escalated tensions with the United Arab Emirates, accusing it of meddling in Somalia's affairs. This move is seen by some analysts as a strategy to distract from his government's failures and the internal crises plaguing the nation. Critics argue that the real issues stem from domestic governance failures rather than external threats, highlighting the complexities of Somalia's political landscape.
