Somalia: Senior NISA commander defects to Al-Shabaab, raising fears of intelligence breach - Somali Guardian

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 18:28:12 GMT

A senior commander from Somalia's National Intelligence and Security Agency (NISA), known as Luqman, has defected to Al-Shabaab, raising significant concerns about a potential intelligence breach. Luqman, who led the Duufaan paramilitary unit trained by Turkey, reportedly left with critical assets, including laptops and communication devices. Analysts, such as Rashid Abdi from Sahan Research, warn that this defection could severely compromise NISA, as Al-Shabaab may now access sensitive intelligence, including the identities of informants in areas controlled by the militant group. NISA is reportedly struggling to manage the fallout from this incident, which could have dire implications for national security. Luqman is believed to be in territory controlled by Al-Shabaab in southern Somalia, further complicating the situation for Somali intelligence efforts.
