Trump’s Obsession With Somalia Isn’t Just Racist. It’s Been Lethal. - The Intercept

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 10:00:00 GMT

The Trump administration has been accused of waging a targeted campaign against Somali people, characterized by military actions, arrests, and deportations. This includes the demonization of Somalis as criminals and terrorists, particularly focusing on Rep. Ilhan Omar, who has faced federal investigations and threats of deportation. Under Trump's leadership, U.S. military strikes in Somalia have surged, resulting in the deaths of hundreds, often without clear evidence of the victims' affiliations with terrorist groups. Critics argue that these actions reflect a broader pattern of Islamophobia and xenophobia, with Trump's rhetoric exacerbating the plight of Somali refugees. The ongoing conflict in Somalia, fueled by U.S. interventions, has led to a significant increase in displaced persons, highlighting the complex relationship between U.S. foreign policy and the Somali diaspora in America.
