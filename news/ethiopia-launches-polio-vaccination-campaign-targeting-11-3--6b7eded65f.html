Ethiopia Launches Polio Vaccination Campaign Targeting 11.3 Million Children

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 05:12:40 GMT

Ethiopia has initiated a polio vaccination campaign aimed at over 11.3 million children across five regions, responding to ongoing outbreaks of circulating variant poliovirus. Launched on November 21, the campaign targets areas with low immunization coverage, including Addis Abeba, Oromia, Central Ethiopia, South Ethiopia, and Southwest Ethiopia. The first round of vaccinations will run until November 24, followed by a second round from December 18 to 21. Health workers and community volunteers are conducting house-to-house visits to administer vaccines and identify under-immunized children. Dr. Loveday Nkwogu from WHO emphasized the importance of collective efforts to protect children, highlighting that each vaccinated child brings Ethiopia closer to a polio-free future. Although Ethiopia is currently free of wild poliovirus, there have been reports of variant poliovirus outbreaks in several regions. WHO is providing extensive support for coordination, monitoring, and surveillance to enhance immunization efforts and prevent further transmission. (158 words)
