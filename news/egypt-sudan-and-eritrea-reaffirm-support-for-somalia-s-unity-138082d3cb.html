Egypt, Sudan and Eritrea reaffirm support for Somalia's unity, reject parallel entities - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 12:03:35 GMT

The foreign ministers of Egypt, Sudan, and Eritrea met on November 21, 2026, during the sixth Aswan Forum for Sustainable Peace and Development in Egypt, where they reaffirmed their commitment to Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity. They rejected the establishment of parallel political entities that could undermine Somalia's state institutions. The ministers emphasized the need for closer regional cooperation, particularly regarding Red Sea security and maritime navigation. They also discussed the ongoing conflict in Sudan, expressing support for its sovereignty and the preservation of national institutions while opposing foreign interference. The meeting built on agreements from a previous summit involving Egypt, Somalia, and Eritrea, highlighting the importance of coordination among these nations to address regional security threats. The ministers reiterated their stance against unilateral actions affecting regional stability and stressed the principle of noninterference in the internal affairs of states, aiming to strengthen regional stability and development in the Horn of Africa.
