Ethiopia, India Seek to Deepen Strategic Trade and Investment Partnership

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 16:34:33 GMT

Ethiopia and India are working to enhance their strategic trade and investment partnership, as discussed during a meeting between Ethiopia's Minister of Trade and Regional Integration, Yasmin Wohabrebbi, and India's newly appointed Ambassador to Ethiopia, Devesh Uttam. The discussions focused on expanding bilateral trade and investment, addressing trade imbalances, and integrating regional markets. Yasmin emphasized the opportunities arising from Ethiopia's economic reforms, which aim to open the economy to private sector participation and improve foreign investment conditions. She highlighted Ethiopia's membership in the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) as a significant advantage for Indian companies looking to invest in the region. Ambassador Uttam reaffirmed India's commitment to strengthening ties with Ethiopia, recognizing it as a vital partner and gateway to Africa. The meeting underscored the longstanding diplomatic relations and mutual respect between the two nations, with both sides expressing interest in further cooperation across various sectors.
