Ethiopia Urges Egypt to Modernize Water Use, Consult On Nile Development Projects

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 05:16:52 GMT

Ethiopia's Minister of Water and Energy, Habtamu Itefa, has called on Egypt to modernize its water management practices and consult Ethiopia on Nile development projects. In an interview, he criticized Egypt's reliance on traditional flood irrigation and water-intensive crops, which he claims lead to significant water wastage. Habtamu emphasized that Egypt should focus on improving its domestic water efficiency rather than blaming upstream countries like Ethiopia for their water challenges. He urged Egypt to abandon unilateral actions regarding the Nile and recognize Ethiopia's development interests, particularly in light of the 1959 Nile Waters Agreement that excluded Ethiopia. He highlighted the importance of cooperation and mutual understanding, advocating for joint investments in water infrastructure to benefit all Nile Basin countries. Habtamu reaffirmed Ethiopia's right to utilize its water resources for national development while considering the needs of downstream nations. He called for constructive dialogue to resolve Nile-related differences and promote equitable water management. (157 words)
