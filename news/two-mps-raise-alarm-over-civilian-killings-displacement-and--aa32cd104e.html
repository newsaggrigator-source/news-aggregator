Two MPs Raise Alarm Over Civilian Killings, Displacement and Insecurity in Ethiopia During Abiy's Parliamentary Appearance

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 11:02:09 GMT

During a parliamentary session, two Ethiopian MPs expressed grave concerns regarding escalating human rights violations, civilian killings, and widespread displacement in the country. Dr. Aweke Hamzaye, representing the Ethiopian Citizens for Social Justice, highlighted alarming findings from the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission, emphasizing that citizens' right to life is under severe threat due to violence from government forces and armed groups. He specifically pointed to the Koore community, which has faced persistent attacks for over a decade. Another MP, Gebremeskel Ademe of the Amhara Democratic Movement, criticized the government's response to insecurity in regions like Amhara and Oromia, referencing a recent massacre in Arsi. He urged the Prime Minister to investigate internal issues within the government that may hinder security efforts and called for measures to protect displaced residents. The session also addressed various other topics, including economic growth and infrastructure projects, amidst ongoing conflicts and humanitarian challenges. (158 words)
