Somalia: GCC Chief Condemns 'Irresponsible' Remarks By Somalia Officials Targeting UAE - allAfrica.com

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 09:29:56 GMT

The Secretary-General of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), Jasem Mohamed Albudaiwi, has condemned what he termed "irresponsible statements" and "misleading media campaigns" by officials from Somalia's federal government targeting the United Arab Emirates (UAE). In a statement from Riyadh, Albudaiwi asserted that these allegations were intended to undermine the UAE's diplomatic efforts, which he described as crucial for promoting regional and international dialogue. While the statement did not specify the officials or the remarks in question, Albudaiwi emphasized the UAE's significant role in fostering security and stability, citing its recent facilitation of talks between Russia and Ukraine as an example of its commitment to peace. He reiterated the GCC's rejection of any media campaigns that disparage its members and affirmed the bloc's dedication to enhancing security and prosperity in the region. The GCC comprises Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE.
