Somalia expels UAE ambassador, recalls envoy from Abu Dhabi - Premium Times Nigeria

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 03:48:25 GMT

Somalia has expelled the UAE ambassador, ordering him to leave within 72 hours, and has recalled its envoy from Abu Dhabi, citing violations of its sovereignty. The announcement was made by Information Minister Abdifatah Mohamud, who emphasized that measures would be taken against anyone cooperating with the UAE, which he accused of invading Somalia's sovereignty and security. This decision escalates tensions between the two nations, following Somalia's annulment of agreements with the UAE earlier this year, including those related to seaport operations and security cooperation. President Hassan Mohamud has accused the UAE of supporting regional Somali governments against the federal government and establishing military bases outside Somalia's command. The UAE has denied any interference, describing Somalia's actions as "escalatory and hostile," and expressed regret over the deterioration of bilateral relations.
