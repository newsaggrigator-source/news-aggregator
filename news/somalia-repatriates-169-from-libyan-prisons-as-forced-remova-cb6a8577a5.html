Somalia Repatriates 169 from Libyan Prisons as Forced Removals from Europe Continue - FTL Somalia

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 12:25:16 GMT

The Federal Government of Somalia has successfully repatriated 169 citizens from Libyan prisons, with 140 arriving in Mogadishu and 29 in Hargeisa. This operation, part of the EU-supported Voluntary Humanitarian Return programme, aims to provide a safe and dignified return for migrants who have faced exploitation and abuse. The International Organization for Migration (IOM) facilitated the return, emphasizing the importance of voluntary participation and family reunification. This repatriation follows a pattern of similar operations, with over 2,000 Somali migrants returned since August 2022. In contrast, forced deportations from European countries, such as Sweden's recent removal of nine Somali nationals, highlight a different approach to migration management, raising concerns about the conditions driving youth to undertake perilous journeys. While the Somali government engages in discussions to prevent irregular migration, the underlying issues of unemployment and insecurity remain unaddressed, necessitating a more proactive policy to create sustainable solutions for young Somalis.
