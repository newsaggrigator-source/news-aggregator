Ghana Beat Somalia To Record First Win In 2027 AFCON Qualifiers - TVC News

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 19:58:00 GMT

Ghana secured its first victory in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers with a 2-0 win against Somalia at the Essipong Sports Stadium. The match, held on November 12, 2026, saw defender Jonas Adjetey score the opening goal in first-half stoppage time, followed by a second goal from substitute Felix Afena-Gyan in the 62nd minute. This win lifted Ghana off the bottom of Group C and alleviated some pressure on interim coach Kim Lars Björkegren, while Somalia remained without a point after three matches. Despite dominating the first half, Ghana struggled initially, with captain Jordan Ayew missing a penalty. The two teams are set to face each other again on November 16, with the match designated as Somalia's home game due to their lack of an approved stadium. Ghana aims to build on this momentum, while Somalia seeks to recover from another defeat. (151 words)
