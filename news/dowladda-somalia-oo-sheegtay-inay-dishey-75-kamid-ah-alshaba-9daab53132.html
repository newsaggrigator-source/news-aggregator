Dowladda Somalia oo sheegtay inay dishey 75 kamid ah Alshabaab. - Horseed Media

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 05:29:22 GMT

Dowladda Federaalka Soomaaliya ayaa sheegtay in Ciidanka Xoogga Dalka Soomaaliyeed ay ku dileen 75 dagaalyahan oo ka tirsan Al-Shabaab, kadib dagaal culus oo ka dhacay deegaanka Jameeca, degmada Buurhakaba ee gobolka Baay, 26-kii Oktoobar 2026. War-saxaafadeed ka soo baxay Wasaaradda Gaashaandhigga Soomaaliya ayaa muujinaya in sidoo kale 56 dagaalyahan oo Al-Shabaab ah lagu dhaawacay dagaalka. Intii uu dagaalku socday, Al-Shabaab ayaa isticmaalay gaadiid laga soo buuxiyey walxaha qarxa si ay weerar ugu fuliyaan ciidamada dowladda, iyadoo dowladda ay ka digtay in la buunbuuniyo wararka kooxda Al-Shabaab. Kooxda Al-Shabaab ayaa sheegtay inay burburisay gaadiid ka tirsan ciidamada dowladda, iyagoo sidoo kale sheegay in ay dileen askar ka tirsan Ciidanka Xoogga Dalka. Dagaalka ayaa muujinaya sii xumeynta xaaladda amni ee dalka.
