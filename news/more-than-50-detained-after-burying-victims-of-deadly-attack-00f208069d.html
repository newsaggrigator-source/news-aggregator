More Than 50 Detained After Burying Victims of Deadly Attacks On Orthodox Faithful in Ethiopia's Oromia

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:07:14 GMT

In Ethiopia's Oromia region, over 50 residents have been detained after burying victims of recent attacks targeting Orthodox Christians. The violence, which occurred in Chole and Birbo kebeles, resulted in at least 48 deaths and numerous injuries, with the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church reporting a total of 60 fatalities. Local authorities have attributed the attacks to a group called "Tsimdo," claiming they aim to escalate internal conflict. Detainees include individuals who assisted in burials and treated the wounded, raising concerns about a security crackdown. Reports indicate that searches and arrests are ongoing, with many detainees being relatives of the deceased or individuals simply mourning. Residents fear further violence, prompting some to flee their homes. The recent killings are part of a troubling pattern of violence against Orthodox communities in the region, with previous incidents resulting in significant casualties and displacement. Calls for government accountability and protection for civilians have intensified amid these ongoing threats.
