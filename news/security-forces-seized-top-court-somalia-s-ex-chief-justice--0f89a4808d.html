Security forces seized top court, Somalia’s ex-chief justice says - Al Jazeera

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 17:02:39 GMT

Somalia's former Chief Justice, Bashe Yusuf Ahmed, reported that over 300 security personnel have "militarily occupied" the Supreme Court following its ruling that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term extension was unconstitutional. This occupation occurred after the court's decision, which challenged the legitimacy of the president's presidency. The situation highlights escalating tensions between the judiciary and the executive branch in Somalia, raising concerns about the rule of law and governance in the country. The military presence at the court underscores the precarious state of Somalia's political landscape, where judicial independence is threatened. The incident reflects broader issues of power struggles and the challenges faced in establishing a stable democratic framework in Somalia. As the situation develops, it remains to be seen how this will impact the country's political stability and the relationship between the government and its institutions.
