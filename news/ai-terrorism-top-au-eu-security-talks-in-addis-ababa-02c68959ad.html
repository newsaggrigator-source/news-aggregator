AI, Terrorism Top AU-EU Security Talks in Addis Ababa

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 09:57:37 GMT

The African Union (AU) and European Union (EU) convened their 9th Annual Joint Retreat in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, to address pressing security challenges in Africa, focusing on terrorism, maritime insecurity, and the governance of artificial intelligence (AI). The meeting, which included members from both the AU Peace and Security Council and the EU Political and Security Committee, aimed to enhance cooperation in tackling persistent and emerging threats across the continent. Key discussion areas included countering terrorism and violent extremism, maritime security, AI's implications for peace and security, and the Women, Peace and Security agenda. Co-chaired by representatives from Côte d'Ivoire and the EU, the retreat emphasized the importance of strategic dialogue and institutional collaboration, reinforcing the AU-EU partnership in addressing Africa's security landscape. This engagement comes amid ongoing security issues in regions like the Sahel and the Horn of Africa, highlighting the need for coordinated responses to ensure stability. (158 words)
