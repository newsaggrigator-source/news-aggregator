Somalia, Djibouti sign agreement to strengthen parliamentary cooperation - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 13:59:49 GMT

Somalia and Djibouti have formalized their commitment to enhancing parliamentary cooperation through a newly signed memorandum of understanding. The agreement, signed in Djibouti City by the Speakers of both nations' parliaments, aims to bolster legislative development, institutional capacity-building, and the exchange of expertise. Key areas of collaboration include improving legislative procedures, training parliamentary staff, conducting legal research, and modernizing institutional operations. The memorandum also emphasizes the importance of cooperation between parliamentary committees, the adoption of modern technology, enhanced data security, and the formation of parliamentary friendship groups. Both countries agreed to align their positions on issues at regional and international parliamentary forums. Following the signing, Djibouti's Speaker expressed satisfaction with the discussions, highlighting the positive outcomes for both nations. The Somali parliamentary speaker reaffirmed Mogadishu's dedication to strengthening ties with Djibouti through knowledge-sharing and institutional collaboration, reflecting broader efforts to enhance bilateral relations.
