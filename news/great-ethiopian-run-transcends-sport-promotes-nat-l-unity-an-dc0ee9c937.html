Great Ethiopian Run Transcends Sport, Promotes Nat'l Unity and Projects Ethiopia's Global Image

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 05:14:54 GMT

The 26th edition of the Great Ethiopian Run took place in Addis Ababa, attracting a record 60,000 participants under the theme "My Run, My Color." This event has evolved beyond a mere sporting competition to become a significant platform for promoting national unity, social cohesion, and cultural exchange. Participants from various ethnic, cultural, and social backgrounds, along with international visitors, gathered to celebrate diversity and foster a sense of belonging through sport. The run not only highlights Ethiopia's multicultural identity but also showcases the country's hospitality and capacity for hosting international events. Its impact extends beyond the race, promoting healthy lifestyles, peaceful coexistence, and a positive global image of Ethiopia as a vibrant and welcoming nation. As participation continues to grow, the Great Ethiopian Run reinforces Ethiopia's potential as a destination for sports, culture, and tourism, emphasizing that its true success lies in the unity it fosters among its people.
