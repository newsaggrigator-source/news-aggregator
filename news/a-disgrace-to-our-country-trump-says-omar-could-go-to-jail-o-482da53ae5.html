'A disgrace to our country': Trump says Omar could go to jail or be deported - KATU

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 22:33:18 GMT

Former President Donald Trump criticized Congresswoman Ilhan Omar, suggesting she could face jail time or deportation. During a recent rally, Trump labeled Omar a "disgrace to our country," accusing her of being unpatriotic and failing to represent American values. He highlighted her controversial statements and actions, arguing that they undermine the nation. Trump's remarks come amid ongoing debates about immigration and the responsibilities of elected officials. The comments have sparked reactions from various political figures and commentators, with some defending Omar and others supporting Trump's stance. The situation reflects the heightened tensions surrounding immigration policy and political discourse in the U.S.
