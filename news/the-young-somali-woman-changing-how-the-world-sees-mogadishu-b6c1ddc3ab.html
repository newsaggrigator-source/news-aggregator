The young Somali woman changing how the world sees Mogadishu - BBC

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 01:19:49 GMT

Sucdi Abdul Fattah Abdul Khader, a 23-year-old Somali woman, is reshaping perceptions of Mogadishu through her tourism efforts. She aims to showcase the city's beauty and culture, contrasting the prevalent images of violence and insecurity. Working alongside her father, Sucdi leads tours that highlight attractions like Jazeera Beach and the local fish market, where visitors can experience Somalia's rich marine life and culinary offerings. Despite the challenges posed by the country's tumultuous history, including decades of civil war, tourism is slowly gaining traction, with a reported 50% increase in visitors in 2024. Many tourists express surprise at the city's welcoming atmosphere and scenic landscapes, which differ significantly from their preconceived notions shaped by media coverage. Sucdi utilizes social media to promote her tours, aspiring to attract more visitors and share a more nuanced narrative about Mogadishu, emphasizing its potential as a vibrant travel destination.
