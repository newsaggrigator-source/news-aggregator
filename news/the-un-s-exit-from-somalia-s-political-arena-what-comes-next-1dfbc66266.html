The UN's exit from Somalia's political arena: What comes next? - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 09:41:29 GMT

The United Nations concluded its political mission in Somalia on October 31, 2026, marking the end of over three decades of involvement. This decision was initiated by the Somali government, which requested the UN's exit in a letter to the Security Council in May 2024. The UN Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) was established in 2013, but its roots trace back to a political office set up in 1995. As the UN departs, concerns arise about the readiness of Somali institutions to manage political disputes, particularly regarding elections and governance. The UN's absence raises questions about who will mediate conflicts between Mogadishu and federal member states, especially with ongoing threats from Al-Shabaab. Observers will be watching key areas: the electoral process, human rights documentation, and security dynamics. While the exit reflects Somalia's desire for self-governance, it also poses risks if local institutions are not sufficiently robust to handle the challenges ahead.
