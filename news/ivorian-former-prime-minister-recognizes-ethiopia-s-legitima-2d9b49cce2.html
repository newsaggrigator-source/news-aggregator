Ivorian Former Prime Minister Recognizes Ethiopia's Legitimate Sea Access Aspirations

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 08:28:43 GMT

Pascal Affi N'Guessan, the former Prime Minister of Côte d'Ivoire, has publicly acknowledged Ethiopia's legitimate aspirations for sovereign access to the sea. In an interview with the Ethiopian News Agency, he emphasized that Ethiopia's pursuit of permanent access to the Red Sea is a valid national goal, deserving recognition and support. Affi N'Guessan highlighted the importance of addressing outstanding issues through acceptable compromises that promote regional cooperation and stability. He noted that Ethiopia's landlocked status hinders its development, stressing that a population of over 130 million cannot remain geographically imprisoned. The former prime minister also pointed out the historical context of Ethiopia's connection to the Red Sea, advocating for dialogue and understanding among nations in the Horn of Africa. He concluded by underscoring the need for African unity and collaborative solutions to ensure collective prosperity across the continent. (157 words)
