Somalia’s Health Minister Calls No-Confidence Motion a “Failed Coup” - Dawan Africa

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 08:50:50 GMT

Somalia’s Health Minister, Dr. Ali Haji Adan, has labeled the recent no-confidence motion against him as a “failed coup.” Speaking at a health sector networking event in Mogadishu shortly after surviving the motion, he expressed confidence that his contributions would soon be recognized. The motion, initiated by at least 20 members of the House of the People, was submitted on October 10 and ultimately rejected by 89 MPs, with 50 in favor and 5 abstentions. Dr. Adan remarked on the support he received from event organizers, who prayed for his success, and he expressed gratitude for their prayers, stating, “God accepted your prayers.” He remains optimistic about his future in office, asserting that in six months, people will appreciate his work. The incident highlights ongoing political tensions within Somalia's government as it navigates challenges in governance and public health.
