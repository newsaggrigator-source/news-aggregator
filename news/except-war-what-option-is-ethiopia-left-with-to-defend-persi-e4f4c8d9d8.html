Except War, What Option is Ethiopia Left With to Defend Persistent Incursions from Eritrea? - horn review

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:55:58 GMT

Ethiopia faces a complex security dilemma regarding persistent incursions from Eritrea, which has historically adopted an antagonistic stance since its secession. The 1998 border war and the 2020 conflict in northern Ethiopia highlight the devastating costs of war, making conflict avoidance crucial. Eritrea's support for anti-Ethiopian armed groups and its military presence near the border exacerbate tensions. Diplomatic efforts have faltered, particularly after the severing of relations in October 2026. Ethiopia's challenge lies in neutralizing Eritrea's threats without escalating to full-scale war, which could draw in regional actors and have catastrophic consequences. A targeted approach against Eritrean leadership may offer a pathway to mitigate risks while avoiding broader conflict, emphasizing the need for international understanding and support to address these security concerns effectively.
