Ethiopia Wins Int'l Award for Institutionalizing Climate-Responsive Auditing

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 12:20:22 GMT

Ethiopia's Office of the Federal Auditor General (OFAG) has received international acclaim for its pioneering work in climate-responsive auditing at the 2026 ClimateScanner Impact Awards in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The award highlights OFAG's commitment to integrating climate change considerations into its institutional frameworks and strategic planning. Notably, the establishment of a dedicated Environmental Performance Audit Department and the incorporation of climate-responsive auditing into its Strategic Plan for 2026-2031 were particularly praised. This recognition underscores OFAG's dedication to embedding climate-related auditing within its operations and long-term strategies. The winners were chosen by an international panel of judges from Supreme Audit Institutions and partner organizations, reflecting a collaborative effort to enhance accountability in government responses to climate change. The ClimateScanner initiative, which includes over 100 countries, aims to evaluate and strengthen public-sector auditing related to climate action.
