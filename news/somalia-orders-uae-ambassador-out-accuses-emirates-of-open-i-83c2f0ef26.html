Somalia Orders UAE Ambassador Out, Accuses Emirates Of ‘Open Invasion’ - Arise News

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 09:57:01 GMT

Somalia has ordered the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador to leave the country within 72 hours, escalating diplomatic tensions between the two nations. The Somali government accused the UAE of violating its sovereignty, labeling the actions as an "open invasion." Information Minister Abdifatah Kasim Mohamud urged Somalis to unite against what he described as UAE interference in Somalia's internal affairs. This move follows Somalia's earlier cancellation of all bilateral agreements with the UAE, including military and economic cooperation, citing threats to national sovereignty. The UAE, which has historically supported Somalia's military and infrastructure, has denied any wrongdoing and characterized Somalia's actions as hostile. The UAE's foreign ministry expressed regret over the situation, warning that it could harm the interests of both countries. The diplomatic rift highlights ongoing struggles within Somalia to assert authority and manage external influences amid internal conflicts and the presence of Islamist militants.
