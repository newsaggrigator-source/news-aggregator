Manipur to Somalia without a stop: Male Amur falcon completes 5,400 km nonstop flight - India Today

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 05:42:50 GMT

In a remarkable feat of endurance, a male Amur falcon named Apapang has completed a non-stop flight of 5,400 kilometers from Manipur, India, to Somalia. This extraordinary journey showcases the bird's incredible migratory capabilities, as it crossed the Indian Ocean without any stops for food or rest. The flight began in Tamenglong, Manipur, and highlights the significance of northeastern India as a crucial stopover for Amur Falcons during their migrations. Supriya Sahu, an IAS officer, celebrated the arrival of Apapang, noting it as a miracle of nature. Another female falcon, Alang, had previously reached Somalia, marking a successful leg of their transcontinental migration. The Amur Falcon's journey from Asia to Africa exemplifies the remarkable navigational skills and physical endurance of these small raptors, emphasizing the interconnectedness of wildlife across continents. Their migration routes span thousands of kilometers, illustrating the challenges and triumphs of long-distance avian travel.
