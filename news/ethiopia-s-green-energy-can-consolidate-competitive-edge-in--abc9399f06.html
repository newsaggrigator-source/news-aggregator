Ethiopia's Green Energy Can Consolidate Competitive Edge in Textile, Garment Markets - German Deputy Ambassador

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 05:20:24 GMT

Ethiopia's renewable energy resources could enhance its competitiveness in the textile, garment, and leather markets, particularly in Europe, according to Germany's Deputy Ambassador, Ferdinand von Weyhe. Speaking at the 12th Africa Sourcing and Fashion Week in Addis Ababa, he emphasized that international buyers are increasingly prioritizing sustainability in their sourcing decisions. German companies have shown interest in sourcing more Ethiopian products, but the country must convert this interest into actual export growth, as its current exports to Germany and Europe are minimal compared to established suppliers like China and Vietnam. The Ambassador highlighted the European Union's Everything but Arms (EBA) initiative, which offers duty-free access to eligible products, as a significant opportunity for Ethiopian manufacturers. He also pointed out the importance of meeting European quality standards and addressing logistical challenges to facilitate sourcing. Germany is actively supporting Ethiopia's textile and garment sectors through development cooperation and participation in the ongoing trade exhibition.
