Ghana Set to Overtake Ethiopia As Africa's Eighth-Largest Economy After Kenya Takes East Africa Lead

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 11:52:13 GMT

Ghana is projected to surpass Ethiopia as Africa's eighth-largest economy in 2026, with an estimated GDP of $128.64 billion compared to Ethiopia's $127.15 billion, according to the IMF's October 2026 World Economic Outlook. This shift follows Kenya's recent emergence as East Africa's largest economy. While Ghana's nominal GDP is set to increase, Ethiopia is expected to experience a higher real growth rate of 9.2%, compared to Ghana's 5.7%. The depreciation of the Ethiopian birr has impacted its dollar-denominated GDP, despite strong domestic growth. Ghana's recovery from a financial crisis, supported by a $3 billion IMF program, is driven by exports and fiscal reforms. However, the change in nominal GDP rankings does not necessarily reflect improved living standards or economic capacity, highlighting the influence of exchange rates and macroeconomic conditions on economic measurements.
