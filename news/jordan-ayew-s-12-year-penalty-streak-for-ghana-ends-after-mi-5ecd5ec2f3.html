Jordan Ayew’s 12-year penalty streak for Ghana ends after miss against Somalia - Pulse Ghana

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 12:56:15 GMT

Ghana captain Jordan Ayew's impressive 12-year penalty streak came to an end during a 2-0 victory against Somalia in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers. Ayew missed his first penalty for the Black Stars in 10 attempts, failing to convert a spot-kick awarded in the 14th minute after defender Alidu Seidu was fouled. Despite this miss, Ghana secured the win with goals from Jonas Adjetey and Felix Afena-Gyan. Adjetey scored with a header just before halftime, while Afena-Gyan doubled the lead in the second half after being assisted by Mohammed Kudus. This victory marked Ghana's first three points in the AFCON 2027 qualifying campaign, following earlier defeats to Côte d'Ivoire and The Gambia. The Black Stars, under interim coach Kim Lars Bjorkegren, are set to face Somalia again on November 16 at the same venue. Ayew had previously scored nine consecutive penalties since 2014.
