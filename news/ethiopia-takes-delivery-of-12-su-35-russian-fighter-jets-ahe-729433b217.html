Ethiopia takes delivery of 12 Su-35 Russian fighter jets ahead of schedule as regional tensions rise - Business Insider Africa

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:37:47 GMT

Ethiopia has received 12 Russian-made Su-35 fighter jets ahead of schedule, alongside a deal for four S-400 air defense system batteries, as regional tensions escalate. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announced the expedited delivery during a visit to Moscow, expressing gratitude to President Vladimir Putin for the support. The specifics of the arms deal, including its value and the timeline for the S-400 delivery, remain undisclosed. This military enhancement comes amid renewed conflict in Ethiopia, particularly in the Tigray region, where a rebel alliance has intensified its offensive, raising fears of a resurgence in violence following the devastating war from 2020 to 2022. Additionally, tensions with Eritrea have heightened, as Ethiopia accuses Asmara of backing the insurgents, a claim Eritrea denies. The acquisition underscores Russia's growing military ties with African nations, as several countries on the continent increasingly rely on Russian military equipment.
