Somalia's supreme court rules the extension of presidential and parliamentary terms is unconstitutional - ConstitutionNet

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 17:59:30 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court has ruled that the extension of presidential and parliamentary terms is unconstitutional, declaring that the mandates of the federal parliament, president, and government expired in 2026. The court's decision invalidates any actions taken by these institutions after their terms ended. While the court dismissed a challenge from former President Sharif regarding the amendment process's constitutionality, it upheld the argument questioning the legality of the extended mandates. The ruling emphasized that constitutional amendments must explicitly state their applicability to existing institutions; otherwise, the original terms remain in effect. This landmark decision reinforces the importance of adhering to constitutional provisions and clarifies the legal standing of elected officials in Somalia. The ruling is a significant step in upholding constitutional integrity in the country.
