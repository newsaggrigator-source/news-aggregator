GSEF 2026: Somalia's Ambassador to Kenya Says Somali Investors Are Tried and Tested - Dawan Africa

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 16:34:49 GMT

At the Global Somali Entrepreneurship Forum (GSEF) 2026 in Nairobi, Somalia's Ambassador to Kenya, Jabril Ibrahim Abdulle, emphasized the resilience and potential of Somali investors, highlighting their ability to contribute significantly to economic growth. He urged the Somali diaspora to invest in their homeland rather than merely sending remittances for consumption. Abdulle noted the importance of the East African Community (EAC), which comprises around 300 million people, in fostering trade and business connections. Despite the entrepreneurial spirit within the Somali community, challenges persist, as pointed out by Dr. Ahmednur Abdi, who called for simpler regulations and improved infrastructure to facilitate business operations. Kenyan Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale echoed these sentiments, stressing the need for political goodwill in Somalia to harness its economic potential. The forum showcased various businesses and aimed to create opportunities for meaningful partnerships among entrepreneurs, policymakers, and investors, ultimately seeking to enhance the region's economic landscape.
