Somalia’s Identity Authority Rolls Out Real-Time Verification for Banks and Service Providers - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 12:10:39 GMT

Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) has launched the Hubiye verification platform, a digital system enabling real-time identity verification for banks and service providers. This initiative aims to combat identity fraud and enhance interactions between citizens and institutions by allowing financial entities, telecom companies, and government agencies to confirm the authenticity of National ID cards against a central database. The verification process involves a dual-layer check, utilizing a One-Time Password (OTP) sent to the citizen's registered device, ensuring that only the ID holder can authorize transactions. NIRA has registered over 1.5 million Somalis with biometric identities and aims to reach 15 million by 2029. However, challenges remain, as NIRA does not operate in certain regions like Puntland and Somaliland, limiting the platform's national effectiveness. The introduction of the Hubiye platform is a significant step in Somalia's digital identity infrastructure, but its success will depend on consistent enforcement of data protection measures and public trust in the system.
