Al-Shabaab claims hundreds dead in ambush on Somali troops - The New Indian Express

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 06:27:05 GMT

Al-Shabaab, the Islamist insurgent group linked to Al-Qaeda, has claimed responsibility for a deadly ambush on Somali government troops, asserting that it killed 321 soldiers. The attack occurred in the Lower Shabelle region, targeting Turkish-trained forces supported by Turkish airstrikes. This incident highlights the ongoing conflict in Somalia, where Al-Shabaab has been fighting the government for two decades and continues to control significant territories despite international military efforts against it. The fighting lasted for three hours, resulting in the capture of prisoners, weapons, and vehicles. Local media reported that the Somali government convened an emergency cabinet meeting to address the heavy casualties and military losses. Meanwhile, Turkey has become a crucial ally for the Somali government as support from the United States and other donors diminishes, particularly after Washington ceased funding a key logistics mission for combat operations, citing the government's lack of progress in unifying the country and addressing the insurgent threat.
