Somalia Baidoa Clashes: Federal and Regional Forces Fight - https://kulu-media.com/

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 20:37:30 GMT

Renewed clashes erupted on October 26, 2026, near Baidoa, Somalia, between federal troops and fighters loyal to former South West State President Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen. The fighting occurred around Burjeedka, with reports of explosions and gunfire as military reinforcements were deployed. A Somali army commander claimed government forces inflicted casualties on their opponents, while a Laftagareen commander asserted his fighters forced federal troops to retreat. The conflict stems from ongoing disputes over regional control, exacerbated by recent territorial gains by Laftagareen's forces. This confrontation follows weeks of military preparations by both sides, with competing claims of success and an unclear casualty toll. The violence raises concerns for civilians in Baidoa, a humanitarian hub, as continued fighting threatens access to essential services and transportation routes. The situation reflects broader instability in South West State, where political disagreements have escalated into armed confrontations, complicating Somalia's military efforts against Al-Shabaab.
