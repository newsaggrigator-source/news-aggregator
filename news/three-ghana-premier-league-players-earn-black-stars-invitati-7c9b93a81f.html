Three Ghana Premier League players earn Black Stars invitations for Somalia double-header - Graphic Online

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 21:34:50 GMT

Black Stars head coach Kim Lars Björkegren has called up three players from the Ghana Premier League for the upcoming double-header against Somalia in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. The new inclusions are Musa Hamzata from Asante Kotoko SC, along with Emmanuel Annor and Etse Dogli from Medeama SC. Hamzata, a defender, has been recognized for his strong performances, while Annor, a forward, and Dogli, a midfielder, have also shown consistent form. This call-up comes as Ghana aims to bounce back from two defeats against Côte d’Ivoire and The Gambia in their initial qualifying matches. The Black Stars commenced their training camp in Accra on November 8, 2026, in preparation for the first match against Somalia at the Sekondi Sports Stadium on November 12, followed by a second match on November 16, both scheduled for 15:00 GMT.
