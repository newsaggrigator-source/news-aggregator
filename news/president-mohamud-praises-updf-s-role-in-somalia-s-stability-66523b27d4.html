President Mohamud Praises UPDF’s role in Somalia’s Stability - SoftPower News

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 07:39:36 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has commended Uganda's significant role in fostering peace and stability in Somalia, particularly through the Uganda People's Defence Forces (UPDF), which have been deployed since March 2007. Speaking at Uganda's 64th independence anniversary at the Ugandan embassy in Mogadishu, he highlighted Uganda's pioneering contribution as the first country to send troops under the African Union Mission in Somalia (AMISOM). Mohamud also reassured Ugandan investors of his government's support for their business interests, emphasizing the commitment to enhance trade and economic cooperation between Somalia and Uganda. This follows a recent mid-term review of the Uganda-Somalia Joint Permanent Commission, which discussed various sectors including trade and investment. The event, attended by senior Somali officials and Ugandan diplomats, underscored the deepening ties between the two nations. As the African Union's military mandate in Somalia approaches a critical juncture, Mohamud stressed the importance of solidifying economic links to sustain security gains achieved over nearly two decades.
