PM Abiy proposes comprehensive Sudanese dialogue hosted by Ethiopia - CapitalEthiopia

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 19:03:06 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has proposed a comprehensive national dialogue for Sudan, inviting military, political, and civilian stakeholders to participate in talks aimed at resolving the ongoing conflict that has escalated since April 2023. In a statement on November 24, Abiy emphasized the need for an inclusive political process to end hostilities, secure a ceasefire, and establish a transitional government. He expressed concern for the humanitarian crisis in Sudan, highlighting the historical ties between Ethiopia and Sudan, which make the situation a matter of domestic interest for Ethiopians. Abiy's initiative seeks to provide a neutral platform for negotiations, focusing on halting violence and fostering political agreements that reflect Sudan's diverse population. He called for regional and international support to prioritize humanitarian needs and ensure that any solution remains Sudanese-led. The ultimate goal is to restore Sudan as a cohesive state, emphasizing the importance of peace and stability for the Horn of Africa.
