Somalia Presents Third-Generation E-Passport to EAC Partners in Kampala - FTL Somalia

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 21:02:22 GMT

Somalia's Immigration and Citizenship Agency unveiled its third-generation electronic passport to East African Community (EAC) partners during a recent meeting in Kampala. This new biometric passport, which includes ordinary, service, and diplomatic categories, is designed to meet regional travel standards and international security benchmarks. The passports feature a polycarbonate construction and enhanced security measures to prevent forgery. The initiative is part of Somalia's broader efforts to modernize its identification systems and improve travel credibility for its citizens, who have historically faced scrutiny due to fraudulent documents. The passport is linked to Somalia's national ID system, requiring applicants to present a national ID card. This presentation marks a significant step in Somalia's integration into the EAC, reflecting its commitment to regional cooperation and mobility for its citizens. However, the success of the passport will ultimately depend on the integrity of the issuance process and the national ID database.
