Welcome to BananasOverdose’s ‘Mogadisco’ - OkayAfrica

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 17:51:22 GMT

BananasOverdose, the Somali Ethiopian artist, is set to release her debut album, "Neo Mogadisco," which draws inspiration from Somalia's musical golden age of the 1960s to 1980s. This era, marked by vibrant experimentation and expressive performances, profoundly influenced Yasmine, the artist behind BananasOverdose. She became particularly captivated by the boldness of female musicians from that time, such as Sahra Dawo and Magool, who defied societal norms. Yasmine's music aims to reclaim female representation and celebrate inclusivity within Somali culture. Her single "MOGADISCO!" exemplifies this fusion of past and present, creating a joyful dance experience. Through her work, Yasmine seeks to provide the representation she wished she had as a child, while also addressing issues like female genital mutilation and cultural alienation. Despite facing challenges, including cyberbullying, she remains committed to her artistic vision, using "Neo Mogadisco" to carve out a space for herself and others who feel marginalized.
