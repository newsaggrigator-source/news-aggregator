UN mission in Somalia ends October 31 after successful two-year transition - Citizen Digital

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 05:51:22 GMT

The United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) will conclude its operations on October 31, 2026, after a successful two-year transition aimed at enhancing Somalia's peacebuilding and governance capabilities. This decision follows Security Council resolutions and a request from the Somali government, marking the end of over 30 years of UN missions in the region. The transition involved transferring UNTMIS's responsibilities to Somali institutions and various UN agencies, promoting national ownership of development and reconciliation efforts. UN Secretary-General António Guterres praised the progress made in Somalia, acknowledging the resilience of its people despite ongoing challenges. He emphasized the need for continued international support to address political, security, and humanitarian issues, as well as to combat threats from Al-Shabaab. Guterres reaffirmed the UN's commitment to assisting Somalia in building a stable and prosperous future, while honoring the contributions of personnel involved in the mission.
