Stakeholder-led nutrition interventions fall short in Eastern Ethiopia, survey finds - Bioengineer.org

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 09:17:56 GMT

A recent survey conducted in Eastern Ethiopia reveals that stakeholder-led nutrition interventions are insufficient in improving dietary diversity among rural households. The study, published in BMC Nutrition, found that the average household dietary diversity score was 4.02, significantly lower than the national average of 5.42, with half of the surveyed households classified as having low dietary diversity. Researchers collected data from 385 households and 84 stakeholders, analyzing factors influencing nutritional status. Positive correlations were identified with access to extension services, irrigation, and women's involvement in farming, while negative associations included household head's gender, credit services, and market distance. The findings highlight a disconnect between the range of interventions and the actual nutritional outcomes, emphasizing the need for stakeholders to address nutrition-related challenges more effectively in the region. The study serves as an observational analysis rather than a causal investigation of specific interventions.
