Ethiopia, Norway Seek Stronger Climate Partnership Ahead of COP32

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 05:24:34 GMT

Ethiopia and Norway are working to enhance their climate partnership in anticipation of COP32, which Ethiopia will host in November 2027. This initiative was highlighted during a meeting between Ethiopia's Minister of Finance, Fitsum Assefa, and Norway's Minister of Climate and the Environment, Sigrun Gjerløw Aasland. The discussions focused on shared priorities and opportunities for collaboration, particularly in relation to COP32 preparations and climate initiatives. Both ministers emphasized the significance of afforestation and environmental restoration, particularly through Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative, which aims to mitigate climate change and build resilience. They expressed a mutual interest in leveraging international partnerships to promote effective climate solutions and advance sustainable development. As Ethiopia gears up for COP32, it is intensifying diplomatic efforts to foster cooperation, mobilize support, and promote an inclusive global climate agenda. This partnership reflects a commitment to addressing climate challenges collaboratively. (160 words)
