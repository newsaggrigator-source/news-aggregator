Eritrea Accuses Ethiopia Of Using Maritime Forums To Mask Sea-Access Ambitions - Dawan Africa

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 13:02:41 GMT

Eritrea has accused Ethiopia of using maritime forums and workshops to conceal its ambitions for sea access in the Red Sea. In a statement from Eritrea's foreign ministry, it was claimed that Ethiopia's ruling Prosperity Party is attempting to create a facade of international legitimacy around its demands for "sovereign sea access." The statement highlighted a recent maritime security workshop led by the Ethiopian navy, which included representatives from Somalia's Puntland and Somaliland but excluded the Federal Government of Somalia. Eritrea criticized remarks from Ethiopian officials asserting that non-coastal states could influence Red Sea security, emphasizing that governance of the area should be reserved for coastal nations. Eritrea reaffirmed its commitment to not cede any territory or maritime rights while expressing openness to commercial use of its ports by Ethiopia, provided its sovereignty is respected. The statement urged international donors to reconsider their support for initiatives involving landlocked states with expansionist agendas. Ethiopia has not yet responded.
