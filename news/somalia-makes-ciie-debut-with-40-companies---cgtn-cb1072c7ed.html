Somalia makes CIIE debut with 40+ companies - CGTN

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 14:10:46 GMT

Somalia is making its debut at the China International Import Expo (CIIE) with over 40 companies participating in the event held in Shanghai. Gamal Mohamed Hassan, Somalia's Minister of Commerce and Industry, emphasized the primary objective of the participation is to forge connections between Somali businesses and Chinese partners, leveraging China's zero-tariff policy. He highlighted key Somali exports such as seafood, frankincense, and minerals, showcasing the country's readiness to engage in trade with China. In addition to the expo, Somali businesses plan to visit other Chinese provinces and participate in a business forum to strengthen partnerships. This initiative marks a significant step for Somalia in enhancing its international trade relations and promoting its products on a global platform.
