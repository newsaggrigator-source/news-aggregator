Somalia Votes Against UN Resolution On Violence Against Women, US And Russia Abstain - Health Policy Watch

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 16:17:17 GMT

On November 17, 2026, Somalia was the only country to vote against a United Nations resolution aimed at intensifying efforts to prevent and eliminate violence against women and girls. The resolution, which received 175 votes in favor, urges member states to adopt comprehensive measures to combat various forms of violence, including digital violence. The United States and Russia abstained from the vote, citing concerns over controversial terminology related to "sexual and reproductive health" and "safe abortion." The resolution emphasizes the need for legislation to protect women from online harassment and violence, reflecting a growing recognition of digital threats. Despite lengthy debates, including discussions on national sovereignty and terminology, the resolution was co-sponsored by France and the UK, highlighting the international community's commitment to addressing gender-based violence. It still requires formal adoption by the General Assembly in December.
