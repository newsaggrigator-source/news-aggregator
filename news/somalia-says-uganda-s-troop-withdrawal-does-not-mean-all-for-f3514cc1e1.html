Somalia Says Uganda's Troop Withdrawal Does Not Mean All Forces Are Leaving - Garowe Online

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 14:02:07 GMT

Somalia's federal government has clarified that the recent withdrawal of Ugandan troops does not signify the complete exit of all Ugandan forces from the country. National Security Adviser Ambassador Aweys Haji Yusuf Ahmed emphasized that the troops being withdrawn are those deployed under a specific bilateral agreement, while Ugandan forces operating under the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) will remain to continue their operations alongside the Somali National Army. This clarification follows the Uganda People's Defence Forces' announcement of troop withdrawals, which are expected to be completed shortly. However, all Ugandan forces, including those under AUSSOM, are anticipated to depart by the end of 2027. The announcement comes amid ongoing efforts to bolster Somalia's security capabilities against al-Shabab militants, highlighting the need for sustained international support to enhance the operational effectiveness of Somali security forces. A recent tripartite meeting reaffirmed commitments to improve coordination and support for AUSSOM. (159 words)
