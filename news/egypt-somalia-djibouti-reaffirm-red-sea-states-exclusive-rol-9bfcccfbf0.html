Egypt, Somalia, Djibouti reaffirm Red Sea states’ exclusive role in securing the waterway - Foreign Affairs - Egypt - Ahram Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 20:44:34 GMT

Egypt, Somalia, and Djibouti have reiterated the exclusive responsibility of Red Sea states in ensuring the security and stability of the vital waterway. This affirmation came during a recent meeting where officials from the three nations discussed collaborative efforts to address maritime security challenges. They emphasized the importance of regional cooperation in safeguarding the Red Sea, which is a critical route for global trade and energy supplies. The leaders highlighted the need for a unified approach to combat piracy, smuggling, and other threats that could disrupt navigation and trade. Additionally, they called for enhanced coordination among Red Sea states to strengthen their collective security measures. This commitment reflects the strategic significance of the Red Sea and the necessity for the countries bordering it to work together to maintain peace and stability in the region. The discussions underscore the growing recognition of the Red Sea's geopolitical importance and the need for a cohesive regional strategy. (151 words)
