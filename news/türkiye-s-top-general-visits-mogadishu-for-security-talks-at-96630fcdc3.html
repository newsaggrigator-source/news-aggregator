Türkiye’s Top General Visits Mogadishu for Security Talks at Villa Somalia - FTL Somalia

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 14:47:53 GMT

Türkiye's Chief of General Staff, General Selçuk Bayraktaroğlu, visited Mogadishu on November 21, 2026, for security discussions with Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud at Villa Somalia. The talks aimed to enhance the strategic defense partnership between Türkiye and Somalia, focusing on deepening bilateral cooperation and joint efforts against international terrorism. This visit follows a series of high-level military engagements, including previous meetings between the two nations' defense leaders. Türkiye has been expanding its military presence in Somalia, supported by a 10-year Defense and Economic Cooperation Framework Agreement established in February 2024. This framework includes military training, equipment support, and maritime security cooperation. Recent developments include the graduation of 275 Somali National Army personnel from commando training and the deployment of Turkish naval frigates and F-16 fighters to Somali waters. While the partnership offers significant benefits for Somalia's security, concerns remain regarding the transparency and accountability of Türkiye's military footprint in the region.
