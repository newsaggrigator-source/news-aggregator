Ethiopia Targets Dec. 18 Settlement to End Nearly Three-Year Eurobond Default

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 05:11:51 GMT

Ethiopia has initiated a formal exchange offer to restructure its defaulted $1 billion sovereign Eurobond, aiming for settlement by December 18, 2026. This move seeks to resolve nearly three years of default and facilitate a return to international capital markets. The Ministry of Finance has invited holders of its 6.625% notes, originally due in December 2024, to exchange them for $880 million in new amortizing bonds maturing in July 2029. The proposed exchange includes a 12% reduction in principal and a 6.15% annual coupon on the new bonds, along with cash payments for unpaid interest. Bondholders have until December 11 to submit their instructions and may receive a consent fee. The restructuring also introduces new-money warrants, allowing holders to subscribe to a future Ethiopian bond. The exchange offer is contingent on obtaining approval from at least 75% of the outstanding principal holders, which would make the exchange binding for all eligible holders.
