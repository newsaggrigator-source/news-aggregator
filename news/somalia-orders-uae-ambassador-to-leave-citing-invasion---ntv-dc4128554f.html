Somalia orders UAE ambassador to leave, citing ‘invasion’ - NTV Kenya

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 10:05:51 GMT

Somalia has ordered the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador to leave the country within 72 hours, citing an "open invasion" of its sovereignty. The Somali Information Minister, Abdifatah Kasim Mohamud, announced this decision, stating that measures would be taken against anyone cooperating with the UAE. The minister urged Somalis to unite against what he described as an invasion affecting the country's security and unity. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud previously accused the UAE of supporting regional governments that oppose the federal government. In response, the UAE condemned Somalia's actions as "escalatory and hostile," expressing regret over the Somali government's attempts to involve the UAE in its internal conflicts. The UAE had previously closed its embassy in Mogadishu and withdrawn its diplomatic staff due to perceived hostility from Somalia. The UAE has historically invested in Somalia, providing military training and funding, but has faced accusations of undermining the Somali federal government's authority. (159 words)
