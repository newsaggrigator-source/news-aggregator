After its U-turn on the Sahara, Somalia expels UAE Ambassador - Yabiladi.com

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 09:14:00 GMT

Somalia has expelled the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador following a significant shift in its stance regarding the Western Sahara conflict. This decision comes after Somalia's government recently reversed its previous position, which had supported Morocco's claims over the disputed territory. The Somali government’s U-turn has been attributed to a desire to strengthen ties with Morocco, a move that has drawn criticism from the UAE, which has historically backed the opposing side in the conflict. The expulsion of the UAE ambassador reflects the growing tensions between Somalia and the UAE, particularly in light of the UAE's involvement in regional politics and its support for rival factions in Somalia. This diplomatic rift underscores the complexities of alliances in the Horn of Africa and highlights Somalia's efforts to navigate its foreign relations amidst competing interests. The situation remains fluid, with potential implications for regional stability and Somalia's international partnerships. (151 words)
