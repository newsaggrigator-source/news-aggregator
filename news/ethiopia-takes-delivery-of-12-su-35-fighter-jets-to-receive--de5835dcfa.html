Ethiopia Takes Delivery of 12 Su-35 Fighter Jets, to Receive S-400 Air Defense Systems: PM Abiy - ENA English

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 18:33:15 GMT

Ethiopia has received its first 12 Su-35 fighter jets and is set to acquire four S-400 air defense system batteries, as announced by Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. This delivery marks a significant advancement in Ethiopia's national defense capabilities, aimed at enhancing security and deterring potential threats. In a social media statement, PM Abiy highlighted that this achievement is part of Ethiopia's broader strategy to ensure peace and security sustainably. He expressed gratitude to Russian President Vladimir Putin for expediting the delivery ahead of schedule. The acquisition positions Ethiopia as a leader in military modernization within Sub-Saharan Africa, reinforcing its sovereignty and defense capabilities. The announcement coincides with PM Abiy's visit to Moscow for the Third Russia–Africa Summit, which aims to strengthen Ethiopia's ties with Russia and other African nations. This development is seen as a crucial step in bolstering Ethiopia's collective security and long-term national objectives. (159 words)
