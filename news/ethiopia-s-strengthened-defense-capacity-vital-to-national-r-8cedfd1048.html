Ethiopia's Strengthened Defense Capacity Vital to National, Regional Security

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 05:20:58 GMT

Ethiopia's defense capabilities have significantly improved, enhancing the country's ability to protect its national interests and contribute to regional stability, according to Gezahegn Fekadu, Commandant of the International Peace Support Training Centre. The Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) has undergone substantial reforms, including advancements in weaponry, training, and capacity-building initiatives. These developments have not only bolstered Ethiopia's defense readiness but also its role in maintaining peace in the region. Gezahegn emphasized the importance of continued military modernization to address evolving security threats. Lieutenant Colonel Aragaw Gebremedhin from the Ethiopian National Defense College highlighted Ethiopia's historical contributions to international peacekeeping, which, combined with its enhanced military capabilities, position the country as a key player in promoting peace and security both domestically and in neighboring regions. He urged academic institutions to document Ethiopia's peacekeeping efforts to better showcase its contributions to the international community. (158 words)
