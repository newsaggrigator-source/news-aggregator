Somalia's parliament speaker has made serious allegations against Kenya, Ethiopia, and Uganda, accusing them of colluding with the militant group Al-Shabaab. During a recent address, he claimed that these neighboring countries are providing support to the insurgents, which poses a significant threat to Somalia's stability and security. The speaker emphasized the need for regional cooperation to combat terrorism and called for a united front against Al-Shabaab's activities. He expressed concerns that the ongoing collaboration between these nations and the militant group undermines efforts to restore peace in Somalia. The accusations have sparked discussions about the geopolitical dynamics in the Horn of Africa and the challenges faced by Somalia in its fight against terrorism. The speaker's statements reflect ongoing tensions in the region and highlight the complexities of international relations in addressing security issues.
