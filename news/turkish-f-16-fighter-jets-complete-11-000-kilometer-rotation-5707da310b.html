Turkish F-16 fighter jets complete 11,000-kilometer rotation between Somalia and Türkiye - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 19:54:16 GMT

Turkish F-16 fighter jets have successfully completed a significant deployment flight covering over 11,000 kilometers between Somalia and Türkiye, as announced by Türkiye's Ministry of National Defence. The operation, which took place on November 17, 2026, involved two flights from Mogadishu to Bandırma, totaling 11,038 kilometers (6,859 miles) without any intermediate landings. Pilots from the 161st Squadron executed the mission over 55 hours, showcasing the Turkish Air Force's capability for long-distance military operations. This deployment is part of Türkiye's ongoing military presence in Somalia, which includes a major training facility in Mogadishu, where thousands of Somali personnel have been trained. The operation underscores Türkiye's commitment to enhancing security and stability in East Africa, particularly as Somalia seeks to bolster its security forces amid evolving regional dynamics. However, details regarding the number of aircraft involved and refueling arrangements were not disclosed.
