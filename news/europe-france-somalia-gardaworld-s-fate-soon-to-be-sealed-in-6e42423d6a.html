Europe, France, Somalia GardaWorld's fate soon to be sealed in EU tender 'Somaligate' affair - Intelligence Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 05:04:10 GMT

The upcoming trial in Paris concerning the 'Somaligate' affair will determine the fate of GardaWorld, a Canadian security firm entangled in a controversy surrounding a €100 million EU contract awarded in 2015 for a diplomatic compound in Mogadishu, Somalia. Seven individuals are accused of influence-peddling related to this contract, which has raised significant concerns about corruption and transparency within the EU's procurement processes. GardaWorld's European subsidiary has been implicated since the beginning of the investigation. The trial is set to commence in January, and it follows a series of appeals regarding the selection of a consortium led by Swedish company Vesper for the EUCAP security contract in Somalia, which has also faced scrutiny due to its substantial budget. The unfolding legal proceedings could have major implications for GardaWorld's operations and reputation in the region.
