Ethiopia War Security Review, November 13, 2026 - Critical Threats

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 23:37:35 GMT

The Ethiopia War Security Review from November 10 to November 13, 2026, highlights ongoing conflicts involving Ethiopian federal forces, the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF), and Eritrean military units. Reports indicate that Amhara Fano National Movement fighters may have allied with TPLF forces against federal troops near the Ethiopia-Eritrea border, although these claims remain unverified. Significant clashes occurred around Badme, with the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) engaging TPLF and Eritrean forces. The interim Tigray regional government is facing internal tensions as it establishes its administration, with reports of forced conscription in Oromia and Addis Ababa. International actors, including U.S. senators and G7 foreign ministers, have expressed concern over escalating tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea, urging diplomatic solutions. Despite calls for de-escalation, both nations continue to blame each other for the conflict's intensification. The situation remains fluid, with ongoing military engagements and political instability in the region.
