Somalia’s top court rules president’s term extension is unlawful, deepening power struggle - Midland Reporter-Telegram

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 14:21:55 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court has declared the recent extension of President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term as unlawful, intensifying the ongoing power struggle in the country. The court's ruling comes amid rising tensions and political instability, as various factions contest the legitimacy of the government's actions. The term extension, which was initially proposed to address security concerns and facilitate elections, has faced widespread criticism from opposition groups and civil society. The ruling is expected to exacerbate divisions within the political landscape, as it undermines the president's authority and raises questions about the future of governance in Somalia. Observers fear that this decision could lead to further unrest and complicate efforts to stabilize the nation, which has been grappling with challenges such as terrorism, humanitarian crises, and political fragmentation. The situation remains fluid, with potential implications for both domestic and international stakeholders involved in Somalia's political affairs.
