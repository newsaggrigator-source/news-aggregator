Black Stars need six points against Somalia but must show respect - Kim Lars Björkegren - GhanaWeb

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 21:28:50 GMT

Ghana's Black Stars face a crucial challenge as they aim to secure six points in their upcoming matches against Somalia. Coach Kim Lars Björkegren emphasized the importance of respect for their opponents, highlighting that while the Black Stars are favorites, they must not underestimate Somalia. Björkegren acknowledged the need for his team to demonstrate professionalism and focus, as complacency could lead to unexpected results. He stressed the significance of preparation and maintaining a strong mentality throughout the matches. The coach also expressed confidence in his players' abilities, urging them to perform at their best to achieve the desired outcomes. The Black Stars are determined to capitalize on this opportunity to strengthen their position in the tournament, and Björkegren's leadership will be vital in guiding the team to success while ensuring they remain grounded and respectful towards their rivals.
