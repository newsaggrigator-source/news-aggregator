At Least 48 Civilians Killed in Weekend Attacks in Ethiopia's Oromia Region, Once Again

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 15:09:23 GMT

At least 48 civilians were killed and over 31 injured in a series of attacks by armed men in the Oromia region of Ethiopia over the weekend. The violence occurred in Chole and Birbo kebeles of Shirka Woreda, where residents reported door-to-door killings, looting, and the use of heavy weaponry. The attacks began on the evening of October 24 and continued into the following morning, targeting primarily followers of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, including women and children. Local residents described being overwhelmed by heavily armed attackers, and many survivors remain too frightened to leave their homes. The Oromia Regional Government attributed the violence to a group referred to as "Tsimdo," although details regarding the attackers and the response from security forces remain unclear. This incident is part of a troubling pattern of violence against civilians in the region, particularly targeting Orthodox Christian communities, with previous attacks resulting in significant casualties and destruction.
