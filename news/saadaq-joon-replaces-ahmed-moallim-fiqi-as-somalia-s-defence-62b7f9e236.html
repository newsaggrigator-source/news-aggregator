Saadaq Joon replaces Ahmed Moallim Fiqi as Somalia's defence minister - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 21:38:41 GMT

Somali Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre appointed Saadaq Omar Mohamed Hassan, known as Saadaq Joon, as the new defence minister on November 23, 2026, replacing Ahmed Moallim Fiqi in a limited Cabinet reshuffle that also included changes to two deputy ministerial positions. The reasons for Fiqi's departure were not disclosed, nor was it confirmed if he would receive another Cabinet role. Saadaq Joon, a member of Somalia's House of the People and former commander of the Banadir Regional Police Force, is believed to be part of a power-sharing arrangement following political changes in Galmudug. His appointment coincides with ongoing military operations against al-Shabab and discussions regarding the future of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia, which is set to expire on December 31. The Prime Minister urged the new officials to perform their duties with integrity and accountability as they assume their roles. (159 words)
