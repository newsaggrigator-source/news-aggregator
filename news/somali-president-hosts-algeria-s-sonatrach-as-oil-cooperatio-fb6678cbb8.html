Somali President Hosts Algeria’s Sonatrach as Oil Cooperation Talks Advance - FTL Somalia

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 16:05:48 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud met with a delegation from Algeria's national oil and gas company, Sonatrach, to discuss enhancing cooperation in oil exploration, gas development, and resource management. The meeting, held at the presidential palace, followed previous engagements between Sonatrach and Somali officials, including technical discussions and a visit to Mogadishu's national oil depot. This collaboration stems from a strategic partnership agreement signed during President Mohamud's 2025 visit to Algeria, focusing on capacity building rather than direct investment. Sonatrach has offered technical expertise and training for Somali petroleum engineers, along with 500 scholarships for students. However, no formal agreement for oil exploration or investment has been established yet. While the partnership signifies growing political ties, Somalia's oil sector remains unproven, with ongoing challenges regarding regulatory frameworks and regional governance disputes. Until a commitment for drilling is made, the potential of Somalia's hydrocarbons sector remains largely untapped.
