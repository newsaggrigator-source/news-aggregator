Somalia state minister storms transport ministry with AK-47 over number plate kickbacks - Somali Guardian

Sat, 28 Nov 2026 09:15:16 GMT

Somalia's State Minister of Transport, Abdikarim Abdow Hayder, caused a stir by storming the Ministry of Transport and Civil Aviation armed with an AK-47, as captured in a viral video. This incident stemmed from a longstanding dispute over kickbacks related to vehicle number plates, with Hayder feeling deprived of his rightful share. Witnesses reported that he aimed to confront his superior, Minister Mohamed Farah Nuh, amidst ongoing tensions that had previously escalated within the ministry. The cabinet's inaction regarding their quarrel has raised alarms about the normalization of armed confrontations in governmental disputes. This incident is part of a troubling trend in Somalia, where internal conflicts among officials often resort to violence, reflecting deep-rooted corruption and self-serving motives within public office. The lack of immediate government response to Hayder's actions has further fueled concerns about the potential for similar violent incidents in the future, marking a dangerous precedent in the already volatile political landscape.
