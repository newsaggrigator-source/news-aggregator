Diplomats, Representatives of Int'l Organizations Visit Rehabilitation Center for Former Combatants in Tigray

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:21:35 GMT

Diplomats and representatives from international organizations recently visited a rehabilitation and reintegration center for former combatants in Mekelle, Tigray, Ethiopia. This center, which previously functioned as a training facility, currently accommodates around 5,500 former combatants who are undergoing training to aid their transition to civilian life. The initiative is part of a broader effort to promote sustainable national peace by equipping these individuals with essential skills and support to reintegrate into their communities. During the visit, officials from the National Rehabilitation Commission, including Commissioner Temesgen Tilahun, provided insights into the program's objectives and progress. The rehabilitation efforts aim not only to assist former combatants but also to foster stability and development in the region following years of conflict. This visit underscores the commitment of international stakeholders to support Ethiopia's peace-building initiatives.
