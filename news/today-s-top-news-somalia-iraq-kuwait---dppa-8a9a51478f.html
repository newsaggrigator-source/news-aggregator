Today's top news: Somalia, Iraq/Kuwait - DPPA

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 22:15:29 GMT

On October 26, 2026, a formal ceremony in Mogadishu marked the conclusion of the UN Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS), which will officially cease operations on October 31. This transition, requested by the Federal Government of Somalia, reflects a significant shift towards national ownership of peacebuilding efforts. The UN Secretary-General praised the progress made in Somalia over the past decades and reaffirmed the UN's commitment to supporting the country through various agencies and partnerships aimed at promoting peace, stability, and sustainable development. In a separate briefing to the Security Council, Mari Yamashita highlighted ongoing efforts to address the fate of 315 missing individuals and the recovery of Kuwaiti property, including national archives, following Iraq’s invasion of Kuwait over 35 years ago. She emphasized the need for enhanced cooperation among Iraq, Kuwait, and the International Committee of the Red Cross to provide answers to affected families and facilitate the return of missing property.
