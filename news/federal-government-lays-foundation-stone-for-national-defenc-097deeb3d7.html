Federal Government Lays Foundation Stone for National Defence University and Army Hospital - FTL Somalia

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 16:53:39 GMT

On November 15, 2026, President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud laid the foundation stone for the National Defence University and the reconstruction of the Somali National Army Hospital in Mogadishu. This initiative aims to enhance institutional development amidst ongoing security challenges and a constitutional crisis. The National Defence University, supported by China, will feature modern facilities for training military personnel, reducing reliance on foreign training programs. Meanwhile, the Army Hospital will be a three-storey facility providing essential medical services to soldiers. The reactivation of the Army Construction Command is intended to expedite infrastructure projects, reflecting the military's expanding role in state-building. However, the timing of the ceremony raises questions about the government's legitimacy and the army's immediate operational needs, as soldiers currently require urgent resources rather than long-term institutional investments. While these developments are positive, they must be accompanied by transparency and accountability in military spending to ensure effective support for the troops.
