Somalia’s Electoral Commission Announces Candidates for Hirshabelle State Leader and Deputy - Dawan Africa

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 15:20:06 GMT

Somalia's National Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (NIEBC) has announced the candidates for the leadership positions of Hirshabelle state, with elections scheduled to take place in Jowhar. For the leader position, Ali Abdullahi Hussein from the Justice and Solidarity Party and Nur Ilmi Halane from Ramaas are competing. The deputy leader candidates include Abdifatah Hassan Afrah, also from the Justice and Solidarity Party, and Abdifatah Bashir Mohamed from Iftiin Qaran. The commission emphasized adherence to the election schedule and procedures, urging cooperation from the House of Representatives, candidates, media, and civil society. Ali Abdullahi Hussein, who has served as the leader since 2020, was nominated for a second term by his party's executive committee. The recent elections for the Hirshabelle House of Representatives, held on September 30, resulted in a significant victory for the Justice and Solidarity Party, which secured 78 out of 99 seats. The Supreme Court confirmed these results on October 19.
