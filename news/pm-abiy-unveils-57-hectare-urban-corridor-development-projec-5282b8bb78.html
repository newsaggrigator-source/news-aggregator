PM Abiy Unveils 57-Hectare Urban Corridor Development Project in Addis Ababa

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 05:27:22 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has launched a significant 57-hectare urban corridor development project in Addis Ababa, designed to enhance the city's productivity, inclusivity, and livability. The initiative encompasses seven key routes, including Bole Medhanialem-Golagul-British Embassy and Bole School-Adey Abeba Model Village, along with various connecting roads. This project is part of the Medemer Government's broader urbanization strategy, which aims to transform urban areas into vibrant centers for enterprise, culture, and employment. The development will feature modern road infrastructure, pedestrian walkways, cycling lanes, and facilities for over 5,000 vehicles, alongside improvements to schools, libraries, and public spaces. PM Abiy emphasized that quality urban development is crucial for enhancing productivity and social order, while also addressing climate challenges and future population growth. This initiative reflects a vision for creating resilient and green cities in Ethiopia. (159 words)
