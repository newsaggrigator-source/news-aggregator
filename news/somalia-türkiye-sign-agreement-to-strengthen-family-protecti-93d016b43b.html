Somalia, Türkiye sign agreement to strengthen family protection and social services - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 08:06:03 GMT

Somalia and Türkiye have signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) aimed at enhancing cooperation in social services, family protection, and the rights of women and children. The agreement was formalized in Antalya, Türkiye, during the COP31 United Nations climate conference by Somalia's Minister of Family and Human Rights Development, Khadija Mohamed Al-Makhzoumi, and Türkiye's Minister of Family and Social Services, Mahinur Özdemir Göktaş. This MoU establishes a framework for developing social welfare policies, sharing expertise, and improving services for vulnerable communities. Key areas of focus include protecting women's and children's rights, preventing violence against women, and safeguarding children from online risks. The agreement also emphasizes health and education services, as well as rehabilitation programs for disabled individuals and the elderly. Al-Makhzoumi highlighted the importance of international cooperation in addressing the challenges posed by climate change, particularly for women and children affected by disasters. This partnership marks a significant step in strengthening Somalia's social services. (158 words)
