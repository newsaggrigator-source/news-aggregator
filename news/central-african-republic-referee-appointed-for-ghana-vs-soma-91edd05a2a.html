Central African Republic referee appointed for Ghana vs Somalia AFCON qualifier - GhanaWeb

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 18:21:04 GMT

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) has appointed a referee from the Central African Republic for the upcoming Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifier between Ghana and Somalia. The match is scheduled to take place on October 12, 2023, at the Accra Sports Stadium. The referee, who has not been named in the announcement, will be supported by officials from the Central African Republic as well. This appointment comes as part of CAF's efforts to ensure that matches are officiated by qualified referees from across the continent. Ghana, aiming to secure a spot in the AFCON tournament, will be looking to capitalize on their home advantage against Somalia. The match is crucial for both teams as they strive to improve their standings in the qualification process. Fans are eagerly anticipating the encounter, hoping for a strong performance from the Black Stars to boost their chances of qualifying for the prestigious tournament.
