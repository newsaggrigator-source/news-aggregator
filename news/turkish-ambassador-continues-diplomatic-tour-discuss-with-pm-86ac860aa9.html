Turkish Ambassador Continues Diplomatic Tour: Discuss with PM Hamza Energy, Security and Space - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 17:12:28 GMT

Turkish Ambassador Ferhat Alkan recently met with Somalia's Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre as part of a diplomatic tour aimed at strengthening bilateral relations. The discussions focused on enhancing cooperation in various sectors, including energy, security, infrastructure, fisheries, health, education, and space. The Turkish Embassy expressed gratitude for the Prime Minister's support in fostering friendship between the two nations. This meeting is part of a broader initiative by Ambassador Alkan, who began his tenure in September 2026, coinciding with the 60th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Turkey and Somalia. His engagements have included discussions with Somali security leaders to improve training programs for the police force. However, Turkey's involvement in Somalia has faced criticism, with some political opponents accusing it of pursuing a "clan-based agenda" through military support. Despite these challenges, the partnership continues to grow, reflecting Turkey's significant role in Somalia's security and development landscape. The ambassador's visit underscores the complexities of this evolving relationship.
