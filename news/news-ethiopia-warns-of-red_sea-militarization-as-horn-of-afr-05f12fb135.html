Ethiopia has issued a warning regarding the increasing militarization of the Red Sea, highlighting concerns over renewed attacks on commercial shipping and escalating foreign rivalries in the Horn of Africa. The Ethiopian government expressed apprehension that the heightened military presence in the region could destabilize maritime security and threaten trade routes vital for the economy. This warning comes amid ongoing conflicts in the Horn of Africa, where various nations are vying for influence and control. Ethiopia's concerns are compounded by the strategic importance of the Red Sea, which serves as a critical passage for global shipping. The Ethiopian authorities emphasized the need for regional cooperation to address these security challenges and prevent further escalation of tensions. As foreign powers engage in military activities in the area, Ethiopia calls for dialogue and collaborative efforts to ensure peace and stability in the region. The situation remains precarious, with potential implications for both regional and international security dynamics.
