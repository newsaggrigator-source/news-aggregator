Ethiopia Needs Political Reconciliation, Not Another Military Victory - Ethiopia Insight

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 23:18:28 GMT

Ethiopia is at a critical juncture where political reconciliation is essential, rather than pursuing further military victories, as emphasized by Ambassador Tibor Nagy. The ongoing conflict, particularly in Tigray, has resulted in immense human suffering and loss, raising questions about the objectives behind such wars. Nagy argues that the focus should shift from military solutions to diplomatic negotiations, especially regarding Ethiopia's access to the Nile and maritime routes. He highlights the need for a realistic approach to alliances, noting that shared opposition does not equate to a unified vision for the future. Furthermore, he critiques the concept of ethnic federalism in Ethiopia, suggesting that the surrounding institutions, rather than the federal structure itself, may be the root of the problem. Ultimately, both Nagy and the author advocate for a political landscape that prioritizes democracy, accountability, and genuine reconciliation over authoritarianism, allowing Ethiopians to build a more prosperous future.
