Somalia to Begin Issuing East African Community E-Passports in January 2027 - Dawan Africa

Sat, 28 Nov 2026 07:25:17 GMT

Somalia is set to begin issuing East African Community (EAC) electronic passports in January 2027, as announced on November 28, 2026. This new passport will be integrated with Somalia's national identification system and will be available at Somali embassies and diplomatic missions abroad. The EAC aims to facilitate the movement of citizens within the region for business, employment, and other opportunities, while also aligning Somalia's immigration and identification systems with EAC standards. This initiative is expected to enhance border security and improve border management efficiency. Other EAC member states, including Burundi, Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda, and Tanzania, have already adopted the electronic passport, with South Sudan making progress towards its issuance. The introduction of the EAC electronic passport marks a significant milestone in Somalia's integration into the East African Community and supports the commitments regarding the free movement of persons under the EAC Common Market Protocol.
