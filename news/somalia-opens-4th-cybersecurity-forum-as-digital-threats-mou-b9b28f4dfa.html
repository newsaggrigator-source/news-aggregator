Somalia Opens 4th Cybersecurity Forum as Digital Threats Mount - FTL Somalia

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 10:07:32 GMT

Somalia recently hosted its fourth National Cybersecurity Forum in Mogadishu, focusing on the increasing digital threats facing the nation. The event brought together various stakeholders, including government officials, telecommunications companies, and cybersecurity experts, under the theme “Building Trust and Cyber Resilience Through Security by Design.” Communications and Technology Minister Mohamed Adan Mo’allin highlighted the establishment of the Somali Computer Incident Response Team (SOMCIRT) and announced plans for a National Cybersecurity Academy. The forum addressed the need for improved cybersecurity measures, especially following significant breaches, such as the 2025 e-visa incident that exposed personal data of over 35,000 individuals. Discussions included the integration of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity efforts, emphasizing the importance of collaboration between the public and private sectors. While the forum marks progress in Somalia's cybersecurity governance, challenges remain, particularly in enforcing regulations and ensuring a robust security infrastructure to protect the rapidly growing digital economy.
