Berhanu Jula’s Accusations and the Geopolitical Interests Behind Ethiopia’s Conflicts - Borkena

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 19:20:36 GMT

Berhanu Jula has accused three neighboring countries of "waging war against Ethiopia," raising critical questions about the threats to Ethiopia's sovereignty and the geopolitical interests at play. The article argues that Ethiopia's internal conflicts cannot be viewed in isolation from the competing interests of regional powers like Eritrea, Sudan, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and Turkey, which may support opposition forces against Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. This external involvement risks escalating violence and prolonging civilian suffering, undermining prospects for a peaceful resolution. The article emphasizes that while foreign interference exacerbates tensions, Ethiopia's internal issues, such as political exclusion and governance failures, also contribute significantly to its instability. It calls for a deeper examination of the responsibilities of both foreign actors and Ethiopian leaders, stressing that sustainable security requires addressing internal grievances and building public trust, rather than solely focusing on external threats. Ultimately, the article questions who truly benefits from Ethiopia's ongoing instability and the implications for its people. (159 words)
