Al Shabaab Ambush in South West State; Algeria Deploys to Niger: Africa File, October 29, 2026 - Critical Threats

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 20:45:22 GMT

Al Shabaab launched a significant ambush against Federal Government of Somalia (FGS) forces in South West State, resulting in heavy casualties during a counteroffensive operation. The FGS had deployed over 2,000 soldiers and military vehicles to reclaim towns captured by opposition forces, but Al Shabaab's ambush near Leego halted their advance, with claims of up to 413 federal soldiers killed. The attack utilized vehicle-borne improvised explosive devices (IEDs) and possibly drones, marking a tactical escalation for Al Shabaab. Meanwhile, Algeria has taken a more assertive stance in the Sahel by deploying troops to northern Niger, aiming to bolster security and economic interests in the region. This deployment follows a military cooperation agreement with Niger and reflects Algeria's strategic competition with neighboring countries. The situation in Somalia remains volatile, with Al Shabaab exploiting political tensions and instability to increase its operational activity.
