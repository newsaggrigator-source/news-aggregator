Safety Net Program Boosts Food Security for Rural Ethiopian Households, Study Finds - Bioengineer.org

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 18:56:37 GMT

A recent study published in Discover Food highlights the positive impact of Ethiopia's Productive Safety Net Program on food security among rural households in Lemo Woreda, Central Ethiopia. The research analyzed survey data from 124 households, equally divided between program beneficiaries and non-beneficiaries. Utilizing descriptive statistics and econometric methods, the study found that participation in the program significantly improved food security for beneficiary households. In contrast, larger family sizes were linked to decreased food security, indicating that bigger households faced more challenges in accessing adequate food. The findings suggest that beneficiaries had a higher likelihood of enhancing their food security compared to non-beneficiaries. However, the study's scope is limited to a single woreda, and the authors recommend ongoing implementation of the program with adjustments to bolster household asset accumulation alongside food security improvements. This research underscores the importance of social protection initiatives in enhancing food security in rural Ethiopia.
