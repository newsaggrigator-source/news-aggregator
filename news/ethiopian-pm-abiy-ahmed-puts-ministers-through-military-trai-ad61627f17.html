Ethiopian PM Abiy Ahmed puts ministers through military training - BBC

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 14:28:15 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has initiated military training for his cabinet ministers and senior government officials, emphasizing that such discipline is essential for effective leadership and national development. The training, conducted at the Hurso military training center, includes prominent figures like the Speaker of the House of Federation and the Finance Minister. Abiy believes that military training can instill values such as teamwork, accountability, and patriotism, which are crucial for achieving Ethiopia's goals of universal prosperity. This unusual move comes amid ongoing conflicts in the northern Tigray region and heightened tensions with Eritrea. Abiy has previously highlighted the importance of military preparedness in addressing Ethiopia's security challenges. The duration of the training remains unclear, but it reflects a strategic approach to leadership development in a country facing multiple crises.
