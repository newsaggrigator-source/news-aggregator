Azerbaijan, Somalia discuss cultural cooperation - azərtac

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 18:52:00 GMT

On October 28, 2026, Azerbaijan's Minister of Culture, Adil Karimli, met with Abdulfatah Kasim Mohamud, Somalia's Minister of Information, Culture, and Tourism, during the 7th World Forum on Intercultural Dialogue in Baku. The discussion focused on enhancing cultural cooperation between the two nations, emphasizing the importance of mutual experience exchange and expanding bilateral relations. Karimli acknowledged Somalia's significant participation in the Forum and expressed gratitude for its support of Azerbaijan's initiatives on international platforms. Mohamud thanked Azerbaijan for the invitation and praised the organization of the event. The meeting also included an exchange of views on various topics of mutual interest, highlighting the potential for collaboration in cultural initiatives. This engagement reflects both countries' commitment to fostering cultural ties and understanding through shared experiences and dialogue.
