Tue, 24 Nov 2026 14:43:26 GMT

Ethiopia and Djibouti are engaging in discussions aimed at enhancing port efficiency and improving trade corridors in anticipation of the upcoming rainy season. The talks focus on optimizing the logistics and transportation systems that connect the two nations, which are vital for Ethiopia's access to international markets through Djibouti's ports. Both countries recognize the importance of these improvements to facilitate smoother trade operations and mitigate potential disruptions caused by seasonal weather changes. The collaboration is expected to bolster economic ties and ensure that goods can be transported more effectively, benefiting both economies. As the rainy season approaches, the urgency of these discussions highlights the need for proactive measures to maintain trade flow and address any challenges that may arise. The outcomes of these talks could significantly impact regional trade dynamics and economic stability for both Ethiopia and Djibouti.
