Somalia expels UAE ambassador, accuses Abu Dhabi of violating sovereignty - Al Jazeera

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 21:32:15 GMT

Somalia has expelled the UAE ambassador, demanding his departure within 72 hours, and has withdrawn its envoy from Abu Dhabi. Somali officials accuse the UAE of violating the country's sovereignty, describing its actions as an "open invasion." Information Minister Abdifatah Kasim Mohamud stated that any cooperation with the UAE would face consequences, urging Somalis to unite against perceived foreign interference. Tensions escalated after President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accused the UAE of supporting regional administrations in opposition to the federal government, particularly regarding port agreements in Somaliland, Puntland, and Jubaland. Mogadishu views these agreements as invalid, asserting that all international dealings should go through the federal government. The UAE, which has previously trained and funded Somali military forces, has denied any meddling in Somalia's internal affairs. This expulsion marks a significant deterioration in relations, following Somalia's earlier cancellation of all agreements with the UAE due to actions deemed harmful to national unity.
