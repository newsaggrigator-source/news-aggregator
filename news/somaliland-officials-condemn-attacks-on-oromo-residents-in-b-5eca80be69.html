Somaliland Officials Condemn Attacks On Oromo Residents in Borama After More Than 150 Homes Reportedly Damaged

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 06:34:12 GMT

Somaliland officials have condemned recent attacks on Oromo residents in Borama, where over 150 homes were reportedly damaged or looted. The violence, attributed to groups of youths, involved vandalism and arson against homes occupied by long-standing Oromo families. Many affected residents sought refuge at Borama airport and other safe locations. Awdal regional governor Boqor Hassan Dahir Haddi denounced the attacks, emphasizing that they contradict the community's values of peaceful coexistence. Authorities have arrested some suspects and are pursuing others, pledging legal action against those responsible. Somaliland Information Minister Barkhad Jama Batun also condemned the violence, warning against taking the law into one's own hands. The attacks were reportedly fueled by inflammatory social media content, prompting community leaders to call for accountability and protection for Oromo residents. They stressed that ethnic or political grievances do not justify violence against innocent civilians, urging unity and caution against incitement.
