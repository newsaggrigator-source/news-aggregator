Honorary Defense Members Push for Stronger Commitment to Ethiopia's Red Sea Access

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 22:41:04 GMT

Honorary members of the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) are advocating for a stronger commitment to Ethiopia's access to the Red Sea. During a ceremony marking the 119th anniversary of the FDRE Defense Forces Day, members such as Biniyam Getachew and Afewerk Bedewi emphasized the importance of peaceful diplomacy and dialogue with neighboring countries. They highlighted that Ethiopia's aspirations for maritime access should be pursued through negotiations, fostering mutual respect and cooperation, particularly with Eritrea. Biniyam called for increased public awareness and youth engagement in discussions about Ethiopia's maritime goals. Afewerk stressed the need for national resolve and military discipline, asserting that the Ethiopian people can achieve their objectives through collective commitment. Both speakers underscored the historical injustices surrounding Ethiopia's landlocked status and the importance of understanding these circumstances to inform future actions. They reiterated that achieving sea access requires a united effort from the Ethiopian populace, emphasizing diplomacy as a key component in maintaining peaceful relations. (159 words)
