Ethiopia : U.S. Embassy in Addis Ababa Issues Security Alert - Borkena

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 20:55:30 GMT

The U.S. Embassy in Addis Ababa has issued a security alert regarding the ongoing volatile situation in Ethiopia, particularly in the Tigray, Afar, and Amhara regions. The alert indicates that while the regional capital, Mekelle, is not currently experiencing active fighting, clashes continue in surrounding areas, especially near the Eritrean border. The Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) has announced preparations for a counter-offensive, suggesting a potential escalation in conflict. The embassy has advised American citizens to reconsider travel to Ethiopia and to avoid the aforementioned regions, urging those already there to leave if safe. Additionally, the embassy is monitoring tensions in Moyale, a border town with Kenya, where recent conflicts have occurred. The situation remains fluid, with warnings to avoid military installations and acknowledgment of recent drone attacks in Addis Ababa, which have not been officially confirmed by Ethiopian authorities.
