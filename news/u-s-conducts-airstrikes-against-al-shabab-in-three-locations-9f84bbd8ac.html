U.S. conducts airstrikes against al-Shabab in three locations across Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 19:23:58 GMT

The U.S. military conducted airstrikes against al-Shabab militants in three locations in southern Somalia from November 13 to 15, 2026, in collaboration with the Somali federal government. The strikes targeted militant positions near Leego, Yora Lafagari, and Jamaame, areas where ongoing operations against the al-Qaeda-linked group are taking place. The Somali government stated that one strike occurred near Leego, about 114 kilometers northwest of Mogadishu, while another targeted Yora Lafagari, roughly 160 kilometers west of Jilib. The third strike was near Jamaame, approximately 47 kilometers northeast of Kismayo. These actions are part of a broader military campaign by Somali forces to eliminate al-Shabab, which has been waging an insurgency against the federal government for nearly two decades. The U.S. has consistently supported these counterterrorism efforts through airstrikes, aiming to disrupt al-Shabab's operations and enhance security in the region. (158 words)
