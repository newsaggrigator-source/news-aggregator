Eritrea’s Antagonistic Policy Threatens Regional Stability, Says Prof. Alemayehu - ENA English

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 14:35:13 GMT

Eritrea's confrontational policies towards Ethiopia pose a significant threat to regional stability, according to Emeritus Professor Alemayehu G. Mariam. He argues that Eritrea's leadership perceives Ethiopia as a strategic adversary, believing that fostering instability in Ethiopia benefits its own regime's survival. Alemayehu highlights that Eritrea is actively supporting armed groups hostile to Ethiopia, supplying them with weapons and facilitating military assistance from Egypt and Sudan. This strategy aims to weaken Ethiopia rather than foster constructive bilateral relations. In contrast, Ethiopia is pursuing regional economic integration and cooperation, which Eritrea's antagonistic stance obstructs. Alemayehu also criticizes Eritrea's authoritarian political system, which lacks elected leadership and contributes to societal costs and regional instability. He cites evidence of Eritrean military incursions into Ethiopian territory and calls for a firm response from Ethiopia, including potential self-defense measures. He emphasizes that continued confrontation will hinder lasting peace and cooperation in the Horn of Africa.
