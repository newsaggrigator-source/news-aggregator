Somalia: Jubaland leader accuses Hassan Sheikh of putting personal interests ahead of national unity - Garowe Online

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 12:02:21 GMT

Jubaland President Ahmed Madobe has criticized former President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud for prioritizing personal interests over national unity, amidst escalating tensions between the federal government and regional leaders in Somalia. Madobe accused Mohamud of failing to honor political agreements essential for the country's state-building process and emphasized the need for renewed dialogue to address ongoing political disputes. He expressed concerns about the security situation in Mogadishu, stating that the capital is not a reliable venue for political discussions, which complicates cooperation with the federal government. Madobe warned that Somalia is on the brink of collapse and fragmentation, urging leaders to come together for urgent consultations to stabilize the nation. His comments reflect the broader challenges facing Somalia, including disputes over constitutional changes and the future of its federal system.
