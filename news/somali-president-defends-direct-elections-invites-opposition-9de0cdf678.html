Somali President defends direct elections, invites opposition to talks amid electoral dispute - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 05:44:41 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has defended the government's initiative for direct elections, emphasizing their significance in restoring democracy after years of indirect voting. In a televised address, he invited opposition leaders to engage in talks to address ongoing disputes regarding the electoral process. Mohamud highlighted recent local and regional elections as progress, noting that 1,824,853 people registered to vote, with 1,435 officials elected through direct voting. He asserted that these elections occurred without violence and adhered to legal frameworks. However, opposition groups have rejected the electoral timetable announced by the National Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission, calling for negotiations before proceeding. The president urged political leaders to resolve disagreements through dialogue rather than confrontation and invited various political parties to a national consultative forum in November to discuss electoral arrangements. Despite the invitation, the potential for reaching an agreement remains uncertain, given past failures in negotiations over the electoral model and related issues.
