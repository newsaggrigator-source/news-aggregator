Somalia rejects Israeli envoy and embassy plans in Somaliland - Somalia Today

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 17:36:32 GMT

Somalia has officially rejected the accreditation of Israel's first ambassador to the self-declared independent region of Somaliland, as well as the establishment of an Israeli embassy in Hargeisa. The Somali foreign ministry stated that these actions were taken without the federal government's consent and declared them "null and void." Israel recognized Somaliland's independence in December 2025, becoming the first UN member to do so. In response, Somalia urged Israel to reverse these measures and called on other nations to refrain from legitimizing them. The Arab League also condemned the developments, asserting they violated Somalia's territorial integrity. Somaliland, which has operated independently since 1991, maintains its own governance and security but is not recognized as a separate state by the African Union or Somalia's federal government. The Somali ministry emphasized that diplomatic relations require mutual consent, warning that actions undermining recognized borders could exacerbate regional tensions.
