Somalia, Kenya welcome enforcement of new IGAD treaty - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 19:15:28 GMT

Somalia and Kenya have expressed their support for the enforcement of the 2023 Treaty Establishing the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD), which took effect on November 16, 2026. This treaty marks a significant advancement in regional cooperation in the Greater Horn of Africa, following Somalia's ratification in January. Both countries issued statements congratulating IGAD member states and the Secretariat, emphasizing the treaty's role in strengthening the institution's legal and operational framework. Somalia highlighted its commitment to regional cooperation, reaffirming principles of sovereignty and mutual respect, while Kenya noted the treaty's importance in addressing ongoing political, security, and economic challenges in the region. The treaty aims to enhance IGAD's capacity for promoting trade, infrastructure development, and climate resilience, marking a pivotal moment as the organization celebrates 40 years since its inception. Both nations are committed to working collaboratively through IGAD to foster peace, security, and economic integration in the region.
