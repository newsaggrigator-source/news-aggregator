Fri, 13 Nov 2026 12:27:09 GMT

UN experts have expressed strong condemnation regarding the recent dismissal of Somalia's chief justice. They have called for the immediate reinstatement of the chief justice, emphasizing the importance of judicial independence in the country. The experts argue that the decision undermines the rule of law and could have detrimental effects on the justice system in Somalia. They highlighted that such actions could erode public trust in judicial institutions and hinder efforts to establish a fair and impartial legal framework. The UN experts urged the Somali government to respect the independence of the judiciary and to ensure that judicial officials can perform their duties without political interference. This situation reflects broader concerns about governance and the protection of human rights in Somalia, as the country continues to navigate complex political challenges. The call for reinstatement underscores the critical role of an independent judiciary in promoting democracy and upholding justice in Somalia.
