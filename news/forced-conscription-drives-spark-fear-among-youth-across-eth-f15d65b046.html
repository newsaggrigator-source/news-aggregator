Forced Conscription Drives Spark Fear Among Youth Across Ethiopia - Borkena

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 22:03:16 GMT

Young men across Ethiopia are experiencing heightened anxiety due to widespread forced conscription drives conducted by local militias and security forces. Reports from regions such as Amhara, Oromia, Wolaita, and Southwest Ethiopia highlight a disturbing pattern of arbitrary street detentions and enforced disappearances targeting individuals, particularly those without state identity documents. Recruitment operations often occur in public spaces after dark, with victims being forcibly taken to undisclosed military facilities. Residents in areas like Sodo and South Wollo have resorted to self-imposed curfews to avoid these patrols. Disturbingly, minors and vulnerable individuals, including orphans and migrant laborers, are also being rounded up to meet local recruitment quotas without any formal notification to families. While some public outcry has temporarily reduced the frequency of these operations, fear remains pervasive among the youth, who view the conscription as a form of irreversible disappearance. Human rights advocates emphasize that these actions violate constitutional protections against arbitrary detention.
