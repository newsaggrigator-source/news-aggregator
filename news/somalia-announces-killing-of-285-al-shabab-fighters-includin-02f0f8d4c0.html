Somalia announces killing of 285 al-Shabab fighters, including founding commanders - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 05:10:50 GMT

The Somali government announced the killing of 285 al-Shabab militants, including 12 senior commanders, during recent military operations across southern and central Somalia. Outgoing Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi revealed these details at a press conference in Mogadishu, highlighting that the operations targeted key figures responsible for various military functions within the group. Significant fighting occurred in the Bay region, particularly around Daynuunay. Among those killed was Isaaq Adan Madgaab, a founding member of al-Shabab, along with other notable commanders involved in communications and logistics. The operations were conducted by the Somali National Army, including Danab special forces, with international air support. Fiqi emphasized that the goal of these operations was to improve security conditions for civilians, allowing them to resume normal activities. The government did not disclose details on any potential losses among Somali forces, and al-Shabab has yet to comment on these claims.
