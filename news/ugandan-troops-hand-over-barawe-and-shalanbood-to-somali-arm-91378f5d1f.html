Ugandan troops hand over Barawe and Shalanbood to Somali army in major security test - Somali Guardian

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 09:58:46 GMT

Ugandan troops have officially handed over security responsibilities for the strategic towns of Barawe and Shalanbood to the Somali National Army (SNA), marking the end of over a decade of military presence in these areas. This transition is a significant test for Somalia's ability to maintain security without foreign military support, particularly in the Lower Shabelle region, where Al-Shabaab remains a formidable threat. The handover coincides with Uganda's gradual troop withdrawal under bilateral agreements with the Somali government, expected to be completed by the end of 2027. The operational readiness of Somali forces is under scrutiny, especially given past instances where they lost control of areas after similar transfers. The political climate in Somalia, characterized by divisions and power struggles, further complicates the situation, potentially diverting focus from security efforts. The outcomes in Barawe and Shalanbood will be critical in assessing the SNA's capability to secure territory and counter militant advances as international support diminishes.
