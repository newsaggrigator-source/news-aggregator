Oman's ambassador to Somalia presents credentials - Times of Oman

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 12:04:00 GMT

Oman's newly appointed ambassador to Somalia, Salim Habib Al Omairi, presented his credentials to Somali President Dr. Hassan Sheikh Mohamud on November 4, 2026. During the meeting, Al Omairi conveyed greetings from His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tarik, expressing wishes for the President's health and happiness, as well as for the stability and prosperity of the Somali people. In response, President Mohamud extended his greetings to the Sultan and the Omani people, wishing them continued progress and prosperity. He also expressed hope for the successful performance of the ambassador's duties and emphasized the importance of further developing the fraternal relations between Oman and Somalia. This diplomatic engagement marks a significant step in strengthening ties between the two nations, reflecting mutual respect and cooperation.
