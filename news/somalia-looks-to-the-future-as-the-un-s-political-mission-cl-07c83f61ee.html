Somalia looks to the future as the UN's political mission closes - UN News

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 08:57:18 GMT

The UN's political mission in Somalia, known as UNTMIS, officially concludes on October 31, marking the end of three decades of international engagement aimed at stabilizing the country. Raisedon Zenenga, the UN Secretary-General's Deputy Special Representative for Somalia, highlighted the mission's role in supporting Somalia's recovery from civil war and state collapse in the early 1990s. Despite significant progress in governance and institutional development, Somalia faces ongoing challenges, including humanitarian crises exacerbated by conflict and climate shocks. With the mission's closure, Somali institutions will assume full responsibility for the country's recovery, supported by various UN agencies still active in the region. The UN continues to emphasize the importance of international support to address pressing issues such as poverty and security threats from groups like Al-Shabaab. Zenenga noted that this transition represents not an end but a new chapter in the partnership between the UN and Somalia.
