Why South West State fighting in Somalia is threatening Kenya's security and miraa trade - The Standard

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 03:00:00 GMT

Fighting in Somalia's South West State is escalating tensions between Kenya and Somalia, posing risks to regional security and trade, particularly affecting the miraa (khat) trade. Somali officials have accused Kenya of harboring Somali terrorists who allegedly plan to destabilize Somalia while receiving protection in Nairobi. This situation has led to increased diplomatic strain, as Somalia's parliament speaker has called on the federal government to confront Kenya about its perceived support for these militants. The ongoing conflict not only threatens the stability of Somalia but also jeopardizes the livelihoods of miraa traders in Kenya, who rely on the export of this cash crop to Somalia. As the violence continues, concerns grow over the implications for both countries' security and economic interests, highlighting the interconnectedness of regional dynamics in the Horn of Africa.
