Somaliland Human Rights Group Demands Investigation Into Mandera Executions and 2024 Erigavo Shooting - FTL Somalia

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 20:28:55 GMT

The Somaliland Human Rights Monitoring Group (SHRMG) has called for an investigation into the recent execution of Akram Buraale Ahmed and eight others at Mandera prison on November 11, 2026. The group condemned the executions, which were authorized by the president without full trial records or family notifications. Ahmed was reportedly involved in a government security operation in Erigavo in May 2024, during which an accidental shooting resulted in the death of an 18-year-old. SHRMG highlighted inconsistencies in official records regarding the incident and argued that executing someone for unintentional killing violates international legal standards. They demanded the publication of court files, operational orders, and an independent inquiry into the executions, alongside a moratorium on capital punishment. This incident raises concerns about judicial transparency and accountability in Somaliland, especially as it seeks recognition as a stable democracy while continuing to impose death sentences.
