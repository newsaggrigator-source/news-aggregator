U.S. Issues Travel Alert as Border Clashes and Troop Build-Up Heighten Ethiopia-Eritrea War Fears, Reports - Borkena

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 04:24:52 GMT

Rising tensions along the Ethiopia-Eritrea border have prompted the U.S. Embassy in Ethiopia to issue a travel alert for American citizens. The warning, released on November 19, 2026, advises against travel to the Tigray, Amhara, and Afar regions, particularly southern Afar and rural Tigray, urging those already in these areas to leave while it is still safe. Recent reports indicate heavy troop deployments and artillery exchanges, raising fears of a potential war as forces from both nations position themselves near the contested Badme area. The situation has escalated since mid-September 2026, with accusations exchanged between the two governments at the United Nations. Local accounts describe artillery and drone strikes displacing residents, while Eritrean officials have dismissed reports of clashes as propaganda. The deteriorating relations stem from Ethiopia's pursuit of sea access and mutual accusations involving armed groups.
