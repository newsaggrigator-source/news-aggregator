Somalia's cabinet approves changes to election laws - TRT Afrika

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 21:19:47 GMT

Somalia's cabinet has approved significant amendments to the country's electoral laws during an extraordinary meeting led by Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre. These changes aim to align the electoral framework with recent constitutional reforms. The revisions affect 23 provisions of the National Electoral Law and eight provisions of the Political Associations and Parties Law. Under the new system, Somali citizens will directly elect members of local councils and federal legislatures, while government leaders, including the president, will be chosen by representative bodies rather than through direct votes. Additionally, the amendments introduce a requirement for political parties to secure at least 10 percent of votes in federal elections to gain national recognition. A closed-list electoral system will also be implemented, allowing voters to select party lists instead of individual candidates. These changes come amid ongoing political disagreements, with opposition groups calling for broader consultations on electoral reforms.
