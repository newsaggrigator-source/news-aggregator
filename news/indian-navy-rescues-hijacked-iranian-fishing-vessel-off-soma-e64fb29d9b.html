Indian Navy rescues hijacked Iranian fishing vessel off Somalia coast - Manorama Yearbook

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 12:06:53 GMT

The Indian Navy successfully rescued an Iranian fishing vessel, the Azini, which had been hijacked by pirates off the coast of Somalia. The operation, lasting over 48 hours, involved the coordinated efforts of a mission-deployed warship and a maritime reconnaissance aircraft. The vessel, with 20 crew members aboard, was taken by an armed group in October, posing a significant maritime security threat as it could have been used as a "mother ship" for further attacks on merchant shipping. The Indian Navy tracked the vessel's movements and employed non-kinetic measures to prevent its escape. Marine commandos were deployed via a precision airdrop from an Indian Air Force C-17 aircraft. The operation concluded with the safe rescue of all crew members, with no casualties reported. This successful intervention highlights India's commitment to being a first responder and a preferred security partner in the Indian Ocean Region. (160 words)
