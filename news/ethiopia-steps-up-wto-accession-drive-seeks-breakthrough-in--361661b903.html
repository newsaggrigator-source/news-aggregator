Ethiopia Steps Up WTO Accession Drive, Seeks Breakthrough in Geneva Talks

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 02:13:54 GMT

Ethiopia is intensifying its efforts to join the World Trade Organization (WTO) as it seeks to finalize long-standing accession negotiations. At the recent Eighth WTO Working Party meeting in Geneva, Ethiopian Minister of Trade and Regional Integration Yasmin Wahabrebbi led the delegation, emphasizing the importance of WTO membership for the nation's economic integration and access to global markets. This renewed push follows the establishment of a new government in October and aims to build on economic reforms initiated since the accession process began in 2003. Minister Yasmin highlighted Ethiopia's progress in aligning its legal frameworks with WTO standards and expressed readiness to conclude bilateral negotiations. WTO officials acknowledged Ethiopia's reform efforts and the potential benefits of integrating its market of over 130 million people into the global trading system. The ongoing negotiations are seen as crucial for enhancing Ethiopia's export competitiveness and fostering regional economic integration, particularly through the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA).
