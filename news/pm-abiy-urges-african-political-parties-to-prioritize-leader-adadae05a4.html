PM Abiy Urges African Political Parties to Prioritize Leadership Renewal, Long-Term Dev't

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 12:23:04 GMT

During the Second African Political Parties Summit in Addis Ababa, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed urged African political parties to prioritize leadership renewal and establish consensus on long-term national development goals. He emphasized that development should extend beyond electoral cycles and government changes, advocating for strategic national objectives with measurable milestones to ensure continuity. Abiy highlighted the importance of leadership succession in sustaining progress, warning that failing to prepare future leaders could jeopardize hard-won achievements. He also shared Ethiopia's experience in political inclusiveness, noting the incorporation of opposition leaders into the government. Additionally, he discussed Ethiopia's Homegrown Economic Reform Agenda and the Green Legacy Initiative, which has seen over 50 billion seedlings planted to combat climate challenges. The summit's theme, "Governing for Growth: Political Parties as Architects of Africa's Economic Future," underscores the need for political parties to collaborate on shared national priorities for enduring development.
