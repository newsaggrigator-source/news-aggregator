A Week in the Horn - MFAEthiopia Blog

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 13:58:21 GMT

Ethiopia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs blog highlights significant diplomatic activities in the Horn of Africa during the first week of November 2026. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed led a high-level delegation to the Third Russia–Africa Summit in Moscow, focusing on enhancing Ethiopia-Russia relations and exploring partnerships in strategic sectors. The summit concluded with a declaration and an action plan to foster cooperation. Additionally, Ethiopia commemorated the 40th anniversary of the UN Declaration on the Right to Development at the African Union, emphasizing the need for sustainable prosperity. Minister Gedion Timothewos engaged with China's Special Envoy for African Affairs to discuss peace and security, while State Minister Ambassador Hadera Abera met with the EU Political and Security Committee to address regional developments. Ethiopia also participated in the International Solar Alliance assembly, reaffirming its commitment to renewable energy. The week underscored Ethiopia's active role in regional diplomacy and its focus on development and security partnerships.
