US media highlights expanded airfields in northern Somalia linked to UAE and American special forces - ssbulten.com.tr

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 15:19:38 GMT

US media has spotlighted two expanded airfields in northern Somalia, specifically in the Somaliland and Puntland regions, which have been developed by the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and are reportedly utilized by American special operations forces. The New York Times describes these facilities as "pirate airbases," raising concerns about the implications of foreign military presence in the Horn of Africa. Critics view the UAE's involvement as indicative of its role as a proxy for Israel in the region. Additionally, Somalia's federal government has entered into defense agreements with Turkey, granting Turkish forces operational authority along the Horn of Africa coastline. This arrangement allows Turkey to potentially deploy warships and establish a maritime blockade around these airfields, which is crucial for Turkey's strategy to secure maritime trade routes and counter foreign military installations in the area. The situation underscores the complexities of international military dynamics in Somalia and the broader region.
