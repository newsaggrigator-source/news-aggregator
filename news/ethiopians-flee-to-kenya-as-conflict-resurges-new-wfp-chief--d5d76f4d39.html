Ethiopians Flee to Kenya as Conflict Resurges, New WFP Chief, UN Mission Ends in Somalia - Mirage News

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 20:40:00 GMT

Approximately 36,600 Ethiopians have fled to northern Kenya due to escalating conflict along the Ethiopian border, as reported by the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The fighting, primarily between forces from the Somali and Oromia regions, has intensified recently, prompting a new wave of displacement. Nearly 11,000 refugees have taken shelter at the Oriental Mission Church, while the remaining 25,600 are being accommodated by local communities. IOM is collaborating with the Kenyan government and the UNHCR to assess humanitarian needs, including water, shelter, food assistance, and health support. Meanwhile, Luke J. Lindberg has assumed the role of Executive Director of the UN World Food Programme (WFP), emphasizing the urgent need for global support to combat hunger exacerbated by conflict. Additionally, the UN's political mission in Somalia is set to conclude, transferring responsibilities to Somali institutions, as the government seeks greater control over its peacebuilding efforts.
