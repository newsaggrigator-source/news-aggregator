Mahama arrives in Ethiopia for historic inauguration of Megech Irrigation Project - Ghana News Agency

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 19:55:05 GMT

President John Dramani Mahama arrived in Gondar, Ethiopia, on October 23, 2026, for the inauguration of the Megech Irrigation Project, following an invitation from Ethiopian Prime Minister Dr. Abiy Ahmed Ali. This visit precedes the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, scheduled to take place in Bahir Dar. Upon his arrival, Mahama joined Prime Minister Abiy in activating the Megech Irrigation Development Project, a significant infrastructure initiative aimed at enhancing agriculture and water supply in the region. The project, which began 18 years ago but was stalled, was revived and completed by Abiy's government. The Megech Dam spans 870 hectares and has a storage capacity of 185 million cubic meters, set to irrigate 17,000 hectares of farmland, thereby improving food security and productivity. Additionally, it will provide clean water to Gondar and nearby towns. Mahama praised Ethiopia's commitment to this transformative project, which promises long-term benefits for the community. He will chair the upcoming Tana Forum to discuss critical security issues in Africa.
