Five Farmers Killed, Over 100 Cattle Looted in South Ethiopia's Burji Zone - Three More Killed in Kore

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:07:49 GMT

In a tragic series of attacks in southern Ethiopia, five farmers were killed and over 100 cattle looted in the Burji Zone on October 22. The incident occurred in Soyama Zuria Woreda while the victims were engaged in communal agricultural work. Armed assailants from the neighboring Guji Zone were identified as the perpetrators. Local sources criticized the South Ethiopia Regional State for its silence on the violence, which they deemed a lack of respect for the Burji people. Additionally, three more individuals, including a 14-year-old boy, were killed in a separate attack in Kore Zone two days later. The Kore Zone Administration condemned the violence and called for increased security measures. These incidents are part of ongoing insecurity in the region, which has seen similar attacks and cattle thefts since early 2021, raising concerns about the safety of local communities.
