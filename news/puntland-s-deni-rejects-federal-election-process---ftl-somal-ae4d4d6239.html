Puntland’s Deni Rejects Federal Election Process - FTL Somalia

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 15:18:06 GMT

Puntland President Said Abdullahi Deni has publicly rejected the federal election process proposed by President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, accusing him of obstructing dialogue and failing to honor agreed-upon electoral timelines. During the opening of Puntland's parliament session, Deni emphasized the need for urgent discussions among Somali stakeholders to prevent further political deterioration, asserting that his administration would not participate in the federal electoral process. This rejection reflects a broader discontent among Puntland and other regions, such as Jubaland, regarding the federal government's electoral timetable and constitutional amendments, which they claim centralize power excessively. The political tensions are compounded by recent violent incidents in Puntland, including the deaths of civilians, which have raised concerns about accountability and foreign military operations in the region. Deni's stance signals a significant rift between Puntland and the federal government, highlighting the fragility of Somalia's political landscape and the urgent need for a collaborative resolution.
