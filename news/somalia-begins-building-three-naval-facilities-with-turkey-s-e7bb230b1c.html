Somalia begins building three naval facilities with Turkey’s support — Jerusalem Post - UA.NEWS

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 09:13:26 GMT

Somalia has commenced the construction of three naval facilities with support from Turkey, as announced by Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud during a foundation-laying ceremony. The project, reported by the Jerusalem Post, includes the establishment of the Somali Navy Command headquarters, a maritime operations coordination center, and a facility for loading and unloading naval vessels. Somali Defense Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi emphasized that this partnership aims to enhance the country's defense capabilities and restore its military potential. He expressed gratitude to Turkey for its ongoing support in rebuilding Somalia's armed forces, which has included military training and infrastructure investments. The collaboration between Somalia and Turkey has been long-standing, with Turkey playing a significant role in enhancing Somalia's maritime security, given its strategic location along the Indian Ocean and Gulf of Aden. This initiative reflects Somalia's commitment to safeguarding its waters and natural resources.
