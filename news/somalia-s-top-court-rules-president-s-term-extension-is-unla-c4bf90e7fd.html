Somalia’s top court rules president’s term extension is unlawful, deepening power struggle - Temple Daily Telegram

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 14:14:50 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court has ruled that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term extension is unlawful, declaring that his term has expired and mandating elections within 90 days. This decision intensifies the ongoing power struggle in the country, which has faced political instability and challenges in governance. The ruling comes amid a backdrop of heightened tensions and concerns over the electoral process, as the nation grapples with security issues, including threats from the militant group Al-Shabaab. The court's order for immediate elections aims to restore democratic processes, but it also raises questions about the potential for further conflict among political factions. As the situation unfolds, the international community is closely monitoring developments in Somalia, hoping for a peaceful resolution that can lead to stability and effective governance in the region.
