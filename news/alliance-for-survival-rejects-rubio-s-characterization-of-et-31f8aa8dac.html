Alliance for Survival rejects Rubio’s characterization of #Ethiopia’s conflict as separatist, terrorist - Facebook

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 12:32:49 GMT

The Alliance for Survival has rejected Senator Marco Rubio's characterization of the conflict in Ethiopia as separatist and terrorist in nature. The group argues that such labels misrepresent the complexities of the situation and undermine the legitimate grievances of various communities involved in the conflict. They emphasize that the ongoing violence is rooted in deeper political and social issues rather than a simple separatist agenda. The Alliance calls for a more nuanced understanding of the conflict and urges for dialogue and peaceful resolution rather than the use of inflammatory rhetoric. They stress the importance of addressing the underlying causes of the unrest to achieve lasting peace in the region.
