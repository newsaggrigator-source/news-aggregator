African Union Somalia Bases Plan Follows Uganda Exit - kulu-media.com

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 19:21:40 GMT

The African Union (AU) plans to secure four military bases in Somalia as Ugandan troops begin their withdrawal, which raises concerns about potential security gaps that could be exploited by Al-Shabaab militants. The bases, located near Mogadishu in the Lower Shabelle region, are critical for maintaining security in the area. The African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) intends to redeploy peacekeepers to Sabiid-Anole, Bariire, Awdheegle, and Bulonagad during troop rotations scheduled for November 2026. Uganda's withdrawal, which has been ongoing since November 2, marks the end of nearly two decades of military involvement in Somalia. The AU has warned that this departure could leave southern Somalia vulnerable to attacks. The transition is complicated by financial and logistical challenges faced by AUSSOM, raising questions about the ability of Somali forces to maintain security in the absence of Ugandan troops. The situation underscores the ongoing threat posed by Al-Shabaab and the need for effective coordination among Somali and AU forces.
