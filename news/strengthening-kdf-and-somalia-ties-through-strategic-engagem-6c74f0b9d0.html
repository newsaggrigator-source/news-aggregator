STRENGTHENING KDF AND SOMALIA TIES THROUGH STRATEGIC ENGAGEMENT - Ministry of Defence – Kenya

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 04:38:45 GMT

The Kenya Defence Forces (KDF) are enhancing their collaboration with Somalia through strategic engagements aimed at fostering peace and stability in the region. Recently, Colonel Daniel Musau, Deputy Commander of AUSSOM Sector II, met with Ahmed Mohamed Islam, President of Jubbaland State, in Kismayo. This meeting focused on strengthening ties between KDF and the Jubbaland administration, emphasizing the importance of cooperation in addressing security challenges and supporting stabilization efforts. Colonel Musau highlighted the necessity of ongoing engagement with local leadership to achieve shared security objectives. This initiative is part of broader efforts to consolidate partnerships and enhance collective actions towards lasting peace in Somalia, showcasing the commitment of KDF to work closely with regional authorities for mutual benefit. The collaboration is crucial for creating a conducive environment for development and stability in the region.
