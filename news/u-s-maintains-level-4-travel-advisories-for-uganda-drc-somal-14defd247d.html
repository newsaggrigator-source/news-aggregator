U.S. maintains level 4 travel advisories for Uganda, DRC, Somalia and South Sudan - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 11:21:29 GMT

The U.S. government has issued a Level 4 travel advisory for Uganda, the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC), Somalia, and South Sudan, urging citizens to avoid these countries due to significant security and health risks. The advisory, released on November 9, highlights concerns such as terrorism, violent crime, kidnapping, political unrest, and health threats, including an ongoing Ebola outbreak in Uganda. Specific warnings include potential terrorist attacks on public places and the risk of violent protests. Somalia faces similar dangers, including crime, terrorism, and systematic mistreatment of marginalized groups. The DRC is flagged for health issues and crime. The U.S. advises Americans in these regions to register with the Smart Traveler Enrollment Program for security updates and to prepare for emergencies. These countries join 22 others on the Level 4 list, emphasizing the serious risks associated with travel to these areas.
