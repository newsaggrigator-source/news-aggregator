The Ethiopia-Djibouti Trade Corridor and the Shared Stakes of Regional Security - horn review

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 16:41:44 GMT

The Ethiopia-Djibouti Trade Corridor is crucial for both nations, with over 95% of Ethiopia's trade relying on this route, which also significantly supports Djibouti's economy. Recent tensions, particularly the TPLF-Shabiya's attempts to disrupt this corridor, pose risks not only to Ethiopia's economic stability but also to Djibouti's port revenues. The Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) has shown military superiority, yet threats to the corridor highlight vulnerabilities that could be exploited by opposing forces. The situation underscores the need for enhanced bilateral security cooperation and integrated defense strategies to protect this vital infrastructure. Recent military developments, including the ENDF's counteroffensive, have temporarily reversed rebel advances, but ongoing vigilance and coordinated efforts are essential to ensure the corridor's security and maintain regional stability.
