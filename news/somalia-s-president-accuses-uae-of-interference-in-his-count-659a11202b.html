Somalia's president accuses UAE of 'interference' in his country - Middle East Eye

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:23:00 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has publicly accused the United Arab Emirates (UAE) of interfering in Somalia's internal affairs. In a televised address, he claimed that the UAE has established military bases near Mogadishu without the Somali government's consent and has been funding armed groups and political opponents. Mohamud emphasized that Somalia should not be treated as a "thoroughfare" or a commodity for foreign interests. This accusation highlights the ongoing tensions between Somalia and the UAE, which have been exacerbated by the UAE's support for Somaliland, a breakaway region seeking independence. The president's remarks reflect growing concerns over foreign influence in Somalia's political landscape and the need for national sovereignty. The situation underscores the complexities of international relations in the Horn of Africa, where external powers often engage in local conflicts for strategic interests.
