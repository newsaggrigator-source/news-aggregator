Türkiye outlines plans to strengthen Somalia’s maritime security - TRT Afrika

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 21:38:16 GMT

Türkiye has announced plans to enhance Somalia's maritime security by supporting the development of its naval forces and military infrastructure. The Turkish Defence Ministry, represented by Rear Admiral Zeki Aktürk, emphasized the importance of a Defence and Economic Cooperation Framework Agreement signed between the two nations. This initiative aims to protect Somalia's coastline and maritime zones from threats such as terrorism, piracy, illegal fishing, and smuggling. Key projects include establishing a new naval headquarters and a Maritime Operations Coordination Centre in Mogadishu, which will bolster Somalia's naval command and operational capabilities. Additionally, Türkiye will provide military training, equipment, and technical assistance to help Somalia manage its marine resources effectively. Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud highlighted the strategic significance of safeguarding Somalia's waters for regional security and economic stability, urging naval officers to prepare for increased responsibilities in protecting the nation's maritime interests. The partnership reflects Türkiye's commitment to supporting Somalia's security and economic development. (158 words)
