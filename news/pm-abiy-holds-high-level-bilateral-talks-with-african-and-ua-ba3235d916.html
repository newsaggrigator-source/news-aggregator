PM Abiy Holds High-Level Bilateral Talks With African and UAE Leaders On Cphia 2026 Sidelines

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 12:43:25 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed engaged in high-level bilateral talks with various African leaders and a senior UAE official during the Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026) in Addis Ababa. The discussions aimed to enhance diplomatic relations, economic cooperation, and address shared regional priorities. Notable meetings included talks with Sierra Leonean President Julius Maada Bio, where they focused on strengthening bilateral ties, and with Burundi's President Évariste Ndayishimiye, aimed at building on previous engagements. Abiy also met UAE Minister of State for Foreign Affairs Sheikh Shakhboot bin Nahyan Al Nahyan to reaffirm longstanding relations and explore deeper partnerships. Additionally, discussions with Ugandan Vice President Jessica Alupo emphasized translating historic ties into stronger cooperation. These engagements highlight the significance of ongoing diplomatic dialogue in fostering Ethiopia's partnerships across Africa and advancing common interests, all set against the backdrop of a conference focused on enhancing Africa's health security and resilience.
