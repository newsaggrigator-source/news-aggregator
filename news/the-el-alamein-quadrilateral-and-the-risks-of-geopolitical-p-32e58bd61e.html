The El-Alamein Quadrilateral and the Risks of Geopolitical Polarization in the North East Africa - horn review

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 10:50:44 GMT

The El-Alamein Quadrilateral, formed by Egypt, Eritrea, Sudan, and Somalia, aims to reshape the geopolitical landscape of North East Africa, particularly concerning the Nile and Red Sea. This grouping seeks to counter Ethiopia's rising influence, particularly following the operationalization of the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD), which Ethiopia views as a means to secure its water rights. The alliance is perceived as a strategic effort by Egypt to maintain dominance in the region while delegitimizing Ethiopia's claims to maritime access. Ethiopia, facing historical injustices and current geopolitical challenges, is striving for fair access to the sea and regional stability. The article warns that the El-Alamein bloc's exclusive approach could exacerbate tensions and hinder cooperative regional development, emphasizing the need for a more inclusive dialogue-based framework to address shared security and economic interests in the Horn of Africa.
