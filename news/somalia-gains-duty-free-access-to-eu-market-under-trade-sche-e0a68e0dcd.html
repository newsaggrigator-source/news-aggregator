Somalia Gains Duty-Free Access to EU Market Under Trade Scheme - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 15:08:05 GMT

Somalia has gained duty-free access to the European Union (EU) market under the Everything But Arms (EBA) initiative, which allows qualifying goods from the world's least developed countries to enter EU markets without import duties or export quotas. This arrangement, announced by the EU Delegation to Somalia, is expected to provide Somali producers with a competitive edge in expanding their overseas sales. However, businesses must comply with EU product standards and import regulations to benefit from this opportunity. The scheme does not have a fixed expiry date, offering ongoing potential for Somali exporters to develop their operations in Europe. While the arrangement removes tariffs and quotas, the actual increase in exports will depend on Somali businesses meeting regulatory requirements and finding buyers in the EU market. The delegation did not specify which products might benefit or provide estimates on potential trade increases resulting from this access.
