Northeastern State: Somalia’s Cornered Frontline Region in the Red Sea Proxy Contest. - pan african visions

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 15:23:38 GMT

A recent geopolitical map presented by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has spotlighted Somalia's newly established Northeastern State, positioning it as a critical player in the escalating Red Sea proxy contest. The map categorizes Somaliland and Puntland as part of a pro-Israel alliance, while the Northeastern State is aligned with Türkiye, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and Pakistan under the "Sunni Diamond" grouping. This classification underscores both the strategic significance and vulnerability of the Northeastern State, which was inaugurated in January 2026 amid tensions with its neighbors. Despite military successes in asserting control over contested territories, the Northeastern State faces hostility from Somaliland and Puntland, both of which have received substantial support from the UAE and Israel. The Northeastern State's survival hinges on robust backing from its allies, particularly in terms of security and reconstruction, as it navigates a complex landscape marked by competing foreign influences. The situation highlights the urgent need for tangible support to ensure its stability and legitimacy.
