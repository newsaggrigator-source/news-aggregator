ENDF at 119: Advancing Ethiopia’s Peace, Regional Stability and Tech Self-Reliance - ENA English

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 19:56:28 GMT

The Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) recently celebrated its 119th anniversary, highlighting its crucial role in safeguarding Ethiopia's sovereignty and contributing to regional stability. The ENDF has evolved significantly, focusing on reforms in organizational structure, professional training, and technological advancements to enhance its operational capabilities and reduce reliance on foreign suppliers. Its historical legacy, rooted in the defense of national independence, informs its current transformation towards a more professional and technologically adept military. The ENDF's responsibilities extend beyond military operations to include promoting national peace through civilian protection and institutional accountability. Additionally, the force plays a vital role in regional security cooperation and international peacekeeping, participating in UN missions since 1951. Recent initiatives aim to bolster Ethiopia's defense technology and domestic production capabilities, exemplified by the establishment of a drone technology center. These efforts are designed to ensure sustainable national defense while fostering regional cooperation and economic development.
