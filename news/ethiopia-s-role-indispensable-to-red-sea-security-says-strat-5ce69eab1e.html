Ethiopia’s Role Indispensable to Red Sea Security, Says Strategic Forum CEO - ENA English

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 04:20:55 GMT

Ethiopia's strategic significance for Red Sea security is increasingly recognized, according to Solomon Tefera, CEO of the newly formed Nile and Red Sea Strategic Forum. He emphasized that Ethiopia's involvement is crucial for achieving lasting stability in the region, given its geographical position, large population, and extensive economic interests. Tefera pointed out that while Ethiopia once relied on the Red Sea, the current geopolitical landscape necessitates that the Red Sea now depends on Ethiopia for security. As a landlocked nation reliant on Djibouti's port for trade, Ethiopia faces risks from disruptions in maritime routes, particularly around the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait. Such disruptions could impact essential imports and the national economy. Tefera argued that Ethiopia's maritime interests should be viewed as vital for regional economic integration and development. He called for increased international focus on Ethiopia's historical loss of sea access, advocating for dialogue and cooperation among Horn of Africa nations to address these issues.
