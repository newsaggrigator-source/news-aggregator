Somalia’s Petroleum Minister to Speak at Libya Energy & Economic Summit (LEES) 2027 - Africa.com

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 17:44:36 GMT

Somalia’s Minister of Petroleum and Mineral Resources, Dahir Shire Mohamed, is set to speak at the Libya Energy & Economic Summit (LEES) 2027, scheduled for January 23–25 in Tripoli. His participation aims to showcase Somalia's initiatives to attract international investment in its burgeoning petroleum and mining sectors, particularly as the country embarks on its first deepwater offshore drilling campaign. The drilling of the Curad-1 well, which began in April 2026, is expected to reveal the commercial viability of Somalia's offshore resources. Additionally, the Somali government is reforming its mining industry, having recently passed a comprehensive Mining Bill to enhance investor confidence. Mohamed's agenda includes fostering international partnerships and addressing fiscal governance to prepare for potential resource revenues. The summit will gather government officials, investors, and industry leaders to explore opportunities in Africa's energy sector, coinciding with Libya's goal of reaching 2 million barrels per day in oil production by 2030.
