Today's top news: Yemen, Occupied Palestinian Territory, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Somalia, Ukraine, Afghanistan, El Niño - OCHA

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 18:54:22 GMT

The United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) highlights critical humanitarian crises across several regions, including Yemen, the Occupied Palestinian Territory, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Somalia, and Ukraine. In Yemen, ongoing conflict has led to severe water and healthcare shortages in Ta’iz city, resulting in thousands of disease cases, while access to essential supplies is drastically limited. In Gaza, humanitarian efforts are focused on protecting displaced families from harsh winter conditions, exacerbated by recent flooding. The Democratic Republic of the Congo faces an expanding Ebola outbreak amid rising violence, complicating response efforts. Somalia grapples with displacement due to climate shocks and insecurity, with funding shortfalls severely impacting aid. In Ukraine, recent attacks have caused civilian casualties and damaged infrastructure, disrupting essential services. OCHA emphasizes the urgent need for humanitarian access and funding to address these escalating crises and support affected populations.
