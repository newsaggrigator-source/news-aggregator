PM Abiy Reviews Ethiopia's Progress Toward Nuclear Energy Development

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 12:02:59 GMT

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed recently visited the Ethiopian Nuclear Energy Commission to assess the country's progress in developing its nuclear energy program. He emphasized the importance of advanced technology in achieving Ethiopia's long-term development goals, stating that reliable and sustainable energy is crucial for industrialization and economic transformation. Abiy highlighted that nuclear technology could enhance energy security and contribute to advancements in various sectors, including healthcare and agriculture. He stressed the need for the development to be peaceful, safe, and sustainable, while also focusing on building skilled human capital and advancing scientific research. The Ethiopian Nuclear Energy Commission is tasked with establishing the necessary foundations for the national nuclear energy program to realize its potential.
