Russia, Ethiopia Advance Nuclear Power Talks, Discuss Expanded Flights and Visa Facilitation

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 04:40:46 GMT

Russia and Ethiopia are advancing discussions on a nuclear power plant project, with a draft intergovernmental agreement being coordinated by Rosatom and the Ethiopian Nuclear Energy Commission. Russian Ambassador Yevgeny Terekhin indicated that signing this agreement would initiate design and engineering work, following a non-disclosure agreement signed in December 2025. Additionally, the two nations are exploring expanded air travel options, including increasing flight frequencies and potentially introducing Russian airline services, contingent on passenger demand. Currently, Ethiopian Airlines operates six direct flights weekly between Addis Ababa and Moscow. Terekhin also mentioned plans to ease visa procedures for tourists and business travelers, enhancing reciprocal travel. This cooperation occurs amid heightened regional tensions, with Russia offering to facilitate dialogue between Ethiopia and Eritrea. The discussions reflect a broader commitment to deepen bilateral relations across various sectors, including trade, education, and military cooperation, as highlighted by recent engagements between leaders from both countries.
