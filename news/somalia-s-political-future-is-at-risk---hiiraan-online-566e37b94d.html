Somalia’s political future is at risk - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 00:28:07 GMT

Somalia's political future is increasingly precarious as tensions rise between the Federal Government of Somalia (FGS), Federal Member States (FMS), and opposition groups. Established as a federal system to distribute power and accommodate regional interests, the political framework is now under severe strain due to disputes over the Constitution, electoral processes, and the president's expired mandate. The situation has escalated into military confrontations, particularly in Southwest State, where federal forces have intervened. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's unilateral electoral timetable and constitutional amendments have deepened mistrust, as opposition groups fear he may attempt to extend his term without consensus. The proposed consultative forum in November has failed to restore confidence, with many viewing it as a tactic to delay meaningful negotiations. The ongoing conflict not only threatens political stability but also diverts the Somali National Army from its security role, exacerbating vulnerabilities to groups like Al-Shabaab. A negotiated settlement addressing these issues is urgently needed to secure Somalia's future.
