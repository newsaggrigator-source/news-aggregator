Unlocking Somalia's Investment Potential- Opportunities in the Blue Economy, Fisheries, Energy and Agriculture - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 22:28:23 GMT

Somalia is emerging as a promising investment destination, particularly in the blue economy, fisheries, energy, and agriculture sectors. Following significant debt relief and the enactment of the Somalia Investment Law 2025, foreign direct investment (FDI) has surged, reaching $834 million in 2025. The country boasts the longest coastline in mainland Africa, abundant fish stocks, and strong renewable energy resources, yet faces challenges such as high electricity costs and underdeveloped infrastructure. The article highlights the interconnectedness of these sectors, emphasizing that investments in renewable energy can enhance cold storage capabilities, thereby boosting fish and meat exports. Additionally, Somalia's fisheries sector shows vast potential, with sustainable yields estimated at over 200,000 tons annually, yet current catches remain significantly lower due to inadequate infrastructure. The government is actively improving the investment climate, making it an opportune time for both domestic and foreign investors to engage in Somalia's evolving economy.
