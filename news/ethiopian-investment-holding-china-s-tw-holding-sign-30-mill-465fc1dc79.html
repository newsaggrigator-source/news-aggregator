Ethiopian Investment Holding, China's Tw Holding Sign $30 Million Deal to Establish Textbook Printing Facility in Ethiopia

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 05:17:43 GMT

Ethiopian Investment Holdings (EIH) has partnered with China's TW Holding Limited to establish a $30 million textbook printing facility in Ethiopia. The joint venture will see TW Holding owning 51% and EIH 49%. The facility aims to address the significant gap in Ethiopia's demand for educational materials, which has been hampered by shortages and reliance on imports. TW Holding will provide the necessary technology and expertise for the facility, which will handle the printing, binding, and distribution of textbooks and teachers' guides. EIH's CEO, Dr. Brook Taye, emphasized that this investment will enhance domestic production capacity and contribute to a more resilient economy. This initiative is part of EIH's broader strategy to attract foreign investment and reduce dependency on imports across various sectors, including a recent partnership with Nigeria's Dangote Group for fertilizer production. The new facility is expected to improve the availability and affordability of educational resources in Ethiopia.
