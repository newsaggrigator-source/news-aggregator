Somaliland detains politician seeking Somalia presidency and journalist who interviewed him - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 07:17:05 GMT

Somaliland authorities have detained Abdikadir Mohamed Sharaay, a politician aspiring to run for Somalia's presidency, along with journalist Abdirahman Egeh, who interviewed him. Sharaay announced his presidential ambitions during a mid-October broadcast on Horn Cable TV, advocating for Somaliland to seek international recognition through dialogue with Mogadishu rather than aligning with foreign nations like Israel. His comments ignited a social media debate, prompting some local politicians to call for action against him. The reasons behind the arrests remain unclear, and it is uncertain if they are directly linked. Egeh's detention has raised alarms among media freedom advocates, who argue that journalists should not face repercussions for interviewing individuals expressing political opinions. Human rights defender Ahmedsadik Yusuf Mohamed criticized the arrests, highlighting concerns over freedom of expression in Somaliland. These detentions occur amid increasing scrutiny of Somaliland's government for its treatment of journalists, following previous arrests related to political commentary.
