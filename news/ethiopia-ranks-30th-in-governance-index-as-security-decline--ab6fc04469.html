Ethiopia ranks 30th in Governance Index as security decline offsets gains - CapitalEthiopia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 22:00:00 GMT

Ethiopia has been ranked 30th among 54 African countries in the 2026 Ibrahim Index of African Governance, sharing this position with Zimbabwe. The country scored 48.4 out of 100 for Overall Governance, which is below the continental average of 50.1 but slightly above the East African average of 47.5. While Ethiopia has seen improvements in economic opportunity and human development, these gains have been overshadowed by a decline in security and the rule of law, which fell by 2.4 points to 43.8. This deterioration has led the Mo Ibrahim Foundation to categorize Ethiopia under "Warning Signs" despite a net improvement over the past decade. In East Africa, Ethiopia ranks lower than Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, and Uganda, but higher than Djibouti, Eritrea, Sudan, Somalia, and South Sudan. The foundation emphasizes that economic and social progress cannot replace the need for stronger institutions and security.
