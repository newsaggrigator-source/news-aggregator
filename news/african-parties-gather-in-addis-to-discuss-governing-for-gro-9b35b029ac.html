African Parties Gather in Addis to Discuss Governing for Growth

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 08:29:16 GMT

Political leaders, heads of state, and policymakers from across Africa convened in Addis Ababa for the Second African Political Parties Summit (APPS) from November 5 to 7, 2026. The summit aims to explore strategies for fostering employment, inclusive development, and economic transformation on the continent. It seeks to shift the focus of political parties from electoral mobilization to becoming effective agents of national change. Key discussions will address structural barriers to economic progress and the political dynamics that perpetuate them. Ethiopia's Prosperity Party, the host, will share insights from its successful economic reforms. The summit emphasizes regional integration, encouraging collaboration on economic zones, energy cooperation, and trade connectivity. By engaging political leaders, the private sector, and academia, the summit aims to unlock Africa's potential in various sectors. The event will conclude with the "Addis Ababa Declaration," a roadmap for sustainable governance and accountability, setting a new standard for growth across the continent. (159 words)
