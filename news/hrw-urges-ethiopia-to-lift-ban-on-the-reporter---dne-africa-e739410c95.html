HRW Urges Ethiopia to Lift Ban on The Reporter - DNE Africa

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 18:03:22 GMT

Human Rights Watch (HRW) has called on the Ethiopian government to lift its ban on the independent news outlet, The Reporter. The organization emphasizes that the ban undermines press freedom and restricts the public's access to vital information. The Reporter has been a significant source of news in Ethiopia, providing coverage on various issues, including human rights and government policies. HRW argues that the government's actions are part of a broader trend of suppressing dissent and limiting journalistic freedom in the country. The organization urges the Ethiopian authorities to respect the rights of journalists and uphold the principles of free expression. This appeal comes amid ongoing concerns about the state of media freedom in Ethiopia, especially in light of recent crackdowns on various media outlets. HRW's statement highlights the importance of a free press in promoting transparency and accountability within the government.
