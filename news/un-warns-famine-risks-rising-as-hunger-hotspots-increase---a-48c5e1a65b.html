UN Warns Famine Risks Rising as Hunger Hotspots Increase - ASHARQ AL-AWSAT English

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 16:20:14 GMT

The United Nations has issued a stark warning about the rising risks of famine in 16 global hotspots, particularly in Africa, as hunger levels are expected to worsen over the next six months. The Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) and the World Food Programme (WFP) identified Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, northern Nigeria, and Yemen as the most severely affected regions, driven by conflict, economic challenges, and extreme weather conditions. The number of hunger hotspots has increased from 13 to 16 since June, with Chad, Uganda, and parts of southern Africa and Central America now included. The report highlights a significant decline in humanitarian funding, which has dropped by two-thirds for the most affected countries between 2022 and 2025. The developing El Niño weather pattern is anticipated to exacerbate the situation, potentially causing droughts and flooding. The report emphasizes the urgent need for increased international support to avert a humanitarian catastrophe. (156 words)
