Somalia: Puntland Health Minister Inspects Dhahar Hospital During Regional Tour - Garowe Online

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 04:54:53 GMT

Puntland Health Minister H.E. Mohamad Abdirahman Mohamed conducted an inspection of Dhahar District Hospital and other health facilities in the Haylaan region on Wednesday. Accompanied by State Minister for Health Ali Mohamed Osman, the delegation met with local officials and traditional elders to discuss healthcare services and community needs. The minister emphasized the importance of improving patient care and maintaining service standards. During the visit, hospital administrators briefed the delegation on the facilities' conditions and future plans for enhancing healthcare quality. This tour is part of the Puntland health ministry's initiative to assess healthcare access and strengthen collaboration with local authorities, particularly in rural areas where medical services are often limited.
