India, Somalia discuss strengthening defence cooperation, focus on maritime security and counter-terrorism - Horseed Media

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 17:01:00 GMT

India and Somalia have engaged in discussions to enhance their bilateral defence cooperation, focusing on maritime security, counter-terrorism, and capacity building. This high-level meeting took place in Mogadishu between India's External Affairs Ministry Secretary, Sripriya Ranganathan, and Somali Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi. The talks addressed the evolving regional security landscape and aimed to strengthen defence ties. Ranganathan's visit marks the first high-level Indian delegation to Somalia in recent years, emphasizing the importance of their longstanding relationship, which dates back to Somalia's independence in 1960. Although India closed its diplomatic mission in Mogadishu in 1991 due to civil unrest, it has maintained engagement through development assistance and maritime security initiatives. Ranganathan's agenda includes meetings with senior Somali officials to review existing cooperation and explore new partnership opportunities in various sectors, reinforcing the commitment to enhance collaboration between the two nations.
