Peacekeepers plan to take over bases near Somalia’s capital as Ugandan troops withdraw - Temple Daily Telegram

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 09:12:46 GMT

Peacekeepers are set to take over four military bases near Mogadishu, Somalia, as Ugandan troops withdraw, aiming to prevent al-Shabab militants from exploiting the resulting security vacuum. This transition is part of the African Union mission's strategy to maintain stability in the region. The Ugandan forces have been a significant presence in Somalia, contributing to efforts against the al-Shabab insurgency. The African Union's letter to the U.N. support office in Somalia highlights the urgency of this handover to ensure continued security and support for the Somali government. As the peacekeepers prepare for this transition, concerns remain about the potential resurgence of militant activities in the absence of Ugandan troops. The situation underscores the ongoing challenges faced in stabilizing Somalia amid persistent threats from extremist groups.
