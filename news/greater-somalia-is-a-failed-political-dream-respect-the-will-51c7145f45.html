Greater Somalia Is a Failed Political Dream Respect the Will of the People - Borkena

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 15:26:13 GMT

The article critiques Farah Maalim Mohamed, a veteran Kenyan politician, for his inconsistent and contradictory views on the political situation in the Horn of Africa, particularly regarding Somaliland's statehood. It argues that Maalim's recent comments, suggesting that Somaliland should be forcibly integrated into Somalia, disregard the historical sacrifices made by the people of Somaliland in their quest for independence. The author emphasizes that political experience does not equate to wisdom and that Maalim's rhetoric could lead to further conflict rather than constructive dialogue. The piece calls for a shift away from the failed political dream of Greater Somalia, advocating instead for respect for the will of the people and a commitment to peace and stability in the region. It urges Maalim to reflect on the implications of his statements and to contribute positively to the political future of the Somali people. The article underscores the need for responsible leadership and diplomatic engagement in addressing regional challenges. (158 words)
