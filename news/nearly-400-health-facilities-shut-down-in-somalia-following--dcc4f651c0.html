Nearly 400 health facilities shut down in Somalia following U.S. aid cuts, minister says - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 05:10:49 GMT

Nearly 400 health facilities in Somalia have shut down due to cuts in funding from the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID), according to Health Minister Ali Haji Adan. The closures highlight Somalia's reliance on international donors for essential healthcare services. Many of the affected facilities depended on humanitarian assistance, which has become unsustainable following the withdrawal of U.S. support. Adan warned that the healthcare system remains vulnerable to fluctuations in foreign aid, as a significant portion of community health services is donor-funded. While nearly 600 other health facilities supported by long-term development programs continue to operate, the loss of funding has severely impacted mobile medical teams serving communities with limited access to healthcare. The situation is exacerbated by ongoing humanitarian challenges in Somalia, including drought, food insecurity, and disease outbreaks. The minister did not provide a plan for emergency funding or a timeline for restoring the closed facilities.
