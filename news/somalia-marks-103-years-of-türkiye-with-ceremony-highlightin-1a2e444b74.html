Somalia marks 103 years of Türkiye with ceremony highlighting close ties - Horseed Media

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:24:24 GMT

Somalia celebrated the 103rd anniversary of the Republic of Türkiye with a ceremony in Mogadishu, emphasizing the strong diplomatic ties and cooperation between the two nations. The event, held at the Turkish Embassy on October 29, coincided with Türkiye's Republic Day, marking its establishment in 1923. Abdi Hashi Abdullahi, Somalia’s Upper House Speaker, attended the ceremony and expressed congratulations to the Turkish people, highlighting the friendship and solidarity that underpin their relationship. He recalled Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan's significant visit to Somalia in 2011 during a severe famine, which marked a pivotal moment in Türkiye's engagement with the country. Since then, cooperation has expanded in various sectors, including development, humanitarian aid, infrastructure, education, and security. The anniversary celebration underscored the commitment of both nations to further strengthen their bilateral relations.
