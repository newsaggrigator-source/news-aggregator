The Payment Problem: Can CIPS Shorten the Route Between Bakara Market and Guangzhou? - FTL Somalia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 21:47:25 GMT

Somalia's Minister of Commerce and Industry, Gamal Mohamed Hassan, recently met with Wang Hongbo, Chairman of China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS), to discuss improving payment processes for Somali businesses trading with China. The talks aimed to ease transactions by potentially allowing direct access to the yuan-denominated clearing system, which could significantly reduce costs and streamline payments for Somali traders. Currently, Somali banks face challenges due to limited direct correspondent relationships, causing payments to pass through intermediaries, which adds time and expense. CIPS could provide a more efficient route for transactions, especially as Somalia seeks to enhance its trade relationship with China, its largest import source. However, the implementation of CIPS in Somalia faces hurdles, including the lack of participating banks and necessary regulatory frameworks. As Somalia approaches WTO accession, establishing a robust financial infrastructure is crucial for integrating into global trade, making the CIPS discussions a vital step towards facilitating smoother trade operations.
