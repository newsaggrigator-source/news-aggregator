Somalia oo kamid noqotay dalalka ogolaaday in la joojiyo qisaasta. - Horseed Media

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 04:23:42 GMT

Somalia ayaa ka mid noqotay 133-ka dal ee taageeray qaraar ka dhan ah xukunka dilka, kaas oo 13-kii November 2026 cod aqlabiyad ah ku ansixiyay Guddiga Saddexaad ee Golaha Guud ee Qaramada Midoobay. Qaraarkan, oo leh lambarka A/C.3/81/L.43, ayaa dalalka ugu baaqaya inay joojiyaan fulinta xukunnada dilka, taasoo loo arko inay horseedi karto in mustaqbalka gebi ahaanba la joojiyo ciqaabtaas. Inkastoo Somalia ay taageertay qaraarkan, ma aha in la fahmo in dalka laga mamnuucay fulinta qisaasta, maadaama qaraarka uu weli u baahan yahay in loo gudbiyo Golaha Guud ee Qaramada Midoobay si loo sii tixgeliyo. Dalal badan oo Islaam ah ayaa ka aamusey ama diiday qaraarka, halka dalal sida Mareykanka, Shiinaha, iyo Sacuudi Carabiya ay ka soo horjeedaan.
