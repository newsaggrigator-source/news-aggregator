Prisoner of History, Not Geography

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 17:23:27 GMT

In October 2026, Ethiopia and Eritrea escalated their conflict by addressing the UN Security Council, with Ethiopia accusing Eritrean troops of advancing into Tigray, a claim Eritrea denies. Ethiopia alleges Eritrea is supporting armed groups in the north, while Eritrea accuses Ethiopia of using its landlocked status as a pretext for military action. The article argues that Ethiopia's geographic predicament is rooted in historical and political decisions rather than natural barriers. It emphasizes that Ethiopia's access to the sea has always been contingent on political arrangements, not geography. The author warns against viewing Ethiopia as a "prisoner of geography," as this perspective may lead to militaristic solutions instead of diplomatic ones. The article advocates for negotiations and regional cooperation to secure Ethiopia's economic interests and access to the sea.
