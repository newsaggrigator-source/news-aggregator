Al-Oromia: Jihad Emerging in Ethiopia Outside the International Radar Screen - Borkena

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 18:52:53 GMT

A concerning jihadist movement is emerging in Ethiopia's Oromia Regional State, largely unnoticed by international observers. While attention is focused on threats like al-Shabaab in Somalia, Oromia is experiencing a nascent pulse of jihadist mobilization fueled by political, security, and socioeconomic instability. The Ethiopian government's reliance on religious actors, coupled with a lack of accountability for jihadist violence, has created an environment conducive to radicalization. Ethnic conflicts are increasingly being overshadowed by religious ideologies, with extremist networks exploiting local grievances. The rise of transnational extremist propaganda, particularly from pro-ISIS media targeting Oromo speakers, signals a shift in recruitment strategies. Al-Shabaab and ISIS-Somalia have already established footholds in the region, further complicating the security landscape. The deteriorating political climate and mass poverty in Ethiopia are likely to exacerbate these threats, making it crucial for regional and international stakeholders to recognize and address the emerging jihadist dynamics in Oromia.
