800,000 Somalis threatened by flooding displacement: UN - Arab News

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 21:40:51 GMT

The UN migration agency, IOM, has warned that over 800,000 people in Somalia could be displaced due to impending flooding in the next three months. The forecasted displacement, estimated at 874,319 individuals, is primarily attributed to flooding, which is expected to account for 92% of the displacements. The rainy season, exacerbated by the strengthening El Niño phenomenon, is anticipated to bring above-average rainfall. Manuel Marques Pereira, the IOM’s Somalia chief of mission, highlighted the urgency of the situation, stating that many affected families have already faced previous displacements and have limited resources. The IOM has only secured 14% of the $16.62 million needed for flood preparedness and response. Signs of flooding are already evident, with rivers nearing flood levels and heavy rainfall damaging homes. The agency has called for immediate international support to enhance preparedness and mitigate the impact of the floods on vulnerable communities, which could also threaten crops and access to essential services.
