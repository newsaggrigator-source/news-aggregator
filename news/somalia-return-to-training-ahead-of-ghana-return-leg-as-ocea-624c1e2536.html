Somalia Return to Training Ahead of Ghana Return Leg as Ocean Stars Seek Response - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 01:23:05 GMT

Somalia's national football team, the Ocean Stars, has resumed training in preparation for their return leg against Ghana on November 16, following a 2-0 defeat in the first leg of their AFCON qualifying campaign. The match, taking place at the Essipong Sports Stadium, is crucial for Somalia, who have lost all three of their group matches thus far. Despite the setbacks, including logistical challenges and a disrupted training session, the team aims to respond positively. Somalia's historic presence in the group stage has been celebrated, with President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud recognizing their achievement as a symbol of national unity. Meanwhile, Ghana seeks to build on their recent victory, having appointed interim coach Kim Lars Björkegren after a poor start to the campaign. The match represents a significant opportunity for both teams, with Somalia focusing on pride and development, while Ghana aims to regain momentum in their qualification efforts.
