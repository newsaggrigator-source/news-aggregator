Somalia: Three Strategic Shifts, One Unchanged Political Mindset - Horseed Media

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:50:53 GMT

Somalia is facing significant challenges as its political elite remains entrenched in power struggles, leaving the country vulnerable to insurgent threats and regional interference. Despite Al-Shabaab adapting its tactics and regional powers recalibrating their interests, Somali leaders are preoccupied with clan influence and institutional control. The current administration, led by Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, is accused of undermining the foundational political settlement based on federalism and power-sharing, opting instead for centralization and unilateral decision-making. This approach has eroded trust in political institutions and prompted rivals to seek external support, further complicating the situation. The article emphasizes the need for genuine dialogue and compromise to address political grievances and rebuild confidence among communities. It warns that without a shift in strategy, the convergence of political ambition, insurgent expansion, and regional exploitation could destabilize Somalia further, jeopardizing its sovereignty and future. The responsibility lies with the federal leadership to recognize these dynamics and prioritize national interests over personal ambitions.
