Ethiopia's Transitional Justice Should Resume, but Not Where It Left Off

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 10:50:31 GMT

Ethiopia's transitional justice process is set to resume after a pause since April 2025, with the Ministry of Justice launching a government-civil society platform on October 23, 2026. However, public consultations on transitional justice laws should only commence after considering the recommendations from the National Dialogue, which were submitted to Parliament on October 3. The previous interruption created uncertainty and weakened momentum, necessitating transparency about the reasons for resuming the process. Key priorities include integrating the National Dialogue's recommendations, addressing existing policy gaps, fulfilling outstanding commitments, and adapting to changes in the institutional landscape during the pause. The process must ensure meaningful participation from civil society and vulnerable groups, while independent experts should be involved in drafting laws to enhance credibility. Overall, the focus should be on recalibrating the transitional justice framework rather than simply picking up where it left off, aiming for a more inclusive and effective approach. (159 words)
