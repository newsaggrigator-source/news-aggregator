Signs of Peace Abound in Mogadishu - Africa Defense Forum

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 17:27:57 GMT

In Mogadishu, signs of peace are emerging as daily life gradually normalizes amid the Somali government's ongoing battle against the al-Qaida-affiliated group al-Shabaab. Senior officials, including Defense Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi and Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre, have noted a significant reduction in security threats, allowing them to travel freely among citizens without heavy security measures. This shift is reflected in the resurgence of public events, such as sporting activities and cultural gatherings, which were previously hindered by violence. Despite these improvements, al-Shabaab remains a persistent threat, recently attacking Somali National Army forces and claiming significant casualties. The Somali government is addressing this challenge not only through military means but also by engaging youth and promoting a cohesive Islamic education framework. Efforts include establishing a Somali Scholars Council to counter extremist interpretations of Islam and targeting online platforms that spread terrorist propaganda. Overall, security efforts have reportedly led to an 87% decrease in attacks in Mogadishu.
