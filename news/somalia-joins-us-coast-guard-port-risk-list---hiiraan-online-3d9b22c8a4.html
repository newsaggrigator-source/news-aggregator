Somalia Joins US Coast Guard Port Risk List - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 14:08:45 GMT

The United States Coast Guard has officially added Somalia to its Port Risk List, indicating that the country does not maintain effective counterterrorism security measures at its ports. This designation, announced in the U.S. Federal Register on October 27, 2026, follows an assessment of Somalia's port security arrangements. Consequently, vessels arriving from Somali ports will now face additional security requirements when entering U.S. waters. Somalia joins a list of 24 countries, including Iran, Iraq, and North Korea, identified as lacking adequate port security measures. The decision, signed by Admiral Nathan A. Moore, the Coast Guard's Deputy Commandant for Operations, took effect immediately. While the notice outlines the new designation, it does not specify the exact security procedures that vessels will need to follow. This move reflects ongoing concerns about maritime security and the potential risks posed by inadequate counterterrorism measures in certain regions.
