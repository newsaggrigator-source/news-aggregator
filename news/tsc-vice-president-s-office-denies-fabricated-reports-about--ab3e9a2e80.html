TSC Vice-President's Office Denies Fabricated Reports About His Welcoming Ethiopian Initiative to End Sudan War

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 11:28:08 GMT

The Office of the Vice-President of Sudan's Transitional Sovereignty Council (TSC) has firmly denied recent media reports claiming that General Malik Agar endorsed Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's initiative to resolve the ongoing war in Sudan. In a press statement, the TSC emphasized that these reports were completely false and that the Vice-President had not made any comments regarding the initiative. The Office condemned the dissemination of misleading information and the attribution of fabricated statements to its leadership. It urged media outlets to adhere to professional standards by verifying information and relying on official sources. Furthermore, the TSC called on those who published the inaccurate reports to retract and correct them, while reserving the right to take legal action against the spread of false information. This statement highlights the importance of responsible journalism in the context of the sensitive political situation in Sudan.
