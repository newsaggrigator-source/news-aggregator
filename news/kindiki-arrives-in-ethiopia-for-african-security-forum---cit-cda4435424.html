Kindiki arrives in Ethiopia for African security forum - Citizen Digital

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 09:06:17 GMT

Kenya's Deputy President Kithure Kindiki arrived in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, on October 24, 2026, to participate in the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa. He was welcomed at the Bahir Dar International Airport by Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. The forum, taking place from October 23 to 25, gathers African leaders, policymakers, and stakeholders to address the continent's peace and security issues under the theme “Pathways to Peace in Africa: Economic Sovereignty and Geopolitical Transformation.” Key discussions will focus on how Africa can leverage its resource management, economic institutions, and regional integration to foster lasting peace. The agenda includes topics such as resource wealth, monetary sovereignty for industrial development, macroeconomic governance, and the effects of illicit economies on regional stability. Approximately 250 participants are expected, including current and former heads of state, diplomats, academics, civil society representatives, and private sector leaders. The Tana Forum, established in 2012, serves as a platform for African-led security solutions.
