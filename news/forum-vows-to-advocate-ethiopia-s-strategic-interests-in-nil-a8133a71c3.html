Forum Vows to Advocate Ethiopia's Strategic Interests in Nile, Red Sea

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 05:20:23 GMT

The newly formed Nile and Red Sea Strategic Forum in Ethiopia aims to advocate for the country's interests in these crucial waterways. Chief Executive Officer Solomon Tefera announced the forum's establishment as a legally recognized entity focused on enhancing Ethiopia's strategic position in the Nile and Red Sea. The forum plans to engage civil society, scholars, and the public to bolster Ethiopia's economic development, regional stability, and geopolitical standing. It will conduct research, organize conferences, and promote public awareness regarding Ethiopia's rights and benefits related to these waters. The forum has outlined 12 objectives, emphasizing the importance of maritime access and regional cooperation. By fostering diplomatic engagement and strategic partnerships, the forum seeks to address gaps in advocacy and research, ensuring that Ethiopia's interests are well-represented and understood both nationally and internationally. This initiative reflects Ethiopia's commitment to safeguarding its rights and enhancing its role in regional affairs. (159 words)
