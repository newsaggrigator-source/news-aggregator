Bitcoin drops below $56,000: Why a stronger U.S. dollar matters to Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 04:35:38 GMT

Bitcoin recently fell below $56,000, marking its lowest point in over two years, primarily due to a stronger U.S. dollar and uncertainties surrounding American interest rates. This decline left Bitcoin over 50% lower than its peak in October 2025. While the cryptocurrency's volatility impacts global markets, it has minimal direct effects on everyday spending in Somalia, where commerce relies more on U.S. dollars transferred via mobile money services. The strengthening dollar increases remittance costs for Somali workers earning in weaker currencies, which can strain families dependent on these funds. In 2025, remittances accounted for about 24% of Somalia's GDP, highlighting their significance for household consumption. Although Bitcoin is viewed as a speculative asset, stablecoins linked to the dollar are used by some Somali traders for international transactions. However, traditional remittance methods remain more relevant for most households, as they navigate the challenges posed by inflation and rising living costs.
