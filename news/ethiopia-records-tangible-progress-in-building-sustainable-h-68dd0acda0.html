Ethiopia Records Tangible Progress in Building Sustainable, Healthy Food System

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 04:54:31 GMT

Ethiopia is making significant strides in transforming its agricultural sector to ensure sustainable and healthy food systems, as highlighted during the 2026 World Food Day celebrations in Addis Ababa. The event, themed "Innovate Today. Nourish Tomorrow," was organized by the Ministry of Agriculture, FAO, and WFP, and attended by various stakeholders, including government officials and farmers. Mandefro Nigussie, Director General of the Ethiopian Agricultural Transformation Institute (ATI), emphasized the sector's vital role in the national economy and the importance of technology transfer, irrigation expansion, and agricultural mechanization. Investments are being directed towards enhancing market systems and fostering a sustainable rural economy, integrating indigenous knowledge for climate-smart practices. FAO's Workicho Jateno and WFP's Zlatan Milišić praised Ethiopia's efforts in food security and economic growth, underscoring the need for continued collaboration among government, researchers, and development partners to sustain progress and leverage the country's resources effectively. This collective effort aims to secure a resilient food system for the future.
