Japan Extends SDF Antipiracy Mission off Somalia for 1 Year - Nippon.com

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 06:10:26 GMT

Japan has officially extended the Self-Defense Forces' (SDF) antipiracy mission in the Gulf of Aden off Somalia for an additional year, lasting until November 19, 2027. This decision, announced on November 6, 2026, also includes the continuation of the SDF's intelligence-gathering operations aimed at ensuring safe maritime navigation in the Middle East. Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi emphasized that the underlying issues contributing to piracy remain unresolved, necessitating ongoing efforts to enhance maritime security. Additionally, the deployment of SDF personnel to the Multinational Force and Observers in Egypt's Sinai Peninsula will be extended until the end of November 2027. This move reflects Japan's commitment to maintaining regional stability and security in response to persistent threats in maritime areas.
