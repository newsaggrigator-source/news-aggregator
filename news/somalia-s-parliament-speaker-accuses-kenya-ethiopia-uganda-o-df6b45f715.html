Somalia's Parliament Speaker Accuses Kenya, Ethiopia, Uganda of Colluding With Al-Shabaab

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 10:18:15 GMT

Somalia's Parliament Speaker, Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur, has accused Kenya, Ethiopia, and Uganda of colluding with the militant group Al-Shabaab. During a conference in Mogadishu on November 24, he claimed that these countries, along with forces loyal to former South West State leader Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen, were part of a coordinated effort against Somalia's federal government, allegedly orchestrated by Israel. Nur warned of foreign powers seeking to recolonize Somalia and called for national sovereignty defense. His allegations, which lack substantiation, could further strain relations with regional partners involved in Somalia's security. The accusations come amid ongoing conflict in South West State, where federal forces recently ousted Laftagareen but faced continued attacks from his aligned forces. Additionally, tensions between Somalia and the UAE have escalated, with Somalia accusing Abu Dhabi of interference in its internal affairs. Nur emphasized the need for economic self-reliance and improved security to attract investment and promote development. (159 words)
