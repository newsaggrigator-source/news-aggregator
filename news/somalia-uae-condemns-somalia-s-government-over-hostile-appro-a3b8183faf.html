Somalia: UAE Condemns Somalia's Government Over 'Hostile Approach' - allAfrica.com

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 09:29:56 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has expressed strong disapproval of the Somali government's recent actions, labeling them as a "hostile approach." This condemnation follows the Somali government's decision to suspend diplomatic relations with the UAE, which has been a significant partner in Somalia's development and security efforts. The UAE criticized the Somali administration for its lack of cooperation and transparency, emphasizing that such measures could jeopardize the progress made in bilateral relations. The UAE has been involved in various initiatives in Somalia, including humanitarian aid and military training. The ongoing tensions between the two nations have raised concerns about the potential impact on stability and development in Somalia, as well as the broader implications for regional security. The UAE's statement reflects its commitment to fostering positive relations and highlights the importance of dialogue in resolving diplomatic disputes. Both countries are urged to engage in constructive discussions to address their differences and restore cooperation. (151 words)
