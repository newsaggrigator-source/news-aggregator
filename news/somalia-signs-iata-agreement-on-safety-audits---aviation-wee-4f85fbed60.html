Somalia Signs IATA Agreement On Safety Audits - Aviation Week

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 16:46:04 GMT

Somalia has officially signed an agreement with the International Air Transport Association (IATA) to enhance aviation safety through safety audits. This agreement aims to improve the country's aviation standards and align them with international practices. By participating in IATA's safety audit program, Somalia seeks to bolster its aviation infrastructure and ensure compliance with global safety regulations. The initiative is part of broader efforts to revitalize Somalia's aviation sector, which has faced challenges in recent years. The agreement is expected to facilitate better training for aviation personnel and improve overall safety measures within the country's air transport system.
