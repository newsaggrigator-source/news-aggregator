UN mission prepares to exit Somalia after over three-decade support - TRT Afrika

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 19:32:46 GMT

The United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) is set to conclude its operations on October 31, marking the end of over three decades of UN support for peacebuilding and governance in Somalia. This decision follows a request from the Somali government and aligns with UN Security Council resolutions. UN spokesperson Stephane Dujarric emphasized that this transition reflects a roadmap agreed upon to transfer responsibilities to Somali institutions and various UN agencies. Despite the mission's closure, UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres assured continued support for Somalia through resident agencies, focusing on promoting peace, stability, and human rights. He called for sustained international assistance to address ongoing political, security, and humanitarian challenges, while reinforcing the UN's commitment to aiding Somali-led efforts for a peaceful and prosperous future. The mission's exit signifies a significant milestone in Somalia's journey towards self-governance and stability.
