NIRA Registers Somali Returnees from Yemen, Linking National ID to Reintegration - FTL Somalia

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 09:41:57 GMT

Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) has begun registering Somali returnees from Yemen, issuing them National ID cards to aid their reintegration into society. This initiative, conducted in collaboration with the National Commission for Refugees and Internally Displaced Persons (NCRI), aims to provide returnees with access to essential services such as banking, education, and healthcare. The registration follows the repatriation of 160 Somali nationals from Yemen, many of whom faced dire living conditions. NIRA's Deputy Director emphasized the importance of legal identity for social reintegration. However, linking access to services with possession of a National ID raises concerns, particularly for those lacking documentation. While mobile registration efforts have been made, challenges remain for the most vulnerable returnees. The success of this initiative will be crucial in determining the effectiveness of Somalia's national ID system in facilitating reintegration.
