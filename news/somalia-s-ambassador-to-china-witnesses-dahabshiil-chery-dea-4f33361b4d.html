Somalia’s Ambassador to China Witnesses Dahabshiil-Chery Deal, Urges Local Assembly Plants - FTL Somalia

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 16:42:18 GMT

Somalia's Ambassador to China, Abukar Osman, attended the signing of a significant deal between Dahabshiil, a prominent Somali money transfer company, and Chery Automobile, a leading Chinese car manufacturer. The agreement aims to enhance trade relations and promote investment in Somalia's automotive sector. During the event, Ambassador Osman emphasized the importance of establishing local assembly plants in Somalia to boost the economy and create job opportunities. He highlighted that such initiatives could lead to technological transfer and skill development within the country. The collaboration between Dahabshiil and Chery is seen as a step towards fostering economic growth and improving the overall business environment in Somalia.
