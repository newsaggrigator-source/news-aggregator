FROM THE ARCHIVES: “Once There Is Corruption, There Is Insecurity” – Kiir’s 2014 Address BAHIR DAR, ETHIOPIA – APRIL 2014 - Eye Radio

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 15:52:20 GMT

In April 2014, South Sudan's President Salva Kiir addressed the Third Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, emphasizing the detrimental effects of corruption on security and economic stability in Africa. Kiir highlighted the illegal movement of public funds as a significant issue, asserting that these illicit financial flows often exceed foreign investments. He linked corruption to the political and security crises in South Sudan, suggesting that individuals who amass wealth through corrupt practices seek political power to protect their interests. Kiir expressed frustration over the disappearance of government revenues and the challenges in recovering misappropriated funds. He called for African nations to take responsibility for addressing corruption and insecurity, advocating for homegrown solutions rather than relying on external assistance. Kiir concluded with a powerful statement: “Once there is corruption, there is insecurity,” underscoring the interconnectedness of these issues.
