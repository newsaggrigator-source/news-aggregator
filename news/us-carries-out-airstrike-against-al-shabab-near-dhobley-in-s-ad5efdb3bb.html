US carries out airstrike against al-Shabab near Dhobley in southern Somalia - Horseed Media

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 18:21:37 GMT

The United States conducted an airstrike against the al-Shabab militant group near Dhobley in southern Somalia. This operation targeted a specific location where al-Shabab was believed to be operating. The airstrike is part of ongoing efforts by the U.S. to combat terrorism in the region and support Somali forces in their fight against the extremist group. The U.S. military has previously carried out similar strikes to disrupt al-Shabab's activities and reduce their influence in Somalia. Further details regarding the impact of the airstrike and any casualties have not been disclosed.
