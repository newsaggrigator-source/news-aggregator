Somalia: Hassan Sheikh Says Israel, Taiwan Threaten Country’s Sovereignty - Garowe Online

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 06:16:26 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has expressed concerns that Israel and Taiwan threaten Somalia's sovereignty. During a ceremony for the construction of the National Defence University in Mogadishu, he stated that both nations pose risks to Somalia's territorial integrity, particularly in light of Israel's growing ties with Somaliland, a breakaway region that Somalia claims as its own. Mohamud emphasized that Taiwan's engagement with Somaliland also undermines Somalia's sovereignty. He highlighted China's support for Somalia's territorial claims, noting that China is financing the university project, which will include a hospital for the armed forces. Additionally, the president criticized opposition leaders for seeking foreign solutions to domestic political disputes, urging them to engage in dialogue instead. His remarks reflect the complex interplay between Somalia's internal political challenges and its foreign policy, particularly regarding relationships with nations that interact with Somaliland.
