Ethiopia's Russian Su-35 Deal Raises Questions Over Egypt's National Security - Sada Elbalad English

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 21:30:00 GMT

Ethiopia's recent acquisition of 12 Russian Su-35 fighter jets and four S-400 air defense systems has raised concerns regarding Egypt's national security. Following Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's announcement, the director of the Russia Federal News Network (RFN) sought to alleviate fears, asserting that Ethiopia's military enhancements should not be viewed as a direct threat to Egypt. He emphasized that possessing advanced aircraft does not equate to the capability for effective long-range military operations, which depend on various factors such as pilot training and logistical support. The RFN director also highlighted that the S-400 system is primarily defensive, aimed at intercepting aerial threats rather than conducting offensive strikes. Additionally, he noted the importance of distinguishing legitimate security concerns from unfounded fears. The discussion comes amid ongoing tensions between Egypt and Ethiopia over Nile water security and follows recent talks between Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi and Russian President Vladimir Putin regarding regional stability.
