Turkish warships to escort fishing vessel in Somalia's EEZ waters - Baird Maritime

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 08:29:12 GMT

The Turkish Ministry of National Defence has announced that two Turkish Navy frigates, TCG Gökçeada and TCG Yıldırım, are heading to Mogadishu to escort the fishing vessel Mustafa Korkmaz A during its exploratory fishing activities in Somalia's exclusive economic zone (EEZ). This operation is part of a broader initiative to rotate the Somali Naval Task Group. The collaboration between Turkey and Somalia is underpinned by a Defence and Economic Cooperation Framework Agreement signed in February 2024, aimed at safeguarding Somalia's maritime jurisdiction, combating piracy, and enhancing the country's naval capabilities. Additionally, Turkey's Grand National Assembly has extended the deployment of Turkish forces in Somalia for two years, focusing on protecting Somali economic resources. The Turkish group OYAK has also established SOMTURK in Somalia to assist in managing international fishing licenses, aligning with Somalia's 2023 fisheries law that regulates fishing activities within its waters.
