Ethiopia Intensifies Efforts to Expand Earning From Pulses and Oilseeds, Sector Contributes $572.9 Million

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 05:24:26 GMT

Ethiopia is enhancing its efforts to increase earnings from pulses and oilseeds, which contributed $572.9 million to the country's export revenue in the 2025/26 fiscal year. Trade and Regional Integration Minister Yasmin Wohabrebbi highlighted the sector's potential during the 15th International Conference on Pulses and Oilseeds (ICOPOS), emphasizing the importance of economic reforms, investment promotion, and improved product quality. Ethiopia aims to become a reliable source of high-quality pulses and oilseeds in global markets, leveraging opportunities from the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) and its ongoing accession to the World Trade Organization. Edao Abdi, President of the Ethiopian Pulses, Oilseeds and Spices Processors-Exporters Association, noted that while the sector's performance is promising, there is significant room for growth. The conference attracted over 600 participants from more than 20 countries, recognizing six Ethiopian exporters for their achievements in the pulses and oilseeds categories.
