Stationing of Ethiopian Peacekeepers over 7 Decades Reflects Commitment to Collective Security: IPSTI - ENA English

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 22:46:05 GMT

Ethiopia's deployment of peacekeepers over the past seven decades underscores its commitment to collective security and international cooperation, according to Brigadier General Gezahegn Fekadu, Commandant of the Ethiopian National Defense Force International Peace Support Training Institute (IPSTI). This engagement began during the Korean War and has included missions in Sudan, South Sudan, and Somalia. At a recent forum, Gezahegn highlighted challenges such as declining funding for peace operations and political disagreements among multilateral institutions, which affect Ethiopia's role as a major troop contributor. The forum also addressed Ethiopia's historical contributions, operational readiness, and the need for modernization and sustainable partnerships. Additionally, the Ethiopian National Defense College representative noted the country's humanitarian efforts, hosting numerous refugees despite economic pressures. French Defense Attaché Colonel Damien Cypryk emphasized the evolving complexities of peacekeeping, including cyber threats and funding constraints, which necessitate adaptive strategies for effective international peace and security operations.
