Ethiopia’s Dams and Water Projects Bring Shared Benefits to Downstream Countries, Not Threats to Their Water Security: Minister - Fana Media Corporation S.C

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 18:15:40 GMT

Ethiopia's Minister of Water and Energy, Engineer Habtamu Itefa, emphasized that the country's dams and water projects are intended to foster shared prosperity for downstream nations, rather than pose threats to their water security. In an interview with Fana Media Corporation, he urged Egypt to support Ethiopia's initiatives to harness its water resources. The Minister highlighted that Ethiopia's water infrastructure benefits regional interests by regulating river flows, enhancing irrigation, generating electricity, and promoting economic cooperation among countries sharing transboundary rivers. He pointed out that the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD) has already provided advantages to downstream countries like Sudan by improving river flow management and reducing flooding risks. Engineer Habtamu reiterated that Ethiopia aims to transform its water resources into a foundation for development and regional collaboration, advocating for mutual recognition of development rights among basin countries to ensure that shared resources benefit all.
