Guterres Calls On Fossil Fuel Companies to Pay for Climate Damage

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:32:20 GMT

At the UN Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Türkiye, UN Secretary-General António Guterres urged governments to impose taxes on the windfall profits of major fossil fuel companies, directing the revenue towards climate recovery efforts. He emphasized that those responsible for environmental damage must contribute to its remediation, highlighting the stark contrast between the substantial profits of oil companies and the limited financial resources available to developing nations facing climate-related challenges. Guterres noted that nine major oil companies earned $95 billion in profits in just one quarter, while the global Loss and Damage Fund has only secured about $600 million. He called for at least $300 billion annually to support vulnerable countries and for global climate finance to reach $1.3 trillion by 2035. Guterres also advocated for innovative financing mechanisms and stronger cooperation to enhance access to technology and capital for developing nations.
