Ethiopia Steps Up Water Resource Mapping Amid Growing Demand

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 05:07:12 GMT

Ethiopia is intensifying its efforts to map and manage its water resources in response to increasing demand driven by population growth, industrialization, and agricultural expansion. This initiative was highlighted during the 3rd Ethiopian Water and Energy Week, particularly on Water Day, which focused on sustainable resource management and progress towards global Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Senior expert Yoseph Abebe from the Ministry of Water and Energy (MoWE) emphasized the need for improved assessment and conservation of water resources, despite Ethiopia's reputation as a water-abundant country. The event also addressed the importance of watershed conservation and showcased advancements in drinking water supply and sanitation over the past decade. Discussions included enhancing infrastructure, planning, and private-sector participation in the water sector. The Green Legacy Initiative was noted for its positive impact on groundwater recharge and reducing soil erosion, indicating progress in the sustainable management of Ethiopia's water resources.
