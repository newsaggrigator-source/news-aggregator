Regional forces clash with federal troops in southern Somalia - Arab News

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 16:22:58 GMT

In southern Somalia, regional forces loyal to ousted leader Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen clashed with federal troops near Baidoa city. This conflict follows President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's deployment of federal troops in March to remove Laftagareen from power. Despite previous attempts to regain control, including a significant assault in September that resulted in casualties, Laftagareen's forces continue to challenge the federal government. On Monday, fighting intensified as Somali security forces established new defensive positions in Burjeedka, with both sides claiming to have inflicted losses on the other. Witnesses reported heavy gunfire and explosions, indicating a fierce confrontation. The situation remains fluid, with the full extent of casualties yet to be determined. The ongoing clashes highlight the deep fragmentation and instability within Somalia, as various regions resist central government authority.
