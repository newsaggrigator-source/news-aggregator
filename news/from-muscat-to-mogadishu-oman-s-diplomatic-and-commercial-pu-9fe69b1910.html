From Muscat to Mogadishu: Oman’s Diplomatic and Commercial Push in Somalia - FTL Somalia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 17:07:07 GMT

Oman is enhancing its diplomatic and commercial ties with Somalia, as evidenced by a recent meeting between Somali Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre and Oman's Ambassador Salim bin Habib Al-Amri. This engagement aims to strengthen historical relations and expand cooperation in trade, transport, and economic sectors, particularly focusing on maritime corridors to facilitate goods movement. Since reopening its embassy in Mogadishu in October 2026 after a 36-year absence, Oman has expressed interest in Somalia's Al-Ula Special Economic Zone and various sectors, including energy and logistics. Despite a modest increase in bilateral trade, reaching approximately $538 million in 2025, the relationship remains underdeveloped compared to its potential. Oman’s support for Somalia's sovereignty is crucial, especially amid regional challenges. The success of this partnership will depend on translating diplomatic engagements into tangible investments and job creation, particularly in fisheries and livestock, which are vital for Somalia's economy.
