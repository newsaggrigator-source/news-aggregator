Ghana vs Somalia: Africa Cup of Nations Qualification stats & head-to-head - BBC

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 15:26:59 GMT

On November 12, 2026, Ghana secured a 2-0 victory over Somalia in the Africa Cup of Nations qualification match held at the Sekondi-Takoradi Stadium. The match saw Ghana take the lead just before halftime with a goal from J. Adjetey in the second minute of stoppage time, assisted by M. Salisu. In the second half, F. Afena-Gyan extended Ghana's lead with a goal in the 62nd minute, assisted by M. Kudus. This win marked Ghana's first victory in the qualification campaign, bringing their points total to three, while Somalia remained winless with zero points after three matches. The match statistics highlighted Ghana's dominance, as they controlled the game and effectively utilized their scoring opportunities. The result places Ghana in a challenging position in Group C, while Somalia continues to struggle in their quest for qualification.
