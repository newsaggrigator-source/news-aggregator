News: Kenya engages Ethiopia to end ongoing Moyale conflict - Addis Standard

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 07:22:46 GMT

Kenya is actively engaging with Ethiopia to address the ongoing conflict in Moyale, a border town that has been experiencing violence and unrest. The clashes, primarily between the Borana and Garre communities, have resulted in numerous casualties and significant displacement of residents. Kenyan authorities are seeking to foster dialogue and cooperation with Ethiopian officials to restore peace and stability in the region. Both governments recognize the need for a collaborative approach to resolve the underlying issues fueling the conflict. Efforts include joint security operations and community engagement initiatives aimed at promoting reconciliation among the affected groups. The situation remains tense, and both nations are committed to finding a sustainable solution to prevent further violence and ensure the safety of civilians in Moyale and surrounding areas. The international community is also being urged to support peacebuilding efforts in this volatile region.
