African Union Rejects Foreign Interference in Somalia Amid Rift With UAE - Garowe Online

Sat, 28 Nov 2026 09:39:49 GMT

The African Union (AU) has firmly rejected any foreign interference in Somalia, particularly amid escalating tensions between Mogadishu and the United Arab Emirates (UAE). In a recent statement, the AU condemned actions that threaten Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity, reaffirming its support for the Somali government in achieving peace and stability. Somalia has declared it will not permit its territory, ports, or airspace to be used for conflicts in neighboring regions. This stance follows a diplomatic rift with the UAE, which Somalia accuses of meddling in its internal affairs. The situation intensified when Somalia expelled the UAE ambassador and recalled its envoy from Abu Dhabi, further straining relations. The AU also endorsed a transition plan for reducing its peacekeeping presence in Somalia, emphasizing the need for respect for the country's sovereignty. The ongoing dispute highlights the complexities of foreign involvement in Somalia's political landscape.
