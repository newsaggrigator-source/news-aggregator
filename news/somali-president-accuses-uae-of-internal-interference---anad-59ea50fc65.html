Somali president accuses UAE of internal interference - Anadolu Ajansı

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 17:33:57 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has accused the United Arab Emirates (UAE) of significant interference in Somalia's internal affairs, claiming that the UAE has established military bases in the country without the federal government's approval. In a televised address, Mohamud described this interference as a blatant violation of Somalia's sovereignty, highlighting that the UAE has signed port deals in Berbera, Bosaso, and Kismayo without authorization. He also alleged that the UAE has funded political divisions and facilitated the movement of a fugitive militia leader from Yemen without consent. Earlier this year, Somalia annulled all agreements with the UAE and closed its airspace to Emirati military and cargo aircraft. Relations between the two nations have been strained, particularly since the UAE condemned Somalia's seizure of $9.6 million from an Emirati plane in 2018, which was intended to support the Somali army. Mohamud expressed his government's willingness to engage in relations based on equality and respect. (158 words)
