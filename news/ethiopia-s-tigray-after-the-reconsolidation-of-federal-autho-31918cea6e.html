Ethiopia’s Tigray After the Reconsolidation of Federal Authority - horn review

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 14:22:36 GMT

Ethiopia's Tigray region is experiencing a significant shift in governance following the federal government's reconsolidation of authority. Field Marshal Birhanu Jula's recent visit to Mekelle and subsequent discussions with international representatives highlight the federal government's regained confidence after recovering key urban centers. The caretaker administration is tasked with restoring security, essential services, and facilitating the return of displaced populations, although challenges remain due to active Tigray Defense Forces (TDF) and ongoing clashes. The TPLF's attempts to restore its pre-war political dominance have undermined the transitional framework established by the November 2022 Pretoria Agreement, leading to further instability. The federal government's control over Mekelle and major towns marks a crucial step towards re-establishing civilian governance and humanitarian access. However, the long-term success of this effort hinges on effective administration, inclusive governance, and the ability to address the underlying tensions exacerbated by Eritrea's strategic interests in the region.
