Libya and Somalia Discuss Economic and Trade Cooperation - Libya Herald

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 22:10:00 GMT

Libya and Somalia have engaged in discussions to enhance economic and trade cooperation between the two nations. The talks focused on potential partnerships in various sectors, aiming to boost bilateral trade and investment. Both countries expressed a commitment to strengthening their economic ties and exploring opportunities for collaboration. The discussions are part of broader efforts to foster regional cooperation and development. Officials from both sides highlighted the importance of sharing resources and expertise to achieve mutual benefits. The meetings reflect a growing interest in establishing stronger economic relationships within the region.
