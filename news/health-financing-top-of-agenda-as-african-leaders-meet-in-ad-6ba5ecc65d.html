Health Financing Top of Agenda As African Leaders Meet in Addis

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 15:25:34 GMT

African leaders and health experts convened in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, on November 23, 2026, for the fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026). The conference aims to address the continent's reliance on foreign health financing and imported medical supplies, emphasizing the need for increased domestic investment in healthcare. With over 30,000 participants, including heads of state, health ministers, and development partners, discussions will focus on developing long-term health investment plans and improving government oversight. The agenda also includes expanding local pharmaceutical manufacturing, enhancing regulatory systems, and increasing research investment. Notably, only three African nations have met the Abuja Declaration target of allocating 15% of their national budgets to health. The conference will also explore pandemic preparedness, digital health, and climate change's impact on health security, culminating in the launch of several continental health initiatives aimed at improving health outcomes across Africa.
