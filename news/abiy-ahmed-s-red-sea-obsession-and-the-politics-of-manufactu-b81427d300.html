Abiy Ahmed’s Red Sea Obsession and the Politics of Manufactured Grievances - Democracy in Africa

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 10:22:21 GMT

Abiy Ahmed's recent focus on Ethiopia's territorial ambitions in the Red Sea has raised concerns about the potential for renewed conflict with Eritrea. This political campaign, which frames Ethiopia's maritime aspirations as a matter of national survival, has gained traction among various political and intellectual circles. However, critics argue that this narrative is an attempt to manufacture grievances where none exist, particularly regarding the historical context of Eritrea's independence in 1993. The Ethiopian government is accused of diverting attention from pressing internal issues, such as economic hardship and political instability, by promoting a narrative of loss and injustice related to Assab. This perspective overlooks the sacrifices made by Eritreans during their struggle for independence and risks perpetuating a cycle of conflict. The article emphasizes the importance of respecting established international boundaries to maintain stability in the region and warns against the dangers of reviving historical territorial claims.
