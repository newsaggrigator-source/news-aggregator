Somalia expresses interest in Azerbaijan’s tourism expertise - azərtac

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:46:00 GMT

On October 29, 2026, Fuad Naghiyev, Chairman of Azerbaijan’s State Tourism Agency, met with Abdulfatah Kasim Mohamud, Somalia’s Minister of Information, Culture, and Tourism, to discuss tourism collaboration. The Somali minister expressed a keen interest in leveraging Azerbaijan's tourism development expertise, particularly in areas such as infrastructure enhancement, destination promotion, investment attraction, and the creation of competitive tourism products. The discussions highlighted Somalia's desire to adopt best practices from Azerbaijan to bolster its own tourism sector. Both parties explored potential joint projects and strategies to strengthen bilateral cooperation in tourism, indicating a mutual commitment to enhancing tourism ties between the two nations. This meeting reflects Somalia's proactive approach to developing its tourism industry by learning from successful models like Azerbaijan’s.
