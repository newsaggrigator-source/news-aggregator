Somali Supreme Court Overrules Federal Government; Saf Advances West On Central Sudan Highway: Africa File, November 12, 2026 - Critical Threats

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 22:10:52 GMT

The Somali Supreme Court has overruled President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term extension, mandating federal elections within 90 days. This ruling intensifies the political standoff between the Federal Government of Somalia (FGS) and the national opposition, particularly the Somali Future Council (SFC), which has mobilized against the FGS since the controversial constitution was approved in March 2026. The FGS has faced armed uprisings in various states, notably from the influential Hawadle clan in Hirshabelle, which has seized key facilities and rejected offers to ease tensions. Meanwhile, the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) have made significant advances along the central highway, attempting to expel the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) from North and South Kordofan. The SAF's recent gains could facilitate further operations into RSF-controlled areas, while the ongoing conflict in Sudan continues to evolve with both sides vying for strategic control. The political instability in Somalia poses risks for security, particularly with al Shabaab potentially exploiting the situation. (157 words)
