Ghana v Somalia: Essipong to host qualifier on Nov 12 - 3News

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 22:01:56 GMT

Ghana will face Somalia in an AFCON qualifier at the Essipong Sports Stadium in Sekondi on November 12, following temporary approval from CAF. The match is scheduled to kick off at 3 PM GMT. The return leg, where Somalia is the home side, will also take place in Ghana on November 16, as Somalia lacks a CAF-approved stadium. Ghana currently sits at the bottom of Group C, having lost both of its previous matches, and needs two victories to improve its standing. While the Essipong Stadium has undergone some renovations, it still requires further upgrades to meet CAF's full licensing standards, including improvements to dressing rooms, pitch drainage, and media facilities. The GFA has opened media accreditation for the match, with a deadline for applications set for November 9. The interim technical team will select the squad for these crucial games, with calls for the inclusion of local players.
