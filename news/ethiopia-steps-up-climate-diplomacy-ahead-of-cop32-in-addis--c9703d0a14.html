Ethiopia Steps Up Climate Diplomacy Ahead of COP32 in Addis Ababa

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 05:20:58 GMT

Ethiopia is enhancing its climate diplomacy as it prepares to host the 32nd United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP32) in Addis Ababa in November 2027. Foreign Minister Gedion Timothewos engaged with international climate stakeholders during COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, to foster strategic partnerships and outline priorities for the upcoming summit. Key discussions included leveraging the United Nations Environment Programme's (UNEP) reports to address the financial resources needed for climate adaptation and expanding renewable energy across Africa. UNEP's Executive Director, Inger Andersen, expressed support for Ethiopia's COP32 presidency. Additionally, Gedion met with Mukhtar Babayev from Azerbaijan, who offered insights from Azerbaijan's COP29 experience, and Manuel Pulgar-Vidal from WWF International, who affirmed WWF's readiness to collaborate. The conference aims to advance international cooperation on climate change mitigation, adaptation, and finance, marking a significant return of global climate negotiations to Africa.
