Veterans Voices: Col. R. Bancroft McKittrick shares personal recollection of Operation Restore Hope in Somalia - Observer Local News

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 15:00:00 GMT

Col. R. Bancroft McKittrick reflects on his experiences during Operation Restore Hope in Somalia, a humanitarian mission initiated in 1992 to address famine and civil unrest. He recounts the challenges faced by U.S. forces, including the complexities of working alongside various international organizations and local factions. McKittrick emphasizes the importance of collaboration and the impact of military intervention in providing relief to the Somali people. He shares personal anecdotes that highlight the bravery of service members and the resilience of the Somali population during this tumultuous period. The operation ultimately aimed to stabilize the region and deliver essential aid, leaving a lasting impression on those involved.
