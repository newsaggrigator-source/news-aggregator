Ethiopia Expels Sudanese Diplomats Amid Regional Tensions - kulu-media.com

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 12:23:44 GMT

Ethiopia has expelled six Sudanese diplomats amid escalating tensions between the two countries, particularly concerning allegations involving Sudan's paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and renewed fighting in northern Ethiopia. The Ethiopian government ordered the diplomats, stationed at the Sudanese consulate in Assosa, to leave within 48 hours, although no specific reason was provided for the expulsion. This diplomatic rift comes as both nations accuse each other of involvement in armed conflicts affecting the Horn of Africa. Sudan has claimed that Ethiopia allows the RSF to launch drone attacks from its territory, while Ethiopia denies these allegations. The situation is further complicated by ongoing conflicts in Sudan and northern Ethiopia, raising concerns about regional stability. The expulsion of the Sudanese diplomats could hinder diplomatic engagement, and the broader implications of these tensions may affect security and relations in the region, particularly with neighboring countries like Eritrea and Egypt.
