Eritrea Accuses Ethiopia of Using Maritime Diplomacy to 'Camouflage Its Irredentist Ambitions'

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 10:44:31 GMT

Eritrea has accused Ethiopia of using maritime diplomacy to disguise its territorial ambitions in the Horn of Africa, particularly regarding access to the Red Sea. In a recent statement, Eritrea's Ministry of Foreign Affairs criticized the Ethiopian government for attempting to gain international legitimacy for its claims to sovereign sea access, which Eritrea views as a form of territorial revisionism. This accusation follows Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's assertion that Ethiopia seeks Red Sea access through dialogue, not invasion. Abiy emphasized Ethiopia's efforts to foster economic cooperation with Eritrea, which he claims have been rejected. Eritrea also expressed concerns over Ethiopia's participation in regional maritime security discussions, arguing that such involvement undermines the sovereignty of recognized coastal states. The Eritrean government reiterated its stance that landlocked nations should negotiate for sea access without infringing on coastal states' rights, citing previous offers of port access that Ethiopia declined. The tensions highlight ongoing disputes over territorial integrity and regional security dynamics. (159 words)
