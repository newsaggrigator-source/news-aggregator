Ethiopia’s Strengthened Defense Capacity Vital to National, Regional Security - ENA English

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 15:33:53 GMT

Ethiopia's enhanced defense capabilities are crucial for both national and regional security, according to Gezahegn Fekadu, Commandant of the International Peace Support Training Centre. He highlighted that the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) has significantly improved its operational readiness through reforms, advanced training, and better weaponry since recent reforms. These developments not only bolster Ethiopia's defense but also its ability to contribute to regional stability. Gezahegn emphasized the importance of ongoing military modernization to address evolving security threats. Additionally, Lieutenant Colonel Aragaw Gebremedhin from the Ethiopian National Defense College noted that Ethiopia's historical role in international peacekeeping, combined with its growing military capabilities, enhances its capacity to maintain peace domestically and in neighboring regions. He urged academic institutions to document Ethiopia's peacekeeping efforts and military advancements to better showcase its contributions to the international community. Overall, a strong defense force is deemed essential for safeguarding national interests and fostering a peaceful environment for development.
