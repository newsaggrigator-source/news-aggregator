World Insights: Sudan-Ethiopia tensions escalate amid mutual accusations, raising regional concerns - Xinhua

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 21:20:15 GMT

Tensions between Sudan and Ethiopia have escalated significantly, marked by mutual accusations of interference in each other's internal affairs and support for armed groups. Sudan has accused Ethiopia of allowing drone attacks against its territory and aiding the Rapid Support Forces (RSF), which have been in conflict with the Sudanese Armed Forces since April 2023. In response, Sudan has lodged complaints with the UN Security Council and the African Union, while Ethiopia has expelled Sudanese diplomats and closed its consulate in Asosa. The situation is further complicated by longstanding disputes over the Fashaga border area and the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, which have historically strained relations. Analysts warn that these tensions could lead to military confrontations, potentially destabilizing the Horn of Africa. The overlapping conflicts and security concerns in both nations highlight the urgent need for diplomatic dialogue to prevent further escalation and ensure regional stability.
