U.S. Customs Releases Ethiopia-Made Solar Cell Shipments After Supply Chain Review

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 16:03:19 GMT

U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has released shipments of solar cells produced at TOYO Co., Ltd.'s facility in Ethiopia after a thorough review of the company's supply chain documentation. The review confirmed the traceability of materials used in manufacturing, including polysilicon and wafers. TOYO, a Japanese solar manufacturer, expressed gratitude for CBP's professional handling of the review and emphasized its commitment to transparency and compliance as it aims to expand its operations in the U.S. This development follows an ongoing investigation into whether Ethiopian solar manufacturers are circumventing U.S. trade duties on Chinese solar products. A petition from eight U.S. solar manufacturers alleged that a significant portion of solar modules exported from Ethiopia contained Chinese components. The investigation is expected to conclude with a preliminary determination by December 10, 2026, and a final decision by May 10, 2027, potentially impacting future imports and duties.
