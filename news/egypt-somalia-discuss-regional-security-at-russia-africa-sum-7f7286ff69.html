Egypt, Somalia discuss regional security at Russia-Africa summit - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 19:15:26 GMT

Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty and Somali Foreign Minister Abdisalam Abdi Ali met during the Russia-Africa Summit to discuss regional security and bilateral relations. Their discussions centered on enhancing cooperation in various sectors, with Abdelatty emphasizing Egypt's commitment to Somalia's unity, sovereignty, and territorial integrity. He expressed strong support for Somalia's efforts to build state institutions and strengthen their capabilities, while rejecting any actions that could undermine Somalia's territorial unity. The ministers also exchanged views on the situation in the Horn of Africa and the Red Sea, highlighting the importance of supporting national institutions and maintaining regional stability. They reviewed the outcomes of a previous consultative summit hosted by Egypt in El-Alamein, aimed at promoting security, development, and de-escalating tensions in the region. This meeting reflects a growing momentum in Egyptian-Somali relations and a shared commitment to regional security and cooperation. (160 words)
