UAE Says Outgoing Somali President Puts Personal Interests Ahead of National Interests - Garowe Online

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:02:22 GMT

Anwar Gargash, the diplomatic adviser to the UAE president, has criticized outgoing Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud for prioritizing personal interests over national concerns. This statement comes amid escalating tensions between Somalia and the UAE, following Mohamud's accusations of UAE interference in Somalia's internal affairs and support for regional administrations that threaten national unity. Gargash expressed that the UAE has historically supported Somalia during challenging times and expressed concern that internal political rivalries could drag Somalia into regional disputes detrimental to its stability. He emphasized that the UAE's relationship with the Somali people would endure beyond temporary political disagreements. The diplomatic rift is further complicated by a ruling from Somalia’s Supreme Court, which declared that Mohamud's presidential term expired in May 2026, urging political leaders to establish an election framework within 30 days. This situation highlights the ongoing political and constitutional crisis in Somalia.
