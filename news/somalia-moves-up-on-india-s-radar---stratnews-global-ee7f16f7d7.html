Somalia Moves Up On India’s Radar - StratNews Global

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 11:25:18 GMT

India and Somalia have initiated a significant diplomatic engagement with their first Foreign Office Consultations (FOC) held in Mogadishu on November 15, 2026. Co-chaired by Indian Joint Secretary Suresh Kumar M and Somalia's Permanent Secretary Hamza Adan Haadoow, the discussions focused on maritime security, counterterrorism, and development. This new framework aims to enhance cooperation, particularly given Somalia's strategic location in the Horn of Africa, which is crucial for India's maritime and economic interests. The consultations follow a Memorandum of Understanding signed on November 14, establishing a structured mechanism for ongoing dialogue. Experts highlight the importance of this engagement in addressing regional security challenges, including piracy and the stability of Somali institutions. Future cooperation may extend to healthcare, education, and capacity building, reflecting India's historical peacekeeping role and its broader Africa policy. The next round of consultations is scheduled to take place in India, aiming to solidify these emerging partnerships. (159 words)
