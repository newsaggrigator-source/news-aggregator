OLA Disputes Rights Commission Findings On Arsi Killings, Accuses Commission of Political Bias

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 10:14:57 GMT

The Oromo Liberation Army (OLA) has rejected the findings of the Ethiopian Human Rights Commission (EHRC) regarding the violent attacks in Arsi woreda, which occurred in October. The OLA accused the EHRC of political bias and questioned its decision to focus on Arsi while ignoring other civilian attacks across Ethiopia. In a statement, the OLA claimed that multiple armed groups, including government forces, were involved in the violence, which resulted in civilian casualties. They argued that those killed were not ordinary civilians but "part-time combatants" aligned with government forces. The OLA criticized the EHRC for relying on government information, suggesting it undermines the commission's credibility. They also framed the violence as part of Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's strategy to exploit ethnic divisions, warning that this approach could lead to greater instability in Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa. The OLA's statement reflects broader concerns about the ongoing civil conflict and the role of government institutions in perpetuating violence.
