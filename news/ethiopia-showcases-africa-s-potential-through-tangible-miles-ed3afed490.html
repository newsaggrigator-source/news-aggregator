Ethiopia Showcases Africa's Potential Through Tangible Milestones in Health Sector

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 12:43:20 GMT

Ethiopia is highlighting Africa's potential through significant advancements in its healthcare sector, as stated by Minister of Health Dr. Mekdes Daba during the 5th International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026) in Addis Ababa. The conference, attended by over 30,000 participants from more than 100 countries, showcases Ethiopia's transformative health initiatives and multisectoral collaboration, with participation from various ministers beyond health. Dr. Mekdes emphasized the country's achievements as evidence of its ability to turn ambitions into tangible results, bolstering international confidence in Africa's capabilities. Key innovations presented include the Electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS) and advancements in artificial intelligence and digital health. These developments have attracted interest from other nations eager to adopt Ethiopia's healthcare models. The Minister also noted Ethiopia's role in global health governance, asserting the continent's position as an active contributor to health solutions rather than a mere recipient of external policies.
