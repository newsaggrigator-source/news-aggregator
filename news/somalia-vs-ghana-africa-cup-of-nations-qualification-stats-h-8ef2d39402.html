This win places Ghana in a competitive position within Group C, where they currently hold third place with six points. Somalia, on the other hand, remains at the bottom of the group without any points after four matches. The match statistics reflect Ghana's dominance, as they managed to maintain control throughout the game. Other notable matches in the qualification round included Eritrea losing to Guinea and Zimbabwe defeating Equatorial Guinea. As the qualification process continues, teams are vying for a spot in the final tournament, with Ghana aiming to improve their standing in upcoming fixtures.
