As Eritrea Continues to Orchestrate Attacks from Its Territory, Ethiopia Retains Its Inherent Right to Self-Defence - horn review

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 08:12:55 GMT

Ethiopia is facing significant security threats from Eritrea, which is accused of supporting armed opposition groups that launch attacks against the Ethiopian state from its territory. The situation is complicated by Eritrea's military incursions and its provision of logistical support to these groups, including a newly formed coalition of Ethiopian armed factions. Despite Ethiopia's diplomatic efforts to address these issues, including appeals to the United Nations, Eritrea's aggressive posture continues, prompting Ethiopia to assert its right to self-defense under international law. The Ethiopian government emphasizes that it cannot be expected to tolerate ongoing threats while relying solely on diplomacy. As tensions escalate, Ethiopia seeks to enhance its border security and maintain the capacity to defend itself against external aggression, while also striving to avoid another interstate conflict. The article underscores Ethiopia's commitment to national security and its right to take necessary measures to protect its sovereignty and constitutional order.
