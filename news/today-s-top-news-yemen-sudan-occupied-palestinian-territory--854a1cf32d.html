Today's top news: Yemen, Sudan, Occupied Palestinian Territory, Somalia - OCHA

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 18:08:10 GMT

The UN's Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) highlights urgent humanitarian crises in Yemen, Sudan, the Occupied Palestinian Territory, and Somalia. In Yemen, UN Relief Chief Tom Fletcher emphasizes the need for increased funding and safe access for aid, as the humanitarian response faces severe underfunding. In Sudan, escalating drone strikes in Blue Nile State have resulted in civilian casualties, with OCHA calling for immediate humanitarian access and additional funding to support affected populations. In the Occupied Palestinian Territory, deteriorating conditions in Gaza have led to a rise in meningitis cases, exacerbated by water and sanitation shortages. Meanwhile, in the West Bank, demolitions have displaced families and destroyed essential infrastructure. In Somalia, a funding crisis has forced the closure of nutrition sites and cut health services, leaving millions without necessary assistance. OCHA warns that without urgent funding, the humanitarian situation will worsen significantly across these regions.
