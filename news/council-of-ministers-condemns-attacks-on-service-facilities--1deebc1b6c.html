Council of Ministers Condemns Attacks On Service Facilities By Drones Launched From Ethiopia

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 21:09:45 GMT

The Sudanese Council of Ministers, led by Prime Minister Dr. Kamil Idris, convened in Khartoum to address the recent drone attacks on service facilities attributed to Ethiopia. The Undersecretary of the Ministry of Culture, Information, and Tourism, Dr. Graham Abdel-Gadir, condemned these attacks as crimes against humanity and emphasized the need for heightened alertness among officials and the public. The Cabinet directed monitoring of conspiracies against the nation and called for severe penalties for those aiding the enemy. The attacks were interpreted as signs of the rebel Rapid Support Forces' failures. The Council also praised the efforts of technicians in the electricity sector for their dedication to repairing damaged infrastructure. Additionally, the Cabinet reviewed reports on petroleum supply, essential commodities, and agricultural progress, while approving three draft acts related to cybersecurity and digital transformation. Enhanced protection for facilities and personnel was also mandated. (159 words)
