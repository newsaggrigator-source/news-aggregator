Somalia seeks livestock exports to Iraq as countries move to revive joint committee - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:48:37 GMT

Somalia is aiming to enhance its livestock exports to Iraq as both nations work towards revitalizing a joint committee focused on trade and economic cooperation. The initiative comes amid efforts to strengthen bilateral relations and boost Somalia's economy through increased trade opportunities. The joint committee, which has been inactive, is expected to facilitate discussions on various sectors, including agriculture and livestock, to foster mutual benefits. Somalia's livestock sector is a vital part of its economy, and expanding exports to Iraq could provide significant financial support. The revival of this committee reflects a broader strategy by Somalia to engage with international markets and improve its trade dynamics.
