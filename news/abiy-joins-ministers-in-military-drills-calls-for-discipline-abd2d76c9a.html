Abiy Joins Ministers in Military Drills, Calls for Disciplined Leadership

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 05:33:07 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed recently participated in military training alongside ministers and senior officials at the Hurso military training center. This initiative, which took place on October 31, aims to instill discipline, accountability, and teamwork among government leaders, aligning with the government's development goals. Abiy emphasized that military experience can enhance civilian leadership by fostering qualities such as physical fitness, ethical conduct, and a commitment to public service. He argued that leaders must be knowledgeable and disciplined to effectively translate national priorities into action. The training is part of a broader strategy to align administrative structures with national objectives and ensure national security. Abiy's statement highlighted the importance of combining military and civilian leadership lessons to cultivate leaders capable of achieving Ethiopia's ambitions. However, details regarding the duration and start of the training program were not disclosed.
