A Week of Denials: Uganda and Türkiye Push Back Against False Somalia Security Claims - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 08:17:51 GMT

In recent days, both Uganda and Türkiye have publicly denied false claims regarding security incidents in Somalia. Uganda's government refuted reports that suggested its soldiers were killed in an Al-Shabaab ambush while withdrawing from Lower Shabelle. Dennis Katungi, a senior official, labeled these reports as fabricated propaganda by the militant group. This denial follows Türkiye's rejection of claims that it planned to deploy 15,000 troops to Somalia, asserting that its current mission is limited to training and advisory support. Both denials highlight a concerning trend of misinformation circulating online, often attributed to unnamed sources. As Uganda withdraws its forces and the African Union faces funding challenges, the spread of unverified claims poses risks to security perceptions in the region. Experts suggest that greater transparency from both governments regarding troop movements and incidents could help counteract misinformation and clarify the evolving security landscape in Somalia.
