Somalia: Djibouti peacekeepers killed in clash with Hawadle militias at Beledweyne airport - Somali Guardian

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 18:08:49 GMT

Two Djiboutian peacekeepers were killed and two others wounded during a clash with Hawadle clan militias at Ugaas Khalif Airport in Beledweyne, Somalia. The incident occurred when the militias attempted to block a plane carrying federal government troops from landing. The fighting erupted as Macawisley fighters occupied the runway, prompting Djiboutian forces to intervene. The aircraft ultimately aborted its landing and returned to its origin. The clash highlights escalating tensions in Beledweyne, where Hawadle militias have mobilized in response to perceived threats from rival clans and the federal government. This mobilization follows calls from clan leader Ugaas Yusuf Ugas Hassan for local security personnel to defend the town. Recent protests against both the federal government and the Hirshabelle administration have further complicated the situation. Control of the airport has become a significant point of contention, raising concerns about potential further confrontations as both sides remain entrenched in their positions.
