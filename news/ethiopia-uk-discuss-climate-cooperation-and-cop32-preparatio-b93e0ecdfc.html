Ethiopia, UK Discuss Climate Cooperation and COP32 Preparations

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 05:37:17 GMT

Ethiopia and the UK engaged in discussions to enhance climate cooperation and prepare for the upcoming COP32 summit, scheduled to take place in Addis Ababa from November 8 to 19, 2027. Ethiopian Foreign Minister Gedion Timothewos, who is the COP32 president-designate, and Finance Minister Fitsum Assefa met with British Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband during the COP31 United Nations climate conference in Antalya. Gedion highlighted Ethiopia's ongoing climate initiatives and emphasized the need to reduce emissions contributing to climate change. Miliband expressed Britain's commitment to supporting Ethiopia's priorities and preparations for the summit. This collaboration marks a significant step in international climate negotiations, as Ethiopia prepares to host a major global event focused on climate action and sustainability. The discussions reflect the growing importance of international partnerships in addressing climate challenges.
