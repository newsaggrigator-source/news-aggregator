IG Kanja Orders Deployment of More Police Officers to Moyale Amid Ethiopia Tensions - Nairobi Leo

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 04:00:00 GMT

Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja has ordered the deployment of additional police officers to Moyale, Marsabit County, in response to escalating tensions and insecurity in neighboring Ethiopia. Following a security assessment conducted on November 5, Kanja met with regional security officials to evaluate the situation and discuss protective measures for local residents. The National Police Service emphasized that this deployment aims to enhance existing security operations and improve the capacity of officers to address potential threats. Kanja reassured the community of the police's commitment to safeguarding their lives and property, urging residents to cooperate with security agencies by providing timely information. He also visited a camp for families displaced by insecurity, promising increased protection and coordinated humanitarian assistance. The situation remains under close monitoring as security agencies work to maintain stability in the region.
