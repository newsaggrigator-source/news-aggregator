Somalia: Islamic Scholars Urge End to Permanent 4.5 System, Call For Stronger Justice Institutions - Dawan Africa

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 20:05:34 GMT

Islamic scholars in Somalia have called for the abolition of the 4.5 power-sharing system, which they argue should not be a permanent political framework. This statement emerged from the sixth annual conference of the Maqasid Center for Research and Studies held in Mogadishu, where scholars emphasized that clans should not be exploited for oppression or resource plunder. The conference advocated for social unity, enhanced governance, and the establishment of robust justice institutions, including an independent judiciary. It stressed the importance of public consultation in governance and the need for clear divisions of power and revenue between the federal government and member states. Additionally, the scholars highlighted the necessity of addressing Somalia's internal challenges before pursuing regional cooperation, calling for improved infrastructure and trade facilitation. They also urged reforms in religious discourse and media practices to prevent societal division, recommending that national policies align with Islamic Sharia objectives. The recommendations will be forwarded to various stakeholders for implementation. (159 words)
