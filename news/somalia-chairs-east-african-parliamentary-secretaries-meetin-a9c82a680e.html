Somalia Chairs East African Parliamentary Secretaries Meeting in Nairobi - FTL Somalia

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 09:32:41 GMT

Somalia recently took a leadership role in regional legislative cooperation by chairing a meeting of East African parliamentary secretaries in Nairobi. Abdirahman Mohamed Abdulle (Jaabiri), Secretary General of the House of the People, led discussions focused on enhancing cooperation among parliamentary administrations, accountability, and legislative efficiency. The meeting involved reviewing the secretariat's report and formulating recommendations for the Assembly of Speakers of East African Community (EAC) member parliaments. This engagement highlights Somalia's growing integration into the EAC since its membership in 2024, extending beyond economic opportunities to governance and legislative collaboration. While the meeting primarily addressed administrative matters, it signals Somalia's intent to actively shape the EAC's parliamentary framework. However, the effectiveness of this engagement will depend on whether Somalia's parliament can apply lessons learned to overcome its domestic challenges, including criticisms regarding its independence and oversight capabilities. The Nairobi meeting underscores the potential for political and institutional development alongside economic benefits.
