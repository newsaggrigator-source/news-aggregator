Ethiopia Committed to Collective Security, Says Brigadier General Adamneh - ENA English

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 14:37:30 GMT

Ethiopia's commitment to collective security was emphasized by Major General Adamneh Mengiste during the International Partners Day at the Defense Peacekeeping Center in Addis Ababa. He highlighted Ethiopia's extensive history in peacekeeping, which dates back to the Korean War in 1951 and includes missions in various conflict zones such as South Sudan and Somalia. Major General Adamneh reaffirmed the nation’s dedication to global peace, rooted in professionalism and sacrifice, while honoring peacekeepers who lost their lives, including four personnel in South Sudan in 2026. Brigadier General Gezahegn Fikadu discussed the role of the International Peace Support Training Institute (IPSTI) in enhancing military and civilian training for peace operations. He stressed the importance of collaboration and shared resources among local and international partners to strengthen peace efforts. Major General Teshome Gemechu called for renewed partnerships to ensure a stable future for Africa and the world, aligning with the theme of the event: “Invest in Peace: Building Resilient Partnerships for Global Security.”
