Somalia: Security Tensions Escalate in Beledweyne as Military Forces Deploy Near Traditional Leader’s Residence - Horseed Media

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 16:21:00 GMT

Security tensions in Beledweyne, the capital of Somalia's Hiiraan region, have escalated as military forces, including Somali National Army (SNA) troops and National Intelligence and Security Agency (NISA) personnel, have deployed near the residence of traditional leader Ugaas Yusuf Ugaas Hassan. This follows Ugaas's recent call for clan members on the front lines to withdraw amid rising tensions between the Hirshabelle administration and local residents. Reports indicate that defected NISA personnel have taken control of Beledweyne Airport, issuing warnings to incoming aircraft as federal delegations prepare to address the crisis. Former NISA Director Abdullahi Mohamed Ali, known as Sanbaloolshe, has also arrived, asserting that Hirshabelle will no longer operate in parts of Hiiraan. Djiboutian military officers are working to mediate between the opposing sides to prevent armed conflict. The situation remains volatile as political and security developments continue to unfold in the region.
