President Mahama arrives in Ethiopia; joins Prime Minister to inaugurate historic Megech Irrigation Project - 3News

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 18:08:02 GMT

President John Dramani Mahama arrived in Gondar, Ethiopia, on October 23, 2026, for an official visit at the invitation of Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed Ali. This visit precedes the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, scheduled to take place in Bahir Dar. Shortly after his arrival, President Mahama joined the Prime Minister in inaugurating the Megech Irrigation Development Project, a significant infrastructure initiative aimed at enhancing agriculture and water supply in the region. The project, which had been stalled for 18 years, was revived and completed under Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's leadership. The Megech Dam spans 870 hectares and has a storage capacity of 185 million cubic meters, set to irrigate 17,000 hectares of agricultural land, thereby improving food security and productivity. Additionally, it will provide clean drinking water to Gondar and nearby towns. President Mahama praised the Ethiopian government and its people for their commitment to this transformative project. He will later chair the Tana Forum to discuss critical security issues in Africa.
