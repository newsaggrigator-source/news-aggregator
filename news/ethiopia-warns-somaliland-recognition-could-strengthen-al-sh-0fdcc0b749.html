Ethiopia Warns Somaliland Recognition Could Strengthen Al-Shabaab, Somaliland Officials Push Back

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 04:52:16 GMT

Ethiopia has expressed concerns that recognizing Somaliland could bolster Al-Shabaab and jeopardize regional counterterrorism efforts. Ethiopian Ambassador to Israel, Tesfaye Yetaye Anteneh, warned that such recognition might lead Somalia to demand the withdrawal of Ethiopian troops, creating a security vacuum that Al-Shabaab could exploit. In contrast, Somaliland officials argue that recognition would enhance stability and benefit Ethiopia's economic interests, citing Somaliland's effective prevention of Al-Shabaab's operational presence. Somaliland's ambassador to Israel, Mohamed Hagi, emphasized that recognition would provide Ethiopia with strategic advantages, particularly through access to the Port of Berbera. The ongoing dispute highlights the complexities of Ethiopia's pursuit of maritime access while maintaining security cooperation with Somalia.
