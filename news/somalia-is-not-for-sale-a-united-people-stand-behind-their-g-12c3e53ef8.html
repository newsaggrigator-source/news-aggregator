Somalia Is Not for Sale — A United People Stand Behind Their Government in Defence of National Sovereignty - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 16:23:40 GMT

The article emphasizes the importance of national sovereignty for Somalia amidst rising diplomatic tensions with the United Arab Emirates (UAE). It asserts that the Somali people, united beyond political and clan divisions, must defend their country's independence and territorial integrity. The Federal Government's diplomatic actions against the UAE highlight a critical question: who determines Somalia's future? The article argues that this responsibility lies with the Somali people through their legitimate institutions. While Somalia welcomes foreign partnerships for development, these must respect its sovereignty and constitutional framework. The piece calls for a collective national commitment to protect Somalia's independence, urging citizens to prioritize national interests over political disagreements. It stresses that defending the nation is a shared responsibility, not just that of political leaders. Ultimately, the article advocates for a united Somalia that speaks with dignity and asserts its right to self-governance, ensuring that its sovereignty is never compromised. (160 words)
