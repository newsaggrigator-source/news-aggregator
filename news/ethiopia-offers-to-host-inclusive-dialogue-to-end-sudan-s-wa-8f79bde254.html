Ethiopia Offers to Host Inclusive Dialogue to End Sudan’s War - Garowe Online

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 12:04:00 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has proposed hosting an inclusive national dialogue to help end the ongoing conflict in Sudan, which began on April 15, 2023. In a statement, he emphasized the importance of peace in Sudan for the stability of the Horn of Africa and invited all Sudanese military, political, and civilian groups to participate in the talks. Abiy described the conflict as a humanitarian tragedy with far-reaching consequences, highlighting Ethiopia's historical ties with Sudan and its commitment to supporting the country's unity and territorial integrity. The initiative aims to create a neutral platform for Sudanese stakeholders to address their differences and work towards a national unity government. Abiy stressed that the dialogue would not favor any party and that the future of Sudan should be determined by its people, warning against externally imposed solutions. No date has been set for the proposed talks, and it remains unclear if Sudanese parties have accepted the invitation.
