Somalia’s 2027 Budget Shrinks by $127 Million, 9.2% as Cabinet Approves Draft - FTL Somalia

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 13:21:02 GMT

Somalia's Council of Ministers has approved a draft budget for 2027 totaling $1.26 billion, reflecting a decrease of $127 million, or 9.2%, from the previous year's budget. This reduction highlights ongoing economic challenges, including significant cuts in foreign aid, climate-related shocks, and disruptions in customs revenue, which has plummeted by approximately 40% due to issues in the Strait of Hormuz. The budget's approval comes amid a backdrop of security concerns, particularly regarding military operations against Al-Shabaab. The draft budget will now undergo parliamentary scrutiny, where lawmakers will assess its adequacy for funding essential services and security operations. The Cabinet's decision indicates a shift towards a more austere fiscal reality, as previous assumptions about revenue generation have proven unsustainable. The upcoming parliamentary discussions will be crucial in determining whether the budget can effectively address the country's pressing needs amidst a contracting economy and rising hardships.
