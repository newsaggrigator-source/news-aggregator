Seyoum Damtew Named CISO Of The Year 2026 - CIO Africa

Sat, 28 Nov 2026 09:11:37 GMT

Seyoum Damtew Metaferia, Vice President of Information System Security at the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE), has been awarded the title of CISO of the Year at the 2026 CIO100 Symposium & Awards, marking his second consecutive win. Previously recognized at the 2025 Connected Banking Summit, Damtew has been acknowledged for his significant contributions to enhancing cybersecurity and resilience in the financial sector. His leadership comes at a crucial time as Ethiopia's banking industry undergoes rapid digital transformation, necessitating robust security measures to protect financial infrastructure and maintain customer trust. With a background in telecommunications and banking, and degrees in electrical and computer engineering, Damtew has advanced from Director to Vice President of Information System Security at CBE. The bank actively promotes cybersecurity awareness, hosting an annual conference in partnership with CIO Africa. Damtew received his award during the symposium held from November 25 to 27, 2026, in Diani, Kenya. (159 words)
