Despite warnings from China, Saudi Arabia and Turkey, Israel advances embassy plans as U.S.-backed Somalia hits back and reasserts its claim to breakaway territory - Business Insider Africa

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 21:26:08 GMT

Somalia has officially condemned Israel's appointment of an ambassador to Somaliland, labeling it illegal and a violation of its sovereignty. This move follows Israel's recognition of Somaliland in December 2025, making it the first UN member to do so since Somaliland's separation from Somalia in 1991. Major global powers, including China, Saudi Arabia, and Turkey, have criticized Israel's actions, emphasizing the importance of Somalia's territorial integrity. Despite U.S. military cooperation with Somalia against Al-Shabaab, the Somali government remains steadfast in opposing Israel's diplomatic initiatives, citing violations of the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations. Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar has reiterated Israel's commitment to strengthening ties with Somaliland, while regional leaders warn that such actions could destabilize the Gulf of Aden and Red Sea. The situation highlights the complex interplay of international relations, sovereignty issues, and regional security dynamics in East Africa.
