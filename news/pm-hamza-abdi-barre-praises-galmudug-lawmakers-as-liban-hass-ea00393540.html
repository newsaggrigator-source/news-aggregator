PM Hamza Abdi Barre Praises Galmudug Lawmakers as Liban Hassan and Garyare Take Office - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:11:39 GMT

Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre has congratulated Liban Ahmed Hassan and Abdiweli Ibrahim Adan (Garyare) on their election as President and Vice President of Galmudug, respectively. He described their victory as a historic milestone in the region's electoral process. Barre praised the Galmudug parliament for conducting the election transparently and in accordance with electoral laws. The election was held by an 89-member House of Representatives, which had undergone a court-ordered rerun after the Supreme Court annulled the original vote due to procedural violations. Hassan secured 72 votes, while Garyare received 64, both defeating candidates from the Sincad party, which accepted the results, reducing the risk of post-election unrest. The Prime Minister emphasized the need for the new leadership to focus on security, reconciliation, and development, as they face challenges such as clan disputes and economic pressures. While Barre's praise reflects political courtesy, it underscores the need for the new administration to build local legitimacy amidst ongoing security concerns.
