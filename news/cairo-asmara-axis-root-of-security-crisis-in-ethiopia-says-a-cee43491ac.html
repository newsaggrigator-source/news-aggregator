Cairo–Asmara Axis Root of Security Crisis in Ethiopia, Says Analyst Prof. Al Mariam - ENA English

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 09:27:36 GMT

Political analyst Prof. Al Mariam has attributed Ethiopia's ongoing security crisis to a strategic alliance between Cairo and Asmara, aimed at undermining the nation's development. In his analysis published on Megatopia, he references historical comments made by Eritrean President Isaias Afewerki, highlighting a long-standing collaboration between Egypt and Eritrea to destabilize Ethiopia. Al Mariam argues that the primary goal of this alliance is to maintain Egyptian control over the Nile waters, with Egypt's strategy focused on keeping upstream nations in turmoil to hinder their development projects. He points out that Afewerki's acknowledgment of a campaign to "give Ethiopia work" reflects a deliberate strategy to exhaust the Ethiopian government. This includes fostering internal conflicts, funding insurgent groups, and conducting military incursions, which divert resources from essential socio-economic initiatives. Al Mariam warns that these destabilizing actions threaten not only Ethiopia's sovereignty but also the broader peace and security of the Horn of Africa and the Red Sea region.
