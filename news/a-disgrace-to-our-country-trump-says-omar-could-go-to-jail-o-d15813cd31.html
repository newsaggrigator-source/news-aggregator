'A disgrace to our country': Trump says Omar could go to jail or be deported - WPEC

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 22:32:07 GMT

Former President Donald Trump criticized Congresswoman Ilhan Omar, suggesting she could face jail time or deportation due to her past comments and actions. During a recent rally, Trump labeled Omar a "disgrace to our country," emphasizing his belief that her statements undermine American values. He called for accountability, arguing that her behavior is unacceptable for someone in her position. Trump's remarks reflect ongoing tensions surrounding Omar's controversial statements and her status as a prominent figure in the Democratic Party. The comments have sparked discussions about immigration, accountability, and the political climate in the U.S. as the 2024 election approaches.
