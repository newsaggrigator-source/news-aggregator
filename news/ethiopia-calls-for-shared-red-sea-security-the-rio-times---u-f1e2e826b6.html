Ethiopia calls for shared Red Sea security — The Rio Times - UA.NEWS

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 19:51:26 GMT

Ethiopia's National Intelligence and Security Service chief, Redwan Hussein, has urged regional countries to collaborate on ensuring security in the Red Sea, highlighting the challenges faced by coastal nations in independently safeguarding this vital maritime route. Speaking at a forum in Bahir Dar on October 23, during the 12th High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, Hussein emphasized the increasing militarization of the region and the threats posed to trade routes, particularly near the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. He called for collective action among regional states, supported by international partners, to address these security concerns. The forum also addressed interconnected crises in the region, including conflicts in Sudan, South Sudan, and Somalia. Participants, including envoys from Germany and France, underscored the importance of multilateral cooperation and regional peace initiatives. Ethiopia, being landlocked, relies heavily on the Red Sea for maritime trade, making the security of this route crucial for its economy.
