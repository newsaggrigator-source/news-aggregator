Somalia Orders UAE Ambassador to Leave Within 72 Hours Amid Escalating Diplomatic Tensions - Horseed Media

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 18:30:07 GMT

Somalia has ordered the UAE ambassador to leave the country within 72 hours and recalled its own ambassador from Abu Dhabi, escalating diplomatic tensions between the two nations. This decision follows accusations from the Somali government that the UAE has violated Somalia’s sovereignty through various political, economic, and military activities, which Somali officials have termed an “open invasion.” Information Minister Abdifatah Kasim Mohamud urged Somalis to unite against these perceived threats and warned of consequences for those cooperating with the UAE. The tensions have been exacerbated by allegations that the UAE has made agreements with regional administrations regarding key ports without federal approval and has established military bases that operate outside Somalia’s national command. This diplomatic rift follows Somalia's earlier cancellation of agreements with the UAE in January, indicating a significant deterioration in relations. The future of bilateral ties remains uncertain as both countries grapple with these escalating tensions.
