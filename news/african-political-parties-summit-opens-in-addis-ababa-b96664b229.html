African Political Parties Summit Opens in Addis Ababa

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 08:29:16 GMT

The Second African Political Parties Summit (APPS) commenced in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, focusing on the role of political parties in driving Africa's economic transformation and shared prosperity. Organized by Ethiopia's ruling Prosperity Party and the African Governance Center, the three-day event is themed "Governing for Growth: Political Parties as Architects of Africa's Economic Future." It aims to foster inter-party dialogue and collective action, emphasizing governance, economic growth, peace, and sustainable development. The summit seeks to redefine political parties' roles beyond electoral competition, positioning them as strategic institutions that can influence economic policies and national transformation. Key discussions will address translating political mandates into practical development outcomes, including job creation and climate resilience. The summit is expected to produce the Addis Ababa Declaration, guiding political parties in their development efforts. This initiative represents a shift towards transformative roles for political parties in shaping Africa's economic future and promoting homegrown solutions to development challenges. (160 words)
