Somalia: Federal Troops Gather at Clan Leader’s Residence in Beledweyne Amid Rising Tensions - Garowe Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 11:52:33 GMT

Federal troops have assembled at the residence of Ugaas Yusuf Ugaas Hassan, a traditional leader in Beledweyne, Somalia, following his call for community self-defense amid rising tensions. This gathering comes after Ugaas Yusuf urged his Hawadle clan to unite against alleged attacks from allied clans supported by the federal government. He accused individuals in military uniforms of targeting his community, prompting fears of escalating conflict. The situation is further complicated by political unrest surrounding the recent Hirshabelle regional elections, which have faced criticism from local authorities and traditional leaders, particularly after the re-election of President Ali Gudlawe Hussein. The tensions highlight a growing divide between federal authorities and local communities, as disputes over electoral processes intertwine with security and clan grievances. As of now, there has been no official response from the federal government regarding these developments.
