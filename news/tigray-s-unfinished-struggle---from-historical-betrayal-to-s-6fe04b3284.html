Tigray's Unfinished Struggle - From Historical Betrayal to Self-Determination

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 14:32:43 GMT

Tigray's complex relationship with Ethiopia is marked by historical oppression and betrayal, juxtaposed with moments of significant political influence. The era of Meles Zenawi initially brought hope to Tigrayans, who believed in a shared identity based on self-determination and equality. However, this trust has been shattered, revealing a deep sense of betrayal as the Ethiopian state has failed to protect Tigray's autonomy and rights. The 2020-2022 war exemplified this betrayal, leading to immense loss of life and destruction of infrastructure. The Prosperity Party's ideology has further marginalized Tigray, fostering resentment and a desire for self-determination among its people. Cultural erasure and historical appropriation have compounded these grievances, as Tigrayans seek to reclaim their identity and future. Amidst ongoing violence and geopolitical manipulation, the aspiration for independence has gained momentum, reflecting a historical struggle for dignity and sovereignty. The question remains: how can Tigray's aspirations for self-determination translate into a sustainable political future? (159 words)
