UAE rejects Somalia's accusations, blames political disputes for worsening relation - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 16:16:42 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has condemned Somalia's recent decision to expel its ambassador, attributing the deterioration of relations to political disputes rather than any wrongdoing on its part. In a statement, the UAE's Ministry of Foreign Affairs criticized Somalia's actions as hostile and detrimental to their longstanding cooperation. This diplomatic rift follows accusations from Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, who alleged that the UAE has interfered in Somalia's internal affairs by supporting regional administrations outside federal control. The UAE has denied these claims, asserting that its policies are not aimed at undermining Somalia's stability. The UAE also highlighted that it had previously closed its embassy in Mogadishu and withdrawn its diplomatic staff due to hostile actions from Somali authorities. Both nations maintain that their disagreements are political and do not reflect on the people of either country, emphasizing the importance of preserving their historical ties.
