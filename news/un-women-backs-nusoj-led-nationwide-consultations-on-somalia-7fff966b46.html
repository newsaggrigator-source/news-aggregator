UN Women backs NUSOJ-led nationwide consultations on Somalia’s accession to CEDAW - Garowe Online

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 18:03:50 GMT

UN Women is supporting the National Union of Somali Journalists (NUSOJ) in conducting nationwide consultations regarding Somalia's potential accession to the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW). These consultations, which began in Mogadishu, aim to enhance public understanding and support for the Convention. They involve discussions among government institutions, civil society, and community representatives about the relevance of CEDAW to Somalia. Acting Minister Mohamed Osman Omar emphasized the importance of informed and inclusive dialogue, while Rukaya Mohammed from UN Women highlighted the need for communities to grasp the implications of CEDAW and the accession process. NUSOJ Secretary-General Omar Faruk Osman stated that the consultations would gather practical recommendations to shape a roadmap reflecting Somalia's realities and commitment to eliminating discrimination against women. The forums include presentations on CEDAW and facilitated discussions addressing constitutional, religious, and customary concerns.
