Museveni's Clout As Mediator Tested As Somalia Opposition Looks To Uganda Ahead Of 2027 Polls - Dawan Africa

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 20:33:51 GMT

Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni's role as a mediator in Somalia is being scrutinized as the opposition looks to him for assistance ahead of the 2027 elections. Museveni, who previously brokered the Kampala Accord in 2011, accepted an invitation to visit Somalia following discussions with President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. However, the recent joint communique from their meeting focused on trade and security rather than electoral mediation, raising doubts about Museveni's current influence. Analysts suggest that while Museveni's status as an elder statesman could be beneficial, Uganda's leverage has diminished significantly since 2011, when its troops were crucial to Somalia's stability. The Somali opposition is wary of relying solely on agreements with the government and seeks a foreign guarantor for any potential deal. Despite strained relations with neighboring countries, Hassan Sheikh has proposed local discussions with opposition leaders, while the U.S. continues to support Ugandan troops in Somalia.
