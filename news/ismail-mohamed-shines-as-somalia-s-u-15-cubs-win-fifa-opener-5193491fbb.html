Ismail Mohamed Shines as Somalia’s U-15 Cubs Win FIFA Opener Against Puerto Rico - FTL Somalia

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 14:34:47 GMT

Somalia's U-15 national football team made history by winning their opening match at the inaugural FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival, defeating Puerto Rico 3-1. Ismail Mohamed was the standout player, scoring two goals and providing an assist, earning him the Man of the Match title. This victory marks Somalia's first win at any FIFA tournament, a significant achievement for a nation rebuilding its football infrastructure after decades of conflict. The match was attended by Somalia's Ambassador to Azerbaijan, highlighting the importance of this moment for the team and the country. The tournament features a unique format with eight-a-side matches and no knockout rounds, allowing for a focus on development and education. Somalia is competing in Group T alongside teams like Venezuela and Madagascar. The players' celebration and patriotic song, "Somalia belongs to Somalis," symbolize a powerful affirmation of national identity and potential, showcasing the positive impact of sports on Somalia's youth.
