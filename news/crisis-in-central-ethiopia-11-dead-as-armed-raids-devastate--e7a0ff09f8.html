Crisis in Central Ethiopia: 11 Dead as Armed Raids Devastate East Gurage - EthiopiaToday

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 13:09:10 GMT

In East Gurage, Central Ethiopia, armed raids have resulted in the deaths of 11 individuals, exacerbating an ongoing crisis in the region. The violence has led to widespread fear and displacement among local communities. Authorities are struggling to maintain order as tensions rise, with reports of increased attacks and retaliatory violence. The situation has drawn attention from humanitarian organizations, which are concerned about the safety and well-being of affected populations. Efforts to restore peace and provide assistance are underway, but challenges remain significant as the conflict continues to escalate. The government has been urged to take decisive action to address the root causes of the violence and protect civilians.
