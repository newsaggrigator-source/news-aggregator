African Union peacekeepers hand over 5 bases to Somali Army - Anadolu Ajansı

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 22:39:57 GMT

The African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) has officially handed over five Forward Operating Bases to the Somali National Armed Forces, marking a significant step in Somalia's security transition. The bases, located in the Barawe Sub-Sector, include Barawe Embresso, Barawe Knoll, Kismayo Road, Seaport, and Runway, all of which are fully operational. This handover comes as the mission's UN mandate is set to expire on December 31, with some troop-contributing countries already beginning to withdraw their forces. AUSSOM's mission has been ongoing since 2007, evolving from the African Union Mission in Somalia (AMISOM) to the African Union Transition Mission in Somalia (ATMIS) in 2022, and now to AUSSOM in 2025. The mission has played a crucial role in supporting the Somali government in its fight against the al-Shabaab terrorist group. AUSSOM urged the Somali Armed Forces and local residents to maintain the peace achieved over the years. (159 words)
