Somalia’s President Says Israel and Taiwan Pose Threat to Country’s Existence - Horseed Media

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 15:46:51 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has expressed concerns that Israel and Taiwan threaten the country's sovereignty and territorial integrity, particularly amid rising tensions over Somaliland, which declared independence in 1991 but lacks widespread recognition. Speaking at the launch of Somalia's National Defence University in Mogadishu, Mohamud stated, "Taiwan poses a threat to Somalia’s existence, and Israel poses another threat to Somalia’s statehood." He emphasized that Israel is the only nation to recognize Somaliland, while Taiwan maintains diplomatic relations in the region. The president criticized opposition leaders for seeking foreign intervention and urged them to engage in dialogue to address Somalia's political challenges. Additionally, he highlighted the potential impact of severe flooding due to a strong El Niño event, which could affect up to 3.4 million people in southern Somalia. Mohamud's remarks underscore the complex issues facing Somalia, from diplomatic disputes to climate-related threats. (158 words)
