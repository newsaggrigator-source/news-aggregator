Endf At 119 - Advancing Ethiopia's Peace, Regional Stability and Tech Self-Reliance

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 22:41:05 GMT

The Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) recently celebrated its 119th anniversary, highlighting its critical role in safeguarding Ethiopia's sovereignty and contributing to regional stability. The ENDF has evolved significantly, adapting to contemporary security challenges through reforms in organizational structure, training, and technology. Its transformation aims to enhance operational capabilities while reducing reliance on foreign suppliers. The ENDF's responsibilities extend beyond military operations to include fostering national peace, political dialogue, and civilian protection. Regionally, Ethiopia plays a vital role in security cooperation, particularly within the Eastern Africa Standby Force, addressing shared threats through collaboration. Internationally, Ethiopia has a long history of participating in UN peacekeeping missions, contributing to global stability. Additionally, the ENDF is focused on advancing domestic defense production and technological capabilities, exemplified by initiatives like establishing a drone technology center. This multifaceted approach underscores the ENDF's commitment to national defense, regional security, and technological self-reliance. (159 words)
