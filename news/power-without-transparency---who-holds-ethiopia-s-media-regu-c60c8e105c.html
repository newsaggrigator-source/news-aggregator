Power Without Transparency - Who Holds Ethiopia's Media Regulator Accountable When It Silences the Press?

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 15:41:25 GMT

On November 2, 2026, Ethiopia's Media Authority (EMA) indefinitely suspended the registration of Media and Communication Center PLC, which publishes the Amharic-language Reporter and the English-language The Reporter. This action raises significant concerns about accountability and transparency in media regulation. The EMA claims the suspension was due to the publisher's repeated dissemination of distorted and unverified information, but it has not provided specific examples or evidence to support these allegations. Critics, including Human Rights Watch and Reporters Without Borders, argue that the decision reflects a broader crackdown on independent journalism in Ethiopia, particularly following the publication of a critical editorial by The Reporter. The lack of transparency in the EMA's decision-making process undermines public confidence and raises questions about whether the suspension was politically motivated. To restore trust, the EMA must clarify its rationale, provide evidence for its claims, and ensure that media regulations are applied fairly and consistently across all outlets.
