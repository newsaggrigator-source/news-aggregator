Allied Gold Pours First Gold At Ethiopia's Kurmuk Mine, Targets Commercial Production By Year-End

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 15:55:49 GMT

Canadian mining company Allied Gold has successfully poured its first gold at the Kurmuk mine in Ethiopia's Benishangul-Gumuz Regional State, marking a significant milestone as it aims for commercial production by the end of 2026. This development positions Kurmuk as Allied Gold's newest and most cost-effective operation. The company is currently finalizing power infrastructure and mobilizing its haulage fleet, with plans to reach full capacity by early 2027. Production estimates for this year range from 25,000 to 35,000 ounces, increasing to between 240,000 and 270,000 ounces in 2027, and approximately 300,000 ounces in 2028. Allied Gold's management praised the support from Ethiopian teams and the government, highlighting Kurmuk as one of the country's first large-scale mechanized mining operations. Additionally, the project has attracted significant investment interest, including a 9.2% stake acquisition by China's Zijin Gold International for nearly $300 million.
