#PM_Abiy warns #Eritrea of ‘extremely severe’ consequences if it rejects #Ethiopia’s peace call amid rising tensions watch - Facebook

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 09:30:09 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has issued a stern warning to Eritrea, stating that the country will face "extremely severe" consequences if it does not respond positively to Ethiopia's call for peace. This warning comes amid escalating tensions between the two nations, which have a history of conflict. Abiy emphasized the importance of dialogue and cooperation to resolve ongoing disputes and urged Eritrea to engage in peaceful negotiations. The situation remains delicate, and the Ethiopian government is closely monitoring Eritrea's response to the peace initiative. The international community is also watching the developments, as stability in the Horn of Africa is crucial for regional security.
