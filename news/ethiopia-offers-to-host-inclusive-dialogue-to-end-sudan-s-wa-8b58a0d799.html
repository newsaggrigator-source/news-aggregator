Ethiopia Offers to Host Inclusive Dialogue to End Sudan’s War - Garowe Online

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 12:04:00 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has proposed hosting an inclusive national dialogue to help end the ongoing war in Sudan, which has caused significant humanitarian crises and regional instability since its outbreak on April 15, 2023. In a statement, Abiy emphasized the importance of Sudan's unity and stability for the security of the Horn of Africa and called for participation from all Sudanese military, political, and civilian groups. He described the conflict as a humanitarian tragedy affecting not only Sudan but also its neighbors. The initiative aims to create a neutral platform for dialogue, allowing Sudanese stakeholders to address their differences and work towards a national unity government. Abiy assured that Ethiopia would remain impartial and that the future of Sudan should be determined by its people, warning against externally imposed solutions. No specific date for the dialogue has been announced, nor has there been confirmation of acceptance from the Sudanese parties.
