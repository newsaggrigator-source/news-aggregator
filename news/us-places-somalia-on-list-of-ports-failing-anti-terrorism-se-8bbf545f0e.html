US places Somalia on list of ports failing anti-terrorism security standards - Garowe Online

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 14:28:49 GMT

The United States Coast Guard has officially designated Somalia as a country whose ports do not meet international anti-terrorism security standards, as announced in the Federal Register on October 27, 2026. This classification requires additional security measures for vessels traveling from Somali ports to the United States. The Coast Guard's assessment revealed that Somalia lacks effective anti-terrorism protocols at its ports, prompting the U.S. to impose special security requirements on ships departing from the country. Somalia is now among 24 nations identified for insufficient port security, alongside countries like Iran, Iraq, and North Korea. The new measures, effective immediately, aim to enhance maritime security and mitigate potential terrorist threats. While specific requirements for affected vessels have not been detailed, this designation could complicate operations for maritime operators using Somali ports, potentially increasing security checks and compliance burdens. Somalia has been striving to improve its port security and attract international investment amidst ongoing security challenges. (158 words)
