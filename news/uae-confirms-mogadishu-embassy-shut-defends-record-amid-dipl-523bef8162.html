UAE Confirms Mogadishu Embassy Shut, Defends Record Amid Diplomatic Row - www.ftlsomalia.com

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 21:12:46 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has confirmed the closure of its embassy in Mogadishu and the withdrawal of its diplomats, a decision made in January 2026 amid escalating diplomatic tensions with Somalia. The UAE's Ministry of Foreign Affairs criticized the Somali government's actions, labeling them as "hostile" and detrimental to bilateral relations. This follows Somalia's termination of all agreements with the UAE, including those related to port and security cooperation. Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accused the UAE of interference in Somalia's internal affairs, including unauthorized port deals and the establishment of parallel security structures. In response, the UAE emphasized its commitment to the Somali people and the historical ties between the two nations, while reframing the dispute as a matter of Somali internal politics. The ongoing stalemate highlights the lack of resolution on key issues, with neither side willing to make concessions to restore diplomatic relations.
