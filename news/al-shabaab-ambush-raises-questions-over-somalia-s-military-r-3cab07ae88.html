Al-Shabaab ambush raises questions over Somalia’s military readiness - Geeska

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 23:14:31 GMT

Al-Shabaab's recent ambush, which the group claims resulted in the deaths of hundreds of Somali soldiers, has raised significant concerns regarding Somalia's military readiness. As the Somali government prepares to take control of military bases currently held by African Union forces, doubts about the army's ability to defend recently reclaimed territories have intensified. The casualty figures from the ambush remain unverified, highlighting the challenges in assessing the true scale of losses. While Somali troops have successfully conducted offensives against Al-Shabaab, maintaining control over these areas poses a greater challenge due to the need for secure supply routes and effective command structures. The impending withdrawal of African Union troops adds to the urgency, as the Somali military must be adequately prepared to assume security responsibilities. The situation underscores the gap between President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's security ambitions and the army's current capabilities, raising questions about the future of the campaign against Al-Shabaab.
