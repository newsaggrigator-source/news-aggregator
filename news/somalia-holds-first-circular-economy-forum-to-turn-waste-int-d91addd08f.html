Somalia Holds First Circular Economy Forum to Turn Waste Into Opportunity - Dawan Africa

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 07:04:52 GMT

Somalia recently hosted its first Circular Economy Forum in Mogadishu, aiming to transform the country's waste management challenges into economic opportunities. Organized by Horn Thrive Hub, the forum gathered environmental stakeholders, business leaders, and international experts, including participants from Kenya. With Somali cities generating over 2,000 tonnes of waste daily, the event focused on innovative strategies to reduce waste and promote recycling and reuse. The central theme, "Turning Waste into Resources and Economic Opportunities," emphasized the potential of recycling to create jobs, particularly for the youth, and foster entrepreneurship. Organizers highlighted that a robust recycling industry could attract private investment and support local manufacturing. A World Bank study underscored the scale of waste generation, with each person contributing approximately 0.5 kilograms per day, illustrating both the environmental challenge and the economic potential of waste management in Somalia. The forum marked a significant shift towards sustainable practices in the country.
