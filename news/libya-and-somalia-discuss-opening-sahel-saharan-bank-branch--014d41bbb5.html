Libya and Somalia Discuss Opening Sahel-Saharan Bank Branch in Mogadishu - Horseed Media

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 15:47:20 GMT

Libya and Somalia are in discussions to establish a branch of the Sahel-Saharan Bank for Investment and Trade in Mogadishu, aimed at enhancing financial cooperation and economic relations between the two nations. The talks occurred on November 8, 2026, between Libya’s Finance Minister Rashid Abu Ghuffa and Somalia’s Foreign Minister Abdulsalam Abdi Ali, as reported by Libya’s Ministry of Finance. The discussions centered on the necessary legal and administrative steps to set up the bank's branch, alongside broader initiatives to strengthen banking cooperation. The proposed branch is anticipated to create a framework for closer financial collaboration, potentially boosting investment and trade opportunities between Libya and Somalia. This initiative reflects both countries' ongoing efforts to fortify bilateral economic ties and explore greater cooperation in the banking and financial sectors.
