Cascades FC signs defender Brecc Evans and Somalia international Mo Mohamed - KMTR

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 21:14:16 GMT

Cascades FC has strengthened its roster by signing experienced defender Brecc Evans and Somalia international Mo Mohamed, as announced on November 17, 2026. Evans, a veteran center back, has been a standout in USL League One since turning professional in 2019, known for his leadership and defensive skills. He had a successful 2026 season with Portland Hearts of Pine, making 32 appearances and ranking high in defensive statistics. Mohamed, an Oregon native, recently debuted for the Somalia national team and is recognized for his attacking abilities as a fullback. He gained attention during his time at Seattle University and made a significant impact with Portland Hearts of Pine. Both players are expected to enhance Cascades FC's defensive and attacking capabilities. Head coach Darren Sawatzky expressed enthusiasm about the signings, emphasizing their potential contributions to the team’s success in the upcoming season. The signings are pending approval from the league and federation.
