Arab League Condemns Israeli Attempts to Establish Diplomatic Representation in Somaliland Region - Qatar news agency

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 18:19:05 GMT

The Arab League has condemned Israel's recent diplomatic actions in Somaliland, a region in the Federal Republic of Somalia. The condemnation follows the Israeli ambassador's presentation of credentials and the laying of a foundation stone for an Israeli embassy in Hargeisa. Nabil Fahmy, the Secretary-General of the Arab League, described these actions as a violation of Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity. He emphasized that such moves are part of ongoing efforts to exploit internal divisions within Somalia and expand Israeli influence in the region. The Arab League reiterated its stance that Somaliland is an integral part of Somalia and rejected any engagement with its authorities outside the framework of Somali national sovereignty. Fahmy also warned of the potential negative impacts on security and stability in the Horn of Africa and surrounding areas. The League expressed solidarity with Somalia and its legitimate institutions, reaffirming its previous resolutions against Israeli attempts to partition the country.
