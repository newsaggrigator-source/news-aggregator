Drone Strike Kills 16, Injures 22 At Hayiq Jari 2 IDP Camp in Amhara Region

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 05:03:09 GMT

A drone strike at the Hayiq Jari 2 internally displaced persons (IDP) camp in Ethiopia's Amhara region resulted in the deaths of 16 civilians and injuries to 22 others, including three pregnant women and an eight-year-old girl. The attack, which occurred around 11:00 a.m., involved multiple strikes at intervals of approximately 15 minutes, catching residents off guard while they were inside their shelters. Witnesses reported that panic ensued, scattering families and leaving many unsure of their relatives' whereabouts. The strike also caused significant damage, with over 20 shelters catching fire. The camp, home to more than 1,800 IDPs, is already facing severe humanitarian shortages, as aid organizations have reduced support due to funding constraints. Survivors are in urgent need of food, shelter, and medical assistance, compounding an already desperate situation for those displaced by ongoing conflict in the region.
