AJF × SDRB Somalia Business Plan Competition 2026 - Opportunity Desk

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 08:49:36 GMT

The AJF × SDRB Somalia Business Plan Competition 2026 is an initiative aimed at fostering entrepreneurship in Somalia. The competition invites aspiring entrepreneurs to submit innovative business plans that address local challenges and contribute to economic development. Participants will have the opportunity to receive mentorship, training, and funding to help bring their ideas to fruition. The competition emphasizes the importance of sustainable and impactful business solutions. Interested individuals can apply through the official website, with the deadline for submissions set for early 2026. This initiative is part of a broader effort to support the growth of the Somali economy and empower the next generation of business leaders in the region.
