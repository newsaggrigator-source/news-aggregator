Opinion | Ethiopia’s Illusions - Daily News Egypt

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 20:14:26 GMT

In the opinion piece "Ethiopia’s Illusions," Prof. Hatem Sadek discusses the implications of Ethiopia's recent military acquisitions, specifically 12 Su-35 fighter jets and four S-400 air defense systems from Russia. He argues that these additions are unlikely to significantly alter the balance of power in the Horn of Africa or the Red Sea, a region already saturated with foreign military bases and strategic alliances. Sadek highlights that while Ethiopia may seek to bolster its military image domestically, the complexities of regional dynamics, including established military capabilities and alliances, will limit any substantial impact from these acquisitions. He emphasizes that effective military operations depend more on operational planning, intelligence, and integration of electronic warfare than on sheer numbers of weapons. Ultimately, he concludes that claims of a fundamental shift in power dynamics due to Ethiopia's arms deal are largely exaggerated, suggesting that the deal may only lead to temporary escalations in military tensions without altering the strategic landscape.
