Türkiye’s Ambassador Visits Maarif Schools as Oxford AQA Accreditation Opens Global Paths for Somali Students - FTL Somalia

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 11:05:57 GMT

Türkiye’s Ambassador to Somalia, Ferhat Alkan, visited the Somali International Maarif Schools in Mogadishu, which recently became the first in Somalia to offer internationally accredited Oxford AQA examinations. Established by the Türkiye Maarif Foundation, these schools have been operational for a decade and have graduated thousands of students. The accreditation allows Somali students to take IGCSE and A-Level exams locally, previously requiring travel to neighboring countries, thus opening pathways to higher education globally. During his visit, Ambassador Alkan praised the efforts of the Maarif Foundation and emphasized the importance of investing in education for Somalia's future. The Maarif schools represent a significant educational partnership between Türkiye and Somalia, offering a curriculum that includes Somali, English, Turkish, and Arabic. While the schools provide valuable educational opportunities, questions remain about their long-term impact on Somali institutional capacity and dependency on external educational providers. The next decade will be crucial in determining the sustainability of this initiative.
