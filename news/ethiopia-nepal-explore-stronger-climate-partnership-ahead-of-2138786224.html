Ethiopia, Nepal Explore Stronger Climate Partnership Ahead of COP32

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 02:13:53 GMT

Ethiopia and Nepal are seeking to enhance their climate partnership as Ethiopia prepares to host COP32 in 2027. Ethiopian Foreign Minister Gedion Timothewos, who is also the COP32 President-Designate, met with Nepal's Foreign Affairs Minister Shisir Khanal during the 31st UN Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Türkiye. Their discussions centered on shared climate challenges and opportunities for collaboration, emphasizing the need for stronger international partnerships in addressing climate change. This engagement reflects Ethiopia's broader diplomatic efforts at COP31 to build international support for its climate priorities and mobilize collective action ahead of COP32. The upcoming conference is positioned as a crucial platform for advancing climate cooperation and translating global commitments into actionable outcomes, particularly for developing countries. As preparations intensify, Ethiopia aims to amplify its climate agenda and foster meaningful dialogue on environmental issues.
