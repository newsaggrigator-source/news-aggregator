Ethiopia Advances WTO Accession Negotiations At Eighth Working Party Meeting in Geneva

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 02:13:53 GMT

During the Eighth Working Party meeting in Geneva, Ethiopia made significant strides in its World Trade Organization (WTO) accession negotiations. Led by Trade Minister Yasmin Wohabrebbi, the Ethiopian delegation emphasized the importance of aligning political commitment with practical measures to achieve a beneficial outcome for all parties involved. The delegation highlighted WTO membership as a national priority, building on economic reforms initiated after the new government took office in October. Key achievements include the review of over 460 legal instruments, submission of updated accession documents, and implementation of reforms to comply with WTO regulations. Minister Yasmin expressed readiness to finalize bilateral market access negotiations while advocating for a flexible, development-sensitive approach. WTO Deputy Director-General Xiangchen Zhang and member states acknowledged Ethiopia's reforms and expressed support for integrating its market of 130 million people into the global trading system. The meeting aimed to address remaining multilateral issues and outline the final steps toward Ethiopia's membership.
