Ethiopian Americans Are Gaining Political Ground: Is Ethiopia’s Policy Keeping Up? - horn review

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 12:35:34 GMT

Ethiopian Americans are increasingly gaining political influence in the United States, presenting both opportunities and challenges for Ethiopia's government. The diaspora, often shaped by experiences of conflict and displacement, has sought to impact domestic political developments in Ethiopia while lobbying U.S. policymakers on issues like human rights. Despite limited success in influencing U.S. policy, the diaspora's ability to mobilize and engage politically is significant. Recent elections have seen Ethiopian Americans, such as Melat Kiros and Mike Simmons, secure political positions, highlighting the potential for future influence. However, the Ethiopian government has historically approached the diaspora with suspicion, often failing to engage inclusively. To leverage the diaspora's potential, Ethiopia should adopt a strategic partnership approach, recognizing the diverse experiences within the community and fostering connections with emerging political figures. Initiatives aimed at strengthening ties with younger generations of the diaspora are crucial for enhancing Ethiopia's international visibility and addressing domestic political challenges.
