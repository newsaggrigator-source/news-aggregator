Somali U-17 Squad Celebrated at Parliament as Calls for Sports Investment Grow - FTL Somalia

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 08:41:16 GMT

The Somali U-17 national football team was honored at a parliamentary event hosted by Speaker Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur (Jaamac) following their impressive performance at the CECAFA U-17 Championship, where they reached the final but lost to Uganda in a penalty shootout. The Speaker praised the players as "young heroes" who have made significant contributions to Somali football history. He emphasized the need for private sector investment in sports to enhance the quality of national teams and nurture youth talent, as the current achievements have been realized with limited resources. The Under-17 team's success has generated excitement and goodwill, culminating in their qualification for the 2027 U-17 Africa Cup of Nations in Morocco. However, the Speaker's call for investment highlights the necessity for a sustainable football infrastructure, including a national academy and a domestic league, to build on this success and ensure the future of Somali football.
