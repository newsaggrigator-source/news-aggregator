Ethiopia receives 12 Russian fighter jets and air defence systems as Tigray war escalates - BBC

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 08:23:31 GMT

Ethiopia has received 12 Su-35 fighter jets and air defense systems from Russia amid escalating conflict in the Tigray region. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, attending the Russia-Africa summit in Moscow, hailed the acquisition as a significant enhancement of Ethiopia's defense capabilities, stating it was crucial for national security against external threats. The deal, which was agreed upon years ago, has been expedited, with plans for Ethiopia to also acquire four S-400 air defense systems. Abiy emphasized the importance of military preparedness, noting that the Su-35 jets are equipped for both air-to-air and air-to-ground combat, capable of detecting incoming threats. Military analysts suggest that this acquisition indicates Ethiopia's readiness for potential conflicts beyond its internal strife. The backdrop of this military buildup includes ongoing tensions with Eritrea and the recent resurgence of fighting between the Ethiopian government and the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF), complicating the regional security landscape.
