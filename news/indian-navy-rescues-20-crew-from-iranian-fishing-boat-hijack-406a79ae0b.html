Indian navy rescues 20 crew from Iranian fishing boat hijacked off Somalia - United News of Bangladesh

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 13:42:11 GMT

The Indian Navy successfully rescued 20 crew members from the Iranian fishing vessel Azini, which had been hijacked off the coast of Somalia. The operation, lasting over 48 hours, concluded on November 1, 2026, in the Arabian Sea, approximately 3,000 kilometers from Mumbai. Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh reported that the Navy disarmed 12 suspected Somali pirates during the operation, confiscating their mobile phones before releasing them onshore in Bandar Bayla, Somalia. Although three individuals sustained minor injuries, it was unclear if they were pirates or security guards. The operation involved an Indian warship, maritime surveillance aircraft, and marine commandos, with additional commandos air-dropped by an Indian Air Force C-17 aircraft. The hijacking of the Azini on October 29 raised concerns about a resurgence of Somali piracy, which has seen six commercial vessels hijacked since April, complicating maritime security in the region.
