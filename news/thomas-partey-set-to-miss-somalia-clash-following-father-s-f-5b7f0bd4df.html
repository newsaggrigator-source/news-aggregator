Thomas Partey set to miss Somalia clash following father’s funeral - GhanaWeb

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 05:28:34 GMT

Ghanaian midfielder Thomas Partey is expected to miss the upcoming international match against Somalia due to the recent passing of his father. Partey, who plays for Arsenal in the English Premier League, is currently in Ghana to attend his father's funeral, which has understandably taken precedence over his football commitments. The Ghana Football Association (GFA) has expressed its condolences to Partey and his family during this difficult time. The absence of the experienced player is a significant loss for the Black Stars as they prepare for the crucial clash. Partey's contributions to the team have been invaluable, and his experience will be missed on the pitch. The GFA is likely to make adjustments to the squad in light of his absence, as they aim to secure a positive result in the match against Somalia. The team and fans alike will be hoping for a swift return for Partey once he has had the opportunity to grieve.
