In the latest Geeska newsletter, significant events in Somalia dominate the discourse, particularly concerning violence and justice. Meanwhile, a Quran teacher in Somaliland was sentenced to 14 years for assaulting minors, igniting backlash from religious communities. Somalia faces a political crisis as President insists on a one-person-one-vote election, a move criticized by experts due to ongoing security issues. A humanitarian report warns of severe hunger affecting millions due to aid cuts and climate shocks. The newsletter also reflects on cultural shifts in Somali society, the plight of women in the Horn of Africa, and ongoing political turmoil in Sudan and Tanzania.
