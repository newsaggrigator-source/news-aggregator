Political Parties Must Lead Africa's Economic Transformation - Pan-African Parliament President

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 05:17:26 GMT

Dr. Fateh Boutbig, President of the Pan-African Parliament, emphasized the critical role of political parties in Africa's economic transformation during his closing remarks at the Second African Political Parties Summit in Addis Ababa. He urged these parties to prioritize economic sovereignty and institutional strength, moving beyond mere power competition to focus on building productive economies. Highlighting the historical significance of the summit's location, he called for a shift from raw material exports to manufacturing and innovation. Boutbig also stressed the importance of investing in youth and women, advocating for their involvement in decision-making processes. He announced plans for the African Center for Legislative Studies and Research, an African Parliamentary University, and an African Conference of Political Parties in 2027 to foster collaboration and conflict prevention. Concluding, he called for unity and proactive measures to shape Africa's future, asserting that the continent must actively build its desired future rather than waiting for it to arrive.
