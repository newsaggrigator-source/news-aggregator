A Forum Without the Opposition: Somalia’s Talks Collapse Before They Begin - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 07:42:51 GMT

The National Consultative Forum in Somalia, scheduled for November 14-18, has been postponed due to disagreements over conditions and procedures for the talks. The opposition, particularly the Somali Future Council, demanded international mediation and guarantees, which the government has not accepted. The forum was initially announced amid a Supreme Court ruling that the government's mandate had expired, and the opposition's insistence on preconditions led to a lack of consensus. Although the Quartet (the U.S., U.K., Türkiye, and the EU) agreed to facilitate the talks, the Future Council decided to boycott unless international mediation was ensured. The National Unity Coalition and Nabad iyo Nolol expressed willingness to attend but lacked clarity on the venue. This situation reflects a failure in the dialogue process, with the government attempting to manage rather than negotiate with the opposition. With a Supreme Court deadline approaching, the postponement raises concerns about a potential new crisis rather than a resolution.
