Sudan files complaints against Ethiopia with UN over deadly drone spree - Al Jazeera

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 10:30:01 GMT

Sudan has formally accused Ethiopia of facilitating drone attacks that have resulted in significant civilian casualties, filing complaints with the United Nations Security Council and the African Union’s Peace and Security Council. The Sudanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs claims that the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) have used Ethiopian territory to launch these attacks, which have killed or injured 484 civilians and resulted in the deaths of 176 Sudanese soldiers since February 2026. The Sudanese military alleges that drones have been launched from various sites in Ethiopia, including Asosa Airport and Bahir Dar airbase. The situation has escalated, with a recent attack on displaced families in the Blue Nile region killing 30 civilians. UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres condemned the drone strikes, emphasizing their violation of international humanitarian law and the severe impact on civilians. The ongoing conflict between Sudan's military and the RSF has intensified since April 2023, with mutual accusations of supporting opposing rebel groups.
