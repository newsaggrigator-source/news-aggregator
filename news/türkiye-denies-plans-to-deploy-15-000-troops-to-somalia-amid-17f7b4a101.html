Türkiye denies plans to deploy 15,000 troops to Somalia amid security transition - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 20:25:50 GMT

Türkiye's Ministry of National Defence has denied reports claiming it plans to deploy 15,000 troops to Somalia at the request of the Somali government. Rear Adm. Zeki Aktürk, the ministry spokesperson, emphasized that these claims are false and reaffirmed Türkiye's commitment to military training and counterterrorism cooperation with Somalia. Türkiye maintains a military presence in Somalia through the Somali-Turkish Task Force and an air component command, focusing on enhancing Somalia's counterterrorism capabilities. This denial follows concerns about security as African Union troop-contributing countries prepare to withdraw forces, with some reports suggesting potential involvement of Syrian fighters, which remain unsubstantiated. Türkiye operates the TURKSOM military training facility in Mogadishu, training Somali military personnel since 2017. Recent discussions between Turkish officials and Somalia's National Security Adviser highlighted ongoing military cooperation and the importance of strengthening national security amid changing dynamics in the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia.
