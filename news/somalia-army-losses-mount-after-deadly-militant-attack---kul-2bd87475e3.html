Somalia Army Losses Mount After Deadly Militant Attack - kulu-media.com

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 20:31:40 GMT

Dozens of Somali soldiers were reportedly killed and over 100 wounded during a fierce battle with Al-Shabaab militants in southern Somalia on October 26, 2026. The fighting occurred in the Jameeco area of the Buurhakaba district, where Somali National Army troops were conducting an offensive against Al-Shabaab positions. Eyewitness accounts from soldiers indicated significant casualties among government forces, with one soldier stating he transported 40 dead and over 100 wounded to hospitals in Mogadishu. The Somali government acknowledged the clash but focused on the deaths of approximately 75 Al-Shabaab militants, without confirming its own military losses. This battle is part of Operation Banooda, aimed at targeting Al-Shabaab along a crucial transportation corridor. The ongoing conflict highlights the complex security situation in southern Somalia, where Al-Shabaab continues to pose a significant threat despite government efforts to regain control. The full extent of casualties and military losses remains unverified, raising concerns about the operational costs for the Somali National Army.
