Qatar Charity Opens Health Center in Somalia to Serve 50,000 People - Qatar news agency

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 17:28:52 GMT

Qatar Charity has inaugurated the Sheikh Eid bin Mohammed Al-Thani Health Center in the Jarsabali district of Banadir region, Somalia, aimed at serving approximately 50,000 residents and displaced individuals. The 2,000-square-meter facility features maternity, emergency, and isolation units, along with radiology, medical laboratories, vaccination services, and a pharmacy, all powered by solar energy to ensure continuous operation. Dr. Hassan Mohamed Hussein, Governor of Banadir Region and Mayor of Mogadishu, commended Qatar Charity for its support, highlighting the center's role in enhancing local healthcare and alleviating the challenges faced by families without access to medical services. This initiative is part of Qatar Charity's broader health projects in the region, which include recent cardiac procedures for children and plans for Somalia's first open-heart surgery campaign.
