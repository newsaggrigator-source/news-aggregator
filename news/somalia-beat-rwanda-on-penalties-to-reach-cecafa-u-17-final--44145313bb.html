Somalia beat Rwanda on penalties to reach CECAFA U-17 final - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 10:09:48 GMT

Somalia's U-17 football team triumphed over Rwanda in a thrilling penalty shootout, securing their place in the CECAFA U-17 Championship final. The semifinal match, held at Kigali Pele Stadium, ended in a 1-1 draw after regulation time. Rwanda initially took the lead with a goal from substitute Patrick Ujeneza, who skillfully maneuvered past Somali defenders. However, Somalia quickly equalized through Mohamud Osman, capitalizing on a defensive error. The match proceeded to penalties, where Somalia emerged victorious with a 5-4 score. Coach Dadir Ami Ali expressed his pride in the team's resilience and focus, emphasizing their preparation for the upcoming final against Uganda, who had already qualified. This victory marks another significant achievement for Somalia, the reigning champions from 2022, as they aim for a second consecutive title. Meanwhile, Rwanda will compete against Ethiopia for third place, with the winner earning a spot in the 2027 CAF U-17 Africa Cup of Nations.
