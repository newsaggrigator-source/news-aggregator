Diversion in Mogadishu: Why Somalia’s President Picked a Fight With the UAE - Kenyan Foreign Policy

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 20:13:39 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has escalated tensions with the United Arab Emirates (UAE) by ordering the expulsion of its ambassador and recalling Somalia's envoy from Abu Dhabi. This diplomatic rift, which the president claims is due to UAE's violations of Somalia's sovereignty and unauthorized agreements with regional administrations, appears to be a strategic distraction from domestic issues. Mohamud's presidency is facing a constitutional crisis following controversial amendments that extended his term and abolished the prime minister's position, leading to violent clashes and a standoff with the judiciary. Additionally, his administration is grappling with corruption scandals and governance failures, ranking poorly on Transparency International's index. Amidst these challenges, Mohamud has sought closer ties with Saudi Arabia, raising concerns about entanglement in regional rivalries. Critics argue that blaming the UAE will not resolve Somalia's internal divisions, which are rooted in local political dynamics. (158 words)
