Alliance for Survival, Amhara Fano Leaders Urge UN Envoy to Facilitate Inclusive Political Dialogue in Ethiopia

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 05:32:24 GMT

Leaders from the Alliance for Survival and the Amhara Fano National Movement (AFNM) have called on the United Nations to facilitate an inclusive political dialogue in Ethiopia. During a conversation with UN Special Envoy Guang Cong on November 14, they emphasized the need for a credible peace process that addresses the existential threats faced by the Amhara population and the broader Ethiopian communities. Zemene Kassie and Asres Mare articulated their alliance's position on the ongoing conflicts, urging the UN to ensure that all Ethiopian communities are represented in the dialogue. They accused the federal government of launching military campaigns against the Amhara region and called for an independent investigation into alleged war crimes. The leaders also highlighted the humanitarian crisis resulting from the conflict, which has displaced millions and disrupted essential services. They reiterated their commitment to defending the rights of the Amhara people while seeking a political resolution to the ongoing violence.
