Satellite images reveal the UAE transformed civilian airports in breakaway regions of Somalia into large military facilities. Flight tracking data shows the U.S. has made visits. Our visual investigations reporter Christoph Koettl explains. - Facebook

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 17:41:58 GMT

Satellite imagery has uncovered that the United Arab Emirates (UAE) has converted civilian airports in the breakaway regions of Somalia into substantial military installations. These transformations indicate a significant shift in the use of these facilities, which were originally intended for civilian air traffic. Additionally, flight tracking data suggests that the United States has conducted visits to these military sites, raising questions about the nature of U.S. involvement in the region. The findings highlight the strategic interests of the UAE in Somalia and the broader implications for regional security dynamics. This investigation, led by visual reporter Christoph Koettl, sheds light on the evolving military landscape in Somalia and the potential geopolitical ramifications of foreign military presence in the area. The transformation of these airports underscores the ongoing complexities in the Horn of Africa, where military and civilian operations increasingly intersect.
