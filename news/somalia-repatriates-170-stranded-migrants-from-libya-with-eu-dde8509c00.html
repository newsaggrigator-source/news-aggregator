Somalia repatriates 170 stranded migrants from Libya with EU support - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 06:52:39 GMT

On November 11, 2026, Somalia successfully repatriated 170 migrants from Libya, facilitated by a European Union-supported voluntary return program. The returnees, including children and young adults, had faced severe humanitarian challenges during their irregular migration journeys and while stranded in Libya, where they encountered exploitation and harsh detention conditions. Somali Interior Minister Ali Yusuf Ali Hoosh highlighted the government's commitment to assist these individuals as they reintegrate into society, offering medical care, employment opportunities, and other support services. The EU emphasized the operation's safe and dignified nature, aiming to protect migrants from trafficking and abuse. Hoosh also urged Somali youth to avoid perilous migration routes, warning of the risks involved. This repatriation is part of ongoing efforts by the Somali government and international partners to assist vulnerable citizens abroad and facilitate their safe return home. Libya remains a significant transit point for migrants seeking to reach Europe, often facing numerous dangers along the way.
