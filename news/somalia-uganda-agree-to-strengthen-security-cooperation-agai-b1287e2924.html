Somalia, Uganda agree to strengthen security cooperation against al-Shabab - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 05:10:51 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni have agreed to enhance military cooperation against the al-Shabab militant group during a recent meeting in Entebbe, Uganda. They emphasized the need for intensified military operations and sustainable funding for the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), especially as Uganda begins a phased withdrawal of its troops. The leaders called for increased offensive actions by AUSSOM and the Somali National Armed Forces while exploring alternative financing for military operations. They also highlighted the importance of political reconciliation within Somalia amid ongoing disputes over constitutional amendments and election timelines. Additionally, the two presidents discussed strengthening economic ties, including a bilateral labor agreement and potential trade facilitation projects. They reaffirmed their commitment to regional integration and called for continued international support for Somalia's security and humanitarian efforts. The meeting concluded with an invitation for Museveni to visit Somalia, which he accepted. (158 words)
