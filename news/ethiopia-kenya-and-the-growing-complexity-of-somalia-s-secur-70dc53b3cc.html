Ethiopia, Kenya and the Growing Complexity of Somalia’s Security Crisis - horn review

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 15:58:26 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's recent visit to Kenya highlighted the complex security dynamics in the Horn of Africa, particularly concerning Somalia. While attending Kenya's Mashujaa Day celebrations, Abiy underscored the importance of bilateral cooperation in areas like trade and infrastructure. However, Somalia's internal political turmoil, especially in the South West State, complicates relations between Ethiopia and Kenya. The removal of regional president Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen has led to ongoing confrontations, raising questions about foreign military involvement in Somalia's domestic disputes. Ethiopia aims to maintain its security commitments without getting entangled in political conflicts, as its military presence is crucial for countering threats from Al Shabaab. Both Ethiopia and Kenya face challenges in balancing their diplomatic ties with Somalia against their national security interests, particularly as instability in Somalia poses risks to regional security and economic initiatives.
