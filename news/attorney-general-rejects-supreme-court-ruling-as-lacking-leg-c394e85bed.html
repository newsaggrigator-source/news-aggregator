Attorney General Rejects Supreme Court Ruling as "Lacking Legal Basis" in Deepening Constitutional Standoff - Somali News today Live - FTL Somalia

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 15:04:09 GMT

Somalia's Attorney General has rejected a Supreme Court ruling regarding a constitutional case, labeling it as "lacking any legal basis." This rejection escalates the ongoing standoff between the executive and judiciary branches. In a statement on November 6, the Attorney General criticized the court for procedural violations, claiming it failed to address legal objections raised by the government and improperly ruled on issues not included in the original petition. The Supreme Court had previously declared the mandates of the President and Parliament expired in 2026 and ordered elections within 90 days. Following the ruling, government troops were deployed around the Supreme Court, raising concerns about the safety of the Chief Justice and court operations. The political crisis is compounded by the withdrawal of international support and a lack of mediation for the electoral dispute. Analysts suggest that the ruling may not be enforced, indicating a troubling trend of diminishing judicial authority in Somalia.
