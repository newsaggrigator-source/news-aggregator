Ethiopia, Djibouti, and Somalia secure €20 million EU partnership for regional trade corridors - CapitalEthiopia

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 19:19:28 GMT

Ethiopia, Djibouti, and Somalia have secured a €20 million partnership with the European Union (EU) aimed at enhancing regional trade corridors in the Horn of Africa. The agreement, signed during the 2026 Africa Trade and Development Forum in Addis Ababa, focuses on improving green transport, digital trade, and overall connectivity. Set to commence in 2027, the four-year initiative will modernize freight transportation systems and bolster digital trade infrastructure, particularly benefiting agricultural exports. Ethiopia’s Minister of Trade, Yasmin Wohabrebbi, emphasized that this partnership aligns with ongoing economic reforms to enhance trade facilitation and border management. The project aims to streamline logistics services and improve commercial corridors linking Ethiopia with its neighbors. By addressing logistical challenges, the initiative seeks to expand access to international markets, thereby boosting regional commerce and economic integration in the Horn of Africa, where transport costs and border procedures have historically hindered competitiveness.
