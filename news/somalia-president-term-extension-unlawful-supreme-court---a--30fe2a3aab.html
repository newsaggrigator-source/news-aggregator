Somalia president term extension 'unlawful': Supreme Court - A News

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 13:52:07 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court declared the one-year extension of President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term as "unlawful," following violent clashes with opposition groups. The court stated that constitutional amendments passed by parliament in March, which extended the presidential term from four to five years, cannot be applied retroactively to Mohamud's current term. In response to the ruling, Mohamud announced the dismissal of the chief justice, citing concerns about the judiciary's efficiency and fairness. However, the Supreme Court rejected this dismissal, reinforcing its authority. The ruling has significant implications for Somalia's political landscape, as it underscores the judiciary's role in upholding constitutional law amidst ongoing tensions between the government and opposition factions. The situation remains volatile, with potential repercussions for governance and stability in the country.
