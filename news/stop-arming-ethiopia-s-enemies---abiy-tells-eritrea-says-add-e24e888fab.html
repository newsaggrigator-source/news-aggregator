'Stop Arming Ethiopia's Enemies' - Abiy Tells Eritrea, Says Addis Abeba Seeks Red Sea Access Through Dialogue

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 05:11:50 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has urged Eritrea to cease its support for armed groups opposing Ethiopia, warning that such actions jeopardize peaceful relations between the two nations. Speaking to the House of Peoples' Representatives, Abiy emphasized Ethiopia's desire for cooperation and shared development with Eritrea, while calling for an end to Eritrea's alleged military assistance to Ethiopia's enemies. He highlighted Ethiopia's efforts to engage Eritrea in joint projects, including infrastructure development, which have been rejected by Asmara. Abiy also addressed Ethiopia's strategic interests in accessing the Red Sea, asserting that the country seeks peaceful negotiations rather than military confrontation. Additionally, he acknowledged ongoing internal security challenges posed by armed groups in various regions, advocating for dialogue and cooperation to resolve conflicts. Abiy reaffirmed the government's commitment to restoring peace and services in Tigray and criticized certain Tigrayan leaders for prolonging unrest at the expense of the population.
