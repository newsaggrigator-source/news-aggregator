President Samia Arrives in Ethiopia Ahead of AU Maternal Health Roadmap Launch

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 05:31:38 GMT

Tanzanian President Samia Suluhu Hassan has arrived in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, for a two-day visit ahead of the launch of the African Union's roadmap aimed at reducing preventable maternal and newborn deaths. The roadmap, which President Samia will unveil during the Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa, focuses on three key priorities known as the "Three Zeros": zero home deliveries, zero preventable maternal and newborn deaths, and zero unvaccinated children. Africa currently accounts for about 70% of global maternal deaths and nearly half of child deaths under five. The roadmap emphasizes the need for improved healthcare access, political commitment, and adequate financing to address these issues. It also highlights Tanzania's progress in reducing maternal mortality and increasing facility-based births. The conference, running from November 23 to 27, 2026, will gather leaders and health professionals to discuss Africa's health priorities under the theme of enhancing health security and reducing dependency on external aid.
