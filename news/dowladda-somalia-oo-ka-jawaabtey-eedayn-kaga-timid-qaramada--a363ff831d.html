Dowladda Somalia oo ka jawaabtey eedayn kaga timid Qaramada Midoobey. - Horseed Media

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 04:09:49 GMT

Dowladda Soomaaliya ayaa si kulul uga jawaabtay eedeymo ay ka timid khubaro madax-bannaan oo ka tirsan Qaramada Midoobay, kuwaas oo walaac ka muujiyay xil ka qaadista Guddoomiyihii hore ee Maxkamadda Sare, Baashe Yuusuf Axmed, iyo saxnaanta muddo kordhinta Madaxweynaha Xasan Sheekh Maxamuud. War-saxaafadeed ka soo baxay Wasaaradda Cadaaladda ayaa sheegay in khubarada QM ay warbixintooda baahiyeen iyaga oo aan ka helin jawaab rasmi ah dowladda, taas oo ay Wasaaradda ku tilmaantay mid ka hor imaanaysa habraacyada shaqo ee khubarada. Xil ka qaadista Baashe Yuusuf Axmed ayaa dhacday 4-tii November, iyadoo Maxkamadda Sare ay gacanta ku haysay dacwad dastuuri ah. Khubarada QM ayaa ku baaqay in xilka loo soo celiyo, iyagoo sheegay in xil ka qaadista aan loo marin habraacyadii dastuuriga ahaa. Wasaaradda Cadaaladda ayaa difaacday habka xil ka qaadista, iyadoo sheegtay in go'aanka maxkamadda aan loo aqoonsanayn mid sharci ah. (159 words)
