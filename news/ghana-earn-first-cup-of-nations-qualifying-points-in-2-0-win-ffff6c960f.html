Ghana earn first Cup of Nations qualifying points in 2-0 win over Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 17:11:19 GMT

Ghana secured their first points in the Africa Cup of Nations qualifying campaign with a 2-0 victory over Somalia in Sekondi. Despite facing a lowly-ranked opponent, Ghana struggled initially, with captain Jordan Ayew's penalty saved in the 14th minute. However, Jonas Adjetey scored with a header just before halftime, giving Ghana the lead. Felix Afena-Gyan added a second goal shortly after the break, solidifying the win. This match followed Ghana's disappointing start to the qualifiers, where they lost their first two games, leading to the dismissal of coach Carlos Queiroz. Other notable results included the Democratic Republic of Congo defeating Sierra Leone 3-1, Cameroon beating Namibia 2-0, and Senegal overcoming Sudan 4-1. The qualifiers continue with more matches scheduled, as teams aim for a spot in the finals co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda next year.
