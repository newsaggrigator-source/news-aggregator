Somalia Could Catch 800,000 Tonnes of Tuna Worth $2 Billion a Year, Fisheries Chief Says - Dawan Africa

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 11:07:54 GMT

Somalia has the potential to catch up to 800,000 tonnes of tuna annually, valued between $1 billion and $2 billion, according to Abdi Dirshe, the director general of the fisheries ministry. Speaking during Somalia’s National Fisheries Week, Dirshe emphasized that this figure pertains solely to tuna and does not account for other fish species. He highlighted Somalia's extensive coastline and abundant marine resources as significant opportunities for investors. Dirshe noted that investments in fishing boats and cold storage facilities are crucial, as fish spoil quickly without refrigeration. He suggested that building a cold storage facility with a capacity of 1,000 tonnes could be lucrative. Currently, fish represent only about 5% of Somalia's exports, with livestock dominating at 73%. The fisheries sector presents an untapped market that could enhance the country's economy and create jobs, particularly in logistics and retail.
