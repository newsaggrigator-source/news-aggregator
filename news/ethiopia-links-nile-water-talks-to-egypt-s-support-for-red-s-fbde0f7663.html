Ethiopia Links Nile Water Talks to Egypt’s Support for Red Sea Access - https://kulu-media.com/

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 09:57:50 GMT

Ethiopia's Water Minister, Habtamu Itefa, has linked negotiations over Nile water management to Egypt's support for Ethiopia's access to the Red Sea, complicating an already tense diplomatic relationship. In a recent interview, he suggested that cooperation on maritime access could foster understanding regarding the Nile dispute. Ethiopia's ambitions for Red Sea access are seen as strategic, especially since it has been landlocked since Eritrea's independence in 1993. Habtamu criticized Egypt's approach to Nile negotiations, arguing that Ethiopia's environmental efforts benefit downstream countries and that Egypt should reconsider its own water-intensive agricultural practices. Meanwhile, Egypt and Sudan have emphasized the need for a legally binding framework to protect their water security, rejecting unilateral Ethiopian actions. The ongoing disagreements highlight the complexities of regional diplomacy, as both nations struggle to reconcile their competing interests over water and maritime access, with no clear resolution in sight. The situation remains precarious amid rising tensions in the Horn of Africa.
