All of Africa Today - October 28, 2026

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 07:38:00 GMT

Marry Mubaiwa, the ex-wife of Zimbabwe's Vice-President Constantino Chiwenga, has been released after a seven-year trial delay concerning allegations of attempting to impede his medical treatment. A magistrate ruled that the prolonged proceedings caused her significant mental, physical, and financial harm. Mubaiwa faced accusations of attempting to kill Chiwenga while he was hospitalized in South Africa. Although her lawyer welcomed the ruling, they argued that her rights had been violated during the lengthy trial. Meanwhile, in Nigeria, an inquiry revealed that overcrowding and poor conditions led to the deaths of 37 detainees, prompting calls for legal action against those responsible. In the Democratic Republic of Congo, Ebola cases have surged past 10,000, marking the fastest-growing outbreak in history. Additionally, Egypt's independent news outlet Mada Masr has been ordered to close, raising concerns about press freedom, while conflict in Ethiopia has displaced around 36,000 people into Kenya.
