China calls US-backed Israel its friend despite earlier warnings over its recognition of Somaliland and military strikes on Iran - Business Insider Africa

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 20:00:59 GMT

In a surprising diplomatic shift, China has labeled Israel as a "friend" and "important partner," despite previously condemning Israel's military actions against Iran and its recognition of Somaliland. This change comes after months of tension, particularly following Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's recognition of Somaliland in December 2025, which China opposed, citing Somalia's sovereignty. Chinese Ambassador to Israel, Xiao Junzheng, emphasized China's neutrality in the Israel-Iran conflict while highlighting ongoing economic cooperation, including investments in Israeli infrastructure. Despite condemning US-Israeli strikes on Iran, China appears willing to prioritize economic interests over political disagreements. The remarks reflect China's complex diplomatic balancing act, as it navigates relationships with both Israel and Iran while maintaining its stance on issues of sovereignty in the Horn of Africa. This development underscores China's strategic interests in the region, particularly given its military base in Djibouti, near Somaliland.
