Scintillating Black Stars beat Somalia in Sekondi - Ghana Football Association

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 20:39:16 GMT

The Black Stars of Ghana secured a convincing 2-0 victory over Somalia in their Africa Cup of Nations qualifying match held at the Sekondi Sports Stadium. Despite an early setback when captain Jordan Ayew missed a penalty, Ghana quickly regained control. Jonas Adjetey netted his first international goal just after halftime, giving Ghana a 1-0 lead. The team showcased strong midfield dominance, particularly through players like Thomas Partey and Mohammed Kudus, who consistently troubled the Somali defense. Felix Afena-Gyan extended the lead to 2-0 in the 62nd minute following a well-coordinated play. Although Somalia managed a late shot on target, it posed no threat to Ghana's goalkeeper, Benjamin Asare. This victory marks Ghana's first points in the qualifying campaign after previous losses to Cote D’Ivoire and The Gambia. The Black Stars are set to face Somalia again on November 16, 2026, in their next qualifying match. (159 words)
