Somalia warns media outlets over alleged terrorist propaganda - Shabelle Media Network

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 06:41:03 GMT

Somalia's government has issued a warning to media outlets regarding the dissemination of content that could be perceived as terrorist propaganda. Authorities are concerned that certain reports may inadvertently support or glorify extremist groups, particularly Al-Shabaab. The government emphasized the importance of responsible journalism and urged media organizations to adhere to ethical standards when reporting on security issues. Officials highlighted the potential consequences of spreading such narratives, which could undermine national security efforts. The warning reflects ongoing challenges in combating terrorism and the role of media in shaping public perception. The government is calling for collaboration with media entities to ensure accurate and constructive reporting.
