Somalia’s Currency Crisis Exposes Failures in Central Bank Leadership - Garowe Online

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 22:51:49 GMT

Somalia's prolonged currency crisis has raised significant concerns regarding the effectiveness of the Central Bank's leadership under Governor Abdirahman Mohamed Abdullahi, who has been in office since April 2019. Despite efforts to restructure the institution and enhance banking supervision, the Somali shilling remains largely absent from the economy, which heavily relies on the U.S. dollar. The Central Bank has not issued new shilling banknotes since 1991, and existing notes are often counterfeit. Abdullahi acknowledges the challenges faced, including limited resources and expertise, but emphasizes the need for a dual-currency system to reintroduce the shilling. However, he has not provided a clear timeline for this initiative. The Central Bank's reliance on international assistance complicates its ability to independently manage monetary policy. As Somalia continues to grapple with its currency crisis, the gap between institutional reforms and the core responsibilities of the Central Bank remains a pressing issue, highlighting the need for measurable progress and accountability.
