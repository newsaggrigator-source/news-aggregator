SCAA Chief Says Somalia Integrating Regional Safety Procedures as Airspace Modernization Continues - FTL Somalia

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 16:21:48 GMT

The Somali Civil Aviation Authority (SCAA) is working to align its aviation security procedures with the East African Community Civil Aviation Safety and Security Oversight Agency (EAC-CASSOA) standards, as announced by SCAA Director General Ahmed Moallin Hassan. This integration aims to enhance the safety and quality of Somali civil aviation by harmonizing national protocols with regional requirements. Following Somalia's admission to the East African Community, discussions with EAC-CASSOA have focused on improving cooperation in civil aviation. While the SCAA has made significant strides in rebuilding its aviation sector, including generating nearly $20 million in air navigation services, challenges remain. Somalia is still on the European Union Aviation Safety Agency's conflict zone list due to security threats, which complicates operational realities despite procedural advancements. Additionally, concerns about the SCAA's financial transparency could undermine its credibility in implementing these new safety measures.
