Ethiopia Tells UN Envoy Eritrea, Sudan Backing Armed Groups and Drone Attacks

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 15:24:58 GMT

Ethiopia has accused Eritrea and Sudan of supporting armed groups within its borders, including facilitating drone attacks. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's adviser, Getachew K. Reda, made these allegations during a meeting with UN Special Envoy Guang Cong, where they discussed regional conflicts, particularly in Sudan and Somalia. Getachew urged both Eritrea and Sudan to cease their support for these groups to foster dialogue and prevent further escalation. The accusations come amid rising tensions, marked by reciprocal expulsions of diplomatic staff between Ethiopia and Sudan, as both nations accuse each other of backing insurgents. Ethiopia has also recently closed its embassy in Eritrea, citing hostile actions from Asmara. The diplomatic situation is further complicated by Sudan's complaints to the UN regarding Ethiopia's alleged facilitation of drone attacks by the Rapid Support Forces. Both countries deny the allegations against them, highlighting the increasingly volatile dynamics in the Horn of Africa. (159 words)
