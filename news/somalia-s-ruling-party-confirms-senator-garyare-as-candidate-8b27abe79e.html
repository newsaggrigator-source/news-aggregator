Somalia’s Ruling Party Confirms Senator Garyare as Candidate for Galmudug Deputy Leader - Dawan Africa

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 04:36:50 GMT

Somalia's ruling party has officially confirmed Senator Abdiqadir Mohamed Nur Garyare as its candidate for the position of Deputy Leader of Galmudug. The announcement comes as part of the party's strategy to strengthen its influence in the region. Senator Garyare, who has previously held various political roles, is expected to play a significant role in the governance of Galmudug if elected. The party's decision reflects its commitment to promoting experienced leaders in key positions to enhance stability and development in the area. The election for the Deputy Leader is anticipated to take place soon, with Garyare's candidacy likely to attract considerable attention and support from party members and constituents alike.
