Kenya to strengthen border security over fighting in Ethiopia — Nation Kenya - UA.NEWS

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 00:34:28 GMT

Kenya is set to enhance security along its border with Ethiopia due to ongoing violence in the Moyale region, as stated by Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja. The conflict, which has been raging for two weeks between Ethiopian forces from the Oromia and Somali regions, has led to thousands of Ethiopians fleeing to Kenya for safety. Stray bullets and rocket-propelled grenades have also forced numerous Kenyan families to evacuate their homes, particularly in areas like Somare, Sessi, and Lami. Kanja announced plans to allocate additional resources and personnel to safeguard Kenyan territory and facilitate the safe evacuation of stranded citizens. The number of displaced individuals has surged from 36,000 to over 50,000, prompting local leaders to urge increased patrols to prevent the conflict from spilling into Kenya. Kanja visited a temporary shelter for displaced Ethiopians, promising to bolster security and support humanitarian efforts in the region.
