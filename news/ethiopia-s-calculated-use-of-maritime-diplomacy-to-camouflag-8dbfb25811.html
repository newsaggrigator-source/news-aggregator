Ethiopia’s Calculated Use of Maritime Diplomacy to Camouflage its Irredentist Ambitions in the Region - Eritrea Ministry Of Information

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 14:19:07 GMT

Ethiopia's recent maritime diplomacy is viewed as a strategic maneuver to obscure its irredentist ambitions in the Horn of Africa, particularly concerning Eritrea. The Eritrean Ministry of Information argues that Ethiopia's Prosperity Party (PP) is using international forums to legitimize its claims for "sovereign sea access," despite being a landlocked nation. This includes hosting maritime security workshops while bypassing the recognized sovereignty of Somalia. The PP's rhetoric suggests a belief that Ethiopia's large population and economic pressures justify territorial expansion, which Eritrea firmly contests. The article emphasizes that international law does not support Ethiopia's unilateral claims over Eritrean ports or military presence on foreign shores. Eritrea maintains that its ports are open for mutually beneficial use, provided its sovereignty is respected. The article warns that the international community must critically assess Ethiopia's intentions, as its aggressive rhetoric poses a threat to regional stability and the principles of international law.
