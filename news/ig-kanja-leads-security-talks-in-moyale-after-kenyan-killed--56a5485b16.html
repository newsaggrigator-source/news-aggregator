IG Kanja leads security talks in Moyale after Kenyan killed in Ethiopia clashes - Citizen Digital

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 19:08:04 GMT

Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja led security discussions in Moyale following the death of a Kenyan woman amid ongoing clashes between armed groups from the Oromo and Somali communities in Ethiopia. The violence, which has persisted since October 22, has resulted in significant unrest, prompting over 50,000 people to flee into Kenya for safety. During his visit, Kanja reassured residents about their security and emphasized the government's commitment to diplomatic efforts aimed at resolving the conflict. A high-level security meeting was convened, involving senior officials and humanitarian agencies, to address the deteriorating situation and the urgent need for stability along the border. The clashes have disrupted cross-border transport, stranding numerous truck drivers and affecting trade. Kanja praised the efforts of humanitarian organizations in supporting displaced individuals and reiterated the government's priority to restore security and normalcy in the region.
