GCC backs UAE, rejects Somalia’s allegations of interference - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 22:33:42 GMT

The Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) has expressed its support for the United Arab Emirates (UAE) while rejecting allegations made by Somali officials regarding UAE interference in Somalia's internal affairs. In a statement released on November 19, GCC Secretary-General Jasem Mohamed Albudaiwi criticized the Somali government's claims as irresponsible and part of a misleading media campaign. He emphasized the UAE's contributions to regional security and international dialogue, citing its role in hosting peace talks between Russia and Ukraine. The statement follows escalating tensions between Somalia and the UAE, particularly after Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accused the UAE of supporting regional administrations and armed groups outside the federal government's control. In response, Somalia ordered the UAE ambassador to leave and recalled its own ambassador from Abu Dhabi. The GCC's statement serves to publicly defend the UAE and reiterate its commitment to regional stability, without imposing any diplomatic or economic measures against Somalia.
