Ethiopia Must Stop Allowing Egypt to Define the Argument - Borkena

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 13:52:15 GMT

Ethiopia is urged to redefine its diplomatic stance regarding the Nile dispute, which has been dominated by Egypt's narrative of survival. The article emphasizes that Ethiopia's development should not be viewed as a threat but rather as a necessity for its population of over 130 million. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's recent remarks at the Tana High-Level Forum highlight the importance of maritime access for Ethiopia's security and development, framing it as a mutual benefit for regional partners. The author argues that Egypt's historical advantages should not translate into perpetual control over Ethiopia's economic choices. A balanced approach is essential, recognizing both countries' needs and fostering cooperation rather than conflict. The article calls for Ethiopia to present its case with clarity and evidence, advocating for equitable water governance that respects the interests of all basin countries. Ultimately, a collaborative strategy is necessary to ensure regional stability and shared prosperity. (151 words)
