Somalia seeks Indian visa processing facility in Mogadishu as countries strengthen diplomatic ties - Hiiraan Online

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 01:27:52 GMT

Somalia and India have signed a memorandum of understanding aimed at enhancing diplomatic relations and cooperation between the two nations. The agreement was formalized by Somalia's Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs, Hassan Mohamed Ali Nadaara, and India's Secretary for Consular Affairs, Sripriya Ranganathan. A key aspect of the discussions was Somalia's request for an Indian visa processing facility in Mogadishu, which would facilitate easier access to consular services for Somalis wishing to travel to India. The two countries also explored potential collaboration in medical tourism, given India's popularity among Somalis seeking specialized medical care. Additionally, India congratulated Somalia on its recent election to the United Nations Security Council, where it will serve a two-year term starting in January 2025. While the memorandum sets a framework for future political consultations, details on the timeline for these discussions or the establishment of the visa facility remain unspecified.
