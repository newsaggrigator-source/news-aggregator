The port of Assab and the risk of an Ethiopia-Eritrea war - Al Majalla

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 04:27:48 GMT

The port of Assab has become a focal point in the escalating tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea, with Ethiopia asserting that access to the Red Sea is an "existential" issue. The historical context of the dispute dates back to colonial times, with Assab symbolizing Ethiopia's loss of direct maritime access following Eritrea's independence in 1993. Ethiopia's population and economy have grown significantly, increasing its need for diversified trade routes beyond its reliance on Djibouti. Recent developments, including Ethiopia's military buildup near the Eritrean border and the breakdown of diplomatic relations, have heightened fears of conflict. Ethiopia's Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has framed the issue as a historical grievance, while Eritrea views Assab as integral to its sovereignty. The situation is further complicated by regional dynamics, including foreign interests from countries like the UAE and Israel. The potential for war poses risks not only to the two nations but also to regional stability, particularly affecting Sudan amidst its ongoing civil conflict.
