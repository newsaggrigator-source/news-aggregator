Somalia, Spain agree to explore formal political consultations - Shabelle Media Network

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 08:52:07 GMT

Somalia and Spain have agreed to initiate formal political consultations to strengthen their bilateral relations. The agreement was reached during a meeting between Somali Foreign Minister Abshir Omar Jama and Spanish Ambassador to Somalia, José Antonio Sainz. Both parties expressed their commitment to enhancing cooperation in various sectors, including security, trade, and development. The discussions also aimed to address mutual interests and challenges faced by both nations. This initiative marks a significant step in fostering diplomatic ties and collaboration between Somalia and Spain, reflecting a shared desire for stability and progress in the region.
