Somalia: Mogadishu’s Daynile district gripped by deadly clashes amid forced evictions - Somali Guardian

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 18:03:16 GMT

Clashes erupted in Mogadishu's Daynile district as Somali police enforced forced evictions, leading to deadly confrontations with armed residents. The violence began on Sunday when police killed three protesters during an operation to demolish homes in the Warlaliska area, resulting in significant casualties and the destruction of numerous residences. Armed residents retaliated, ambushing police and engaging in intense gunfire, which forced officers to abandon their wounded colleagues. Videos circulated online showed residents aiding injured police officers. Former President Sheikh Ahmed urged residents to keep the wounded officers away from the government, while former Prime Minister Hassan Ali Khaire accused senior police officials of orchestrating the violence and evictions. The standoff continued into Monday, with both sides holding firm to their positions, although no further clashes were reported that day. The situation highlights ongoing tensions and instability in the region amid forced evictions and government actions.
