Ethiopia To Become Hub For Pharmaceutical Manufacturing In Africa - News Agency of Nigeria

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 19:11:41 GMT

Ethiopia is positioning itself to become a leading hub for pharmaceutical manufacturing in Africa, as announced by the State Minister for Health, Muluken Argaw. The country plans to leverage the African Continental Free Trade Area to enhance regional supply chains and regulatory cooperation, alongside implementing joint procurement mechanisms. These initiatives aim to boost local production of essential medicines, vaccines, and medical equipment, which are vital for health security across the continent. Argaw highlighted Ethiopia's ambition to not only serve its domestic market but also to expand opportunities for African-made health products. Additionally, the country is looking to develop medical tourism. Projections indicate that Ethiopia's pharmaceutical market could exceed $4 billion by 2030, reflecting its commitment to strengthening the healthcare sector and fostering economic growth through enhanced pharmaceutical capabilities.
