News - 13-Year-Old Girl Killed After Flag Dispute in Borana's Moyale - Protesters Block Transport Services

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 17:22:34 GMT

A 13-year-old girl was killed during a flag dispute in Moyale, Borana zone, Ethiopia, on October 18, 2026. The incident occurred when Somali regional forces raised their flag at a new administration office, leading to gunfire between them and Oromia local militias. Residents accuse Somali police of shooting the girl, while others claim she was caught in crossfire. Protests erupted following her death, disrupting transport services and closing businesses. Security officials attempted to mediate but failed to reach an agreement. The girl's uncle reported that her body had not been returned to the family, highlighting ongoing tensions in the region linked to territorial disputes between the Oromia and Somali communities. This incident is part of a broader pattern of violence in the area.
