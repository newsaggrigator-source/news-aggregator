African Leaders Call for Action, Domestic Investment to Advance Health Sovereignty

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 05:43:34 GMT

At the Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026) in Addis Ababa, African leaders emphasized the urgent need for political commitment and increased domestic investment to achieve health sovereignty across the continent. African Union Commission Chairperson Mahmoud Ali Youssouf highlighted that public health security is essential for Africa's self-determination in health matters. Tanzanian President Samia Suluhu Hassan and Sierra Leonean President Julius Maada Bio called for actionable steps to translate commitments into real progress, stressing the importance of national ownership and partnerships. Burundian President Évariste Ndayishimiye urged a shift from political promises to enhanced domestic health financing, asserting that Africa's health sovereignty relies on African investments. The conference, co-hosted by the Ethiopian government and Africa CDC, aims to transition Africa from dependence on external health systems to greater ownership of its health priorities and resources, reinforcing the continent's commitment to shaping its health future.
