Somalia Showcases Natural Resources and Investment Opportunities at International Trade Exhibition - Somali National News Agency

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 14:16:25 GMT

A high-level delegation from the Federal Government of Somalia, led by Minister of Commerce and Industry H.E. Jamal Mohamed Hassan, participated in an international trade exhibition aimed at showcasing the country's economic potential. The event highlighted Somalia's diverse natural resources, including metallic minerals, gemstones, and decorative stones, attracting international investors. The delegation emphasized the importance of these resources in promoting trade and investment opportunities within Somalia. This participation aligns with the Federal Government's broader strategy to strengthen international trade relations, encourage foreign direct investment, and enhance domestic production. By showcasing its natural resources, Somalia aims to position itself as an emerging destination for trade and investment, reaffirming its commitment to economic cooperation with global partners. The exhibition served as a platform for Somalia to demonstrate its potential and engage with international stakeholders interested in its growing economic sectors.
