Somalia expels UAE ambassador, gives him 72 hours to leave country - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 02:05:28 GMT

The Somali federal government has expelled the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador, giving him 72 hours to leave the country, and has recalled its own ambassador from Abu Dhabi for consultations. This decision, announced after a Cabinet meeting led by Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre, follows concerns over perceived threats to Somalia's sovereignty and national security. Information Minister Abdulfatah Qasim Mohamud stated that the government would take lawful measures against actions it views as violations of its territorial integrity. Tensions have escalated recently, with President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accusing the UAE of supporting armed groups and establishing military facilities that undermine the federal government. The UAE has denied these allegations, with diplomatic adviser Anwar Gargash criticizing the Somali leader for allowing personal politics to affect bilateral relations. The dispute has intensified since January, when Somalia annulled agreements with the UAE regarding security and economic cooperation, asserting that all foreign engagements must go through the federal government.
