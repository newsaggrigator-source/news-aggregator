PM Abiy Invites Russian Institutions to Partner With Ethiopia's AI University

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 05:09:54 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has invited Russian institutions to collaborate with the newly established Medemer University of Artificial Intelligence in Addis Ababa. Speaking at the 3rd Russia-Africa Summit, he highlighted the need for enhanced scientific cooperation and local technological capabilities, emphasizing that AI development should cater to Ethiopian needs. Abiy stated, "An African researcher should be able to create tools in the language of our people," advocating for local control over data usage. He also identified food security and energy, including plans for a nuclear power plant, as key areas for partnership. The Prime Minister underscored the importance of actionable cooperation, asserting that innovation must translate into production and trade. Reflecting on the historical ties between Ethiopia and Russia, he called for modern collaborations to be supported by clear action plans, asserting that true independence involves both the right to make decisions and the capability to implement them.
