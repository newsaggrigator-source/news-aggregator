Somalia Condemns Israel’s Plans for Somaliland Embassy - allAfrica.com

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 13:00:51 GMT

The Somali government has condemned Israel's plans to establish an embassy in Somaliland, asserting that this move violates Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity. The Foreign Ministry in Mogadishu declared the decision as legally null and void, emphasizing that Hargeisa and the northwestern regions are integral parts of Somalia. Somalia warned that Israel's actions could escalate security tensions in the region and stated its intention to pursue diplomatic, political, and legal measures to protect its territorial rights. This condemnation follows Israel's recognition of Somaliland in December 2025, marking a significant diplomatic shift since Somaliland declared self-governance nearly 35 years ago. Somaliland, however, has rejected Somalia's claims, asserting its right to establish foreign relations independently, including its ties with Israel. The situation highlights the ongoing complexities of sovereignty and diplomatic recognition in the Horn of Africa.
