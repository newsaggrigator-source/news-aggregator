As Tens of Thousands Flee Ethiopia to Kenya, UN Agency Warns of Growing Humanitarian Needs

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 07:42:14 GMT

The UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR) has reported a significant increase in humanitarian needs as tens of thousands flee Ethiopia to Kenya due to ongoing clashes. Since October 22, approximately 50,000 people have crossed the border at Moyale, with many being women and children. While some have found refuge with relatives or in host communities, others are sheltering in temporary locations like schools and churches. The most urgent needs identified include food, safe drinking water, emergency shelter, sanitation, and healthcare. UNHCR has deployed an emergency team to assist and is collaborating with various humanitarian partners to provide essential supplies and support. Efforts are also underway to find safer accommodations for those fleeing violence. The situation remains fluid, with ongoing assessments of protection and humanitarian needs as the crisis unfolds.
