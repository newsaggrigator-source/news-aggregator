Trump Asks Why US Takes Immigrants From 'Sh*thouse' Countries Like Somalia: 'Why Not Denmark?' - Yahoo

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 00:23:00 GMT

During a recent rally in Georgia, former President Donald Trump expressed his preference for immigrants from countries like Norway and Sweden, questioning why the U.S. accepts immigrants from what he referred to as "sh*thouse" countries, specifically mentioning Somalia. Trump’s comments reflect his ongoing stance on immigration, advocating for a shift towards immigrants from nations he perceives as more desirable. He contrasted these countries with Denmark, suggesting that the U.S. should prioritize immigrants from nations with higher standards of living. This rhetoric aligns with Trump's previous statements on immigration policy, which have often sparked controversy and debate regarding race and national origin. The remarks have drawn criticism from various quarters, highlighting the divisive nature of immigration discourse in American politics. Trump's comments continue to resonate with his base, as he positions himself for potential future political endeavors, including the 2024 presidential election.
