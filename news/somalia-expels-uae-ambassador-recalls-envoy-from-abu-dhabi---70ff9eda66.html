Somalia expels UAE ambassador, recalls envoy from Abu Dhabi - Peoples Gazette Nigeria

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 19:17:04 GMT

Somalia has expelled the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador, ordering him to leave within 72 hours, and has recalled its own envoy from Abu Dhabi for consultations. This decision was made during a Council of Ministers meeting in Mogadishu, following accusations that the UAE has undermined Somalia's sovereignty. Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud publicly criticized the UAE for interfering in Somalia's internal affairs and for signing port agreements in Berbera, Bosaso, and Kismayo without the Somali government’s approval. He also accused the UAE of establishing armed forces outside Somalia’s national security framework. In response, the UAE condemned Somalia's actions as escalatory and emphasized the need for bilateral relations to be based on mutual interests, free from political conflicts. The diplomatic tensions have been ongoing since January, when Somalia annulled all agreements with the UAE, including those related to ports and security.
