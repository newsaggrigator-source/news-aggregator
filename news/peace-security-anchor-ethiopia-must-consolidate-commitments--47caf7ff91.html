Peace-Security Anchor Ethiopia Must Consolidate Commitments to Peace and Security: Former PM - ENA English

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 17:13:04 GMT

Former Ethiopian Prime Minister Hailemariam Desalegn emphasized the importance of Ethiopia's role as a peace and security anchor in the region during the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa. He urged the country to strengthen its commitment to peace, highlighting Ethiopia's diplomatic efforts during the Sudan crisis and its ongoing involvement in Somalia as key examples. Hailemariam praised the Tana Forum for fostering open dialogue among African leaders, distinguishing it from other international platforms by encouraging candid discussions on pressing security challenges. He noted that the Forum has successfully provided actionable recommendations to the African Union over the past 12 years. Additionally, he pointed out Ethiopia's internal peace initiatives, stressing that both domestic and regional stability are crucial for the nation’s well-being. Hailemariam called for continued efforts in peacebuilding, advocating for dialogue and compromise to address underlying issues and achieve lasting peace across the region.
