Sodo Woreda Attacks Leave 11 Dead as Residents Plead for Federal Intervention - Borkena

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 16:49:08 GMT

In Sodo Woreda, a series of violent attacks have resulted in the deaths of 11 individuals, prompting local residents to urgently call for federal intervention. The violence has raised concerns about security and stability in the region, with community members expressing fear for their safety. Eyewitnesses report that the attacks were carried out by armed groups, leading to widespread panic among the population. Residents are appealing to the federal government to take immediate action to restore order and protect them from further violence. The situation remains tense as the community grapples with the aftermath of the attacks and seeks assistance to prevent future incidents.
