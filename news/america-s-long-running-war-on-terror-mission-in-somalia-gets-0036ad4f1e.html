America’s Long-Running War On Terror Mission In Somalia Gets Another Chapter - The Maine Wire

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 15:40:00 GMT

American military personnel are reportedly returning to Baledogle Airbase in Somalia, just weeks after a withdrawal due to a deteriorating runway. This move extends the U.S.'s long-standing counterterrorism mission in the region, particularly against the al-Shabab militant group. The return follows a new agreement between the American security contractor Bancroft Global Development and Somali authorities to train local special forces. Although AFRICOM has stated it is not directly involved with this contract, the presence of U.S. personnel at the airbase raises questions about the costs and implications of America's military engagement in Somalia. The situation reflects a broader trend of increasing U.S. military activity in East Africa, including renewed funding for the U.N. Mission in Somalia and discussions of enhanced military cooperation in the region, particularly in response to rising threats from groups like ISIS in Mozambique. Concerns about the oversight and accountability of contractors like Bancroft also persist.
