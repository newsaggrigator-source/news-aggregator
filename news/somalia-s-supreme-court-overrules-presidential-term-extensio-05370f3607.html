Somalia’s Supreme Court overrules presidential term extension - The EastAfrican

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 19:30:00 GMT

Somalia’s Supreme Court has overruled a controversial presidential term extension announced by President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, creating uncertainty about his administration. The court's decision, supported by five of the nine judges, mandates that political leaders devise an electoral plan within 30 days and conduct elections within 90 days. This ruling follows a lawsuit from opposition leaders, including former president Sheikh Sharif Ahmed, challenging the constitutional amendments that allowed the term extensions. While the court did not invalidate the amendments made in March, it deemed the extensions for both the presidency and parliament illegal. The ruling has intensified tensions between the presidency and the judiciary, especially after Mohamud dismissed Chief Justice Bashe Yusuf Ahmed just a day prior. The opposition continues to reject the proposed electoral model, advocating for the reinstatement of the old constitution. The situation remains fluid as the country grapples with political disputes and the need for a clear electoral process.
