A Mediator’s Outreach: Alkan Holds Talks with Farmaajo, Khaire, and Sheikh Sharif Ahead of November Forum - FTL Somalia

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 17:37:56 GMT

Türkiye’s Ambassador to Somalia, Ferhat Alkan, has engaged in discussions with key opposition figures, including former Presidents Mohamed Abdullahi Farmaajo and Sheikh Sharif Sheikh Ahmed, as well as former Prime Minister Hassan Ali Khaire, ahead of a significant political forum scheduled for November 14-18. These meetings aim to address Somalia's political crisis and emphasize the importance of dialogue. Alkan's outreach is part of Türkiye's broader role as a mediator, despite some opposition parties expressing distrust due to Türkiye's military support for the current government. The Quartet, comprising Türkiye, the U.S., the U.K., and the EU, is facilitating the upcoming dialogue to ensure broader participation and address concerns about impartiality. Sheikh Sharif's involvement is crucial, as he is part of the Somali Future Council, which is deliberating on attending the forum. The success of these diplomatic efforts remains uncertain, hinging on whether the opposition perceives the process as fair and inclusive.
