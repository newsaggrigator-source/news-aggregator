AU plans to take over four Somalia bases as Uganda withdraws - Somalia Today

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 22:49:06 GMT

The African Union (AU) plans to take over four military bases in Somalia—Sabid-Anole, Bariire, Awdheegle, and Bulonagad—as Uganda begins withdrawing its troops from the country. This transition is part of the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), which aims to maintain security in the capital, Mogadishu. Uganda's military announced that it has started the withdrawal process, with a complete exit expected by the end of 2027. The withdrawal raises concerns about the security vacuum left behind, particularly in Lower Shabelle, a region critical for protecting Mogadishu. Funding shortages have accelerated Uganda's departure, and the Somali army is urged to remain vigilant against Al-Shabaab, which continues to pose a threat despite international military efforts. The AU mission's current UN mandate allows for nearly 12,000 personnel, but funding and logistical support remain uncertain as the UN political mission in Somalia has recently closed.
