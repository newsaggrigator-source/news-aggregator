MP Urges Permanent 'Treason Law' Targeting Ethiopians Accused of Collaborating With Egypt

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 11:01:42 GMT

A member of Ethiopia's House of Peoples' Representatives, MP Kejela Merdassa, has called for the establishment of a permanent "treason law" aimed at punishing Ethiopians accused of collaborating with Egypt to undermine national sovereignty. This proposal was made during a parliamentary session where Kejela questioned Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed about regional security and Ethiopia's pursuit of maritime access. He argued that individuals receiving support from Egypt to destabilize Ethiopia should face treason charges. Kejela accused Egypt of waging an undeclared war against Ethiopia, particularly regarding water rights over the Blue Nile and other rivers, while also opposing Ethiopia's access to the Red Sea. His comments come amid rising tensions in the region, particularly between Ethiopia and Eritrea, and highlight concerns over security and diplomatic relations in the Horn of Africa. Other parliamentarians echoed worries about insecurity and civilian displacement, emphasizing the need for diplomatic engagement as tensions escalate.
