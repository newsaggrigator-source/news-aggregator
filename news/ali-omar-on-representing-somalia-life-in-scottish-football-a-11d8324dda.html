Ali Omar On Representing Somalia, Life In Scottish Football And Playing Across The UK - Yahoo Sports

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 15:00:00 GMT

Ali Omar, a defender for Dumbarton and a regular international player for Somalia, expressed immense pride in representing his country, highlighting the stark contrast between club and international football. He described the thrill of playing in front of large crowds and facing world-class players like Riyad Mahrez and those from Ivory Coast. Omar reflected on his journey, noting the challenges and growth he experienced at clubs like Stevenage and QPR, as well as the setbacks from injuries during his time at Larne. He emphasized the importance of resilience and adaptability in football, particularly in the unique environment of Scottish football. Omar also cherishes his connection with Dumbarton fans, appreciating their support and aiming to give back to the community. Looking ahead, he is excited about his career's next phase and aims to help Somalia qualify for major tournaments.
