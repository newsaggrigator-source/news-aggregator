Villa Somalia oo dalacsiin ciidan ku raadineysa taageerada beesha Xawaadle xili kacdoon ka jiro Hiiraan - Garowe Online

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 20:39:58 GMT

Villa Somalia, led by President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, is reportedly promoting military personnel from the Hiiraan region, specifically from the Xawaadle clan, amid rising political and security tensions in Beledweyne. This military promotion is seen as an effort to quell unrest and opposition against the Hirshabelle administration and the federal government. Sources indicate that some individuals have been elevated from lower ranks to positions as high as General, including civilians not previously affiliated with the military. However, the total number of those promoted and their identities remain undisclosed, as does the official process behind these promotions. The situation is further complicated by ongoing political disputes following the recent Hirshabelle elections, which have faced rejection from various local leaders. Additionally, the Somali Supreme Court has ruled that President Mohamud's term ended on May 15, 2026, prompting calls for a political agreement on upcoming elections.
