Somalia: Puntland President Warns of Threat to Somalia’s Federal System - Horseed Media

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 14:23:09 GMT

Puntland President Said Abdullahi Deni has raised alarms about the current political climate in Somalia, asserting that the federal government is undermining the federal system established to ensure stability and governance. Speaking at the Puntland parliament's 59th session, Deni criticized the federal government for abandoning key political agreements, including the provisional constitution, and for rejecting consensus-based elections. He expressed concern that Somalia is entering a perilous phase in its state-building efforts, with allegations that federal security forces are contributing to instability and targeting officials. Deni emphasized that Puntland would not accept an election process dictated solely by the federal government in Mogadishu and called for a national conference to address ongoing disputes over elections and constitutional reforms. He vowed to communicate Puntland's position to both the Somali public and the international community, highlighting the need for a collaborative approach to safeguard the federal system and resolve political tensions.
