Great Ethiopian Run Marks 26 Years With Record 60,000 Runners, Showcasing Ethiopia to the World

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 05:13:38 GMT

The Great Ethiopian Run celebrated its 26th anniversary with a record-breaking participation of over 60,000 runners in Addis Ababa. This year's event, recognized as Africa's largest 10-kilometer road race, attracted around 200 international participants from 25 countries, highlighting Ethiopia's prominence in long-distance running. Launched in 2001 by athletics legend Haile Gebrselassie, the run has evolved into a significant cultural and sporting event, promoting Ethiopian identity and unity through sport. The anniversary festivities featured music, cultural performances, and a vibrant atmosphere, with notable attendees including Addis Ababa's Mayor and government officials. Beyond athletics, the Great Ethiopian Run has fostered community bonds, supported social development through fundraising, and boosted tourism and local businesses. Its recent designation as a World Athletics Heritage event underscores its global significance and contribution to the sporting culture. Overall, the run exemplifies Ethiopia's rich running tradition and the unifying power of sports. (160 words)
