Ethiopia’s Strategic Recalibration Concurrent with Uganda’s Withdrawal from Somalia - horn review

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 16:59:18 GMT

Ethiopia is undergoing a strategic recalibration in response to Uganda's planned withdrawal from Somalia, which is set to conclude by the end of 2027. Uganda has been a crucial player in the African Union's military efforts in Somalia for nearly two decades, and its exit creates a security vacuum amid political instability in Mogadishu. This situation presents Ethiopia with an opportunity to reassess its military posture and alliances, particularly along its border with Somalia. The Ethiopian strategy involves reducing its forces under the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission (AUSSOM) while establishing a defensive zone within Somalia to counter Al Shabaab and limit external influences. Additionally, Ethiopia aims to strengthen ties with regional administrations like South West State and Jubaland, fostering local partnerships to enhance security. Uganda's shift towards closer relations with the UAE may also facilitate regional coordination on security matters. Ethiopia's ability to navigate this transition effectively will be crucial for its long-term stability and influence in the Horn of Africa.
