The United Nations mission in Somalia has ended after more than 30 years of operation. - Vietnam.vn

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 15:08:18 GMT

The United Nations mission in Somalia officially concluded its operations on October 31, 2026, after over 30 years of involvement in the country. The mission, initially aimed at supporting the Somali government in establishing a democratic transition and rule of law, transitioned to the United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) in 2024. This change followed a proposal by Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud to end the mission earlier than planned. Despite the mission's conclusion, Raisedon Zenenga, the Acting Head of UNTMIS, emphasized that this marks the end of one phase and the beginning of another, with ongoing cooperation between the UN and Somalia. The responsibilities of the mission will now be transferred to Somali agencies and other UN entities operating in the region. The decision to end the mission comes amid a complex security situation, with the militant group Al-Shabaab continuing to control significant areas of Somalia despite military efforts against them.
