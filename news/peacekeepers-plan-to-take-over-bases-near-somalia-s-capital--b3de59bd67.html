Peacekeepers plan to take over bases near Somalia’s capital as Ugandan troops withdraw - WRAL

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 09:16:50 GMT

Peacekeepers are set to take over four bases near Mogadishu, Somalia, as Ugandan troops begin their withdrawal, which is expected to be completed by the end of 2027. This transition aims to prevent al-Shabab militants from exploiting the security vacuum left by the departing Ugandan forces. The withdrawal is accelerated by funding shortages and follows a bilateral agreement between Uganda and Somalia. The African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia will assume control of the bases—Sabid-Anole, Bariire, Awdheegle, and Bulonagad—during troop rotations scheduled for November. Somali army commander Maj. Gen. Ibrahim Mohamed Mohamud has urged troops to remain vigilant against al-Shabab, which has previously used the region to launch attacks and smuggle weapons. The withdrawal coincides with the closure of the United Nations’ political mission in Somalia, raising concerns about the country's security amid ongoing militant threats and political disputes.
