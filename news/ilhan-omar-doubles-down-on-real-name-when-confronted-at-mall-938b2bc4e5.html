Ilhan Omar doubles down on ‘real name’ when confronted at Mall of Somalia - Fox News

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 20:56:08 GMT

Rep. Ilhan Omar, a Democrat from Minnesota, reaffirmed that her "real name" is "Ilhan Abdullahi Omar" during a recent appearance at the Karmel Mall of Somalia. This statement comes in response to a federal court filing that identified her as "Ilhan Nur Said Elmi," a name associated with her ex-husband, Ahmed Nur Said Elmi, who has been alleged to be her brother. Omar insisted on her identity, stating, "It’s Ilhan Abdullahi Omar." The controversy arises amid an ongoing federal investigation into potential immigration fraud involving Omar and her family, which has included a raid on her Washington, D.C. residence by Homeland Security Investigations. The court filing has raised questions about the legitimacy of her name and could lead to scrutiny of her immigration status and documents. Omar's spokesperson has dismissed claims regarding her name and the alleged familial ties as "completely false" and a "disgusting conspiracy theory." The investigation continues, with subpoenas issued to her family members.
