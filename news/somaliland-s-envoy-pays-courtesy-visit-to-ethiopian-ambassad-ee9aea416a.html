Somaliland's Envoy Pays Courtesy Visit to Ethiopian Ambassador Teshome Shunde

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 17:17:45 GMT

Somaliland's newly appointed envoy to Ethiopia, Sharmarke Gelle, recently visited Ethiopian Ambassador Teshome Shunde at the Ethiopian mission in Somaliland. This courtesy visit marks Gelle's preparation to begin his diplomatic assignment in Addis Ababa. During the meeting, Gelle expressed gratitude for the warm reception and emphasized his commitment to enhancing the relationship between Somaliland and Ethiopia. He highlighted the significance of trust and mutual respect in sustaining diplomatic ties, stating that strong relations are built not just on geography but on the respect cultivated by diplomats. Gelle pledged to collaborate closely with Ethiopian officials to strengthen the enduring bonds of friendship between the two nations and their peoples. His remarks reflect a dedication to fostering cooperation and goodwill as he embarks on his new role.
