EU, Turkish Envoys Meet Opposition Figures as Hamza Declares “No International Community Left” - FTL Somalia

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 23:37:33 GMT

On November 19, 2026, a Senior Officials’ Meeting on Somalia’s security transition took place in Brussels, co-chaired by EU and Somali officials, to discuss the upcoming renewal of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission (AUSSOM) mandate. This meeting included representatives from various international partners, emphasizing the need for coordination ahead of the UN Security Council vote. Concurrently, EU and Turkish diplomats engaged with opposition figures in Mogadishu amid escalating political tensions. Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre, however, dismissed the relevance of international mediation, asserting that “no international community left” exists to facilitate dialogue, following the expulsion of the UAE ambassador and the dismantling of key international frameworks. His remarks highlight a contradiction, as the government seeks international support for security while rejecting external mediation in political disputes. This situation underscores the Somali Future Council's demand for international mediation, reflecting a broader crisis in Somalia's political landscape.
