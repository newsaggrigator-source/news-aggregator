President Hassan Sheikh Inaugurates Jamhuuriya Road in Mogadishu’s Kaaraan District - www.ftlsomalia.com

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:33:47 GMT

On November 18, 2026, President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud inaugurated the rehabilitated Jamhuuriya Road in Mogadishu's Kaaraan district, emphasizing the importance of federal infrastructure initiatives for national reconstruction. The ceremony marked the completion of vital roadworks aimed at improving transit corridors in the capital, which have suffered from years of conflict and flooding. During his address, President Mohamud urged citizens to engage actively in rebuilding efforts and called for solidarity against security threats. The reopening of Jamhuuriya Road is part of broader municipal rehabilitation projects targeting strategic transport links across Banadir. However, concerns remain regarding the long-term durability of these infrastructure projects, as many newly paved roads have deteriorated quickly due to inadequate drainage systems and limited municipal maintenance. Residents in districts like Kaaraan seek not only improved roads but also transparent governance and security against extortion and violence. The event highlights the ongoing challenges of urban development in Mogadishu amidst security and infrastructure deficits. (159 words)
