Trump posts meme tagging Ilhan Omar ‘scam from Somalia’ - Peoples Gazette Nigeria

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 22:26:03 GMT

Former President Donald Trump recently posted a controversial meme targeting Minnesota Congresswoman Ilhan Omar, referring to her as a "scam from Somalia." The meme featured a screenshot of an incoming phone call displaying Omar's image without her hijab, accompanied by the text "Scam likely from Mugadishu, Somalia." This post came shortly after Omar criticized Trump on social media, labeling him the "paedophile protection party’s president" and accusing him of promoting right-wing conspiracy theories through the Department of Justice. Omar's remarks were in response to Trump's call for the House speaker to declare her congressional seat vacant. The tension between the two escalated further following a recent FBI raid on Omar's home related to allegations of immigration fraud involving her and her former husband, Ahmed Nur Said Elmi, who is rumored to be her brother. The exchange highlights the ongoing political animosity and controversies surrounding both figures in the current political landscape.
