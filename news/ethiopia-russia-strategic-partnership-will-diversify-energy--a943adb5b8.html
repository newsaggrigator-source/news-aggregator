Ethiopia-Russia Strategic Partnership Will Diversify Energy Options - PM Abiy

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 05:04:53 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed emphasized the importance of the strategic partnership between Ethiopia and Russia during the 3rd Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow. He stated that this collaboration will diversify Ethiopia's energy options, accelerate national prosperity, and strengthen bilateral relations. Abiy highlighted the need for collective commitment and cooperation to transform historic decisions into tangible outcomes. He outlined Ethiopia's focus on three key pillars for sustainable development: food security, reliable energy supply, and the transfer of skills and knowledge. The Prime Minister noted that Ethiopia's diplomatic ties with Russia, rooted in mutual respect, have lasted over a century. To further enhance this partnership, Ethiopia is working with Russia to construct a world-class nuclear power plant, which is expected to play a significant role in the country's energy landscape. This initiative reflects Ethiopia's commitment to advancing its energy infrastructure and fostering economic growth.
