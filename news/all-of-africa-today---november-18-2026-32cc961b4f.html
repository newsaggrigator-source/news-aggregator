All of Africa Today - November 18, 2026

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 07:07:07 GMT

On November 18, 2026, significant developments across Africa were reported. South Africa reprimanded Nigeria's High Commissioner, Femi Fani-Kayode, over a dispute regarding the broadcasting of his credentials ceremony, emphasizing the need for diplomatic communication through proper channels. In Uganda, Winnie Byanyima called for the release of her husband, opposition leader Kizza Besigye, citing serious health concerns while he remains detained. These events reflect ongoing political tensions and humanitarian crises across the continent. (159 words)
