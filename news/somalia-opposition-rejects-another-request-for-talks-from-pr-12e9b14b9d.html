Somalia opposition rejects another request for talks from president Hassan Sheikh Mohamud - Somali Guardian

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 07:27:05 GMT

Somalia's opposition leaders have firmly rejected President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's latest request for talks, which he proposed through former Prime Minister Abdi Farah Shirdon. The president's offer included major concessions and aimed to establish a consensus for the country's future and upcoming elections. However, opposition members convened and unanimously decided against engaging with Mohamud, citing previous failed negotiations where he made no substantial concessions and insisted on a one-person, one-vote election model that they believe would secure him a third term. They demand that the election model be decided through a consensus involving all political stakeholders, alongside international mediation and security assurances for regional leaders. This refusal comes amid increasing international pressure for dialogue, as the political crisis deepens, with Somalia's Constitutional Court declaring Mohamud's term extension unlawful and ordering elections within 90 days. The standoff highlights the ongoing political, constitutional, and security challenges facing the nation.
