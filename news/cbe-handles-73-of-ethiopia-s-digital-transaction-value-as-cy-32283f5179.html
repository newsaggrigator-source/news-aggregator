CBE Handles 73% of Ethiopia’s Digital Transaction Value as Cybersecurity Spending Rises - Birr Metrics

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 14:09:12 GMT

The Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) is significantly enhancing its cybersecurity measures as it processes 73% of Ethiopia's annual digital transaction value, which amounts to over 21.9 trillion birr. With 92% of its transactions conducted digitally, CBE is at the forefront of safeguarding the banking system against cyber threats. During the bank's third Cybersecurity Awareness Month conference, President Abie Sano highlighted the importance of investing in cybersecurity to protect customer information and maintain trust. The bank is implementing advanced security technologies, including a 24/7 monitoring system and a zero-trust security framework. Additionally, CBE is focusing on educating customers about safe banking practices and the risks associated with digital financial services. As the financial sector increasingly relies on digital platforms, CBE's proactive approach aims to mitigate cybersecurity risks while supporting the growth of digital banking services across Ethiopia. The bank's commitment to cybersecurity is crucial for maintaining public confidence in its digital offerings.
