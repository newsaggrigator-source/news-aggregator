AUSSOM chief, Swedish ambassador discuss Somalia security and international support - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 05:27:41 GMT

The head of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), Amb. El Hadji Ibrahima Diene, met with Swedish Ambassador Hans Lundquist in Mogadishu to discuss Somalia's security situation and the implementation of AUSSOM's mandate. They reviewed recent developments and emphasized the importance of international support for peace and stabilization efforts in the country. Diene acknowledged Sweden's long-standing commitment to Somalia and the African Union, particularly in peacebuilding initiatives. Lundquist reiterated Sweden's dedication to collaborating with the Somali government and AUSSOM to promote sustainable peace and stability. Sweden's cooperation strategy for 2026-2028 focuses on state-building, democratic governance, human rights, and economic opportunities. AUSSOM, which took over from the African Union Transition Mission in January 2025, aims to support Somali security forces, combat al-Shabab, and assist in the broader security transition as Somalia gradually takes on more responsibility for its national security.
