Ethiopia Vows Direct Role in Shaping Red Sea Security Architecture, Maritime Governance

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 12:43:59 GMT

Ethiopia has reaffirmed its commitment to playing a direct role in shaping the security architecture and maritime governance of the Red Sea, which it considers integral to its national security. During the fifth edition of the Horn Dialogue Series in Addis Ababa, Ambassador Adam Tesfaye emphasized the importance of regional cooperation to address challenges such as interstate tensions and internal conflicts. He highlighted the strategic significance of the Horn of Africa, advocating for coordinated responses to economic, social, and security issues. Ethiopia aims to enhance regional connectivity through investments in infrastructure and active participation in peace initiatives under the frameworks of IGAD, AU, and UN. The dialogue series aims to foster strategic trust and cooperative interdependence among Horn nations, with experts stressing the need for locally driven solutions to ensure sustainable regional stability. The discussions reflect a critical juncture for the region, marked by renewed instability and the necessity for collaborative efforts to transform geopolitical competition into shared economic interests.
