US forces leave important airfield in Somalia - Yahoo

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 22:01:09 GMT

The U.S. military has withdrawn its troops from the Baledogle Military Airfield in Somalia, a key base used for over a decade in counterterrorism efforts against the militant group al-Shabaab. Located approximately 60 miles southwest of Mogadishu, Baledogle has been significant in the ongoing Somali civil war, previously falling into the hands of al-Shabaab before being reclaimed by African Union forces. While the exact number of troops stationed at the airfield prior to the withdrawal is unclear, U.S. forces have historically maintained a presence there to support regional operations and train Somali commandos. Although the troops have left Baledogle, they will remain in Somalia, with the possibility of returning to the airfield in the future. U.S. Africa Command emphasized its adaptable military strategy but did not confirm specific details about the troop movements. The withdrawal comes amid broader shifts in U.S. military operations across Africa.
