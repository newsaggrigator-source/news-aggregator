Egypt and Somalia discuss ties and regional security at Russia-Africa summit - Horseed Media

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:16:19 GMT

At the Russia-Africa Summit, Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty and Somali Foreign Minister Abdisalam Abdi Ali met to discuss enhancing bilateral relations and regional security in the Horn of Africa and the Red Sea. The talks aimed to strengthen cooperation in economic, development, and investment sectors, with Abdelatty emphasizing Egypt's commitment to Somalia's unity, sovereignty, and territorial integrity. He condemned any unilateral actions that could threaten Somalia's stability. The ministers also exchanged views on regional developments, highlighting the importance of supporting national institutions and respecting state sovereignty. Their discussions included outcomes from a previous quadrilateral summit in El-Alamein, aimed at fostering security and reducing tensions in the region. This meeting underscores the ongoing efforts by Egypt and Somalia to deepen political and economic ties while addressing shared security challenges in a strategically vital area for international trade and regional stability.
