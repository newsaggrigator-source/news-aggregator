35 Years of State-Building Failure and Political Uncertainty in Somalia regime - Borkena

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 18:52:18 GMT

The article discusses the ongoing political instability and failures in state-building in Somalia over the past 35 years, emphasizing the need for intellectuals and politicians from former Italian Somalia to focus on their own country's challenges rather than the affairs of Somaliland. The author, Eng. Abdi Ali Barkad, criticizes the lack of effective governance, fragile national institutions, and persistent armed conflicts in Somalia, contrasting this with Somaliland's progress in establishing its own political systems and conducting democratic elections since declaring independence in 1991. He urges Somali leaders to redirect their efforts towards rebuilding national institutions, resolving internal conflicts, and addressing corruption, rather than obsessing over Somaliland's political situation. The message calls for political maturity and a commitment to addressing Somalia's pressing issues, advocating for a focus on national unity and effective governance to restore public confidence and build a better future for the Somali people.
