World: Sudan files complaint to UN Security Council over Eth - NewVision.co.ug

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 09:00:05 GMT

Sudan has lodged formal complaints with the UN Security Council and the African Union, accusing Ethiopia of facilitating drone strikes by the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) against Sudanese civilian targets. Since April 2023, Sudan's military has claimed that Ethiopia has allowed these drone attacks to be launched from its territory, which has resulted in significant civilian casualties and damage to infrastructure. The Sudanese army's foreign ministry described Ethiopia's actions as "blatant aggression" and a violation of international law. In a report, Sudan's Transitional Sovereignty Council documented 70 drone attacks this year, resulting in nearly 500 civilian casualties. Ethiopia has denied the allegations and accused Sudan of supporting rebels linked to the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF). The ongoing conflict has led to widespread electricity outages in several Sudanese states due to attacks on power stations. The situation remains tense as both nations continue to exchange accusations.
