Ethiopia: Ministry of Agriculture (MoA) Engagement with Civil Society Organizations to Advance Agricultural Development - Africa.com

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 10:46:23 GMT

The Ethiopian Ministry of Agriculture (MoA) has emphasized the vital role of Civil Society Organizations (CSOs) in promoting agricultural development during a recent consultative meeting. Mr. Yilma Sisay, Head of the Minister’s Office, highlighted the importance of collaboration between the government and CSOs to achieve national development goals. He noted that both international and local CSOs are crucial in supporting Ethiopia’s agricultural transformation agenda, which aims to ensure food sovereignty and enhance productivity. The meeting aimed to strengthen partnerships and align the initiatives of development partners with national agricultural policies. Dr. Mulugeta Deme, Director of the Programs and Projects Coordination Unit, stated that the workshop would facilitate closer cooperation and improve coordination and support mechanisms for CSOs. Presentations covered key topics such as agricultural policy and major programs, with the goal of fostering a more effective contribution to Ethiopia’s agricultural objectives through enhanced collaboration.
