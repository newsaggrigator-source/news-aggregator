NUSOJ report finds widespread harassment, poor working conditions for women journalists in Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 10:14:36 GMT

A report by the National Union of Somali Journalists (NUSOJ) reveals alarming conditions for women journalists in Somalia, with only 10% feeling safe at work. The report, titled "Unsafe, Underpaid and Unheard," was released on November 2, coinciding with the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes against Journalists. It surveyed 127 women journalists and found that 52% felt unsafe, while 48% reported experiencing harassment. Job insecurity is prevalent, with only 49.6% having written contracts and 26% holding full-time positions. Many face chronic salary issues, and 63.8% have not received workplace training. The report highlights significant gender disparities, with only 11% believing they have equal advancement opportunities compared to men. NUSOJ Secretary-General Omar Faruk Osman emphasized the need for media organizations to improve working conditions, including timely wage payments and equal access to training. The report was supported by the UN Development Programme and funded by the European Union. Somalia ranks 126th out of 180 in the World Press Freedom Index.
