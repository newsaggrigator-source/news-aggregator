As foreign troops withdraw from Somalia, could al-Shabab take over? - BBC

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 02:06:10 GMT

As foreign troops withdraw from Somalia, concerns are rising about the potential resurgence of the Islamist militant group al-Shabab. The withdrawal coincides with political disputes that threaten the country's first direct democratic elections in decades. Analysts fear that the Somali government may struggle to maintain security without external support, particularly as al-Shabab continues to control significant territory and has recently claimed attacks on elite Somali forces. The Ugandan troops, who have been crucial in stabilizing the region since 2007, began their withdrawal last month, with Ethiopia also planning to reduce its military presence. While Somali officials assert that local forces are ready to take over security responsibilities, many experts warn that the timing of these withdrawals, amid ongoing political turmoil and a lack of military independence, could lead to a scenario reminiscent of the Taliban's takeover in Afghanistan. The situation remains precarious, with Somalia's reliance on foreign aid further complicating its security landscape.
