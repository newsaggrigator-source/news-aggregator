Puntland’s Deni calls Somalia president’s UAE interference claim ‘unfortunate’ - Somali Guardian

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 10:11:35 GMT

Puntland President Said Abdullahi Deni has criticized Somalia President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's allegations of United Arab Emirates (UAE) interference, labeling them as "unfortunate" and a diversionary tactic. Deni described the claims as an outdated political habit that embarrasses the nation, suggesting that leaders often redirect public frustration towards external entities when domestic issues arise. He emphasized that Somalia's internal conflicts were created by Somalis and must be resolved by them, asserting that external actors like the UAE arrived after these issues had already developed. Deni's remarks come amid a political backdrop where the Somali government has been accused of unilaterally amending election laws to favor the ruling party. He indicated that Puntland would uphold Somali governance and hinted at the possibility of establishing a parallel authority if the central government continues to disregard negotiated solutions. The ongoing tensions reflect deeper fractures within Somalia's federal structure and its relationships with regional administrations and international partners.
