Somalia’s real problem is at home, not elsewhere - Gulf News

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 02:46:00 GMT

The recent diplomatic tensions between Somalia and the UAE have highlighted Somalia's internal political struggles rather than external influences. Following the Somali government's order for the UAE ambassador to leave, accusations of "open invasion" were made against the UAE. However, these claims appear to be a distraction from Somalia's longstanding issues, such as power-sharing disputes among its regions. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's push for reforms has faced resistance from regions like Puntland and Jubaland, which feel their autonomy is threatened. The UAE has been a significant humanitarian and developmental partner for Somalia, providing emergency aid and supporting military training. Critics argue that blaming the UAE is politically convenient for Somali leaders facing domestic challenges. The UAE’s calm response to the accusations reflects its commitment to the Somali people, emphasizing the need for Somalia to address its internal issues rather than deflecting blame onto foreign nations. Effective diplomacy requires recognizing the difference between political rhetoric and genuine national interests. (160 words)
