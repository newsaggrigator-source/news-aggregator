Ethiopia Advances African Leadership, National Development and Strategic Interests - ENA English

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 16:28:48 GMT

Ethiopia is actively advancing its leadership role in Africa while focusing on national development and strategic interests. Recent events, including the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa and the inauguration of the Megech Dam, illustrate Ethiopia's commitment to fostering continental cooperation and achieving tangible development outcomes. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed emphasized the need for African-led solutions and economic sovereignty during the forum, which gathered leaders to discuss peace and security amid global uncertainties. The Megech Dam, with a capacity of 185 million cubic meters, aims to enhance agricultural productivity and food security while providing drinking water to local communities. Ethiopia's development strategy also prioritizes indigenous capacity, aiming to build a skilled workforce and effective institutions. Additionally, discussions on regional geopolitics, particularly concerning Red Sea access and the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD), highlight the complex interplay between national ambitions and regional stability. The ongoing National Dialogue seeks to address political and social issues, fostering consensus for sustainable progress.
