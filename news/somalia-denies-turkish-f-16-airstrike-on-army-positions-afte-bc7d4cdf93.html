Somalia denies Turkish F-16 airstrike on army positions after deadly clashes with al-Shabab - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 11:09:48 GMT

The Somali federal government has refuted claims that Turkish F-16 fighter jets mistakenly bombed Somali National Army (SNA) positions near Leego following intense clashes with al-Shabab. In a statement, the Office of National Security labeled the reports as "false and baseless," asserting that no such airstrikes occurred during the military operations on October 26, 2026. The denial came after reports indicated heavy casualties among Somali troops, with soldiers claiming dozens were killed and over 100 wounded during the fighting. Al-Shabab reportedly employed explosive-laden vehicles in their attacks, destroying military vehicles and inflicting significant losses. While the Somali Defense Ministry acknowledged the use of such tactics by al-Shabab, it did not confirm the number of government casualties. The government emphasized its strategic military partnership with Turkey, focusing on counterterrorism and national security, and urged the public to rely on verified information to maintain morale and public confidence.
