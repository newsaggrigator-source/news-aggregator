A Pattern of Probe Attacks: How Al-Shabaab Is Testing Kenya’s Border Defenses - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 13:14:20 GMT

Al-Shabaab has intensified its probe attacks on security installations along Kenya's northeastern border, particularly in Garissa and Mandera counties. Recent assaults include a failed attack on the Kamuthe Quick Response Unit Camp on November 11, where security forces successfully repelled the militants. However, a more deadly ambush on November 10 in Mandera resulted in the deaths of eight individuals, including road construction workers and police reservists. These incidents reflect a strategic campaign by Al-Shabaab to test and exploit weaknesses in Kenya's border defenses. The frequency of these attacks has raised concerns among security officials, who warn of potential follow-up assaults. Despite the Kenyan government's efforts to bolster security, including troop deployments and investigations, the porous border remains a significant vulnerability. The ongoing violence underscores the challenges faced by security forces in protecting communities from a conflict that has persisted since Kenya's military intervention in Somalia in 2011.
