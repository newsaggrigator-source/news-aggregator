Opposition Parties, UN Agencies Sound Alarm Over Security Incidents, Civilian Safety In Ethiopia - The Reporter Ethiopia

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 08:31:16 GMT

Opposition parties and UN agencies in Ethiopia have raised urgent concerns regarding escalating security incidents, civilian safety, and widespread displacement amid ongoing regional violence. The Ethiopian Citizens for Social Justice (EZEMA) criticized the government for failing to protect citizens and highlighted the lack of media coverage on civilian casualties from recent violence in the Oromia Region. Other parties, including the Enat Party and the Ethiopian People’s Revolutionary Party (EPRP), echoed these concerns, calling for thorough investigations into violence against civilians, particularly targeting Orthodox Tewahedo believers. Reports indicate that clashes near the Ethiopian-Kenyan border have led to the displacement of up to 50,000 individuals, prompting a humanitarian response from agencies like the UNHCR. UN Secretary-General António Guterres emphasized the need for adherence to international law and political solutions to achieve lasting peace. The situation remains critical, with calls for accountability and protection of civilians intensifying amidst the ongoing unrest. (158 words)
