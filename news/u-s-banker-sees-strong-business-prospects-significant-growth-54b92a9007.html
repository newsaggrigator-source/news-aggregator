U.S. Banker Sees Strong Business Prospects, Significant Growth Potential in Ethiopia

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:32:19 GMT

Kenneth Kelly, Chairman of the American Bankers Association and CEO of First Independence Bank, highlighted Ethiopia's significant potential for attracting foreign investment during a recent financial sector engagement in Addis Ababa. He praised the country's evolving business environment and the decision to open its banking sector to foreign investors, which he believes presents substantial opportunities for international financial institutions. The event, themed around the future of banking, included discussions with representatives from fintech companies, microfinance institutions, and telecommunications operators. Kelly emphasized the importance of expanding access to finance for small businesses as a catalyst for broader economic growth. He also noted that Ethiopia's ongoing regulatory reforms and the rise of digital financial services are enhancing competition and financial inclusion. US Embassy Public Diplomacy Officer Jonathan Koehler echoed these sentiments, stressing the need for innovative approaches to strengthen Ethiopia's financial landscape and promote economic development.
