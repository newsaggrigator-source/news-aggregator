Sudan Once Again Accuses Ethiopia of Involvement in Drone Strikes Targeting Power Stations, Civilian Sites

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 06:35:06 GMT

Sudan has accused Ethiopia of involvement in recent drone strikes targeting civilian infrastructure, including power stations and fuel depots. Sudanese officials claim that the drones were launched from Ethiopian territory, specifically citing Bahir Dar Airport as a launch site. During a press conference in Khartoum, Sudanese authorities presented flight data, satellite imagery, and drone fragments to support their allegations. These accusations follow a series of attacks that have disrupted electricity supplies and targeted various facilities across Sudan. Sudan's Undersecretary of Foreign Affairs, Muawiya Osman Khalid, called for international intervention against what he termed Ethiopian-Emirati aggression. Meanwhile, Sudan's Defence Minister Hassan Daoud Kabroun alleged that over 20 countries are supporting the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) with resources, reiterating accusations against Ethiopia and the UAE, which have denied such claims. The ongoing conflict between Sudan's army and the RSF has escalated since April 2023.
