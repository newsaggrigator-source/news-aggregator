UN Report Says UAE Firm Hired Colombian Mercenaries to Fight in Sudan, Raising Questions Over Abu Dhabi's UN Chief Bid

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 10:15:31 GMT

A confidential UN report reveals that a UAE-based security firm, Global Security Services Group (GSSG), allegedly hired hundreds of Colombian mercenaries to support Sudan's Rapid Support Forces (RSF) in their conflict against the Sudanese Armed Forces. This recruitment operation, which reportedly involved the Colombian fighters being organized into a battalion called the "Desert Wolves," raises significant concerns about the UAE's involvement in the ongoing violence in Sudan, where tens of thousands have died and millions have been displaced since the conflict began in April 2023. The findings complicate the UAE's nomination of diplomat Lana Nusseibeh for the UN Secretary-General position, as she has previously denied allegations of Emirati military support to the RSF. Human rights organizations have called for investigations into GSSG and the recruitment network, emphasizing the need for accountability regarding external military support contributing to the humanitarian crisis in Sudan. The UAE has denied all allegations of involvement in the conflict. (157 words)
