Somalia Welcomes First-Ever WTO Visit as Accession Process Nears Final Stage - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 11:30:20 GMT

Somalia recently welcomed its first-ever visit from the World Trade Organization (WTO) as part of its ongoing accession process, which has been in progress for nearly a decade. The visit, led by Somalia’s Chief Trade Negotiator, Sadiq Haji Ibrahim, involved discussions on the country's readiness to meet WTO requirements, including market access and technical documentation. The UK has expressed its support for Somalia's accession, emphasizing the potential for increased business opportunities and job creation. The visit is seen as a significant step, indicating that the WTO is taking Somalia's candidacy seriously. However, while the Somali government claims to have fulfilled all necessary conditions, experts caution that the real challenges lie ahead, particularly in terms of market competitiveness and production capacity. The next key milestone will be a Working Party meeting in early 2027, which will assess Somalia's technical preparations and commitments. Ultimately, WTO membership will provide a framework for trade but will not automatically lead to economic growth without further development efforts.
