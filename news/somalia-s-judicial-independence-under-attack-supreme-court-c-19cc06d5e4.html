Somalia's judicial independence under attack: Supreme Court chief warns of threats to his life as armed forces reportedly seize court - Sunatimes.com

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 16:14:22 GMT

Somalia is experiencing a severe crisis regarding its judicial independence, as armed forces reportedly loyal to former President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud have taken control of the Supreme Court. This intervention follows a significant ruling by the court that declared Mohamud's presidential term had expired, raising concerns about the safety of Chief Justice and the integrity of the judiciary. The Chief Justice has expressed fears for his life due to threats from these armed forces, which have created an intimidating atmosphere within the court. Additionally, there are allegations of cyberattacks on the court's digital systems, further complicating the situation. Eyewitness accounts describe armed personnel occupying the court premises, undermining its independence. The UN has called for respect for judicial independence and an investigation into these actions, emphasizing that any coercion against judges poses a grave threat to the rule of law in Somalia. The international community is urged to monitor these developments closely.
