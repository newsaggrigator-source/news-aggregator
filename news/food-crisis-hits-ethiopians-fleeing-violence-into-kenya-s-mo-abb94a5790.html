Food Crisis Hits Ethiopians Fleeing Violence Into Kenya's Moyale

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 12:45:52 GMT

Thousands of Ethiopian refugees fleeing violence have crossed into Kenya's Moyale area, facing severe food shortages. A recent humanitarian assessment revealed that over 70% of the 50,000 displaced individuals lack sufficient food, with 80% identifying food as their most urgent need. The assessment, conducted by the Government of Kenya and supported by the International Organization for Migration (IOM) and other agencies, highlights the dire living conditions, including inadequate access to clean drinking water, shelter, and healthcare. Many families are staying with relatives or in temporary displacement sites, adding strain to already limited resources. Children make up more than half of the displaced population, raising concerns about their health amid these shortages. The IOM and humanitarian partners are urgently calling for funding to enhance assistance for food distribution, emergency shelter, clean water, sanitation, and healthcare, as the situation continues to deteriorate in the border region.
