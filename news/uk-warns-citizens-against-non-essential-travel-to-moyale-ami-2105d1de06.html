UK warns citizens against non-essential travel to Moyale amid Ethiopia clashes - The Star, Kenya

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 18:30:00 GMT

The UK government has issued a travel advisory warning its citizens against non-essential travel to Moyale, Kenya, due to escalating violence from clashes in neighboring Ethiopia. The advisory, updated on November 6, reflects concerns over the deteriorating security situation and cross-border incidents affecting the area. The warning specifically targets Moyale town and a five-kilometer radius around it, as fighting that began on October 22 has resulted in casualties, injuries, and the displacement of thousands. Kenyan authorities are enhancing security measures along the border, with police deploying additional officers to prevent violence from spilling into Kenya. Tragically, stray bullets have already caused fatalities and injuries among residents. Approximately 10,000 displaced individuals are currently in temporary shelters, receiving humanitarian assistance. The advisory also highlights ongoing security threats in other regions of northern and eastern Kenya, particularly from the al Shabaab militant group, while emphasizing that the warning does not constitute a general travel ban on Kenya.
