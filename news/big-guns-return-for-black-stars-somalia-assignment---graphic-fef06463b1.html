Big guns return for Black Stars' Somalia assignment - Graphic Online

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 17:59:25 GMT

The Ghanaian national football team, the Black Stars, has welcomed back key players for their upcoming Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers against Somalia. Notable returns include Mohammed Kudus, who missed the FIFA World Cup in June, and Daniel-Kofi Kyereh, who is making his first appearance in nearly four years after recovering from an ACL injury. Mohammed Salisu also rejoins the squad following a similar injury that kept him out of the World Cup. Midfielder Thomas Partey, absent since the last World Cup, is also back. Interim coach Kim Lars Björkegren aims to revitalize Ghana's AFCON qualifying campaign after two defeats left them at the bottom of Group C. The squad features a mix of experienced players and three locally based outfield players. The Black Stars are hopeful that the return of these key figures will enhance their chances of securing a victory against Somalia and improving their qualification prospects for the 2027 AFCON.
