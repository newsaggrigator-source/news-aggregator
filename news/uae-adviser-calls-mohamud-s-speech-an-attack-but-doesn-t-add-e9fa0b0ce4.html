UAE Adviser Calls Mohamud’s Speech an “Attack,” But Doesn’t Address the Allegations - www.ftlsomalia.com

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 21:11:07 GMT

In response to President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's recent speech accusing the UAE of interference in Somalia, Dr. Anwar Gargash, the UAE's Diplomatic Adviser, labeled the address an "attack" and questioned Mohamud's commitment to national interests. Gargash's comments, made on social media, emphasized the UAE's historical support for Somalia, while failing to address the specific allegations raised by Mohamud, which included unauthorized agreements over key ports and military bases. The Somali government quickly countered, with ministers asserting that the UAE's framing of their relationship as one of friendship was misleading. Notably, Gargash referred to Mohamud as the "outgoing" president, aligning with opposition views on the legitimacy of his mandate. This exchange highlights a broader narrative struggle, with both sides remaining entrenched in their positions. The lack of direct engagement on the allegations suggests a stalemate, leaving unresolved tensions regarding Somalia's sovereignty and the UAE's role in the region.
