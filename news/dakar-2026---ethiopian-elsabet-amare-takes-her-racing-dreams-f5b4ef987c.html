Dakar 2026 - Ethiopian Elsabet Amare Takes Her Racing Dreams from Injibara to Dakar

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 12:25:50 GMT

Ethiopian runner Elsabet Amare, 17, is set to represent her country at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, inspired by her father and the legacy of Ethiopian distance-running legends like Meseret Defar. Growing up in Injibara, she began her athletic journey with encouragement from her father, who fostered her interest in sports. Elsabet has already made a name for herself, winning silver in the 1,500 meters and gold in the 800 meters at an under-18 competition in Nigeria in 2025, showcasing her tactical prowess. She credits her community and teachers for their support, which has been crucial in balancing her training with academics. As she prepares for the Games, Elsabet expresses confidence in her abilities and hopes to inspire future athletes from her region. She emphasizes the importance of continued support for young runners, aiming to follow in the footsteps of her idols and contribute to Ethiopia's rich athletic heritage.
