Fort Gordon hosts fitness event to honor fallen hero - WRDW

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 13:36:42 GMT

Fort Gordon hosted a fitness event on Saturday to honor Master Sgt. Gary Gordon, who lost his life while serving in Somalia and is the installation's namesake. The event featured a 5K run and various fitness challenges, including deadlifting, sled drags, and rope climbs. Participants, comprising both soldiers and civilians, engaged in these activities to commemorate Gordon's legacy and promote the military's culture of excellence. First Sgt. Alexander Buza, the NCOA Headquarters Branch Chief at Fort Gordon, emphasized the importance of such events in sharing military traditions and values with the community. The gathering not only served as a tribute to a fallen hero but also fostered a sense of unity and understanding between the military and local residents. Overall, the event highlighted the commitment to honor those who have served and the values they represent.
