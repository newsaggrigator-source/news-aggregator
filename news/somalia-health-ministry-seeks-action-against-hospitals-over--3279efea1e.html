Somalia Health Ministry Seeks Action Against Hospitals Over Unethical Social Media Adverts - Dawan Africa

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 07:57:07 GMT

The Somalia Health Ministry is taking action against hospitals for unethical advertising practices on social media, as stated by Health Minister Dr. Ali Haji Adan. He emphasized the need for hospital advertisements to adhere to ethical standards, highlighting the prevalence of unacceptable content in the media. The ministry has approached the National Health Professionals Council (NHPC) to address these issues, which include reviewing and registering health facilities in Mogadishu, where approximately 160 hospitals and over 200 polyclinics operate. Adan noted that the NHPC is responsible for licensing health facilities and practitioners, with some unqualified individuals already facing legal consequences. Additionally, the ministry is drafting regulations for fertility centers to ensure compliance with Islamic law. While the minister did not specify penalties for hospitals violating advertising standards, he mentioned that security agencies would assist in enforcing compliance. The initiative aims to enhance the integrity of health services in Somalia. (159 words)
