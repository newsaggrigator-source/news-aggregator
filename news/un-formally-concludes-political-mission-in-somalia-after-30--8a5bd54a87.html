UN formally concludes political mission in Somalia after 30 years - سانا

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 19:53:00 GMT

The United Nations has officially concluded its political mission in Somalia after 30 years of engagement, marking a significant transition in the country's governance. This milestone follows a structured two-year process that involved transferring responsibilities to federal Somali institutions. Raisedon Zenenga, the Acting Mission Head, emphasized that this closure does not signify the end of UN cooperation with Somalia but rather the beginning of a new partnership phase. Despite ongoing security and institutional challenges, Somalia has made notable progress in state-building since the UN's initial involvement, which began in 1995 with the establishment of the United Nations Political Office for Somalia (UNPOS). The mission evolved into the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNSOM) in 2013 and concluded under the United Nations Transition Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS). The decision to terminate the mission was formally requested by Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud in May 2024, leading to the UN Security Council's phased transition plan. (158 words)
