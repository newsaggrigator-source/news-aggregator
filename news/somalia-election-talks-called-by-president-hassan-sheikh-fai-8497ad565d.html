Somalia election talks called by president Hassan Sheikh fail to start as opposition boycotts - Somali Guardian

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 16:19:49 GMT

Somalia's anticipated election talks, called by President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, failed to commence as the main opposition alliance boycotted the meeting scheduled at Villa Somalia. The Somali Future Council, which includes former leaders and regional authorities, informed the international community that it would not participate unless there was international mediation and guarantees. Puntland and Jubaland also rejected the invitation, arguing it was a tactic to extend the mandates of current institutions, which they believe have expired. The absence of these key players left the talks without significant opposition representation. Despite the setback, Villa Somalia stated that communication with invited parties was ongoing and that the talks would proceed soon, although no new date or details were provided. This failure to initiate dialogue comes amid a Supreme Court ruling that mandates a new electoral framework within thirty days, highlighting the urgency of resolving Somalia's political impasse. The situation remains precarious as the December deadline approaches.
