Somalia Says Army Is Ready to Take Over as Uganda Begins Troop Withdrawal - Horseed Media

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 06:36:04 GMT

Somalia's government has announced that its armed forces are ready to take over security responsibilities as Uganda begins withdrawing its troops after nearly two decades of support against the Al-Shabaab insurgency. The transition gained momentum on November 15 when Ugandan troops handed over five military bases in the Lower Shabelle region to the Somali National Army (SNA). Somalia's Information Minister, Abdifatah Qasim Mahmoud, expressed confidence in the SNA's ability to maintain security, despite concerns from some lawmakers about the readiness of Somali forces to fill the security gaps left by departing African Union peacekeepers. Uganda's military plans to withdraw all its troops by the end of 2027, with the African Union also coordinating a broader transition of peacekeepers by December 2029. The success of this transition is critical for Somalia's security, as it seeks to confront Al-Shabaab independently while ensuring that recent military gains are not compromised.
