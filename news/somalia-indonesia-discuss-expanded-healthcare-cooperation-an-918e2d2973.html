Somalia, Indonesia Discuss Expanded Healthcare Cooperation and Bilateral Relations - Horseed Media

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 15:43:00 GMT

Somalia's Deputy Prime Minister, Salah Ahmed Jama, met with Indonesia's Minister of Health, Budi Gunadi Sadikin, in Mogadishu on November 24, 2026, to discuss enhancing bilateral relations and healthcare cooperation. The discussions centered on improving medical training, access to essential medicines, and maternal and child healthcare, as well as overall health system development. The delegations also addressed Indonesia's candidacy for the Director-General position of the World Health Organization (WHO) for the 2027-2032 term, seeking support from member states ahead of the upcoming election. Jama emphasized the need for broader cooperation beyond healthcare, including education, trade, and private-sector investment, to create sustainable opportunities for both nations. Somalia's healthcare system faces significant challenges, including reduced donor support and shortages of qualified professionals. Strengthening ties with Indonesia could help Somalia leverage its pharmaceutical manufacturing capabilities and medical training resources, addressing critical healthcare needs and diversifying international partnerships.
