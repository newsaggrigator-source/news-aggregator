Mozambique: Chapo heads to Ethiopia for Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa - Club of Mozambique

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 04:01:43 GMT

Mozambican President Daniel Chapo is set to attend the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, scheduled from October 23 to 25, 2026, in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia. He will serve as the keynote speaker for a panel titled “From the Margins to the Centre: Building an African Model for Peace and Sovereignty in an Era of Geopolitical Rivalry.” The forum's overarching theme is “Pathways to Peace in Africa: Economic Sovereignty and Geopolitical Transformation,” focusing on African-led solutions to the continent's peace, security, and development challenges. Chapo will be accompanied by First Lady Gueta Selemane Chapo, Minister of the Interior Paulo Chachine, and Mozambique’s Ambassador to Ethiopia, Nuno Tomás, along with other officials. This forum is a crucial platform for discussing strategies to enhance economic sovereignty and address geopolitical issues affecting Africa. The event aims to unite voices across the continent to collaboratively define Africa's future in the context of ongoing global challenges.
