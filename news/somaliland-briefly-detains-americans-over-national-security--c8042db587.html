Somaliland briefly detains Americans over national security threat - Somali Guardian

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 19:07:05 GMT

The National Intelligence Agency (NIA) of Somaliland briefly detained five American nationals over concerns regarding national security. The agency stated that it had been monitoring the individuals since their arrival and conducted an extensive investigation into their activities. The Americans were reportedly working for a company named Parallel Black, which had been invited by Somaliland’s Foreign Affairs Minister to conduct feasibility studies for potential investments. The NIA did not provide further details, and both the Somaliland government and the U.S. State Department did not immediately respond to inquiries. The five Americans left Somaliland on an Ethiopian Airlines flight shortly after their detention. Somaliland, which declared independence from Somalia in 1991, is seeking international recognition, having recently gained acknowledgment from Israel as a sovereign state. The incident highlights the delicate balance Somaliland maintains in its quest for legitimacy and security in a strategically significant region. (158 words)
