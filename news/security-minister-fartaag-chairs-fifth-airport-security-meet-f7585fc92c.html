Security Minister Fartaag Chairs Fifth Airport Security Meeting, Pushes Demolition Plan - FTL Somalia

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 09:28:40 GMT

Somalia's Minister of Internal Security, Abdullahi Sheikh Ismail (Fartaag), chaired the fifth airport security meeting at Aden Adde International Airport, emphasizing the need to expedite plans for relocating or demolishing structures near the airport. The meeting aimed to enhance cooperation among security and aviation agencies to improve passenger safety amid ongoing threats from Al-Shabaab. The government’s push for clearance around the airport reflects a broader trend of forced evictions in Mogadishu, where over two million people have been displaced since 2017. Critics highlight a lack of transparency regarding the number of affected structures and compensation for residents, raising concerns about the potential for private interests to benefit from the demolitions. The government has yet to provide specific details about the plan, leaving residents uncertain about their future.
