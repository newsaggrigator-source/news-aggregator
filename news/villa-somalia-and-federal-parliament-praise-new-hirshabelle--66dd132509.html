Villa Somalia and Federal Parliament Praise New Hirshabelle Speakership - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 08:02:22 GMT

Villa Somalia and the Federal Parliament have expressed their support for the newly elected leadership of the Hirshabelle State House of the People, following the election of Speaker Abdiqani Lukman Haji Abdi and his deputies. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud congratulated the new leaders, urging them to fulfill their constitutional responsibilities with integrity and to enhance governance and democracy in the region. Speaker Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur echoed these sentiments, emphasizing the need for transparency and accountability in serving the people of Hirshabelle. The election of this parliamentary leadership paves the way for the upcoming regional presidential vote, scheduled for October 24. The Justice and Solidarity Party (JSP), which holds a significant majority in the parliament, has nominated incumbent President Ali Abdullahi Hussein Guudlaawe for re-election. However, unresolved tensions regarding power-sharing between the Hawadle and Abgaal communities pose challenges to the legitimacy of the electoral process. The new leadership must navigate these complexities to foster political trust in Hirshabelle.
