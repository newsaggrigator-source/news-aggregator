SAUDI–HOUTHI WAR: The fighting has moved inside Saudi Arabia - Garowe Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 21:31:45 GMT

The ongoing conflict between Saudi Arabia and Yemen's Houthi rebels has escalated, with fighting now spilling into Saudi territory. Following the collapse of a truce in July 2022, the Houthis have intensified their attacks, targeting significant locations within Saudi Arabia, including Riyadh's financial district, oil facilities in the Eastern Province, and the Red Sea ports of Jeddah and Jazan. This shift marks a significant change in the dynamics of the war, which had previously been largely confined to Yemen. The increased hostilities pose new challenges for Saudi Arabia, as it grapples with the implications of the conflict reaching its borders. The situation remains fluid, with potential repercussions for regional stability and security as both sides navigate this new phase of the war.
