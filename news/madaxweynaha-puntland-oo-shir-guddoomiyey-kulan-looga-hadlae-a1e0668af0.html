Madaxweynaha Puntland oo shir guddoomiyey kulan looga hadlaeyy ammaanka & xaaladda kala guurka Somalia. - Horseed Media

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 20:06:05 GMT

Madaxweynaha Puntland, Mudane Siciid Cabdullaahi Deni, ayaa shir la qaatay madaxda hay’adaha amniga iyo taliyeyaasha ciidamada Puntland si looga wada hadlo arrimaha amniga iyo xaaladda kala guurka Soomaaliya. Kulanka, oo dhacay 4 November 2026, ayaa diiradda lagu saaray amniga guud ee Puntland, dhammeystirka howlgalka Calmiskaad, iyo diyaarinta howlgalka Onkod ee Calmadow. Sidoo kale, waxaa la falanqeeyay la dagaalanka burcad-badeedka iyo isbadellada amni ee ka soo kordhay qaybo ka mid ah Soomaaliya, kuwaas oo Dowladda Puntland si dhow ula socoto. Kulankan ayaa muujinaya dadaalka Puntland ee ku aaddan xoojinta amniga iyo ka hortagga khataraha cusub ee ka imaanaya gudaha dalka. Madaxweyne Deni ayaa sidoo kale ka helay talooyin khubaro amni si loo xoojiyo qorshayaasha amniga ee gobolka.
