Somalia’s president dismisses chief justice to forestall ruling on constitution - Somali Guardian

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 18:51:21 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has dismissed Chief Justice Bashe Yusuf in a surprising move that critics argue undermines judicial independence. This decision comes just days after Yusuf began addressing an opposition petition challenging the government's new constitution, which was ratified by parliament earlier this year. Local media reports suggest that Yusuf was close to delivering a verdict on this petition, prompting Mohamud to act preemptively to avoid a potentially damaging ruling against his administration. In Yusuf's place, Mohamud appointed Ibrahim Eidle Suleiman, a ruling party member and close ally, as the new president of the Supreme Court. The opposition and various regional states have condemned the constitution's ratification process as unilateral and unconstitutional. Analysts view this dismissal as a calculated effort by Mohamud to maintain control over the judiciary and prevent any adverse legal decisions that could threaten his government.
