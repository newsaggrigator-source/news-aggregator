Eritrea's Migration Economy - Can the Nation Survive Without Forced Remittance?

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 12:26:00 GMT

Eritrea's economy heavily relies on remittances from its diaspora, driven by a system of indefinite national service that compels emigration. The Eritrean government, led by President Isaias Afwerki, promotes self-reliance while simultaneously depending on financial flows from citizens abroad, which are crucial for the regime's survival. A recent ultimatum from the UK threatens visa bans unless Eritrea cooperates on returning failed asylum seekers, highlighting the contradiction in its policies. The regime's control over the economy, characterized by party ownership and a lack of a comprehensive national budget, perpetuates a cycle of repression and economic stagnation. As a result, Eritrea's migration economy is not merely a consequence of isolation but a fundamental aspect of its political structure, with significant implications for both the nation and its diaspora.
