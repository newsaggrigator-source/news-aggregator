Addis Ababa and Asmara Trade War Accusations as Moscow Prepares to Host Talks - FTL Somalia

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 09:34:36 GMT

Tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea have escalated, prompting Russia to offer to mediate talks during the upcoming Russia-Africa Summit. Moscow's Deputy Foreign Minister, Alexander Alimov, expressed concern over the deteriorating relations and urged both nations to engage in dialogue. Eritrea's Information Minister accused Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed of waging a war for direct access to the Red Sea, which Eritrea views as a threat to its sovereignty. Eritrea has requested the UN Security Council to condemn Ethiopia's actions, while Ethiopia has countered with accusations of Eritrean aggression. The conflict is rooted in Ethiopia's desire to re-establish a naval presence after losing its coastline in 1993. Ethiopia's naval expansion aims to secure its interests in the Red Sea, which it links to economic growth. The situation also affects Somalia, which fears similar territorial threats from Ethiopia's ambitions, particularly regarding a recent agreement with Somaliland. The regional implications of this standoff remain significant, complicating diplomatic relations.
