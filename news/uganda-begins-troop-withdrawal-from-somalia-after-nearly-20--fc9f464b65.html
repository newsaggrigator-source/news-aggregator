Uganda Begins Troop Withdrawal from Somalia After Nearly 20 Years - Horseed Media

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 15:33:00 GMT

Uganda has initiated the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia after nearly 20 years of military involvement, as announced by General Muhoozi Kainerugaba, the Chief of Defence Forces. This move marks the beginning of a security transition to Somali forces, with Uganda being the largest troop contributor to the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM). The exact number of troops being withdrawn and the timeline for the process remain unspecified. Uganda first deployed troops in March 2007 as part of the African Union Mission in Somalia (AMISOM) and has been involved in significant operations against the militant group al-Shabab. Despite the withdrawal, al-Shabab continues to exert control over parts of Somalia. The African Union has emphasized that troop withdrawal should be based on security conditions and coordinated with the Somali government to ensure stability. The future of AUSSOM is uncertain due to ongoing financial challenges.
