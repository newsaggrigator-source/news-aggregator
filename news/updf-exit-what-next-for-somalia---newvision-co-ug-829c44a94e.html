UPDF exit: What next for Somalia? - NewVision.co.ug

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 19:00:05 GMT

Uganda has initiated the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia, marking a significant shift in its foreign security commitment. The Uganda People's Defence Force (UPDF) has been stationed in Somalia since March 2007, becoming the first foreign military to intervene in the country, which was then plagued by clan warfare. Currently, approximately 4,500 troops are part of the African Union (AU) mission in Somalia, supplemented by around 2,000 additional troops through bilateral agreements. The withdrawal raises questions about the future security landscape in Somalia, particularly regarding the stability and effectiveness of local forces in the absence of UPDF support. As Uganda begins this exit, the implications for Somalia's ongoing conflict and the broader regional security dynamics remain uncertain, prompting discussions on the next steps for both Somalia and the international community involved in peacekeeping efforts.
