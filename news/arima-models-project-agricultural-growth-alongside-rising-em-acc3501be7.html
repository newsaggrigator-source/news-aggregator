ARIMA Models Project Agricultural Growth Alongside Rising Emissions in Somalia Through 2030 - Bioengineer.org

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 19:13:23 GMT

A recent study utilizing ARIMA models has projected that agricultural output in Somalia will increase through 2030, despite a rise in carbon dioxide emissions. The research, conducted by Mohamed Sheikh Ali Jirow and Siok Kun Sek, analyzed time-series data to assess future trends in food supply, environmental pressure, and farming productivity. The findings indicate that while agricultural output is expected to grow from 3.670 in early 2024 to 4.138 by late 2030, food availability may slightly decline before stabilizing. Specifically, food availability is projected to decrease from 97.568 to approximately 97.55 during the same period. The study emphasizes that improvements in agricultural yield may not necessarily translate to enhanced food availability due to ongoing environmental stress. The researchers highlight the importance of these projections for informing agricultural planning and climate adaptation strategies in Somalia, despite some methodological limitations in data collection. Overall, the study provides critical insights for national food security policies. (160 words)
