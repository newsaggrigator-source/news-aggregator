Somali president hails Türkiye’s role in rebuilding Somalia’s naval capabilities - TRT Afrika

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 16:25:19 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has commended Türkiye's significant role in rebuilding Somalia's naval capabilities, emphasizing the importance of maritime security for regional stability and international trade. During a groundbreaking ceremony for new naval facilities in Mogadishu, he defended the bilateral defense and economic cooperation agreement with Türkiye, which has faced criticism. Mohamud highlighted that the partnership has yielded tangible results, aiding in military development and addressing issues like illegal fishing and maritime crime. He stressed the necessity of a capable navy to protect Somalia's vast maritime resources and secure key shipping routes. The agreement, signed in February 2024, includes support for training, capacity-building, and combating piracy. Mohamud urged Somali naval personnel to prepare for their crucial responsibilities, which extend beyond national interests to the broader Horn of Africa and global security. The event was attended by high-ranking Turkish military officials, underscoring the strengthening defense ties between Somalia and Türkiye.
