Somalia Faces Critical Security Challenges - Africa Defense Forum

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 18:20:42 GMT

Somalia is grappling with severe security challenges as international support dwindles, political divisions deepen, and threats from the militant group al-Shabaab persist. The recent handover of a military base from Ethiopian forces to the Somali National Army (SNA) in Burhakaba highlights the urgent need for Somalia to independently manage its security. Analysts express concern over the SNA's capacity to handle these responsibilities, especially with significant funding cuts to the United Nations Support Office in Somalia (UNSOS) looming. This reduction could lead to operational collapse for the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), which relies on international aid for essential services. As Somali troops lose battlefield assistance, the potential for al-Shabaab to exploit security gaps increases, threatening major urban centers like Mogadishu. The situation is further complicated by regional tensions and dissatisfaction among state governments regarding federal influence, raising questions about the sustainability of Somalia's security and political infrastructure.
