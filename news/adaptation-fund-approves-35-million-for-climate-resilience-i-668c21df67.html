Adaptation Fund approves $35 million for climate resilience in Kenya, Somalia, Ethiopia and Rwanda - Africa Sustainability Matters

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 10:32:43 GMT

The Adaptation Fund has approved nearly $35 million for climate resilience initiatives in Kenya, Somalia, Ethiopia, and Rwanda, focusing on early warning systems, water infrastructure, rangeland restoration, and climate-smart agriculture. This funding, announced during the Fund's 47th Board meeting on October 23, 2026, includes a $30 million program for the Mandera Triangle, which encompasses parts of Kenya, Somalia, and Ethiopia, and a $4.97 million project for smallholder farmers in Rwanda. The Mandera Triangle project aims to assist 500,000 people directly and 1.2 million indirectly by enhancing water systems and improving cross-border cooperation in response to climate hazards. In Rwanda, the initiative will support 10,000 farmers by providing access to weather forecasts and promoting sustainable agricultural practices. These programs highlight the importance of local institutions and community involvement in adaptation efforts, addressing immediate climate vulnerabilities while aiming for long-term resilience in the face of increasing climate variability.
