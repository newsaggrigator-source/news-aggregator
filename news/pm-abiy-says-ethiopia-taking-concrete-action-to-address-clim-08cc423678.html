PM Abiy Says Ethiopia Taking Concrete Action to Address Climate Change

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:29:19 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has emphasized the country's proactive measures to combat climate change, highlighting its integration into national economic policy. Speaking at the COP31 conference in Antalya, he outlined Ethiopia's commitment to renewable energy, extensive tree-planting initiatives, climate-resilient agriculture, and urban environmental protection. Abiy noted that climate change significantly impacts agricultural production and energy generation, with droughts and floods exacerbated by El Niño affecting livelihoods and economic recovery. He showcased Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative, which has seen the planting of over 56 billion seedlings, fostering community engagement and job creation. The Prime Minister also discussed diversifying energy sources, including wind and solar, to mitigate reliance on hydropower, which is vulnerable to changing rainfall patterns. He called for predictable climate financing to support vulnerable nations and announced Ethiopia's plans to host COP32, emphasizing early action and accessible funding in climate preparedness.
