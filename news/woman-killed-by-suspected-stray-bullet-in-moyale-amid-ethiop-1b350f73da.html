Woman Killed By Suspected Stray Bullet in Moyale Amid Ethiopia Conflict

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 06:58:29 GMT

A 65-year-old woman named Amina Guchi was killed in Moyale, Kenya, after being struck on the head by what is believed to be a stray bullet linked to ongoing conflicts in neighboring Ethiopia. The incident occurred on a Monday while Guchi was at the Marsabit County offices, conversing with a soldier on duty. Suddenly, she was hit from behind and collapsed, losing consciousness. Emergency services transported her to Moyale Sub-County Referral Hospital, where she was pronounced dead upon arrival. Police have indicated that the bullet likely originated from the cross-border hostilities, although further details regarding the shooting have not been disclosed. Following a post-mortem examination, her body was released to her family for burial. This tragic event highlights the dangers posed by the conflict in Ethiopia, which has repercussions across the border in Kenya.
