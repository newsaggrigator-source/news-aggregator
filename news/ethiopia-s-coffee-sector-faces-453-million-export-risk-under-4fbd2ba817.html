Ethiopia's Coffee Sector Faces $453 Million Export Risk Under EU Deforestation Rules

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 15:25:59 GMT

Ethiopia's coffee sector faces a potential loss of up to $453 million in export revenue due to the European Union's new deforestation regulation (EUDR). A report by ODI Global highlights that compliance challenges could exclude smallholder farmers, who produce about 95% of the country's coffee, from EU markets, which account for roughly 30% of Ethiopia's coffee exports. The study indicates that difficulties in tracing coffee to individual farms and collecting necessary geolocation data could put up to 60% of smallholders at risk of exclusion. This disruption could reduce Ethiopia's GDP by up to 0.5% and exacerbate foreign exchange shortages. The EUDR requires proof that coffee is deforestation-free, but Ethiopia's current traceability systems are inadequate. The report warns that excluding smallholders may not effectively address deforestation, as coffee production often incorporates sustainable practices. The Ethiopian government is urged to engage with EU authorities to clarify compliance requirements and support smallholders in meeting these challenges.
