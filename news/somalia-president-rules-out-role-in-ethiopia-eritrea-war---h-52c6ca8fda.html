Somalia President Rules Out Role in Ethiopia-Eritrea War - https://kulu-media.com/

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:55:37 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has declared that Somalia will not engage in the ongoing conflict between Ethiopia and Eritrea, emphasizing the country's commitment to regional neutrality. In a recent interview with Sky News, Mohamud stated that Somalia maintains good relations with both nations and has no intention of interfering in their disputes. He also rejected the establishment of foreign military bases in Somalia, asserting that such arrangements would not be permitted. The president expressed concerns about the growing competition for influence in the Red Sea and highlighted the need for stronger coastal security and economic opportunities to combat piracy. Additionally, he addressed domestic challenges, including the fight against Al-Shabaab, youth unemployment, and the reliance on international humanitarian aid. Mohamud reiterated Somalia's focus on strengthening its own security and fostering economic growth, while maintaining its territorial integrity amid regional tensions. His remarks come as Ethiopia and Eritrea's relations continue to deteriorate.
