Hassan Anwar Carries Somalia’s Boxing Hopes to the Youth Olympics in Senegal - Dawan Africa

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 16:48:58 GMT

Hassan Anwar Mohamed, a 17-year-old boxer from Somalia, is set to represent his country at the Youth Olympic Games in Dakar, Senegal, starting on October 31, 2026. Competing in the 50kg category, Hassan is seen as Somalia's best hope for a medal, with his coach, Mohamed Ahmed Abdullahi, expressing confidence in his readiness for the competition. Hassan has trained extensively in Somalia, Sierra Leone, and Kenya, honing his skills under the guidance of experienced coaches. Meanwhile, Kenya's Ellah Edemesi, a 16-year-old boxer, also prepares for her bouts, inspired by role models like Claressa Shields. Both athletes highlight the growth of boxing in their respective countries, despite challenges such as a lack of sponsorship. The Somali Boxing Association President noted the sport's development since 2018, while Hassan looks forward to showcasing his talent on an international stage. The upcoming Games are also seen as a stepping stone towards the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.
