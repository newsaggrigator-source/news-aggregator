Somali Disaster Agency Delivers NFIs to Vulnerable Households in Bay Region - FTL Somalia

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 23:37:33 GMT

The Somali Disaster Management Agency (SoDMA) has distributed non-food items (NFIs) to vulnerable households and displaced families in Baidoa, located in the Bay region of South West State. The supplies, which included rainproof sheets and mosquito nets, aim to address the basic needs of those living in displacement camps as the region braces for potential flooding due to El Niño. Baidoa has seen a significant influx of displaced individuals, with over 14,800 people newly displaced from January to September 2026. Humanitarian agencies warn that up to 3.5 million people nationwide could be affected by flooding, with a projected 1 million likely to be displaced. Despite the distribution efforts, the response to the humanitarian crisis remains critically underfunded, receiving only 35% of the required financing. Local officials have praised SoDMA's efforts, but concerns persist regarding the agency's capacity and the underlying political conflicts that exacerbate the humanitarian situation.
