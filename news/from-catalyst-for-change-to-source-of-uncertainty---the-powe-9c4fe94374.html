From Catalyst for Change to Source of Uncertainty - the Power--and Peril--of the Political Disrupter

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 15:25:21 GMT

In Ethiopia, political disrupters—individuals who influence politics from outside formal structures—play a complex role in shaping the political landscape. These figures can catalyze change by challenging entrenched injustices or destabilize movements by deepening divisions. The distinction between constructive disruption and destructive interference hinges on the intentions and outcomes of their actions. For instance, Jawar Mohammed, a prominent Oromo political voice, exemplifies this duality. Initially instrumental in mobilizing support during protests, his later criticisms of political alliances raise questions about his strategic direction. Recent conflicts, such as the violence in Moyale, illustrate the risks of oversimplifying complex issues, as they involve broader political dynamics beyond local disputes. As Ethiopia navigates its political challenges, understanding the impact of disrupters like Jawar is crucial, as their influence can either foster accountability or perpetuate instability, highlighting the need for constructive alternatives in political discourse.
