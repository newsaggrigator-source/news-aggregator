Ethiopia Strengthens Pandemic Preparedness, Health Security, Minister Says - ENA English

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 14:47:42 GMT

Ethiopia is enhancing its pandemic preparedness and health security, as announced by Health Minister Dr. Mekdes Daba during the 21st Pandemic Fund Governing Board meeting. The country has improved its International Health Regulations (IHR) capacity score from 62 to 70, thanks to a $50 million grant from the Pandemic Fund and an additional $63 million in national co-financing. Key advancements include increased laboratory capacity, with more facilities achieving international accreditation, and a rise in antimicrobial resistance surveillance sites from 26 to 41. Ethiopia has also expanded its specimen transportation services and operationalized an electronic emergency management platform across eight regions. The National Emergency Team has become the first civilian team in Africa to achieve the highest emergency response classification. Dr. Mekdes emphasized the need for sustained investment and accountability to ensure lasting results in pandemic preparedness. Additionally, the Ministry of Agriculture has expanded veterinary training, enhancing animal disease detection and reporting capabilities.
