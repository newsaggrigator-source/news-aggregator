Somalia rejects Israeli ambassador to Somaliland and planned embassy in Hargeisa - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 18:13:04 GMT

Somalia has officially rejected Israel's appointment of an ambassador to Somaliland and the establishment of an embassy in Hargeisa, deeming these actions violations of its sovereignty and territorial integrity. The Somali Foreign Ministry stated that these developments occurred without the federal government's consent, rendering them "null and void." Israel's first ambassador to Somaliland, Michael Lotem, recently presented his credentials to Somaliland's President, followed by a cornerstone ceremony for the embassy. Somalia insists that Somaliland, which declared independence in 1991, remains an integral part of the nation and cannot independently engage in foreign diplomatic relations. The ministry cited the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations to support its stance. Somalia has called on Israel to reverse its actions and urged international bodies not to legitimize them. The African Union and other regional organizations have previously rejected Israel's recognition of Somaliland, emphasizing support for Somalia's territorial claims. Somalia reserves the right to pursue legal and diplomatic measures to defend its sovereignty. (158 words)
