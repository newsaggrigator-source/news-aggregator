Ethiopia-style guerrilla insurgencies could emerge in Somalia: official - Somali Guardian

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 17:29:24 GMT

Former Somali intelligence chief Abdirahman Turyare has warned that Somalia may face the emergence of guerrilla insurgencies similar to those in Ethiopia, particularly in regions like Hiran, Middle Shabelle, and Bay. Turyare, now Somalia’s envoy to the Islamic Military Counter-Terrorism Coalition, expressed concerns that political rivalries and external military support could lead to the formation of armed factions that might align with Al-Shabaab, the militant group opposing the federal government. Reports indicate that former intelligence chief Abdullahi Mohamed Ali Sanbalolshe is seeking military backing from Ethiopia to establish a force in Hiran amid political disputes over regional leadership. Additionally, businessman Abdullahi Mohamed Nur is allegedly planning to form a guerrilla group in Middle Shabelle with support from the UAE. The situation is exacerbated by ongoing guerrilla warfare in Bay, where forces loyal to ousted leader Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen have gained territory. Turyare's warnings highlight the potential for escalating violence and instability in Somalia.
