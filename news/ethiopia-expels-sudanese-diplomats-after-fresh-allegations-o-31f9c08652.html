Ethiopia expels Sudanese diplomats after fresh allegations of support for RSF - WSAU

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 12:21:01 GMT

Ethiopia has expelled six Sudanese diplomats amid escalating tensions between the two nations, driven by mutual accusations of supporting each other's adversaries. Sudan claims that Ethiopia is aiding the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF), while Ethiopia alleges that Sudan is backing rebels in its Tigray region. These allegations have raised concerns about the potential for the ongoing conflicts in both countries to merge into a larger regional crisis. Recently, Sudan lodged a formal complaint with the United Nations Security Council, accusing Ethiopia of permitting the RSF to launch drone attacks from its territory. In response, Ethiopia ordered the Sudanese consulate staff in Assosa to leave within 48 hours, although no specific reasons were provided for this decision. The situation remains delicate, with both countries denying the accusations against each other, while the humanitarian crisis resulting from Sudan's civil war continues to worsen, displacing over 14 million people since April 2023.
