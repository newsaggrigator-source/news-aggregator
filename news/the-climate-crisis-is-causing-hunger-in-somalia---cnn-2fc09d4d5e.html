The climate crisis is causing hunger in Somalia - CNN

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 12:15:19 GMT

The climate crisis is severely impacting Somalia, leading to widespread hunger among its population. Currently, approximately 5.4 million people in the country are facing food insecurity, primarily due to prolonged drought conditions that have devastated livestock, which many Somalis rely on for their livelihoods. Mohamed Abdi, the Somalia country director for the Norwegian Refugee Council, emphasizes the dire situation, highlighting that the ongoing climate challenges are exacerbating existing vulnerabilities. The combination of climate change and its effects on agriculture and livestock is creating a humanitarian crisis, necessitating urgent international attention and assistance. As the situation worsens, the need for sustainable solutions to address both immediate food needs and long-term climate resilience becomes increasingly critical for the affected communities in Somalia.
