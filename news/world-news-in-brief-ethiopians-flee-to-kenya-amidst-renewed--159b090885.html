World News in Brief: Ethiopians flee to Kenya amidst renewed conflict, new head of UN’s food programme, UN mission in Somalia concludes - The European Sting

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:30:59 GMT

Amidst renewed conflict in Ethiopia, approximately 36,600 people have fled to northern Kenya, according to the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The fighting, primarily between forces from the Somali and Oromia regions over boundary disputes and resources, has escalated recently, prompting this wave of displacement. Many of the displaced individuals have sought refuge at the Oriental Mission Church, while others are being hosted by local communities. IOM is collaborating with the Kenyan government and UNHCR to assess humanitarian needs, which include water, sanitation, shelter, food assistance, and health support. In related news, Luke J. Lindberg has taken over as the new Executive Director of the UN World Food Programme, emphasizing the urgent need for global support to combat hunger exacerbated by conflict and climate issues. Additionally, the UN's political mission in Somalia is concluding after over three decades, transferring responsibilities to Somali institutions amid ongoing challenges in the region.
