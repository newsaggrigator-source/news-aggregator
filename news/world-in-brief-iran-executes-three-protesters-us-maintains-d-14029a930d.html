WORLD IN BRIEF: Iran executes three protesters, US maintains deportation drive, Somalia court rejects president's term extension and other stories - Business News Nigeria

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 05:03:45 GMT

Iran has executed three men linked to anti-government protests that erupted in January, marking a continuation of the state's crackdown on dissent. The men were accused of violent acts, including using petrol bombs and firearms, and were said to have operated on behalf of foreign adversaries. Their executions follow a series of death sentences related to the protests, which began over rising living costs and escalated into widespread demonstrations against the government. In other global news, the U.S. administration has reaffirmed its commitment to a strict immigration policy despite recent electoral setbacks, while Poland mourns six victims of a shooting in Krakow. Additionally, Somalia's Supreme Court has ruled against President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's attempt to extend his term, declaring it unconstitutional and calling for elections within 90 days. The political landscape remains tense as various nations grapple with internal and external challenges. (159 words)
