Fighting erupts near Baidoa as Somali army advance on Burhakabo stalls - Somali Guardian

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 12:47:54 GMT

Fighting erupted near Baidoa on October 26, 2026, as the Somali National Army clashed with forces loyal to Abdiaziz Laftagareen, the former president of Southwest State. The confrontation occurred when army units were ambushed while traveling through the Burjed area, en route to Burhakabo, a town recently captured by Laftagareen’s forces. An official from Laftagareen’s faction claimed their fighters attacked to prevent the soldiers from claiming a false victory. Reports indicate significant casualties, with claims of dozens of soldiers killed and military vehicles destroyed, although these figures remain unverified. The clashes coincide with ongoing militant attacks that have stalled approximately 2,000 Somali troops attempting to retake Burhakabo and other towns in the Bay region. The troops are currently stranded in Leego, hindered by fears of landmines and ambushes, while local residents have been warned to evacuate due to potential air strikes. The federal government is striving to consolidate control in Southwest State following a military offensive earlier this year.
