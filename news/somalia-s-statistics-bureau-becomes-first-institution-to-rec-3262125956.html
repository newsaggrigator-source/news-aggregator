Somalia’s Statistics Bureau Becomes First Institution to Receive Swiss Funds Through Government Treasury - FTL Somalia

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 15:10:10 GMT

Somalia's National Bureau of Statistics has become the first institution to receive Swiss funding directly through the Somali government treasury, marking a significant shift in donor funding practices. This pilot project, supported by the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation, aims to strengthen national statistical capacity and is implemented through Somalia's public financial management systems rather than through parallel donor structures. The initiative reflects a growing trust in Somalia's financial management capabilities, which have historically been bypassed due to concerns over corruption and inefficiency. The project includes a research component in collaboration with Somali universities and focuses on modernizing statistical production using advanced technologies. While this pilot represents a small step, its success could pave the way for broader direct funding models, challenging the traditional reliance on international organizations for aid. However, the initiative carries risks, as the effectiveness of Somalia's financial systems remains fragile and untested on a larger scale.
