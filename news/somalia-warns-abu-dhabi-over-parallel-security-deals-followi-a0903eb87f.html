Somalia Warns Abu Dhabi Over Parallel Security Deals Following President Mohamud’s National Address - FTL Somalia

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 14:04:16 GMT

Somalia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs issued a stern warning to the United Arab Emirates (UAE) on November 18, 2026, regarding its ongoing interference in Somalia's internal affairs. This statement followed President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's national address, where he condemned the UAE for establishing parallel security arrangements that undermine Somalia's sovereignty. The government emphasized that all bilateral relations should occur exclusively through federal institutions, in line with international law. President Mohamud accused the UAE of funding divisions and supporting armed groups outside Somalia’s national command structure. Relations between the two nations have been strained since January 2026, when Somalia terminated various agreements with the UAE, asserting that the UAE did not respect Somalia's status as an independent state. The Foreign Ministry's warning reflects the challenges Somalia faces in asserting its authority amid ongoing regional divisions, which foreign powers continue to exploit.
