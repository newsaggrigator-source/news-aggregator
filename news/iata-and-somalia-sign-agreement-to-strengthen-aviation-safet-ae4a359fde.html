IATA and Somalia sign agreement to strengthen aviation safety - Horseed Media

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 18:04:37 GMT

The International Air Transport Association (IATA) has signed a significant agreement with the Somali government aimed at enhancing aviation safety in the country. This partnership is part of IATA's broader initiative to support the development of safe and efficient air transport systems globally. The agreement will focus on improving regulatory frameworks, training for aviation personnel, and implementing safety management systems. IATA's Director General emphasized the importance of collaboration in achieving high safety standards, which are crucial for the growth of Somalia's aviation sector. This agreement marks a positive step towards bolstering the country's aviation infrastructure and ensuring safer travel for passengers.
