Somalia-Türkiye ties built on development, not interference: defence minister Fiqi - TRT Afrika

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 15:49:39 GMT

Somalia's outgoing Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi has emphasized the strong ties between Somalia and Türkiye, highlighting that their relationship is founded on development rather than interference. During the inauguration of a Turkish-backed school and the foundation laying for a new hospital in Warsheikh, Fiqi noted that the partnership focuses on tangible achievements in infrastructure, education, healthcare, and emerging technologies, particularly in space. He criticized external plots aimed at destabilizing Somalia, contrasting them with Türkiye's constructive contributions. The Turkish initiatives in Warsheikh include educational and healthcare projects, as well as plans for a spaceport to enhance Somalia's technological capabilities. Fiqi underscored Türkiye's role as a key strategic partner, providing military training and support for Somalia's defense forces. He expressed gratitude to the Turkish government and its representatives for their ongoing assistance, marking a significant chapter in the bilateral relationship as he concludes his tenure.
