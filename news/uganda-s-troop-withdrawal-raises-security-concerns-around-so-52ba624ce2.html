Uganda's Troop Withdrawal Raises Security Concerns Around Somalia's Capital - Garowe Online

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 15:36:34 GMT

Uganda's recent withdrawal of troops from key military positions in southern Somalia has raised significant security concerns regarding the capital, Mogadishu. The withdrawal affects four strategically important locations—Sabid, Caanoole, Bariire, Awdheegle, and Buulo-Gaduud—crucial for connecting southern Somalia to Mogadishu and protecting vital routes from Al-Shabaab militants. Analysts warn that this troop reduction could create security gaps, allowing Al-Shabaab to regain influence in the region. Uganda has already repatriated 109 soldiers and plans to complete its withdrawal by the end of 2027. The Somali federal government intends for its national army to take over these positions, but doubts remain about their capability to maintain control without adequate manpower and resources. The situation poses a critical test for Somalia's government, which must prove its ability to independently secure strategic areas and prevent militant resurgence amid ongoing financial constraints faced by the African Union mission.
