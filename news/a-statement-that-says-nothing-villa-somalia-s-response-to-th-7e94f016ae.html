A Statement That Says Nothing: Villa Somalia’s Response to the Collapse of Its Own Dialogue Process - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 09:22:51 GMT

Villa Somalia's recent statement regarding the stalled National Consultative Forum has drawn criticism for lacking substance. Issued on November 14, 2026, the statement claimed ongoing contacts with invited parties and suggested the forum would "open soon," yet it provided no specifics on the date, venue, or attendees. This vague communication followed reports of the forum's postponement due to unresolved disagreements among parties. The Somali Future Council announced it would boycott the talks unless held under international mediation, while Nabad iyo Nolol denied accepting the invitation, accusing the government of misinformation. Despite the Quartet's involvement to facilitate discussions, the government's approach remains unchanged, treating opposition demands as manageable rather than negotiable. The Supreme Court has set a December 5 deadline for an electoral framework agreement, but the government's lack of a credible plan or acknowledgment of opposition concerns raises doubts about its commitment to a negotiated settlement. The statement has been perceived as a refusal to engage with the pressing realities of the situation.
