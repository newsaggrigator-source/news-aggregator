Turkish Envoy Alkan Meets Police Chief as Ankara Expands Security Footprint - FTL Somalia

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 15:53:06 GMT

Turkish Ambassador Ferhat Alkan recently met with Somali Police Commissioner Brigadier General Asad Osman Abdullahi to reinforce Turkey's commitment to training Somalia's elite Haramcad special police unit. This meeting, part of a high-level Turkish delegation's visit to the Somali Police Force headquarters, emphasized ongoing security cooperation between Turkey and Somalia. The Turkish Embassy highlighted that training activities for the Haramcad unit, which specializes in high-risk operations, are continuing without interruption. Turkey has previously trained over 1,000 personnel within the Somali Police Force, reflecting a broader strategy to develop elite forces capable of operating independently of the Somali National Army. Ambassador Alkan's visit aligns with his diplomatic efforts since taking office, including discussions on various sectors such as energy and health. However, the effectiveness of the Haramcad unit hinges on its integration within a comprehensive security framework, raising concerns about accountability and the potential for misuse of power.
