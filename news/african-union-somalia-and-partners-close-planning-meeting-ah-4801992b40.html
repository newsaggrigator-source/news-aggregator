African Union, Somalia and Partners Close Planning Meeting Ahead of AUSSOM Mandate Renewal - FTL Somalia

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 21:41:47 GMT

The African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) recently concluded a planning meeting in Mogadishu, led by Ambassador El Hadji Ibrahima Diene. This meeting involved representatives from the African Union, the Federal Government of Somalia, troop-contributing nations, and various UN bodies, focusing on the mission's future as the UN Security Council prepares to vote on renewing AUSSOM’s mandate in December 2026. The discussions come amid ongoing funding shortfalls, with the African Union estimating a need for approximately $190 million in 2025 to support the transition from its predecessor, ATMIS. Despite commendable coordination among international partners, key challenges remain unresolved, including sustainable funding, troop withdrawal sequencing, and ensuring Somali forces can take over security responsibilities. The upcoming UN vote will be crucial in determining AUSSOM’s future, highlighting the need for concrete commitments beyond mere planning. The meeting signals preparation for these decisions, but does not guarantee favorable outcomes. (156 words)
