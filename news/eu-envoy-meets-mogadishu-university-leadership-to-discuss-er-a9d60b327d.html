EU Envoy Meets Mogadishu University Leadership to Discuss Erasmus+ Participation - FTL Somalia

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 21:26:46 GMT

European Union Ambassador to Somalia, László Máthé, met with Mogadishu University leadership to discuss the institution's involvement in Erasmus+ and its role in enhancing higher education in Somalia. The meeting emphasized the importance of quality education for Somalia's development and innovation, although no specific agreements or funding were announced. Mogadishu University is already engaged in several Erasmus+ initiatives, including the Renewable Energy Advancement for Inclusive Development (READ) project, which aims to improve renewable energy curricula and foster collaboration across universities in Sub-Saharan Africa. Additionally, the university participates in projects focused on sustainable housing and smart city development. Despite these initiatives, the EU's academic engagement in Somalia is limited compared to the country's needs, facing challenges such as underfunded institutions and a lack of research infrastructure. Furthermore, issues like passport recognition for Somali students hinder access to scholarships, highlighting a gap between the EU's commitments and the realities faced by Somali students.
