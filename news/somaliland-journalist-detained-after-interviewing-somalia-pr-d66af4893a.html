Somaliland journalist detained after interviewing Somalia presidential hopeful - Horseed Media

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 15:34:27 GMT

A journalist from Horn Cable TV, Abdirahman Egeh, has been detained by Somaliland authorities following his interview with Abdikadir Mohamed Sharaay, a politician who announced his candidacy for Somalia's presidency in May 2027. Sharaay's interview, which aired in mid-October, sparked controversy as he advocated for Somaliland to pursue international recognition through negotiations with Somalia, rather than relying on external support. This prompted backlash from some Somaliland politicians and commentators, leading to Sharaay's arrest shortly after. The exact reasons for Egeh's detention remain unclear, and authorities have not confirmed whether it is directly related to the interview or Sharaay's arrest. The situation has raised concerns among journalists and media freedom advocates, who argue that reporters should not face legal repercussions for interviewing individuals expressing political opinions. This incident follows a pattern of increased detentions of journalists in Somaliland, highlighting ongoing tensions regarding press freedom and political expression in the region.
