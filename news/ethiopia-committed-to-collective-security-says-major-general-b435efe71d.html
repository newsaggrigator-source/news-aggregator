Ethiopia Committed to Collective Security, Says Major General Adamneh

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 16:11:03 GMT

Ethiopia's commitment to collective security was emphasized by Major General Adamneh Mengiste during the International Partners Day at the Defense Peacekeeping Center in Addis Ababa. He highlighted Ethiopia's extensive history of peacekeeping, dating back to the Korean War in 1951, and its involvement in various missions across Africa. Major General Adamneh reaffirmed the nation's dedication to global peace, underscoring the professionalism and sacrifices of Ethiopian peacekeepers, including the four personnel who lost their lives in South Sudan in 2026. Brigadier General Gezahegn Fikadu discussed the role of the International Peace Support Training Institute (IPSTI) in enhancing military and civilian training for peace operations. He stressed the importance of collaboration and shared resources among local and international partners to strengthen peace support efforts. Major General Teshome Gemechu called for renewed partnerships to ensure a stable future for Africa and the world, emphasizing the need for cooperation and knowledge sharing. The event was attended by military attaches and representatives from various international organizations.
