Malawian Nabbed in Ethiopia Over Cocaine Plot Bound for Malaysia

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 06:47:12 GMT

A 47-year-old Malawian man named Allan Gondwe has been arrested in Ethiopia for attempting to smuggle cocaine from Malawi to Malaysia, concealed within portraits. His arrest occurred on October 20, 2026, at Bole International Airport in Addis Ababa while he was en route to Kuala Lumpur. The information was confirmed by Malawi's Ambassador to Ethiopia, Khwauli Msiska, in a report dated October 28. Gondwe, who traveled on a Malawian passport, is facing drug smuggling charges under Ethiopian law. He is currently detained at the Mexico-Kera Road Federal Police Station and is expected to appear in court soon. The embassy has had limited access to him due to Ethiopian regulations. In a related case, the family of another detainee, Anne Muhammad, who was arrested in 2023 for cocaine possession, has requested that she serve her sentence in Malawi.
