Somalia army faces waves of desertions as soldiers abandon frontlines following clan leader’s call - Somali Guardian

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 18:20:09 GMT

Somalia's military is experiencing a significant crisis as soldiers from the Hawadle clan abandon their posts in response to a call from their traditional leader, Ugas Yusuf Ugas Hassan. This mass withdrawal, which has seen numerous soldiers return to Beledweyne, poses a serious challenge to the federal government's efforts to maintain military cohesion amid rising political and clan tensions. The situation is particularly precarious as Somalia transitions to greater national defense responsibility while foreign troops withdraw. Ugas Hassan has indicated plans for a potential military offensive, raising fears of escalating conflict. The departures from key regions, such as Galgaduud and Lower Shabelle, could weaken defenses against Al-Shabaab, complicating ongoing military operations. This crisis underscores the difficulties of establishing a unified national army in a context where clan loyalties often supersede institutional allegiance, threatening the effectiveness and stability of Somalia's military forces at a critical juncture in its security landscape.
