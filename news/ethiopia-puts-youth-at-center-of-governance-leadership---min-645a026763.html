Ethiopia Puts Youth At Center of Governance, Leadership - Minister

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:20:53 GMT

Ethiopia is prioritizing youth empowerment and participation in its governance and development agenda, with over 50% of federal leadership positions held by young people, according to Minister of Youth and Sports Aklilu Tadesse. Speaking at the African Youth in Politics Forum and the Africa Women Political Leadership Summit in Addis Ababa, he emphasized that young people are not just future leaders but are actively leading today. The minister highlighted the importance of investing in youth through employment, entrepreneurship, and leadership development, advocating for their meaningful involvement in governance. He called on African political parties to recognize youth as partners in democratic leadership rather than just voters. Aklilu noted that Africa's demographic structure necessitates youth engagement for the continent's prosperity and stability, urging leaders to create systems that empower young voices. He concluded that addressing challenges like unemployment and climate change requires the active participation of young people, who are essential for driving progress and transformation in Africa.
