Trump Says Ilhan Omar Could Be Deported Or Go To Jail, Calls Somalia A S***house: how 7 outlets framed it - NewsCord

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 05:58:51 GMT

During an Oval Office event, former President Donald Trump claimed that Democratic Representative Ilhan Omar could face deportation or jail time amid an ongoing immigration fraud investigation. Trump described Omar as "a sick, a very dishonest person who hates our country" and referred to Somalia, her birthplace, as a "real s***house." His comments followed a federal search of Omar's Washington home by Homeland Security Investigations and Immigration and Customs Enforcement, which was part of the inquiry into her immigration records. Omar has denied any wrongdoing, labeling the search as political intimidation. Legal experts noted that deportation typically applies to noncitizens, and any action against a naturalized citizen like Omar would require a separate legal process. The investigation is part of a broader scrutiny of alleged immigration fraud involving Somali networks in Minnesota.
