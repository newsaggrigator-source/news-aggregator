Indian Navy Rescues 20 Crew from Iranian Vessel Hijacked off Somalia - Vajiram & Ravi

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 08:07:09 GMT

On November 2, 2026, the Indian Navy successfully rescued 20 crew members from the Iranian fishing vessel Azini, which had been hijacked by pirates off the northeastern coast of Somalia. The vessel was seized on October 29, and the Indian Navy conducted a coordinated operation involving naval warships and a maritime reconnaissance aircraft to locate and track the hijacked ship. Utilizing non-kinetic measures to restrict the vessel's movement, Marine Commandos (MARCOS) were deployed via a precision airdrop, supported by an Indian Air Force C-17 aircraft. The operation lasted over 48 hours and concluded without any casualties. This mission underscores the Indian Navy's capability to conduct long-range maritime operations and its commitment to ensuring maritime security in the Indian Ocean Region, reinforcing India's role as a key player in combating piracy and protecting international shipping routes.
