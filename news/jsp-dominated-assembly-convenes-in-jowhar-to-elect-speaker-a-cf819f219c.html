JSP-Dominated Assembly Convenes in Jowhar to Elect Speaker and President - FTL Somalia

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 10:13:43 GMT

The newly elected members of Hirshabelle’s House of the People were sworn in on October 21, 2026, in Jowhar, following the Justice and Solidarity Party's (JSP) significant victory in the September 30 elections, where they secured 78 out of 99 seats. This marks the first direct election of regional lawmakers in Hirshabelle, replacing the previous indirect selection system. The assembly is set to elect a speaker and president, with applications for these positions submitted on October 21. However, political tensions persist, particularly regarding the presidency, as the JSP has nominated incumbent President Ali Abdullahi Hussein Guudlaawe, which has sparked disputes with the Hawadle community over representation. While the new parliament represents a procedural advancement, concerns about political inclusivity and legitimacy remain unresolved as the elections proceed.
