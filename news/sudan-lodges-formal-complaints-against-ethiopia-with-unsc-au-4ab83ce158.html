Sudan Lodges Formal Complaints Against Ethiopia With UNSC, AU Peace and Security Council

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 04:57:58 GMT

Sudan has formally lodged complaints against Ethiopia with the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) and the African Union Peace and Security Council (AUPSC), accusing it of permitting the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) to launch drone attacks from its territory. These allegations, made public around November 1-2, claim that since February 2026, the RSF has conducted 70 attacks using one-way drones, resulting in significant civilian casualties and damage to critical infrastructure, including power stations. Sudan reported that these attacks have killed or injured 484 civilians and resulted in the deaths of 176 soldiers. One notable incident involved an October 9 strike that killed 30 civilians in the Blue Nile region. Sudan argues that these actions violate international law and has called for urgent international intervention to protect its sovereignty and hold the RSF accountable. The ongoing conflict between Sudan's Armed Forces and the RSF has exacerbated the humanitarian crisis in the region. (158 words)
