Ugandan troops start to quit Somalia - National Security News

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 11:36:01 GMT

Uganda has commenced the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia, raising concerns about the ability of the Somali government to combat al-Shabaab, an affiliate of al-Qaeda. After nearly 20 years of military support, Uganda plans to fully withdraw its forces by the end of 2027. This includes approximately 4,500-6,000 troops deployed under the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) and an additional 2,000-2,500 under a bilateral agreement, with the latter group being the first to leave. The withdrawal coincides with growing disillusionment among Somalia's allies regarding the federal government's effectiveness in addressing security issues and ongoing political strife. As Ugandan troops depart, Somali forces are moving into their bases, but analysts warn that the exit could lead to increased al-Shabaab activity. The situation is further complicated by potential funding cuts from key supporters like the United States, which could undermine ongoing stabilization efforts in the region.
