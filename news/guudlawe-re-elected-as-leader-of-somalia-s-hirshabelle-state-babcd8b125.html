Guudlawe Re-elected as Leader of Somalia’s Hirshabelle State with 81 votes - Dawan Africa

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 13:45:08 GMT

Ali Abdullahi Hussein Guudlawe has been re-elected as the leader of Somalia’s Hirshabelle state, securing 81 votes in a recent election held in Jowhar. The announcement was made by the speaker of the state House of Representatives, Abdihakim Luqman. Guudlawe, representing the Justice and Solidarity Party, received 86.2% of the valid votes, while his opponent, Nur Ilmi Halane from the Ramaas party, garnered only 13 votes. Out of 99 members, 98 participated in the voting process, with four ballots spoiled. Additionally, Abdifatah Hassan Afrah was elected as deputy leader with 79 votes. In his acceptance speech, Guudlawe expressed gratitude to the lawmakers for their trust and emphasized his commitment to justice and unity in Hirshabelle. He noted that during his previous term, there were no disagreements among the executive, legislature, or parliament. Guudlawe has been in leadership since 2020, and his new term will last for five years.
