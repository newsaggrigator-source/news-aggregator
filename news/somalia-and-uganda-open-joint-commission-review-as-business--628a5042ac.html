Somalia and Uganda Open Joint Commission Review as Business Forum Enters Second Day - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 10:55:31 GMT

Somalia and Uganda have launched a two-day Somali-Ugandan Business Forum alongside the Mid-Term Review of the Uganda-Somalia Joint Permanent Commission (JPC) in Mogadishu. The forum, inaugurated by Somalia’s Foreign Affairs Minister Abdisalam Abdi Ali, aims to enhance cooperation in security, trade, investment, tourism, fisheries, and labor. A 35-member Ugandan delegation, led by Ambassador Richard Kabonero, is participating in discussions to translate shared priorities into tangible investment opportunities. Historically, the relationship has been dominated by security, with Uganda contributing troops to peacekeeping missions in Somalia. However, trade has lagged, with Uganda's exports to Somalia increasing from $2.75 million in 2023 to $6.78 million in 2024, while Somalia's exports remain minimal. Despite the optimism surrounding the forum, gaps in implementation of previous agreements, such as the Joint Permanent Investment and Business Council, remain. The forum has resulted in a bilateral labor agreement, but significant challenges in infrastructure and regulatory harmonization persist, hindering trade expansion.
