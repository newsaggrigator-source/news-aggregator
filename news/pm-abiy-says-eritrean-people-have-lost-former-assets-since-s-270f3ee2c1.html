PM Abiy Says Eritrean People Have Lost Former Assets Since Secession

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 12:26:00 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed stated that the Eritrean people have lost significant industrial capacity and urban vitality since Eritrea's secession from Ethiopia. In an interview with the Ethiopian Broadcasting Corporation, he highlighted that Eritrean cities, once vibrant economic centers, have deteriorated, with Asmara now smaller than many Ethiopian cities. He noted that Massawa and Assab have seen a decline in economic activity, with Massawa being "emptied" and Assab reduced to a "ghost town." Abiy attributed these issues to the Eritrean government's failure to prioritize development and warned that external confrontations would not resolve Eritrea's challenges. He emphasized the importance of friendship, good neighborliness, and development cooperation as the way forward for both nations.
