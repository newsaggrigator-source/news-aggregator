Media accreditation opens for Ghana-Somalia 2027 AFCON qualifier - Ghana Football Association

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 20:57:01 GMT

The Ghana Football Association (GFA) has announced that media accreditation for the upcoming 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifier between Ghana and Somalia is now open. Journalists and photographers wishing to cover the match at the Essipong Sports Stadium must submit their applications via email by 5:00 PM GMT on November 9, 2026. Each application must include a recent passport-sized photograph; those without will be disregarded. Successful applicants can collect their accreditation tags from designated locations based on their region. Due to limited space in the media tribune, specific allocations have been set for different media types: three for television, two for radio, and one for print/online portals. The total capacity for journalists at the stadium is 150, and a shortlist of accredited media will be published on November 9, 2026.
