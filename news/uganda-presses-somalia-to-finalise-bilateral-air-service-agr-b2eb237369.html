Uganda presses Somalia to finalise bilateral air service agreement - Daily Monitor

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 21:00:02 GMT

Uganda is urging Somalia to finalize a Bilateral Air Service Agreement (BASA) to enable Uganda Airlines to operate flights through Somalia. The call was made by Gen Henry Tumukunde, Uganda's Minister of Gender, Labour and Social Development, during discussions in Mogadishu. The BASA is crucial for establishing legal frameworks for air transport between the two nations, which is expected to enhance bilateral trade that has already seen significant growth. Uganda's exports to Somalia increased from approximately $2.75 million in 2023 to nearly $6.78 million in 2024. The agreement will also address operational details such as flight frequencies and safety protocols. Additionally, Uganda plans to sign a Bilateral Labour Agreement to protect Ugandan workers in Somalia. Both countries aim to strengthen their economic ties and remove trade barriers, with officials emphasizing the importance of tangible outcomes from their cooperation. The discussions are part of the Uganda-Somalia Joint Permanent Commission's efforts to enhance regional integration.
