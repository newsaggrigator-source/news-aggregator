Pirates hijack Iranian fishing vessel off Somalia’s northeastern coast - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 02:09:39 GMT

Armed pirates have hijacked an Iranian fishing vessel, the Azini, off Somalia’s northeastern coast, raising fears it could serve as a mothership for launching attacks on larger commercial ships. The European Union Naval Force’s Maritime Security Centre Indian Ocean reported that the vessel was seized around 0900 UTC, approximately 185 nautical miles east of Bandarbayla, with 23 crew members aboard, including 21 Iranian fishermen and two Somali security guards. The hijacking comes amid renewed piracy concerns in the region, which had seen a decline in such incidents in recent years. The Azini has since moved closer to a major shipping route, prompting warnings about its potential use by pirates. This incident follows a recent operation in August 2026, where Turkish and Somali forces rescued another hijacked vessel, highlighting ongoing security challenges in combating maritime piracy off the Somali coast.
