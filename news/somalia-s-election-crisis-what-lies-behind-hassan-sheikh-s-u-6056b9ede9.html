Somalia’s Election Crisis: What Lies Behind Hassan Sheikh’s Uganda Trip? - Garowe Online

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 12:20:35 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's recent trip to Uganda comes amid escalating political tensions surrounding the country's electoral process, which has been marred by disputes and uncertainty. His presidential term expired in May 2026, and he has invited opposition groups and leaders from Puntland and Jubaland for talks to address these issues. The timing of his visit is significant, as Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni has urged him to engage with opposition factions and seek a resolution to the political crisis. Museveni has warned that Uganda may withdraw its troops from Somalia if a consensus is not reached, raising concerns about the stability of the African Union mission against Al-Shabaab. The ongoing political deadlock not only threatens Somalia's democratic transition but also impacts security operations, as Uganda plays a crucial role in supporting Somali forces. The situation remains delicate, with the potential for significant implications for Somalia's future governance and security landscape.
