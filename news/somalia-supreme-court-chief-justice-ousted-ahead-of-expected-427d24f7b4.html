Somalia: Supreme Court Chief Justice Ousted Ahead of Expected Ruling on Constitutional Amendments - Garowe Online

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 21:46:49 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has dismissed Supreme Court Chief Justice Baashe Yusuf Ahmed, appointing Ibrahim Idle Suleiman as his successor. This decision comes just before a crucial ruling on controversial constitutional amendments, which opposition figures claim are unconstitutional. Sources suggest that Ahmed's removal was not only due to his annulment of recent Galmudug election results but also to prevent him from delivering a potentially damaging court decision against the president's administration. The anticipated ruling was expected to challenge the legitimacy of the constitutional changes that have faced significant opposition. The appointment of Suleiman, previously the president's legal affairs secretary, raises concerns about judicial independence amid ongoing political tensions. Baashe is reportedly preparing to respond to accusations from the presidency, adding to Somalia's deepening political crisis, which includes disputes over the electoral process and the legitimacy of the current political transition. Neither the presidency nor the Supreme Court has provided detailed explanations regarding the dismissal.
