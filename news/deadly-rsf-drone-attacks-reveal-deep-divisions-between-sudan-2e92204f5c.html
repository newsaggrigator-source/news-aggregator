Deadly RSF drone attacks reveal deep divisions between Sudan and Ethiopia - Al Jazeera

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 10:40:43 GMT

Recent drone attacks attributed to Sudan's Rapid Support Forces (RSF) have heightened tensions between Sudan and Ethiopia, revealing deep-rooted divisions and escalating regional conflicts. Sudanese officials have accused Ethiopia of facilitating these attacks, which have targeted civilian infrastructure, including power stations, leading to significant damage and casualties. The Sudanese government has lodged complaints with the UN Security Council and the African Union, claiming that over 70 drone assaults were launched from Ethiopian territory between February and October 2026. The situation has prompted calls within Sudan's military for a potential declaration of war against Ethiopia. Despite the rising hostilities, experts suggest that both nations are wary of a full-scale confrontation due to the potential for further destabilization. The ongoing conflict is compounded by disputes over water resources related to the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, which has strained relations between Sudan, Ethiopia, and Egypt. The humanitarian impact is severe, with millions displaced and thousands facing famine.
