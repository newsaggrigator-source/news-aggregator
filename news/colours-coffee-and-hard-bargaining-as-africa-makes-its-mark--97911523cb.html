Colours, Coffee And Hard Bargaining As Africa Makes Its Mark At COP31

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 17:04:00 GMT

At COP31 in Antalya, Africa showcased its vibrant culture, including traditional attire and Ethiopian coffee, while emphasizing its critical mineral resources essential for the global energy transition. Delegates from various African nations highlighted the importance of cobalt, lithium, and manganese in discussions about investment and technology, aiming to retain more value from these resources through local processing facilities. Ethiopia, preparing to host COP32 in 2027, set up a pavilion to promote its climate initiatives and traditional coffee ceremonies. The African Group of Negotiators focused on securing adaptation finance and addressing the impacts of climate-related trade measures, seeking clarity on commitments made at previous summits. The summit also featured discussions on the effects of climate change on health, with initiatives like Unitaid's SHIELD program addressing the stability of essential medicines in extreme heat. Overall, African representatives called for increased investment in renewable energy and infrastructure to support their climate goals and development needs.
