US Forces Carry Out Overnight Airstrikes Against ISIS Positions in Somalia’s Puntland Region - Horseed Media

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 17:12:48 GMT

US forces conducted targeted airstrikes in Somalia's Puntland region, specifically in the Qandala district, against positions believed to be used by ISIS militants. The operation, which took place overnight, was part of ongoing efforts to dismantle ISIS's presence in the strategically significant Al-Miskad mountain range. The airstrikes focused on areas such as Dabalyal and Halbaladi, following four weeks of intelligence gathering that identified concentrations of ISIS operatives involved in planning attacks. Security assessments indicate that ISIS has maintained a stronghold in the Ja’il Valley, utilizing it for command and operational activities. Despite ongoing military pressure, including recent operations by Puntland's Darawiish forces, concerns remain about ISIS's ability to regroup and launch further attacks. The group, which has been active in Puntland since 2015, reportedly consists of 200 to 300 fighters, many of whom are foreign recruits. The airstrikes underscore the importance of intelligence-led operations in combating ISIS's resurgence in the region.
