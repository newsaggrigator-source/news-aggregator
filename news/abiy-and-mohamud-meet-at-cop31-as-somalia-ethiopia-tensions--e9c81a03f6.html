Abiy and Mohamud Meet at COP31 as Somalia-Ethiopia Tensions Simmer Beneath Climate Talks - FTL Somalia

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 13:04:54 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed and Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud met at the COP31 Leaders’ Summit in Antalya, marking their first encounter since Somalia's diplomatic shift towards Egypt and Algeria heightened tensions with Ethiopia. The leaders discussed the security situation in Somalia and regional developments, emphasizing the need for ongoing dialogue and cooperation. However, their brief exchange did not yield any specific agreements. The meeting occurs amid complex relations shaped by competing security partnerships and unresolved sovereignty issues, particularly regarding Somaliland. Somalia’s growing military ties with Egypt, including troop deployments, are viewed by Ethiopia as a strategic threat. Additionally, Somalia's alignment with the Alamein Declaration, which rejects recognition of Somaliland, further complicates matters. While the meeting is a positive sign of continued communication, the underlying tensions remain unresolved, requiring sustained negotiation and potential external mediation to address the broader regional dynamics influencing their relationship.
