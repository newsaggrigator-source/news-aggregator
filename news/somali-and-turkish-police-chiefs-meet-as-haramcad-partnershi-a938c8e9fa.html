Somali and Turkish Police Chiefs Meet as Haramcad Partnership Continues - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 12:59:13 GMT

Somali Police Commissioner Brigadier General Asad Osman Abdullahi recently met with General Hüseyin Ediz Tercanoğlu, the Commander of the Turkish Police Special Operations Department, in Türkiye to enhance security cooperation. Their discussions focused on improving the operational capabilities of the Somali Police Force, particularly the Haramcad special police unit, which has received extensive training and equipment from Turkey. This partnership aims to strengthen Somalia's security framework amid ongoing political tensions in Mogadishu. Critics argue that Turkey's support may be too closely aligned with President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's administration, raising concerns about potential political bias and civilian harm during operations. The effectiveness of the Haramcad unit, while improved through training, is contingent upon its integration within a broader security architecture that includes accountability mechanisms. As the partnership continues, the challenge remains to ensure that the unit serves the public interest rather than merely the political agenda of the current government.
