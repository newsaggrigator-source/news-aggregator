SoDMA Delegation Arrives in Dhusamareb Ahead of Somalia Country Humanitarian Forum - www.ftlsomalia.com

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 14:58:55 GMT

A delegation from the Somali Disaster Management Agency (SoDMA), led by Deputy Commissioner Dr. Ahmed Abdi Adan, arrived in Dhusamareb on November 24, 2026, to participate in the Somalia Country Humanitarian Forum. The team was welcomed by Galmudug’s outgoing Minister of Humanitarian Affairs, Abdirahman Makaawi, and other local officials. The forum aims to enhance coordination among federal disaster management officials, regional authorities, and humanitarian stakeholders to address emergency response strategies in vulnerable districts. This visit occurs amid a political transition in Galmudug, following the inauguration of President Liibaan Ahmed Hassan and a new Council of Ministers. As Somalia faces severe climate challenges and funding shortages, SoDMA's presence underscores the urgency of addressing humanitarian needs exacerbated by droughts, floods, and conflict. Effective coordination and operational protocols will be crucial to ensure timely assistance for displaced families in the region, particularly during this administrative transition.
