Jowhar Celebrates Guudlaawe’s Return While Hiiraan Mobilizes for Confrontation - FTL Somalia

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 16:17:23 GMT

Jowhar is celebrating the return of re-elected Hirshabelle President Ali Abdullahi Hussein Guudlaawe, with a ceremony attended by senior officials from the Federal Government and regional administrations. Guudlaawe, representing the Justice and Solidarity Party (JSP), secured a commanding majority in the regional parliament, reinforcing the party's control over Hirshabelle. However, the Hawadle community in Hiiraan is in open revolt against his re-election, claiming it violates a prior agreement that allocated the presidency to them. Tensions escalated as Hawadle clan leader Ugaas Yusuf called for armed mobilization in Beledweyne, warning of a federal government attack. Reports indicate that local residents and defecting soldiers are preparing for confrontation. The situation poses a significant challenge to the JSP's authority, as the mobilization could undermine efforts against Al-Shabaab and reflects deep political divisions. While the inauguration in Jowhar symbolizes JSP's dominance, it does not resolve the ongoing crisis in Hiiraan, where dissent continues to grow.
