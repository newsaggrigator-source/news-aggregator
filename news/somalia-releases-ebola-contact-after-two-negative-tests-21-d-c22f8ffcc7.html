Somalia releases Ebola contact after two negative tests, 21-day quarantine - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 01:09:00 GMT

Somalia's Health Ministry announced the release of a man from Ebola quarantine after he tested negative twice and completed a 21-day monitoring period without showing any symptoms. The individual had been isolated following direct contact with a person infected with the Bundibugyo Ebola virus, who later died in Kenya. He was transferred to an Ebola treatment center in Mogadishu on October 12, where he underwent two tests—one locally and another sent abroad for confirmation—both of which returned negative. The ministry confirmed that no Ebola cases have been reported in Somalia. Health Minister Dr. Ali Haji Adan emphasized the importance of ongoing surveillance as the regional outbreak evolves, particularly in light of Kenya's confirmed cases linked to the original imported infection. As of October 20, Kenya had reported nine cases, including fatalities, related to this outbreak. Somali health authorities continue to monitor the situation closely.
