Gargash says UAE-Somalia ties deeper than political differences - Emirates 24|7

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 14:09:00 GMT

Dr. Anwar Gargash, the UAE Presidential Diplomatic Advisor, emphasized that the relationship between the UAE and Somalia transcends political disagreements. In a recent statement, he criticized the outgoing Somali President's stance, asserting that the ties between the two nations are rooted in a deep historical connection with the Somali people. Gargash highlighted the UAE's commitment to supporting Somalia, particularly in areas such as humanitarian aid and development initiatives. He reaffirmed that despite any political differences, the UAE remains dedicated to fostering a strong partnership with Somalia, focusing on mutual interests and cooperation. This statement reflects the UAE's broader foreign policy approach, which seeks to strengthen ties with various nations while navigating complex political landscapes. The UAE's involvement in Somalia has included various initiatives aimed at stability and development, showcasing its role as a key player in the region.
