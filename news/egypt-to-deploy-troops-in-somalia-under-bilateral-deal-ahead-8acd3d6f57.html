Egypt to Deploy Troops in Somalia Under Bilateral Deal Ahead of Aussom Integration

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 04:57:23 GMT

Egypt is set to deploy troops to Somalia under a bilateral military cooperation agreement, ahead of integrating approximately 1,000 personnel into the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM). The initial deployment will consist of a brigade-sized battle group stationed in Middle Shabelle, with a tactical headquarters planned at Ceelcade, currently held by Burundian troops. Concerns have arisen regarding operational coordination and command structures, as the African Union has not clarified how the Egyptian forces will integrate with AUSSOM. Additionally, Burundi's troops have not been formally notified of the changes and are prepared to withdraw once replaced. Egypt's military cooperation with Somalia, which began in August 2024, includes weapon shipments and military training. The deployment comes amid financial pressures on AUSSOM and regional tensions, particularly between Egypt and Ethiopia over the Nile waters. Analysts warn that competing security partnerships could complicate Somalia's security arrangements. (159 words)
