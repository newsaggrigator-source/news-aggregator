Türkiye plans $400M investment in space programme, advances plans for spaceport in Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 19:30:33 GMT

Türkiye is set to invest approximately $400 million in its space program over the next three years, focusing on lunar exploration and the development of a spaceport in Somalia. This funding aims to enhance the capabilities of the Turkish Space Agency and reduce reliance on foreign aerospace technology. The investment will support high-tech entrepreneurship, research and development, and the domestic production of essential space systems. Annual allocations are projected to increase from $117.2 million in 2027 to $147.4 million by 2029. Key initiatives include achieving a hard landing on the moon by next year and establishing a spaceport in Somalia for independent satellite launches. Additionally, the Turkish Space Agency plans to develop advanced propulsion systems and a space weather application center, with significant progress expected by 2029. This ambitious plan underscores Türkiye's commitment to advancing its space capabilities and fostering innovation in the aerospace sector.
