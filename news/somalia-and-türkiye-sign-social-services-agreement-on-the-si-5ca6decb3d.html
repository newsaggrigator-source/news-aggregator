Somalia and Türkiye Sign Social Services Agreement on the Sidelines of COP31 - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 08:53:17 GMT

Somalia and Türkiye have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to enhance cooperation in social services, family protection, and the rights of women and children during COP31 in Antalya. The agreement, signed by Somalia’s Minister of Family and Human Rights Development, Amb. Khadija Mohamed Al-Makhzoumi, and Turkish Minister Mahinur Özdemir Göktaş, aims to support families, empower women, and protect children's rights, including measures against violence and risks in digital environments. The MoU also emphasizes health, education, and rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities and the elderly, with plans for joint training and expert exchanges. While the agreement marks a significant expansion of the Somalia-Türkiye partnership beyond security and economic ties, its success will depend on effective implementation, as previous agreements have often lacked concrete outcomes. The focus on social issues is critical given Somalia's high rates of gender-based violence and the vulnerability of children.
