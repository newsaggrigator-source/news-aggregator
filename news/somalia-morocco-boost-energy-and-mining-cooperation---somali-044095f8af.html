Somalia, Morocco Boost Energy and Mining Cooperation - Somali National News Agency

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 08:09:06 GMT

Somalia and Morocco have agreed to enhance cooperation in the energy and mining sectors. The agreement was reached during a meeting between Somali Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources, Abdirahman Mohamed Abdullahi, and Moroccan Minister of Energy Transition and Sustainable Development, Leila Benali. Both nations aim to share expertise and resources to develop their energy and mining industries. The collaboration is expected to facilitate investment opportunities and promote sustainable practices in these sectors. This partnership reflects a growing trend of African nations working together to leverage their natural resources for economic development.
