Deputy Prime Minister Oversees Ports Ministry Handover as Macalin Takes Office - FTL Somalia

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 11:05:03 GMT

Deputy Prime Minister Jibril Abdirashid Haji Abdi oversaw the handover ceremony at the Ministry of Ports and Maritime Transport, where Abdulkadir Omar Macalin officially took over from acting minister Abdulkadir Mohamed. This transition follows Macalin's appointment on October 17, filling the vacancy left by Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur, who became Speaker of the House. The Deputy Prime Minister emphasized the government's commitment to enhancing port administration and services, crucial for Somalia's sustainable development. However, Macalin's dual role as a federal lawmaker has raised constitutional concerns, as the new constitution prohibits cabinet members from holding parliamentary seats. The ministry, which manages Somalia's port infrastructure and maritime policies, faces immediate challenges, including disputes over port tariffs and customs revenue harmonization. The handover formalizes Macalin's responsibilities but does not resolve ongoing legal issues, leaving significant work ahead in a sector vital to Somalia's economic and security landscape.
