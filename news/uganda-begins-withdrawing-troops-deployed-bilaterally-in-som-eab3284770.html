Uganda begins withdrawing troops deployed bilaterally in Somalia - Somali Guardian

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 17:28:33 GMT

Uganda has initiated the withdrawal of its troops deployed in Somalia under bilateral agreements with the Somali federal government, according to a statement from the Uganda People’s Defence Forces (UPDF) spokesperson, Chris Magezi. The phased withdrawal began immediately, with the first group of troops already returning to Uganda. The UPDF plans to complete the withdrawal of all its forces, including those involved in the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), by the end of 2027. This move signifies the conclusion of Uganda's two-decade-long peacekeeping and stabilization efforts in Somalia, allowing the Somali Armed Forces to take full responsibility for the country's security. The decision reflects a significant shift in the security landscape of Somalia as it transitions towards self-reliance in maintaining peace and stability.
