Somalia condemns Netanyahu map showing Somaliland, Puntland separately from rest of country - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 21:51:13 GMT

The Somali federal government has condemned a map presented by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu that depicts Somaliland and Puntland separately from the rest of Somalia, asserting that it undermines the country's internationally recognized borders. During a security briefing on October 18, Netanyahu displayed the map, which categorized Somaliland and Puntland as territories friendly to Israel and the UAE, while the remainder of Somalia was grouped with Türkiye, Egypt, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia. Somalia's Information Ministry issued a statement rejecting the map as misleading and a violation of its sovereignty, emphasizing that both regions are integral parts of the Somali nation. The controversy arises amid already strained relations following Israel's recognition of Somaliland in December 2025, a move Somalia deems an infringement on its territorial integrity. The Somali government has sought support from international organizations to uphold its sovereignty and unity, highlighting the growing geopolitical tensions in the Horn of Africa.
