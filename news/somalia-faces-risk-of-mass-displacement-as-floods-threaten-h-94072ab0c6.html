Somalia faces risk of mass displacement as floods threaten hundreds of thousands - Horseed Media

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:51:40 GMT

Somalia is facing a critical situation as heavy rainfall and rising river levels threaten to displace approximately 874,000 people over the next three months, according to the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The warning highlights the risk of widespread flooding from October to December, which could leave many without homes or livelihoods. This crisis is exacerbated by the ongoing recovery from prolonged drought and previous displacements. IOM's chief of mission in Somalia, Manuel Marques Pereira, emphasized the urgency of the situation, stating that many affected families have already lost their homes and have limited resources to cope. He called for immediate funding to help protect homes and livelihoods and to facilitate safe relocation. Humanitarian organizations are concerned that the impending floods could deepen the existing humanitarian crisis, leading to severe food shortages and famine. The situation is further complicated by El Niño conditions, which can disrupt weather patterns and increase the risk of flooding in vulnerable regions.
