Ethiopia, Sudan close consulates over alleged civil war interference - Free Malaysia Today

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 06:56:06 GMT

Ethiopia and Sudan have closed each other's consulates amid escalating tensions over allegations of interference in each other's internal conflicts. Sudan accuses Ethiopia of supporting the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF), while Ethiopia claims Sudan is backing rebels in its Tigray region. This diplomatic rift follows a civil war in Sudan that began in April 2023, displacing over eight million people and creating a severe humanitarian crisis. Sudan has lodged complaints with the UN Security Council, alleging that Ethiopia allows the RSF to launch attacks from its territory. In response, Ethiopia closed the Sudanese consulate in Assosa and expelled its diplomats, prompting Sudan to reciprocate by closing the Ethiopian consulate in Gedaref. Both nations deny the accusations against each other, but fears persist that their conflicts could merge, leading to a broader regional crisis in the Horn of Africa. Sudan's government emphasizes its commitment to avoiding open war with Ethiopia.
