Somalia passes Digital Transactions Bill with 137 votes - tech dot africa

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 12:46:44 GMT

Somalia's House of the People has successfully passed the Digital Transactions Bill, receiving 137 votes in favor during a plenary session on October 21, 2026. This legislation aims to establish a comprehensive legal framework for digital payments, aligning Somalia's regulations with technological advancements and the increasing demand for digital services. The Ministry of Communications and Technology, which collaborated with various institutions to draft the bill, highlighted its significance in regulating electronic payment systems, digital currencies, and financial transactions on digital platforms. The widespread use of mobile money services in Somalia underscores the necessity for a clear legal structure to bolster economic development and enhance public confidence in digital transactions. Minister Mohamed Adam Moalim expressed gratitude to the MPs and the parliamentary committee for their efforts in advancing the bill. However, the statement did not clarify the bill's specific provisions, its effective date, or the regulatory body responsible for enforcement, leaving some details pending.
