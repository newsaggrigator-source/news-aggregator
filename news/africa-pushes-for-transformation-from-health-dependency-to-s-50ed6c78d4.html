Africa Pushes for Transformation From Health Dependency to Self-Reliance

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 12:39:58 GMT

The Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026) is currently taking place in Addis Ababa, focusing on transforming the continent's health sector from dependency to self-reliance. Under the theme "Africa's Health Security and Sovereignty: Transformation from Health Dependency and Vulnerability to Ownership and Resilience," the conference gathers heads of state, public health experts, and officials to discuss strategies for enhancing Africa's health systems. The aim is to foster greater ownership, institutional capacity, and resilience in addressing public health challenges. Emphasizing African-led solutions and regional cooperation, the conference seeks to mobilize resources and build sustainable health infrastructures. By prioritizing health sovereignty, the gathering reflects a collective ambition for African nations to take charge of their health priorities and reduce reliance on external support. Addis Ababa serves as a pivotal platform for advancing this vision, reinforcing the continent's commitment to a healthier and more self-reliant future. (158 words)
