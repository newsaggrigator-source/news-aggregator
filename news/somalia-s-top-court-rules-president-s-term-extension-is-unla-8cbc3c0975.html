Somalia’s top court rules president’s term extension is unlawful, deepening power struggle - The Killeen Daily Herald

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 14:14:50 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court has declared President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term extension unlawful, intensifying the ongoing power struggle in the country. The court ruled that Mohamud's term had expired and mandated elections within 90 days, following his dismissal of the chief justice just a day prior. The ruling contradicts the president's claim that constitutional amendments allowed him to remain in office until 2027. The court's decision comes amid escalating tensions, particularly after the closure of the UN's political mission, which had facilitated negotiations among rival factions. The judges have given political leaders 30 days to agree on an election process while limiting the government's actions to routine administration. The situation remains precarious, with concerns about the enforcement of the ruling and the potential for further conflict, especially as armed clashes have already erupted in the capital, Mogadishu. The political landscape is further complicated by differing views on electoral processes and security challenges posed by the militant group al-Shabab.
