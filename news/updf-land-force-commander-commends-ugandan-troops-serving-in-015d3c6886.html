UPDF Land Force Commander Commends Ugandan Troops Serving in Somalia - Horseed Media

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 15:50:11 GMT

Lieutenant General Kayanja Muhanga, the UPDF Land Force Commander, praised Ugandan troops serving in Somalia under the United Nations Guard Unit (UNGU 12) for their dedication to protecting UN facilities and personnel in Mogadishu. During a recent operational visit, he addressed the troops at a baraza held at the UN compound, emphasizing the importance of discipline, integrity, and physical fitness for effective mission execution. He noted that the success of their operations relies on professionalism and adherence to military standards. The visit aimed to assess the troops' performance, welfare, and the ongoing drawdown process. Lieutenant Colonel Nathan Akankunda, the Commanding Officer of UNGU 12, expressed gratitude for the UPDF leadership's support regarding personnel welfare. The baraza allowed both senior and junior officers to engage with Lt Gen Muhanga on deployment matters, highlighting the crucial role Ugandan forces play in supporting UN operations in Somalia.
