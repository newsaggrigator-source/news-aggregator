Ethiopia Pavilion Officially Opens At COP31 in Antalya, T&#xFC;rkiye

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 12:21:53 GMT

The Ethiopia Pavilion was officially inaugurated at the 31st United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Türkiye, on November 9, 2026. The event was attended by Gedion Timothewos, the Minister of Foreign Affairs and COP32 President-Designate, and Fitsum Assefa, the Minister of Finance and COP32 Chief Negotiator. The pavilion aims to showcase Ethiopia's leadership in climate initiatives, including the Green Legacy Initiative, renewable energy projects, and climate-resilient economic policies. It serves as a platform for Ethiopia to present its strategic preparations for hosting COP32, emphasizing its role as a significant voice in global climate diplomacy and sustainable development. The opening ceremony attracted diplomats, members of the Ethiopian delegation, and representatives from international organizations, highlighting Ethiopia's commitment to addressing climate change and promoting environmental sustainability on a global scale.
