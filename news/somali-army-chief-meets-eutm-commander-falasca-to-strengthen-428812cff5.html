Somali Army Chief Meets EUTM Commander Falasca to Strengthen Military Training Cooperation - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:40:46 GMT

Somali Army Chief Brigadier General Ibrahim Mohamed Mahmoud met with Brigadier General Ivan Falasca, the newly appointed Commander of the European Union Training Mission in Somalia (EUTM-Somalia), to enhance military training cooperation. The discussions aimed to improve the quality of training for Somali soldiers and officers, focusing on developing their skills and knowledge to create a professional national force capable of addressing the country's security needs. General Falasca's appointment follows a leadership transition in EUTM-Somalia, which has been active since 2010, and he inherits a mission that has trained thousands of Somali soldiers. The EU has allocated €75 million for the African Union mission in Somalia, supporting initiatives to professionalize the Somali National Armed Forces. Italy plays a significant role in this training effort, leading both the EUTM and a bilateral training mission for Somali and Djiboutian police forces. Despite these efforts, the Somali National Army still relies heavily on international support for training and resources, indicating ongoing challenges in achieving self-sufficiency.
