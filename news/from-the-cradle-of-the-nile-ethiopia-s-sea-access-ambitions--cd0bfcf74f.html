From The Cradle Of The Nile: Ethiopia’s Sea-Access Ambitions Take Center Stage At Tana - The Reporter Ethiopia

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 07:41:11 GMT

Ethiopia's aspirations for sea access were a focal point at the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, held in Bahir Dar, a city symbolically located at the source of the Blue Nile. The summit, themed "Pathways to Peace in Africa: Economic Sovereignty and Geopolitical Transformation," brought together policymakers and military leaders to discuss pressing regional issues. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed emphasized the necessity of maritime access for Ethiopia's over 130 million citizens, framing it as essential for economic stability rather than a diplomatic luxury. However, analysts raised concerns about the feasibility of these ambitions amid regional tensions and the complex geopolitical landscape of the Red Sea. The forum also addressed broader security challenges in Africa, including the ongoing conflicts in Sudan and the DRC, highlighting the need for economic sovereignty to achieve lasting peace. The discussions underscored the interconnectedness of security and economic stability across the continent. (157 words)
