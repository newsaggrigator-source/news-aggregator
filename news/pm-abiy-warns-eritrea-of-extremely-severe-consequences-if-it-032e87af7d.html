PM Abiy Warns Eritrea of 'Extremely Severe' Consequences If It Rejects Ethiopia's Peace Call Amid Rising Tensions

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 10:36:55 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has issued a stern warning to Eritrea, threatening "extremely severe" consequences if it continues to reject Ethiopia's calls for peace and cooperation. He accused Eritrea of supporting destabilizing forces against Ethiopia and urged its government to focus on economic development instead. In a recent interview, Abiy also addressed the Tigrayan youth, encouraging them to abandon armed struggle and participate in disarmament efforts, criticizing the Tigrayan People's Liberation Front (TPLF) for prolonging conflict. He emphasized the need for Tigray's reconstruction and urged its residents to work with federal authorities until a democratic government is established. Abiy's remarks come amid rising tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea, exacerbated by ongoing conflicts in northern Ethiopia.
