Satellite images reveal the UAE transformed civilian airports in breakaway regions of Somalia into large military facilities. Flight tracking data shows the U.S. has made visits. Our visual investigations reporter Christoph Koettl explains. - Facebook

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 08:25:19 GMT

Satellite imagery has uncovered that the United Arab Emirates (UAE) has converted civilian airports in the breakaway regions of Somalia into substantial military installations. These transformations indicate a significant shift in the use of these facilities, which were originally intended for civilian air traffic. Additionally, flight tracking data suggests that the United States has conducted visits to these military sites, raising questions about the nature of U.S. involvement in the region. The findings highlight the strategic interests of both the UAE and the U.S. in Somalia, particularly in the context of regional security and military operations. This development underscores the ongoing geopolitical dynamics in the Horn of Africa, where military infrastructure is increasingly being prioritized over civilian needs. The report by visual investigations reporter Christoph Koettl sheds light on these critical changes and their implications for the region.
