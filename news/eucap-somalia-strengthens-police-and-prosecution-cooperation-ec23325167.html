EUCAP Somalia Strengthens Police and Prosecution Cooperation Through Joint Criminal Investigation Training - EEAS

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 13:30:00 GMT

From November 8 to 12, 2026, EUCAP Somalia conducted a joint training program aimed at enhancing cooperation between the Attorney General’s Office (AGO), Criminal Investigation Department (CID), and Marine Coast Unit (MCU). The training focused on critical skills such as interrogation techniques, interviewing, and the preparation of criminal cases for court. This five-day initiative aimed to bolster participants' abilities in gathering and preserving evidence, conducting effective interviews, and building stronger prosecution cases. Additionally, it facilitated the exchange of experiences among officials from various institutions, promoting improved information sharing and a clearer understanding of their roles within the criminal justice system. Delivered by both Somali and international experts, the training integrated international standards with the local context, fostering closer collaboration between law enforcement and prosecution entities, ultimately contributing to a more effective justice delivery system in Somalia.
