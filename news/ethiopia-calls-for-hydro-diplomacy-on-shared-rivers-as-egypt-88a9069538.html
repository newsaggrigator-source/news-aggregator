Ethiopia Calls for Hydro-Diplomacy On Shared Rivers As Egypt's El-Sisi Reiterates Water Security Is a 'Red Line'

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 04:57:22 GMT

Ethiopia has called for enhanced regional cooperation and hydro-diplomacy regarding shared water resources, particularly in light of ongoing tensions with Egypt over the Nile River. During Ethiopia's Water and Energy Week, officials emphasized the importance of constructive engagement and science-based policymaking to address shared water challenges. Conversely, Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi reiterated that Egypt's water security is a "red line," asserting that any compromise on this issue would be unacceptable. He highlighted the Nile's critical role in providing over 98% of Egypt's renewable water resources and called for prior notification and consultation among Nile Basin countries. Despite both nations advocating for cooperation, their statements reveal a lack of progress in resolving disputes related to the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD). The challenge remains reconciling Ethiopia's development goals with Egypt's water security concerns, as well as addressing Sudan's interests as a downstream country.
