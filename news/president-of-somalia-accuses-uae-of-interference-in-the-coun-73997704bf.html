President of Somalia accuses UAE of 'interference' in the country - Middle East Eye

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:03:00 GMT

President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud of Somalia has accused the United Arab Emirates (UAE) of interfering in Somalia's internal affairs. In a televised address, he claimed that the UAE has established military bases outside the national security framework and funded armed groups opposing the central government. Mohamud emphasized that Somalia should not be treated as a territory to be exploited or traversed without permission. Tensions between the two nations have escalated, particularly due to the UAE's support for the breakaway region of Somaliland. Earlier this year, Somalia annulled agreements with the UAE and restricted its airspace to Emirati military and cargo flights. The UAE has been implicated in signing deals at key Somali ports without government consent, and it has been linked to Israel's recognition of Somaliland. Somaliland, which declared independence in 1991, has attracted foreign interest, including potential military bases from both the UAE and Israel.
