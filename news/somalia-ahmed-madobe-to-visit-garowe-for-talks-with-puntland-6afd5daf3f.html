Somalia: Ahmed Madobe to Visit Garowe for Talks With Puntland President Amid Election Dispute - Garowe Online

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 19:11:58 GMT

Jubaland President Ahmed Madobe is set to visit Garowe for discussions with Puntland President Said Abdullahi Deni amid ongoing disputes regarding Somalia's electoral process. This visit follows the postponement of a crucial meeting in Mogadishu, originally scheduled for November 14, called by Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. The talks between Madobe and Deni aim to address the political tensions surrounding the electoral arrangements and seek a negotiated framework for future elections. Both Puntland and Jubaland have been critical of the federal government's handling of the electoral process, raising concerns about political consensus as Somalia approaches another electoral cycle. The delay of the Mogadishu meeting, which was intended to unite federal representatives and political stakeholders, has further complicated the situation, with no new date announced amid opposition boycotts. Madobe's visit is seen as an opportunity for vital consultations in light of these challenges.
