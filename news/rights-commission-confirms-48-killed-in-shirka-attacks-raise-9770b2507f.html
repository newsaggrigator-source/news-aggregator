Rights Commission Confirms 48 Killed in Shirka Attacks, Raises Alarm Over Informal Armed Groups

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 10:32:19 GMT

The Ethiopian Human Rights Commission (EHRC) has confirmed that 48 individuals were killed during attacks in Shirka Woreda, Southeast Oromia, on October 24. This figure aligns with earlier reports based on eyewitness accounts. The attacks, attributed to an armed group known as the Oromo Liberation Army (OLA), resulted in significant casualties, including 23 security personnel, five militia members, and 13 civilians, alongside injuries to 20 others. The violence also led to the destruction of homes and the displacement of 247 families. The EHRC expressed alarm over the prevalence of informal armed groups and urged the government to enhance civilian protection and address security challenges. The Commission called for a thorough investigation into the incidents and emphasized the need for effective measures to prevent future attacks. In response to the violence, the Oromia regional government has detained 73 individuals linked to the attacks and is providing assistance to displaced residents.
