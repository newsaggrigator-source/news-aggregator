Somalia unveils new electronic passport in drive to regional integration - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 17:49:04 GMT

Somalia has introduced a new third-generation electronic passport as part of its efforts to modernize travel documentation and enhance immigration cooperation within the East African Community (EAC). The passport was unveiled during a recent meeting of immigration chiefs in Kampala, Uganda, attended by Somalia’s Director-General of the Immigration and Citizenship Agency, Hussein Qasim Yusuf. This new travel document features advanced security measures and reflects Somalia’s cultural heritage. The initiative aligns with Somalia's commitment to harmonizing immigration procedures and facilitating regional mobility, following its official accession to the EAC in March 2024. The EAC electronic passport, which replaces older national passports, aims to strengthen travel security and improve border management. Somalia's passport currently ranks 99th globally, allowing access to 32 destinations without a visa. The introduction of this electronic passport marks a significant step in Somalia's integration into regional frameworks and its modernization efforts.
