Somalia finally joins the Africa free trade agreement - The EastAfrican

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:00:00 GMT

Somalia has officially joined the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), becoming the 50th State Party to the agreement. The formal ratification took place when Somalia deposited its instrument at the African Union headquarters in Addis Ababa on September 4, 2026. This membership allows Somali businesses and investors preferential access to a market of over 1.4 billion consumers, significantly enhancing trade opportunities. AfCFTA Secretary-General Wamkele Mene announced Somalia's membership during the 26th Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (Comesa) Summit in Harare, Zimbabwe. The Somali government views this agreement as a vital platform for accelerating industrialization, attracting foreign investment, and fostering economic growth. Minister of Commerce and Industry Gamal Mohamed Hassan emphasized the importance of aligning Somalia's tariff schedules with AfCFTA commitments and engaging with the private sector to maximize benefits. Somalia's participation is expected to strengthen regional value chains and contribute to a more integrated African economy, alongside its existing memberships in the East African Community and Comesa.
