Somalia dismisses security vacuum fears as Ugandan troops withdraw - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 09:00:11 GMT

The Somali federal government has dismissed fears of a security vacuum following the withdrawal of Ugandan troops, asserting that the Somali National Army (SNA) is ready to take over their positions. Information Minister Abdifatah Qasim Mohamud emphasized that the government has invested significantly in training and strengthening its armed forces in recent years. He noted that Ugandan troops have already begun handing over some positions to Somali forces as part of a planned security transition. Despite concerns from some lawmakers about the SNA's readiness, Abdifatah argued that Somalia must not remain reliant on foreign troops for security. He highlighted the SNA's role in liberating areas from the militant group al-Shabab, countering claims that the country is unprepared for the transition. While international officials have raised concerns about potential vulnerabilities, the Somali government remains confident in its military's capacity to manage security as foreign forces withdraw.
