UAE divided Libya and Yemen; It now seeks the same for Somalia and Sudan, president Mohamud declares - Somali Guardian

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 17:04:02 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has publicly criticized the United Arab Emirates (UAE), accusing it of fostering division in Libya and Yemen and now attempting to do the same in Somalia and Sudan. This statement, made in a video message, comes amid a political crisis in Somalia, where the Constitutional Court recently ruled the extension of the president's term unlawful, demanding elections within 90 days. The government has rejected this ruling, leading to tensions with opposition leaders and regional administrations, resulting in military clashes. The situation is further complicated by armed uprisings and insurgencies across the country. Mohamud's remarks appear to be a strategic move to divert attention from these internal challenges, as Somalia's fragile government struggles to establish a consensus for free and fair elections, which are essential for a political transition. The ongoing instability highlights the urgent need for a resolution to the electoral impasse.
