Africa's biggest airline digs into hackers targeting 5.38 million customer accounts as it connects the U.S., Canada and South Africa - Business Insider Africa

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 21:35:54 GMT

Ethiopian Airlines, Africa's largest airline, is addressing a cybersecurity incident involving its ShebaMiles loyalty program, which has over 5.38 million member accounts. The airline has launched a thorough investigation into the breach but reassured customers that their financial data remains secure, with no evidence suggesting that such information was accessed. The airline promptly informed affected members about the incident, describing it as a "cybersecurity incident of limited scope." Despite the breach, Ethiopian Airlines confirmed that its flight operations connecting the U.S., Canada, and South Africa were not disrupted. The airline continues to operate normally while cybersecurity teams assess the situation and reinforce network systems. CEO Mesfin Tasew emphasized the importance of safeguarding customer information and maintaining operational integrity during this challenging period. The airline's commitment to customer security and uninterrupted service remains a priority as it navigates this cybersecurity challenge.
