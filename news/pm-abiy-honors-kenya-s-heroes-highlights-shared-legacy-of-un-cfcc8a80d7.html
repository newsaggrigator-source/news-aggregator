PM Abiy Honors Kenya's Heroes, Highlights Shared Legacy of Unity

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 13:37:04 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed honored Kenya's freedom fighters during the Mashujaa Day celebrations in Eldoret, emphasizing the shared legacy of courage and unity between the two nations. In his remarks, he acknowledged the sacrifices made by Kenya's heroes and drew parallels to Ethiopia's own history, particularly the Battle of Adwa, where unity was crucial in defending the homeland. PM Abiy stressed the importance of collaboration between Ethiopia and Kenya in areas such as clean energy, telecommunications, and trade, aiming to foster greater opportunities and prosperity for both countries. He underscored that their intertwined histories should guide their future cooperation, particularly in regional security and economic integration across the Horn of Africa.
