Today's top news: Afghanistan, Cyprus, Sudan, Somalia - DPPA

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 21:42:11 GMT

On November 2, 2026, the United Nations highlighted key developments in Afghanistan, Cyprus, Sudan, and Somalia. In Afghanistan, UNAMA's Special Representative Rabab Fatima visited Nangarhar Province, engaging with local authorities and communities to address pressing needs and reaffirm the UN's commitment to supporting the Afghan people. In Cyprus, Personal Envoy María Ángela Holguín held discussions with both Turkish and Greek Cypriot leaders to facilitate ongoing dialogue. Meanwhile, in Sudan, Personal Envoy Pekka Haavisto concluded a visit to Bahrain, participating in the Manama Dialogue and planning further engagements in Sudan to address violence and civilian protection. Lastly, the UN Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) officially ended its operations, marking a significant transition in the country as it looks toward future stability. These developments reflect the UN's ongoing efforts to promote peace and stability in these regions.
