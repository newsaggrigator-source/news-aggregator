Federal and Regional Leaders Congratulate Guudlaawe on Hirshabelle Re-election - FTL Somalia

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 19:54:47 GMT

Ali Abdullahi Hussein Guudlaawe has been re-elected as President of Hirshabelle State, receiving 81 votes in a parliamentary election held in Jowhar. His opponent, Nur Ilmi Halane, garnered only 13 votes, with four ballots invalidated. Guudlaawe's victory prompted congratulations from various federal and regional leaders, including President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre, who emphasized the need for peace, reconciliation, and effective governance. Abdifatah Hassan Afrah was elected as Vice President, both representing the Justice and Solidarity Party (JSP), which holds a significant majority in the assembly. However, the election has sparked controversy, particularly among the Hawadle community, who feel excluded from power-sharing agreements. Despite the JSP's dominance, unresolved tensions may challenge Guudlaawe's administration, particularly regarding the demands of the Hiiraan region for a rotational presidency. The new leadership faces ongoing security issues and political disputes that could impact stability in Hirshabelle.
