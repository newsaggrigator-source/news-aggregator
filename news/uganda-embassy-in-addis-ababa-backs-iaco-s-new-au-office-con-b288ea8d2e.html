Uganda Embassy in Addis Ababa Backs IACO's New AU Office, Continental Coffee Strategy

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 05:22:59 GMT

The Uganda Embassy in Addis Ababa has expressed strong support for the Inter-African Coffee Organisation (IACO) and its newly established office at the African Union (AU) headquarters. This support was highlighted during a meeting between Ambassador Solomon Rutega, IACO's Secretary-General, and Margaret Kafeero, Chargé d'Affaires a.i. The discussions centered on IACO's efforts to transform Africa's coffee sector, particularly following the AU's recognition of coffee as a strategic commodity. Ambassador Rutega emphasized the need for industrial value addition, advocating for the processing of coffee locally to enhance economic returns, which could significantly benefit over 10 million households across Africa. Kafeero reaffirmed Uganda's commitment to this initiative, noting the potential for regional trade growth under the African Continental Free Trade Area. Both parties agreed to collaborate on advancing AU Agenda 2063, aiming to ensure fair economic returns for local farmers and strengthen Africa's position in the global coffee market. (158 words)
