Ugandan Traders Seek New Market in Mogadishu - Dawan Africa

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 19:46:18 GMT

More than 100 Ugandan traders and 146 Somali participants convened in Mogadishu from October 20 to 22, 2026, for a cooperation conference aimed at enhancing trade and investment ties between Uganda and Somalia. This event included a review of existing agreements, discussions on bilateral relations, and a business exhibition where traders showcased their products and explored potential partnerships. Junior Nuwandinda from Warfaa Group Holdings Ltd expressed optimism about the Somali market, particularly for spices and flavors. Somali businesswoman Faadumo Sheikh Ali noted the benefits of discovering new products and fostering connections with Ugandan counterparts. The conference highlighted the longstanding security cooperation between the two nations, with Uganda supporting Somalia in combating terrorism. The initiative aims to create economic opportunities, allowing Somalia to access new markets while enabling Uganda to expand its consumer base. Additionally, the conference announced free visas for diplomatic passport holders, facilitating easier movement for official relations, although the practical impact will depend on implementation.
