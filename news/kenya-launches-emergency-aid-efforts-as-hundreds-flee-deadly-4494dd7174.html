Kenya launches emergency aid efforts as hundreds flee deadly clashes in Ethiopia - Citizen Digital

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 06:24:55 GMT

Kenya has initiated emergency aid operations in Moyale, Marsabit County, to assist families fleeing violent clashes in Ethiopia. The Kenya Red Cross Society aims to support 1,000 households that have arrived with minimal belongings, urgently requiring shelter, food, safe water, and healthcare. The humanitarian response includes establishing temporary shelters and distributing essential supplies such as tents, blankets, and kitchen sets. Additionally, the organization is providing fortified porridge for vulnerable groups and medical assistance. The violence in Ethiopia erupted over a regional flag dispute between the Oromia and Somali regions, leading to armed confrontations and significant unrest. Reports indicate heavy gunfire and destruction of homes and businesses, forcing many residents to flee. The exact casualty figures remain unclear. The Kenya Red Cross is conducting assessments to identify urgent needs and is collaborating with local authorities to ensure the safety and dignity of the displaced families. This border area is crucial for communities in both countries, highlighting the ongoing humanitarian crisis.
