Kenya Red Cross Launches Emergency Response for Families Fleeing Moyale Clashes Into Marsabit

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 10:35:33 GMT

The Kenya Red Cross Society (KRCS) has initiated an emergency response for families fleeing violence in Moyale, Marsabit County, due to renewed clashes across the border in Ethiopia. The conflict, which erupted on October 22, 2026, has forced civilians to seek refuge in Kenya. KRCS aims to assist 1,000 households by providing temporary shelters, tents, and essential supplies such as blankets, sleeping mats, and kitchen sets. Additionally, they are distributing Super Cereal, a fortified porridge, to support the nutritional needs of vulnerable groups, including young children and pregnant women. A rapid needs assessment is currently underway, revealing urgent requirements for food, clean water, sanitation, and healthcare, particularly for women, children, and the elderly. The clashes stem from territorial disputes between Ethiopia's Oromia and Somali regions, exacerbated by the raising of a Somali regional flag in a contested area. The situation has led to significant civilian displacement, echoing past crises in the region.
