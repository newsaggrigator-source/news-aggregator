Ethiopia's Green Legacy Showcases Commitment to Global Climate Action - Pmo Press Secretary

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 17:05:53 GMT

Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative exemplifies the country's dedication to climate action through extensive afforestation and sustainable development, as highlighted by Press Secretary Billene Seyoum during a briefing on Ethiopia's involvement in COP31 in Türkiye. Over the past eight years, Ethiopia has planted over 56 billion seedlings, aiming to combat climate change and restore ecosystems. Billene emphasized that the initiative's success relies not only on the number of seedlings planted but also on their survival and care, urging public participation in environmental conservation. Additionally, she discussed Ethiopia's investments in renewable energy, including hydropower, wind, and solar projects, to meet domestic electricity needs and enhance regional power supply. The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD) is a key project in this transition. Billene reiterated that Ethiopia's environmental efforts contribute to global climate initiatives, while also preparing to showcase its achievements at COP32 in 2027.
