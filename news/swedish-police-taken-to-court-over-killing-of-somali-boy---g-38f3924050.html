Swedish Police Taken to Court Over Killing of Somali Boy - Garowe Online

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 22:03:55 GMT

The Swedish Equality Ombudsman (DO) has filed a lawsuit against the Swedish Police regarding the killing of Mohamed, a 14-year-old Somali boy, in July 2023. The case, set for hearings on May 13, 14, and 27, 2027, will determine if Mohamed's family faced discrimination due to their ethnic background and if they are entitled to 300,000 Swedish kronor in compensation. Mohamed went missing from a care facility, and despite his family's warnings about his safety, police failed to act, leading to his abduction and murder by members of an organized crime network. The DO claims the police did not provide an interpreter for Mohamed's mother and did not follow up as promised. Although the police admitted to violating administrative law and the Convention on the Rights of the Child in 2025, they denied any discrimination. This case marks the first discrimination lawsuit the DO has brought against the Swedish Police, raising concerns about their treatment of immigrant families.
