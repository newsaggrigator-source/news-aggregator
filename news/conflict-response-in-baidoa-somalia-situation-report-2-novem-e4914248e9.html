Conflict Response in Baidoa, Somalia: Situation Report #2 (November 25, 2026) - ReliefWeb

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 19:35:13 GMT

The conflict in Baidoa, Somalia, has led to extensive damage to civilian infrastructure and a surge in humanitarian needs, displacing many residents. The deteriorating security situation has disrupted access to essential services, particularly healthcare, and increased the number of internally displaced persons (IDPs) seeking assistance. Control over various territories has shifted, escalating tensions in the region. In response, the International Medical Corps (IMC) has been active in Baidoa since 1990, providing vital health services, including primary and secondary healthcare, mental health support, and nutrition assistance. Recently, IMC deployed two primary health units to deliver integrated health and nutrition services to IDPs and vulnerable communities. Additionally, a standby emergency response team has been established to assist Bay Regional Hospital during mass-casualty incidents. Ongoing projects are also providing lifesaving nutrition support to drought-affected communities, helping those displaced by the conflict access essential services.
