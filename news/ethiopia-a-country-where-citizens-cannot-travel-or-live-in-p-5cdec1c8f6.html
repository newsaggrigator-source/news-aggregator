Ethiopia: A Country Where Citizens Cannot Travel or Live in Peace - Borkena

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 18:23:23 GMT

Ethiopia is currently facing a severe security crisis, with citizens unable to travel or live peacefully due to rampant violence and insecurity. The ethnically led government has failed to ensure the safety of its people, leading to fears of kidnapping, attacks, and violence during everyday activities. Conflicts in regions like Tigray, Amhara, and Oromo have exacerbated the situation, causing deaths and displacements. The recent resurgence of violence in Tigray threatens the fragile peace established by a 2022 agreement. Citizens are increasingly reluctant to travel by road, opting for expensive air travel instead, as the roads are fraught with danger and numerous checkpoints. Additionally, ongoing attacks on religious and ethnic communities have heightened fears and insecurity. The government is urged to prioritize public safety and infrastructure development, as the current state of affairs undermines citizens' trust and hampers economic growth. Without tangible improvements, civil disobedience and protests may escalate across the nation.
