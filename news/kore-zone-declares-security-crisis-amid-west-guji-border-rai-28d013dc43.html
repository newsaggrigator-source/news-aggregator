Kore Zone Declares Security Crisis Amid West Guji Border Raids - Borkena

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 22:17:08 GMT

Local officials in the Kore Zone of South Ethiopia have declared a security crisis due to ongoing cross-border raids from armed groups in the West Guji Zone of Oromia. These incursions, which occurred between October 22 and 24, 2026, have overwhelmed regional security forces, leaving communities vulnerable and infrastructure paralyzed. Reports indicate that the attacks resulted in four deaths and multiple injuries, including children. Local authorities have highlighted the identity-based nature of these assaults and the lack of federal security presence, which exacerbates the situation. The ongoing violence has disrupted vital commercial routes, with the main highway to Dilla closed for 11 years due to security threats. Residents have expressed frustration over delayed responses from regional police, who often arrive after attackers have retreated. The situation calls for urgent federal intervention to restore stability and protect the affected communities.
