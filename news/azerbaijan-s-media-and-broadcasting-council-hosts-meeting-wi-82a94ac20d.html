Azerbaijan’s Media and Broadcasting Council hosts meeting with Somali Minister of Information, Culture and Tourism - AZƏRTAC

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 11:36:00 GMT

On October 28, 2026, Azerbaijan’s Media and Broadcasting Council hosted a meeting with Abdulfattah Kasim Mohamud, the Somali Minister of Information, Culture and Tourism. Ahmad Ismayilov, the Council's Chairman, emphasized the potential for enhancing cooperation between Azerbaijan and Somalia in the fields of media and communications. He highlighted the importance of information exchange and the media's role in fostering mutual understanding and strengthening bilateral relations. Minister Mohamud expressed Somalia's interest in expanding collaborative efforts, particularly in joint media initiatives and addressing contemporary challenges in the information landscape. The discussions also covered digital media trends, combating disinformation, information security, and the influence of artificial intelligence and new technologies on the media sector. This meeting reflects a commitment to building stronger ties and sharing professional experiences between the two nations in the evolving media environment.
