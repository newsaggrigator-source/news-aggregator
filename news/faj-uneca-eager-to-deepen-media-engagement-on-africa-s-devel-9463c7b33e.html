FAJ, Uneca Eager to Deepen Media Engagement On Africa's Development Priorities

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 09:48:37 GMT

The Federation of African Journalists (FAJ) and the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa (UNECA) are keen to enhance media engagement regarding Africa's development priorities. During discussions, UNECA's Claver Gatete emphasized the importance of journalists in shaping the African narrative and facilitating access to reliable information, which is crucial for advancing Sustainable Development Goals. FAJ President Omar Faruk Osman highlighted the essential role of independent journalism in fostering informed public debate and understanding of policies affecting citizens' lives. The FAJ delegation raised concerns about journalists' working conditions and the significance of public interest journalism. They welcomed UNECA's acknowledgment of journalists' contributions to development discussions and expressed commitment to ensuring that journalists are involved in shaping Africa's future. The meeting aimed to strengthen collaboration between FAJ and UNECA, enabling journalists to better articulate developmental priorities and bring African perspectives into public discourse.
