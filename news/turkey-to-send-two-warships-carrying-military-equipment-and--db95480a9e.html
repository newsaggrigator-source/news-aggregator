Turkey to send two warships carrying military equipment and supplies to Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 04:21:43 GMT

Turkey's Ministry of National Defence announced the deployment of two naval warships, TCG Gökçeada and TCG Yıldırım, to Somalia on October 30, 2026. These vessels will carry military equipment and supplies as part of ongoing efforts to enhance defense cooperation between Turkey and Somalia. While the specifics regarding the type and quantity of military equipment were not disclosed, the supplies may be intended for either the Somali National Armed Forces or Turkish personnel stationed in Somalia. This announcement follows a recent meeting between the commanders of the Turkish Land Forces and their Somali counterparts, focusing on strengthening bilateral military cooperation, training, and security. Turkey has been a significant military partner for Somalia, providing training and technical assistance, and operates a major military training facility in Mogadishu. The deployment of these warships underscores the deepening military relationship between the two nations, aimed at improving Somalia's defense capabilities and regional security. (158 words)
