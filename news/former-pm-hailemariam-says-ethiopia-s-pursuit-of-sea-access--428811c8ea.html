Former PM Hailemariam Says Ethiopia's Pursuit of Sea Access Rational

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 12:05:51 GMT

Former Ethiopian Prime Minister Hailemariam Desalegn has articulated that Ethiopia's quest for sustainable sea access is a rational national aspiration that should be pursued through dialogue and negotiation. In an interview with the Ethiopian News Agency, he emphasized that the focus should be on how to achieve this access peacefully, rather than questioning its necessity. With a population of around 130 million, Ethiopia is the most populous landlocked country, and Hailemariam described the lack of maritime access as a historical scar affecting its economic and strategic interests. He urged neighboring countries to consider Ethiopia's aspirations as part of a broader framework for regional growth and stability. Hailemariam also highlighted the historical context of Ethiopia's landlocked status, tracing it back to colonial-era decisions. He called for constructive discussions to find mutually beneficial solutions, warning that opposition to Ethiopia's maritime ambitions could undermine regional power dynamics. His remarks underscore the importance of sea access for trade and development in the region.
