Ethiopia Strengthening Peace in Tigray Region Through Former Combatants' Reintegration

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 05:45:19 GMT

Ethiopia is enhancing peace in the Tigray Region by focusing on the rehabilitation and reintegration of former combatants through its Disarmament, Demobilization and Reintegration (DDR) program. This initiative aims to transition former fighters into productive civilian life, emphasizing skills development and economic self-reliance as crucial elements of post-conflict recovery. Recently, Minister of Labor and Skills Awolu Abdi and Prime Minister's Advisor Getachew Reda visited a rehabilitation center at Mekelle University to assess the progress of training programs for former combatants. They highlighted the importance of equipping these individuals, particularly youth, with practical skills to foster sustainable livelihoods. The National Rehabilitation Commission reiterated its commitment to promoting lasting peace by facilitating the transition from armed conflict to meaningful community engagement. This approach recognizes that sustainable peace involves not only ending violence but also restoring livelihoods and social ties, ultimately creating opportunities for those affected by conflict to reintegrate into society.
