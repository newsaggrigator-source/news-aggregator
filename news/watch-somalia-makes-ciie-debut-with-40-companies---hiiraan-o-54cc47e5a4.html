WATCH: Somalia makes CIIE debut with 40+ companies - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 19:48:27 GMT

Somalia made its debut at the China International Import Expo (CIIE) with over 40 companies showcasing their products and services. The event, held in Shanghai, aims to promote international trade and cooperation, providing a platform for countries to present their goods to a global audience. Somali businesses participated in various sectors, including agriculture, textiles, and handicrafts, highlighting the nation’s potential for investment and trade. The Somali delegation emphasized the importance of fostering economic ties with China, seeking to attract Chinese investors and enhance bilateral trade relations. The participation at CIIE is seen as a significant step for Somalia in re-establishing its presence in the global market and promoting its diverse offerings. The event also serves as an opportunity for Somali entrepreneurs to network and explore partnerships with international businesses, ultimately aiming to boost the country's economic growth and development.
