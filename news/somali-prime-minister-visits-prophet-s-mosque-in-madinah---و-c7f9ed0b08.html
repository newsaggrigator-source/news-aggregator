Somali Prime Minister Visits Prophet's Mosque in Madinah - وكالة الأنباء السعودية

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 18:34:26 GMT

Somali Prime Minister Hamse Abdi Barre visited the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, Saudi Arabia, as part of his official trip to the Kingdom. During his visit, he performed prayers and engaged in spiritual reflection at the mosque, which is one of the holiest sites in Islam. The Prime Minister expressed his gratitude for the opportunity to visit such a significant religious landmark. His trip underscores the strong ties between Somalia and Saudi Arabia, particularly in the context of religious and cultural connections. The visit also highlights the importance of fostering diplomatic relations and cooperation between the two nations. Barre's presence at the mosque signifies a commitment to strengthening these bonds, as well as promoting peace and unity within the Muslim community. The visit was well-received, and it is expected to enhance collaboration in various sectors, including religious, cultural, and economic areas.
