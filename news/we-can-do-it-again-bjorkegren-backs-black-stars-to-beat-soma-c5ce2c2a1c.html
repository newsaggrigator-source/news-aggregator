"We can do it again": Bjorkegren backs Black Stars to beat Somalia on Monday - Graphic Online

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 16:18:05 GMT

Ghana's Black Stars head coach, Kim Lars Bjorkegren, expressed confidence in his team's ability to secure another victory against Somalia in their upcoming match on Monday. Following a 2-0 win in the first leg of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier, where Jonas Adjetey and Felix Afena-Gyan scored, Bjorkegren praised the team's second-half performance but noted some struggles in the first half due to the heat and tempo. He emphasized the importance of the three points gained and reflected on the team's improved understanding of Somalia after their initial encounter. Bjorkegren remains focused on supporting the team and believes they can qualify for the AFCON. He acknowledged the unpredictability of football but is optimistic that the energy and performance from the last game can be replicated. Overall, he is pleased with the team's progress and is determined to continue striving for success in the qualifiers.
