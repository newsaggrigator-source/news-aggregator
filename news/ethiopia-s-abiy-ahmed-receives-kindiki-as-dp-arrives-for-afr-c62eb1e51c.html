Ethiopia’s Abiy Ahmed receives Kindiki as DP arrives for Africa security forum - The Kenyan Diaspora Media

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 10:45:25 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed welcomed Kenyan Deputy President Kindiki as he arrived for an Africa security forum. This meeting highlights the ongoing collaboration between Ethiopia and Kenya in addressing regional security challenges. The forum aims to tackle pressing issues such as terrorism, climate change, and political instability that affect the continent. Both leaders emphasized the importance of unity and cooperation among African nations to enhance security and foster development. The discussions are expected to focus on strategies for combating extremist groups and improving intelligence sharing among countries. The event underscores the critical role of leadership in promoting peace and stability in Africa, particularly in the Horn of Africa region, which has faced numerous conflicts. This gathering also serves as a platform for African leaders to engage in dialogue and share best practices for security management. The outcomes of the forum could significantly influence future policies and collaborative efforts in the region.
