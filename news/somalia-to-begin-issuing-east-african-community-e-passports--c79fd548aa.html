Somalia to Begin Issuing East African Community E-Passports in January 2027 - Horseed Media

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 15:07:37 GMT

Somalia is set to begin issuing East African Community (EAC) electronic passports in January 2027, marking a significant advancement in the country's integration into the regional bloc. The Federal Republic of Somalia announced that the introduction of this regional travel document is a crucial step in aligning its immigration systems with EAC frameworks and enhancing regional cooperation. The new EAC e-Passport will be connected to Somalia's national identification system, and issuance services will also be available through Somali diplomatic missions abroad, facilitating access for citizens living overseas. This initiative aims to ease movement across EAC member states, promote cross-border trade, and create more employment opportunities. Additionally, it is designed to improve border management and security by enhancing travel document standards. With this rollout, Somalia will join other EAC nations—Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Burundi—that are already issuing the EAC electronic passport, further supporting regional integration efforts.
