AFCON 2027 Q: Black Stars land in Takoradi for crucial Somalia double-header - GBC Ghana Online

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 08:02:55 GMT

Ghana's Black Stars have arrived in Takoradi for their crucial 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Somalia, landing late Tuesday after a final training session in Accra. The team was greeted with a vibrant welcome featuring a brass band and traditional masquerades as they prepare for the important Group C matches. Interim coach Kim Lars Björkegren, who recently took over from Carlos Queiroz, is scheduled to address the media before the first match on Thursday. Ghana is in dire need of points after suffering defeats to Côte d’Ivoire (2-0) and The Gambia (4-2), placing them at the bottom of Group C with no points. The 26-man squad includes notable players like Mohammed Kudus and Jordan Ayew, aiming to turn their campaign around. Only the top two teams from Group C will qualify for the 2027 AFCON, which will be hosted in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. The first match is set for November 12, 2026, at Essipong Sports Stadium.
