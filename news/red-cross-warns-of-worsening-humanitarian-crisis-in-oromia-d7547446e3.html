Red Cross Warns of Worsening Humanitarian Crisis in Oromia

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 13:20:07 GMT

The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has issued a warning regarding the worsening humanitarian crisis in Ethiopia's Oromia region, exacerbated by ongoing armed conflict, civilian attacks, and increasing displacement. The ICRC reported that communities across various zones in Oromia are facing severe hardships as insecurity disrupts their lives. Since 2019, Oromia has been embroiled in conflict involving federal forces and the Oromo Liberation Army, alongside rising intercommunal violence. Recent attacks have resulted in significant civilian casualties and displacement, particularly along the Oromia-Somali border. The Red Cross has intensified its humanitarian efforts, providing medical assistance, rehabilitating health centers, and constructing water points to aid affected populations. Despite these efforts, the organization faces challenges in reaching all those in need due to the escalating crisis. The ICRC continues to engage with local authorities and armed groups to promote adherence to international humanitarian law while monitoring the situation closely.
