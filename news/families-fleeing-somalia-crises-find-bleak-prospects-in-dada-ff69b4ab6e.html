Families fleeing Somalia crises find bleak prospects in Dadaab refugee camps - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 12:22:24 GMT

Families fleeing crises in Somalia are facing dire conditions in the Dadaab refugee camps in Kenya. The camps, originally established to accommodate a temporary influx of refugees, are now home to over 200,000 people, leading to overcrowding and limited resources. Many refugees are struggling with inadequate access to food, clean water, and healthcare. The ongoing conflict, drought, and famine in Somalia have driven families to seek safety, but the camps offer little hope for a better future. Aid organizations are overwhelmed and unable to meet the growing needs of the population, leaving many refugees in a state of uncertainty and despair. The situation highlights the urgent need for international support and sustainable solutions to address the challenges faced by displaced families.
