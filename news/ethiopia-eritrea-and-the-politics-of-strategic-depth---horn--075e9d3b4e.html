Ethiopia, Eritrea and the Politics of Strategic Depth - horn review

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 13:55:45 GMT

Ethiopia faces a persistent national security challenge from Eritrea, which has fostered alliances with various Ethiopian rebel groups to destabilize the government in Addis Ababa. The Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) has been a key player in this strategy, serving as a proxy for Eritrea until a recent Ethiopian counteroffensive forced it into retreat. Eritrea's influence extends beyond Tigray, involving the Afar Revolutionary Democratic Unity Front (ARDUF) and other regional rebels, forming a coalition known as the "Alliance for Survival." Despite recent military successes, the threat from Eritrea and its proxies remains, as they continue to seek to undermine Ethiopia's stability. Ethiopia has several strategic options to counter this threat, including establishing a buffer zone within Eritrea and enhancing its diplomatic efforts to expose Eritrea's aggressive posture. The ongoing dynamics highlight the complexity of Ethiopia's security landscape and the enduring challenge posed by Eritrea's strategic ambitions. (159 words)
