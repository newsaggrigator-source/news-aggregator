All of Africa Today - November 24, 2026

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 06:44:30 GMT

Amnesty International has called for an investigation into potential war crimes following artillery attacks by Ethiopian government forces and their Tigray allies that resulted in the deaths of at least 14 civilians in Alamata, Tigray. The organization highlighted that the shelling targeted populated areas, while also noting that Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) fighters had established military bases in civilian zones. Meanwhile, in Sudan, nearly 19.5 million people are facing severe hunger due to ongoing conflict, with calls for a ceasefire and increased humanitarian aid. In the Democratic Republic of the Congo, fighting has trapped Ebola responders in a hospital, disrupting containment efforts. In Nigeria's Borno State, school enrollment has nearly doubled after years of Boko Haram violence, although challenges remain. Additionally, Zimbabwean preacher Uebert Angel has been arrested on sexual assault allegations, and the Court of Arbitration for Sport is set to rule on Senegal's appeal regarding the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations title dispute.
