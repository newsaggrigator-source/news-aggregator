Somalia, Djibouti Sign Agreement to Strengthen Parliamentary Cooperation - Dawan Africa

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 20:40:07 GMT

On November 2, 2026, Somalia and Djibouti signed a memorandum of understanding to enhance parliamentary cooperation between their legislative bodies. The agreement was formalized in Djibouti City by Somalia's House of the People Speaker, Abdoulkadir Mohamed Nour ‘Jaamac’, and Djibouti's National Assembly President, Dileita Mohamed Dileita. This memorandum aims to create a framework for sharing expertise, exchanging institutional experiences, and developing partnerships. Key areas of focus include improving legislative processes, enhancing the skills of parliamentary staff, organizing training programs, conducting legal research, and modernizing parliamentary procedures. Additionally, the agreement promotes closer coordination between standing committees, the adoption of modern technology, improved data security, and the establishment of parliamentary friendship groups. Both leaders expressed optimism about the agreement's potential to strengthen bilateral relations and enhance cooperation on regional and international issues. This initiative reflects ongoing efforts by Somalia and Djibouti to deepen their governmental collaboration.
