Survivors Flee Hayiq Jari IDP Camp After Deadly Drone Strike

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 06:34:12 GMT

Survivors are fleeing the Hayiq Jari 2 IDP camp in Ethiopia's Amhara region following a deadly drone strike on October 29 that killed at least 18 civilians and injured 22 others. The attack, which struck the camp multiple times, has left residents in fear and uncertainty, prompting many to leave in an unorganized manner to seek shelter in nearby communities. Essential support services, including markets and classrooms, have been shut down, exacerbating the plight of the displaced. Witnesses reported that victims included children and pregnant women, with some buried together due to space constraints. The camp, home to over 1,800 displaced individuals, has faced ongoing challenges, including threats of eviction and unsafe conditions for return. Survivors express confusion about their future, with some considering traveling to the regional capital, Bahir Dar, to seek assistance. The drone strike has intensified the already precarious situation for those displaced by conflict in the region.
