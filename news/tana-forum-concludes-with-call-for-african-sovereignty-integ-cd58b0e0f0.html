Tana Forum Concludes With Call for African Sovereignty, Integration

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 22:41:07 GMT

The 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa concluded in Bahir Dar, emphasizing the need for African sovereignty and regional integration. Current and former leaders gathered to address pressing security issues, advocating for a shift from dialogue to concrete action. Ghana's President John Dramani Mahama, in his closing remarks, highlighted the importance of the State of Peace and Security in Africa Report as a vital tool for understanding and responding to conflicts. He stressed that sustainable peace requires more than military solutions; it necessitates economic integration, regional solidarity, and inclusive societies that empower youth and marginalized groups. Mahama called for improved governance focused on public welfare and urged stronger collaboration among African nations and with the Global South. He challenged participants to demonstrate measurable progress by the next forum, aiming for a future where conflicts diminish and opportunities expand for all Africans. The event underscored Tana Forum's role as a key platform for dialogue on peace and security in Africa. (159 words)
