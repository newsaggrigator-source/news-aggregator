From Somalia’s polluted streets, an entrepreneur seeks bravery in the corridors of Cop31 - The Irish Times

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 19:48:23 GMT

Fadumo Ahmed, a 32-year-old entrepreneur from Somalia, is making strides in environmental sustainability by aiming to convert 5,000 petrol tuk tuks in Mogadishu to electric alternatives through her social enterprise, eBajaaj. Currently, the city has 75,000 petrol tuk tuks, contributing to severe air pollution and high carbon emissions. Ahmed and her partner, Hassan Mumin, have already converted 20 tuk tuks and are seeking investment at Cop31 to expand their project. The initiative not only addresses environmental concerns but also offers economic benefits to drivers, who currently spend significant amounts on fuel and maintenance. By switching to electric, drivers can save money and improve their livelihoods. The project also facilitates easier driving for women and those with disabilities, while speeding up security checks at checkpoints. Ahmed emphasizes the need for investment to realize their vision of a cleaner, more sustainable future for Somalia.
