The President of Puntland attacks the Somali President and calls for his resignation to pave the way for the formation of a transitional council - صوت الإمارات

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 10:18:10 GMT

Said Abdullahi Deni, the President of Puntland, has publicly criticized Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, calling for his resignation to facilitate the establishment of a transitional council. Deni dismissed Mohamud's proposed national dialogue, scheduled for November 14-18 in Mogadishu, as a futile effort that would not lead to meaningful outcomes. He expressed skepticism about the federal government's ability to conduct free and fair elections, arguing that the current leadership has failed to provide viable solutions for the electoral process. Deni's remarks highlight the deepening political divide between Puntland and the federal government, as he advocates for a transitional council to oversee the country's governance and ensure a transparent electoral process. He emphasized the need for broad-based consultations for national salvation, reflecting the escalating tensions and disagreements over governance and electoral frameworks in Somalia.
