Al Shabab inflicts heavy losses on Somali army in southern battle - Middle East Online

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 12:01:43 GMT

In a recent battle in southern Somalia, al Shabab inflicted significant casualties on the Somali army, with reports indicating dozens of soldiers were killed. A soldier named Ahmed reported assisting in the transport of 40 dead and over 100 wounded soldiers to hospitals in Mogadishu. The Somali defense ministry confirmed that government forces killed approximately 75 militants and reported 56 soldiers wounded in the fighting in the Jameeco area of the Buurhakaba district. The ministry noted that al Shabab launched the attack using vehicles laden with explosives, damaging several army vehicles. Another soldier, Farah, mentioned that the explosions from al Shabab's car bombs destroyed five military vehicles and contributed to the high number of casualties. Somalia has been embroiled in conflict and insurgency for nearly two decades, primarily due to al Shabab's efforts to impose its strict interpretation of Sharia law amid a backdrop of ongoing clan battles and a lack of a strong central government since 1991.
