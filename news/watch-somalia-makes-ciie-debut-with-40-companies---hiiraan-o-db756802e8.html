WATCH: Somalia makes CIIE debut with 40+ companies - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 13:42:02 GMT

Somalia is making its debut at the China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai, showcasing over 40 companies. This marks a significant step for the nation as it seeks to establish trade connections with China. In an interview, Gamal Mohamed Hassan, Somalia's minister of commerce and industry, emphasized the aim of linking Somali businesses with Chinese partners and leveraging China's zero-tariff policy. He highlighted key products such as seafood, frankincense, and minerals that Somalia is eager to trade. Additionally, Somali businesses plan to visit other provinces in China and participate in a business forum to strengthen partnerships. This participation reflects Somalia's readiness to engage in international trade and enhance its economic prospects. The event is seen as a vital opportunity for Somalia to promote its products and foster economic growth through collaboration with Chinese enterprises.
