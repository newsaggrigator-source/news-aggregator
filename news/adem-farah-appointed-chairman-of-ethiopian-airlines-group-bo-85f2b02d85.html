Adem Farah Appointed Chairman of Ethiopian Airlines Group Board

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 17:49:36 GMT

Adem Farah has been appointed as the Chairman of the Board of Ethiopian Airlines Group, as announced by the Ethiopian News Agency on November 17, 2026. Farah, who serves as the Vice President and Head of the Main Office of the ruling Prosperity Party, is welcomed by Ethiopian Airlines Group, which expressed its honor in having him lead the board. The airline conveyed its best wishes for a successful and productive tenure as Farah takes on this significant role. This appointment reflects the airline's commitment to strong leadership as it continues to navigate the complexities of the aviation industry in Ethiopia and beyond. The Ethiopian Airlines Group is a key player in the region's transport sector, and Farah's leadership is expected to guide its strategic direction and operational effectiveness.
