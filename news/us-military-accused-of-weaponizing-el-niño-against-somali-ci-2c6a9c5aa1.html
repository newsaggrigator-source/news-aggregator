US Military Accused of “Weaponizing” El Niño Against Somali Civilians - Mother Jones

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 11:33:57 GMT

The U.S. military is facing accusations of "weaponizing" El Niño against Somali civilians amid ongoing airstrikes targeting al-Shabaab militants. Reports from residents in Jilib, a city under attack, reveal that relentless airstrikes are forcing families into flood-prone areas near the River Juba, exacerbating the humanitarian crisis. Civilians describe constant drone activity, resulting in significant casualties and destruction. The U.S. Africa Command (Africom) has not disclosed operational details but denies targeting civilians or using weather as a warfare tactic. However, analysts suggest that the military strategy may be exploiting the impending El Niño-induced flooding to weaken al-Shabaab. The situation is dire, with many civilians lacking basic necessities and facing severe malnutrition. As the rainy season approaches, concerns grow over potential large-scale deaths due to flooding and ongoing violence, raising ethical questions about the military's tactics in a region already vulnerable to extreme weather and conflict.
