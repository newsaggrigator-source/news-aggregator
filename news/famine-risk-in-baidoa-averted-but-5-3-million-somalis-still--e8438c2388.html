Famine Risk in Baidoa Averted, but 5.3 Million Somalis Still Face Acute Food Insecurity - FTL Somalia

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 12:46:06 GMT

The risk of famine in Baidoa, Somalia, has been averted, according to a recent IPC projection, but 5.3 million people across the country still face acute food insecurity. The updated analysis indicates that while the situation for internally displaced persons (IDPs) in Baidoa remains critical, with 65% experiencing Crisis or worse conditions, the worst-case scenario of extreme flooding has not occurred. Factors contributing to this improvement include lower-than-expected flood displacements and better access to agricultural labor. However, acute malnutrition remains a significant concern, with nearly 1.92 million children projected to need treatment. Despite these improvements, humanitarian assistance is alarmingly low, reaching only 0.4% of those in need, and funding cuts have led to the closure of numerous health facilities. The report emphasizes that while famine has been averted, the underlying issues of conflict, high food prices, and inadequate humanitarian response continue to pose serious threats to the population's well-being.
