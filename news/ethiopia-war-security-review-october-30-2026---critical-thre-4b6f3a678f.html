Ethiopia War Security Review, October 30, 2026 - Critical Threats

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 23:14:43 GMT

The Ethiopia War Security Review for October 30, 2026, highlights ongoing military activities and clashes involving the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) and the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF). The ENDF has conducted drone strikes in TPLF-held Rama, resulting in civilian casualties, while fighting continues around the Rama-Adwa highway. Clashes are reported northwest of Shire as the ENDF seeks to consolidate control in the region. In the mountainous Tigray interior, federal forces are engaged in combat with the Tigray Defense Forces (TDF). Additionally, the Amhara Fano National Movement (AFNM) has faced drone strikes, allegedly hitting civilian areas. Political developments include the departure of AFNM figure Eskinder Nega, citing TPLF influence. Internationally, Ethiopia and Russia are strengthening ties, with Russia delivering fighter jets and discussing nuclear cooperation. Ethiopia also supports Emirati diplomat Lana Nusseibeh's candidacy for UN Secretary-General and hosted a naval security forum with regional partners.
