Ethiopia's Deputy PM Urges African Nations to Move Beyond Raw Material Exports, Boost Value Addition

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 05:41:38 GMT

Ethiopia's Deputy Prime Minister, Temesgen Tiruneh, has called on African nations to shift from raw material exports to enhancing value addition and industrialization. Speaking at a trade forum in Addis Ababa, he emphasized the need for Africa to retain more economic value from its natural resources and to foster intra-African trade. Temesgen highlighted the continent's potential, stating that with over 1.5 billion people and abundant resources, Africa must develop strong manufacturing industries and improve product quality to compete globally. He warned that poor product quality leads to financial losses and damages Africa's reputation in international markets. The Deputy PM advocated for an integrated continental quality infrastructure to streamline product testing and certification across African countries. He also urged investment in quality infrastructure and support for small and medium-sized enterprises, particularly those led by women and youth, to enhance trade efficiency and reliability. Temesgen concluded by expressing Ethiopia's commitment to strengthening intra-African trade and promoting high-quality African products. (159 words)
