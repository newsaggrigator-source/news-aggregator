How Is Washington Positioning Itself Amid Ethiopia’s Security Challenges? - horn review

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 07:27:29 GMT

Washington is recalibrating its approach to Ethiopia amid escalating security challenges, particularly concerning tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea. Senator Marco Rubio highlighted the risk of a broader conflict, urging Ethiopia to respond wisely to separatist attacks while advocating for dialogue based on the 2018 peace framework. The U.S. has shifted its messaging, labeling the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) as a terrorist group, which aligns with Ethiopia's previous designation of the TPLF. This change in characterization reflects a strategic pivot from conflict management to a clearer stance supporting Ethiopia's territorial integrity. Concurrently, the U.S. is reopening diplomatic channels with Eritrea, lifting sanctions to facilitate engagement. However, concerns persist regarding Eritrea's support for armed groups within Ethiopia and its aggressive regional posture. Washington's objectives include preventing wider conflict, maintaining stability in the Horn of Africa, and ensuring secure Red Sea shipping routes, though the effectiveness of this diplomatic outreach remains uncertain given Eritrea's historical behavior.
