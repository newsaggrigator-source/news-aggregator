PM Advisor Getachew Underscores Ethiopia’s Pivotal Geopolitical Position - ENA English

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 17:35:54 GMT

During the Tana Forum 2026 Special Envoys Dialogue in Bahir Dar, PM Advisor Getachew Reda emphasized Ethiopia's crucial geopolitical role, asserting that developments within the country significantly impact the broader Horn of Africa and Red Sea region. He highlighted Ethiopia's population of over 130 million and its rapid economic growth, positioning it as an economic powerhouse and a key contributor to international peace and security. Getachew stressed the importance of addressing internal conflicts through peaceful dialogue, referencing the Pretoria Agreement aimed at resolving the northern conflict. However, he warned that external factors, particularly interference from regional actors like Eritrea, pose challenges to these peace efforts. UN Special Envoy Guang Cong described the interconnected security challenges in the region as a "polycrisis," necessitating a coordinated regional response. The dialogue underscored the urgency for multilateral cooperation to stabilize the region, with panelists advocating for regional-led peace initiatives and infrastructure development to enhance connectivity and integration.
