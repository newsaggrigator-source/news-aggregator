Somalia opens first national pavilion at China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 15:58:18 GMT

Somalia has inaugurated its first national pavilion at the China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai, marking a significant step in its efforts to enhance trade relations with China. The pavilion, opened by Minister of Commerce and Industry Jama Mohamed Hassan and Ambassador Dr. Hodan Osman Abdi, showcases Somali agricultural products and investment opportunities. Featured products include sesame seeds, frankincense, and myrrh, alongside promotions for investments in livestock, fisheries, and minerals. The Somali government aims to connect local producers with Chinese importers, understand market demands, and secure new partnerships. The expo, running from November 5 to 10, has attracted over 4,000 exhibitors from 162 countries. The Somali Embassy in China emphasized the importance of this participation for expanding exports and fostering long-term commercial relationships. The initiative is part of Somalia's broader strategy to diversify export markets and boost domestic production capabilities.
