Lethal Artillery Strikes On Civilian Neighbourhoods in Tigray's Alamata City Must Be Investigated As War Crimes

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 05:12:17 GMT

Amnesty International has called for an investigation into artillery strikes by Ethiopia's military and its allied Tigray Peace Forces (TPF) on civilian neighborhoods in Alamata, Tigray, from September 26 to 28, which resulted in numerous civilian casualties. The organization reported that at least 14 civilians were killed, with many more injured, as the Ethiopian National Defense Forces (ENDF) and TPF shelled the city, disregarding the safety of its 80,000 residents. Amnesty's findings indicate that the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) also endangered civilians by establishing military bases in populated areas, including schools. Despite the TPLF's denial of wrongdoing, Amnesty emphasized that such actions do not excuse the ENDF's indiscriminate use of artillery in civilian areas, which violates international humanitarian law. Eyewitness accounts and investigations revealed that many attacks targeted residential homes and public spaces, leading to significant civilian suffering and loss of life.
