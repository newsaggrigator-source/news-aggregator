Putin Hails Ethiopia-Russia Trade Surge to $1.3 Billion

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 05:09:52 GMT

Russian President Vladimir Putin has praised the significant increase in trade between Russia and Ethiopia, which reached over $1.3 billion in the first nine months of 2026, more than doubling the total for 2025. During discussions with Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed at the Third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow, Putin emphasized the importance of enhancing economic cooperation across various sectors, including energy, agriculture, industry, and healthcare. He described the relationship between the two nations as friendly and constructive, noting their collaboration in international forums like the UN and BRICS. Putin expressed support for Africa's role in the emerging multipolar world and highlighted Russia's commitment to food and energy security on the continent. He also wished Ethiopia success in hosting the 2027 United Nations Climate Change Conference. The summit is set to conclude with a new Action Plan for the Russia-Africa Partnership Forum covering the years 2027-2029. (160 words)
