Somalia plunged deeper into political crisis after Supreme Court rules presidential term extension unlawful - Horseed Media

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 16:39:28 GMT

Somalia is facing a deepening political crisis following a Supreme Court ruling that deemed President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term extension unconstitutional. The ruling has intensified tensions between the government and opposition groups, with accusations of judicial interference and concerns over constitutional integrity. While some residents support the court's decision as a necessary affirmation of the rule of law, others criticize it, arguing that the court was operational during the approval of the new constitution. The president's declaration of a transition period to extend his presidency has been met with resistance, as opponents view it as a power grab. The Supreme Court has called for political parties to reach a new agreement within 30 days and urged elections within 90 days. The situation has escalated further with the president's dismissal of the Chief Justice, which the court rejected, leading to accusations of an assault on the judiciary. Amidst ongoing security challenges from Al-Shabaab, Somalia's political stability remains precarious.
