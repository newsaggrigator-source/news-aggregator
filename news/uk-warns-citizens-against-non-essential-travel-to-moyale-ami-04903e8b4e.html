UK warns citizens against non-essential travel to Moyale amid Ethiopia clashes - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 20:02:08 GMT

The UK government has issued a warning to its citizens advising against non-essential travel to Moyale, a town on the Ethiopia-Kenya border, due to ongoing clashes in the region. The Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) highlighted the potential risks associated with the violence, which has reportedly resulted in casualties and displacement of residents. The situation has escalated in recent weeks, prompting concerns over safety and security for travelers. The FCDO's advisory emphasizes the importance of staying informed about local conditions and suggests that those already in the area should exercise caution and remain vigilant. Travelers are encouraged to monitor updates from local authorities and consider their safety before making travel plans. The advisory reflects the UK’s commitment to ensuring the safety of its citizens abroad amid rising tensions in the region.
