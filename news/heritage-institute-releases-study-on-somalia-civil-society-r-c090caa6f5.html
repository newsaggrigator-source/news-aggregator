Heritage Institute Releases Study on Somalia Civil Society Role - Dawan Africa

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 19:39:56 GMT

The Heritage Institute for Policy Studies has released a comprehensive study on the role of civil society organizations in Somalia, focusing on their effectiveness and the challenges they face. The report highlights how these organizations have become crucial in providing essential services like healthcare and education since the collapse of the central government in the early 1990s. It emphasizes their contributions to promoting transparency, government accountability, and public participation in national affairs. Mursal Mahamuud Saney, the Institute's Director, noted that Somali civil society has been instrumental in the state-building process and the establishment of Somalia's Third Republic. The study underscores the need to recognize both the significant contributions of civil society and the obstacles that may hinder their effectiveness in fostering a more accountable and transparent governance framework in Somalia.
