Ethiopian politics: Who is new Parliamentary Speaker Shide? - TRT Afrika

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 03:19:31 GMT

Ahmed Shide Mohamed has been elected as the new Speaker of Ethiopia's House of Peoples’ Representatives, marking a significant milestone as he is the first politician of Somali origin to hold this position. His election occurred during the inaugural session of the seventh parliamentary term, which began on October 5, 2026. Shide, representing the Chereti constituency in the Somali Regional State, previously served as Ethiopia’s Minister of Transport and Logistics and has held various senior government roles, including Minister of Finance. His extensive experience in economic policymaking has earned him recognition as one of Africa's influential finance ministers. Shide holds multiple degrees, including a Master of Arts from the University of Sussex and an MBA from the University of Greenwich. His election is seen as a step towards greater representation for Somali-origin politicians in Ethiopia's federal government. The new parliamentary leadership also includes Muferihat Kamil as Speaker of the House of Federation.
