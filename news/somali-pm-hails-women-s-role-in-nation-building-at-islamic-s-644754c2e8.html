Somali PM Hails Women’s Role in Nation-Building at Islamic Scholars Conference - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:40:46 GMT

At the closing ceremony of the Somali Women Islamic Scholars Assembly in Mogadishu, Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre emphasized the crucial role of women in nation-building and community development. He described women as the "backbone" of Somalia, acknowledging their contributions to education, social awareness, and the reconstruction of the nation following decades of conflict. The conference aimed to strengthen the moral and social fabric of Somali society, with discussions addressing the challenges posed by extremist ideologies. While Barre's remarks align with the government's efforts to enhance women's participation in public life, challenges remain, particularly in meeting the 30% quota for women in parliamentary seats. Recent elections in the South West State highlighted this gap, with women securing only 17% of the seats. The Prime Minister urged women scholars to take active roles in state-building and community faith, signaling a potential shift in policy focus towards empowering women in leadership and decision-making roles.
