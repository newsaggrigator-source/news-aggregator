WHERE ARE THE SOMALI INTELLECTUALS WHO WILL STAND UP TO SAVE SOMALIA? - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 14:58:48 GMT

The article emphasizes the urgent need for Somali intellectuals to step up and address the country's ongoing political challenges, drawing lessons from the past. It reflects on the collapse of Somalia's state institutions in 1991, which led to civil war and national fragmentation. The author, Amb. Ali Mohamed Mohamud Cagabarur, argues that today's intellectuals must take responsibility to prevent history from repeating itself. While Somalia has made strides in establishing government institutions, political disputes and a lack of public trust threaten stability. Intellectuals are called upon to facilitate dialogue, promote national unity, and engage with all political stakeholders without aligning with specific factions. The article stresses that true leadership involves speaking uncomfortable truths and fostering a collective civic voice to address constitutional disagreements and electoral processes. Ultimately, it calls for a national gathering of respected figures to mediate political tensions and safeguard the future of Somali statehood, emphasizing that this responsibility lies with all citizens, not just political leaders.
