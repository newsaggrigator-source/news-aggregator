Mastercard Introduces AI and Tokenization to Enhance Digital Payment Security in Ethiopia - 2Merkato.com

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:19:46 GMT

Mastercard has announced the introduction of advanced AI and tokenization technologies to bolster digital payment security in Ethiopia. This initiative aims to enhance the safety and efficiency of online transactions, addressing the growing concerns over digital fraud and cyber threats. By implementing tokenization, Mastercard will replace sensitive payment information with unique identification symbols, ensuring that actual card details are not exposed during transactions. This move is part of Mastercard's broader strategy to support the digital economy in Ethiopia, which is experiencing rapid growth in e-commerce and mobile payments. The company emphasizes that these innovations will not only protect consumers but also foster trust in digital payment systems, encouraging more users to engage in online financial activities. Mastercard's efforts align with the Ethiopian government's goals to promote financial inclusion and digital transformation across the country.
