Ethiopia Working to Become Continental Pharmaceutical Manufacturing, Says Health State Minister

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 04:46:17 GMT

Ethiopia is actively working to establish itself as a continental hub for pharmaceutical manufacturing, according to Health State Minister Dr. Muluken Argaw. Speaking at the African Healthcare Manufacturing Trade Exhibition and Conference (AHMTEC) 2026 in Addis Ababa, he emphasized the importance of local production of essential medicines, vaccines, and medical devices for health security and economic transformation. The initiative aims to leverage the African Continental Free Trade Area, enhance regional value chains, and improve regulatory cooperation. Dr. Muluken highlighted Ethiopia's goal to reduce import dependence and strengthen medical tourism, projecting the pharmaceutical market to exceed $4 billion by 2030. He noted that the African Medicines Agency and pooled procurement could streamline supply and demand across the continent. Additionally, industry leaders underscored the need for African manufacturers to enhance supply security and collaborate effectively to address challenges in healthcare product provision. Ethiopia offers robust infrastructure and regulatory support to attract pharmaceutical investments.
