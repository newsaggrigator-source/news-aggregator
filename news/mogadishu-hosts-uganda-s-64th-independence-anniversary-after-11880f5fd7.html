Mogadishu Hosts Uganda’s 64th Independence Anniversary After Landmark Trade Talks - FTL Somalia

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 15:20:07 GMT

Mogadishu recently hosted Uganda's 64th Independence Anniversary, marking the conclusion of three days of significant bilateral discussions aimed at enhancing trade, tourism, investment, and labor relations between Somalia and Uganda. The celebrations took place at the Ugandan embassy, with Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud attending as the chief guest. He praised the enduring friendship between the two nations and acknowledged the contributions of Ugandan troops in Somalia since 2007. The event followed the first Somali-Ugandan Business Forum and a review of the Uganda-Somalia Joint Permanent Commission, which focused on various sectors, including security and health. A bilateral labor agreement was signed to protect workers' rights and regulate employment between the two countries. Despite the growing economic ties, trade remains modest, with Uganda's exports to Somalia increasing but still low overall. Both nations aim to leverage their membership in the East African Community to foster deeper integration and economic collaboration, although significant infrastructure and institutional challenges persist.
