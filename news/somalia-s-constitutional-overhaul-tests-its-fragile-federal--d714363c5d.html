Somalia’s constitutional overhaul tests its fragile federal bargain - Al Jazeera

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 00:42:22 GMT

Somalia's ongoing constitutional overhaul, led by President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, is testing the fragile federal arrangement established in 2012. The president's recent dismissal of the chief justice amid a legal dispute over his term extension has intensified tensions between the government and the judiciary. Proposed amendments aim to strengthen national institutions and clarify power distribution, but they have sparked resistance from regional states like Puntland and Jubaland, which view the changes as undermining their autonomy. The political landscape is further complicated by violent clashes and disputes over elections, particularly in the South West state. While some regions support Mohamud's reforms, others argue that they threaten the delicate balance of power that has kept Somalia united. Experts warn that the push for a centralized federal system could exacerbate divisions and lead to further instability, as unresolved issues from the original federal bargain continue to hinder governance and cooperation among the states.
