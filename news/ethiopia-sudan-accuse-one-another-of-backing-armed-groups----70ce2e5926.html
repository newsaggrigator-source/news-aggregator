Ethiopia, Sudan Accuse One Another of Backing Armed Groups - https://dailyasianage.com/

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 22:53:33 GMT

Tensions between Ethiopia and Sudan have escalated as both nations accuse each other of supporting armed groups within their territories. This diplomatic dispute raises concerns about a potential wider conflict in the politically unstable Horn of Africa. Sudan's military has alleged that Ethiopia is harboring the Rapid Support Forces (RSF), which are engaged in a civil war against Sudan's Armed Forces, claiming that Ethiopian territory is being used for military operations against Sudan. In response, Ethiopia has accused Sudan of backing rebels in its northern Tigray region, where fighting has resumed, threatening a previous peace agreement. The situation has led to reciprocal closures of consulates and expulsions of diplomatic staff, further straining relations. Historical disputes, particularly over the Al-Fashaga border region and the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, complicate matters. The humanitarian impact is severe, with thousands dead and millions displaced, highlighting the urgent need for international diplomatic intervention to prevent further escalation and promote regional stability.
