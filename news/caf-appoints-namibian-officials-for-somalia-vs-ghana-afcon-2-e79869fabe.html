CAF appoints Namibian officials for Somalia vs Ghana AFCON 2027 Qualifier - Ghana Football Association

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 22:56:20 GMT

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) has appointed Namibian officials for the upcoming AFCON 2027 Qualifier match between Somalia and Ghana, scheduled for November 16, 2026, at the Essipong Sports Stadium. The match will be officiated by referee Mweshitsama Negongo Naftal, supported by assistants Alex Muzibwane Lumponjani and Klaus Masati Bonifatius. Petrus Amutenya will serve as the Fourth Official, while Sekamana Abdoul Khaliq from Rwanda will act as the Match Commissioner, and Brian Nsubuga Miiro from Uganda will be the Referee Assessor. Ghana, having suffered two defeats in their initial qualifying matches against Côte d'Ivoire and The Gambia, is looking to secure a crucial victory against Somalia to revive their campaign and aim for six points in the qualifiers. The Ghana Football Association is focused on improving the team's performance in this critical match.
