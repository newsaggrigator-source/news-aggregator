Ethiopia reportedly plans to withdraw more than 3,000 troops from Somalia - Horseed Media

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:15:00 GMT

Ethiopia is reportedly planning to withdraw over 3,000 troops from Somalia amid escalating internal conflicts, particularly in the Tigray region. This potential troop reduction has not been officially confirmed by either the Ethiopian or Somali governments, nor by the African Union. A Somali Defence Ministry official stated that they have not received any notification regarding the withdrawal. Ethiopian forces have been involved in various capacities in Somalia, including under the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission (AUSSOM) and through bilateral agreements with Mogadishu. The withdrawal could significantly impact security in Somalia, where Ethiopian troops have played a crucial role in combating al-Shabab. However, the specifics of the withdrawal, including which units will be affected and whether replacements will be deployed, remain unclear. This situation reflects Ethiopia's shifting military priorities as it reallocates resources to address domestic conflicts. The implications for Somalia's security landscape could be substantial if the withdrawal proceeds.
