Seven Former Ethiopian Diplomats Urge U.S. to Distinguish Opposition Alliance From Separatist Movements

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 11:18:28 GMT

Seven former Ethiopian diplomats, including ex-Foreign Affairs Minister Gedu Andargachew, have urged U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio to differentiate between the opposition alliance and separatist movements in Ethiopia. In a letter dated October 26, 2026, they expressed concern that Rubio's recent comments on C-SPAN oversimplified the complex political and security crisis in Ethiopia. They argued that labeling the Ethiopian Peoples' Forces Alliance for Survival as "separatists" misrepresents their goals, which focus on achieving an inclusive democratic transition rather than dismantling the state. The diplomats emphasized the need for the U.S. to assess the alliance based on its stated objectives and conduct, while also calling for accountability for human rights violations by all parties involved. They warned that continued instability in Ethiopia could have broader regional implications and urged for a diplomatic approach that supports a peaceful political process. Their concerns were echoed by 11 Ethiopian American civic organizations, advocating for a nuanced understanding of the conflict. (159 words)
