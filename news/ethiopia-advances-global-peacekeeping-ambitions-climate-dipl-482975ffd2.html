Ethiopia Advances Global Peacekeeping Ambitions, Climate Diplomacy and Strategic Red Sea Aspirations - ENA English

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 16:47:05 GMT

Ethiopia is actively enhancing its global peacekeeping role, climate diplomacy, and strategic interests in the Red Sea, as highlighted in recent developments. The country aims to strengthen its legacy in international peacekeeping, adapting to a complex security landscape while preparing to host COP32 in 2027, which it views as a pivotal opportunity to showcase its climate initiatives and attract investment. Ethiopia's longstanding contributions to peacekeeping, including missions in various conflict zones, underscore its commitment to regional stability. Additionally, discussions around securing sovereign access to the Red Sea emphasize the need for Ethiopia to establish its maritime interests independently. The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD) is recognized as a significant achievement for energy production and regional cooperation. Furthermore, Ethiopia's diplomatic engagements are bolstered by the arrival of new ambassadors, signaling a commitment to enhancing bilateral relations across various sectors. Overall, Ethiopia seeks to leverage its historical strengths to increase its influence in regional and global affairs. (160 words)
