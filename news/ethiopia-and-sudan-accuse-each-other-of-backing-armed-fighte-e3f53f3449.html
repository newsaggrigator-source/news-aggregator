Ethiopia and Sudan accuse each other of backing armed fighters as fears of a wider conflict grow - myMotherLode.com

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 06:53:00 GMT

Tensions between Ethiopia and Sudan have intensified as both nations accuse each other of supporting armed groups that threaten their stability. Sudan's military claims Ethiopia has allowed Sudanese paramilitary forces to use its territory for operations against the Sudanese army, prompting Sudan to file complaints with the U.N. Security Council. In retaliation, Ethiopia expelled Sudanese consulate staff and closed its mission. Ethiopia alleges that Sudan supports Tigray rebels, reigniting conflict in the Tigray region, which had seen a peace agreement in 2022. The ongoing war in Sudan, which began in April 2023, has resulted in significant casualties and displacement. Analysts warn that the situation could escalate into a broader proxy war, fueled by external alliances and unresolved territorial disputes, particularly over resources like the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam and the contested Al-Fashaga region. If left unaddressed, these tensions could lead to further instability and humanitarian crises in both countries.
