Somalia orders UAE ambassador to leave within 72 hours — Cyprus Mail - UA.NEWS

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 23:15:15 GMT

Somalia has ordered the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador to leave the country within 72 hours, citing violations of its sovereignty and labeling the UAE's actions as an "open invasion." The Somali government has also recalled its envoy from Abu Dhabi. Information Minister Abdifatah Qasim Mohamud emphasized the need for national unity to defend Somalia's sovereignty and security. The decision follows accusations from President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud that the UAE has been supporting regional governments that oppose the federal authorities. In response, the UAE's Ministry of Foreign Affairs condemned Somalia's actions as escalatory and hostile, suggesting they are attempts to involve the UAE in Somalia's internal conflicts. The UAE had previously closed its embassy in Mogadishu and withdrawn diplomatic personnel due to tensions with the Somali government, which had terminated various agreements with the UAE earlier this year. The situation highlights ongoing diplomatic strains between the two nations.
