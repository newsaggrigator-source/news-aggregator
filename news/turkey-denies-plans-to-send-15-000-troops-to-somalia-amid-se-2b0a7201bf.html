Turkey denies plans to send 15,000 troops to Somalia amid security concerns - Somali Guardian

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 16:28:29 GMT

Turkey has officially denied reports claiming it plans to deploy 15,000 troops to Somalia, stating that such assertions are inaccurate. The Turkish Ministry of National Defense clarified that its current forces in Somalia will continue their training and advisory roles, as emphasized by Rear Admiral Zeki Aktürk during a press briefing. The rumors originated from Somali media earlier in November, which suggested that Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud had requested foreign troops, including 15,000 from Turkey, to address security concerns following Uganda's decision to withdraw its forces from Somalia. This withdrawal has raised fears of a security vacuum that could be exploited by the militant group Al-Shabaab. Turkey's military presence in Somalia, centered around Camp TURKSOM in Mogadishu, focuses on training and capacity-building rather than large-scale combat, with ongoing cooperation under a long-term defense agreement. The Turkish government intends to maintain its current role while rejecting the notion of a significant troop increase. (159 words)
