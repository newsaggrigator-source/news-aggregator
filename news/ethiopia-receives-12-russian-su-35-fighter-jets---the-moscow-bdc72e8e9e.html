Ethiopia Receives 12 Russian Su-35 Fighter Jets - The Moscow Times

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 16:19:16 GMT

Ethiopia has received 12 Russian Su-35 fighter jets as part of its efforts to enhance military capabilities, according to Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. He announced on X that this delivery, facilitated by Russian President Vladimir Putin, is a significant step in strengthening Ethiopia's national security and deterring potential aggression. The jets were delivered ahead of schedule, although the financial details of the agreement and the timeline for the subsequent delivery of four S-400 air defense systems remain undisclosed. Ethiopia is currently facing various security challenges, particularly in the Tigray region, where renewed conflict has emerged following a rebel offensive. The previous conflict highlighted the importance of air power, particularly through the use of foreign drones. The situation has strained relations with neighboring Eritrea, which Ethiopia accuses of supporting the rebels, a claim Eritrea denies. The Russian state-owned defense conglomerate Rostec has not commented on the matter.
