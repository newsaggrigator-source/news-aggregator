Azerbaijan, Somalia explore prospects for cooperation in culture and communications - AzerNews

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 11:37:00 GMT

Azerbaijan and Somalia are exploring potential cooperation in the fields of culture and communications, as discussed in a meeting on October 28, 2026, between Ahmad Ismayilov, Chairman of the Media and Broadcasting Council of Azerbaijan, and Abdulfatah Kasim Mohamud, Somalia’s Minister of Information, Culture and Tourism. Ismayilov highlighted the significance of enhancing media collaboration to foster mutual understanding and strengthen bilateral relations. Mohamud expressed Somalia's eagerness to partner with Azerbaijan, noting the favorable conditions for joint initiatives in media and communications. The discussions also covered contemporary challenges in the media landscape, including digitalization, combating disinformation, and the influence of artificial intelligence and new technologies. Both parties recognized the importance of sharing experiences and developing professional ties to address these challenges effectively. This meeting marks a step towards establishing a framework for collaboration between the two nations in the evolving information environment.
