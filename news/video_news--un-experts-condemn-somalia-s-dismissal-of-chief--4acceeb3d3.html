Fri, 13 Nov 2026 13:31:37 GMT

UN experts have expressed strong condemnation regarding the recent dismissal of Somalia's chief justice, calling for his immediate reinstatement. The experts highlighted that the removal of the chief justice undermines the independence of the judiciary and poses a significant threat to the rule of law in the country. They emphasized that such actions could lead to a deterioration of human rights and judicial integrity in Somalia. The UN officials urged the Somali government to respect judicial independence and adhere to international standards regarding the appointment and dismissal of judicial officials. The experts' statement reflects growing concerns over political interference in the judiciary, which they believe could hinder efforts to establish a stable and fair legal system in Somalia. They reiterated the importance of upholding democratic principles and ensuring that judicial processes remain free from political influence. The call for reinstatement underscores the need for accountability and respect for the judiciary in the nation’s governance.
