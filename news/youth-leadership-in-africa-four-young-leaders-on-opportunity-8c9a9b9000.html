Youth Leadership in Africa: Four Young Leaders on Opportunity, Inclusion, and Change

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 13:30:16 GMT

The article highlights the voices of four young African leaders—Benson Wando, Brenda Adong, Shukurat Adijat Idowu, and Lidiya Alemayehu—who advocate for greater youth involvement in decision-making processes across the continent. They emphasize that young people should not only be heard but trusted to lead, as their experiences and insights can drive meaningful change. Wando discusses the importance of recognizing informal expertise, particularly among women vendors in Kenya. Adong shares her transformative journey from hardship to empowerment through community support, while Idowu stresses the need for practical pathways to opportunity for young people. Alemayehu, a disability advocate, calls for inclusive systems that consider the needs of young people with disabilities. Collectively, they urge institutions to invest in youth leadership, create accessible opportunities, and trust young people as partners in shaping their futures, thereby fostering stronger communities and economies across Africa.
