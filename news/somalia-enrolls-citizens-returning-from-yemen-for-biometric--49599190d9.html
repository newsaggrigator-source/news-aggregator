Somalia enrolls citizens returning from Yemen for biometric national IDs - Biometric Update

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 18:40:00 GMT

Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) has initiated a program to enroll citizens returning from Yemen into its biometric national ID system. This effort, announced on October 21, aims to facilitate the reintegration of these returnees into society and improve their access to public services. The enrollment process is conducted in collaboration with the National Commission for Refugees and Internally Displaced Persons (NCRI), which manages a reception camp for the returnees. NIRA's Deputy Director General, Abdirahman Said Ahmed, assured the returnees of support in accessing identity services essential for their social and economic reintegration. Since launching its biometric ID system in 2023, Somalia has enrolled around two million individuals, with a goal of reaching 15 million by 2029. This initiative is part of a broader strategy to integrate the national ID with various public services, including banking and travel, and is aligned with global efforts to enhance identity systems for displaced populations.
