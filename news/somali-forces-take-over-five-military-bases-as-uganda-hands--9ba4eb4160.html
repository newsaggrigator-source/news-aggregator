Somali Forces Take Over Five Military Bases as Uganda Hands Over Security Duties in Barawe - Garowe Online

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 06:31:26 GMT

Ugandan troops have officially handed over five military bases in the Barawe area to the Somali National Army (SNA) as part of a gradual transfer of security responsibilities. The handover ceremony took place at the headquarters of Uganda's 45th Battalion, led by Col. Dr. Justus Musenene. The bases transferred include Barawe Embresso, Barawe Knoll, Kismayo Road, Seaport, and Runway, all of which were operational at the time of the transfer. Musenene emphasized the importance of the SNA's growing capabilities and urged local residents to support ongoing peace efforts. Col. Mohamed Ahmed Abd of the SNA praised the historic cooperation with Ugandan forces and acknowledged their contributions to local security and development. The handover is part of broader efforts to enhance Somalia's security forces, with continued support from the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) to build national security institutions.
