Somalia Opens Party Registration for 2027 Elections Amid Opposition Dispute - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 19:41:05 GMT

Somalia has initiated the registration process for political parties aiming to participate in the 2027 federal elections, despite ongoing disputes among opposition groups regarding the electoral framework. The registration period, from November 1 to November 22, 2026, allows parties to apply for a one-person, one-vote system that will enable citizens to directly elect their parliamentary representatives. The National Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission has set a timetable for parliamentary elections on April 15, 2027, followed by a presidential election on May 15. However, opposition factions are demanding a political agreement before proceeding, citing unresolved constitutional issues and the need for consensus on electoral rules. The overlapping deadlines for registration and candidate submissions create pressure on political parties, while the commission continues its preparations despite opposition objections. The situation raises concerns about whether the electoral process can gain broader acceptance before campaigning begins on February 27, 2027.
