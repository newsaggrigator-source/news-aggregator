Ethiopia’s Strategic Interests, Historic Ties Bind It to Red Sea: Ambassador Zerihun - ENA English

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 07:31:12 GMT

Ethiopia's connection to the Red Sea is crucial for its national interests, as emphasized by Ambassador Zerihun Abebe during the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa. He highlighted that Ethiopia's pursuit of sea access is deeply rooted in its historical, geographical, and security contexts, asserting that having a sea outlet is vital for the country's survival. The ambassador noted that Ethiopia's strategic position and security interests necessitate its inclusion in discussions regarding the Red Sea. He called for regional cooperation that acknowledges Ethiopia's stakes, especially in light of evolving geopolitical dynamics. The forum also addressed the growing militarization and competition in the Red Sea, which pose risks to regional stability. National Intelligence and Security Service Director General Redwan Hussein stressed the importance of collective action among regional states to secure maritime routes. The Tana Forum serves as a platform for policymakers and diplomats to explore African-led solutions to peace and security challenges in the region.
