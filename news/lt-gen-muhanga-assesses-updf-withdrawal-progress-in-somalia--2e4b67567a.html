Lt Gen Muhanga Assesses UPDF Withdrawal Progress In Somalia - UPDF

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 10:51:22 GMT

Lieutenant General Kayanja Muhanga, the Commander of the Uganda Peoples’ Defence Forces (UPDF) Land Force, recently visited Ugandan troops in Somalia to evaluate the progress of their withdrawal under the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission (AUSSOM). During his visit to Sector One Headquarters and Forward Operating Base Bulo Mareer, he praised the troops for their professionalism and commitment to peace operations. He highlighted the significance of teamwork and Pan-Africanism in aiding Somalia's quest for stability. Brigadier General Jackson Kayanja commended Battle Groups 44 and 45 for their disciplined withdrawal and successful handover of responsibilities to the Somali National Army (SNA). He stressed the need for vigilance, adherence to Standard Operating Procedures, and collaboration with local communities to sustain peace. The visit underscores ongoing efforts by AUSSOM and Somali forces to combat Al-Shabaab and enhance security in the region. Lt Gen Muhanga was accompanied by several senior officers during this assessment.
