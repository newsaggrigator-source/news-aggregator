Ghana eye back-to-back wins against Somalia today to revive AFCON qualification hopes - Pulse Ghana

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 06:31:58 GMT

Ghana's interim coach Kim Lars Björkegren is aiming for a second consecutive win against Somalia to secure six points in the ongoing 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers. Following a 2-0 victory in their first match at the Essipong Sports Stadium, where Jonas Adjetey and Felix Afena-Gyan scored, Björkegren is optimistic about the team's potential despite a rocky start to the qualifying campaign. He emphasized the importance of starting strong and capitalizing on scoring opportunities in the upcoming match. The coach has analyzed footage of Somalia to strategize for the return fixture, scheduled for November 16. Björkegren believes that if Ghana qualifies for AFCON, they have the capability to compete for the title. After losing their initial two matches against Côte d'Ivoire and The Gambia, a win against Somalia would significantly improve Ghana's chances of qualifying for the tournament. The coach praised the players' motivation and the positive atmosphere within the squad.
