Peace-Security Anchor Ethiopia Must Consolidate Commitments to Peace and Security - Former PM

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 04:46:26 GMT

Former Ethiopian Prime Minister Hailemariam Desalegn emphasized the need for Ethiopia to reinforce its commitment to peace and security during a recent interview at the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa. He highlighted Ethiopia's role as a regional peace anchor, citing its diplomatic efforts in the Sudan crisis and ongoing involvement in Somalia. Hailemariam praised the Tana Forum for fostering open dialogue among African leaders, distinguishing it from other international platforms by allowing candid discussions on pressing security issues. He noted that Ethiopia's internal peace initiatives are commendable and reflect a commitment to resolving conflicts through dialogue and compromise. With a population exceeding 130 million, he stressed that both domestic and regional stability are crucial for the nation. Hailemariam called for continuous strengthening of peace initiatives to achieve meaningful results across the region, underscoring that peace benefits everyone involved.
