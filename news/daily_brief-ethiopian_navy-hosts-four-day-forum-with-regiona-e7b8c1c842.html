The Ethiopian Navy is currently hosting a four-day regional maritime forum in Addis Ababa, which has gathered naval commanders from Yemen, Oman, Djibouti, and Somaliland. This forum aims to enhance collaboration and dialogue among the participating nations regarding maritime security and regional stability. The event serves as a platform for sharing best practices, addressing common challenges, and fostering partnerships in naval operations. The Ethiopian Navy's initiative reflects its commitment to strengthening maritime cooperation in the region, particularly in light of increasing maritime threats and the need for coordinated responses. The forum is expected to facilitate discussions on various topics, including piracy, illegal fishing, and smuggling, ultimately contributing to a safer maritime environment. The gathering underscores the importance of regional alliances in addressing shared security concerns and promoting peace in the waters surrounding the Horn of Africa.
