PM Abiy Holds Talks With Qatar's Amir On Sidelines of COP31 Leaders' Summit

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:29:19 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed met with Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al-Thani, the Amir of Qatar, during the COP31 Leaders' Summit. The bilateral discussions aimed to enhance the longstanding diplomatic relations between Ethiopia and Qatar, focusing on expanding cooperation in various sectors of mutual interest. Prime Minister Abiy highlighted the importance of the meeting through a social media post, emphasizing the commitment to strengthen ties and explore new opportunities for collaboration. This engagement underscores the ongoing high-level diplomatic efforts between the two nations, reflecting a shared interest in fostering bilateral relations. The meeting is part of broader efforts to enhance cooperation in areas that benefit both countries, marking a significant step in their diplomatic journey.
