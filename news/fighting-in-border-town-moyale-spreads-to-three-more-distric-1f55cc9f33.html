Fighting in Border Town Moyale Spreads to Three More Districts, Exchange of Heavy Gunfire

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 04:54:30 GMT

Fighting in the border town of Moyale, Ethiopia, has escalated from a local dispute over the Somali Regional State flag to widespread violence affecting three additional districts: Gumi Eldallo, Wachile, and Arero. The conflict began on October 18, following the killing of a 13-year-old girl, which heightened tensions between security forces from the Oromia and Somali regions. Clashes erupted early on October 22, with reports of heavy gunfire and significant property damage, including the burning of homes. Residents have fled the area, and transport services have been disrupted. Both sides accuse each other of provocation, with claims of attacks and retaliatory violence. Eyewitnesses noted that the Ethiopian National Defense Force and Federal Police have not intervened. The ongoing violence reflects deeper territorial disputes between the Borana and Garre communities, exacerbated by administrative changes in the Somali Region that have previously led to significant unrest and displacement. The situation remains volatile, with casualties reported but unverified.
