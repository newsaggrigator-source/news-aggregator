How Somalia’s ties with the UAE unravelled over a decade - Somalia Today

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 17:34:46 GMT

Somalia's diplomatic relations with the United Arab Emirates (UAE) have deteriorated significantly over the past decade, culminating in the recent expulsion of the UAE ambassador from Somalia. The Somali government accused the UAE of "blatant aggression" against its sovereignty, prompting a reciprocal withdrawal of its envoy from Abu Dhabi. Tensions escalated following disputes over port and security agreements, particularly as regional administrations like Somaliland pursued independent deals with the UAE, which Mogadishu deemed unconstitutional. The situation worsened in 2018 when Somalia seized $9.6 million from an Emirati aircraft, leading to the UAE halting military training programs. Although relations briefly improved after a public apology from Somalia in 2022, they soured again after Israel recognized Somaliland as independent in late 2025, a move Somalia condemned. The ongoing rivalry between Saudi Arabia and the UAE further complicates Somalia's position, as recent incidents have raised concerns about violations of its sovereignty.
