Uganda, Somalia urge private sector to tap growing market - The Independent Uganda

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 04:11:55 GMT

Uganda and Somalia are encouraging their private sectors to capitalize on the growing trade opportunities between the two nations. Recent data shows that Uganda's exports to Somalia surged from sh11.4 billion ($2.75 million) in 2023 to sh28 billion ($6.78 million) in 2024, with tobacco products leading the exports. However, trade from Somalia to Uganda remains minimal, highlighting untapped potential. During a mid-term review of the Uganda-Somalia Joint Permanent Commission in Mogadishu, officials emphasized the need for businesses to explore partnerships and new markets. Both countries see significant opportunities in sectors like trade, investment, tourism, and agriculture. Uganda's strategic location and Somalia's extensive coastline could facilitate trade routes, reducing shipping costs and enhancing market access. The forum aims to foster cooperation and implement agreements that drive economic growth. Key priorities include executing existing commitments and establishing effective follow-up mechanisms to ensure tangible benefits for both nations. The event culminates in the signing of a Mogadishu Declaration on trade and investment.
