Somalia to Get First Genomics Lab as Dr. Sumait Hospital Expands - Dawan Africa

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 20:43:05 GMT

Somalia is set to establish its first Genomics Laboratory at Dr. Sumait Hospital, following the laying of the foundation stone for a new building funded by Direct Aid. This facility, affiliated with SIMAD University, will include modern inpatient and outpatient services, as well as dedicated areas for patient reception and medical consultations. The expansion aims to enhance Somalia's capabilities in specialized medical testing, research, and the training of healthcare professionals. The foundation stone was laid by key figures, including SIMAD University President Dr. Abdikarim Mohyiddin Ahmed and the Director General of the Ministry of Health, Yusuf Hassan Isak. Additionally, the project plans to attract international doctors to provide specialized healthcare services, allowing Somali patients to access advanced medical care locally. This initiative coincides with SIMAD University’s 27th anniversary, highlighting its commitment to improving education, research, and healthcare services in Somalia.
