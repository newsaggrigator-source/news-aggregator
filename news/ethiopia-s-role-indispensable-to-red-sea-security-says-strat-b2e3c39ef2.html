Ethiopia's Role Indispensable to Red Sea Security, Says Strategic Forum CEO

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 08:45:07 GMT

Ethiopia's strategic significance for Red Sea security is increasingly recognized, according to Solomon Tefera, CEO of the Nile and Red Sea Strategic Forum. He emphasized that Ethiopia's involvement is crucial for achieving lasting stability in the region, given its geographical position and population of approximately 130 million. Previously reliant on the Red Sea, Ethiopia is now seen as essential for regional maritime security, especially as the Horn of Africa becomes a hotspot for geopolitical competition. As a landlocked nation dependent on Djibouti's port for trade, Ethiopia faces risks from disruptions in maritime routes, particularly around the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait. Solomon highlighted that reliable maritime access is vital for Ethiopia's economic resilience and national security. He also called for a deeper understanding of Ethiopia's historical and legal loss of sea access, advocating for constructive dialogue to foster regional cooperation. The newly established forum aims to advance Ethiopia's interests through research and policy advocacy. (159 words)
