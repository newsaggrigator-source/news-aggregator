Ethiopia Receives 12 Russian SU-35 Fighter Jets, Plans to Acquire Four S-400 Air Defense Batteries

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 05:06:59 GMT

Ethiopia has received 12 Russian-made SU-35 fighter jets and plans to acquire four S-400 air defense batteries, marking a significant enhancement of its military capabilities amid rising security concerns in the Horn of Africa. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announced the delivery, which occurred a year ahead of schedule, during his visit to Moscow for the Russia-Africa summit. He emphasized that these acquisitions would bolster Ethiopia's national defense and regional stability, positioning the country as a leader in military capability among Sub-Saharan nations. The SU-35s are designed for multi-role combat, while the S-400 system is intended for long-range air defense. This development follows previous military cooperation with Russia, which has supplied various aircraft and electronic warfare systems to Ethiopia. The ongoing military partnership reflects Ethiopia's reliance on Russian arms, which constituted 43% of its major arms imports from 2016 to 2025.
