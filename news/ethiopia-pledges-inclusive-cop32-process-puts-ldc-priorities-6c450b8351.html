Ethiopia Pledges Inclusive COP32 Process, Puts LDC Priorities At Center of Climate Agenda

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 05:24:35 GMT

Ethiopia has committed to an inclusive and transparent process for the upcoming COP32, scheduled for November 2027 in Addis Ababa, emphasizing the priorities of Least Developed Countries (LDCs). Foreign Affairs Minister Gedion Timothewos, during discussions with LDC Group Chair Ambassador Adão Soares Barbosa at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, highlighted the importance of collaboration and consultation to ensure LDC interests are central to the conference's agenda. Ethiopia aims to build consensus and advance climate outcomes that reflect the aspirations of vulnerable nations. The country is actively using its participation in COP31 to strengthen international partnerships and facilitate constructive negotiations for COP32. Gedion noted that Ethiopia has made significant progress in its preparations, including establishing a dedicated team and learning from previous COP experiences. The Ethiopian Pavilion at COP31 serves as a platform for dialogue and showcases the nation’s climate initiatives, aiming to generate interest and encourage broad participation in the upcoming summit.
