Kenya monitoring security situation as Ethiopian refugees flock Moyale - Citizen Digital

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 14:18:30 GMT

Kenya is closely monitoring the security situation in Moyale as an influx of Ethiopian refugees arrives due to ongoing conflict between armed groups from the Oromo and Somali communities in Ethiopia. Interior Principal Secretary Raymond Omollo expressed concerns that the increasing number of refugees is straining Kenya's security resources, with incidents of stray bullets crossing the border. To address these challenges, the Inspector General of Police and national government officials have been deployed to the area to help manage the situation. Omollo emphasized the importance of community engagement to prevent local tensions and potential retaliatory actions. Additionally, he highlighted the need for adequate medical supplies to mitigate the risk of disease outbreaks among the growing refugee population. Ongoing discussions between Kenyan and Ethiopian officials aim to ensure a coordinated response to the crisis while maintaining peace and stability in the region.
