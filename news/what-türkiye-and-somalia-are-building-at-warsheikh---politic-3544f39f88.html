What Türkiye and Somalia Are Building at Warsheikh - Politics Today

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 06:22:21 GMT

Türkiye is establishing a launch site in Warsheikh, Somalia, aimed at supporting its National Space Program and catering to the growing global demand for satellite launches. Announced by Turkish officials, the site will enable Türkiye to develop and launch its own rockets while providing Somalia with potential revenue and technical training opportunities. The location, situated near the equator, offers logistical advantages for launching rockets, making it a cost-effective option compared to other sites. However, the specifics regarding land use, revenue sharing, and operational oversight remain undisclosed. The project builds on Türkiye and Somalia's existing cooperation, which includes military training and maritime security. While the Somali government has welcomed the initiative, local communities are concerned about the implications for their land and fishing rights. The success of this venture will depend on establishing a reliable safety record and attracting customers in a competitive market. The first launch is tentatively targeted for 2027.
