Ethiopia War Security Review, November 13, 2026 - Critical Threats

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 00:29:16 GMT

The Ethiopia War Security Review from November 10 to November 13, 2026, highlights ongoing conflicts involving the Ethiopian federal forces, the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF), and Eritrean military units. Reports indicate that Amhara Fano National Movement fighters may have allied with TPLF forces against federal troops near the Ethiopia-Eritrea border, although these claims remain unverified. Significant clashes occurred around Badme, with the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) engaging TPLF and Eritrean forces. The interim Tigray government is establishing its administration amid internal tensions, while forced conscription by Ethiopian security forces has been reported in Oromia and Addis Ababa, leading to protests. International actors are advocating for de-escalation between Ethiopia and Eritrea, with U.S. senators urging increased mediation efforts. Despite calls for restraint, both nations continue to accuse each other of escalating tensions, reflecting the complex and volatile situation in the region.
