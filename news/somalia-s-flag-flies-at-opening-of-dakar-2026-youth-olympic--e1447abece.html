Somalia's Flag Flies at Opening of Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games - Dawan Africa

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 09:26:38 GMT

Somalia's flag was prominently displayed at the opening ceremony of the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, marking a significant moment as it is the first Olympic event held on African soil. The ceremony took place at the Abdoulaye Wade Stadium in Senegal, with the Games running from October 31 to November 13, featuring around 2,700 athletes aged 14 to 17 competing in 25 disciplines across 153 medal events. Somalia is represented by 17-year-old boxer Hassan Anwar, who competes in the 50-kilogram category. Anwar, who began his boxing career in Mogadishu, expressed pride in participating, stating that the experience itself is a victory. His coach, Mohamed Ahmed Abdullahi, emphasized their goal of showcasing Somali athletes' potential on the international stage. Anwar's participation is seen as a vital opportunity for Somalia's youth to gain exposure and elevate the country's presence in global sports.
