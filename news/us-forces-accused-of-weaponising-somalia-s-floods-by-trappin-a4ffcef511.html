US forces accused of ‘weaponising’ Somalia’s floods by trapping women and children in the path of El Niño - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 05:30:03 GMT

US forces are facing accusations of "weaponising" floods in Somalia by conducting airstrikes that force civilians, particularly women and children, towards the flood-prone banks of the Juba River. As El Niño intensifies the rainy season, residents in areas like Jilib report constant airstrikes that have devastated their communities and left them with no safe refuge. Eyewitness accounts describe significant civilian casualties, with many bodies left in the streets. Critics argue that the US military strategy appears to exploit the flooding to weaken al-Shabaab, the militant group controlling much of the region. Although US officials deny targeting civilians or using weather as a weapon, the situation raises ethical concerns about the military's operations and the potential for large-scale humanitarian crises. With inadequate flood defenses and dwindling resources, the looming threat of severe flooding exacerbates the already dire conditions for those living in al-Shabaab territory, where hunger and displacement are rampant.
