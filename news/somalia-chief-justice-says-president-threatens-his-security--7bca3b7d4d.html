Somalia chief justice says president threatens his security after court ruling - Somali Guardian

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 16:40:20 GMT

Somalia's Chief Justice, Bashe Yusuf Ahmed, has expressed concerns for his safety following a court ruling that deemed President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term expired. During a press briefing, Bashe stated that he felt threatened by the president, particularly after his dismissal by Mohamud, which was later ruled unlawful by five Supreme Court judges. These judges also faced security threats after the ruling, leading them and Bashe to seek refuge at the home of former President Sharif Sheikh Ahmed. The situation escalated as troops took control of the Supreme Court building in Mogadishu, and the chief justice's security detail was withdrawn. Bashe lamented the insecurity in Mogadishu, questioning how ordinary citizens could feel safe when even high-ranking officials were at risk. Despite the turmoil, he expressed hope for a future where justice prevails and all individuals are treated equally under the law.
