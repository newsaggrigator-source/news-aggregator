Min. Fiqi Thanks EU Amb. Máthé for Security Support as Mogadishu Pushes for Faster International Cooperation - FTL Somalia

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 13:54:47 GMT

Somalia's Defence Minister, Ahmed Moallim Fiqi, recently met with the European Union’s Ambassador to Somalia, László Máthé, to discuss enhancing bilateral cooperation in defense and security. The meeting, held in Mogadishu, focused on the EU's support for Somalia's efforts to combat terrorism and strengthen the Somali National Army. Minister Fiqi expressed gratitude for the EU's ongoing assistance in developing security institutions and building military capacity. He emphasized the need for expedited international cooperation to bolster Somalia's security and stability. Ambassador Máthé reaffirmed the EU's commitment to supporting Somalia's security and development initiatives. This meeting is part of Máthé's broader introductory tour of Somali institutions, which includes engagements with various government branches. The EU remains a crucial security partner for Somalia, having recently approved significant funding for security missions. However, the EU's support is contingent on progress in governance and democratization, highlighting the need for Somalia to develop a self-sufficient security sector.
