Ethiopia has raised alarms regarding the increasing militarization of the Red Sea amid escalating conflicts and foreign rivalries in the Horn of Africa. The Ethiopian government expressed concerns that the growing military presence in the region could destabilize the already fragile security situation. This warning comes as various nations vie for influence in the Horn of Africa, a strategically significant area that connects the Middle East and Africa. Ethiopia's officials highlighted that the militarization poses risks not only to regional stability but also to international shipping routes that traverse the Red Sea. The situation is further complicated by ongoing tensions between neighboring countries and the involvement of external powers, which could exacerbate existing conflicts. Ethiopia's call for caution emphasizes the need for diplomatic solutions to prevent an arms race and promote peace in the region. As the geopolitical landscape evolves, the focus remains on maintaining security and fostering cooperation among Horn of Africa nations.
