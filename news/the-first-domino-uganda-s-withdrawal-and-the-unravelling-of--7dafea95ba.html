The First Domino: Uganda’s Withdrawal and the Unravelling of Somalia’s Security Architecture - FTL Somalia

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 18:24:56 GMT

Uganda has initiated the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia, as announced by Chief of Defence Forces General Muhoozi Kainerugaba. This decision comes shortly after Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud requested a delay in the withdrawal from key locations to allow Somali forces to prepare for security responsibilities. The withdrawal is driven by a funding crisis affecting the African Union's support for the mission, which has been exacerbated by the U.S. decision to halt logistical support. Uganda contributes approximately 4,500 troops under the African Union mission and maintains a separate bilateral force of around 2,000 personnel. The withdrawal raises concerns about the Somali National Army's readiness to fill the security gap, particularly in areas vulnerable to Al-Shabaab attacks. The situation underscores the fragility of Somalia's security architecture, as the international support that has sustained it for two decades begins to unravel, leaving the Somali government to navigate a challenging transition.
