Somali Ministers Call for Dialogue as Somaliland Elders Attend Mogadishu Event - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 19:57:31 GMT

Somali federal ministers have called for national unity and dialogue during a welcoming event for traditional elders from Somaliland in Mogadishu. Defence Minister Ahmed Moalim Fiqi emphasized the need to address historical grievances stemming from the military government era, arguing that the issues surrounding the 1960 union agreement should be discussed collectively. Information Minister Abdulfatah Kasim Mohamud reinforced this message, stating that separation cannot achieve what unity has failed to accomplish. Their remarks come amid heightened political sensitivity regarding Somaliland's status, especially following Israel's recognition of Somaliland as a sovereign state, which the federal government vehemently opposes. The ministers' statements aim to foster cross-regional cooperation in federal state-building, despite the leadership in Hargeisa rejecting unity. However, critics note that the government's call for dialogue lacks a concrete framework, and meaningful discussions must address power-sharing and recognition issues that have long defined the Somalia-Somaliland impasse. The ministers' appeals resonate with those inclined toward unity but may not sway separatists.
