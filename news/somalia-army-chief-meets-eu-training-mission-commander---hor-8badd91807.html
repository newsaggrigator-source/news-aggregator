Somalia army chief meets EU training mission commander - Horseed Media

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:50:00 GMT

Somalia's army chief, Major General Ibrahim Mohamed Mahmoud, recently met with Brigadier General Ivan Falasca, the commander of the European Union Training Mission in Somalia (EUTM-Somalia), to discuss military training and enhancing the capabilities of the Somali National Army (SNA). The meeting, held on October 23, 2026, focused on expanding cooperation in military training and improving the skills of SNA personnel. Both officials emphasized the importance of developing a professional and combat-ready national army that can effectively manage security responsibilities throughout Somalia. They also highlighted the need for ongoing support to bolster the operational capacity of the SNA. The EUTM-Somalia is part of the EU's broader initiative to assist Somalia in strengthening its security sector, which includes providing military training and advice to develop the country's security forces. This collaboration is crucial as Somalia seeks to enhance its national forces and take on greater responsibility for security operations across the nation.
