U.S. Senators Send Second Letter to Rubio Urging Action to Avert Widening Conflict in Ethiopia

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:17:43 GMT

A group of U.S. Democratic senators has sent a second letter to Secretary of State Marco Rubio, urging immediate diplomatic action to prevent escalating conflict in Ethiopia. The letter, led by Senator Jeanne Shaheen, highlights the risks of renewed fighting in Tigray and ongoing tensions with Eritrea, which could lead to a broader regional conflict. The senators express concern over the fragile peace established by the 2022 Pretoria Agreement, warning that recent hostilities have already resulted in significant casualties and humanitarian crises. They propose several measures, including facilitating U.S. citizen evacuations, securing a ceasefire, and suspending arms sales to Ethiopia. The senators also emphasize the need for civilian protection and increased humanitarian assistance amid ongoing violence and displacement. This renewed appeal follows a previous letter in September, reflecting growing congressional pressure for a more assertive U.S. response to the situation in Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa.
