Ethiopia's Governance Trajectory Shows 'Warning Signs' Despite Modest Gains: Ibrahim Index

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:21:31 GMT

Ethiopia's governance performance has shown modest improvement over the past decade, according to the 2026 Ibrahim Index of African Governance (IIAG). The country ranked 30th out of 54 African nations, with an overall governance score increase of 1.8 points from 2016 to 2025. However, the report highlights "Warning Signs" due to deteriorating indicators in security, rule of law, and human rights. Notably, the Security and Rule of Law category declined by 2.4 points, with significant drops in the Absence of Armed Conflict and media freedom. While there were gains in Accountability, Gender Equality, and Regional Integration, these were overshadowed by challenges in security and rights. The report emphasizes a fragile domestic situation amid Ethiopia's regional ambitions, particularly concerning its relationship with Eritrea. Overall, the findings present a mixed picture of governance, with improvements in some areas but significant setbacks in others, indicating a need for continued focus on security and rights.
