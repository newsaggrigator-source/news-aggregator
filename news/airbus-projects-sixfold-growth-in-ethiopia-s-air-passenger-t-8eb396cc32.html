Airbus Projects Sixfold Growth in Ethiopia's Air Passenger Traffic Over Next 20 Years

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 12:28:40 GMT

Airbus has projected a sixfold increase in Ethiopia's air passenger traffic over the next 20 years, highlighting Ethiopian Airlines' potential to establish Addis Ababa as a major aviation hub in Africa. Key factors contributing to this growth include the airline's expanding network, rising demand, and planned airport infrastructure, particularly the new airport project in Bishoftu, which aims to accommodate up to 110 million passengers annually. Airbus Vice President Antonio Da Costa emphasized the airline's strong position to leverage this growth, noting that Ethiopian Airlines has been a significant customer for Airbus, including being the first African operator of the A350. The anticipated growth in Ethiopian air traffic aligns with Airbus's forecast of a tripling of air traffic across Africa, enhancing the continent's connectivity and capacity.
