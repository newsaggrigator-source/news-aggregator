Tens of Thousands Flee Into Kenya As Clashes Erupt in Ethiopia's Moyale

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 04:50:51 GMT

Tens of thousands of people have fled into Kenya from Ethiopia's Moyale due to violent clashes linked to a long-standing dispute between the Oromia and Somali regions. Approximately 50,000 individuals have crossed the border, facing severe shortages of food, water, and shelter. Many displaced families are staying with local relatives, putting additional strain on already limited resources. Others have sought refuge in schools or temporary sites, with some sleeping outdoors. The violence escalated after a Somali flag was raised in Moyale, leading to gunfire between local militias and resulting in multiple fatalities, including a 13-year-old girl. The Kenyan government, in collaboration with the UN refugee agency (UNHCR) and other humanitarian organizations like the Kenya Red Cross, is working to register new arrivals and assess their needs. As assessments continue, the number of displaced individuals may rise further.
