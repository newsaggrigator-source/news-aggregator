#Daily_Brief: Netanyahu map depicts #Ethiopia, #Sudan’s RSF as ‘friendly’ to #Israel, #UAE - Addis Standard

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:11:21 GMT

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has released a map indicating that Ethiopia and Sudan's Rapid Support Forces (RSF) are considered "friendly" to Israel and the United Arab Emirates (UAE). This development highlights a shift in regional alliances and reflects Israel's growing diplomatic engagement with African nations. The map aims to strengthen ties with these countries amid ongoing geopolitical changes in the region. Netanyahu's government is actively seeking to expand Israel's influence in Africa, which has been a focus of Israeli foreign policy in recent years. The implications of this map could affect Israel's relationships with neighboring countries and its strategic interests in Africa.
