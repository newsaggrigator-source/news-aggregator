'A disgrace to our country': Trump says Omar could go to jail or be deported - KVII

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 23:57:59 GMT

President Donald Trump stated that Congresswoman Ilhan Omar could potentially face jail time or deportation as federal authorities investigate her immigration records. During an Oval Office event, Trump referred to Omar as "a disgrace to our country" and accused her of harboring anti-American sentiments. He also criticized Somalia, her country of birth, calling it a "real s***house." The investigation reportedly focuses on whether Omar or her late father misstated her age on immigration documents, which could impact her citizenship status. Omar, who arrived in the U.S. as a child after living in a Kenyan refugee camp, has denied any wrongdoing, with her attorneys asserting that there is no legitimate basis for the investigation and demanding the return of property seized during a recent search of her home. No criminal charges have been filed against her.
