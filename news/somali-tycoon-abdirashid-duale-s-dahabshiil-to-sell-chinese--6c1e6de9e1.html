Somali tycoon Abdirashid Duale's Dahabshiil to sell Chinese carmaker Chery's cars in Somalia - Billionaires.Africa

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 12:29:39 GMT

Somali tycoon Abdirashid Duale's Dahabshiil Group has entered into a partnership with Chinese carmaker Chery to become an authorized dealer of Chery vehicles in Somalia. The agreement, signed in Wuhu, China, allows Dahabshiil Motors to sell Chery cars, enhancing its existing portfolio that includes brands like Toyota, Hyundai, and BYD. The deal, which includes warranty coverage for buyers, aims to improve access to Chery vehicles for Somali consumers and strengthen commercial ties between Somalia and China. The Somali Embassy in China highlighted the potential for further investment, suggesting that Chery consider establishing a vehicle manufacturing plant in Somalia to create jobs and develop local skills. Dahabshiil Motors, established in 2019, has been expanding its automotive offerings and services in the region.
