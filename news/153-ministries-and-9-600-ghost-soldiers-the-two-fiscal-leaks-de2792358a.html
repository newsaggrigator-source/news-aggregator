153 Ministries and 9,600 Ghost Soldiers: The Two Fiscal Leaks Draining Somalia’s Budget - FTL Somalia

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 08:27:39 GMT

Somalia's budget is significantly impacted by two major fiscal leaks: an overabundance of ministries and the presence of approximately 9,600 "ghost soldiers" in the Somali National Army (SNA). A World Bank report highlights that the government operates with around 153 ministries, which strains limited resources and hampers effective service delivery. Concurrently, the SNA payroll includes nearly 47,438 personnel, yet only about 42,000 are verified as active, leaving a substantial gap of unverified soldiers. This inefficiency leads to fiscal leakage and undermines operational readiness. Although the government has initiated biometric verification and a national force registry, the gap persists. The report suggests that consolidating ministries could yield significant savings, allowing for better funding in critical areas like education and infrastructure. However, political will to implement these reforms remains lacking, posing risks as Somalia's security forces are expected to take on greater responsibilities amid ongoing transitions.
