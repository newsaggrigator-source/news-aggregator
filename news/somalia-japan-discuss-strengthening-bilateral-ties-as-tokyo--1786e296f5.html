Somalia, Japan Discuss Strengthening Bilateral Ties as Tokyo’s Envoy Visits Mogadishu - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 12:05:20 GMT

Somalia's Minister of Foreign Affairs, Abdisalam Abdi Ali, met with Japan's Ambassador to Kenya, Matsuura Hiroshi, in Mogadishu to discuss enhancing bilateral relations. The meeting aimed to strengthen the long-standing friendship between the two nations and explore collaboration in areas such as peace, stability, and development. Japan has been actively involved in Somalia, funding a police training program and supporting the Somali Police Force with equipment and training for female officers. Economically, Japan has contributed significantly to Somalia's blue economy, launching a $2 million project to revitalize the fisheries sector, focusing on sustainable practices and job creation. Additionally, Japan has supported women's empowerment through the LEAP project, which has provided training and livelihood opportunities to over 440 women. The relationship has also included debt relief agreements, enhancing Somalia's diplomatic presence in East Asia. Japan's approach emphasizes multilateral cooperation, ensuring impactful contributions while minimizing direct involvement, although sustainability remains a challenge.
