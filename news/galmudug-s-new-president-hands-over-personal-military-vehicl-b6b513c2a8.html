Galmudug’s New President Hands Over Personal Military Vehicle to Regional Security Ministry - FTL Somalia

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 19:18:47 GMT

Galmudug's new President, Liibaan Ahmed Hassan, has made a significant gesture by handing over a personal military vehicle to the Ministry of Security during his inauguration ceremony in Dhusamareb, attended by federal President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. This act symbolizes a commitment to state resources over personal power, particularly in a region facing security challenges from Al-Shabaab and clan disputes. Liibaan, elected by the Galmudug House of Representatives on October 22, aims to prioritize security and has pledged to intensify operations against Al-Shabaab. The federal government's involvement in his election and the inauguration highlights its investment in Galmudug's stability. However, experts caution that while the vehicle handover is a positive symbol, it does not address the structural security issues the region faces, such as a porous border and ongoing threats. The success of Liibaan's presidency will depend on substantial support in funding, training, and coordination for the Ministry of Security.
