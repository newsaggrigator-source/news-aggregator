Germany Cautions 'Any Escalation in the Horn of Africa Must Be Avoided'

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 04:11:51 GMT

Germany's Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul has emphasized the need to avoid any escalation in the Horn of Africa, particularly as tensions rise between Ethiopia, Eritrea, and Sudan. In a recent conversation with Ethiopian Foreign Minister Gedion Timothewos, Wadephul highlighted the importance of a stable Ethiopia for regional peace and reiterated Germany's commitment to supporting stability in northern Ethiopia. This warning follows renewed fighting in northern Ethiopia and troop movements near the Eritrean border, alongside Ethiopia's aspirations for sovereign access to the Red Sea. Diplomatic relations between Ethiopia and Sudan have also soured, with both nations expelling diplomats amid accusations of supporting armed opposition groups. The African Union has stepped up mediation efforts, while the U.S. and EU have called for de-escalation. Concerns are mounting that the overlapping conflicts and regional rivalries could lead to a broader confrontation, despite Sudanese officials expressing a desire to avoid open war.
