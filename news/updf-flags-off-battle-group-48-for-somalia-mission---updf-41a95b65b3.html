UPDF Flags Off Battle Group 48 For Somalia Mission - UPDF

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 17:11:41 GMT

The Uganda Peoples' Defence Forces (UPDF) has officially deployed Battle Group 48 (BG XLVIII) for the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) after completing their pre-deployment training at the International Peace Support Operations Training Centre in Singo, Nakaseke District. During the flag-off ceremony, Deputy Commander Land Force, Maj Gen Felix Busizoori, emphasized the importance of teamwork, discipline, and integrity among the troops, reminding them that their behavior reflects on the UPDF. He also highlighted the need for positive relations with local communities and warned against the misuse of social media, which could jeopardize operational security. Col Moses Nabaasa, Commandant of IPSO-TC, reinforced the values of discipline and patriotism, urging the troops to operate as a cohesive unit throughout their mission. The ceremony was attended by both senior and junior officers, marking a significant step in Uganda's commitment to peacekeeping efforts in the region.
