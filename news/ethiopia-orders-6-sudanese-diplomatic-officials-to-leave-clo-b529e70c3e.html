Ethiopia orders 6 Sudanese diplomatic officials to leave, closes consulate: Reports - Anadolu Ajansı

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 13:14:53 GMT

Ethiopia has ordered six Sudanese diplomatic officials to leave the country within 48 hours and has closed Sudan's consulate in Assosa, a city in the Benishangul-Gumuz region. This decision follows Sudan's allegations that drone attacks, reportedly launched from Ethiopian territory, have killed hundreds of civilians. Sudan's Foreign Ministry has accused Ethiopia of facilitating operations by the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF), which has been in conflict with Sudan's army since April 2023. Sudan claims that between February and October 2023, 70 drone attacks originating from Ethiopia resulted in over 400 civilian deaths. In response, Sudan has asserted its right to defend its sovereignty. The diplomatic tensions reflect a broader escalation in relations between the two countries, with each accusing the other of supporting armed groups involved in their respective internal conflicts. Ethiopian authorities have not publicly clarified their reasons for the expulsion of the Sudanese officials.
