We need six points: Bjorkegren demands focus as Black Stars prepare for Somalia Qualifiers - Ghana Football Association

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 20:06:02 GMT

Ghana's Black Stars head coach, Kim Lars Bjorkegren, emphasized the need for the team to secure six points in their upcoming double-header against Somalia for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. Speaking in Takoradi, he acknowledged the importance of respecting their opponents while maintaining confidence in their own abilities. Bjorkegren, who is in his first camp as head coach, highlighted the return of key players such as Kudus, Partey, and Jordan as crucial for the team's performance. He noted that the players must feel free to express themselves on the pitch and work together to achieve victory. The coach also expressed optimism about the support from the Takoradi crowd, which he believes will enhance the team's performance. Overall, Bjorkegren's message was clear: focus on winning the first match to set the tone for the qualifiers.
