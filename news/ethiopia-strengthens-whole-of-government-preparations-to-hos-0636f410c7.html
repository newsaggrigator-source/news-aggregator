Ethiopia Strengthens Whole-of-Government Preparations to Host COP32

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 05:25:43 GMT

Ethiopia is intensifying its preparations to host COP32, the global climate summit scheduled for 2027, as Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed convenes the COP32 National Steering Committee. The committee's focus is on reviewing progress and ensuring a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to the event. Prime Minister Ahmed emphasized the importance of collaboration among various task forces to facilitate effective preparations and successful execution of the summit. He highlighted that hosting COP32 presents Ethiopia with a strategic opportunity to promote its climate and environmental priorities, enhance international partnerships, attract investments, and achieve sustainable development outcomes. The summit is expected to serve as a significant platform for Ethiopia to engage in global climate action while showcasing its development initiatives and commitment to sustainable growth. This event is seen as a pivotal moment for the country to advance its environmental agenda on the international stage.
