Drone Strikes Kill Couple Fleeing Clinic, Father With Four Children, in Tigray's Medebay Terer

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 04:40:47 GMT

Two recent drone strikes in Medebay Terer, Tigray, have resulted in the deaths of seven civilians, including a couple fleeing a clinic and a father with four children. The first strike occurred on October 9, targeting a clinic where a husband and wife had taken their daughter in labor. As they attempted to escape the initial strike, they were killed by a subsequent attack. The second strike on October 21 hit a household, killing a father and his four children, including an eight-year-old girl, while the mother survived as she was outside. These incidents are part of a troubling trend of increased drone strikes in Tigray amid renewed conflict, raising serious concerns about civilian safety and humanitarian access. Reports indicate that these strikes have also targeted educational facilities and humanitarian operations, prompting calls for accountability and investigations into potential war crimes. The situation remains dire for civilians in the region, with ongoing displacement and insecurity.
