The Bone-Setters' Ward - How Ancient Healers Are Filling Ethiopia's Modern Healthcare Vacuum

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 04:50:51 GMT

In Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, traditional bone-setters like W/ro Birqe Yibre are increasingly sought after as modern healthcare remains inaccessible for many. Birqe, a Wogesha with over 20 years of experience, treats patients based on their ability to pay, often providing free care to those in need. A study indicated that nearly half of the surveyed individuals in the Mecha district had utilized traditional bone-setting services, primarily due to social acceptance and personal trust. However, the risks associated with delaying hospital care for serious injuries are significant. While Birqe refers complex cases to hospitals, some practitioners may mishandle severe injuries, leading to complications like malunion or permanent damage. Dr. Biruk Ferede, an orthopaedic specialist, emphasizes the need for public education on injury assessment and the importance of timely medical intervention. The ongoing reliance on traditional healers highlights the challenges in Ethiopia's healthcare system, necessitating a balance between traditional practices and formal medical care. (159 words)
