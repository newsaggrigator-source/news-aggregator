Somalia approves bill to establish legal framework for digital transactions - Horseed Media

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:45:00 GMT

Somalia's House of the People has approved a Digital Transactions Bill aimed at creating a legal framework for digital payments and electronic transactions, as announced by the Ministry of Communications and Technology. On October 21, 2026, 137 MPs voted in favor of the bill during a plenary session in Mogadishu. The legislation, developed in collaboration with various institutions, seeks to align Somalia’s regulatory framework with technological advancements and the increasing reliance on digital services. It encompasses a wide range of digital transactions, including electronic payment systems and digital currencies. The ministry emphasized that establishing clear regulations would bolster economic growth and enhance public trust in digital financial services. Communications and Technology Minister Mohamed Adam Moalim expressed gratitude to the MPs and the parliamentary committee for their efforts in advancing the legislation. However, the ministry did not disclose specific provisions of the bill or details regarding its implementation, leaving questions about regulatory oversight and further approvals necessary for it to become law.
