Trump says Ilhan Omar ‘could go to jail’ amid federal probe, calls Somalia a ‘s***house’ - شبكة تواصل الإخبارية

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 02:29:03 GMT

Former President Donald Trump has intensified his criticism of Congresswoman Ilhan Omar, suggesting she "could go to jail" amid a federal investigation into alleged immigration document irregularities. This follows a search conducted by Homeland Security Investigations and Immigration and Customs Enforcement at her Washington, DC residence, where DNA evidence was reportedly collected. Omar has criticized the investigation as politically motivated and an act of intimidation. Trump also made derogatory remarks about Somalia, Omar's country of birth, calling it a "s***house" and questioning her legitimacy as a congresswoman. The controversy has heightened speculation about Omar's public absence and the nature of the federal inquiry, while her family has been seen at her residence during this time.
