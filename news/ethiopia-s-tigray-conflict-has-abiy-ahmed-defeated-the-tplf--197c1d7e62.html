Ethiopia's Tigray conflict: Has Abiy Ahmed defeated the TPLF? - BBC

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 00:20:24 GMT

Ethiopia's Tigray conflict has seen a resurgence of violence, raising questions about the defeat of the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) by Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's government. Following a brief war that began in late September, federal forces quickly took control of Tigray's capital, Mekelle, with TPLF forces claiming a "strategic withdrawal." Despite government assertions that the conflict is over, reports indicate ongoing fighting in various regions of Tigray, including heavy artillery use. Analysts suggest the TPLF has lost popular support and military capability, but TPLF officials maintain they are regrouping for a counteroffensive. The situation remains tense, with many residents expressing fear and uncertainty about the future. While some experts predict a prolonged conflict, others believe the TPLF's diminished strength will prevent a large-scale resurgence. The need for political solutions to address Tigray's grievances is emphasized, as ordinary citizens yearn for peace and stability.
