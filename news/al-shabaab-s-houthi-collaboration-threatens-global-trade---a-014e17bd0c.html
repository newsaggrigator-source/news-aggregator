Al-Shabaab’s Houthi Collaboration Threatens Global Trade - Africa Defense Forum

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 15:39:14 GMT

Recent maritime attacks by Yemen's Houthi rebels, backed by Iran, are posing significant threats to global trade and reviving Somali piracy. Following the Houthis' control of Yemen's Port of Mokha, they gained strategic dominance over the Bab al-Mandeb Strait, a crucial passage for approximately 12% of the world's trade. This shift has compelled shipping routes to divert around the Horn of Africa, increasing vulnerability to piracy, particularly in Somalia, where the Houthis are reportedly supplying the al-Shabaab group with weapons and training. The resurgence of piracy has been marked by violent incidents, including the recent hijacking of the tanker Honour 25, which resulted in casualties. Analysts warn that the Houthis' strengthened position could escalate tensions in the Persian Gulf and further disrupt maritime traffic, leading to increased insurance costs that will ultimately affect consumers, particularly in African economies already struggling with geopolitical challenges.
