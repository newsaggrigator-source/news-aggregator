Somalia offers condolences to Egypt after fuel tanker fire kills 31 in Giza - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 05:08:37 GMT

The Somali federal government has expressed its condolences to Egypt following a tragic fuel tanker fire that resulted in the deaths of at least 31 people and injuries to 21 others in Giza. The incident occurred on November 25 when a petroleum truck overturned and ignited on Cairo's Ring Road, causing flames to engulf nearby vehicles. Somalia's statement conveyed deep sympathy to Egypt's leadership and the families affected by the disaster, emphasizing solidarity during this difficult time. Emergency services responded with 39 ambulances, transporting the injured to local hospitals. Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sissi also extended condolences and directed medical assistance for the injured. An investigation has been launched to determine the cause of the accident, with preliminary assessments indicating that around 27 vehicles were damaged in the fire. Somalia's condolences were echoed by other regional organizations, including the Organization of Islamic Cooperation and the Gulf Cooperation Council.
