Somalia Faces Mounting Security and Political Pressures as Foreign Troops Prepare to Withdraw - Garowe Online

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 20:36:46 GMT

As foreign troops prepare to withdraw from Somalia's Lower Shabelle region, the federal government faces escalating security and political challenges. The African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), particularly Ugandan forces, is set to reduce its presence, raising concerns about a security vacuum that could lead to renewed instability. This troop withdrawal coincides with a political dispute between President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's administration and opposition groups over constitutional changes and the electoral process ahead of the 2027 elections. Tensions are further exacerbated by instability in South West State, where control of strategic towns remains contested. Diplomatic warnings highlight the potential consequences of these overlapping crises, as the government struggles to maintain order and navigate political divisions. The situation is critical, with the international community closely monitoring developments in the lead-up to the sensitive electoral period.
