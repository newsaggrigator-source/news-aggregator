Ethiopia as a Focal Point Between the Quad and Quintet Coalition on Sudan - horn review

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 16:27:09 GMT

Ethiopia has emerged as a crucial mediator in the ongoing conflict in Sudan, particularly as tensions escalate between the Quad (the United States, United Kingdom, Japan, and India) and the Quintet (which includes the African Union, the Arab League, the United Nations, and the European Union). The Ethiopian government is leveraging its geographical and political position to facilitate dialogue and peace efforts among the conflicting parties. As violence continues to disrupt Sudan, Ethiopia's role is increasingly recognized as vital for regional stability. The Quad and Quintet coalitions are seeking to coordinate their approaches to address the humanitarian crisis and support peace initiatives. Ethiopia's involvement is seen as essential in bridging the gap between international interests and local realities, aiming to foster a collaborative environment for conflict resolution. The situation remains fluid, with Ethiopia's diplomatic efforts being closely monitored by both coalitions as they strive for a sustainable solution to the turmoil in Sudan. (152 words)
