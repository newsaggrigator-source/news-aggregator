#Video_News :-United Bank for Africa shelved Ethiopia banking expansion over Nigeria’s 10% foreign investment cap watch - Facebook

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 17:03:39 GMT

United Bank for Africa (UBA) has decided to postpone its banking expansion plans in Ethiopia due to concerns regarding Nigeria's 10% cap on foreign investments. The bank's management indicated that the regulatory environment in Nigeria poses challenges for its international growth strategy, particularly in expanding its operations in Ethiopia. This decision reflects the broader implications of Nigeria's investment restrictions, which can hinder the ability of financial institutions to diversify and tap into emerging markets. UBA's focus on maintaining compliance with local regulations has led to a reevaluation of its expansion timeline. The bank's leadership remains committed to exploring opportunities in Ethiopia but must navigate the complexities of investment policies in Nigeria before proceeding. This situation highlights the impact of national regulations on the strategic decisions of banks operating in multiple countries.
