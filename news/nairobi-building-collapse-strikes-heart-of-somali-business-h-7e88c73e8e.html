Nairobi Building Collapse Strikes Heart of Somali Business Hub in Eastleigh - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 18:12:29 GMT

A multi-storey building under construction collapsed in Nairobi's Eastleigh neighborhood, a crucial commercial hub for the Somali community, on October 23, 2026. The incident occurred around 1:00 p.m., leading to ongoing rescue operations as officials confirmed lives were lost and businesses destroyed. The building, developed by 9th Street Plaza Limited and constructed by Stromax Holdings E.A Limited, lacked an occupation certificate, raising serious concerns about regulatory failures. Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja acknowledged that the building's illegal occupancy contributed to the tragedy. Eastleigh is vital for Somali traders, who have invested over $1.5 billion in Nairobi's economy over the past two decades. The collapse not only represents a human tragedy but also threatens the economic ecosystem connecting Somali businesses across East Africa. Witnesses reported seeing cracks before the collapse, highlighting a pattern of negligence in construction safety. The incident underscores the vulnerability of Somali migrants in Kenya, who often operate in the informal economy with limited protections.
