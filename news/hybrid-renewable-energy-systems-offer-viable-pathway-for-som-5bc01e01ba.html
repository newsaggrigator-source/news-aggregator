Hybrid Renewable Energy Systems Offer Viable Pathway for Somalia Power Grid - Bioengineer.org

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 03:01:32 GMT

A recent feasibility analysis highlights the potential of hybrid renewable energy systems, combining solar photovoltaics and wind power, as a sustainable alternative to Somalia's reliance on diesel generators for electricity. Currently, Somalia faces low electrification rates and high electricity costs, largely due to dependence on imported fossil fuels. The study, which reviewed literature and case studies from 2010 to 2025, emphasizes the country's abundant solar and wind resources, suggesting that integrating these renewable sources could meet the growing power demand driven by economic growth and population increase. The research outlines the technical and economic frameworks necessary for deploying hybrid systems, including optimal sizing for solar and wind components, energy storage solutions, and the use of simulation software for performance evaluation. Despite the clear benefits, the study identifies structural barriers to large-scale implementation and proposes a strategic roadmap to facilitate clean energy adoption, emphasizing local engagement and workforce development to ensure sustainable energy solutions for Somalia.
