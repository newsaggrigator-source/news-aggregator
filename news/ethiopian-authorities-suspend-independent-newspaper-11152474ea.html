Ethiopian Authorities Suspend Independent Newspaper

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 05:04:23 GMT

Ethiopian authorities have indefinitely suspended the Reporter, one of the country's oldest independent newspapers, citing violations of national laws and media ethics. The Ethiopian Media Authority announced the suspension on November 2, claiming the outlet published content detrimental to "national interests," which they argued negatively impacts foreign investment, tourism, and diplomacy. This action reflects a broader trend of government repression against media and civil society, with numerous outlets banned and journalists facing arbitrary arrests and intimidation since 2025. The Reporter, which publishes in English and Amharic, is the last privately owned Amharic newspaper in Ethiopia. Prior to its suspension, the outlet warned of a precarious situation for the private press in the country and called for urgent reforms. Human Rights Watch has urged the government to lift the ban and end its attacks on media freedom, emphasizing the need for a more open and democratic environment for journalism in Ethiopia.
