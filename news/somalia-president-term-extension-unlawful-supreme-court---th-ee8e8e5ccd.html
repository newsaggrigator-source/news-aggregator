Somalia president term extension 'unlawful': Supreme Court - The Standard

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 13:54:17 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court has declared the extension of President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term by one year as "unlawful." This ruling follows significant unrest and opposition from rival factions who view the extension as a power grab amid ongoing efforts to transition to a fully democratic electoral system. Mohamud's term officially ended in May, but his government initiated a "transition period" to prepare for nationwide elections scheduled for April and May 2027. The Supreme Court stated that recent constitutional amendments, which the president claimed justified the extension, did not apply to his current term. In response to the ruling, Mohamud dismissed the chief justice, Bashe Yusuf Ahmed, and appointed a replacement, a move the court deemed an attempt to obstruct justice. The political turmoil has exacerbated Somalia's security crisis, particularly with the ongoing threat from the Al-Shabaab insurgency, leading to reduced international support for military operations in the country.
