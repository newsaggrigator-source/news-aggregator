Somalia, Türkiye discuss security cooperation and counterterrorism efforts - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:15:41 GMT

Somalia and Türkiye held discussions in Mogadishu to enhance their military and security cooperation, focusing on counterterrorism efforts against al-Shabab. Türkiye’s ambassador to Somalia, Ferhat Alkan, met with Somalia’s National Security Adviser, Awes Hagi Yusuf Ahmed, to address the current security landscape and the importance of ongoing collaboration between the two nations. The Turkish Embassy reaffirmed Türkiye's commitment to supporting Somalia's peace and stability, highlighting its historical role in military training and logistical assistance. The talks also included discussions with Somali Defense Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi on strengthening defense ties and building the capacity of the Somali National Armed Forces. Türkiye operates the TURKSOM military training facility in Mogadishu, which has trained thousands of Somali personnel. The Somali government views Türkiye as a strategic partner in bolstering national security and reducing dependence on foreign military forces. These discussions come amid Somalia's ongoing military operations against al-Shabab and shifts in international security support. (157 words)
