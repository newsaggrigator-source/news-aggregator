Somalia is on the brink of a humanitarian catastrophe: drought, civil war and displacement - la Repubblica

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 19:48:52 GMT

Somalia is facing a severe humanitarian crisis, driven by a combination of recurrent droughts, ongoing civil conflict, and widespread displacement. According to the Somalia Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan 2026, over 4.8 million people urgently require assistance, including food, clean water, hygiene supplies, and medical care. The situation is exacerbated by significant cuts in international funding, leading to the closure of numerous healthcare and nutrition centers. Currently, 5.1 million people are experiencing acute food insecurity, with projections indicating this could rise to 5.4 million by year-end. The country has been embroiled in civil war for over 40 years, resulting in the erosion of state institutions and the rise of tribal factions and militias, including Al-Shabaab. With more than half the population living below the poverty line, urgent intervention is needed to address the escalating crisis and provide essential support to those affected.
