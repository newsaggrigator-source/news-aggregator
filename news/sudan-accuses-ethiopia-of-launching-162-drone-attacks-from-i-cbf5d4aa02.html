Sudan Accuses Ethiopia of Launching 162 Drone Attacks From Its Territory

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 04:42:57 GMT

Sudan has accused Ethiopia of launching 162 drone attacks from its territory, resulting in over 400 civilian and 102 military deaths. These claims, presented by Sudanese military and intelligence officials, allege that the attacks targeted various locations, including El Damazin and El Obeid, and destroyed numerous schools, health centers, and fuel stations. Sudanese officials assert that the drones were launched from bases in Ethiopia and included various models, such as Chinese-made drones and Turkish Bayraktar Akinci and TB2. Sudan's Foreign Affairs Undersecretary, Muawiya Osman Khalid, emphasized the need for international intervention to halt what he termed aggression against Sudan's sovereignty. Additionally, Sudan's Defense Minister claimed that over 20 countries are providing support to the Rapid Support Forces (RSF), complicating the conflict further. Ethiopia has denied these allegations, labeling them as unfounded. The ongoing attacks are said to hinder the return of displaced people and threaten regional stability. (159 words)
