Ethiopian Arrivals Fleeing Moyale Conflict Into Northern Kenya Rise to Nearly 50,000 - 'Situation Remains Fluid': UN

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 05:03:10 GMT

Nearly 50,000 people have fled from Ethiopia into northern Kenya due to escalating violence in Moyale, according to the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The conflict, which erupted over a dispute regarding the Somali Regional State flag and the killing of a young boy, has led to significant civilian casualties and property destruction, prompting mass displacement. Many of the arrivals are being sheltered by local communities, while others are in temporary sites. The IOM warns that the influx could overwhelm local resources, as the immediate needs include water, healthcare, and basic supplies. The Kenyan government and humanitarian partners are preparing to assist up to 80,000 people, anticipating further arrivals. The situation remains fluid, with ongoing assessments and emergency planning in collaboration with various agencies to address the needs of both the displaced individuals and host communities.
