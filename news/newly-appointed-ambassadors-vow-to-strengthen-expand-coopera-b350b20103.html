Newly Appointed Ambassadors Vow to Strengthen, Expand Cooperation With Ethiopia

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 05:32:26 GMT

Newly appointed ambassadors to Ethiopia have pledged to enhance cooperation across various sectors following a ceremony where President Taye Atske Selassie received their letters of credence. The ambassadors emphasized their commitment to strengthening bilateral and multilateral ties in areas such as trade, investment, peace, security, and cultural exchange. Norwegian Ambassador Yngvild Berggrav highlighted Ethiopia's strategic importance, while Finnish Ambassador Erik Lundberg and Maltese Ambassador Bernard Hamilton expressed gratitude for their reception and a desire to elevate relations. South Korean Ambassador Koo Hong-seok aimed to attract more South Korean businesses to Ethiopia, particularly in agriculture and healthcare, while honoring Ethiopia's historical contributions during the Korean War. Barbados Ambassador William Alexander, marking his country's first diplomatic presence in Ethiopia, emphasized the cultural significance of Ethiopia and announced ongoing healthcare collaborations. He also voiced support for Ethiopia's bid to host the upcoming UN Climate Change Conference, underscoring a commitment to climate advocacy. (158 words)
