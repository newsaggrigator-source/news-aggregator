Police reservist injured as Kenyan security officers clash with Ethiopian militia - The Standard

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 21:00:00 GMT

Tensions have escalated along the Kenya-Ethiopia border following violent clashes between Kenyan security forces and the Ethiopian Nyangatom militia. The conflict erupted when over 300 armed militia crossed into Kenya, blocking local herders from accessing a crucial watering point in Kibish, Turkana North. A police reservist was seriously injured during the ensuing gunfire, which lasted several hours. Turkana County Police Commander John Tarus confirmed the reservist is in stable condition. The armed group has reportedly taken control of key access routes and ambushed vehicles, prompting a multi-agency security response. Concerns are growing that these confrontations could lead to a broader conflict, as the Nyangatom community threatens to seize control of Kibish and surrounding areas. Special Envoy for Peace John Munyes has called for urgent intervention, warning that ongoing disputes over resources could undermine years of peace and stability in the region. The situation remains precarious, with calls for enhanced security measures and diplomatic efforts to de-escalate tensions.
