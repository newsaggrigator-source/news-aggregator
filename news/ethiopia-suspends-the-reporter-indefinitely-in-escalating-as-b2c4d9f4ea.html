Ethiopia Suspends the Reporter Indefinitely in Escalating Assault On Independent Media

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 04:19:52 GMT

Ethiopia's regulatory body, the Ethiopian Media Authority (EMA), has indefinitely suspended The Reporter, one of the country's oldest independent newspapers, citing repeated violations of national interests and journalistic ethics. The EMA accused the publication of disseminating unverified and distorted information that could harm Ethiopia's image and threaten national security. This suspension follows a year of monitoring the outlet's content and discussions with its management, culminating in a final warning before the suspension was enacted. The Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) condemned the EMA's actions, labeling them as censorship and an attack on independent journalism. The Reporter had recently published an editorial advocating for press freedom and criticizing the government's treatment of independent media as a national security threat. This suspension adds to a troubling trend in Ethiopia, where several media outlets and journalists have faced similar punitive measures in recent years.
