Ethiopia, UK Discuss COP32 Preparations and Climate Cooperation

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 05:20:14 GMT

Ethiopia and the United Kingdom have engaged in discussions regarding preparations for the 32nd UN Climate Change Conference (COP32), emphasizing climate cooperation and Ethiopia's priorities ahead of the 2027 summit. These talks occurred during COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, where Ethiopian Foreign Affairs Minister Gedion Timothewos and Finance Minister Fitsum Assefa met with UK Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband. Gedion highlighted Ethiopia's climate action initiatives and the necessity of reducing greenhouse gas emissions to combat climate change. Miliband expressed the UK's commitment to supporting Ethiopia's goals and preparations for COP32, reinforcing the importance of international collaboration. This engagement reflects Ethiopia's strategy to forge global partnerships and garner support as it prepares to host COP32, aiming to enhance global climate action and elevate the concerns of developing nations. The discussions signify a proactive approach to addressing climate challenges and fostering cooperation between Ethiopia and the UK. (160 words)
