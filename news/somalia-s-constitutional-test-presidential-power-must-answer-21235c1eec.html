Somalia’s Constitutional Test: Presidential power must answer to law - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 21:23:16 GMT

Somalia is facing a critical constitutional challenge as President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's administration confronts the judiciary over the limits of presidential power. On November 7, 2026, Mohamud attended the swearing-in of Ibrahim Idle Suleiman as Chief Justice, emphasizing the importance of judicial independence. However, this appointment follows a Supreme Court ruling that declared the mandates of federal institutions expired, questioning the legality of extending terms for incumbents. The court mandated political stakeholders to establish an electoral framework within 30 days. The situation escalated when Mohamud dismissed the previous Chief Justice, a move the Supreme Court deemed unconstitutional, raising concerns about executive interference. The article stresses the need for transparency and adherence to constitutional processes in judicial appointments and electoral reforms. It warns that failing to respect constitutional limits could set a dangerous precedent, undermining the rule of law and stability in Somalia. International partners are urged to support judicial independence and a credible electoral process. (158 words)
