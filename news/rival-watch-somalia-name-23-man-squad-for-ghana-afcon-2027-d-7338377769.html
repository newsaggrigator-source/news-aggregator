Rival Watch: Somalia name 23-man squad for Ghana AFCON 2027 double-header - Pulse Ghana

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 11:50:31 GMT

Somalia's head coach, Yusuf Ali Nur, has announced a 23-man squad for the upcoming Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) 2027 qualifying matches against Ghana, scheduled for November 12 and 16. Both teams are currently winless in Group C, with Somalia sitting third and Ghana fourth due to goal difference. Somalia has lost its first two matches, suffering a 2-1 defeat to The Gambia and a 2-0 loss to Côte d'Ivoire. The squad includes three goalkeepers, eight defenders, six midfielders, and six forwards. Notably, Somalia will play its home match at the Essipong Sports Stadium in Sekondi, as it lacks a CAF-approved venue. The matches are crucial for both teams as they aim to secure their first points in the qualifying campaign, with the top two teams from the group advancing to the finals, co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.
