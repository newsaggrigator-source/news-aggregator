Trump: Ilhan Omar could be deported, slams Somalia as a ‘s—house’ - Al Jazeera

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 20:37:39 GMT

Former President Donald Trump suggested that Congresswoman Ilhan Omar could face deportation, criticizing her for her Somali heritage. During a rally in North Carolina, Trump referred to Somalia as a "s—house" and implied that Omar's background disqualified her from serving in Congress. His comments sparked backlash, with critics accusing him of promoting xenophobia and racism. Omar, a Somali-American and one of the first Muslim women elected to Congress, has previously faced similar attacks from Trump and other political figures. The remarks have reignited discussions about immigration, race, and the treatment of minority lawmakers in the U.S. political landscape.
