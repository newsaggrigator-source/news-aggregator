PM Abiy Says Newly Acquired Defense Systems Boost Ethiopia's Strategic Deterrence

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 05:07:16 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has announced that the country's recent acquisition of advanced military equipment, including 12 Su-35 fighter jets and four S-400 air defense system batteries, will significantly enhance Ethiopia's defense capabilities. This development follows discussions with Russian President Vladimir Putin and marks a pivotal moment in Ethiopia's military modernization efforts. The Su-35 jets are noted for their advanced technology, capable of both air-to-air and air-to-ground combat, and equipped with sophisticated radar systems for detecting various threats. Prime Minister Abiy emphasized the importance of preparedness in maintaining national security and asserted that these upgrades position Ethiopia as a leader in military capability within Sub-Saharan Africa. He expressed gratitude to President Putin for his support and highlighted the ongoing training of personnel to ensure effective use of the new systems. Abiy concluded by stressing that continuous capacity building is essential for a resilient nation. (157 words)
