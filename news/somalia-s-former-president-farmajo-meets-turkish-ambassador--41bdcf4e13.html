Somalia's Former President Farmajo Meets Turkish Ambassador in Mogadishu - Dawan Africa

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 17:52:20 GMT

On November 9, 2026, former Somali President Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed Farmajo met with Türkiye's Ambassador to Somalia, Ferhat Alkan, in Mogadishu. The meeting was part of Alkan's courtesy visits to various Somali leaders, both past and present. They discussed the historical and current state of relations between Türkiye and Somalia, as well as recent developments within Somalia. The two officials exchanged views on regional and global issues affecting the geopolitical landscape. The Turkish Embassy in Mogadishu highlighted the strengthening ties between the two nations, noting Türkiye's involvement in significant projects in Somalia, including oil exploration and the construction of a satellite and rocket launch facility. Farmajo, who served as president from 2017 to 2022, oversaw a period marked by enhanced cooperation between Türkiye and Somalia. This meeting underscores the ongoing diplomatic engagement and collaboration between the two countries.
