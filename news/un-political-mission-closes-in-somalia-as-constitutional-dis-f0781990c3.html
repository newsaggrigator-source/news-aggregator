UN Political Mission Closes in Somalia as Constitutional Dispute Remains Unresolved - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 10:08:32 GMT

The United Nations has officially closed its political mission in Somalia, concluding over three decades of involvement in mediating political disputes. This withdrawal comes amid ongoing tensions between the Somali government and opposition regarding constitutional amendments and the timing of upcoming elections. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's administration claims that recent amendments allow for an extension of his and parliament's terms until 2027, a stance contested by opposition leaders. The UN Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) ended its operations after a two-year transition period aimed at transferring responsibilities to Somali institutions. While the UN's political mediation role has ceased, its humanitarian and development agencies will remain active in the country. The Somali government had requested the mission's closure, citing interference in domestic matters. Currently, there is no established framework for resolving the constitutional dispute, and major opposition groups have yet to respond to invitations for dialogue from the president.
