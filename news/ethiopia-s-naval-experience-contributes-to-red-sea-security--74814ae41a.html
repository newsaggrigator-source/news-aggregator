Ethiopia’s Naval Experience Contributes to Red Sea Security: Aregawi Berhe - ENA English

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 14:45:21 GMT

Ethiopia's naval experience is playing a significant role in enhancing security in the Red Sea, according to Aregawi Berhe, the Director-General of the Ethiopian Maritime Affairs Authority. He emphasized that Ethiopia's strategic position and its commitment to maritime security are crucial for regional stability. The country has been actively involved in collaborative efforts with neighboring nations to address security challenges in the Red Sea, including piracy and illegal fishing. Aregawi highlighted the importance of building a capable navy and fostering partnerships to ensure safe navigation and protect maritime resources. Ethiopia's engagement in maritime security reflects its broader goals of contributing to peace and stability in the region.
