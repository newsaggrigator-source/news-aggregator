Ethiopia: Over 52 Killed in Benishangul Gumuz Region Attack - Borkena

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 19:15:27 GMT

In a tragic incident on Thursday morning, over 52 individuals were killed in the Buldiglo district of Ethiopia's Benishangul-Gumuz region. Among the victims, 21 were civilians, while the rest were local security forces. The attack occurred in Daleti kebele around 5 a.m. and has been attributed to the Oromo Liberation Army (OLA), a militant group designated as a terrorist organization by the Ethiopian government. Regional police commissioner Endris Mohammed reported that the assailants fled the scene shortly after the attack. This incident is part of a troubling pattern of violence linked to the OLA, which has been responsible for similar attacks across various regions, including Oromia and Amhara. Following the assault, the Ethiopian Defense Force and regional security forces were deployed to restore order, and discussions with local residents were held to facilitate their return home. The OLA has not yet commented on this latest attack.
