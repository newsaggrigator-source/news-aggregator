Mohamud Accuses UAE of Funding Division and Building Parallel Forces in Televised Address - FTL Somalia

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 18:51:46 GMT

In a recent televised address, Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accused the United Arab Emirates (UAE) of undermining Somalia's sovereignty by funding political divisions and establishing parallel security forces. He claimed that the UAE has signed port agreements with regional administrations in Somaliland, Puntland, and Jubaland without federal approval, which he argues threatens Somalia's constitutional order. Mohamud highlighted the contentious Berbera port deal, alleging that the UAE has developed military facilities there for strategic use by foreign powers. He also linked the UAE to Israel's recognition of Somaliland, criticizing its lack of condemnation for this decision. Despite annulling all agreements with the UAE in January 2026, Mohamud warned that the UAE's actions could lead Somalia down a path of conflict similar to that seen in Libya and Yemen. While he expressed a willingness for a respectful relationship, he emphasized that Somalia would not accept any agreements that compromise its statehood.
