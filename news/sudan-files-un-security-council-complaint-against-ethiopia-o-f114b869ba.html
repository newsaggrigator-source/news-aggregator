Sudan files UN Security Council complaint against Ethiopia over RSF drone attacks - The Peninsula Qatar

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:00:36 GMT

Sudan has lodged formal complaints with the UN Security Council and the African Union, accusing Ethiopia of permitting the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) to conduct drone strikes against Sudanese civilian targets from Ethiopian territory. This accusation follows ongoing conflict between Sudan's military and the RSF, which has escalated since April 2023. Sudan's military claims that Ethiopia's actions constitute "blatant aggression" and violate international law. In its report, Sudan's army-led Transitional Sovereignty Council documented 70 drone attacks this year, resulting in nearly 500 civilian casualties and targeting critical infrastructure such as hospitals and power stations. The use of drones has increased significantly, with over 1,100 deaths reported from drone strikes in Sudan during the first half of the year. Ethiopia has denied these allegations and countered by accusing Sudan of supporting rebels linked to the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF). The situation remains tense as both nations continue to exchange accusations.
