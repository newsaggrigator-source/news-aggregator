Death of Three, Injuries of 20 Kenyans Attributed to Ethiopia's Moyale Conflict

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 10:32:20 GMT

The ongoing conflict in Ethiopia's Moyale has resulted in the deaths of three Kenyans and injuries to at least 20 others. A Kenyan woman, Amina Guchi, was killed by a suspected stray bullet while speaking with a security official on November 2. This incident is linked to clashes in Moyale, which have escalated since October 22, following a dispute over administrative control between the Oromia and Somali regional states. The violence has led to significant civilian casualties, damage to property, and a humanitarian crisis, with over 50,000 Ethiopians reportedly fleeing to Kenya. The conflict has disrupted education, forcing the relocation of students taking the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education exams. Residents have reported hearing gunfire, and authorities have dealt with stray bullets and unexploded ordnance in the area. The situation remains tense, raising security and humanitarian concerns for both countries.
