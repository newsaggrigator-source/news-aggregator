Brussels Meeting Signals Coordination Push Before AUSSOM Mandate Decision - FTL Somalia

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 21:47:33 GMT

On November 19, 2026, a Senior Officials’ Meeting was held in Brussels, focusing on Somalia's security transition ahead of the United Nations Security Council's decision on renewing the AUSSOM mandate. Co-chaired by Olof Skoog from the European External Action Service and Somali Chief of Staff Abdihakim Mohamed Yusuf, the meeting included representatives from the UN, AUSSOM, and troop-contributing nations like Djibouti, Ethiopia, Kenya, and Uganda. The EU reaffirmed its commitment to Somalia, highlighting its financial support for AU missions, totaling nearly €2.8 billion over two decades. Participants emphasized the need for close coordination and an inclusive political settlement to ensure a sustainable security architecture. However, the meeting lacked specific details on the Somali-owned security transition, funding, and benchmarks for AUSSOM's future. This ambiguity raises concerns about the effectiveness of the transition, especially given Somalia's internal political divisions and the competing interests of international partners involved in the security process.
