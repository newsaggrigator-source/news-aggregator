Renewed Somali Piracy Raises Concerns - Africa Defense Forum

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 17:41:45 GMT

Recent reports indicate a resurgence of Somali piracy, particularly in the Gulf of Aden, with incidents increasing since April 2026. Although overall piracy rates are at their lowest in over three decades, the recent uptick highlights ongoing threats. The most significant incident involved the hijacking of the tanker Honour 25, resulting in casualties during a rescue attempt, marking the first pirate-related killings in over a decade. Contributing factors include the influence of Houthi rebels and illegal fishing, which has devastated local fishermen's livelihoods. As foreign trawlers deplete fish stocks, some locals have turned to piracy, exacerbating community tensions. In response, local authorities have restricted fishing activities, further straining food security. International efforts to combat piracy continue, with proposals for a new counter-piracy task force and ongoing operations by the European Naval Force. Analysts emphasize the importance of adhering to Best Management Practices to protect seafarers and vital trade routes.
