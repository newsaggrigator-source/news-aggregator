Egypt, Somalia, Djibouti reaffirm Red Sea states’ exclusive role in securing the waterway - Hiiraan Online

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 20:22:06 GMT

Egypt, Somalia, and Djibouti have reaffirmed their exclusive responsibility for the security and governance of the Red Sea during recent meetings held on the sidelines of the CEN-SAD session in Tripoli, Libya. Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty met separately with Somali Foreign Minister Abdisalam Abdi Ali and Djiboutian Foreign Minister Abdoulkader Hussein Omar to discuss regional stability and cooperation. Abdelatty emphasized Egypt's commitment to supporting Somalia's state-building efforts and maintaining its sovereignty and territorial integrity. The discussions also focused on enhancing bilateral relations, particularly in economic and investment sectors, and included topics such as energy, logistics, and maritime connectivity. Both ministers acknowledged the importance of strengthening national institutions and preserving regional unity amidst ongoing political and security challenges in the Horn of Africa, particularly in Sudan and Somalia. They agreed to continue coordination efforts to promote peace, stability, and development in the region.
