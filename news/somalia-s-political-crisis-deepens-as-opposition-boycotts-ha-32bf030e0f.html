Somalia's Political Crisis Deepens as Opposition Boycotts Hassan Sheikh's November 14 Talks Amid International Pressure - Sunatimes.com

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 16:04:00 GMT

Somalia's political crisis has escalated as key opposition leaders have boycotted President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's invitation to national consultations set for November 14. The opposition, particularly the Somali Future Council, argues that the President lacks constitutional legitimacy due to a disputed mandate and a controversial Supreme Court ruling issued on November 5, which deemed recent amendments extending presidential terms unlawful. This ruling has intensified the debate over the President's authority, with opposition leaders calling for internationally mediated negotiations involving major global powers. Additionally, UN experts have condemned the alleged unconstitutional dismissal of the Supreme Court Chief Justice, raising concerns about judicial independence. Amidst these tensions, President Hassan Sheikh is reportedly planning a visit to Kampala, Uganda, on November 17, although details remain unconfirmed. The ongoing conflict over constitutional legitimacy poses significant risks to Somalia's political stability, electoral preparations, and overall governance as the country faces pressing security and economic challenges.
