Somalia opens fourth national cybersecurity forum as digital security efforts expand - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 22:28:53 GMT

Somalia has launched its fourth National Cyber Security Forum in Mogadishu, aimed at enhancing the country's digital security infrastructure. Organized by the National Communications Authority (NCA) and Mogadishu University, the two-day forum focuses on cybersecurity governance, protecting digital assets, and improving national incident response capabilities. The theme, “Building Trust and Cyber Resilience Through Security by Design,” emphasizes the importance of integrating security measures into digital systems from the outset. Mogadishu University President Ibrahim Mohamed Mursal stressed the collaborative nature of cybersecurity, involving government, academia, and the private sector. Key achievements highlighted include the establishment of the Somali Computer Incident Response Team (SOMCIRT) and a cybersecurity certification program for government officials. Communications and Technology Minister Mohamed Adan Mo’allin announced plans for a National Cybersecurity Academy, aiming to produce practical outcomes for the Somali populace. This forum continues the dialogue on cyber threats and policy development, following previous editions that have fostered collaboration across sectors.
