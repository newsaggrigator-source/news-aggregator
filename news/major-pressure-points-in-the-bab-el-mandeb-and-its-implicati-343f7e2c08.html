Major Pressure Points in the Bab el-Mandeb and Its Implications for Ethiopian Trade Security - horn review

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 13:55:48 GMT

The Bab el-Mandeb Strait, a crucial maritime chokepoint between Yemen and Djibouti, is increasingly becoming a focal point of geopolitical tensions that significantly impact Ethiopian trade security. As Ethiopia relies heavily on this route for its imports and exports, any disruption in the strait could have dire consequences for its economy. The ongoing conflict in Yemen, coupled with the presence of various military forces in the region, raises concerns over the safety of shipping lanes. Additionally, the rise of piracy and regional instability further complicates the situation. Ethiopia's government is urged to enhance its maritime security measures and diversify trade routes to mitigate risks. The implications of these pressures extend beyond trade, potentially affecting regional stability and international shipping dynamics. As the situation evolves, stakeholders must closely monitor developments in the Bab el-Mandeb to safeguard Ethiopia's economic interests and ensure the security of maritime operations in this vital corridor.
