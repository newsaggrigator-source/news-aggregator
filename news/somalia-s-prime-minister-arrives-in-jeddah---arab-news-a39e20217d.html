Somalia’s prime minister arrives in Jeddah - Arab News

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 19:31:32 GMT

Somalia’s Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre arrived in Jeddah on October 24, 2026, where he was welcomed at King Abdulaziz International Airport by Jeddah Governor Prince Saud bin Abdullah bin Jalawi and other officials. This visit underscores the ongoing efforts to strengthen bilateral relations between Somalia and Saudi Arabia across various sectors. The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia has reiterated its support for Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity, emphasizing its commitment to rejecting any threats to Somalia’s security and stability. Both nations are collaborating to enhance the security of international waterways and ensure freedom of navigation, which is crucial for regional stability. This visit is part of a broader initiative to foster cooperation and mutual support between the two countries, reflecting their shared interests in maintaining peace and security in the region. The discussions during this visit are expected to pave the way for further diplomatic and economic engagements in the future.
