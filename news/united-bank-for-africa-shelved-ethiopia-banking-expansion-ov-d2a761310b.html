United Bank for Africa Shelved Ethiopia Banking Expansion Over Nigeria's 10% Foreign Investment Cap

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 05:15:25 GMT

United Bank for Africa (UBA) has halted its plans to expand into Ethiopia due to Nigeria's central bank imposing a 10% cap on foreign investments by banks. Tony Elumelu, UBA's chairman, expressed disappointment at this regulatory limitation during a book launch on November 21, 2026. He noted that the bank aimed to be the first international bank in Ethiopia, especially after the country began opening its banking sector to foreign entities. However, the Central Bank of Nigeria's policy restricts investments in foreign subsidiaries, which also affected UBA's potential expansion into Angola. Elumelu emphasized the importance of consistent regulatory practices to build investor confidence, highlighting that predictable regulations attract capital to Nigeria. Despite Ethiopia's recent legislative changes allowing foreign banks to operate, challenges such as ownership ceilings and capital requirements remain barriers to entry. Elumelu praised Nigeria's broader economic reforms under President Bola Tinubu, which he believes are essential for fostering a stable investment environment.
