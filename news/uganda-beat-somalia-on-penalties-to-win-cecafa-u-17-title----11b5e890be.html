Uganda beat Somalia on penalties to win CECAFA U-17 title - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 20:26:43 GMT

Uganda triumphed over Somalia in a penalty shootout, winning 6-5 after a goalless draw in regulation time to claim the CECAFA U-17 title in Kigali, Rwanda. Uganda's goalkeeper, David Ssegirinya, played a pivotal role, making crucial saves during the match and stopping three penalties in the shootout. Both teams had already qualified for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations U-17 tournament by reaching the final. Somalia had opportunities to score, with notable efforts from Abdullahi Hashi and others, while Uganda threatened with a shot that hit the crossbar. Uganda's coach Kingston Laryea expressed pride in his team's hard work, and captain Henry Muhoozi celebrated the victory. Somalia's coach Dadir Amin Ali commended his players for their efforts in reaching the final. This victory marks Uganda's third CECAFA U-17 title in recent years.
