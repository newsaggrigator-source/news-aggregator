NSA seeks full CAF approval for Essipong Stadium ahead of Ghana-Somalia fixture - 3News

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 11:42:39 GMT

The National Sports Authority (NSA) is seeking full approval from the Confederation of African Football (CAF) for the Essipong Stadium in Sekondi-Takoradi ahead of the upcoming international fixture between Ghana and Somalia. The stadium, which has undergone renovations, is being evaluated to ensure it meets CAF's standards for hosting international matches. The NSA is working diligently to address any outstanding issues to secure the necessary certification. This approval is crucial for the match scheduled to take place as part of the qualifiers for the Africa Cup of Nations. The NSA is optimistic about meeting the requirements in time for the fixture.
