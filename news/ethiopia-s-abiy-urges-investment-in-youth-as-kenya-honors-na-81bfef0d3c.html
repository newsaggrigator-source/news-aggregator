Ethiopia's Abiy Urges Investment in Youth As Kenya Honors National Heroes

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 10:36:50 GMT

During the Mashujaa Day celebrations in Eldoret, Kenya, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed emphasized the importance of investing in youth for the country's future development. He stated that Kenya's progress hinges on transforming freedom into economic opportunities for the younger generation. Abiy highlighted the need for job creation, strengthening public institutions, and equipping young people to contribute to national development. He broadened the definition of heroism to include various professions, urging young Kenyans to take an active role in building their nation. His remarks come at a time when addressing youth unemployment and fostering entrepreneurship are critical policy priorities in Kenya. The annual Mashujaa Day honors individuals who have significantly contributed to Kenya's independence and development.
