Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud Escalates UAE Tensions as Political and Corruption Crises Mount at Home - Garowe Online

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 19:25:00 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has intensified tensions with the United Arab Emirates (UAE) amid escalating political and corruption crises at home. In a recent speech, Mohamud accused the UAE of meddling in Somalia's internal affairs, funding political divisions, and making unauthorized agreements regarding key ports. Following these accusations, Somalia's cabinet ordered the UAE ambassador to leave within 72 hours and recalled its own ambassador from Abu Dhabi. The UAE condemned Somalia's actions as hostile and damaging to bilateral relations. This diplomatic rift coincides with a political crisis stemming from a controversial constitutional amendment that extended the president's term and postponed elections, leading to widespread opposition. Additionally, allegations of corruption and nepotism plague Mohamud's administration, with Somalia ranking among the most corrupt nations globally. As the political landscape becomes increasingly volatile, the legitimacy of Mohamud's presidency is under scrutiny, with calls for electoral reforms and accountability growing louder. (159 words)
