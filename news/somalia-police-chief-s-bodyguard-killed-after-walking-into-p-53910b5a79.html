Somalia police chief's bodyguard killed after walking into plane's spinning propeller | To watch a video from the scene of the incident | Inshorts - Inshorts

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 12:46:44 GMT

A tragic incident occurred at Dhusamareb Airport in Somalia, where Mukhtar Qasim Samay, a bodyguard for Mogadishu Police Chief Ma'alin Mahdi, was killed after walking into a spinning aircraft propeller. The accident happened on Tuesday as Samay had just disembarked from the plane. Reports indicate that the propeller struck him in the head, leading to his death. Chief Mahdi and his team had arrived at the airport to attend the inauguration of Galmudug's leadership, scheduled for Thursday. This unfortunate event highlights the dangers associated with airport operations and the need for heightened safety measures around aircraft. The incident has drawn attention to the risks faced by security personnel in high-stakes environments.
