Somalia’s top court rules president’s term extension is unlawful, deepening power struggle - Toronto Star

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 14:16:25 GMT

Somalia's Supreme Court has ruled that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term extension is unlawful, declaring that his term has expired and ordering elections within 90 days. This decision intensifies the ongoing power struggle in the country, particularly following Mohamud's dismissal of the chief justice just a day prior. The court rejected the president's claim that constitutional amendments allowed him to remain in office until 2027, stating that the parliamentary decision to extend terms did not apply retroactively. Political leaders have been given 30 days to agree on election procedures, while the government is restricted to routine administration. The ruling comes amid heightened tensions, with armed clashes reported in Mogadishu and the recent closure of the UN political mission, which had facilitated negotiations among rival factions. Analysts express skepticism about the ruling's enforcement, given the existing electoral disputes and the challenges posed by the al-Shabab militant group.
