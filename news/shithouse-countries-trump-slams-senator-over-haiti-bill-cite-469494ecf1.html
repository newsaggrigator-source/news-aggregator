"Shithouse Countries": Trump Slams Senator Over Haiti Bill, Cites Immigration from Libya, Somalia - Open Magazine

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 10:48:34 GMT

At a campaign rally in Perry, Georgia, former President Donald Trump criticized Democratic Senator Jon Ossoff for supporting a bill that would grant temporary protected status to Haitian migrants. Trump revived his controversial "shithole countries" remarks, labeling Haiti, Libya, Yemen, and Somalia as "terror-infested countries." He made derogatory claims about Somali immigrants in the U.S., alleging that 98% do not work and benefit from theft, while asserting they live better than Americans. Trump's comments echo his past statements from 2018, where he expressed a preference for immigrants from countries like Norway over those from Africa. The legislation, S. 4814, was introduced by Senator Ed Markey and aims to protect Haitian migrants until January 2029. Trump's rhetoric has reignited discussions on immigration as he campaigns for Republican candidates ahead of the upcoming midterm elections.
