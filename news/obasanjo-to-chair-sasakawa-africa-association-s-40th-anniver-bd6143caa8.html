Obasanjo to Chair Sasakawa Africa Association's 40th Anniversary Symposium in Addis Ababa

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 12:41:55 GMT

Former Nigerian President Olusegun Obasanjo will chair the Sasakawa Africa Association's (SAA) 40th anniversary symposium on December 1-2, 2026, in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. The event aims to gather African leaders, policymakers, agricultural experts, and development partners to discuss food and nutrition security across the continent. Under the theme "Reimagining Africa's Food and Nutrition Security," the symposium will reflect on four decades of agricultural transformation and explore innovative strategies to enhance agrifood systems. Obasanjo, a proponent of agricultural development, emphasizes the critical role of food security in national stability. The symposium will feature discussions on agricultural extension, climate resilience, and inclusive value chains, alongside a commemorative exhibition showcasing SAA's achievements. Established in 1986, SAA has supported agricultural development in 17 African countries, focusing on improving the livelihoods of smallholder farmers. The gathering will also review SAA's 2026-2030 Strategic Plan, aiming to build sustainable agrifood systems and enhance access to nutritious food. (159 words)
