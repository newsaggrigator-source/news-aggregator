Ministry of Health Participates in Community Health Forum On Sidelines of CPHIA 2026 in Addis Ababa

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 18:11:03 GMT

The Ethiopian Ministry of Health participated in the Community Health Forum during the Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026) in Addis Ababa. The forum focused on enhancing community health systems and primary healthcare services, emphasizing the vital role of community health workers, particularly in maternal and child healthcare. The Ministry highlighted the need for Sudan to benefit from the upcoming REACH Africa initiative and called for technical support to develop a national framework for community health workers. Key discussions included training, capacity-building, and the importance of sustainable funding and resources for community health workers. The delegation also stressed the need for effective monitoring and evaluation systems and the integration of digital tools to improve health data management. This engagement underscores the Ministry's commitment to strengthening the national community health system, improving health service delivery, and addressing the health needs of communities, especially in challenging humanitarian contexts.
