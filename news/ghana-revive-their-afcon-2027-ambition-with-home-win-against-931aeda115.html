Ghana Revive Their AFCON 2027 Ambition with Home Win Against Somalia - THISDAYLIVE

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 01:02:30 GMT

Ghana's national football team revitalized their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifying campaign with a 2-0 victory over Somalia in Sekondi-Takoradi. This win comes after the Black Stars faced setbacks, losing their first two Group C matches against Ivory Coast and The Gambia, which led to the departure of coach Carlos Queiroz. Interim coach Kim Bjorkegren took charge for this crucial double-header. Despite an early penalty miss by Jordan Ayew, Ghana took the lead just before halftime when Jonas Adjetey scored from a corner. Felix Afena-Gyan secured the win with a goal shortly after the hour mark. This victory places Ghana three points behind group leaders Ivory Coast and The Gambia, who are set to face each other later. The win is vital for Ghana, who risk missing a second consecutive AFCON finals if they do not improve their performance in the upcoming matches.
