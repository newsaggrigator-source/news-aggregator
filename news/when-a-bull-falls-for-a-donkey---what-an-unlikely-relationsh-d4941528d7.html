When a Bull Falls for a Donkey - What an Unlikely Relationship Reveals About Animal Sexuality

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 04:46:32 GMT

In Ethiopia, a bull named Achille has formed an unexpected sexual relationship with a male donkey named Patroclo, raising intriguing questions about animal sexuality. Observations revealed that the two animals engaged in affectionate behaviors, including licking and rubbing, alongside repeated penetrative sexual acts. This relationship mirrors the bond of Achilles and Patroclus from Greek mythology, prompting researchers to explore why these two males of different species would choose each other over available females. The study highlights that same-sex sexual behavior is common in over 1,500 animal species and may serve social functions, such as strengthening bonds or managing conflicts. The researchers argue that understanding such relationships challenges traditional views on animal sexuality and encourages a broader perspective on the evolutionary significance of sexual behavior, suggesting that it may not always be directly linked to reproduction but could instead enhance social connections and survival.
