Nearly 700,000 Displaced By Moyale Conflict in Southern Ethiopia: Islamic Relief

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 05:18:09 GMT

Nearly 700,000 people have been displaced due to ongoing conflict in Moyale, Ethiopia, along the borders with Kenya and Somalia, according to Islamic Relief Ethiopia. The humanitarian organization reported that approximately 329,000 individuals were displaced from the Dawa Zone in the Somali Region, while another 359,798 fled from southern Oromia, totaling around 688,800 displaced persons. The violence, which began on October 22 between Somali and Oromo communities, has escalated, affecting areas up to 300 kilometers from Moyale. Despite mediation efforts, fighting resumed on November 16, leading to further destruction of homes and essential services. The conflict has also impacted cross-border trade and pastoralist livelihoods. Islamic Relief is preparing to provide assistance, including cash support and hygiene kits, while the European Union has allocated €550,000 in emergency aid for displaced individuals. The situation remains precarious, with the potential for renewed clashes and a deepening humanitarian crisis.
