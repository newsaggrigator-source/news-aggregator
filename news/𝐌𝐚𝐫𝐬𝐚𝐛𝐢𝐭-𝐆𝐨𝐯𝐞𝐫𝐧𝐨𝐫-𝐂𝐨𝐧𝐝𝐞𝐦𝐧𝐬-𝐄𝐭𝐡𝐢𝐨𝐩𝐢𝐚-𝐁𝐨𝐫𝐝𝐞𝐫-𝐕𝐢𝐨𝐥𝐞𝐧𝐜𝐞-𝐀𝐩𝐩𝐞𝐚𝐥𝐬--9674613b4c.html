Marsabit Governor Mohamud Ali has strongly condemned the escalating violence along the Ethiopia-Kenya border, urging for an immediate ceasefire and peaceful dialogue to resolve the conflict. He expressed deep concern over the humanitarian crisis resulting from the violence, which has led to significant displacement and suffering among affected communities. Governor Ali emphasized the need for increased humanitarian assistance to support those impacted by the conflict, highlighting the urgency of addressing the dire conditions faced by displaced individuals. He called upon both the Ethiopian government and international organizations to prioritize peace efforts and provide necessary aid to alleviate the suffering of those caught in the crossfire. The governor's appeal underscores the importance of regional stability and cooperation in addressing the challenges posed by border violence and its humanitarian implications. His statements reflect a commitment to fostering peace and ensuring the well-being of vulnerable populations in the region.
