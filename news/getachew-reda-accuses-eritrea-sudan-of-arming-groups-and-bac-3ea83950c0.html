Getachew Reda Accuses Eritrea, Sudan of Arming Groups and Backing Drone Attacks in Ethiopia - https://kulu-media.com/

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 19:14:35 GMT

Getachew Reda, an adviser to Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, has accused Eritrean President Isaias Afwerki and Sudanese military leader General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan of arming groups within Ethiopia and facilitating drone attacks against the Ethiopian government. In a Facebook post on November 11, 2026, Reda discussed these allegations during a meeting with UN Special Envoy for the Horn of Africa, Guang Cong, focusing on regional security challenges. He claimed that Eritrea and Sudan's involvement in supporting armed groups is destabilizing and must cease for meaningful dialogue to occur. Despite the serious nature of these accusations, Reda emphasized the importance of diplomacy over military action, stating that discussions are preferable to conflict. The allegations, which have not been independently verified, come amid rising tensions in the Horn of Africa, particularly concerning the ongoing conflict in Sudan and Ethiopia's strained relations with its neighbors.
