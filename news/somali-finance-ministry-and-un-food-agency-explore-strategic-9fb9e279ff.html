Somali Finance Ministry and UN Food Agency Explore Strategic Investments for Sustainable Growth - FTL Somalia

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:23:47 GMT

On November 18, 2026, Somalia's Minister of Finance, Bihi Iman Egeh, met with Etienne Peterschmitt, the FAO Representative in Somalia, to discuss strategic investments aimed at fostering sustainable economic growth. The meeting focused on aligning priority development programs in agriculture, food security, and rural resilience, emphasizing the need for long-term investments over short-term emergency relief. This dialogue is part of ongoing cooperation between the Somali government and the UN food agency, which includes initiatives like the EmergenSys project for digital modernization in agriculture. However, significant challenges remain, including concerns over public financial management and insecurity in key farming areas, which hinder the effective execution of investments. The Somali government aims to attract concessional capital and support for agricultural reforms, but without improved fiscal accountability and rural security, the potential for meaningful development may be limited.
