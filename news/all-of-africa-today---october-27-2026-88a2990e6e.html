All of Africa Today - October 27, 2026

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 07:13:34 GMT

On October 27, 2026, significant events across Africa were reported. In Ethiopia's Oromia region, over 60 individuals were killed in attacks targeting Christian communities, with the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church labeling the violence a massacre. Local officials attributed the assaults to a newly formed rebel coalition, the Alliance for Survival, which includes the Oromo Liberation Army (OLA). Meanwhile, Algeria's parliament approved a troop deployment to Niger to combat armed groups linked to al-Qaeda and ISIS, marking Algeria's first military action abroad since 1973. In Uganda, a report indicated that 3.6 million people face acute food insecurity, with projections rising to 4.4 million by mid-2027, driven by climate shocks and economic challenges. Lastly, Amnesty International warned that the Central African Republic's Special Criminal Court may close due to funding shortages, jeopardizing justice for war crimes. These developments highlight ongoing security, humanitarian, and governance challenges across the continent.
