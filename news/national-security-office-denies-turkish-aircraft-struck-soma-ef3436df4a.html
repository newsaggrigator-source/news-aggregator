National Security Office Denies Turkish Aircraft Struck Somali Troops, But Questions Remain - FTL Somalia

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 15:16:56 GMT

The Office of National Security in Somalia has denied allegations that Turkish aircraft, specifically F-16s or drones, struck Somali National Army (SNA) positions near Leego on October 26, 2026. The office labeled these claims as “false and misleading,” emphasizing the importance of relying on verified information. This denial comes amidst conflicting reports regarding a significant battle where the Somali Defence Ministry claimed to have killed 75 Al-Shabaab militants, while some soldiers reported much heavier casualties, including 40 dead and over 100 wounded SNA troops. The government’s statement did not clarify the discrepancies in casualty figures or the role of Turkish aircraft during the engagement. Critics argue that the government must provide a transparent account of the events rather than merely denying claims. The situation highlights ongoing concerns about the Somali government’s communication and accountability regarding military operations and partnerships, particularly with Turkey. The public remains skeptical without further clarification.
