Ethiopia Elevates Sea Access to Existential Security Issue at Tana Forum - Birr Metrics

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 17:45:17 GMT

Ethiopia's Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed emphasized the critical importance of maritime access for the country's national security and economic sovereignty during the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, held on October 24, 2026. He argued that Ethiopia's reliance on foreign ports, particularly through Djibouti, poses significant strategic and economic challenges, as over 90% of its international trade passes through this corridor. Abiy highlighted the need for Ethiopia to diversify its trade routes and reduce dependency on Djibouti, which is vulnerable to disruptions affecting logistics and supply chains. The Prime Minister's remarks align with broader discussions among African leaders about economic independence and regional integration. Ethiopia's pursuit of maritime access has also led to diplomatic tensions, notably with Somalia, following a memorandum of understanding with Somaliland. The Tana Forum serves as a platform for discussing these issues, focusing on economic sovereignty and geopolitical transformation in Africa, with participation from various leaders and policymakers across the continent.
