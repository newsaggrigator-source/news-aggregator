Mogadishu Hosts Somalia–Uganda Joint Commission Meeting for First Time - Dawan Africa

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:28:17 GMT

Mogadishu is hosting the first Somalia–Uganda Joint Permanent Commission (JPC) meeting on October 21, 2026, with over 100 Ugandan delegates and 146 Somali representatives in attendance. The meeting aims to review the implementation of previous agreements and explore avenues for enhanced economic cooperation. Key topics include security, defense, labor, health, energy, and tourism. A business forum is also part of the agenda, showcasing investment opportunities and featuring presentations from various organizations, including Uganda Airlines. Delegates are expected to endorse outcomes and sign the Mogadishu Declaration on bilateral investment and trade cooperation on October 22. This meeting follows the inaugural session held in Kampala in October 2025 and highlights the ongoing collaboration between the two nations, both members of the East African Community.
