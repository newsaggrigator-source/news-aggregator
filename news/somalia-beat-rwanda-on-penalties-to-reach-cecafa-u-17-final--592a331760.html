Somalia beat Rwanda on penalties to reach CECAFA U-17 final - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 23:32:36 GMT

Somalia triumphed over Rwanda in a tense penalty shootout to secure a place in the CECAFA U-17 final. The match ended in a draw after regulation and extra time, leading to penalties where Somalia emerged victorious. This win marks a significant achievement for the Somali U-17 team, showcasing their resilience and skill in a competitive tournament. The final will see Somalia compete for the championship title, further highlighting the progress of youth football in the region.
