Somalia warns that Houthis are training Al-Shabaab members to use drones - صوت الإمارات

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 08:51:07 GMT

The Somali federal government has raised alarms about the growing military cooperation between Yemen's Houthi group and the Al-Shabaab militant organization. Officials warn that this collaboration could enhance Al-Shabaab's combat capabilities, particularly in drone technology, posing a significant threat to both civilians and government forces in Somalia. Ali Mohamed Omar, a Minister of State in Somalia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, acknowledged the ideological differences between the two groups but highlighted their technical cooperation. Reports indicate that the Houthis are providing training to Al-Shabaab members in drone operations and explosive device manufacturing. This relationship has evolved from arms trafficking to a more organized military partnership, raising concerns about regional security. A report from the Seldg research institute suggests that the cooperation now includes direct political, media, and military dimensions, further complicating the security landscape in Somalia. The situation underscores the ongoing risks posed by illicit arms smuggling routes from Yemen to Somalia.
