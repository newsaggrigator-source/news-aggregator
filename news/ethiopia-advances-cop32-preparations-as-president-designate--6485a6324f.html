Ethiopia Advances COP32 Preparations As President-Designate Arrives in T&#xFC;rkiye

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 05:17:30 GMT

Ethiopia is making significant strides in its preparations for COP32, with President-Designate Gedion Timothewos arriving in Antalya, Türkiye, to participate in COP31. His engagement with the COP31 Presidency, participating nations, and international partners aims to facilitate a seamless transition to COP32, scheduled for November 2027 in Addis Ababa. Timothewos is focused on fostering trust and consensus among stakeholders to ensure that COP32 is inclusive and implementation-oriented. These discussions are crucial for strengthening international cooperation and advancing Ethiopia's readiness to host the global climate conference. The country is poised to welcome delegates from around the world, emphasizing its commitment to addressing climate change and promoting collaborative efforts in environmental sustainability. As Ethiopia prepares for this pivotal event, it underscores its role in the global climate dialogue and its dedication to fostering meaningful outcomes at the upcoming summit.
