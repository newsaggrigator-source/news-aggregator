Ethiopia, France Vow to Deepen Military and Strategic Cooperation

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 12:26:52 GMT

Ethiopia and France have committed to enhancing their military and strategic cooperation, focusing on military capacity building, knowledge transfer, and regional stability. This agreement was reached during discussions between Ethiopian Defense Minister Mohammed Idris and French Ambassador Alexis Lamek in Addis Ababa. The talks aimed to strengthen the historical ties between the two nations and expand their collaboration in defense and military affairs. Minister Mohammed expressed gratitude for France's support in improving Ethiopia's military capabilities and called for assistance in safeguarding the country's interests in the region. He also urged France to support the ongoing Disarmament, Demobilization, and Reintegration (DDR) process in the Tigray Region. Ambassador Lamek reaffirmed France's commitment to fostering relations with Ethiopia and enhancing joint efforts to promote peace and stability in the region. This renewed commitment reflects both countries' desire to translate their diplomatic relations into practical cooperation in defense and security matters. (160 words)
