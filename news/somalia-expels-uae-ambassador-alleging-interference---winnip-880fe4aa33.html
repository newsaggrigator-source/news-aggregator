Somalia expels UAE ambassador, alleging interference - Winnipeg Sun

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 01:24:42 GMT

Somalia has expelled the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador, citing interference in its internal affairs. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud condemned the UAE's actions, which he described as "irresponsible." The diplomatic rift stems from various disputes, including the UAE's perceived support for Israel's recognition of Somaliland, a breakaway region in Somalia. The UAE government responded to the expulsion by criticizing Somalia's decision and calling it irresponsible. This incident highlights the ongoing tensions between Somalia and the UAE, which have been exacerbated by differing political interests and alliances in the region. The expulsion of the ambassador marks a significant escalation in diplomatic relations between the two nations, reflecting broader geopolitical dynamics in the Horn of Africa. As Somalia navigates its internal challenges, the impact of foreign influence and intervention remains a contentious issue.
