Ethiopia's Abiy Inaugurates Major Megech Irrigation Dam

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 17:05:38 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed inaugurated the Megech Irrigation Dam Project in Gondar on October 23, 2026. This significant infrastructure initiative aims to enhance agricultural production, improve water supplies, and create economic opportunities for local communities. With a storage capacity of 185 million cubic meters, the dam is set to irrigate over 17,000 hectares of farmland, benefiting tens of thousands of farmers. Abiy highlighted that the project had faced numerous delays but was completed through the government's Medemer development philosophy. Beyond irrigation, the dam will provide reliable drinking water to Gondar and nearby towns, supporting essential services. The artificial lake created by the dam is also expected to promote urban agriculture, fisheries, and tourism, generating job opportunities for local youth. Abiy emphasized the need for further development investment in Gondar and reaffirmed the government's commitment to inclusive economic growth that benefits all regions and citizens of Ethiopia.
