China, Djibouti oppose recognition of Somaliland, back Somalia - Anadolu Ajansı

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:37:42 GMT

China and Djibouti have jointly expressed their opposition to the recognition of Somaliland, reaffirming their support for Somalia's territorial integrity. This stance was articulated in a statement following discussions between Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi and Djiboutian Foreign Minister Abdoulkader Houssein Omar in Beijing. The two nations emphasized their commitment to Somalia's sovereignty, explicitly rejecting any diplomatic recognition of Somaliland as an independent entity. They reiterated that Somaliland is an integral part of the Federal Republic of Somalia and condemned any potential collaboration between Somaliland and Taiwan. This declaration comes in the wake of Israel's recognition of Somaliland as an independent state in December 2025, which sparked backlash from Somalia and other nations. Somaliland has operated as a de facto independent region since declaring independence from Somalia in 1991, yet it has not received international recognition. Somalia views any direct agreements made by Somaliland with foreign entities as infringements on its sovereignty.
