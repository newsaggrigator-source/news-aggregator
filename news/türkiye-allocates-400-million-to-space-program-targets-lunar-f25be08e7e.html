Türkiye Allocates $400 Million to Space Program, Targets Lunar Landing and Somali Spaceport - FTL Somalia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 11:19:46 GMT

Türkiye has announced a significant investment of approximately $400 million to enhance its space program over the next three years, with a focus on achieving a lunar landing by 2027 and establishing a spaceport in Somalia. The Turkish Space Agency's budget will increase annually, supporting the National Space and Aviation Program and aiming to reduce reliance on foreign aerospace technology. The lunar mission involves a spacecraft designed for research and a hard landing on the Moon, while the Somali spaceport aims to provide Türkiye with independent access to orbit. However, the project faces scrutiny due to its dual-use potential for military applications, raising concerns about regional security. The spaceport's success depends on Somalia's stability and infrastructure, which are currently lacking. Despite these challenges, Turkish officials view the spaceport as a milestone for Somalia, with plans for the first satellite launch from Somali soil slated for 2027. The project reflects Türkiye's broader strategic ambitions in space and defense.
