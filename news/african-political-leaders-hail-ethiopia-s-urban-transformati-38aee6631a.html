African Political Leaders Hail Ethiopia's Urban Transformation, Development Gains

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 02:13:54 GMT

African political leaders have commended Ethiopia's significant urban transformation and infrastructure development during the African Political Party Summit. Delegates, including Douglas Togara Mwonzora from Zimbabwe and Irene Mphahama from Lesotho, highlighted Ethiopia's effective management of natural resources and its commitment to increasing women's participation in leadership roles. Mwonzora emphasized that Africa's natural resources should promote shared prosperity rather than conflict, urging political parties to prioritize development over electoral competition. Mphahama praised Ethiopia's urban development and women's leadership initiatives, expressing her intent to implement similar strategies in Lesotho. Both leaders called for enhanced cooperation among African political parties to foster stability and accountability, stressing the importance of collective action in unlocking the continent's development potential. They underscored the need for African nations to take ownership of their resources and development priorities to build a prosperous future. Their insights reflect a shared vision for a united and self-determined Africa. (160 words)
