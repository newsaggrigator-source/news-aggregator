Famine threat 'looms larger than ever' as Somalia drought worsens, warns UN - Yeni Safak English

Sat, 28 Nov 2026 09:48:16 GMT

The United Nations has issued a stark warning regarding the worsening drought in Somalia, stating that the threat of famine is now more severe than ever. Following a historic fourth consecutive failed rainy season, nearly half of Somalia's population—approximately 7.1 million people—are facing crisis-level food insecurity or worse. The situation has deteriorated dramatically, with the number of individuals at immediate risk of catastrophic hunger rising from 81,000 in April to 213,000. The UN agencies, including the World Food Program and UNICEF, highlighted that skyrocketing food prices and insufficient humanitarian aid have exacerbated the crisis. Adam Abdelmoula, the UN humanitarian coordinator for Somalia, emphasized the urgent need for action to prevent a repeat of the devastating famine that claimed 260,000 lives in 2010-2011. As the country grapples with one of its worst droughts in 40 years, President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has called for international assistance to avert a humanitarian catastrophe.
