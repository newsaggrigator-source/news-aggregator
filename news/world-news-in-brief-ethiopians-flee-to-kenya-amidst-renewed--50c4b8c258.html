World News in Brief: Ethiopians flee to Kenya amidst renewed conflict, new head of UN’s food programme, UN mission in Somalia concludes - UN News

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 20:35:12 GMT

Amidst escalating conflict in Ethiopia, approximately 36,600 individuals have fled to northern Kenya, according to the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The violence, primarily between forces from the Somali and Oromia regions over boundary disputes and resources, has intensified recently, prompting a new wave of displacement. Nearly 11,000 refugees have found shelter at the Oriental Mission Church, while the remaining 25,600 are being hosted by local communities. IOM is collaborating with the Kenyan government and UNHCR to assess humanitarian needs, focusing on water, sanitation, shelter, and food assistance. In other news, Luke J. Lindberg has assumed the role of Executive Director of the UN World Food Programme, emphasizing the urgent need for global support to combat hunger exacerbated by conflict and climate issues. Additionally, the UN's political mission in Somalia will conclude after over three decades, transferring responsibilities to Somali institutions, as the government seeks greater control over its peacebuilding efforts.
