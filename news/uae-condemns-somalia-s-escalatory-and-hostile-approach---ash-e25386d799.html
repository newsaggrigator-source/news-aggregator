UAE Condemns Somalia’s ‘Escalatory and Hostile Approach’ - ASHARQ AL-AWSAT English

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 17:02:32 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has condemned Somalia's recent actions, labeling them as an "escalatory and hostile approach" that threatens bilateral relations. This condemnation follows Somalia's expulsion of the UAE ambassador, which Somalia justified by accusing the UAE of meddling in its internal affairs. The UAE's Ministry of Foreign Affairs expressed regret over Somalia's attempts to intertwine its internal power struggles with diplomatic relations, emphasizing that such actions could harm the interests of both nations. The UAE highlighted its commitment to maintaining strong ties with the Somali people, despite the diplomatic tensions. The UAE had previously closed its embassy in Mogadishu in January 2026 due to hostile practices from the Somali government, including the cancellation of bilateral agreements. The UAE's statement underscores the importance of mutual respect and cooperation in international relations, free from political conflicts.
