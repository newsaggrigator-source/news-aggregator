Why Somalia’s president turned against his former ally, the UAE - Al Jazeera

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 07:01:19 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has dramatically shifted his stance towards the United Arab Emirates (UAE), moving from a position of support to open hostility. Initially, Mohamud praised the UAE's role in Somalia's fight against al-Shabab and sought to strengthen ties upon his return to power in 2022. However, relations soured significantly this year, culminating in the expulsion of the Emirati ambassador and the annulment of various agreements, as Somalia accused the UAE of undermining its sovereignty and exacerbating political divisions. Mohamud's government claims the UAE has been financing rival regional administrations and supporting armed factions outside federal control. The UAE, in turn, has accused Somalia of allowing internal disputes to strain bilateral relations. This diplomatic rift reflects broader power struggles within Somalia, where regional leaders, often aligned with the UAE, resist Mohamud's efforts to centralize authority. The UAE's involvement has deepened domestic divisions, complicating Somalia's political landscape.
