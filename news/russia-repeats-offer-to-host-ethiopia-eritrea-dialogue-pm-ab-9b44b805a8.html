Russia Repeats Offer to Host Ethiopia-Eritrea Dialogue, PM Abiy Arrives in Moscow for Africa Summit

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 11:18:28 GMT

Russia has reiterated its offer to host dialogue between Ethiopia and Eritrea, as Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed arrives in Moscow for the third Russia-Africa Summit. Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov emphasized Moscow's readiness to facilitate discussions aimed at restoring relations between the two countries, which have faced renewed tensions since the signing of the Pretoria Cessation of Hostilities Agreement in November 2022. This agreement ended the Tigray conflict but has led to escalating disputes, particularly regarding Ethiopia's aspirations for access to the Red Sea, which Eritrea perceives as a threat to its sovereignty. While Lavrov expressed hope for a meeting if both nations show interest, neither government has confirmed their willingness to engage in talks or accept Russia's offer. Eritrean President Isaias Afwerki also attended the summit, highlighting the ongoing diplomatic efforts amidst regional tensions. The situation remains fluid, with no official confirmation of any bilateral meetings.
