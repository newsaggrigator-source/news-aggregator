PM Abiy Stresses for Stronger Indigenous Capacity to Drive National Development

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 16:57:09 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has called for enhanced domestic institutional and human capacity to drive national development, emphasizing the need for better coordination among government entities and the elimination of systemic obstacles. During the inauguration of the Megech Irrigation and Water Supply Project in Gondar, he highlighted that sustainable progress relies on local expertise rather than external dependencies. The project, situated on the Megech River, aims to boost agricultural output, provide clean drinking water, and stimulate economic growth, with a storage capacity of 185 million cubic meters intended to irrigate over 17,000 hectares of farmland. PM Abiy noted the 19-year delay in the project's completion as indicative of structural weaknesses and stressed the importance of effective collaboration between federal and regional governments. He urged the local community to actively participate in safeguarding the project and ensuring its benefits are maintained, linking peace and development as essential for realizing Ethiopia's potential.
