Alliance for Survival Statement Calls for United Action - https://kulu-media.com/

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 21:38:20 GMT

The Ethiopian Peoples’ Forces Alliance for Survival, a coalition of armed and political opposition groups, has issued a statement calling for united action against Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's government. In a four-page document dated October 31, 2026, the alliance accused the federal government of exacerbating political divisions, prolonging conflicts, and failing to protect civilians. They argue that Ethiopia's challenges necessitate fundamental political change and emphasize the importance of cooperation among opposition organizations and communities. The statement highlights widespread insecurity, human rights violations, and economic deterioration attributed to the government. While the alliance seeks to replace the current administration with an inclusive transitional government, it does not confirm any new military offensives or agreements. The coalition, formed in September 2026, includes historically rival groups like the Tigray People's Liberation Front and the Oromo Liberation Army, reflecting a shared opposition to the federal government despite their differing political agendas. The statement comes amid renewed fighting in northern Ethiopia.
