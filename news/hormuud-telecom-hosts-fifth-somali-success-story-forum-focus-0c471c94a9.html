Hormuud Telecom hosts fifth Somali Success Story Forum focused on healthcare sector - Garowe Online

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 09:49:32 GMT

Hormuud Telecom hosted the fifth edition of the Somali Success Story Forum (BGS26) in Mogadishu, focusing on healthcare investment and improvement. The event, themed "Towards a Healthy Somalia," attracted around 350 participants, including government officials and healthcare professionals. Health Minister Dr. Ali Haji Adam emphasized the necessity of healthcare investment for societal development, while Hormuud Telecom Chairman Ahmed Mohamed Yusuf highlighted the importance of recognizing healthcare workers' contributions. Discussions included the challenges facing Somalia's healthcare system and potential solutions for enhancing service delivery. The forum concluded with Deputy Prime Minister Jibril Abdirashid Haji praising Hormuud's role in promoting community initiatives and essential services. The event aimed to foster collaboration between the government and private sector to improve healthcare accessibility and quality in Somalia.
