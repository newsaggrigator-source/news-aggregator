Ghana and Somalia Name Lineups for Crucial AFCON Qualifier as Both Chase First Points - FTL Somalia

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 15:25:32 GMT

Ghana and Somalia have unveiled their lineups for a crucial AFCON 2027 Group C qualifier at the Essipong Sports Stadium, both teams seeking their first points after losing their opening matches. Ghana, under interim coach Kim Lars Björkegren, features a mix of experienced players and local talents, with key figures like Thomas Partey and Jordan Ayew aiming to reverse the team's fortunes. Conversely, Somalia, coached by Yusuf Ali Nur, fields a squad that includes players from both the diaspora and local leagues, marking their historic presence in the group stage. Both teams face immense pressure, with Ghana needing a win to avoid further crisis after two defeats, while Somalia aims to build on their resilience shown in previous matches. The match highlights the disparities in African football, with Ghana expected to dominate, yet both teams are motivated to secure a significant result. The stakes are high as they strive for respect and survival in the tournament.
