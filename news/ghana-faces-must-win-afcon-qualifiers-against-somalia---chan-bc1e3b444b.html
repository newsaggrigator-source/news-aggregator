Ghana faces must win AFCON Qualifiers against Somalia - Channel Africa

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 08:00:43 GMT

Ghana's national football team is under pressure as they prepare for crucial Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers against Somalia, needing to secure victories to keep their qualification hopes alive. The Black Stars have had a disappointing start to their campaign, managing only one point from their first two matches, which led to the dismissal of head coach Carlos Queiroz. In a bid to turn their fortunes around, Ghana has welcomed back key players, including Tottenham Hotspur midfielder Mohammed Kudus, who returns from injury. Captain Jordan Ayew has also been recalled to provide leadership, although the team will miss winger Antoine Semenyo due to injury. The upcoming back-to-back matches against Somalia are critical for Ghana, as they aim to revive their chances of qualifying for the 2027 AFCON tournament.
