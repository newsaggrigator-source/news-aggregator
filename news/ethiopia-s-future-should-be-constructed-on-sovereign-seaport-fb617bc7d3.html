Ethiopia’s Future Should be Constructed on Sovereign Seaport: Queen’s University Belfast Lecturer - ENA English

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 06:14:53 GMT

Ethiopia's future maritime strategy should focus on establishing sovereign control over a Red Sea port, according to Bizuneh Yimenu, a political scientist at Queen’s University Belfast. He argues that regaining coastal access, lost after Eritrea's independence in 1993, is crucial for enhancing Ethiopia's security and economic prospects. Yimenu criticizes the transitional government for not negotiating Ethiopia's maritime rights during Eritrea's independence, suggesting that current discussions should prioritize diplomatic and political negotiations over rigid legal frameworks. He emphasizes the importance of direct maritime connectivity for Ethiopia, as 95% of its trade currently relies on a single corridor through Djibouti, posing significant economic risks. Yimenu sees potential in the Assab port, advocating for closer economic ties between Ethiopia and Eritrea to foster mutual benefits and reduce conflict risks. He envisions a more integrated Horn of Africa with shared infrastructure and improved trade relations, which could promote regional stability and cooperation.
