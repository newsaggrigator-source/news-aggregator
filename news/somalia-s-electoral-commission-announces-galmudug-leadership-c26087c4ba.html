Somalia’s Electoral Commission Announces Galmudug Leadership Candidates - Dawan Africa

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 20:12:25 GMT

Somalia's Electoral Commission has announced the candidates for the leadership positions in the Galmudug region. This announcement marks a significant step in the electoral process as the region prepares for upcoming elections. The commission has outlined the qualifications and criteria for the candidates, emphasizing the importance of transparency and fairness in the electoral process. The leadership elections in Galmudug are crucial for the region's stability and governance, and the commission aims to ensure that the elections are conducted smoothly. The announcement has been met with anticipation from the public and political stakeholders, who are keen to see how the new leadership will address the challenges facing the region.
