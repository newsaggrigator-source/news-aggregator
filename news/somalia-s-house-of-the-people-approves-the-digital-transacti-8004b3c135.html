Somalia’s House of the People Approves the Digital Transactions Bill with 137 Votes in Favour - Africa.com

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 13:08:33 GMT

Somalia's House of the People has approved the Digital Transactions Bill with 137 votes in favor. This legislation, developed by the Federal Ministry of Communications and Technology, aims to create a legal framework for digital transactions, aligning Somalia's regulatory environment with technological advancements and the increasing demand for digital services. The bill underwent various parliamentary stages, including readings and deliberations, before reaching the plenary vote. Minister of Communications and Technology, H.E. Mohamed Adam Moalim, expressed gratitude to the MPs for their support and acknowledged the contributions of the Parliamentary Committee on Communications and Technology. The approval is seen as a crucial step in enhancing Somalia's legal and regulatory framework for the digital economy, particularly as mobile money services gain popularity in the country.
