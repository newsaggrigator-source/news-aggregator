UN Experts Condemn Somalia’s Chief Justice Dismissal as Unlawful, Call for His Restoration - FTL Somalia

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 22:10:44 GMT

United Nations human rights experts have condemned the Somali government's dismissal of Chief Justice Bashe Yusuf Ahmed as unlawful, calling for his reinstatement. In a statement released on November 12, experts Margaret Satterthwaite and Ben Saul criticized the unilateral removal by presidential decree, which bypassed constitutional procedures, including parliamentary approval. They highlighted that this dismissal occurred just before the Supreme Court was set to rule on the legality of recent presidential and parliamentary term extensions. The court subsequently declared these extensions unlawful and deemed the Chief Justice's removal unconstitutional. The UN experts emphasized that the government's actions undermine judicial independence and the rule of law, which are essential for stability in Somalia, especially amid ongoing security challenges from Al Shabaab. They also condemned the intimidation of judges and the deployment of security forces to disrupt court proceedings. The government's response has been dismissive, claiming the Supreme Court's ruling lacks legal effect, further complicating the ongoing constitutional crisis.
