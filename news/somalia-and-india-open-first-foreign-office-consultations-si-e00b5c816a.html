Somalia and India Open First Foreign Office Consultations, Sign MoU on Bilateral Dialogue - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 22:12:27 GMT

Somalia and India held their inaugural Foreign Office Consultations in Mogadishu, marking a significant step in bilateral relations. Deputy Foreign Minister Hassan Mohamed Ali welcomed an Indian delegation led by Sripriya Ranganathan, who is responsible for consular affairs. The two nations signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to establish a structured dialogue for regular reviews of their relationship. Discussions focused on trade, medical cooperation, education, and regional geopolitical issues. Additionally, Ranganathan met with Somalia's Defence Minister to enhance defence cooperation, particularly in maritime security and counter-terrorism. This engagement formalizes a relationship that has been developing since India re-established diplomatic ties after closing its mission in 1991. While the MoU is a positive development, it remains to be seen if it will lead to substantial cooperation, especially in maritime security, which is crucial for Somalia's strategic interests. The consultations reflect Somalia's broader strategy to diversify its international partnerships. (160 words)
