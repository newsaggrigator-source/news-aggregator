Govt Warns Nigerians With Underlying Health Conditions Against Travelling to Addis Ababa

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 06:08:29 GMT

The Nigerian government has issued a warning to citizens with pre-existing health conditions, advising them to seek medical advice before traveling through Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs highlighted the city's high altitude of approximately 2,355 meters (7,726 feet), which can lead to lower atmospheric pressure and reduced oxygen levels, posing health risks, particularly for those with cardiovascular and respiratory issues. In 2026, Nigeria's Diplomatic Mission in Addis Ababa reported 16 deaths of Nigerians transiting through the city, attributed to health complications linked to high altitude. The government emphasized that the advisory aims to inform vulnerable travelers rather than discourage travel. It encourages individuals to take necessary precautions and make informed decisions regarding their health and safety while traveling. The Ministry remains committed to providing timely information to safeguard the welfare of Nigerians abroad.
