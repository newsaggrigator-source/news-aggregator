Somali opposition coalition calls for talks on election agreement - Horseed Media

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 18:00:30 GMT

The Somali opposition coalition has called for negotiations to reach an agreement on the upcoming elections. The coalition emphasizes the need for a consensus among political factions to ensure a fair and transparent electoral process. They argue that dialogue is essential to address ongoing disputes and to foster stability in the country. The opposition's appeal comes amid concerns over the electoral timeline and the potential for delays. Leaders from the coalition are urging the government to engage in discussions to facilitate a smooth electoral transition and to uphold democratic principles. The call for talks reflects the coalition's commitment to a collaborative approach in resolving electoral challenges in Somalia.
