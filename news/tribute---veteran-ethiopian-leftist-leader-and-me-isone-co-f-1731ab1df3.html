Tribute - Veteran Ethiopian Leftist Leader and Me'isone Co-Founder Dr. Negede Gobeze Dies At 83

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 10:24:39 GMT

Dr. Negede Gobeze, a prominent Ethiopian leftist leader and co-founder of the All-Ethiopia Socialist Movement (Me'isone), passed away in Brussels on November 13, 2026, at the age of 83. Born in Dessie in October 1943, he studied economics at Addis Abeba University College and later earned a Ph.D. in law from Aix-Marseille University in France. Negede became a significant figure in Ethiopia's student movement during the 1960s, serving as president of the Ethiopian Students' Union in France and Europe. He co-founded Me'isone in 1968 and initially aligned with the Derg military government after the 1974 revolution, later breaking away and going into exile. Throughout his life, he remained active in political opposition, founding organizations and independent media initiatives. A prolific writer, Negede's works addressed Ethiopian politics and history, leaving a contested legacy as a revolutionary intellectual advocating for democratic reform. His death marks the end of a significant chapter in modern Ethiopian history. (160 words)
