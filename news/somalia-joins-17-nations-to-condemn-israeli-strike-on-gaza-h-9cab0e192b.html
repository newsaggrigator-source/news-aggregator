Somalia Joins 17 Nations to Condemn Israeli Strike on Gaza Hospital - Dawan Africa

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 19:40:58 GMT

Somalia, along with 17 other nations, has condemned an Israeli airstrike on the Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis, Gaza, which reportedly resulted in casualties among patients and healthcare workers. In a joint statement released by Djibouti's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the foreign ministers highlighted that the attack violated international humanitarian law, which protects medical facilities and personnel. The statement criticized Israeli strikes against Palestinian civilians and warned that such actions jeopardize ceasefire agreements and efforts to restore stability in Gaza. The ministers expressed deep concern over Israeli restrictions on humanitarian aid, exacerbating the humanitarian crisis in the region, particularly as winter approaches. They called for Israel to adhere to its obligations under various agreements and ensure the safe delivery of humanitarian assistance. The statement also urged the United States and the UN Security Council to enhance efforts to implement the ceasefire and facilitate humanitarian operations in Gaza.
