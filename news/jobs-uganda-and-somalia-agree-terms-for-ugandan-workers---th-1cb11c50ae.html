JOBS: Uganda and Somalia agree terms for Ugandan workers - The Independent Uganda

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 19:15:04 GMT

Uganda and Somalia have reached an agreement regarding the employment of Ugandan workers in Somalia, aimed at enhancing labor mobility and addressing unemployment issues. The agreement was formalized during a meeting between Uganda's Minister of Gender, Labour and Social Development, Betty Amongi, and Somalia's Minister of Labour and Social Affairs, Salim Aliyow Ibraahim. This collaboration is expected to facilitate the recruitment of Ugandan workers in various sectors, including construction, healthcare, and hospitality, thereby providing them with better job opportunities abroad. The Ugandan government has emphasized the importance of ensuring that workers are adequately trained and equipped with the necessary skills before deployment. Additionally, the agreement includes provisions for the protection of workers' rights and welfare, ensuring that they are treated fairly and compensated appropriately. This initiative is part of Uganda's broader strategy to tackle unemployment and promote economic growth through international labor partnerships. The agreement is seen as a significant step towards strengthening bilateral relations between the two countries.
