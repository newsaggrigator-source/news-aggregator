Somalia puts Hubiye national ID verification platform into operation - Biometric Update

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 20:57:00 GMT

Somalia has officially launched the Hubiye national ID verification platform, developed by the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA), to enhance digital identity integration in banking and public services. Announced in late October, Hubiye allows institutions to verify identity credentials against NIRA’s national database, ensuring the authenticity of identity documents while protecting citizens' data. This platform is part of a broader digital infrastructure initiative that includes the Certificate Delivery System and the eAqoonsi app, which provides citizens with a digital version of their national ID. The verification process involves a one-time password sent to the ID holder, facilitating secure access to services. As of April 2026, Hubiye supports real-time identity verification for 25 services, benefiting approximately 1.2 million citizens. The Somali government aims to enroll 15 million people in the national ID system by 2029, furthering its digital transformation goals and improving service delivery across sectors.
