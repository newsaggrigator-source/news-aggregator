Erdogan Hospital Brings First Advanced Cancer Diagnostic Machines to Somalia - Dawan Africa

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 19:13:34 GMT

Recep Tayyip Erdogan Hospital in Mogadishu has introduced three advanced cancer diagnostic machines, marking the first time such equipment has been available in Somalia. The machines include Immunohistochemistry (IHC), which helps differentiate cancer types, a Frozen Section machine for rapid testing during surgeries, and a liquid-based preparation system for tissue samples. Dr. Ismail Mahmoud Abdullahi, head of the hospital's Pathology department, emphasized the significance of the IHC machine in determining cancer types and guiding treatment decisions. The Frozen Section machine provides immediate results during surgery, crucial for assessing cancer spread and determining surgical extent. This advancement is particularly beneficial for children with Hirschsprung disease, who previously faced long wait times for test results. The introduction of these machines comes at a critical time, as cancer poses a significant health challenge in Somalia, with over 6,000 cases reported between 2023 and 2026.
