Somalia has accused the United Arab Emirates (UAE) of undermining its national unity and supporting armed militias within the country. The Somali president specifically claimed that the UAE has been involved in efforts to recognize Somaliland, a self-declared independent region, by Israel. Additionally, he alleged that the UAE utilized the Berbera port in January 2026 to facilitate these actions. The accusations highlight ongoing tensions between Somalia and the UAE, with the Somali government expressing concerns over foreign interference in its internal affairs. This situation underscores the complex geopolitical dynamics in the Horn of Africa, where regional powers are often engaged in influence operations that can exacerbate local conflicts and challenge national sovereignty. The Somali administration's statements reflect a broader struggle for control and legitimacy in the region, as it navigates relationships with various international actors.
