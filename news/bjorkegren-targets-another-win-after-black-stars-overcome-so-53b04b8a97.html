Bjorkegren targets another win after Black Stars overcome Somalia - Graphic Online

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 14:18:31 GMT

Interim Black Stars coach Kim Lars Bjorkegren expressed satisfaction following Ghana's 2-0 victory over Somalia in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier. The win marked Ghana's first in the qualifiers, with goals from Jonas Adjetey and Felix Afena-Gyan. Bjorkegren praised the team's second-half performance, noting they created numerous scoring opportunities and could have won by a larger margin. He acknowledged the team's struggles in the first half, attributing it to a drop in tempo and the hot weather, but emphasized the importance of securing all three points. Looking ahead to the return fixture, he expressed confidence in the team's ability to replicate their performance, highlighting the need for improved ball movement and intensity. Regarding his future, Bjorkegren stated his focus is on supporting the team and achieving qualification for the AFCON, leaving long-term decisions to the relevant authorities. He remains optimistic about the team's potential to succeed. (160 words)
