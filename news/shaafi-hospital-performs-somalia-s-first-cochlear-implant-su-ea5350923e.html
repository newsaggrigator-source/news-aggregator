Shaafi Hospital Performs Somalia's First Cochlear Implant Surgeries - Dawan Africa

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 09:22:59 GMT

Shaafi Hospital in Mogadishu has made history by performing Somalia's first cochlear implant surgeries, marking a significant advancement in the country's healthcare capabilities. Announced on October 27, 2026, this achievement positions Somalia as the ninth African nation to offer this procedure. The surgeries were conducted by Dr. Ismail Mohamed Ali, an ENT specialist with expertise in ear surgery. This initiative was made possible through a collaboration with MED-EL, a global manufacturer of cochlear implants. The cochlear implant is a vital device for individuals with severe hearing loss, converting sounds into signals that stimulate the auditory nerve directly. Shaafi Hospital will be the sole provider of cochlear implant surgeries and related speech therapy in Somalia, which is expected to alleviate the financial burden on families who previously had to seek treatment abroad. This development is a significant step towards improving healthcare access for those with hearing impairments in the region.
