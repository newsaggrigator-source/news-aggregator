Turkey’s Somalia spaceport linked to military ambitions and long-range missile development - Nordic Monitor

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 23:43:46 GMT

Turkey is constructing a rocket launch facility in Somalia, presented as a civilian spaceport for satellite launches, but it is also linked to military ambitions and long-range missile development. Official documents reveal a financial allocation of 2.1 billion Turkish lira (approximately $42 million) from the Turkish Space Agency to the Defense Industry Support Fund, indicating a connection between civilian space initiatives and military funding. The facility, located near Warsheikh, is strategically positioned for both satellite launches and missile testing, benefiting from Somalia's proximity to the equator. This site could facilitate Turkey's development of long-range missile capabilities, with President Erdogan aiming to enhance Turkey's missile inventory significantly. The project is expected to evolve in phases, initially supporting Turkish satellites and eventually offering services to foreign clients. While the government emphasizes the civilian aspects, the overlap in technologies between satellite launch vehicles and military missiles raises concerns about the true nature of the facility's use.
