From Operating Rooms to Water Wells: How Has the UAE Helped Support Essential Services for Somalis? - Garowe Online

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 19:43:12 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has significantly contributed to essential services in Somalia, particularly in healthcare, education, and water provision. Notably, Sheikh Zayed Hospital in Mogadishu, which reopened in February 2024 after renovations, offers free medical services to a population facing healthcare access challenges. Since 2010, the UAE has invested approximately AED 1.2 billion in various humanitarian projects, including well drilling and educational facilities, addressing the basic needs of Somali communities. The UAE's efforts have been recognized by the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, which commended over $200 million in assistance since early 2023, aimed at alleviating the humanitarian crisis exacerbated by drought. These initiatives reflect a commitment to sustainable development, providing long-term benefits rather than short-term relief. The collaboration between local authorities and international partners is crucial for maintaining these essential services amid ongoing challenges in Somalia.
