Trump says Rep. Ilhan Omar could ‘go to jail,’ disparages Somalia - Washington Times

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 19:30:16 GMT

Former President Donald Trump suggested that Rep. Ilhan Omar could face jail time during a recent rally, where he criticized her and her Somali heritage. Trump claimed that Omar, a Democrat from Minnesota, has been involved in activities that warrant legal scrutiny. He also made disparaging remarks about Somalia, implying that it is a failed state. Trump's comments have sparked backlash from various quarters, with critics accusing him of promoting xenophobia and racism. Omar, who is one of the first two Muslim women elected to Congress, has previously faced similar attacks from Trump and others. The remarks come amid ongoing tensions surrounding immigration and race in the U.S. political landscape.
