Somalia sends more than 200 military vehicles toward Burhakabo - Somali Guardian

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 07:36:41 GMT

The Somali federal government has dispatched over 200 military vehicles to Burhakabo following the recent seizure of the town by forces loyal to former Southwest president Abdiasis Laftagaren. This military buildup aims to reclaim Burhakabo and other territories lost to Laftagaren's forces. The convoy, which included armored personnel carriers, troop trucks, and supply vehicles, was observed traveling along the Mogadishu-Baidoa road, passing through Wanlawayn and Yaqbariwayne. Local residents reported seeing a long line of military vehicles early Saturday. The road has been a site of multiple Al-Shabaab attacks, with recent reports indicating that at least three military vehicles were destroyed by roadside bombs, resulting in casualties among soldiers. Additionally, Ethiopian forces, stationed in towns recaptured by Laftagaren's troops, have expressed their intention to remain neutral in the conflict but warned of self-defense if attacked. The situation remains tense as the federal government has yet to comment on the military movements and recent assaults. (160 words)
