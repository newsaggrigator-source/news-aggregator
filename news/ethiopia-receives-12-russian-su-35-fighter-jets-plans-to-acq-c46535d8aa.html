Ethiopia receives 12 Russian Su-35 fighter jets, plans to acquire S-400 air defenses - Anadolu Ajansı

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 18:00:26 GMT

Ethiopia has received 12 Russian-made Su-35 fighter jets and plans to acquire four S-400 air defense systems, as announced by Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. This military enhancement comes amid rising tensions with Eritrea and renewed conflict in the Tigray region, raising concerns about regional stability. Abiy expressed gratitude to Russian President Vladimir Putin for the early delivery of the jets, which were received a year ahead of schedule. He emphasized that these acquisitions are crucial for strengthening Ethiopia's military capabilities and safeguarding its sovereignty. The Su-35s are designed for air superiority and ground attack, while the S-400 system is capable of engaging various aerial threats. The strained relations between Ethiopia and Eritrea stem from disputes over access to the Red Sea and security issues in northern Ethiopia, following a history of conflict between the two nations. The situation remains tense as Ethiopia seeks to bolster its defense amidst these challenges.
