Hassan Sheikh Reportedly Fails to Secure Museveni’s Commitment to Keep Ugandan Troops in Somalia - Garowe Online

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 22:30:33 GMT

Former Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's recent visit to Uganda aimed to secure a commitment from President Yoweri Museveni for the continued presence of Ugandan troops in Somalia. The meeting, held on October 26, 2026, at State House Entebbe, focused on Somalia's security challenges, particularly the fight against Al-Shabaab. Despite Hassan Sheikh expressing gratitude for Uganda's military contributions and hoping for an extension of troop deployment, no new commitments were made. Museveni emphasized the need for political unity in Somalia, attributing the country's issues to ideological divisions and misdiagnosed problems. He urged Somali leaders to unite against Al-Shabaab, warning that ongoing political disputes could jeopardize stability. This visit comes amid Uganda's reconsideration of its military role in Somalia, highlighting the urgency of political cohesion and coordinated efforts against extremist threats. Hassan Sheikh's efforts to secure military support reflect the ongoing complexities of Somalia's security landscape.
