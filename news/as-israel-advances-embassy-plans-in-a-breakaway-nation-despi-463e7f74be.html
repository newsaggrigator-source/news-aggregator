As Israel advances embassy plans in a breakaway nation despite warnings from China and other global powers, the US reveals why it left a key airbase in Somalia after 14 years - Business Insider Africa

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 16:00:14 GMT

The United States has vacated the Baledogle Military Airfield in Somalia after 14 years, citing the deteriorating condition of the runway as the primary reason for the departure. U.S. Army Lt. Gen. John Brennan emphasized that the decision was economically motivated, as repairing the runway would have been prohibitively expensive. Despite the withdrawal, the U.S. maintains a strong partnership with Somalia, particularly in counter-terrorism efforts against Al-Shabaab. Meanwhile, Israel is advancing plans to establish an embassy in Somaliland, a breakaway region of Somalia, despite opposition from global powers like China and Saudi Arabia. Israel's recognition of Somaliland marks a significant diplomatic move, as it is the first UN member state to do so since Somaliland declared independence in 1991. This development has heightened tensions between Somalia and Israel, yet U.S.-Somalia collaboration remains intact, focusing on security and counter-terrorism initiatives.
