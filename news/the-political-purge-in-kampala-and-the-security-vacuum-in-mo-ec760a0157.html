The Political Purge in Kampala and the Security Vacuum in Mogadishu - FTL Somalia

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 13:18:59 GMT

The political turmoil in Uganda, particularly the purge of senior military officers by General Muhoozi Kainerugaba, is significantly impacting Somalia's security landscape. Kainerugaba's actions, driven by internal succession politics, are linked to Uganda's planned withdrawal of troops from Somalia, a move that threatens to exacerbate the security vacuum in the region. The Ugandan military has been a crucial component of the security framework in Somalia for nearly two decades, particularly in the Banadir and Lower Shabelle regions. As Uganda withdraws, the Somali National Army faces increased pressure, especially in areas prone to clan disputes and weak governance. This withdrawal coincides with a broader decline in international support for Somalia's security architecture, further complicating the situation. The Ugandan exit, motivated by domestic political struggles rather than consultations with Somali leadership, leaves Somalia more vulnerable to threats from groups like Al-Shabaab, highlighting the precariousness of its security situation amidst ongoing political divisions.
