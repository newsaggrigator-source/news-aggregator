UN mission in Somalia closes after three decades - Somali Guardian

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 17:51:14 GMT

The United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) officially concluded its operations on October 28, 2026, marking the end of over 30 years of UN missions in the country. This closure was initiated at the request of the Somali government and followed a two-year transition period under Security Council resolutions 2753 and 2809. The UN has transferred the mission's responsibilities to Somali institutions and various UN agencies. Secretary-General Antonio Guterres acknowledged the ongoing challenges in Somalia, including political, security, and humanitarian issues, and emphasized the need for continued international support. Despite the mission's closure, the UN will maintain its presence in Somalia through resident agencies and partnerships with Somali authorities and the African Union. A farewell ceremony in Mogadishu celebrated this transition, with Deputy Prime Minister Salah Ahmed Jama calling it a "day of victory." The closure reflects a shift in the UN's role rather than a complete withdrawal of support. (159 words)
