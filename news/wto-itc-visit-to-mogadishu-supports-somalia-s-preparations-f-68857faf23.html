WTO-ITC visit to Mogadishu supports Somalia's preparations for next phase of WTO accession - World Trade Organization

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 15:35:22 GMT

The World Trade Organization (WTO) and the International Trade Centre (ITC) recently visited Mogadishu to assist Somalia in its preparations for the next phase of its accession to the WTO. The visit aimed to enhance Somalia's understanding of the WTO's rules and processes, as well as to provide technical support for the country's trade policies and regulations. During the visit, discussions focused on building institutional capacity and fostering a conducive environment for trade. The collaboration is part of ongoing efforts to integrate Somalia into the global trading system, which is seen as crucial for the country's economic development and stability. The WTO and ITC emphasized the importance of inclusive trade policies that benefit all segments of Somali society.
