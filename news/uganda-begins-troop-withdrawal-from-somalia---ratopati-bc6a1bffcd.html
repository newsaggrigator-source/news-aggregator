Uganda Begins Troop Withdrawal from Somalia - Ratopati

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 19:27:19 GMT

Uganda has initiated the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia, marking the end of a significant military presence that began in 2007 when Uganda became the first country to contribute forces to the African Union's campaign against Islamic insurgents. The Ugandan army announced that the withdrawal process is underway under a bilateral agreement with the Somali government, with plans to complete the exit by the end of 2027. This decision comes amid uncertainty regarding the future of the international military efforts in Somalia, particularly after major financial donors, including the United States, reduced their support. The Ugandan contingent, which included between 4,500 to 6,000 soldiers under the African Union mission and an additional 2,000 to 2,500 under a bilateral agreement, has been crucial in combating the Al-Shabaab group, which continues to control significant areas in Somalia. The Ugandan army emphasized that this withdrawal will allow Somali forces to assume full responsibility for national security.
