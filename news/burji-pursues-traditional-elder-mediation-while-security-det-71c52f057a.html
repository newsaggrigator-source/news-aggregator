Burji Pursues Traditional Elder Mediation while Security Deteriorates Across Southern Border Zones - Borkena

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 01:27:13 GMT

In southern Ethiopia, the Burji Zone is adopting traditional elder mediation methods to address escalating security issues stemming from cross-border raids, particularly from the West Guji Zone. Regional security officials report that armed incursions are threatening local agrarian and pastoral livelihoods, exacerbated by the collapse of government structures in West Guji. Burji Zone Police Commander Inspector Zemede Bantore highlighted that these attacks have resulted in civilian casualties, property destruction, and mass displacement. In response, Burji authorities are leveraging local elder networks and the Geda system to facilitate dialogue and curb violence. Conversely, the Kore Zone, facing similar threats, lacks functional local administrative structures and relies on directives from regional and federal authorities for security. Both zones are calling for immediate federal intervention and troop deployments to restore stability and protect agricultural communities. The contrasting approaches reflect the urgent need for effective security measures in the region.
