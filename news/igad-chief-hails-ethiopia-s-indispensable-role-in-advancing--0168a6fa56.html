IGAD Chief Hails Ethiopia's Indispensable Role in Advancing Horn of Africa Peace, Unity

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 20:56:41 GMT

Dr. Workneh Gebeyehu, the Executive Secretary of the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD), has praised Ethiopia for its crucial role in fostering peace and unity in the Greater Horn of Africa. In an interview, he highlighted Ethiopia's strategic leadership and commitment to regional integration, noting its foundational role in IGAD and its leadership over the past eight years. Workneh emphasized that Ethiopia's diplomatic influence is vital for promoting dialogue and mobilizing coordinated responses to shared challenges, such as drought, food insecurity, and security threats. He called for enhanced cooperation among IGAD member states, asserting that collective action is essential to address the region's persistent issues. Workneh believes that if Ethiopia collaborates effectively with other member states, they can overcome the challenges facing the region and work towards a more stable and prosperous future. His remarks underscore Ethiopia's indispensable position in advancing regional solidarity and cooperation. (158 words)
