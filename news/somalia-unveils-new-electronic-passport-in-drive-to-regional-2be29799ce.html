Somalia unveils new electronic passport in drive to regional integration - TRT Afrika

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 18:10:39 GMT

Somalia has introduced a new third-generation electronic passport aimed at enhancing regional integration and modernizing its immigration system. The passport was unveiled during a three-day meeting of East African Community (EAC) immigration chiefs in Kampala, Uganda, led by Hussein Qasim Yusuf, the Director-General of the Immigration and Citizenship Agency. This new travel document features advanced security measures while showcasing Somalia's national identity and cultural heritage. The initiative aligns with Somalia's commitment to harmonize immigration procedures and improve cooperation with EAC member states, following its official membership in the bloc in March 2024. The EAC electronic passport, which replaces older national passports, is designed to bolster travel security and facilitate regional mobility. Somalia's efforts to modernize its travel documentation reflect its broader goal of aligning with regional standards and enhancing participation in the EAC. Currently, Somalia ranks 99th globally in the Henley Passport Index, allowing access to 32 destinations without a visa.
