Man who travelled to Somalia to join Daesh jailed for 12 years - Counter Terror Business

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 08:00:00 GMT

Mohamed Mohamoud, a 22-year-old man who traveled from Heathrow to Somalia to join the terrorist group Daesh, has been sentenced to 12 years in prison following a conviction for preparation of acts of terrorism. The verdict was delivered after a three-week trial at Winchester Crown Court, which concluded on August 18, 2026. Mohamoud, originally from Somalia, was arrested at his home on January 20, 2026, where authorities seized digital devices, including a mobile phone containing evidence of his travels and extremist communications. He had expressed intentions to join the 'Mujahedeen' and sought assistance via Telegram, stating his desire to become a 'martyr.' The investigation revealed his radicalization through online materials, including Daesh propaganda featuring violent content. Detective Chief Superintendent Oliver Wright emphasized the seriousness of Mohamoud's actions and the importance of public vigilance in reporting potential radicalization. Mohamoud will serve a 16-year extended sentence, including four years on license after his prison term.
