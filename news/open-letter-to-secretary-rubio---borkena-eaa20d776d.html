Open Letter to Secretary Rubio - Borkena

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 01:25:06 GMT

In an open letter addressed to U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, a coalition of Ethiopian American civic organizations expressed their concerns regarding his recent comments about "separatists" and "acts of terrorism" in relation to the ongoing conflict in Ethiopia. The signatories, committed to Ethiopia's sovereignty and democratic governance, urged for clarification on these terms, emphasizing the need to distinguish between various armed groups and their objectives. They highlighted that the Ethiopian Peoples’ Forces Alliance for Survival, a coalition of diverse political and armed factions, aims to prevent national fragmentation and promote peace through an inclusive political process. The letter stresses that opposition to the government does not equate to terrorism or separatism. Furthermore, it calls for accountability for human rights violations by all parties, including the Ethiopian government, which has faced accusations of serious abuses. The organizations advocate for U.S. support in humanitarian efforts and a balanced approach to foster dialogue and peace in Ethiopia.
