'No decision yet on interim coaches’ future after Somalia clash' – Henry Asante Twum - 3News

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 18:27:39 GMT

The future of interim coaches for the Ghanaian national football team remains uncertain following their recent match against Somalia. Henry Asante Twum, a spokesperson for the Ghana Football Association, stated that no decision has been made regarding the coaches' positions. The team faced criticism after the clash, which has raised questions about the coaching staff's effectiveness. Asante Twum emphasized that discussions are ongoing, and a resolution will be reached in due course. The association is considering various factors before making any announcements about the coaching situation.
