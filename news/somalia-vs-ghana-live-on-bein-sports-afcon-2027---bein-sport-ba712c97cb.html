Somalia vs Ghana LIVE on beIN SPORTS | AFCON 2027 - beIN SPORTS

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 18:32:52 GMT

Somalia will face Ghana on November 16 in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) Qualifiers, with the match taking place at Essipong Sports Stadium in Sekondi-Takoradi, Ghana. Although Somalia is the designated home team, they are unable to host the match in their country due to the lack of a CAF-approved stadium. Ghana, having secured a 2-0 victory in their previous encounter, aims to build on that momentum after ending their winless streak. Somalia, currently at the bottom of Group C without any points, seeks its first points after three consecutive losses. Ghana, now with three points, is looking to secure consecutive victories to enhance their chances of qualifying for the tournament. The match is crucial for both teams, with the top two from Group C qualifying for the AFCON, hosted by Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. The kickoff is set for 10:00 AM ET, with coverage available on beIN SPORTS CONNECT in the United States.
