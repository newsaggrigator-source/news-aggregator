What Ilhan Omar’s Memoir Reveals About Her Date of Birth, Family History - Newsweek

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 21:46:00 GMT

U.S. Representative Ilhan Omar's memoir, *This Is What America Looks Like: My Journey From Refugee to Congresswoman*, recounts her early life, including her family's escape from Somalia's civil war and their resettlement in the United States. Omar describes her childhood in Mogadishu, the journey through Kenya, and the refugee screening process that led to their immigration. The memoir has faced scrutiny, particularly regarding her birth date and allegations surrounding her former husband, Ahmed Nur Said Elmi, which Omar has consistently denied. Critics have questioned her age based on her accounts of the civil war, but her memoir aligns with her publicly listed birth date of October 4, 1982. Omar reflects on the challenges of adapting to life in America and the misconceptions surrounding her identity. Despite facing allegations of immigration fraud, no legal findings have substantiated these claims. Omar's narrative emphasizes resilience and the complexities of her journey as a refugee turned congresswoman.
