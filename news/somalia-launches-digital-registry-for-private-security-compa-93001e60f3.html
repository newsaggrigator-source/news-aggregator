Somalia Launches Digital Registry for Private Security Companies, Bans Soldiers from Working for Firms - FTL Somalia

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 15:55:48 GMT

Somalia has launched a digital registry aimed at regulating private security companies, a sector that has long operated without adequate oversight. The initiative, announced by Minister of Internal Security Abdullahi Sheikh Ismail, focuses on the registration, management, and training of private security personnel to enhance professionalism and accountability. A significant aspect of this new framework is the prohibition of active soldiers from working for private security firms, ensuring a clear separation between state forces and private entities. The Ministry emphasizes that private security companies must operate independently, with their own resources. Additionally, a digital weapons registry will help monitor firearms held by these firms. This move follows a Cabinet decision in May 2026 to regulate private security services, addressing concerns raised by the UN regarding the need for improved accountability and monitoring in the sector. However, the effectiveness of these regulations will depend on the government's capacity to enforce compliance and ensure ongoing oversight.
