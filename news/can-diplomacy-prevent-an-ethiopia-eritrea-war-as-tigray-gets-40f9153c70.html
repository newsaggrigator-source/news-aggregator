Can diplomacy prevent an Ethiopia-Eritrea war as Tigray gets a new administration? - DW.com

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 05:44:15 GMT

As Ethiopia establishes a new administration in the Tigray region, concerns are rising about potential conflict with Eritrea. The recent political changes in Tigray could exacerbate tensions between the two nations, particularly given their historical animosities and the ongoing humanitarian crisis in the region. Diplomatic efforts are being called upon to mitigate the risk of war, with international stakeholders urging dialogue and cooperation. Analysts emphasize the importance of addressing underlying issues to foster stability and peace. The situation remains precarious, and the effectiveness of diplomatic interventions will be crucial in preventing escalation into armed conflict.
