Hawadle Clan Leader Calls for Armed Mobilization in Beledweyne as Federal Forces Face Defections - FTL Somalia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 22:07:23 GMT

Hawadle clan leader Ugaas Yusuf has called for armed mobilization in Beledweyne, Hiiraan, as tensions escalate between the clan and federal government forces. This call comes amid reports of defections from the Somali National Army by soldiers of Hawadle origin, who are joining the mobilization against what they perceive as an impending attack by federal troops. The Hawadle clan has expressed dissatisfaction with the recent re-election of Hirshabelle President Ali Gudlawe and the appointment of a new governor, arguing that leadership should rotate among communities as per a power-sharing agreement established in 2016. The situation has worsened since armed Macawiisley fighters disrupted the electoral process, and the return of former NISA director Abdullahi Mohamed Ali Sanbaloolshe has further complicated matters. The federal government is under pressure to address the grievances of the Hawadle community, as failure to do so could lead to significant armed conflict in the region.
