Ethiopia’s Defence Modernization Signals New Era of Strategic Deterrence - ENA English

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 14:30:37 GMT

Ethiopia's recent acquisition of advanced Russian-made Su-35 fighter jets and plans to receive S-400 air defense systems mark a pivotal step in its defense modernization efforts. Announced by Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed during his visit to Moscow for the Third Russia–Africa Summit, these developments aim to enhance national security, protect airspace, and reinforce strategic deterrence. The Su-35, known for its advanced capabilities, will significantly improve Ethiopia's air superiority and combat operations. The S-400 system will bolster long-range air defense, addressing evolving regional security challenges. This modernization reflects Ethiopia's commitment to building a capable defense force that can respond to threats while promoting peace and stability. However, effective implementation will require trained personnel, maintenance infrastructure, and integration of advanced technologies. Ultimately, Ethiopia seeks to safeguard its sovereignty and foster regional cooperation, emphasizing that military preparedness is essential for preventing conflict and ensuring long-term national development.
