The world’s most powerful military exits a pivotal airfield in the Horn of Africa, as Türkiye reportedly eyes a takeover - Business Insider Africa

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 08:00:00 GMT

The United States military has officially withdrawn from the Baledogle Military Airfield in Somalia, a crucial site for intelligence and training operations since 2012. Control of the airfield has now transitioned to the Uganda People's Defence Forces and the Somali National Army. The U.S. utilized this base to train elite Somali forces and conduct counterinsurgency missions, but its presence has fluctuated due to changing political directives from Presidents Trump and Biden. The airfield has faced significant threats, including a major attack by militants in 2019. Following the withdrawal, reports indicate that the U.S. destroyed military vehicles and hired logistics companies to relocate equipment. Meanwhile, Türkiye has expressed interest in taking over the airfield, signaling a potential shift in military dynamics in the region. This development highlights the ongoing strategic importance of the Horn of Africa in global military operations.
