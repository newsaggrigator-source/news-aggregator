Families Who Fled Ethiopia for Kenya's Moyale Need Food, Water and Shelter

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 04:56:17 GMT

Tens of thousands of Ethiopian refugees have fled to Kenya's Moyale region due to violent clashes, with urgent needs for food, water, and shelter. The International Organization of Migration (IOM) reports that approximately 50,000 individuals have crossed the border since late October, facing severe shortages. A rapid assessment revealed that 71% of the refugees do not have enough to eat, with food being their most pressing concern. Water and sanitation issues are also critical, as 44% of households lack sufficient access to drinking water, and over 75% do not have handwashing facilities. Healthcare access is limited due to high costs and medicine shortages. Most refugees are staying with relatives or in temporary shelters, further straining local resources. The situation is particularly alarming for children, who make up more than half of the displaced population, raising concerns about their health and wellbeing amid deteriorating living conditions.
