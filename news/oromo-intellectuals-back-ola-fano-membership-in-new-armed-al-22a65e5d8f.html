Oromo Intellectuals Back Ola-Fano Membership in New Armed Alliance, Call for Inclusive Transition in Ethiopia

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 04:52:16 GMT

A group of Oromo intellectuals has expressed support for the Oromo Liberation Army's (OLA) involvement in a new opposition coalition, the Ethiopian Peoples' Forces Coalition for Survival, which includes the Amhara Fano movement. They view this alliance as a potential solution to Ethiopia's escalating political crisis and advocate for an inclusive transition that respects the rights of all communities. The statement highlights the necessity of cooperation among historically divided groups to address underlying political grievances. However, it also warns that merely removing the current government will not ensure democracy or justice. The intellectuals call for a credible transitional process involving diverse political stakeholders to prevent further exclusion and conflict. The OLA's participation has sparked debate among Oromo constituencies, with differing opinions on whether it serves or undermines Oromo interests. The statement emphasizes the importance of addressing historical grievances while pursuing a unified political future.
