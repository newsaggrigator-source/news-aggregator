All of Africa Today - November 2, 2026

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 06:40:09 GMT

One year after the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) took control of El Fasher in North Darfur, the city remains largely deserted, with reports of widespread destruction and ongoing human rights abuses. Thousands of residents are missing, and many are believed to be detained under inhumane conditions. The UN estimates around 6,000 deaths, though the actual toll may be higher. In Zambia, Human Rights Watch has accused security forces of abductions and torture of opposition members and journalists following the August elections. Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has initiated military training for cabinet ministers to instill discipline amid ongoing conflicts. In Gauteng, South Africa, authorities have shut down 20 liquor outlets to combat gender-based violence, while in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, over 28 million people face acute food insecurity due to conflict and rising prices. Malawi's President Mutharika reassured citizens of his control during a recent absence in South Africa.
