Somalian media: 'Somalia’s federal government is planning to formally sever diplomatic ties with the United Arab Emirates as the cabinet holds an extraordinary meeting' - CGTN

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 10:07:04 GMT

Somalia's federal government is reportedly planning to formally sever diplomatic ties with the United Arab Emirates (UAE). This decision comes as the cabinet convenes for an extraordinary meeting to discuss the matter. The move indicates a significant shift in Somalia's foreign relations, particularly with the UAE, which has been a key partner in various sectors, including security and development. The reasons behind this decision have not been detailed in the reports, but it reflects ongoing tensions that may have arisen from political or economic disagreements. The situation is developing, and further updates are expected as the cabinet deliberates on the implications of this potential diplomatic break. This decision could have far-reaching consequences for Somalia's international relations and its partnerships in the region.
