PM Abiy's Medemer Govt Senior Leadership Taking 'Military Training'at Hurso Center

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 06:50:50 GMT

Senior leaders of Ethiopia's Medemer Government, under Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, are currently undergoing military training at the Hurso Military Training Center. Abiy emphasized that this initiative aims to enhance leadership capacity, discipline, and national security, aligning with the government's broader development vision. He stated that military science and experience could foster essential qualities such as accountability, coordination, and patriotism among government officials. The training is designed to prepare leaders physically, morally, and intellectually for their responsibilities, contributing to the government's goal of building a prosperous and resilient Ethiopia. Abiy highlighted the importance of harmonizing military discipline with civilian purpose to create a generation capable of achieving both targets and broader societal goals. However, details regarding the number of participants, the duration of the training, and specific activities involved have not been disclosed. This initiative reflects the government's commitment to strengthening leadership and ensuring a secure future for the nation.
