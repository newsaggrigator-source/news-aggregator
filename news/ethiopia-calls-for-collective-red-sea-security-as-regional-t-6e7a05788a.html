Ethiopia Calls for Collective Red Sea Security as Regional Tensions Rise - https://kulu-media.com/

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 15:34:38 GMT

Ethiopia has called for a collective approach to Red Sea security amid rising regional tensions, emphasizing that the responsibility for safeguarding this vital maritime trade route should not fall solely on coastal nations. During a security dialogue in Bahir Dar, national security adviser Redwan Hussein highlighted the increasing instability due to militarization, foreign rivalries, and threats to commercial shipping. Despite being landlocked, Ethiopia's economic reliance on maritime routes makes it a key stakeholder in regional security. Redwan pointed out that developments in the Red Sea impact countries beyond its immediate coastline, urging cooperation among regional governments and international partners. He identified various factors contributing to regional instability, including historical grievances and competition among foreign powers. His remarks come at a sensitive time, with tensions escalating between Ethiopia and Eritrea over territorial disputes. Redwan's call for stronger regional cooperation aims to address both immediate security threats and long-term political and economic challenges in the Horn of Africa.
