UN mission in Somalia to end operations on Oct. 31 after decades of support - Anadolu Ajansı

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 18:03:44 GMT

The United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) will conclude its operations on October 31, marking the end of over three decades of UN involvement in the country. This decision follows a two-year transition period and is in line with Security Council resolutions and a request from Somalia’s federal government. UN spokesman Stephane Dujarric emphasized that this milestone signifies the transfer of UNTMIS’s responsibilities to Somali institutions and other UN agencies. Despite the mission's closure, UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres assured continued support for Somalia through resident agencies and partnerships with the African Union and international allies. He highlighted the ongoing political, security, humanitarian, and development challenges in Somalia, calling for sustained international assistance to foster progress, inclusive dialogue, and human rights protection. Guterres reaffirmed the UN's commitment to aiding Somalia in building a peaceful and prosperous future.
