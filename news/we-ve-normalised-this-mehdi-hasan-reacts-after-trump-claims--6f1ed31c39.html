"We've normalised this": Mehdi Hasan reacts after Trump claims Ilhan Omar should go to jail & criticizes Somalia - AOL.com

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 08:26:28 GMT

Journalist Mehdi Hasan has condemned President Donald Trump's recent comments regarding Representative Ilhan Omar and Somalia, labeling them as derogatory and indicative of a troubling normalization of inflammatory rhetoric. During an Oval Office event, Trump suggested that Omar could face jail time or deportation, calling her "sick" and "dishonest," while also disparaging Somalia as a "garbage" country. Hasan criticized Trump's language on social media, recalling a time when such remarks sparked significant controversy. Omar, who is currently under investigation for alleged immigration fraud, responded to Trump's statements by calling them "vile" and "bigoted." The backlash includes support for Omar from civil rights groups and local officials, while some Trump supporters echoed his sentiments. Omar's legal team has denied any wrongdoing and demanded the return of seized items from her home.
