Sudan Closes Ethiopian Consulate in Gedaref, Expels Diplomats in Retaliation for Assosa Closure

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:14:37 GMT

Sudan has closed Ethiopia's consulate in Gedaref and expelled its diplomats in response to Ethiopia's closure of Sudan's consulate in Assosa and the expulsion of six Sudanese diplomats. This diplomatic tit-for-tat escalates tensions between the two nations, which have been exchanging accusations of supporting armed groups within each other's borders. Sudan's government has formally complained to the United Nations Security Council and the African Union, alleging that Ethiopia is allowing the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) to operate from its territory. Ethiopia denies these claims and has accused Sudan, Egypt, and Eritrea of backing Tigrayan rebels. The situation is further complicated by Ethiopia's deteriorating relations with Eritrea, leading to additional diplomatic actions. Amid these tensions, international calls for dialogue and restraint have increased, with the European Peace Foundation offering to mediate. The ongoing conflict in Sudan has already displaced over 14 million people, highlighting the urgent need for resolution to prevent broader regional instability.
