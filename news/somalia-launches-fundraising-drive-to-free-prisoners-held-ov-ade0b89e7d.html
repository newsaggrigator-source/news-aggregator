Somalia Launches Fundraising Drive to Free Prisoners Held Over Debts - Dawan Africa

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 10:31:17 GMT

Somalia has initiated a fundraising campaign aimed at freeing prisoners who have completed their sentences but remain incarcerated due to unpaid debts and financial obligations. Launched by the Ministry of Justice and Constitutional Affairs at a conference in Mogadishu, the initiative, named Advocacy for Indebted Prisoners, highlights that 292 inmates at Mogadishu's Central Prison are currently detained over debts totaling approximately $11.1 million. The campaign targets those from disadvantaged backgrounds who have served their time but cannot pay their dues, excluding inmates still serving sentences for other charges. Justice Minister Hassan Moalim will lead the initiative, collaborating with various stakeholders, including businesses and charitable organizations, to gather funds. A five-member committee will oversee the donations. This initiative comes in response to the overcrowding crisis at Mogadishu's Central Prison, which is currently housing around 1,600 inmates, double its intended capacity of 800.
