Somalia hosts IGAD conflict early warning meeting in Mogadishu for first time - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 13:44:10 GMT

Somalia has hosted the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD) Committee of Permanent Secretaries on Conflict Early Warning and Response in Mogadishu for the first time, marking a significant step in its role in regional peace and security. The two-day meeting, which concluded on November 12, involved representatives from IGAD member states, the organization's secretariat, and experts from the Conflict Early Warning and Response Mechanism (CEWARN). Discussions focused on enhancing regional cooperation in conflict prevention and coordinated responses to security threats. Participants emphasized the need for improved identification of potential conflicts and better collaboration among member states to address security challenges proactively. The meeting underscored the importance of sharing early warning information to mitigate conflicts affecting communities in the Horn of Africa. Somalia's hosting of this committee reflects its increasing involvement in regional initiatives aimed at promoting peace and stability amid ongoing security challenges in the region. (158 words)
