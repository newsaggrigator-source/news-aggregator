Türkiye inaugurates renovated school, breaks ground for hospital expansion in Somalia’s Middle Shabelle region - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 06:28:29 GMT

Türkiye has inaugurated a renovated school and commenced a hospital expansion project in Warsheikh, located in Somalia’s Middle Shabelle region, as part of its ongoing support for Somalia's education and healthcare sectors. The Turkish Embassy in Mogadishu announced that the projects were executed in collaboration with Türkiye’s Defense Ministry, the Somali Turkish Task Force Command, and Albayrak Group. The renovated school includes both primary and secondary education facilities, while the hospital expansion aims to enhance healthcare services for local residents and surrounding communities. These developments follow Türkiye's recent groundbreaking for the new headquarters of the Somali Naval Forces in Mogadishu, which occurred during a visit by Turkish Chief of General Staff Gen. Selcuk Bayraktaroglu. Türkiye has been a significant security partner for Somalia, with deepening ties since Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan's visit in 2011, including military training initiatives to bolster Somalia's counterterrorism efforts against groups like al-Shabaab and Daesh.
