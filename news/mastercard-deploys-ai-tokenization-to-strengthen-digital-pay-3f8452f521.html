Mastercard deploys AI, Tokenization to strengthen digital payment security - Capital Newspaper

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 22:00:00 GMT

Mastercard is enhancing digital payment security in Ethiopia by implementing artificial intelligence (AI) and tokenization technologies. As the country transitions from cash to digital payments, including mobile wallets and virtual cards, the rise in transaction volumes has heightened the need for robust cybersecurity measures. The National Bank of Ethiopia reported significant fraud losses and a sharp increase in cyberattacks, underscoring the urgency of these security enhancements. Mastercard's Senior Vice President, Shabir Ahmed, emphasized the importance of secure payment platforms for fostering trust in digital transactions. The company has invested heavily in AI to analyze transactions in real-time, improving fraud detection and reducing false positives. Additionally, tokenization replaces sensitive card information with secure tokens, minimizing the risk of fraud. This technology not only protects consumer data but also supports various payment services, including contactless transactions and digital wallets. As Ethiopia's digital payment landscape evolves, these advancements are crucial for building consumer confidence and encouraging broader adoption of electronic transactions.
