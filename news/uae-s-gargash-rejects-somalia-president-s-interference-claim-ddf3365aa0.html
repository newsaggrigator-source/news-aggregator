UAE’s Gargash rejects Somalia president’s interference claims - Somali Guardian

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 15:52:42 GMT

The UAE's Minister of State for Foreign Affairs, Anwar Gargash, has dismissed claims made by Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud regarding alleged interference by the UAE in Somalia's internal affairs. Gargash emphasized that the UAE's involvement in Somalia is focused on humanitarian aid and development, rather than political interference. He stated that the UAE respects Somalia's sovereignty and is committed to supporting the country's stability and development. The remarks come amid rising tensions between the two nations, with Somalia accusing the UAE of meddling in its political processes. Gargash reiterated the UAE's dedication to fostering a positive relationship with Somalia, highlighting the importance of cooperation in addressing mutual challenges. The UAE has been involved in various initiatives in Somalia, including infrastructure projects and humanitarian assistance, aiming to bolster the nation's recovery and growth.
