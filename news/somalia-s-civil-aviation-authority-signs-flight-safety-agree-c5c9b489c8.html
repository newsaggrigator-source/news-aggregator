Somalia’s Civil Aviation Authority signs flight safety agreement with International Air Transport Association - The Tanzania Times

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 15:58:37 GMT

Somalia’s Civil Aviation Authority has signed a flight safety agreement with the International Air Transport Association (IATA). This agreement aims to enhance aviation safety standards in Somalia and improve the overall safety of air travel in the region. The collaboration is expected to facilitate the implementation of international safety protocols and best practices in the Somali aviation sector. IATA's support will assist Somalia in developing its aviation infrastructure and regulatory framework, ultimately contributing to the growth of the country's air transport industry. The agreement marks a significant step towards ensuring safer skies for both domestic and international flights operating in and out of Somalia.
