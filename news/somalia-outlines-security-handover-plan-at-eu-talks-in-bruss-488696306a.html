Somalia outlines security handover plan at EU talks in Brussels - Somalia Today

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 21:42:56 GMT

At a conference in Brussels co-hosted by the European Union, Somalia presented its security handover plan, aiming to assume full responsibility for national security ahead of the UN Security Council's review of the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) in December. Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi emphasized the need for international support tailored to Somalia's priorities. The plan was discussed with representatives from 50 countries, including troop-contributing nations like Djibouti, Ethiopia, Kenya, and Uganda. While the talks focused on the transition roadmap, participants highlighted the necessity for an inclusive political settlement to bolster security arrangements. Somalia's federal government faces a political deadlock with the opposition over election rules and constitutional amendments. Meanwhile, AUSSOM has begun transferring operational bases to the Somali National Army, with Uganda planning to withdraw its troops by the end of 2027, marking the end of a two-decade military presence in the country.
