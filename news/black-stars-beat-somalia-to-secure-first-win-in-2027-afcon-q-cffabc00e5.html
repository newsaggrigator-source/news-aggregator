Black Stars beat Somalia to secure first win in 2027 AFCON qualifiers - GBC Ghana Online

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 17:08:01 GMT

The Black Stars of Ghana achieved their first victory in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers by defeating Somalia 2-0 at the Essipong Sports Stadium. This win was crucial for the team, which had been under pressure after a disappointing streak of five matches without a victory. Interim head coach Kim Lars Björkegren aimed to boost team morale with this match. Despite an early penalty miss by captain Jordan Ayew, Ghana took the lead with a goal from defender Jonas Adjetey. The team maintained their momentum in the second half, with forward Felix Afena-Gyan scoring the second goal, securing the win. The victory not only marks a turning point in Ghana's qualifying campaign but also provides a significant confidence boost as they prepare for upcoming matches in Group C, aiming for qualification for the tournament to be hosted by Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.
