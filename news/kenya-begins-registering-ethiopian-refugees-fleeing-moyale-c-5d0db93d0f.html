Kenya Begins Registering Ethiopian Refugees Fleeing Moyale Clashes - Arrivals Surpass 50,000

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 04:57:59 GMT

Kenyan authorities, in collaboration with the UNHCR and the Kenya Red Cross Society, have initiated the registration of over 50,000 Ethiopian refugees fleeing violent clashes in Moyale. The registration began on October 31 at temporary reception sites in Marsabit County, where teams are issuing blue wristbands with unique identification codes to document the arrivals. This process aims to establish an initial count of displaced individuals, including children, and will be followed by household profiling to assess needs and coordinate humanitarian aid. The influx has strained local resources, with many refugees seeking shelter in schools, churches, and camps. Humanitarian needs are significant, encompassing food, clean water, emergency shelter, and healthcare. The situation escalated following a dispute over the Somali Regional State flag and the tragic killing of a young girl, leading to heavy gunfire and civilian casualties. Humanitarian agencies have appealed for $8.9 million to support the response over the next three months.
