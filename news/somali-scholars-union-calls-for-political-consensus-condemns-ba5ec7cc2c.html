Somali Scholars’ Union Calls for Political Consensus, Condemns UAE Interference - Dawan Africa

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 15:37:46 GMT

The Union of Somali Scholars’ Councils has urged Somalia's political leaders to engage in dialogue and reach a consensus to strengthen state institutions and prevent disputes that could hinder state-building efforts. In a statement released on November 22, 2026, the union condemned foreign interference, specifically from the United Arab Emirates, and expressed full support for the Somali government's stance against such actions. The union emphasized the importance of national unity and territorial integrity, rejecting any attempts to fragment the country. It called for solidarity among Somalis to confront militant groups and warned against the politicization of the armed forces. Additionally, the union appealed to traditional elders and community leaders to help resolve clan conflicts and promote peace. In conclusion, the scholars encouraged citizens to uphold national unity and support the government, while also calling for prayers for lasting peace and stability in Somalia.
