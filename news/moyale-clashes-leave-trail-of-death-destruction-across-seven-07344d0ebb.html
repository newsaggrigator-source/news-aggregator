Moyale Clashes Leave Trail of Death, Destruction Across Seven Districts - Tens of Thousands Flee to Kenya

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 15:08:44 GMT

Fighting in the Ethiopian border city of Moyale, sparked by a dispute over the Somali regional state flag, has escalated into widespread violence across seven districts, resulting in numerous deaths, injuries, and significant property destruction. The clashes, which began on October 22, have forced over 50,000 residents to flee into Kenya, straining local resources and infrastructure. Reports indicate that the conflict has led to retaliatory attacks against Oromo residents in Kenya's Mandera County, raising fears of further violence. Local leaders have called for urgent humanitarian assistance for displaced families and emphasized the need to protect civilians. The situation remains dire, with many fleeing without adequate food, water, or shelter. As the conflict continues, concerns grow about the potential for increased instability in the region, with calls for peaceful coexistence among communities on both sides of the border. Humanitarian agencies are mobilizing to provide emergency support to those affected. (159 words)
