Somalia Moves to Finalize Sesame Good Agricultural Practices Policy with UNIDO, EU Support - FTL Somalia

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 21:22:22 GMT

Somalia is advancing its Sesame Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) Policy with support from the United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO) and funding from the European Union. The Ministry of Agriculture and Irrigation held its third technical consultation to finalize the policy, which aims to standardize sesame cultivation, harvesting, and post-harvest handling. This initiative is crucial for improving the quality and market access of Somali sesame, a significant cash crop for the country. Despite being one of the largest producers globally, Somalia faces challenges such as limited government support and weak agricultural practices. The GAP framework will establish essential guidelines for pesticide use and soil management, helping farmers meet international quality standards. However, structural issues like poor irrigation, lack of access to finance, and insecurity remain unaddressed. While the GAP policy lays a foundation for improvement, effective implementation and investment in agricultural infrastructure are necessary for realizing its potential and diversifying Somalia's export base beyond livestock.
