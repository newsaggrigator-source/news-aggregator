U.S. Backs One-Year Renewal of UN, AU Security Missions in Somalia After Reversal

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 10:14:57 GMT

The United States has reversed its earlier stance and now supports a one-year renewal of the UN Support Office in Somalia (UNSOS) and the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM). This decision aims to bolster efforts against al-Shabaab and prevent broader security crises in the region. A State Department spokesperson emphasized that this support is not an indefinite commitment, urging Somalia and the African Union to expedite preparations for sustainable regional security. The renewal follows a previous US position in July 2026, which indicated a withdrawal of support. The Somali Ministry of Defence welcomed the decision, highlighting the US as a key partner in countering al-Shabaab and aiding in the strengthening of Somali security institutions. However, significant challenges remain, including ongoing political tensions and the need for Somalia to assume greater security responsibilities. The UN Security Council is expected to review the mandate renewals before the current authorizations expire on December 31, 2026. (159 words)
