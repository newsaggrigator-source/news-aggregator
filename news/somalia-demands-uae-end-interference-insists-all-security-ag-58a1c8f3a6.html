Somalia demands UAE end interference, insists all security agreements go through federal government - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 10:58:02 GMT

Somalia's federal government has formally demanded that the United Arab Emirates (UAE) cease its interference in the country's internal affairs, insisting that all diplomatic and security agreements must be conducted through Mogadishu. In a statement released on November 18, Somalia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs expressed serious concerns over the UAE's actions, which it claims undermine national unity and sovereignty. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accused the UAE of supporting regional administrations and armed groups that threaten Somalia's integrity, particularly criticizing agreements related to strategic ports that he argues should be negotiated exclusively by the federal government. The UAE's diplomatic adviser, Anwar Gargash, countered these claims, suggesting that the Somali president is prioritizing personal political interests. Despite the escalating tensions, Somalia's Foreign Ministry clarified that its grievances are directed at UAE government policies, not its people, and warned of lawful measures to protect its sovereignty. The situation follows Somalia's earlier decision to terminate security agreements with the UAE.
