Sudan Files Complaint Against Ethiopia at UN Security Council Over Drone Attacks - ASHARQ AL-AWSAT English

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:20:49 GMT

Sudan has lodged complaints against Ethiopia with the UN Security Council and the African Union Peace and Security Council, alleging drone attacks on civilian facilities originating from Ethiopian territory. The Sudanese Foreign Ministry claims that Ethiopia is supporting the Rapid Support Forces (RSF), which have been engaged in conflict with the Sudanese army for over three years. This situation has heightened tensions between the two nations, raising concerns about the potential for the conflict to escalate across borders. Sudan's Sovereignty Council reported that over 180 drones from Ethiopia have attacked Sudan this year, resulting in approximately 500 civilian casualties and damage to residential areas, hospitals, and infrastructure. The UN has warned of a surge in drone attacks, which have claimed more than 1,100 lives in 2026 alone. The ongoing war in Sudan has led to tens of thousands of deaths and millions displaced, exacerbating a humanitarian crisis marked by widespread famine. (158 words)
