874 000 people could be displaced in Somalia in 3 months: UN - SABC News

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:52:39 GMT

The United Nations has warned that nearly 874,000 people in Somalia could be displaced within the next three months due to rising rivers and heavy rainfall, exacerbating an already dire situation marked by drought and repeated displacements. UN spokesperson Stephane Dujarric highlighted that flooding is expected to account for 92% of the anticipated displacements as the country enters a rainy season predicted to bring above-normal rainfall, influenced by strengthening El Niño conditions. The International Organisation for Migration (IOM) and the Danish Refugee Council are collaborating with Somali authorities to enhance early warning systems and prepare for emergency assistance. However, the IOM has only secured 14% of the $16.6 million required to respond effectively to the challenges posed by El Niño-related events. This significant funding gap threatens to leave most of the necessary response efforts unfunded, as authorities and aid workers brace for the potential flooding that could displace hundreds of thousands of individuals.
