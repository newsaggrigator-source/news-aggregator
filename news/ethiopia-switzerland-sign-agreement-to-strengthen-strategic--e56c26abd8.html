Ethiopia, Switzerland Sign Agreement to Strengthen Strategic Cooperation On Migration

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 17:05:07 GMT

Ethiopia and Switzerland have signed a Statement of Intent to enhance their strategic cooperation on migration management. The agreement was formalized by Ambassador Meles Alem from Ethiopia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Ambassador Hendrick Krauskopf from Switzerland's State Secretariat for Migration. This initiative aims to deepen bilateral engagement and address the challenges posed by irregular migration. Both countries reaffirmed their commitment to their longstanding relationship, emphasizing mutual understanding and shared responsibilities. They highlighted the importance of ongoing dialogue and expressed intentions to explore further areas of cooperation that would benefit both nations. This partnership reflects a proactive approach to managing migration issues and strengthening ties between Ethiopia and Switzerland. The agreement marks a significant step in addressing migration-related challenges collaboratively.
