Satellite imagery has uncovered that the United Arab Emirates (UAE) has converted civilian airports in the breakaway regions of Somalia into substantial military installations. This transformation raises concerns about the strategic military presence in a region already marked by instability. Additionally, flight tracking data indicates that the United States has conducted visits to these facilities, suggesting a potential collaboration or interest in the UAE's military activities in Somalia. The visual investigations team at The New York Times, led by reporter Christoph Koettl, has provided insights into these developments, highlighting the implications for regional security and international relations. The findings underscore the evolving dynamics in Somalia and the involvement of foreign powers in its ongoing conflicts. This situation warrants further scrutiny as it could impact both local populations and broader geopolitical interests in the Horn of Africa.
