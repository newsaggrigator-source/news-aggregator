Somalia’s Election Crisis Deepens as Talks Collapse - Horseed Media

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 17:02:50 GMT

Somalia's political landscape faces a deepening crisis as the planned National Consultative Forum, set for November 14-18, has been postponed due to a lack of agreement among political leaders on the dialogue's terms. Opposition groups are boycotting the talks, questioning the legitimacy of President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's electoral roadmap and demanding internationally mediated negotiations. The government insists on proceeding with its electoral timetable, scheduling parliamentary elections for April 15, 2027, and presidential elections for May 15, 2027, despite opposition calls for a suspension of electoral activities until a political agreement is reached. The situation is further complicated by a Supreme Court ruling regarding the government's mandate, raising concerns about the constitutional basis for ongoing political arrangements. The absence of consensus among opposition factions, particularly the Somali Future Council, and the government's unilateral approach to the forum highlight a significant trust deficit, risking further institutional instability in Somalia as it approaches critical elections.
