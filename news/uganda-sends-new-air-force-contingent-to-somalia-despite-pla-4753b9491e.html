Uganda sends new air force contingent to Somalia despite planned troop drawdown - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 17:44:08 GMT

Uganda has deployed a new air force unit to Somalia as part of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission (AUSSOM), despite plans for a gradual troop drawdown. The Uganda Peoples' Defence Forces (UPDF) announced the deployment of Aviation Unit VI, which was officially flagged off at Soroti Air Force Base. Col. Simon Andiandu will lead the unit, with Maj. Ssempa Ndaula as deputy commander. Ugandan Air Force Commander Lt. Gen. Charles Okidi emphasized the importance of discipline and professionalism for the troops during their mission. This deployment follows a meeting between Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni, where they discussed intensified military operations against al-Shabab and the need for sustainable funding for AUSSOM. Uganda has been a significant contributor to peacekeeping efforts in Somalia since 2007, although it has begun withdrawing troops and plans to reduce its presence by the end of 2027. The specifics of the deployment, including personnel numbers and aircraft details, remain undisclosed. (159 words)
