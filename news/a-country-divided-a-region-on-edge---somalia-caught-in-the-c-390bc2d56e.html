A Country Divided, a Region On Edge - Somalia Caught in the Crossfire of Foreign Rivalries

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:17:43 GMT

Somalia is facing a critical political and security crisis, exacerbated by foreign rivalries among powers like Turkey and the UAE. The country is grappling with contentious constitutional amendments that extend presidential terms and alter electoral processes, leading to disputes between the federal government and opposition groups. As the government asserts its authority, opposition leaders claim the president's mandate has expired, heightening tensions. The situation is complicated by external influences, with foreign nations backing different Somali factions, which risks transforming a domestic dispute into a broader conflict. The strategic significance of Somalia's coastline, vital for global trade routes, attracts international interest, further entangling local politics with foreign agendas. The ongoing instability threatens to undermine Somalia's sovereignty and complicate efforts for a unified national framework. As the UN political mission concludes, the potential for violence looms, with calls for negotiations remaining unfulfilled amid deepening divisions.
