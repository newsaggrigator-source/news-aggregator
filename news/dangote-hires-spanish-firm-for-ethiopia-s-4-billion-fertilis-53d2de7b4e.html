Dangote Hires Spanish Firm for Ethiopia's $4 Billion Fertiliser Project

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:34:06 GMT

Dangote Group, led by Nigerian billionaire Aliko Dangote, has contracted Spanish engineering firm Técnicas Reunidas for its $4 billion fertiliser project in Gode, Ethiopia. The contract involves the supply of two hydrogen reformers, which are expected to produce over 200,000 normal cubic metres of hydrogen per hour. The project, initially estimated at $2.5 billion, has expanded to include a gas pipeline, a power plant, and a blending facility, increasing the total investment. The complex aims to produce three million tonnes of urea and two million tonnes of NPK fertiliser annually, utilizing natural gas from the Calub field. Construction began in October 2025, with operations anticipated to start in 2029. This initiative is part of Ethiopia's strategy to reduce reliance on imported fertiliser and enhance agricultural productivity, with plans for local investor participation in the venture once production stabilizes.
