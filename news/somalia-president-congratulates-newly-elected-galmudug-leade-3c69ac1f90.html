Somalia president congratulates newly elected Galmudug leaders - Horseed Media

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 15:39:01 GMT

Somalia’s President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has extended his congratulations to Liban Ahmed Hasan, the newly elected leader of Galmudug state, and his deputy, Abdiweli Ibrahim Garyare. The election took place on Thursday in Dhusamareb, where members of the Galmudug State Assembly voted for the new leadership. President Mohamud emphasized the importance of security and urged the new administration to prioritize resolving internal conflicts, enhancing social cohesion, and improving infrastructure and public services. He characterized the election as historic, highlighting the significant participation of citizens from major towns and rural areas in Galmudug, which reflects their role in Somalia’s democratization and state-building efforts. The president expressed hope that the new leaders would meet the aspirations of the Galmudug populace for peace, effective governance, and development. He concluded by praying for divine assistance for the newly elected officials in fulfilling their responsibilities. This election marks a pivotal moment in the region's political landscape.
