Ilhan Omar’s two secret London siblings pictured with man accused of Somali genocide - New York Post

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 22:35:00 GMT

Ilhan Omar's family connections have come under scrutiny as her two siblings, Mohamed Nur Said Elmi and Leila Nur Said Elmi, were recently photographed with Mohamed Said Hersi "Morgan," a man accused of committing genocide in Somalia. Sources indicate that Omar has sought to distance herself from her siblings and her rumored brother, Ahmed Nur Said Elmi, whom she married in 2009. The Elmi siblings, originally from Somalia, now reside in London, while Omar arrived in the U.S. as a refugee in 1995. Family friends claim that all siblings share the same parents and have maintained ties despite Omar's political career. Mohamed is involved in Puntland politics and has expressed admiration for Morgan, who led a massacre against the Isaaq clan in 1988. This revelation raises questions about Omar's past and her family's history, particularly regarding her marriage to Elmi, which has been described as potentially fraudulent for immigration purposes.
