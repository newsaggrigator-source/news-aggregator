Green Initiatives in Ethiopia Catalyst to Drive Climate Action Across Africa - UN-Habitat Official

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 05:04:55 GMT

Ethiopia's green development initiatives are being highlighted as a model for climate action across Africa, according to Oumar Sylla, Director of the UN-Habitat Regional Office for Africa. In an interview, Sylla praised Ethiopia's efforts to reduce emissions through clean cooking, cleaner energy for vehicles, and extensive tree planting, urging other African nations to adopt similar strategies. He emphasized the importance of leadership in these initiatives and noted that Ethiopia has planted over 56 billion trees since launching the Green Legacy Initiative in 2019. With Ethiopia set to host the 2027 United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP32), Sylla sees this as a pivotal opportunity for Africa to amplify its voice in global climate discussions and promote green investments. He called for African cities to collaborate, share knowledge, and develop a unified agenda for climate resilience, highlighting the need for coordinated action to address the challenges posed by climate change in rapidly urbanizing areas.
