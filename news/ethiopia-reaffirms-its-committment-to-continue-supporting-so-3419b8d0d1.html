Ethiopia reaffirms its committment to continue supporting Somalia’s Security Transition - Zawya

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 15:11:00 GMT

Ethiopia has reaffirmed its commitment to support Somalia's security transition during a Senior Officials’ Meeting held by the European External Action Service in Brussels on November 19, 2026. General Yimer Mekonen, head of the Ethiopian delegation, emphasized the importance of maintaining the security achievements made in recent years to ensure regional peace and stability. He stressed the need for regular consultations, coordinated planning, and predictable resource mobilization to facilitate a successful and sustainable Somali-led security transition. This commitment reflects Ethiopia's ongoing role in fostering security and stability in Somalia, which is crucial for the broader Horn of Africa region. The meeting underscores the collaborative efforts required to address security challenges and enhance the effectiveness of Somalia's security forces as they transition towards greater self-reliance.
