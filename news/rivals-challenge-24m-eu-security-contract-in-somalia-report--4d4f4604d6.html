Rivals challenge €24m EU security contract in Somalia: report - Somalia Today

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 23:20:23 GMT

Two companies, Canada’s GardaWorld and France’s Amarante International, have formally challenged the awarding of a €24 million security contract to Sweden’s Vesper Group for the European Union’s civilian mission in Somalia, according to a report by Intelligence Online. The contract, which is set to begin on December 1, follows the expiration of the current contract on November 30. Vesper Group, along with its partners Janus Global Operations Somalia and Risk Consultancy Global, was selected by the EU Capacity Building Mission in Somalia (EUCAP) on October 1. The challengers have raised concerns regarding the adequacy of the winning bid's drone defenses and whether the proposed budget can support the deployment of necessary expatriate guards. The EU’s foreign service has classified the security risk in Somalia as critical, necessitating armed escorts for mission personnel. The EU has extended the mission's mandate until February 28, 2027, emphasizing its role in training and equipping Somalia’s federal police.
