Trade, Energy and Regional Peace Dominate Abiy-Erdo&#x11F;an Talks in Antalya

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 11:01:16 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed and Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan engaged in bilateral talks in Antalya on November 10, 2026, during the COP31 UN Climate Change Conference. Their discussions focused on enhancing trade, investment, and regional peace. Erdoğan reiterated Türkiye's commitment to strengthening cooperation with Ethiopia across various sectors, while Abiy described the talks as productive, emphasizing opportunities for deeper collaboration, especially as Ethiopia prepares to assume the COP32 Presidency. The dialogue also addressed Türkiye's mediation efforts to ease tensions between Ethiopia and Somalia, following a recent diplomatic agreement. Additionally, Turkish investments in Ethiopia have surged, reaching nearly $2.5 billion in the 2023/24 fiscal year, making Türkiye one of the top investors in the country. Energy cooperation was highlighted, with a memorandum of understanding signed earlier in the year to foster collaboration in renewable energy and infrastructure. Both nations pledged to continue working together on climate diplomacy and shared priorities. (159 words)
