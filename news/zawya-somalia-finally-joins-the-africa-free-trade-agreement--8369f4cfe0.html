ZAWYA: Somalia finally joins the Africa free trade agreement - TradingView

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 07:44:57 GMT

Somalia has officially joined the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) as its 50th State Party, marking a significant milestone in its economic development. The formal ratification occurred on September 4, 2023, when Somalia deposited its instrument of ratification at the African Union headquarters in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. AfCFTA Secretary-General Wamkele Mene announced this development during the 26th Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (Comesa) Summit in Harare, Zimbabwe. Somalia's membership will provide its businesses with preferential access to a market of over 1.4 billion consumers, enhancing opportunities for local enterprises, attracting foreign investment, and diversifying exports. The Somali government views the AfCFTA as a platform for accelerating industrialization and economic growth. The Ministry of Commerce and Industry plans to align Somalia's tariff schedules with AfCFTA commitments and support local businesses in preparing for the new trade environment. This move is expected to strengthen regional trade and integration across Africa. (160 words)
