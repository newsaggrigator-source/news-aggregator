Holy Synod Confirms 60 Orthodox Faithful Killed in Latest Attacks in Oromia, Calls for Protection, Justice

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 04:57:22 GMT

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church's Holy Synod has confirmed the deaths of 60 Orthodox Christians in recent attacks in Oromia's Arsi Zone, occurring on October 20 and 24. The attacks resulted in over 20 injuries and the destruction of 20 homes. Specifically, 14 individuals were killed in Angada Kebele on October 20, while 46 were killed in various locations on October 24. The Holy Synod condemned these acts as "extremely tragic cruelty," highlighting the ongoing violence against Orthodox Christians in the region. They called for government action to protect civilians and ensure justice for the victims. The Synod also urged security agencies to prioritize the safety of affected communities and to implement measures to prevent further violence. This escalation of attacks reflects a broader trend of targeted violence against Orthodox Christians in East Arsi, prompting calls for accountability and investigations into these incidents. The Synod plans to hold prayers for the victims and engage with government officials to address the security crisis.
