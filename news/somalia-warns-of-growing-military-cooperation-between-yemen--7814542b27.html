Somalia warns of growing military cooperation between Yemen’s Houthis and Al-Shabaab - Horseed Media

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 16:44:40 GMT

Somalia's federal government has expressed alarm over the increasing military cooperation between Yemen's Houthi movement and the Al-Shabaab militant group, which is linked to Al-Qaeda. Officials warn that this partnership could enhance Al-Shabaab's capabilities, particularly in drone technology and weapons development, posing a greater threat to civilians and government forces. Ali Mohamed Omar, Somalia’s Minister of State for Foreign Affairs, highlighted that despite differing ideologies, the two groups are collaborating in technical areas. Reports indicate that the relationship has evolved from mere arms trafficking to structured military cooperation, including training in drone operations and explosive device manufacturing. This development raises significant security concerns for Somalia, which has been battling Al-Shabaab for years. The growing ties between armed groups across the Red Sea could also have broader implications for regional stability and international maritime trade, as highlighted in a recent report by the Somali research institute Seldg.
