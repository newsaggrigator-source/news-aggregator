#Video_News: Ethiopia’s return to a sea port is inevitable”: Navy Commander calls for regional maritime cooperation watch - Facebook

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 03:00:10 GMT

Ethiopia's Navy Commander has emphasized the necessity of regional maritime cooperation, asserting that the country's return to a sea port is inevitable. In a recent statement, he highlighted the strategic importance of access to maritime routes for Ethiopia, a landlocked nation since Eritrea's independence in 1993. The Commander called for collaborative efforts among neighboring countries to enhance maritime security and facilitate trade. He underscored that establishing a reliable maritime presence is crucial for Ethiopia's economic development and regional integration. The discussion reflects broader regional dynamics, as Ethiopia seeks to strengthen its maritime capabilities and partnerships. The Navy Commander’s remarks indicate a proactive approach to overcoming geographical limitations and fostering economic growth through enhanced maritime access. This call for cooperation aims to build stronger ties with regional partners, ensuring that Ethiopia can effectively engage in international trade and bolster its economic prospects.
