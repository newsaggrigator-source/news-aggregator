Sat, 14 Nov 2026 15:45:00 GMT

In the upcoming Africa Cup of Nations qualifier, Ghana is heavily favored to defeat Somalia, having recently secured a 2-0 victory against them. Somalia's struggles continue, as they sit at the bottom of Group C after three consecutive losses. The match will take place at the Essipong Stadium in Ghana, where Somalia is technically the away team due to venue restrictions. Ghana's interim coach, Kim Lars Bjorkegren, aims to build on their recent win, which ended a five-match winless streak. Despite Ghana's offensive challenges, they are expected to maintain a strong defense against a Somalian side that has struggled to score, averaging just 0.16 goals per game in recent outings. Betting predictions suggest that the match will feature under 2.5 goals, with Ghana likely to win again without conceding. Historically, Ghana has dominated their encounters with Somalia, winning all three previous matches by a combined score of 9-0.
