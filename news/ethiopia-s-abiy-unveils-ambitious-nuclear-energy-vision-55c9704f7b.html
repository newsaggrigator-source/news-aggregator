Ethiopia's Abiy Unveils Ambitious Nuclear Energy Vision

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 05:07:51 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed recently visited the Ethiopian Nuclear Energy Commission to assess the progress of the country's nuclear energy program, emphasizing its potential to drive economic transformation through advanced technology. During his visit, Abiy articulated the need for Ethiopia to invest in scientific and technological advancements that would benefit future generations, stating that the nation must leverage its natural resources for sustainable development. He highlighted the critical role of reliable energy in fostering industrialization, manufacturing, and digital infrastructure. Abiy also pointed out the diverse applications of nuclear technology beyond electricity generation, including healthcare, agriculture, and environmental protection. He underscored the importance of developing skilled professionals and enhancing scientific research to ensure the peaceful and responsible advancement of nuclear energy. The Ethiopian government views nuclear technology as a vital component of its long-term strategy to expand energy capacity and support economic growth, aiming to create a lasting legacy for future generations.
