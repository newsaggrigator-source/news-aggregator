Somalia’s Future Council Condemns Dismissal of Supreme Court Chief Justice, Welcomes Court’s Election Ruling - Horseed Media

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 18:07:58 GMT

The Somalia Future Council, which includes representatives from Puntland, Jubaland, and the Salvation Forum, has condemned President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's dismissal of Supreme Court Chief Justice Baashe Yusuf Ahmed, labeling it a coup that threatens judicial independence and constitutional order. The Council expressed concern over reports that security agencies are actively searching for Ahmed following his removal. In contrast, the Council welcomed the Supreme Court's ruling that mandates elections within 90 days, marking the end of the Federal Government's term. This statement reflects the rising political tensions in Somalia regarding judicial authority, constitutional processes, and the overall political transition. The situation underscores the fragility of Somalia's governance and the critical need for adherence to democratic principles amid ongoing disputes. The Council's position highlights the importance of maintaining an independent judiciary as a cornerstone of Somalia's political stability.
