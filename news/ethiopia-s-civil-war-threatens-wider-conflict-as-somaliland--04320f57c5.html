Ethiopia's civil war threatens wider conflict as Somaliland offers Addis Ababa a route to the sea - i24NEWS

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 08:33:22 GMT

Ethiopia's ongoing civil war poses a risk of escalating into a broader regional conflict, particularly as tensions rise in the Horn of Africa. Somaliland has extended an offer to Ethiopia, proposing to provide a route to the sea, which could enhance trade and access for the landlocked nation. This development comes amid increasing instability in Ethiopia, where clashes between government forces and Tigrayan rebels have persisted. The potential for Somaliland's involvement highlights the complex geopolitical dynamics in the region, as neighboring countries monitor the situation closely. Analysts warn that if the conflict spreads, it could have significant implications for regional security and economic stability.
