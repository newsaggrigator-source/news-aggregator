ENDF Chief of Staff Vows to Safeguard Ethiopia's Interests in Abay River, Red Sea

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 22:41:06 GMT

Field Marshal Birhanu Jula, Chief of Staff of the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF), has emphasized Ethiopia's commitment to protecting its interests in the Abay River and the Red Sea. Speaking at the ENDF's 119th anniversary, he highlighted the strategic importance of these resources for the country's development and future prosperity. Birhanu warned against attempts by "greedy factions" to monopolize these shared resources, asserting that Ethiopia would resist any efforts to deny it a fair share of benefits. He criticized individuals who betray national interests by aligning with foreign forces, reaffirming the ENDF's role in safeguarding Ethiopia's sovereignty and territorial integrity. While advocating for peaceful relations with neighboring countries, he stressed that Ethiopia's pursuit of cooperation would not compromise its determination to protect its essential interests. The ENDF remains a central pillar of the state, dedicated to defending the nation's security and ensuring that its resources benefit both current and future generations. (159 words)
