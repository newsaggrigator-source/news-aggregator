Somalia Orders UAE Ambassador to Leave Within 72 Hours Amid Diplomatic Row - Garowe Online

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 12:27:26 GMT

Somalia's federal government has ordered the UAE ambassador to leave the country within 72 hours, escalating diplomatic tensions between the two nations. This decision, made during a Cabinet meeting led by Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre, also includes recalling Somalia's ambassador to the UAE for consultations. Information Minister Abdulfatah Sheikh Abdulkadir announced these measures, citing Emirati interference in Somalia's internal affairs as the primary concern. The Somali government plans to take legal action against what it views as violations of its sovereignty and territorial integrity. Tensions have been rising since January 2026, when Somalia canceled security and economic agreements with the UAE due to allegations of interference. The UAE has denied these accusations, asserting its support for Somalia during challenging times. The Somali Foreign Ministry emphasized that it is the sole authority for conducting foreign relations and rejected any agreements made without its approval.
