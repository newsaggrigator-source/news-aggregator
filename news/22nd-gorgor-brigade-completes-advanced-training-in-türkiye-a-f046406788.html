22nd Gorgor Brigade Completes Advanced Training in Türkiye as Somali Army Prepares to Fill Security Gaps - FTL Somalia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 12:12:00 GMT

The 22nd Gorgor Brigade of the Somali National Army has successfully completed an advanced military training program in Türkiye, marking a significant milestone as it becomes the second brigade to finish such training as a complete unit. This training, conducted under a military cooperation agreement between Somalia and Türkiye, included instruction from the Turkish Armed Forces and focused on counterterrorism, urban operations, and close combat. The completion of this training comes at a crucial time as Somalia prepares to take on greater security responsibilities following the withdrawal of international troops, particularly from the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission. The Gorgor brigades are expected to fill the security gaps left by departing forces, but challenges remain regarding equipment, logistics, and strategic deployment. The ongoing training relationship with Türkiye is vital for enhancing the operational capacity of the Somali National Army as it confronts threats from groups like Al-Shabaab.
