Ernest Nuamah set to miss Black Stars’ double-header against Somalia - GhanaWeb

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 16:26:23 GMT

Ghanaian footballer Ernest Nuamah is set to miss the Black Stars' upcoming double-header against Somalia due to injury. The 19-year-old winger, who plays for FC Nordsjælland in Denmark, sustained a muscle injury during a recent match, which has ruled him out of the crucial fixtures. The Black Stars are preparing for the 2026 FIFA World Cup qualifiers, and his absence is a significant blow to the team's attacking options. Coach Chris Hughton will need to adjust his squad as they aim to secure vital points against Somalia. Nuamah has been a promising talent for the national team, and his injury raises concerns about the team's depth and strategy moving forward. The Ghana Football Association has not yet announced a replacement for Nuamah, but fans and analysts alike will be watching closely to see how the Black Stars adapt to this setback in their campaign.
