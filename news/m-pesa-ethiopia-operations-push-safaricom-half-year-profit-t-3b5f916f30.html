M-Pesa, Ethiopia Operations Push Safaricom Half-Year Profit to Sh58.2bn

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 08:21:52 GMT

Safaricom reported a significant 35.9% increase in net profit, reaching Sh58.2 billion for the six months ending in September, driven by robust M-Pesa revenue and growth in its Ethiopian operations. Total service revenue rose by 13.1% to Sh226.1 billion, while EBITDA surged by 27.5% to Sh129.2 billion. In Kenya, service revenue grew by 11.3% to Sh216 billion, with earnings before interest and tax exceeding Sh100 billion for the first time in a half-year period. M-Pesa revenue also crossed the Sh100 billion mark, increasing by 13.6%. Notably, 53% of M-Pesa transactions were free, with 81% of merchant payments incurring no charges. In Ethiopia, service revenue soared by 71.6% to Sh10.6 billion, contributing significantly to overall growth. The company aims for EBITDA break-even in Ethiopia by the end of the financial year, as it recorded 15.4 million active customers, a 38.4% increase year-on-year.
