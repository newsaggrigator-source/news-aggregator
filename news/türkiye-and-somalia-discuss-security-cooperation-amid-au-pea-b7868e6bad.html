Türkiye and Somalia Discuss Security Cooperation Amid AU Peacekeeping Troop Withdrawal - Horseed Media

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 17:07:50 GMT

Türkiye and Somalia have engaged in discussions regarding military and security cooperation, particularly in light of the withdrawal of African Union peacekeeping troops from Somalia. The meeting took place on November 9, 2026, in Mogadishu between Turkish Ambassador Ferhat Alkan and Somalia’s National Security Adviser, Awes Hagi Yusuf Ahmed. The talks focused on counterterrorism efforts, political developments, and the ongoing fight against the Al Shabab terrorist group. The Turkish Embassy emphasized Türkiye's commitment to Somalia's peace and stability. A key aspect of their cooperation is the TURKSOM mission, which provides military training and logistical support to Somali forces. This partnership is crucial as Somalia faces evolving security challenges and adjusts to the changing international peacekeeping landscape. The discussions highlighted the importance of continued collaboration to enhance Somalia's security capabilities and ensure stability in the region.
