Ethiopia's Sovereign Access to Seaport Crucial for Regional Shared Prosperity - Scholars

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 05:35:37 GMT

Scholars emphasize the importance of Ethiopia's sovereign access to a seaport for regional prosperity. Historically, Ethiopia thrived in international trade due to its access to the sea, but has lost this advantage due to political challenges. Current efforts by the Ethiopian government aim to reclaim this right through peaceful diplomacy, with support from the international community. Logistics researcher Temesgen Walelign highlights that lack of port access increases logistics costs, affecting both exports and imports, which in turn impacts consumers. He argues that securing a seaport is essential for Ethiopia's economic competitiveness and long-term development goals. Economist Nemomsa Mengesha also notes that access to a seaport will enhance economic activities and improve living standards for Ethiopians, contributing to regional peace and cooperation.
