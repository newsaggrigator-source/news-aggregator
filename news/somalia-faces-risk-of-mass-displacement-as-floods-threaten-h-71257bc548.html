Somalia faces risk of mass displacement as floods threaten hundreds of thousands - Horseed Media

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:51:40 GMT

Somalia is facing a significant risk of mass displacement, with approximately 874,000 people potentially forced from their homes due to impending floods, according to the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The warning highlights the threat posed by heavy rainfall and rising river levels expected between October and December, exacerbated by El Niño conditions that are likely to bring above-average rainfall. Many communities are still recovering from previous droughts and displacements, leaving them particularly vulnerable. IOM’s chief of mission in Somalia, Manuel Marques Pereira, emphasized the urgency of the situation, stating that hundreds of thousands could lose their homes within weeks. He called for immediate funding to help protect homes and livelihoods and to facilitate safe relocation. The floods could lead to dangerous journeys for those seeking safety, further complicating an already dire humanitarian crisis characterized by food shortages and limited access to essential services. The situation underscores the ongoing challenges posed by climate-related disasters in Somalia.
