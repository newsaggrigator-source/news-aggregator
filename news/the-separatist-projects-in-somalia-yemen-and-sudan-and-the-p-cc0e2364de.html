The separatist projects in Somalia, Yemen, and Sudan and the power game in the Red Sea region - pism.pl

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 10:43:07 GMT

The Polish Institute of International Affairs (PISM) is hosting a roundtable discussion on November 6, 2026, focusing on separatist movements in Somalia, Yemen, and Sudan, as well as the geopolitical dynamics in the Red Sea region. This event coincides with the release of a PISM report titled “New Flags in the Red Sea Region,” which analyzes the rise of separatist projects. Key developments include the formation of a quasi-state in Sudan by the 'Tasis' coalition, Somaliland's push for international recognition following Israel's acknowledgment, and the Southern Transitional Council's control over former Yemeni territories. These movements are influenced by internal state dynamics and external support from regional powers like the UAE and Israel, which aim to reshape the regional security landscape. The discussion will explore the implications of these separatist movements for international law and the responses of the EU and the US to these developments. Participants will engage in a debate under the Chatham House Rule.
