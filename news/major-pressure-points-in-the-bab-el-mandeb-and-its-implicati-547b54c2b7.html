Major Pressure Points in the Bab el-Mandeb and Its Implications for Ethiopian Trade Security - horn review

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 13:55:48 GMT

The Bab el-Mandeb Strait, a crucial maritime passage for global trade, has recently seen increased military activity, particularly from Houthi forces in Yemen. Their advancements threaten the security of this vital corridor, which is essential for Ethiopia's trade, as approximately 97% of the country's imports and exports transit through the port of Djibouti. The shifting territorial dynamics have raised concerns about the potential for heightened risks to maritime security, impacting Ethiopian consumers and exporters. Ethiopia's economic stability hinges on the reliability of this trade route, necessitating proactive measures to safeguard it. Recommendations include enhancing cooperation with Djibouti on maritime security, engaging with Gulf partners to ensure sanctions are enforced without disrupting legitimate trade, and diversifying port access options. These strategies aim to protect Ethiopia's economic interests while promoting regional stability in a complex geopolitical landscape. The situation underscores the interconnectedness of local conflicts and global trade security. (159 words)
