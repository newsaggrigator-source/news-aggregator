Uganda begins pulling troops out of Somalia - The Week

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 23:45:32 GMT

Uganda has commenced the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia, marking the end of nearly two decades of military presence in the country. The initial group of 109 soldiers departed at the end of October, with plans for all 4,500 troops to return home by the end of 2027. This decision follows significant funding cuts from the United States, which has raised concerns about the security situation in Somalia, particularly regarding the Al Shabab insurgency. Somali officials acknowledge that while the current situation is manageable, a sudden troop withdrawal could lead to complications. The African Union is set to replace Ugandan forces in critical areas to prevent a security vacuum. This transition is seen as a crucial test for the Somali National Army's ability to take on greater security responsibilities amid ongoing political divisions that could hinder military efforts against insurgents.
