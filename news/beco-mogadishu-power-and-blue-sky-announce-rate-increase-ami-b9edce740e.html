BECO, Mogadishu Power and Blue Sky Announce Rate Increase Amid 70% Fuel Spike - FTL Somalia

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 10:51:23 GMT

Three major electricity providers in Somalia—BECO, Mogadishu Power, and Blue Sky Energy—have announced a five-cent increase in electricity rates per kilowatt-hour, effective Sunday. This decision comes in response to a staggering 70% rise in global fuel prices, largely attributed to the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. The new rates will see Mogadishu Power's price rise from 33 cents to 38 cents, while BECO and Blue Sky Energy will increase their rates from 41 cents to 46 cents. This price hike highlights the ongoing structural issues within Somalia's unregulated electricity market, where three companies dominate and set prices independently. The lack of effective regulation has led to rising costs for consumers, with many households already struggling to afford electricity. The situation is exacerbated by broader economic pressures, including inflation in fuel and food prices, driven by regional conflicts affecting supply chains. The Somali government aims to reduce electricity costs and increase renewable energy sources, but achieving these goals will require significant regulatory reforms.
