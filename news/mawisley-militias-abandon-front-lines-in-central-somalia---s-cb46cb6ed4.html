Mawisley militias abandon front lines in central Somalia - Somali Guardian

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 16:57:04 GMT

Mawisley militias, affiliated with the Hawadle clan, have abandoned their front-line positions in Somalia's central Hiran region, according to reports. This withdrawal follows the clan's efforts to establish an independent administration separate from the federal government. The fighters, known as Macawisley, have vacated areas in the Mataban and Buulo Barde districts, raising concerns about potential further defections from the federal army in Hiran, which could leave government-held territories vulnerable to Al Shabaab attacks. Recently, the clan seized control of Beledweyne, the regional capital, as part of their political maneuvering. Former intelligence chief Abdullahi Mohamed Ali Sanbalolshe indicated plans to confront the federal government, alleging that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud reneged on promises regarding the Hirshabelle presidency. The situation remains tense, with uncertainty about whether the federal government will reinforce its positions in the region.
