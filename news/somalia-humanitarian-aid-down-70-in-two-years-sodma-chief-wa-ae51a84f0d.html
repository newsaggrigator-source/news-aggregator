Somalia Humanitarian Aid Down 70% in Two Years, SoDMA Chief Warns - Dawan Africa

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 14:16:43 GMT

Humanitarian aid to Somalia has plummeted by 70% over the last two years, raising concerns that it could cease entirely, according to Mahmoud Moallim Abdulle, the commissioner of the Somali Disaster Management Agency (SoDMA). Speaking at a meeting in Mogadishu, he emphasized the urgent need for the Somali government and citizens to enhance self-reliance and collaboratively contribute to relief efforts. The meeting, organized by SoDMA, aims to address the risks associated with El Niño and involves various stakeholders, including diaspora members, religious leaders, and humanitarian organizations. Discussions will focus on fostering community solidarity, coordinating relief efforts, and improving systems to mitigate El Niño-related challenges. Moallim highlighted that the two-day event serves as a platform for exploring how different sectors of society can work together to tackle these pressing issues. The situation underscores the critical need for sustained support and proactive measures to address the humanitarian crisis in Somalia.
