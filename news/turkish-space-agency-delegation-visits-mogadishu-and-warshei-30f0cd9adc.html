Turkish Space Agency Delegation Visits Mogadishu and Warsheikh as Defence Cooperation Expands - FTL Somalia

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 10:01:06 GMT

A high-level delegation from the Turkish Space Agency, led by Fatih Dulkan, recently visited Somalia, focusing on expanding defense and technological cooperation. The delegation's itinerary included meetings and inspections in Mogadishu and Warsheikh, where a significant Turkish satellite launch facility is under construction. This facility, estimated at $350 million, is positioned to enhance Turkey's capabilities in space technology and potentially military applications. The partnership between Turkey and Somalia has deepened since 2011, with Turkey becoming a key ally in various sectors, including security and infrastructure. The recent visit underscores Turkey's ambition to establish a sovereign space launch base, marking a strategic shift in Somalia's international relations. However, the project raises concerns about sovereignty and potential risks, as Somalia could become a target for adversaries of Turkey. The evolving relationship reflects both opportunities and challenges for Somalia, which must navigate its growing ties with Turkey amidst internal and external pressures.
