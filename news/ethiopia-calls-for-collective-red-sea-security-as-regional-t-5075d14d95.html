Ethiopia Calls for Collective Red Sea Security as Regional Tensions Rise - https://kulu-media.com/

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 15:34:38 GMT

Ethiopia has called for a collective approach to security in the Red Sea region, emphasizing the need for broader cooperation among countries to protect this vital maritime trade route. During a high-level security dialogue in Bahir Dar, national security adviser Redwan Hussein highlighted that the responsibility for safeguarding the Red Sea should not fall solely on coastal nations. He pointed out that Ethiopia, despite being landlocked, has significant interests in maritime security due to its reliance on regional shipping for international trade. Redwan warned of increasing instability caused by militarization, foreign rivalries, and threats to commercial shipping, which could affect not just coastal states but the entire Horn of Africa. He urged for collaborative efforts involving regional governments and international partners to address these challenges. His remarks come amid heightened tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea, underscoring the urgency for diplomatic solutions and stronger regional cooperation to ensure maritime stability and economic development.
