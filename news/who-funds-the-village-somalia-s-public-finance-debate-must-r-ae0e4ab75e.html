Who funds the village? Somalia’s public finance debate must reach local communities - Dawan Africa

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 07:58:51 GMT

Somalia's public finance debate is increasingly relevant to local communities, as the availability and quality of essential services like healthcare and education depend on effective fiscal management. The World Bank's 2026 Public Finance Review highlights the challenges of fiscal federalism, where financial responsibilities and resources are unevenly distributed among different government levels. Local authorities, tasked with frontline service delivery, often lack adequate funding, complicating their ability to meet community needs. The report emphasizes the necessity for clearer responsibilities and predictable funding to enhance local governance. As external financial support declines, Somalia must improve domestic revenue mobilization and resource allocation. The review calls for stronger cooperation between federal and state authorities to ensure that public funds are effectively managed and reach the communities that need them. Ultimately, the success of these reforms hinges on their implementation and the tangible improvements in services that citizens experience.
