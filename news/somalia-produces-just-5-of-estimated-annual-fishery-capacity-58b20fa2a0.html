Somalia Produces Just 5% of Estimated Annual Fishery Capacity - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 13:32:18 GMT

Somalia's fishing industry is currently underperforming, producing only 30,000 tonnes of fish annually, which is just 5% of its estimated capacity of 600,000 tonnes, according to a report by The EastAfrican. Abdi Dirshe, director general of the Ministry of Fisheries and Blue Economy, highlighted a significant gap of nearly 570,000 tonnes, suggesting that with proper development, the sector could generate between $1.8 billion and $3 billion in revenue and create over 210,000 jobs. The country, with a 3,333-kilometre coastline, faces challenges such as inadequate equipment, limited processing facilities, and security concerns that hinder industry growth. The government aims to attract private investment through its National Transformation Plan 2025–2029 and has introduced legal measures to encourage investment. However, exporters struggle with quality assurance and certification for international markets, while security remains a primary concern for potential investors. Experts emphasize the need for better management and environmental protection to enhance production. (160 words)
