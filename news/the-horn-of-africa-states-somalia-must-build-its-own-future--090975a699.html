The Horn Of Africa States: Somalia Must Build Its Own Future – OpEd - Eurasia Review

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 00:16:20 GMT

The Horn of Africa, particularly Somalia, faces significant challenges that require the nation to take charge of its own future. The article emphasizes the importance of self-reliance and the need for Somalia to develop its governance, economy, and social structures independently. External assistance, while valuable, should not replace the responsibility of Somali leaders and citizens to create sustainable solutions. The piece argues that Somalia must prioritize building strong institutions, fostering economic growth, and addressing internal conflicts to achieve stability. It highlights the role of regional cooperation and the necessity for Somalia to engage with its neighbors to enhance security and development. Ultimately, the article calls for a collective effort from the Somali people to shape their destiny, underscoring that true progress can only come from within. By focusing on internal capabilities and resilience, Somalia can pave the way for a brighter future. (151 words)
