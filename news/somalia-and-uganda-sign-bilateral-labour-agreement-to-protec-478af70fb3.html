Somalia and Uganda Sign Bilateral Labour Agreement to Protect Workers’ Rights - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:21:14 GMT

Somalia and Uganda have signed a bilateral labour agreement aimed at protecting workers' rights and regulating employment between the two nations. The agreement was formalized by Somalia’s Minister of Labour, Prof. Salim Alio Ibro, and Uganda’s Minister of Gender, Labour and Social Development, Lt. Gen. (Rtd.) Henry Tumukunde, during the Somali-Ugandan Business Forum in Mogadishu. This framework establishes guidelines for the recruitment of citizens, outlines employer responsibilities, and sets protections against exploitation. Both governments are committed to ensuring fair recruitment processes and enhancing the welfare of workers. The agreement also includes mechanisms for monitoring implementation and resolving disputes. This formalization addresses vulnerabilities faced by Somali and Ugandan workers, who have historically engaged in informal employment exchanges. However, the effectiveness of the agreement will depend on its implementation, given existing challenges in labour inspection and regulatory frameworks in both countries. The agreement is part of broader efforts to strengthen economic ties and cooperation between Somalia and Uganda.
