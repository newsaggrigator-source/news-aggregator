Overnight Drone, Artillery Strikes Reported Around Badme Amid Ethiopian, Eritrean Military Buildup

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 16:05:59 GMT

Intense military activity has been reported around the border town of Badme, with overnight drone strikes and heavy artillery bombardments occurring as Ethiopian and Eritrean forces bolster their positions along the shared border. Local residents described continuous explosions into Thursday morning, while a foreign diplomat indicated significant military reinforcements on both sides, raising concerns of potential clashes. The Ethiopian military has claimed its operations are in response to alleged threats from the Tigray People's Liberation Front and Eritrean forces. Meanwhile, pro-government social media accounts reported successful strikes against military supplies in the area, although these claims remain unverified. The situation has escalated following months of deteriorating relations, with both countries exchanging accusations of military provocations. The historical context of the Badme region, a flashpoint in the 1998-2000 border war, adds to the urgency of the situation, as fears of renewed conflict loom large.
