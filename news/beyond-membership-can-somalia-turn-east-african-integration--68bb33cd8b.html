Beyond membership: Can Somalia turn East African integration into jobs and public revenue? - Dawan Africa

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 08:15:33 GMT

Somalia's recent membership in the East African Community (EAC) presents significant opportunities for economic growth, but challenges remain in translating this integration into jobs and public revenue. Officially joining the EAC on March 4, 2024, Somalia now has access to a larger market of over 300 million people, which could enhance trade and investment. However, the country faces a narrow domestic revenue base, collecting only 6.3% of its GDP in 2025, compared to 15-17% in peer nations. The World Bank emphasizes the need for Somalia to strengthen its institutions, align laws with EAC frameworks, and improve customs administration to fully benefit from regional integration. While increased trade could stimulate economic activity, it must be accompanied by a rise in local production and effective tax administration. Ultimately, the success of EAC membership will depend on Somalia's ability to create a productive economy that generates sustainable revenue and job opportunities for its citizens.
