Uganda and Ethiopia Deepen Cooperation On Strategic Diaspora Mobilisation, Investment, and Knowledge Transfer

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 10:19:46 GMT

Uganda and Ethiopia are enhancing their cooperation in diaspora mobilization, investment, and knowledge transfer ahead of the 3rd Annual Uganda Diaspora Convention scheduled for December 2026. A Ugandan delegation, led by Minister Hon. Amos Lugoloobi, engaged in bilateral talks with Ethiopian officials to learn from Ethiopia's successful diaspora engagement strategies. The discussions focused on adapting Ethiopia's frameworks to operationalize Uganda's National Diaspora Support and Engagement Policy. Hon. Lugoloobi emphasized the importance of moving beyond remittances to leverage diaspora contributions for national projects and technology transfer. Ethiopian Ambassador Lencho Bati highlighted the role of diaspora in fostering national cohesion and economic transformation, advocating for proactive engagement and recognition of their contributions. Insights from this mission will inform legislative proposals and financial products aimed at enhancing Uganda's diaspora engagement. Following Ethiopia, the delegation plans to benchmark in India, with findings to be presented at the upcoming convention in December. (158 words)
