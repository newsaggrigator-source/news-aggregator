Strategic Location Qualifies Ethiopia to Invigorate Trade Connectivity in Continent - Egyptian Comesa Expert

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 05:16:58 GMT

An Egyptian expert from the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA) has highlighted Ethiopia's strategic location as a crucial link for trade connectivity across Eastern, Western, and Central Africa. Neamatalla Azzam Mohamed emphasized that Ethiopia, referred to as a "Land of Origin," is positioned to enhance trade relations within the continent. She noted the country’s significant investment opportunities, particularly in transport and logistics, which are essential for facilitating trade. COMESA is actively promoting mutual recognition agreements among member states to reduce technical barriers and improve product movement. So far, agreements have been signed between Kenya and Uganda, and Zambia and Malawi, with another planned between Rwanda and Zambia. Mohamed also indicated that COMESA aims to establish similar agreements with Ethiopia, enhancing its trade relations. The organization is committed to promoting free trade under the Duty-Free, Quota-Free Trade framework, advocating for harmonized trade standards across Africa to strengthen economic integration.
