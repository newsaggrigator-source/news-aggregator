Ethiopia faces risk of regional war ahead of COP32 climate summit - Ynetnews

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 10:18:04 GMT

Ethiopia is facing escalating tensions with regional rivals Eritrea and Sudan, raising concerns about potential conflict ahead of the COP32 climate summit scheduled for November 2027 in Addis Ababa. As Ethiopia prepares to host this significant event, which aims to position the country as a leader in climate finance and renewable energy, the deteriorating security situation poses a challenge to its diplomatic ambitions. Recent clashes near the contested Badme region and strained relations with Eritrea have heightened fears of direct confrontation. Additionally, Ethiopia's quest for access to the Red Sea has sparked opposition from Somalia, complicating regional dynamics. The African Union has called for restraint, but the involvement of other nations, including Egypt and Sudan, adds pressure on Ethiopia. The COP32 summit could serve as a platform for dialogue, but it also places Ethiopia under international scrutiny, making it imperative for the government to manage regional tensions to ensure the event's success and maintain its credibility.
