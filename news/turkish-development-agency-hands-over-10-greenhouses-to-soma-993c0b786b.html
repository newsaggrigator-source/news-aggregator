Turkish development agency hands over 10 greenhouses to Somalia’s Agriculture Ministry - A News

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 21:34:00 GMT

The Turkish Cooperation and Coordination Agency (TIKA) has officially handed over 10 greenhouses to Somalia's Agriculture and Irrigation Ministry, along with irrigation systems and farming inputs, to enhance food security in the country. This initiative is part of a broader effort to reduce food imports and create job opportunities for the youth in Somalia. The recent handover builds on a previous donation of 10 greenhouses to young women, bringing the total to 20. TIKA has been a significant development partner for Somalia since establishing its office in Mogadishu in 2011, contributing over $1 billion in humanitarian and technical assistance across various sectors, including agriculture, health, and education. The partnership between Somalia and Türkiye has strengthened since 2011, reflecting a commitment to improving livelihoods and supporting Somali institutions.
