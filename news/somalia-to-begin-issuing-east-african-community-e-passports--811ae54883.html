Somalia to begin issuing East African Community e-passports in January 2027 - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 28 Nov 2026 01:00:31 GMT

Somalia is set to begin issuing East African Community (EAC) electronic passports in January 2027, following the completion of production for this new travel document. This initiative aims to integrate Somalia's immigration system with those of other East African nations. The announcement was made after a meeting of EAC immigration chiefs in Kampala, where Somali officials confirmed the final preparations for the passport's rollout. The new e-passport will be linked to Somalia's national identification system and will be available through Somali diplomatic missions abroad. It incorporates modern security features compliant with International Civil Aviation Organization standards and reflects Somalia's national identity. This move is part of Somalia's broader efforts to modernize its immigration services and align with regional standards, facilitating trade and mobility within the EAC. Somalia, which joined the EAC in March 2024, is committed to enhancing regional integration and cooperation among member states.
