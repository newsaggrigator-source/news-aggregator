Somalia Participates in African Skills Policy Forum Amid Ongoing Domestic Youth Unemployment Challenges - FTL Somalia

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:18:30 GMT

Somalia's Minister of Labour and Social Affairs, Prof. Salim Alio Ibro, attended the African Skills Policy Forum in Oran, Algeria, on November 18, 2026, alongside 28 other delegations. The forum focused on workforce development and technical training, addressing significant challenges in youth employment across the continent. Key discussions included modernizing national skills policies and enhancing technical and vocational education and training (TVET) curricula to align with market demands. Somalia, facing a youth demographic crisis with over two-thirds of its population under 30, is under pressure to improve vocational pathways. Despite participation in continental initiatives, Somalia's vocational training infrastructure remains severely underfunded and fragmented, with a lack of qualified instructors and outdated equipment. The government has engaged with international partners to rebuild technical institutes and create a national strategy for economic ties with Africa. However, without adequate funding and regulatory reforms, the gap between high-level policy frameworks and actual employment outcomes for Somali youth persists.
