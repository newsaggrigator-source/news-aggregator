Young Innovator Aims to 'Solve Ethiopia's Medical Oxygen Shortages'

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 13:21:19 GMT

Mudhataa Bariisoo, a 30-year-old biomedical engineering technician from Adola Hospital in Ethiopia, has developed an innovative medical oxygen production and purification device named Abayitu. This device addresses the ongoing oxygen shortages in Ethiopian healthcare facilities by supplying oxygen directly to the hospital's medical gas pipeline network. Mudhataa's invention, which uses minimal electrical power to extract oxygen from atmospheric air, earned him second place in a national competition, where he competed against over 1,000 participants. He received a cash prize of 500,000 birr and access to a loan facility to expand production. With nine years of experience at Adola Hospital, Mudhataa was motivated to create a reliable, locally produced oxygen source after witnessing the challenges posed by conventional oxygen cylinders. Abayitu has been operational for about a year, producing 30 cubic meters of oxygen per hour, significantly improving the hospital's oxygen supply reliability. Mudhataa plans to officially launch commercial manufacturing of the device in April 2027. (159 words)
