The Somalia-Djibouti Parliamentary MoU: Where is Somaliland in the Equation? - horn review

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 13:34:49 GMT

The recent signing of a parliamentary Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Somalia and Djibouti has been framed as a reflection of their fraternal ties and shared security interests. However, this perspective overlooks the underlying economic motivations, particularly concerning Somaliland. Djibouti's support for Somalia's territorial integrity is driven by its desire to maintain its port monopoly and prevent Somaliland from emerging as a commercial competitor, especially with Ethiopia seeking alternative trade routes. The MoU, which includes provisions for legislative exchanges and training, highlights Somalia's ongoing institutional weaknesses and reliance on external partners for security and legitimacy. The agreement does not address Somalia's internal governance issues or the contentious recognition of Somaliland, instead serving to reinforce existing dynamics that favor Djibouti's interests. Ultimately, the MoU represents continuity rather than progress, allowing both nations to claim diplomatic success while preserving the status quo that benefits the stronger partner.
