Ethiopia Reaffirms Commitment to Peace and Security in South Sudan - ENA English

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 15:23:19 GMT

Ethiopia has reaffirmed its commitment to fostering peace and security in South Sudan, as stated by State Minister of Foreign Affairs Ambassador Hadera Abera. This commitment was expressed during a meeting with Anita Kiki Gbeho, the Special Representative of the United Nations Secretary-General for South Sudan and Head of the United Nations Mission in South Sudan (UNMISS). The discussions focused on the upcoming elections in South Sudan, the current political and security landscape, and the implementation of the Revitalized Agreement on the Resolution of the Conflict in South Sudan (R-ARCSS). Ambassador Hadera highlighted Ethiopia's support for UNMISS and its mandate, emphasizing the importance of collaboration with South Sudanese stakeholders and regional and international partners to achieve sustainable peace and security. The meeting reflects Ethiopia's ongoing role in addressing the operational challenges faced by UNMISS and its commitment to the stability of South Sudan. (157 words)
