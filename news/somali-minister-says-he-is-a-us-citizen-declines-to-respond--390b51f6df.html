Somali Minister Says He Is a US Citizen, Declines to Respond to Trump’s ‘Garbage’ Remarks - Garowe Online

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 09:06:56 GMT

Somalia's State Minister for Foreign Affairs, Ali Omar (Ali Balcad), has publicly identified himself as a U.S. citizen while addressing derogatory comments made by President Donald Trump regarding Somalia and its immigrants. In an interview with Al Jazeera English, Balcad chose not to engage with Trump's remarks, which included calling Somali immigrants "garbage" and describing Somalia as "filthy" and "plagued by crime." He emphasized that Trump was elected by American citizens and thus holds the presidency. When asked if Somalis were offended by Trump's comments, Balcad refrained from responding directly, indicating that the focus should not be on the president's statements. Trump's history of controversial remarks about Somalia and its diaspora has sparked discussions about the perception of Somali immigrants in the U.S. Balcad's stance reflects a complex relationship between national identity and the political rhetoric surrounding immigration.
