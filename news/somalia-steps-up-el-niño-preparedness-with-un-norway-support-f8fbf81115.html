Somalia steps up El Niño preparedness with UN, Norway support - Shabelle Media Network

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 06:40:54 GMT

Somalia is enhancing its preparedness for the El Niño phenomenon with support from the United Nations and Norway. The country is taking proactive measures to mitigate the potential impacts of the climate event, which is expected to bring heavy rainfall and could lead to flooding and other challenges. The collaboration aims to strengthen Somalia's resilience against climate-related disasters, ensuring that communities are better equipped to handle the adverse effects. Authorities are focusing on improving early warning systems and response strategies to safeguard vulnerable populations. This initiative highlights the importance of international cooperation in addressing climate change and its repercussions on food security and livelihoods in Somalia.
