UPDF starts Somalia exit as financial pressures, diplomatic shifts reshape peace mission - Daily Monitor

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 01:09:50 GMT

Uganda has officially begun the withdrawal of its troops, the Uganda People’s Defence Forces (UPDF), from Somalia after nearly two decades of military involvement. This decision, confirmed by Chief of Defence Forces Gen Muhoozi Kainerugaba, marks a significant transition in the security landscape of Somalia as the UPDF hands over responsibilities to Somali forces. The withdrawal is influenced by financial constraints and diplomatic changes affecting the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM). Notably, the United States has indicated it will not support the renewal of the mission's mandate, coupled with a 25% funding cut from the United Nations. Despite these challenges, Ugandan President Museveni expressed confidence in African nations' ability to manage their security independently. The UPDF has played a crucial role in combating the Al-Shabaab militant group and supporting the Somali National Army, contributing to the stabilization of the region over the years.
