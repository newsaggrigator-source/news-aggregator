ATALANTA, EUTM Somalia Strengthen Ties with Somali Coast Guard Aboard Flagship - FTL Somalia

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 15:07:42 GMT

On November 17, 2026, the European Union Naval Force Operation ATALANTA hosted Colonel Mohamed Abdulqadir “Shariif,” the Commander of the Somali Navy and Coast Guard, aboard its flagship to enhance maritime cooperation and operational capabilities. The visit, welcomed by ATALANTA Force Commander Commodore José Rodrigues Pedra and EUTM Somalia's Acting Mission Force Commander Colonel Mattias Johnsson, aimed to strengthen the Somali maritime forces through knowledge-sharing and practical exercises. This engagement comes amid a resurgence of piracy off Somalia's coast, with at least 15 attacks reported since April, highlighting the Somali Navy's limitations in patrolling its extensive coastline. Despite international support, including training from Türkiye, the Somali Navy remains underfunded and reliant on external assistance. The visit underscores the ongoing partnership between ATALANTA and Somali forces, but it also reveals the critical need for Somalia to invest in its maritime security to effectively combat piracy and secure its waters independently.
