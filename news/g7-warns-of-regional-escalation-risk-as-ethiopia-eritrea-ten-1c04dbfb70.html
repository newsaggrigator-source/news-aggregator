G7 Warns of Regional Escalation Risk As Ethiopia-Eritrea Tensions Worsen

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 15:25:21 GMT

The G7 foreign ministers have raised alarms over escalating tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea, warning that the situation could destabilize the Horn of Africa and threaten regional security, humanitarian efforts, and international trade, particularly in the Red Sea. In a joint statement issued after their meeting in Paris on November 9-10, 2026, they expressed deep concern regarding the deteriorating security landscape and the risk of regional escalation due to rising cross-border tensions. The ministers urged all parties to exercise restraint, seek diplomatic solutions, and ensure safe humanitarian access while protecting civilians, especially women and children. The G7 reaffirmed the importance of respecting the sovereignty and territorial integrity of all regional nations and supported UN and African Union efforts to facilitate dialogue. The tensions stem from Ethiopia's demand for access to the Red Sea through Eritrea's port of Assab, which Eritrea has firmly rejected, raising fears of renewed conflict despite a peace agreement established in 2018.
