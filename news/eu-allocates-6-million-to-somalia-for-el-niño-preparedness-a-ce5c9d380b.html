EU Allocates €6 Million to Somalia for El Niño Preparedness as Flood Risks Mount - FTL Somalia

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 15:20:43 GMT

The European Union has allocated €6 million to Somalia as part of a larger €13.5 million emergency funding package aimed at addressing the anticipated impacts of the 2026-2027 El Niño event across Eastern and Southern Africa. This funding is intended to enhance evacuation site preparedness, provide emergency cash assistance, and support health and nutrition services. The urgency of this allocation arises from forecasts predicting that this El Niño could be the strongest on record, with up to 3.4 million people in Somalia at risk of flooding. Despite this funding, the scale of need far exceeds the resources available; the International Organization for Migration (IOM) has indicated a requirement of $16.62 million for flood preparedness, of which only 14.4% has been secured. The Somali Disaster Management Agency has placed 29 districts on high alert, highlighting the critical situation as flooding has already begun to displace families. The EU's funding, while a positive step, remains insufficient to fully address the looming crisis.
