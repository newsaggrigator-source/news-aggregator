African Political Parties Summit Delegates Witness Addis Ababa's Urban Transformation

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 04:20:09 GMT

Delegates attending the African Political Parties Summit in Addis Ababa explored the city's significant urban transformation, showcasing its development into a modern and inclusive metropolis. Adam Farah, Deputy Prime Minister and Vice President of the Prosperity Party, highlighted Addis Ababa as Africa's political heart, emphasizing the importance of unity and cooperation among African nations. He invited delegates to appreciate Ethiopia's hospitality and cultural richness, hoping the summit would yield impactful outcomes for a prosperous Africa. The delegates toured major urban projects, gaining insights into Ethiopia's integrated urban development approach, which focuses on improved infrastructure and pedestrian-friendly spaces. Their visit included museum tours and presentations detailing the city's development phases. The program concluded with a cultural reception featuring traditional music and performances, which received positive feedback from the delegates, reflecting Ethiopia's diverse heritage. This event was part of the Second African Political Parties Summit, themed "Governing for Growth: Political Parties as Architects of Africa's Economic Future." (158 words)
