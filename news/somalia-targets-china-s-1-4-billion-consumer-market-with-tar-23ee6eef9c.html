Somalia targets China’s 1.4 billion-consumer market with tariff-free access - Dawan Africa

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 13:35:12 GMT

Somalia has launched its first national pavilion at the China International Import Expo in Shanghai, aiming to access China's vast consumer market of 1.4 billion people with tariff-free benefits. The initiative, led by Commerce and Industry Minister Jamal Mohamed Hassan, showcases Somali products such as sesame, frankincense, and myrrh. Since December 2024, China has granted zero-tariff access to Somalia and 32 other least developed African nations, a policy extended to all 53 African countries with diplomatic ties in May. Somalia's ambassador to China, Hodan Osman Abdi, emphasized that this access allows Somali goods to be more competitively priced than those from other regions. The pavilion not only highlights agricultural exports but also promotes investment opportunities in livestock, fisheries, and minerals. The expo, running until November 10, provides Somali traders a platform to connect with potential buyers and explore new markets, marking a significant step in Somalia's industrialization efforts.
