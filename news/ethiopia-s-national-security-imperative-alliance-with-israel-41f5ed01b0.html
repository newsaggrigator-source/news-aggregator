Ethiopia's national security imperative: Alliance with Israel and Somaliland - The Jerusalem Post

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 14:00:00 GMT

Ethiopia is strengthening its national security by forging alliances with Israel and Somaliland. This strategic partnership aims to enhance military cooperation and intelligence sharing, addressing regional threats and instability. The collaboration with Israel is particularly significant, as it provides Ethiopia with advanced technology and expertise in counter-terrorism efforts. Meanwhile, the alliance with Somaliland is focused on bolstering security in the Horn of Africa, where Ethiopia faces challenges from various militant groups. These alliances are seen as essential for Ethiopia to safeguard its interests and maintain stability in a volatile region. The Ethiopian government emphasizes that these partnerships will contribute to a more secure and prosperous future for the nation.
