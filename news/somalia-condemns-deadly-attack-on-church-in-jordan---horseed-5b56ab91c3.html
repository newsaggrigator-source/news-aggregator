Somalia condemns deadly attack on church in Jordan - Horseed Media

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 15:51:36 GMT

Somalia has condemned a deadly attack on a Greek Orthodox church in Jordan's Karak governorate, expressing solidarity with the Jordanian government and the victims' families. The attack occurred during a funeral in Hmoud village, resulting in eight fatalities and numerous injuries, with the death toll rising after one critically injured victim succumbed to their wounds. Somalia's Foreign Ministry issued a statement denouncing the terrorist act, emphasizing the country’s understanding of the human cost of terrorism and its rejection of violence and extremism. The attack has prompted Jordan to declare three days of national mourning, with flags at government institutions flown at half-staff. Jordanian authorities have labeled the shooting a terrorist attack, with ongoing efforts to identify and apprehend the perpetrators. King Abdullah II and Prime Minister Jafar Hassan condemned the violence, asserting that it is an affront to all Jordanians. The incident has garnered widespread condemnation from various governments and international organizations as investigations continue.
