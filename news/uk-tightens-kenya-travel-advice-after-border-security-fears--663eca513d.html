UK Tightens Kenya Travel Advice After Border Security Fears - https://www.thetraveler.org/

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 05:16:13 GMT

The UK government has updated its travel advice for Kenya, expanding areas to "avoid travel" near the Ethiopia border due to security concerns linked to unrest. The Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) now advises against all but essential travel to Moyale Town and its surrounding 5-kilometer radius, citing increased violence from the Ethiopian conflict. This revision follows earlier warnings about the Kenya-Somalia border and highlights the ongoing threat of terrorism, particularly from groups like Al Shabaab. While popular tourist destinations like Nairobi and the Maasai Mara remain unaffected, travelers are urged to stay vigilant and avoid political gatherings. The updated advice also impacts travel insurance, which may be void if travelers enter restricted areas. As the peak safari season approaches, British tourists are advised to confirm their itineraries with tour operators and ensure they have adequate insurance coverage. The advisory reflects a careful balance between security concerns and the importance of Kenya as a travel destination.
