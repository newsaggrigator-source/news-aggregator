Jordan Must Retire, Partey Is Irreplaceable: Social media reacts to Black Stars' win over Somalia - GhanaWeb

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 16:02:12 GMT

The Black Stars of Ghana secured a convincing victory over Somalia, leading to a flurry of reactions on social media. Fans and analysts expressed their opinions on various aspects of the match, particularly highlighting the performance of key players. Many supporters called for veteran goalkeeper Richard Kingson, often referred to as Jordan, to retire, suggesting that it is time for new talent to take the reins in the national team. Conversely, Thomas Partey received widespread praise for his irreplaceable role in the squad, with many fans emphasizing his importance in both defense and midfield. The match not only showcased the team's strengths but also sparked discussions about the future of the Black Stars, with calls for a blend of experience and youth to ensure continued success. Overall, the victory was seen as a positive step forward for Ghanaian football, igniting hope for upcoming competitions.
