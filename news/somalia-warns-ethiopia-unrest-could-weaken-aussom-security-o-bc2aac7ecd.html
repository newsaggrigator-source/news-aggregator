Somalia warns Ethiopia unrest could weaken AUSSOM security operations - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 07:00:17 GMT

Somalia has expressed concerns that escalating unrest in Ethiopia could jeopardize security operations within its borders, particularly those involving the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM). State Minister for Foreign Affairs Ali Mohamed Omar Balcad emphasized the interconnectedness of the two nations' security, citing their extensive border and historical ties. He warned that ongoing instability in Ethiopia might compel the Ethiopian government to withdraw troops from AUSSOM to address domestic issues, thereby weakening efforts against the militant group al-Shabab. Ethiopian forces play a crucial role in AUSSOM, which includes around 2,500 troops from Ethiopia, Uganda, Kenya, Djibouti, and Egypt. The warning comes amid renewed violence in Ethiopia, particularly in the Tigray and Amhara regions, raising fears for ethnic Somalis in Ethiopia's Somali Region. Balcad highlighted the need for coordinated efforts to prevent security gaps as Somalia transitions to greater national security responsibility.
