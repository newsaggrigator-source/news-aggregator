COP32 Ethiopia Crucial Platform for Global South Consensus Beyond Climate Finance

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 08:28:43 GMT

Ethiopia's upcoming COP32 in 2027 is seen as a pivotal opportunity for Global South nations to unify their negotiating positions and expand climate discussions beyond mere financing, according to Manssour Bin Mussallam, Secretary-General of the Organization of Southern Cooperation (OSC). He highlighted that the conference could redefine the narrative surrounding developing countries, positioning them as proactive contributors to climate solutions rather than just victims of climate change. Bin Mussallam emphasized the need for strategic cooperation among resource-rich nations to enhance their roles in renewable energy technology production, advocating for a shift from exporting raw materials to developing local industries. He urged developed countries to complement climate finance with investments in industrial development in the Global South, which could accelerate the clean energy transition and create jobs. COP32 aims to align climate adaptation with Africa's development priorities, addressing issues like food security and sustainable energy while fostering economic growth.
