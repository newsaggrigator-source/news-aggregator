ENDC–IFA Policy Dialogue Concludes with a Focus on Ethiopia’s National Security and Strategic Interests - IFA - Institute of Foreign Affairs

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 15:16:07 GMT

The Ethiopian National Defence College (ENDC) and the Institute of Foreign Affairs (IFA) recently concluded a policy dialogue titled “Unity or Betrayal? Where Is the Anti-Ethiopia Coalition Headed?” This event gathered policymakers, security experts, and researchers to discuss the emerging security challenges in the Horn of Africa and their implications for Ethiopia's national interests. Key topics included evolving political alliances, insurgent movements, and the geopolitical landscape, particularly concerning Ethiopia–Eritrea relations. Participants emphasized the necessity of safeguarding Ethiopia’s sovereignty and territorial integrity through enhanced defense capabilities and coordinated policy responses. The dialogue highlighted the importance of national unity, internal stability, and peaceful conflict resolution to mitigate vulnerabilities to external pressures. Additionally, it underscored the significance of proactive diplomacy and regional cooperation, particularly regarding Ethiopia's maritime access and the strategic importance of the Red Sea. The discussions concluded with a call for translating strategic assessments into actionable diplomatic and institutional efforts to promote Ethiopia's long-term interests and regional stability.
