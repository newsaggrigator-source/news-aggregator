A State Cannot Be Held By A Port Alone: Lessons From Puntland’s First Crisis – OpEd - Eurasia Review

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 17:05:40 GMT

The article discusses the recent crisis in Puntland, Somalia, highlighting the limitations of relying solely on port revenues for state stability. It emphasizes that while the port of Bosaso is a significant economic asset, the region's governance and security depend on broader factors, including political cohesion, social services, and effective leadership. The crisis revealed vulnerabilities in Puntland's administration, as the reliance on port income failed to address underlying issues such as clan tensions and public discontent. The author argues that sustainable development requires a diversified economy and inclusive governance, rather than dependence on a single revenue source. The lessons from Puntland's experience serve as a cautionary tale for other regions facing similar challenges, underscoring the need for comprehensive strategies to ensure long-term stability and resilience.
