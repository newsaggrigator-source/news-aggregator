Sudan complains to UN Security Council over drone attacks from Ethiopia - Kuwait Times

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 10:47:53 GMT

Sudan has lodged complaints with the UN Security Council and the African Union, accusing Ethiopia of permitting the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) to launch drone strikes on Sudanese civilian targets from its territory. The Sudanese military claims that 187 drones have been launched from Ethiopia this year, resulting in nearly 500 civilian casualties from attacks on residential areas, hospitals, and infrastructure. Sudan's foreign ministry described these actions as "blatant aggression" and a violation of international law. In response, Ethiopia has denied the allegations and accused Sudan of supporting rebels associated with the Tigray People’s Liberation Front (TPLF). The ongoing conflict between Sudan’s military and the RSF, which began in April 2023, has escalated, with drone warfare becoming a significant aspect of the hostilities. The United Nations reported over 1,100 deaths from drone attacks in Sudan during the first half of the year, exacerbating the humanitarian crisis.
