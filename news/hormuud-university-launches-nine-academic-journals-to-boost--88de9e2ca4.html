Hormuud University Launches Nine Academic Journals to Boost Research in Somalia - Dawan Africa

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 12:54:32 GMT

Hormuud University in Mogadishu has launched nine academic journals aimed at enhancing research and academic publishing in Somalia. This initiative, announced on October 22, 2026, is designed to provide a dedicated platform for academics and researchers to publish their findings, thereby increasing the overall research output in the country. Professor Abdi Omar Shuuriye, the university's chairman, emphasized that the primary goal of these journals is to promote scientific research and establish a robust academic publishing framework that serves Somalia's needs. The journals are expected to facilitate the dissemination of research findings, advance knowledge, and encourage studies that address societal challenges. Additionally, this initiative aims to strengthen the connection between research and higher education, motivating academics to produce work that contributes to the nation's development. Hormuud University views this launch as a significant step towards fostering scientific research and enhancing the academic landscape in Somalia, ultimately benefiting the country's growth and progress.
