Somalia’s public spending squeeze: What does it mean for families seeking healthcare and education? - Dawan Africa

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 08:05:32 GMT

Somalia is facing a significant public spending squeeze that directly impacts families seeking healthcare and education. The World Bank's 2026 Public Finance Review highlights that government expenditure is only about 11.3% of GDP, which is low compared to regional peers. This financial constraint is exacerbated by a narrow domestic revenue base and declining external assistance, projected to drop from 5% of GDP to below 1% by 2030. Health and education spending is alarmingly low, with only 0.2% and 0.5% of GDP allocated, respectively. The report emphasizes the need for improved revenue collection and more efficient public spending to address these challenges. Additionally, it points out the importance of clear accountability and effective resource distribution among various government levels. As Somalia navigates these fiscal challenges, the central question remains whether it can enhance service delivery and ensure that public funds translate into tangible benefits for its citizens.
