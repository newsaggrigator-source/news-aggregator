Ethiopia Promotes Aviation Achievements, Pursues Stronger Global Representation

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 05:42:19 GMT

Ethiopia is actively promoting its aviation achievements at the 7th Worldwide Air Transport Conference in Montreal, Canada, which is organized by the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO). Led by Ambassador Tewodros Girma, the Ethiopian delegation aims to showcase the country's growing aviation capabilities and its contributions to the sector in Africa and globally. This participation is also strategically important as Ethiopia seeks support for its candidacy for a position on the ICAO Council, with elections scheduled for later this week. The country has set up a dedicated pavilion at the conference to exhibit its technical expertise and achievements in aviation. The event gathers global aviation stakeholders to discuss developments and challenges in the industry, and Ethiopia's involvement is part of its broader goal to enhance its influence in international aviation governance and contribute to shaping the future of civil aviation worldwide.
