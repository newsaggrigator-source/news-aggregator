Somalia seeks $6.3 billion in climate adaptation funding through 2035 - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 06:38:01 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has announced the country's need for $6.3 billion in climate adaptation funding through 2035, emphasizing the urgency for disaster preparedness funds and easier access to international climate financing. Speaking at the 31st United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Turkey, he urged wealthy nations to honor their climate commitments and support countries like Somalia, which, despite contributing minimally to global greenhouse gas emissions, faces severe climate vulnerabilities. He highlighted the importance of early financing to help communities prepare for disasters, thereby reducing humanitarian and economic impacts. Additionally, he called for climate funding to be primarily in the form of grants, warning that loans could exacerbate existing financial pressures. Somalia has been severely affected by climate-related emergencies, including droughts and floods, which threaten its rain-fed agriculture and livestock-dependent economy. Access to reliable climate financing is crucial for enhancing resilience, improving water management, and strengthening disaster preparedness in the country.
