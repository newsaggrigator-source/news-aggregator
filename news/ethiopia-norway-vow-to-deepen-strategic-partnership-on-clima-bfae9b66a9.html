Ethiopia, Norway Vow to Deepen Strategic Partnership On Climate Action and Sustainable Development

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 05:43:36 GMT

Ethiopia and Norway have committed to enhancing their strategic partnership focused on climate action and sustainable development. In a recent bilateral meeting, Ethiopian Finance Minister Fitsum Assefa and Norwegian Minister of International Development Åsmund Grøver Aukrust discussed cooperation in climate finance, forest conservation, and multilateral development partnerships. They explored opportunities to mobilize additional resources for Ethiopia's climate priorities, including land restoration and the Green Legacy Initiative. The ministers also addressed preparations for a significant carbon-credit transaction and afforestation initiative, aligning with Ethiopia's goals for COP32 in 2027. State Minister Tiguist Fisseha highlighted the importance of Norway's support through bilateral cooperation and multilateral development banks, particularly the World Bank's International Development Association. Both nations agreed to strengthen their collaboration on climate change and sustainable development, aiming to support Ethiopia's development ambitions while contributing to global climate objectives. This partnership underscores a shared commitment to addressing environmental challenges and promoting sustainable practices. (160 words)
