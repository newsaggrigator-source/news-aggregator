Oman’s Embassy Reopens in Mogadishu as Al-Umairi Presents Credentials - FTL Somalia

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 07:35:51 GMT

Oman's embassy in Mogadishu has officially reopened after 36 years, marking a significant restoration of diplomatic relations between Oman and Somalia. On October 25, 2026, Somali Foreign Minister Abdisalam Abdi Ali received the credentials of Oman’s new ambassador, Salim bin Habib Al-Umairi. This reopening is seen as a demonstration of Oman’s confidence in the current Somali government and its commitment to Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity. The two nations have been strengthening ties over the past two years, with discussions on economic cooperation, including trade and investment opportunities. The embassy's reopening not only facilitates direct diplomatic engagement but also enhances trade and consular services, which were previously managed through third countries. However, the future of this relationship will depend on translating diplomatic momentum into tangible economic benefits, as the current trade volume remains modest. The reopening is viewed as a crucial step in Somalia's diplomatic landscape amidst complex regional dynamics.
