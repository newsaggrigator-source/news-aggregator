AU Commission Chief Urges Parties to Focus On Serving Citizens and Strengthening Nat'l Interests

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 12:26:52 GMT

At the second African Political Parties Summit 2026 in Addis Ababa, AU Commission Chairperson Mahmoud Ali Youssouf urged political parties to prioritize serving citizens and enhancing national interests over merely seeking power. He emphasized the critical role of political parties in fostering democratic governance, peace, and development aligned with the African Union's Agenda 2063. Youssouf outlined three key responsibilities for political parties: crafting policies that benefit citizens, bridging the gap between citizens and state institutions, and raising awareness of national interests. He also highlighted the importance of opposition parties in ensuring government accountability and the necessity for constitutional governance. Stressing the need for transparency in political financing, he called for strict regulations to combat corruption. Youssouf advocated for inclusive political participation, particularly for women and youth, and emphasized the importance of consensus on development priorities like education and infrastructure. He reaffirmed the AU's commitment to promoting democracy and stability across member states. (158 words)
