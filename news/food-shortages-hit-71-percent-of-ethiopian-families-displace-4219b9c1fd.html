Food Shortages Hit 71 Percent of Ethiopian Families Displaced Into Kenya's Moyale

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 15:55:19 GMT

A recent assessment by the International Organization for Migration (IOM) reveals that 71% of Ethiopian families displaced into Kenya's Moyale are facing severe food shortages. Approximately 50,000 individuals fled escalating conflict in Ethiopia's Borana region, arriving with minimal belongings and relying on already strained host communities for basic needs. The violence erupted on October 18 over a dispute regarding a regional administration office, leading to armed confrontations and significant civilian casualties. The IOM's survey indicated that 80% of displaced families identified food as their most urgent need, with many lacking access to clean water and healthcare. Conditions are particularly dire in temporary displacement sites, where access to sanitation is limited. The IOM and humanitarian partners are urgently calling for funding to provide food assistance, emergency shelter, clean water, and healthcare to both displaced families and their hosts, as the situation continues to deteriorate.
