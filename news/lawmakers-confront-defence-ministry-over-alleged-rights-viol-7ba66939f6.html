Lawmakers Confront Defence Ministry Over Alleged Rights Violations in the Army - FTL Somalia

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 15:53:19 GMT

Lawmakers in Somalia's Defence Committee held a session with Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi to address concerns over alleged rights violations within the army, including salary deductions for soldiers and the welfare of orphans and disabled veterans. The meeting, led by Acting Committee Chairman MP Abdirazak Hassan Dhookir, highlighted the unacceptable treatment of military personnel who have sacrificed for the nation. The committee also discussed the ongoing fight against Al-Shabaab and the need for military professionalism, emphasizing the importance of keeping the armed forces free from political interference. This session is part of a broader parliamentary effort to oversee the security sector and push for reforms, including a review of the flawed military promotion system. While the Defence Ministry has pledged accountability, the effectiveness of these discussions will depend on the committee's ability to ensure follow-through on commitments made to improve the conditions for soldiers and veterans.
