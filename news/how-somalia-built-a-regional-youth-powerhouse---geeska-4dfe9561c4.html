How Somalia Built a Regional Youth Powerhouse - Geeska

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 11:03:31 GMT

Somalia's U17 football team has emerged as a regional powerhouse, showcasing significant progress through extensive training camps and nationwide player selection. Their recent performance in the CECAFA U17 tournament, where they reached the finals, highlights this development. Despite narrowly losing to Uganda in a penalty shootout, Somalia's journey has been marked by a commitment to youth football, particularly since the success of the 2006-born generation. The Somali Football Federation has prioritized this age group, organizing long training camps and scouting across the country. Key players like Mohamud Mohamed Osman and Abdullahi Bashir have excelled in local leagues, contributing to the team's success. Under the guidance of coach Daadir Amin Ali, the squad is preparing for the upcoming U17 Africa Cup of Nations in Morocco, where they have a historic opportunity to qualify for the FIFA U-17 World Cup. With a strong local base and potential diaspora additions, Somalia aims to capitalize on this momentum.
