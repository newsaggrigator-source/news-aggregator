Somalia repatriates 169 citizens from Libyan prisons - Horseed Media

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:29:25 GMT

Somalia has successfully repatriated 169 citizens from Libyan prisons, as confirmed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The returnees arrived through the Voluntary Humanitarian Return programme, with 140 landing in Mogadishu and 29 in Hargeisa. This initiative, supported by the European Union and implemented by the International Organization for Migration (IOM), aims to provide a safe and dignified return for migrants, allowing them to reunite with their families and rebuild their lives. Ibrahim Guure, a ministry official, welcomed the returnees in Mogadishu. This repatriation is part of ongoing efforts to address irregular migration, particularly among young Somalis. The IOM has facilitated over 2,000 returns since August 2022, highlighting the challenges faced by Somali migrants in Libya, a key transit route for those attempting to reach Europe. The programme emphasizes voluntary participation, informed decision-making, and post-arrival support for returnees.
