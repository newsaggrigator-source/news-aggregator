Somalia Marks World Fisheries Day With Pledge Against Illegal Fishing - Dawan Africa

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 07:55:38 GMT

On November 22, 2026, Somalia's Ministry of Fisheries and Blue Economy commemorated World Fisheries Day by committing to enhance efforts against illegal fishing and to safeguard the nation's marine resources. The ministry emphasized the need to combat illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing, which poses a significant threat to sustainable marine management. Additionally, it pledged to improve seafood quality and safety standards to facilitate Somali fish products' access to international markets while supporting small-scale fishers, women, and youth in the fisheries sector. With a coastline of 3,898 kilometers, the longest in mainland Africa, Somalia's Exclusive Economic Zone spans over 830,000 square kilometers, housing more than 112 fishing villages. The ministry highlighted the critical role of the sea in ensuring food security and economic stability, expressing gratitude to Somali fishers who contribute to their communities' livelihoods. World Fisheries Day served as a platform to underscore the importance of marine resource protection for future generations.
