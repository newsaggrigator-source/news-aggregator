Somalia: UAE carries out reported drone strike against al-Shabab in Puntland - Horseed Media

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 16:02:25 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has reportedly conducted its first drone strike against al-Shabab militants in Puntland, Somalia, in nearly a year, according to security officials. The strike occurred on October 22 at the Galgala mountain pass in the Al-Madow mountain range, an area where the al-Qaeda-linked group has maintained a presence. This operation follows reconnaissance missions conducted by UAE drones after an al-Shabab explosive device targeted Puntland security forces. The drones, launched from a UAE military base at Bosaso airport, targeted four militants allegedly planting roadside bombs, injuring two. Puntland paramilitary forces later killed the wounded militants. This strike marks the UAE's first military action against al-Shabab since November 2025 and comes amid rising political tensions in Somalia, where some regional administrations oppose the federal government's plans for national elections in 2027. The federal government in Mogadishu has not commented on the operation, and there are concerns regarding the future of international support in combating al-Shabab.
