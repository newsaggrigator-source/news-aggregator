Trump mocks Somalia's airports in fresh Omar attack - Breakingthenews.net

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 00:09:00 GMT

In a recent rally, former President Donald Trump targeted Congresswoman Ilhan Omar, mocking her home country of Somalia by making disparaging remarks about its airports. Trump’s comments came as part of a broader critique of Omar, who has been a frequent target of his attacks due to her progressive policies and outspoken criticism of his administration. The remarks drew a mixed response, with some supporters cheering while others condemned the comments as xenophobic. Omar, who has faced similar rhetoric in the past, responded by emphasizing the importance of unity and respect for all communities. This incident highlights the ongoing tensions in American politics, particularly surrounding issues of race and immigration, as Trump continues to leverage controversial statements to energize his base ahead of the upcoming elections. The exchange underscores the polarized nature of political discourse in the U.S. and the challenges faced by minority representatives in Congress.
