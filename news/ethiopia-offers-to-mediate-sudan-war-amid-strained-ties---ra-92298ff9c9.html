Ethiopia offers to mediate Sudan war amid strained ties - Radio Tamazuj

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 17:50:19 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has proposed to mediate the ongoing conflict in Sudan by inviting all military, political, and civilian factions to an inclusive national dialogue in Ethiopia. This initiative aims to end hostilities and establish a Sudanese-led peace process, reflecting Ethiopia's commitment to Sudan's unity and stability, which is crucial for the Horn of Africa's security. The conflict, which began on April 15, 2023, has resulted in a humanitarian crisis affecting both nations. However, relations between Ethiopia and Sudan are strained, with accusations from Sudan that Ethiopia supports the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) and allows drone attacks from its territory. Ethiopia denies these claims and has faced retaliatory actions from Sudan, including the closure of diplomatic missions. Abiy emphasized the need for a neutral platform for dialogue, urging all parties to prioritize national interests over military gains, asserting that Sudan's peace is intrinsically linked to Ethiopia's peace.
