Satellite images have uncovered that the United Arab Emirates (UAE) has converted civilian airports in the breakaway regions of Somalia into substantial military installations. This transformation highlights the UAE's increasing military presence in the region, which has raised concerns among international observers. Additionally, flight tracking data indicates that the United States has conducted visits to these facilities, suggesting a potential collaboration or interest in the UAE's military operations in Somalia. The findings, reported by visual investigations reporter Christoph Koettl, shed light on the strategic developments in the area, where the UAE's actions may have broader implications for regional stability and security. The situation underscores the complex dynamics involving foreign military involvement in Somalia, a country that has faced ongoing conflict and instability for decades.
