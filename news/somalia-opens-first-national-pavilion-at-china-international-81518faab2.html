Somalia opens first national pavilion at China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 20:24:06 GMT

Somalia has made a significant debut by opening its first national pavilion at the China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai. This event marks a historic moment for Somalia, showcasing its diverse range of products and investment opportunities to a global audience. The pavilion aims to enhance Somalia's visibility in international markets and foster economic partnerships with China and other countries. Somali officials emphasized the importance of this participation in promoting the nation’s agricultural, livestock, and fishery sectors. The expo serves as a platform for Somalia to attract foreign investment and strengthen trade relations, highlighting the country's potential for growth and development. The initiative reflects Somalia's commitment to engaging with the global economy and seeking collaborative opportunities to boost its economic landscape. Overall, the national pavilion at CIIE represents a step forward for Somalia in its efforts to enhance trade and investment ties on an international scale. (151 words)
