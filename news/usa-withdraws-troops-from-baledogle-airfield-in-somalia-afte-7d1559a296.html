USA Withdraws Troops From Baledogle Airfield in Somalia After 14 Years of Presence - Мілітарний

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 12:29:13 GMT

The U.S. Armed Forces have officially withdrawn their troops from Baledogle Airfield in Somalia after a 14-year presence, as reported by Task & Purpose. This withdrawal occurred last month, although U.S. forces will remain in Somalia, redeploying to other regions. The exact number of personnel at Baledogle prior to the withdrawal has not been disclosed, but the U.S. previously stationed dozens of specialists there. The airfield, located about 96 km southwest of Mogadishu, has been a critical site for counterterrorism operations against militants in the Horn of Africa. U.S. troops began their deployment in 2012, focusing on training Somali special forces and conducting operations against Al-Shabaab. Despite the withdrawal, U.S. Africa Command (AFRICOM) emphasized the military's mobility for optimizing counterterrorism efforts. Additionally, the U.S. will cease funding for the United Nations Support Office for Somalia and the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission due to Somalia's insufficient progress against Al-Shabaab.
