Somalia warns Ethiopia unrest could weaken AUSSOM security operations - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 07:00:17 GMT

Somalia has expressed concerns that ongoing unrest in Ethiopia could undermine the effectiveness of the African Union Transition Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM). The Somali government highlighted that instability in Ethiopia, particularly in the Oromia region, poses a significant threat to regional security and could hinder AUSSOM's operations aimed at stabilizing Somalia. Officials emphasized the importance of a stable Ethiopia for the success of peacekeeping efforts in Somalia, as both nations share a border and face similar security challenges. The Somali administration urged the Ethiopian government to address the unrest promptly to ensure that AUSSOM can continue its mission without disruptions. The situation is critical, as any weakening of AUSSOM's capabilities could exacerbate the security situation in Somalia, which is already grappling with threats from militant groups. The Somali government remains vigilant and is closely monitoring developments in Ethiopia while advocating for regional stability. (151 words)
