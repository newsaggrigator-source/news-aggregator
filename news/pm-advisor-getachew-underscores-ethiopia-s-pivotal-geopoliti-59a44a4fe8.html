PM Advisor Getachew Underscores Ethiopia’s Pivotal Geopolitical Position - ENA English

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 21:53:49 GMT

During the Tana Forum 2026 Special Envoys Dialogue in Bahir Dar, PM Advisor Getachew Reda emphasized Ethiopia's crucial geopolitical role, asserting that developments within the country significantly impact the broader Horn of Africa and Red Sea region. He highlighted Ethiopia's population of over 130 million and its rapidly growing economy, positioning it as both an economic powerhouse and a vital contributor to international peace and security. Getachew stressed the Ethiopian government's commitment to resolving internal conflicts through peaceful dialogue, referencing the Pretoria Agreement aimed at addressing northern Ethiopia's issues. However, he warned of external challenges, particularly interference from regional actors like Eritrea, which he accused of exacerbating crises. The dialogue featured various international envoys who echoed the need for a coordinated regional approach to address interconnected security threats, including those from Sudan and Somalia. They called for stronger multilateral cooperation and regional integration to foster stability and enhance trade routes critical for global commerce.
