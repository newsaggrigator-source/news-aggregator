How the Black Stars of Ghana could line-up against Somalia - Pulse Ghana

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 19:25:34 GMT

The Black Stars of Ghana are gearing up to face Somalia in their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifying campaign, with the match set for Thursday at the Essipong Sports Stadium. Caretaker coach Kim Lars aims to secure the team's first victory after a disappointing start, which included losses to Gambia and Côte d’Ivoire. The predicted line-up features goalkeeper Benjamin Asare, who has been in excellent form, along with defenders Alidu Seidu, Jonas Adjetey, Jerome Opoku, and Gideon Mensah. In midfield, Thomas Partey is expected to anchor the team, supported by Caleb Yirenkyi and Daniel Kofi-Kyereh. The attacking trio will likely consist of captain Jordan Ayew, Mohammed Kudus, and Prince Adu Kwabena as the central striker. With a 4-3-3 formation, the Black Stars are determined to turn their fortunes around and revive their hopes of qualifying for the tournament.
