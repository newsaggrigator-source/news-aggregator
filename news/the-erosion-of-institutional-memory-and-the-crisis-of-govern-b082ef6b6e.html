The Erosion Of Institutional Memory And The Crisis Of Governance In Somalia And Puntland - Analysis - Eurasia Review

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 03:49:12 GMT

The article analyzes the erosion of institutional memory in Somalia and Puntland, arguing that this loss is a fundamental cause of governance failure. It highlights how knowledge is primarily retained within clans and oral traditions rather than through formal records and procedures, which hampers administrative continuity. Key examples include the destruction of the Somali National Archives and the lack of historical education in schools. The author, Ismail H. Warsame, draws on his experiences as a founding figure in Puntland to support his claims, noting that clan-centric governance suppresses administrative expertise and disrupts institutional memory. While some functional order persists through customary practices and informal governance, the article concludes that effective and accountable governance requires the establishment of institutional frameworks that can preserve and transfer knowledge. The analysis calls for a renewed focus on building these frameworks to enhance public capacity and governance in Somalia and Puntland. (160 words)
