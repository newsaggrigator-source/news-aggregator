Warbixin: Duqaymo Maraykanku ka fuliyey Somalia oo rayid waxyeello gaarsiiyey. - Horseed Media

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 11:12:12 GMT

Warbixin cusub oo uu baahiyey telefishinka Al Jazeera ayaa muujisay in duqeymo ay Mareykanku ka fuliyey agagaarka degmada Jamaame ee gobolka Jubbada Hoose, bishii Nofeembar 2025, ay waxyeello gaarsiiyeen rayid. Qoysas badan ayaa sheegay inay ku waayeen xubno qoysaskooda ka tirsan, carruurna ay dhaawacyo culus soo gaareen, taasoo keentay in dad badan ay ka barakacaan deegaannadoodii. Warbixintu waxay sidoo kale xustay in shabakad dabagal oo ka tirsan UNHCR ay diiwaangelisay dhimashada 12 qof oo rayid ah intii u dhexeysay 16-kii iyo 18-kii Nofeembar 2025. Taliska AFRICOM ayaa sheegay in aysan helin caddeymo ku filan oo muujinaya in hawlgallada Mareykanku ay sababeen khasaaraha rayidka, taasoo muujinaysa baahida loo qabo baaritaan hufan.
