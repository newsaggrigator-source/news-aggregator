EU Naval Mission Investigates Reported Hijacking of Iranian Vessel Off Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 14:27:43 GMT

The European Union's naval mission is currently investigating the reported hijacking of an Iranian fishing vessel, the Azini, off the northeastern coast of Somalia. The incident, which occurred on October 29, has raised significant security concerns regarding commercial shipping in the region. The Maritime Security Centre–Indian Ocean (MSCIO) indicated that the vessel could potentially be used as a mothership for further pirate attacks, although no confirmed attacks have been reported from the Azini. The safety of the crew remains unknown, and the identity of the hijackers has not been established. MSCIO has advised vessels operating in the vicinity to remain vigilant and report any suspicious activity. The situation is being closely monitored as Operation ATALANTA continues its investigation into the circumstances surrounding the hijacking, with concerns that the Azini's movement towards a shipping lane could pose a broader threat to maritime security in the area.
