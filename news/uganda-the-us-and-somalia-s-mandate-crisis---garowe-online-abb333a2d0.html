Uganda, the US and Somalia's mandate crisis - Garowe Online

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 03:42:17 GMT

Uganda is leveraging its troop withdrawal from Somalia to mediate a political crisis marked by a disputed presidential mandate. As Uganda begins to pull out its forces, President Yoweri Museveni has positioned himself as a broker between President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and the opposition, which refuses to negotiate under current terms. The U.S. has pledged funding for the UN Support Office in Somalia but is urged by Museveni to apply pressure for a political settlement. The legitimacy of the Somali government is in question following a Supreme Court ruling that mandates elections within 90 days, a deadline the president contests. Uganda's withdrawal, which started on November 2, 2026, could impact security in the region, particularly against al-Shabaab. The success of Uganda's mediation hinges on the participation of opposition groups, the security of Ugandan forces, and the confirmation of U.S. financial support before the UN authorization expires. Failure to resolve these issues could leave Somalia in a precarious political situation.
