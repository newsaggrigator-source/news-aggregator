Ethiopia Calls for Scaled-Up, Accessible Climate Finance for Developing Countries

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 05:32:27 GMT

Ethiopia's Foreign Affairs Minister and COP32 President-Designate, Gedion Timothewos, has emphasized the urgent need for scaled-up and accessible climate finance for developing countries. Speaking at COP31, he highlighted that adequate access to finance and technology is crucial for these nations to implement their climate commitments effectively. Timothewos pointed out that insufficient resources can delay climate action, exacerbate vulnerabilities, and undermine confidence in global efforts. He welcomed the initiative aimed at enhancing the capitalization of the Global Environment Facility (GEF) and its associated funds, including the Least Developed Countries Fund (LDCF) and the Special Climate Change Fund (SCCF). As Ethiopia prepares to host COP32 in Addis Ababa, the Minister reaffirmed the country's commitment to collaborating with partners to mobilize climate finance, ensuring that the priorities of developing nations are met.
