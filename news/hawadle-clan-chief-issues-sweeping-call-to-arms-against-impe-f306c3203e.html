Hawadle clan chief issues sweeping call to arms against impending federal offensive in central Somalia - Somali Guardian

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 10:06:19 GMT

Hawadle clan chief Ugas Yusuf Ugas Hassan has issued a significant call to arms, urging his clan members to defend their territory in the Hiran region against an anticipated federal offensive. This appeal comes as the federal government prepares to deploy 1,500 troops to reclaim Baladweyne, recently taken by Hawadle militias led by former NISA chief Abdullahi Mohamed Ali Sanbalolshe. Tensions escalated after reports of Djiboutian forces facilitating the entry of Hirshabelle Vice-President Abditah Hussein Afrah into Baladweyne, leading to gunfire in the area. Ugas Hassan called on all able-bodied Hawadle men, including those in the Somali National Army, to abandon their federal allegiance and join the defense of their clan. He warned of consequences for those who do not comply, invoking a recent supreme court ruling that questioned the legitimacy of President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's administration. The situation remains volatile as armed groups continue to mobilize in support of the chief's call.
