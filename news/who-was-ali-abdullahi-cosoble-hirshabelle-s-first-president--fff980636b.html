Who Was Ali Abdullahi Cosoble, Hirshabelle’s First President Who Died in Egypt? - Garowe Online

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 08:26:51 GMT

Ali Abdullahi Cosoble, the first president of Somalia's Hirshabelle state, passed away in Egypt at the age of 65 after a recent illness. His death was confirmed by family members and Somali politicians, prompting condolences from current Hirshabelle President Ali Gudlawe Hussein and others. Born in Mogadishu on June 15, 1961, Cosoble pursued higher education in the United States, graduating from Sam Houston State University. Before his political career, he worked with the Somali Development Bank and Oxfam, focusing on community development in Beledweyne and Nairobi. Cosoble served in Somalia’s Transitional Federal Parliament from 2004 to 2012 and was appointed federal minister of education in 2005. He became Hirshabelle's first president in 2016, a role marked by political challenges that led to his removal in 2017. Despite his short tenure, he is remembered for his contributions to Somalia's federal political landscape and the establishment of Hirshabelle.
