Heartbreak for Ocean Stars as Uganda Win CECAFA Final Shootout - FTL Somalia

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 15:54:18 GMT

In a dramatic CECAFA final, Uganda triumphed over the Ocean Stars in a penalty shootout, leaving the Somali team heartbroken. The match, held in a tense atmosphere, ended in a 1-1 draw after regulation and extra time, leading to the decisive penalties. Uganda showcased composure under pressure, converting all their spot-kicks, while the Ocean Stars struggled, missing crucial penalties. This victory marks Uganda's significant achievement in the regional tournament, while Somalia's hopes for a first CECAFA title were dashed. The match highlighted the growing competitiveness of football in the region, with both teams displaying skill and determination throughout the tournament.
