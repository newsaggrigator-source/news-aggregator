Türkiye and Somalia have commenced the construction of a new naval headquarters in Mogadishu, marking a significant step in enhancing Türkiye's naval presence in the region. This development is closely tied to Türkiye's ongoing offshore hydrocarbon exploration activities along Somalia's coast. The establishment of the naval base is expected to bolster security and stability in the area, which has been affected by piracy and other maritime threats. Türkiye's involvement in Somalia has been growing, with various initiatives aimed at strengthening bilateral relations and supporting Somalia's development. The new naval headquarters will serve as a strategic asset for Türkiye, facilitating its maritime operations and contributing to regional security efforts. This collaboration underscores the importance of maritime security in the context of economic interests and geopolitical dynamics in the Horn of Africa.
