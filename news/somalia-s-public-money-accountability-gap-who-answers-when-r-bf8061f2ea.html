Somalia’s public money accountability gap: Who answers when resources cross government lines? - Dawan Africa

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 07:50:37 GMT

Somalia faces significant challenges in public money accountability, particularly as resources traverse various government levels. The World Bank's 2026 Public Finance Review highlights the complexities citizens encounter in tracking how tax revenues are collected, allocated, and spent. While progress has been made in public financial management, issues persist, including overlapping tax systems and unclear expenditure responsibilities among the Federal Government and Federal Member States. This lack of clarity complicates accountability, leaving citizens unsure of which government entity is responsible for services. The report emphasizes the need for stronger fiscal frameworks, improved coordination, and transparency to ensure that public funds effectively translate into essential services like education and healthcare. As Somalia seeks to enhance domestic revenue amid declining external support, establishing clear accountability mechanisms will be crucial for rebuilding trust between the government and its citizens. Ultimately, effective public finance reform hinges on answering fundamental questions about resource management and service delivery.
