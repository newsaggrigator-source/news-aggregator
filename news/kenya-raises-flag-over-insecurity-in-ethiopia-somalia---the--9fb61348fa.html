Kenya raises flag over insecurity in Ethiopia, Somalia - The EastAfrican

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 12:05:38 GMT

Kenya has raised alarms regarding the escalating insecurity in neighboring Ethiopia and Somalia, which poses a potential threat to its national interests. President William Ruto highlighted these concerns in the State of National Security report presented to Parliament, noting the risks associated with political instability, extremist activities, and the upcoming elections in South Sudan. The report, covering September 2025 to August 2026, indicates that Kenya faces persistent threats from terrorist groups, particularly Al-Shabaab, which exploits regional instability to conduct operations. Vulnerabilities to radicalization are spreading beyond traditional hotspots, driven by factors such as youth unemployment and political tensions. The report documented 37 terror-related incidents during the period, emphasizing the need for enhanced border security and intelligence-sharing among security agencies. Despite ongoing counter-terrorism efforts, porous borders and socio-economic grievances continue to facilitate militant activities. The report also warns of potential refugee flows and increased tensions due to electoral disputes in Ethiopia and South Sudan.
