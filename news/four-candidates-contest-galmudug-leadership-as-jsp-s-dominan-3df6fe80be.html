Four Candidates Contest Galmudug Leadership as JSP’s Dominance Looms Over Election - FTL Somalia

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 10:03:44 GMT

Four candidates are vying for leadership positions in Galmudug, with the presidential election set for October 22, 2026. The candidates include Liban Ahmed Hassan from the Justice and Solidarity Party (JSP) and Abduqadir Abdullahi Diriye Adawe from the SINCAD Party. During their campaign speeches to the regional parliament, both candidates outlined their priorities, with Hassan focusing on transparency, security, and economic development, while Adawe emphasized education, healthcare, and infrastructure. The election follows a controversial rerun of the parliamentary vote that excluded the Towfiiq political organization, raising questions about the legitimacy of the electoral process. With the JSP holding a significant majority in the assembly, Hassan's victory is widely anticipated, although concerns remain about his ability to govern inclusively given the contentious political backdrop.
