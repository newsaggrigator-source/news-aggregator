“Preparedness must reach people before the floods do”: Women and girls in Somalia are standing strong against climate change - United Nations Population Fund

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 22:28:43 GMT

In Somalia, women and girls are facing severe challenges due to climate change, compounded by ongoing conflict and displacement. The Northern and Central regions are experiencing both droughts and floods, which have led to increased food insecurity affecting 1.9 million people. Pregnant women are particularly vulnerable, facing risks such as complications from malnutrition and gender-based violence. Local organizations, in partnership with the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), are working to enhance healthcare facilities, like the Baxnaano Health Centre, to withstand climate impacts and provide essential reproductive health services. Despite these efforts, funding constraints threaten the sustainability of these initiatives, with a $20 million gap hindering the expansion of services. UNFPA has reached over 55,000 individuals with critical support, emphasizing the need for preparedness before disasters strike. As the 2026 United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) approaches, the importance of global cooperation to address these crises and protect women's health and rights is underscored.
