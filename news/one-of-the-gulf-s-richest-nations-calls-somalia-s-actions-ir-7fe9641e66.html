One of the Gulf's richest nations calls Somalia's actions irresponsible as the conflict over East Africa’s disputed region intensifies - Business Insider Africa

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 10:15:24 GMT

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has condemned Somalia's recent actions, labeling them as irresponsible amid escalating tensions over East Africa's disputed regions. Somalia expelled the UAE ambassador and recalled its own envoy, accusing the UAE of supporting military efforts that undermine Somalia's federal authority, particularly concerning Somaliland, a self-declared region. Anwar Gargash, a UAE presidential adviser, criticized Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud for prioritizing personal interests over national stability and emphasized that internal disputes should not affect foreign relations. The UAE's Ministry of Foreign Affairs reiterated the importance of maintaining bilateral relations based on shared interests, despite the ongoing hostilities. The tensions have roots in Somalia's cancellation of agreements with the UAE earlier this year, citing threats to its sovereignty. The UAE has historically supported Somalia, and the current discord reflects broader geopolitical complexities in the region, particularly regarding Somaliland's recognition and the UAE's investments in its ports.
