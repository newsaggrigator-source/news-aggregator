Indian navy recaptures hijacked Iranian ship off Somalia - Khaleej Times

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 10:00:13 GMT

The Indian Navy successfully recaptured a hijacked Iranian fishing vessel, the Azini, off the coast of Somalia on November 3, 2026. The ship was seized by armed pirates on October 29, near Puntland, raising concerns it could be used as a "mother ship" for further attacks on merchant vessels. In response, Indian naval commandos parachuted onto the ship to rescue all 20 crew members. This operation was part of a broader effort to combat a resurgence of Somali piracy, which has seen at least 15 attacks since April, the highest rate since 2013. The Iranian embassy in India expressed gratitude for the Indian government's swift action, commending the professionalism and coordination of the Indian Armed Forces. The successful mission highlights India's commitment to humanitarian assistance and maritime security in the region. The Azini incident marks the third Iranian fishing boat hijacked off the Somali coast since July.
