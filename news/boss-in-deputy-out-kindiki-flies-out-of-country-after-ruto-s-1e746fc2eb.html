Boss in, deputy out: Kindiki flies out of country after Ruto's return - Tuko News

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 08:54:01 GMT

Deputy President Kithure Kindiki traveled to Ethiopia on October 24, 2026, just a day after President William Ruto returned from Zimbabwe. Kindiki arrived in Bahir Dar aboard a Kenya Air Force aircraft to attend the 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa, which focuses on economic sovereignty and geopolitical transformation as pathways to peace. Upon arrival, he was welcomed by Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, and the two leaders held a brief bilateral meeting. The forum, running from October 23 to 25, aims to address how Africa can leverage its natural resources for industrial growth and tackle issues like illicit economies that undermine governance. Notable attendees include Ghana's President John Mahama and former African leaders. This gathering is part of a broader initiative established in 2012 to promote African-led solutions to peace and security challenges. Kindiki's trip underscores the close partnership between Kenya and Ethiopia in regional security matters.
