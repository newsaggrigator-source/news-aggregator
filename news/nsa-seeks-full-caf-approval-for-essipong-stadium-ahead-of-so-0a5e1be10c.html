NSA Seeks Full CAF Approval for Essipong Stadium Ahead of Somalia Fixture - Modern Ghana

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 19:01:33 GMT

The National Sports Authority (NSA) is seeking full approval from the Central Affairs Committee (CAF) for the Essipong Stadium in preparation for an upcoming fixture involving the Somalia national football team. The NSA aims to ensure that the stadium meets all necessary standards and requirements set by CAF to host international matches. This initiative is part of the broader efforts to enhance the infrastructure and facilities available for football in the country. The approval process is crucial for the successful organization of the fixture, which is expected to draw significant attention and support from fans and stakeholders alike. The NSA is committed to providing a safe and conducive environment for both players and spectators during the event.
