Somalia expels UAE ambassador - govt - CGTN

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 12:01:59 GMT

Somalia has officially expelled the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador, marking a significant diplomatic shift between the two nations. The Somali government announced the decision, although specific reasons for the expulsion were not disclosed. This move comes amid ongoing tensions between Somalia and the UAE, particularly over issues related to military cooperation and financial support. The UAE has previously been involved in various development projects in Somalia, but relations have soured in recent years, with accusations of interference in Somalia's internal affairs. The expulsion of the ambassador is expected to further strain diplomatic ties and could have implications for future cooperation between the two countries. This development highlights the complexities of international relations in the Horn of Africa, where geopolitical interests often collide. The situation remains fluid, and further reactions from both governments are anticipated.
