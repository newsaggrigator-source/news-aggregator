Somalia and India Seek to Deepen Bilateral Ties Through Expanded Cooperation - Horseed Media

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 15:00:00 GMT

Somalia and India are actively working to enhance their bilateral relations through expanded cooperation in various sectors, including trade, healthcare, education, and consular affairs. Recent discussions between Somalia’s State Minister for Foreign Affairs, Ali Mohamed Omar, and India’s Minister of State for External Affairs, Pabitra Margherita, focused on implementing a memorandum of understanding signed in Mogadishu. This agreement aims to establish a framework for cooperation and to advance shared interests. The talks included plans to create joint technical committees to oversee initiatives in priority areas, ensuring that both governments can effectively coordinate and monitor the execution of bilateral agreements. The discussions emphasized the need to translate diplomatic commitments into tangible benefits for the citizens of both nations. With a history of diplomatic relations, this engagement marks a renewed effort to strengthen ties through institutional coordination and practical cooperation programs, reflecting both countries' commitment to deepening their longstanding partnership.
