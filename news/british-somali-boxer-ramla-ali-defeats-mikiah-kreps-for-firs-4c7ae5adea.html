British-Somali boxer Ramla Ali achieved a significant milestone by defeating Mikiah Kreps to win her first world title, claiming the IBO and WBF junior featherweight belts in a split-decision victory on November 8, 2026, in Arlington, Texas. The fight was intense and controversial, as Kreps outlanded Ali in punches, with a final tally of 115 to 78. Despite this, two judges awarded the fight to Ali, leading to mixed reactions, including criticism from veteran promoter Lou Di Bella. Ali, who fled Somalia's civil war and settled in London, expressed deep emotion after the win, dedicating it to her late father. She emphasized the importance of her victory for Somalia and hopes to inspire young people. Ali's journey, which includes being the first Somali boxer at the Olympics and a UNICEF ambassador, has been documented in the film "In the Shadows." Her win marks a significant achievement in her boxing career and personal story. (159 words)
