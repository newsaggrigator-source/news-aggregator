Trump Asks Why US Takes Immigrants From ‘Sh*thouse’ Countries Like Somalia: ‘Why Not Denmark?’ - Yahoo

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 00:46:00 GMT

During a rally in Perry, Georgia, President Donald Trump expressed his preference for immigrants from Scandinavian countries, questioning why the U.S. accepts immigrants from what he referred to as "sh*thouse countries" like Somalia. He recounted a conversation with Democratic Senators, asking, "Why do we always have to take people from sh*thouse countries?" and suggested that the U.S. should instead welcome individuals from countries like Norway, Sweden, and Denmark. Trump criticized Somali immigrants, claiming that a significant percentage do not work and engage in criminal activities, which he believes undermines their value to the U.S. He reiterated his stance from the previous year, expressing a desire to limit immigration from Somalia, particularly targeting Rep. Ilhan Omar. Trump's comments were met with mixed reactions, including laughter from the crowd, and he continued to mock Omar amid ongoing legal issues involving her community.
