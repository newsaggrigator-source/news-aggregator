Somalia rejects map shown by Israeli Prime Minister as violation of its sovereignty - Somali National News Agency

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 22:09:44 GMT

Somalia has officially rejected a map presented by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, which depicts parts of Somali territory as belonging to Israel. The Somali government condemned the map as a violation of its sovereignty and territorial integrity. In a statement, officials emphasized that Somalia's borders are well-defined and recognized internationally. The government called on the international community to respect its sovereignty and to refrain from actions that could undermine peace and stability in the region. This incident highlights ongoing tensions regarding territorial claims and the importance of diplomatic recognition in international relations.
