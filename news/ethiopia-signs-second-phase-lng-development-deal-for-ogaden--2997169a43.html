Ethiopia Signs Second-Phase LNG Development Deal for Ogaden Gas Field

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 10:36:56 GMT

Ethiopia has signed a deal with Chinese companies GCL Group and Jurong Group to develop the second phase of a liquefied natural gas (LNG) project at the Calub gas field in the Somali Region. The agreement, signed on October 16, 2026, includes engineering, construction, and natural gas liquefaction services, following a strategic cooperation framework established in May 2026. The first phase of the project, commissioned in October 2025, processes 200,000 cubic meters of natural gas daily, producing 50,000 metric tonnes of LNG annually for domestic use. The second phase aims to boost production for international markets, although details on financing, capacity, and export routes remain unclear. This initiative is part of Ethiopia's broader efforts to commercialize its gas resources in the Ogaden Basin after previous delays and disputes.
