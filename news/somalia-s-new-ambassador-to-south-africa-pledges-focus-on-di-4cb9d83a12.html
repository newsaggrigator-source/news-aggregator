Somalia's New ambassador to South Africa Pledges Focus on Diaspora - Dawan Africa

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 14:08:11 GMT

Somalia's new ambassador to South Africa, Aden Ishaq Ali, officially presented his credentials to President Cyril Ramaphosa on November 14, 2026. In his address, Ali emphasized his commitment to prioritizing the welfare of Somali nationals residing in South Africa, aiming to enhance the services provided by the embassy. He expressed intentions to strengthen diplomatic ties and foster cooperation between Somalia and South Africa, focusing on mutual interests. Ali succeeds Ismail Abdalla Somane, who managed the embassy temporarily, following the tenure of former ambassador Mohamed Sheikh Ishaq. The relationship between the two nations has been bolstered since 2013, when Somalia established its embassy in Pretoria. South Africa hosts a significant Somali community, many of whom operate small businesses and have faced challenges, including xenophobic attacks in recent years. Ali's appointment signals a renewed focus on the diaspora and the importance of their contributions to both countries.
