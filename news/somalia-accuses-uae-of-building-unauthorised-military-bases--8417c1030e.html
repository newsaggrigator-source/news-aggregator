Somalia accuses UAE of building unauthorised military bases - Somalia Today

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 18:11:07 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has accused the United Arab Emirates (UAE) of constructing unauthorized military bases in Somalia and establishing armed units that operate independently of the federal government. In a televised address, Mohamud claimed that the UAE's actions are part of a broader strategy to undermine Somalia's national unity and sovereignty, particularly following the cancellation of agreements between Mogadishu and Abu Dhabi ten months prior. He emphasized that Somalia is a nation with a constitution and a government determined to protect its dignity and independence. Mohamud also highlighted historical ties with the UAE but expressed that these have been overshadowed by disputes over sovereignty. He warned against the UAE's interference, which he likened to its involvement in conflicts in Libya and Yemen. The Somali government has taken measures to restrict Emirati military presence and called for accountability regarding local politicians allegedly collaborating with the UAE.
