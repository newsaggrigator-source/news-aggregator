Somalia • Burundi hurries to withdraw troops before end of AUSSOM - Africa Intelligence

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:42:56 GMT

Burundi is expediting the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia, where they have been part of the African Union peacekeeping mission since 2007. The Burundian contingent has relied heavily on logistical support from the United Nations, which is crucial as they prepare to leave. The urgency of this withdrawal comes as the African Union's Somalia mission (AUSSOM) is set to conclude. Despite the challenges, including a lack of a fallback plan for continued operations, the Burundian government is moving forward with its exit strategy. This development highlights the ongoing complexities in Somalia's security landscape, as the country grapples with political instability and the presence of militant groups. The situation remains fluid, with international stakeholders closely monitoring the implications of Burundi's troop withdrawal on the overall stability of the region.
