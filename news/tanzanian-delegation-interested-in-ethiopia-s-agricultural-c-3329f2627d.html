Tanzanian Delegation Interested in Ethiopia's Agricultural Commodity Standards Practice

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 09:55:16 GMT

A Tanzanian delegation, led by Capital Markets Tribunal Chairperson Ntemi Nimilwa Kilekamajenga, has shown keen interest in Ethiopia's agricultural commodity standards practices. The delegation was particularly impressed by the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange's quality assessment methods and its specialized academy, which ensures that agricultural products meet international standards. Kilekamajenga noted that Ethiopia offers a broader range of traded agricultural products compared to Tanzania, highlighting maize as an example of a product not currently traded in Tanzania. The delegation expressed a desire to learn from Ethiopia's experience, especially regarding the quality assurance system used for coffee exports. Tanzanian lawyer Eliad E. Mndeme emphasized the financial advantages of quality certification, which allows farmers to secure bank loans. The Tanzanian Ministry of Finance is considering facilitating a visit to Ethiopia for further learning. Overall, the delegation aims to enhance Tanzania's agricultural commodity exchange by adopting successful practices from Ethiopia.
