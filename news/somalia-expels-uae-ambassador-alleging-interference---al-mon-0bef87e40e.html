Somalia expels UAE ambassador, alleging interference - Al-Monitor

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 20:06:21 GMT

Somalia has expelled the United Arab Emirates (UAE) ambassador, citing interference in its internal affairs, a move that has been condemned by Abu Dhabi as "irresponsible." President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accused the UAE of supporting political discord within Somalia, particularly in relation to the breakaway region of Somaliland, which the UAE has recognized. Following a cabinet meeting, Somalia's Information Minister announced that the UAE ambassador must leave within 72 hours, while Somalia's ambassador to the UAE is also being recalled. The Somali government has expressed concerns over the UAE's actions undermining its sovereignty and unity. This diplomatic rift follows previous tensions, including Somalia's cancellation of agreements with the UAE after allegations of the UAE aiding a Yemeni separatist leader. The UAE operates several strategic ports in Somalia, complicating the situation as the Somali federal government struggles to assert control over semi-autonomous regions. The UAE has denied accusations of meddling in Somalia and other regional conflicts.
