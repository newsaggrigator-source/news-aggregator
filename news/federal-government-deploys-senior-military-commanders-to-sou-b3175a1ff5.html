Federal Government Deploys Senior Military Commanders to South West Frontlines as Fighting Intensifies - FTL Somalia

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 10:39:58 GMT

The Federal Government of Somalia has deployed senior military commanders to the South West State frontlines amid escalating fighting against Al-Shabaab and forces loyal to former President Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen. Led by Major General Sahal Abdullahi Omar, the delegation visited areas where recent battles resulted in significant casualties. General Khaalid acknowledged losses on both sides, reporting at least 70 Al-Shabaab fighters killed, slightly differing from the Defense Ministry's earlier figures. The conflict has strained relations with Kenya, with accusations that Nairobi is harboring Somali terrorists. This political tension complicates the government's counterterrorism efforts, as it faces challenges on two fronts: combating Al-Shabaab and addressing the Laftagareen-aligned militias. The presence of military court officials indicates a dual focus on military operations and legal accountability. The situation remains precarious, with potential implications for vital trade and security cooperation between Somalia and Kenya.
