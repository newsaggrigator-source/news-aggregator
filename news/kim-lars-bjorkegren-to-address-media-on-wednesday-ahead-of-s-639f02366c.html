Kim Lars Bjorkegren to address media on Wednesday ahead of Somalia clash - Ghana Football Association

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 23:35:28 GMT

Kim Lars Bjorkegren, the head coach of Ghana's national football team, will hold a media briefing on Wednesday at 4 PM at the Essipong Sports Stadium. This press conference is in anticipation of Ghana's crucial 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Somalia. During the session, Bjorkegren is expected to provide updates on the team's preparations, player fitness, and strategies for the upcoming matches. Ghana is looking to recover from a challenging start to the qualifying campaign, with the first match against Somalia scheduled for Thursday, November 12, followed by a second encounter on Monday, November 16, 2026. The Ghana Football Association has emphasized the importance of these matches as the team aims to secure vital points in the qualification process.
