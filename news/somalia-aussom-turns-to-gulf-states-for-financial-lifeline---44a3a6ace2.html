Somalia • AUSSOM turns to Gulf states for financial lifeline - Africa Intelligence

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 05:40:25 GMT

Somalia's peacekeeping mission faces financial uncertainty as the U.S. withdraws its contributions, prompting the African Union's Somalia mission (AUSSOM) to seek assistance from Gulf states, particularly Saudi Arabia. The Somali government is actively engaging with these nations to secure a financial lifeline essential for the mission's continuation. The lack of a backup plan raises concerns about the sustainability of peacekeeping efforts in the region. This situation underscores the broader challenges facing Somalia, which is grappling with ongoing instability and threats from militant groups. The Somali administration's outreach to Gulf states reflects a strategic pivot to diversify its funding sources amid dwindling Western support. As the geopolitical landscape shifts, the outcome of these negotiations could significantly impact Somalia's security and governance framework.
