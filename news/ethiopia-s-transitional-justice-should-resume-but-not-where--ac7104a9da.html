Ethiopia’s transitional justice should resume, but not where it left off - ISS Africa

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 09:49:39 GMT

Ethiopia's transitional justice process is set to resume following a pause that began in April 2025, with the Ministry of Justice launching a government-civil society platform on October 23, 2026. Public consultations on draft laws are anticipated, but they should only commence after considering the recommendations from the National Dialogue, which were submitted to Parliament on October 3. The article emphasizes that the resumption should not merely restart the previous process but recalibrate it by addressing existing gaps and outstanding commitments. Key priorities include integrating the National Dialogue's recommendations, resolving legal ambiguities, and ensuring participation from marginalized communities. The framework must also recognize mental health support and economic rights violations as critical components. Additionally, independent experts should be involved in the consultation process to enhance transparency and credibility. The article concludes that the conclusion of the National Dialogue presents a pivotal opportunity to clarify and strengthen Ethiopia's transitional justice framework. (159 words)
