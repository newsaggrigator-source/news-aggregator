From Regional Identity to Shared Nationhood - Reimagining Tigray's Future Within a United Ethiopia

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 15:07:52 GMT

In recent weeks, the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) and the Tigray Peace Force (TPF) have regained control of Tigray's major cities, including Mekelle, following the withdrawal of Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) forces. This shift has sparked discussions about Tigray's political future, yet much of the debate remains focused on immediate power dynamics rather than a broader vision for Tigray's role within a united Ethiopia. The article argues for a reimagined Ethiopian identity that embraces both ethnic and national identities, promoting unity over division. It suggests that Tigray's historical significance can serve as a foundation for national cohesion, advocating for reforms that decentralize federal institutions, encourage cross-regional cooperation, and ensure minority representation. By fostering a shared sense of belonging and recognizing the contributions of all ethnic groups, Ethiopia can move towards a stable and prosperous future, transcending the limitations of ethnic federalism while respecting cultural diversity.
