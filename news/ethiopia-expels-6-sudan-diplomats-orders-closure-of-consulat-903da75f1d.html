Ethiopia expels 6 Sudan diplomats, orders closure of consulate, reports say - Nepali Patro

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 07:42:52 GMT

Ethiopia has expelled six Sudanese diplomats and ordered the closure of Sudan's consulate in Asosa, a border town, within 48 hours, according to reports from Al Jazeera. This decision reflects escalating tensions between the two nations, with Sudan accusing Ethiopia of backing the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) amid ongoing conflicts. The diplomatic rift highlights the deteriorating relations between Ethiopia and Sudan, particularly in light of recent military confrontations and border disputes. The expulsion of diplomats and the closure of the consulate signify a significant diplomatic setback and could further strain bilateral relations. As the situation develops, both countries are likely to navigate a complex landscape of regional politics and security concerns.
