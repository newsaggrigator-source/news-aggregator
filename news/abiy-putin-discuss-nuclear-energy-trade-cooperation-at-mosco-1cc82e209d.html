Abiy, Putin Discuss Nuclear Energy, Trade Cooperation At Moscow Summit

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 15:27:39 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed met with Russian President Vladimir Putin during the third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow to discuss enhancing cooperation in nuclear energy, health, and trade. The discussions aimed at strengthening the longstanding relations between Ethiopia and Russia, with Abiy emphasizing the importance of bilateral and multilateral collaboration. He described the meeting as fruitful and highlighted the shared commitment to dialogue and mutual understanding. Additionally, Abiy presented Putin with a Russian edition of his book, "Medemer," symbolizing their commitment to advancing Ethiopia-Russia relations. The summit, held on October 28-29, gathered various Russian and African leaders to explore ways to bolster cooperation across multiple sectors. This meeting follows a banquet hosted by Putin at the Grand Kremlin Palace, attended by both Abiy and Ethiopia's First Lady, Zinash Tayachew. The discussions reflect a growing interest in deepening ties between the two nations. (159 words)
