Netanyahu’s Somalia Map Revives Fears of Partition - ASHARQ AL-AWSAT English

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:30:58 GMT

A map presented by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has incited significant backlash in Somalia, as it depicts Puntland and Somaliland as separate entities, which Somalia claims undermines its sovereignty and territorial integrity. The Somali federal government has categorically rejected the map, labeling it a "misleading" representation that fragments the nation. This controversy comes amid ongoing tensions between the federal government and Puntland, which has been asserting its autonomy and challenging the legitimacy of President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. The Somali government emphasized that Puntland and Somaliland are integral parts of a unified state and warned that any foreign attempts to alter its political map would violate international law. Experts suggest that the map's implications could exacerbate existing divisions within Somalia, particularly with Somaliland's strategic significance and its recent recognition by Israel. The Somali government is urged to engage in diplomatic efforts to address these issues and maintain national unity amidst external pressures and internal disputes.
