Somalia: At Least Three Civilians Killed in Clashes Over Home Demolitions in Mogadishu - Horseed Media

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 15:55:07 GMT

At least three civilians were killed and several others injured during clashes in Mogadishu on Sunday, as residents protested against forced evictions and home demolitions in the Warlaaliska neighborhood. Former Prime Minister Hassan Ali Khaire reported that security forces opened fire on demonstrators who were opposing the unlawful destruction of their properties. The violence erupted during a coordinated operation that resulted in the demolition of over 200 homes, despite previous government assurances that the area would remain unaffected. Residents allege that the land has been sold to a businessman with ties to President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, although these claims remain unverified. The incident has raised serious concerns regarding forced evictions, property rights, and the role of security forces in land disputes. Somalia continues to grapple with political instability and conflict, stemming from the collapse of its central government in 1991.
