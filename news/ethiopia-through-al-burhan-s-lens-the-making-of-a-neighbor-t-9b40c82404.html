The article "Ethiopia Through Al-Burhan’s Lens: The Making of a Neighbor Turned Adversary" explores the evolving relationship between Ethiopia and Sudan, particularly through the perspective of Sudanese military leader Abdel Fattah al-Burhan. It highlights how historical ties and shared interests have deteriorated into rivalry, primarily due to border disputes and differing political agendas. The piece examines key events that have strained relations, including conflicts in the Tigray region and the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam project, which has raised tensions over water resources. Al-Burhan's leadership style and military strategies are analyzed, revealing how they have influenced Sudan's stance towards Ethiopia. The article also discusses the broader implications of this adversarial relationship for regional stability in the Horn of Africa, emphasizing the need for diplomatic engagement to address underlying issues and prevent further escalation. Overall, it provides a comprehensive overview of the complexities surrounding Ethiopia and Sudan's interactions in recent years.
