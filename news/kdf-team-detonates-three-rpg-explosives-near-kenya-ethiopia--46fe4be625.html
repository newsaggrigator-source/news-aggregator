KDF team detonates three RPG explosives near Kenya-Ethiopia border amid crisis - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 21:10:53 GMT

A team from the Kenya Defence Forces (KDF) successfully detonated three rocket-propelled grenades (RPGs) near the Kenya-Ethiopia border in Moyale, Marsabit County, amid escalating tensions in Ethiopia. The explosives were discovered following reports of two RPGs landing near a slaughter site, believed to have been fired from Ethiopia. This incident is part of a broader crisis, with approximately 40,000 Ethiopians fleeing to Kenya due to ongoing conflicts between the Oromia and Somali regional administrations. Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi has urged Ethiopian authorities to stabilize the situation to facilitate the return of displaced families. In response to security concerns, Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja has increased police presence in the area and emphasized the need for community cooperation to ensure safety. The situation remains critical, with reports of stray bullets injuring civilians and the UN warning of growing humanitarian needs as more people seek refuge in Kenya.
