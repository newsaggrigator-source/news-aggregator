Feisal Mohamed Hashi: The Vision Behind Somalia's Revenue Reforms - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 01:22:38 GMT

Feisal Mohamed Hashi has been appointed as the Director General of Somalia's newly established Somali Revenue Authority (SRA), marking a significant step in the country's revenue reforms. After years of dependency on external aid, Somalia is focusing on domestic revenue mobilization to support its economic growth. Hashi, who previously led the Revenue Directorate at the Ministry of Finance, has been instrumental in modernizing tax administration and customs operations. His expertise in public financial management and economic policy positions him well to lead the SRA. Key reforms under his direction include the Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS), which aims to streamline tax collection and enhance transparency. Additionally, Hashi has contributed to the development of crucial legislation that will govern Somalia's fiscal institutions. As the SRA becomes operational, it is expected to play a vital role in financing public services and reducing reliance on foreign aid, particularly as the country prepares for potential revenues from its emerging petroleum sector.
