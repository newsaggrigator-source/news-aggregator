Immigration to UK from third-world countries, including Pakistan, will end entirely, British MP Rupert Low - The Economic Times

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 03:01:22 GMT

British MP Rupert Lowe has announced a controversial plan to completely end immigration to the UK from several developing countries, including Pakistan, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Somalia, and Iran. Speaking at the Restore Britain party conference on October 3, 2026, Lowe stated, “Immigration into Britain from the Third World will end entirely,” emphasizing a strict policy to halt civilian immigration from these nations. His proposals include deporting undocumented migrants, removing foreign nationals who do not meet stringent requirements, abolishing the refugee system, and tightening British citizenship rules. Lowe, who has represented Great Yarmouth since July 2024, has become a prominent figure in the UK’s hardline immigration debate, advocating for measures that extend beyond merely reducing net migration. He criticized the current immigration system as ineffective and corrupt, asserting that his party aims to implement a “red list” to discriminate against certain nationalities. This approach marks a significant shift from the UK's existing immigration framework, which allows various forms of entry for foreign nationals.
