Black Stars vs Somalia Clash: Fans to pay GH¢50 - GhanaWeb

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 04:11:34 GMT

The upcoming football match between Ghana's Black Stars and Somalia has set ticket prices at GH¢50 for fans. This highly anticipated clash is part of the preparations for the upcoming international fixtures. The match is expected to draw significant attention, with fans eager to support their national team. The Ghana Football Association (GFA) has emphasized the importance of fan attendance in boosting team morale and creating a vibrant atmosphere at the stadium. In addition to the ticket pricing, the GFA is implementing various measures to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for attendees. Fans are encouraged to purchase tickets early to secure their spots for this exciting encounter. The match not only serves as a platform for the players to showcase their skills but also strengthens the bond between the team and its supporters. Overall, the Black Stars vs Somalia match promises to be an exhilarating event for football enthusiasts in Ghana. (158 words)
