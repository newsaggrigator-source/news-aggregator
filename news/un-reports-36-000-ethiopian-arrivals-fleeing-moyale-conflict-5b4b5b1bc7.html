UN Reports 36,000 Ethiopian Arrivals Fleeing Moyale Conflict, Kenya Rights Commission Urges Aid

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 04:40:47 GMT

Approximately 36,000 Ethiopians have fled to Kenya due to escalating conflict in Moyale, a border town. The UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR) reported that many of the arrivals are women and children, with urgent needs for food, clean water, shelter, sanitation, and healthcare. Some refugees are staying with relatives, while others are in temporary shelters, including schools and churches. The Kenya National Commission on Human Rights (KNCHR) noted that around 40,000 people have sought refuge in Moyale, with over 15,000 in temporary accommodations. The commission has called for a coordinated humanitarian response, emphasizing the need for emergency medical care and protection services. It also highlighted the risks posed by potential rainfall, which could exacerbate sanitation issues and disease outbreaks. The conflict, which intensified following a fatal incident involving a teenager, has led to widespread displacement and disrupted transport, prompting many to cross into Kenya for safety.
