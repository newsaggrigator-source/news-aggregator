Peace Over Rivalry - Somalia's Future Lies in Cooperation, Not Confrontation With Ethiopia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 04:52:40 GMT

Somalia's future hinges on fostering cooperation with Ethiopia rather than succumbing to historical rivalries, according to President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. Emphasizing the need for a foreign policy centered on collaboration, he argues that Somalia must prioritize its sovereignty while engaging with its largest neighbor. The extensive shared border impacts daily life for many, making diplomatic relations crucial for trade and security. Historical grievances stemming from Ethiopia's past interventions complicate this relationship, yet both nations must acknowledge these issues to build a stable partnership. The appointment of Somali leaders in Ethiopian governance reflects a growing integration that could enhance cooperation. Somalia's leadership should draw lessons from Djibouti's successful engagement with Ethiopia, focusing on economic ties that can withstand political tensions. By ensuring that partnerships contribute to Somalia's security and recovery, President Hassan Sheikh can navigate the complexities of regional dynamics while fostering a constructive relationship with Ethiopia. Ultimately, peace and collaboration can create opportunities for both nations and their citizens.
