Ethiopia called for stronger African Union (AU)-European Union (EU) cooperation in the field of Peace and Security - Social News XYZ

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 06:07:59 GMT

Ethiopia has called for enhanced cooperation between the African Union (AU) and the European Union (EU) in the realm of peace and security. This appeal was made by H.E. Ambassador Eshete Tilahun during the 2nd OACPS–EU Joint Parliamentary Assembly held on November 11, 2026, at the European Parliament in Brussels. The Ambassador emphasized the importance of effective multilateral governance that ensures equitable representation to tackle persistent global challenges. He argued that strengthening AU-EU collaboration is vital for the prevention and management of conflicts across Africa. Additionally, he highlighted the need for improved OACPS–EU cooperation through trade, investment, technology, and regional connectivity. Ambassador Tilahun reaffirmed Ethiopia's commitment to partnering with international entities to foster global peace, security, and sustainable development. This initiative reflects Ethiopia's proactive stance in addressing security issues and promoting stability within the continent and beyond.
