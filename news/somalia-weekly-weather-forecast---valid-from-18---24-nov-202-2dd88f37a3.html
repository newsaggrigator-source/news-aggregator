Somalia Weekly Weather Forecast - Valid From 18 - 24 Nov 2026 - ReliefWeb

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 01:05:22 GMT

The Somalia Weekly Weather Forecast for November 18-24, 2026, indicates significant rainfall in the southern regions, while the central and northern areas experienced minimal precipitation. Notable rainfall amounts were recorded in Bualle (159.9 mm), Balcad (115.0 mm), and Awdheegle (88.4 mm), whereas many stations in Bay, Hiraan, and central Somalia reported little to no rain. In the north, Las Dacawo and Elafweyn recorded 50.0 mm and 31.5 mm, respectively. Satellite estimates suggest 30-55 mm of rain over parts of Lower Juba and Middle Juba. By mid-November, 37 stations had met or exceeded their Deyr normal rainfall, with Baidoa receiving 405.5 mm, significantly above average. River levels are rising but remain below flood-risk thresholds, with Jowhar's floodwaters receding. Reports from Radio Ergo highlighted flash floods causing livestock deaths in Sanaag and a fatality in Hirshabelle, while many in the south welcomed the rains.
