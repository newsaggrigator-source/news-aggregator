Somalia Rejects Israeli Ambassador, Planned Embassy in Somaliland - allAfrica.com

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 04:30:54 GMT

Somalia has officially rejected Israel's appointment of an ambassador to Somaliland and the establishment of an Israeli embassy in Hargeisa, deeming these actions a violation of its sovereignty. The Somali Foreign Ministry stated that these moves were made without the consent of the Federal Government, labeling them as "null and void." Israel's ambassador, Michael Lotem, recently presented his credentials to Somaliland's President Abdirahman Mohamed Abdullahi Irro, coinciding with a ceremony for the new embassy. Somalia insists that Somaliland is an integral part of its territory and lacks the authority to engage in independent diplomatic relations. Following Israel's recognition of Somaliland in December 2025, Somalia has urged Israel to retract its decisions and called on the international community to refrain from actions that could threaten its territorial integrity, warning that these developments might escalate tensions in the strategically significant Red Sea and Gulf of Aden region.
