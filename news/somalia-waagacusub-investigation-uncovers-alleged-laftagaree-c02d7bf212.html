Somalia: Waagacusub Investigation Uncovers Alleged Laftagareen–Al-Shabaab Cooperation - Sunatimes.com

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 01:32:09 GMT

An investigation by Waagacusub Media has revealed alarming allegations of cooperation between forces loyal to Abdiaziz Laftagareen and the terrorist group Al-Shabaab in Somalia's South West State, particularly around Diinsoor. The report claims that these groups have coordinated military operations, shared resources, and interacted without hostility, raising serious concerns about the integrity of counterterrorism efforts. Local witnesses reported seeing Laftagareen-aligned troops and Al-Shabaab fighters sharing food and water, suggesting possible informal arrangements. Additionally, the investigation highlights troubling interactions between Ethiopian military personnel and Al-Shabaab fighters, including instances where they accessed the same water sources and allegedly assisted each other. If substantiated, these findings could indicate a broader pattern of collaboration that undermines regional security and civilian protection. The investigation calls for accountability from the involved parties and further scrutiny of the reported incidents to determine the nature and extent of these alleged alliances.
