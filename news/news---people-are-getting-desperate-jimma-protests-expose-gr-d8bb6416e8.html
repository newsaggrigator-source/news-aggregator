News - 'People Are Getting Desperate': Jimma Protests Expose Growing Anger Over Forced Conscription, Extortion

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 15:21:17 GMT

Protests erupted in Jimma city, Ethiopia, driven by widespread grievances over forced conscription, arbitrary arrests, extortion, and displacement. The demonstrations, particularly intense in the Bore neighborhood, were sparked by the killing of a former Ethiopian National Defense Force member but reflect deeper discontent regarding deteriorating security and administrative abuses. Residents report excessive taxation, demands for purchase receipts, and mistreatment by police and local militia, leading to a growing sense of desperation. Many young men have reportedly disappeared after being forcibly taken by security forces, leaving families in distress. The protests emerged from accumulated frustration rather than being organized by any specific group. Security forces have responded with arrests and pressure on residents to identify protest organizers. Allegations of forced conscription and extortion have been documented across Oromia, with regional authorities denying these claims and labeling them as propaganda. The situation highlights a climate of impunity and increasing unrest among the population. (158 words)
