Washington Protest Brings Horn of Africa Diaspora Over Ethiopia Conflicts, Regional Instability

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 05:32:25 GMT

On November 16, 2026, a coalition of Horn of Africa diaspora communities protested outside the U.S. Department of State in Washington, D.C., urging international intervention in the ongoing conflicts in Ethiopia and regional instability. Demonstrators, representing various Ethiopian ethnic groups and other Horn of Africa communities, carried flags and chanted slogans demanding an end to violence and accountability for atrocities. They criticized Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's military actions, which they claim have caused significant civilian suffering. The protest coincided with renewed fighting in Ethiopia and escalating tensions with Eritrea, prompting five Democratic senators to call for increased U.S. diplomatic efforts to prevent a broader conflict. The senators highlighted the fragile peace established by the November 2022 Pretoria Agreement and warned that continued hostilities could destabilize the Horn of Africa. They urged for a ceasefire and inclusive mediation involving all relevant parties, emphasizing the need for U.S. support against any aggressive territorial ambitions by Ethiopia.
