EU investigates reported hijacking of Iranian fishing vessel off Somalia - Horseed Media

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 14:49:14 GMT

The European Union's naval mission is investigating the reported hijacking of an Iranian fishing vessel, the Azini, off the northeastern coast of Somalia. The incident occurred on October 29, 2026, and has raised alarms regarding the security of commercial shipping in the region. The Maritime Security Centre–Indian Ocean (MSCIO) reported that the vessel was approaching a maritime traffic lane, raising concerns it could be used as a "mothership" for further piracy operations. The condition and location of the crew remain unknown, and the identities of the hijackers have not been established. The Azini reportedly held a fishing license from Puntland authorities. This incident follows recent maritime security efforts in Puntland, where local forces freed three cargo vessels from suspected pirates. Authorities are closely monitoring the situation to assess the potential threat to shipping in the area, as the use of fishing vessels by pirates could expand the operational range for attacks.
