Somali Army Chief Meets U.S. Chargé d’Affaires to Strengthen Defence Cooperation - FTL Somalia

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 06:11:15 GMT

Somali Army Chief General Odowa Yusuf Rageh met with the U.S. Chargé d’Affaires, Larry André, to discuss enhancing defense cooperation between Somalia and the United States. The meeting focused on strategies to combat terrorism and improve the capabilities of the Somali National Army. General Rageh emphasized the importance of U.S. support in strengthening Somalia's security forces, particularly in the fight against the militant group Al-Shabaab. The discussions also highlighted ongoing efforts to stabilize the region and ensure peace and security in Somalia. Both officials expressed commitment to deepening the partnership and addressing security challenges collaboratively.
