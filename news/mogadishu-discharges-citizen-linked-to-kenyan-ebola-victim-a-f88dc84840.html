Mogadishu Discharges Citizen Linked to Kenyan Ebola Victim After 21-Day Monitoring - FTL Somalia

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 10:34:59 GMT

Mogadishu's Ministry of Health and Human Services has discharged a Somali citizen who was under quarantine after being linked to a Kenyan Ebola victim. The individual was placed in quarantine on October 12, following direct contact with the deceased patient who died on October 5. After 21 days of monitoring and two negative test results—one conducted in Somalia and another confirmed by an international laboratory—the Ministry confirmed he was not infected with the virus. The Ministry emphasized that no Ebola cases have been confirmed in Somalia, and the quarantined individual was a case originating from outside the country. The release highlights the importance of disease surveillance and preparedness, especially as the region grapples with ongoing Ebola outbreaks. The Ministry continues to urge the public to seek information from official sources and remain vigilant about Ebola symptoms. Despite the successful discharge, concerns remain about Somalia's limited capacity to detect and respond to potential Ebola cases at its borders.
