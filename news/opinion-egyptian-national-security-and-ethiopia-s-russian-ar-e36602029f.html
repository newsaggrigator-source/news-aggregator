Opinion | Egyptian National Security and Ethiopia’s Russian Arms Deal: Strategic Interests and the Pressure of Sanctions - Daily News Egypt

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 20:36:37 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed recently announced the acquisition of 12 Su-35 fighter jets and S-400 air defense systems from Russia, marking a significant arms deal that enhances Ethiopia's military capabilities amid ongoing regional conflicts. This development raises concerns for Egypt, which previously faced U.S. pressure that halted its own purchase of Su-35s due to potential sanctions under the Countering America’s Adversaries Through Sanctions Act (CAATSA). The disparity in U.S. responses to the two nations stems from Egypt's deep military ties with the U.S., making sanctions more impactful, while Ethiopia's military relationship is less integrated. The article emphasizes that while the arms deal does not explicitly threaten Egypt, it could alter the regional balance of power. Egypt is urged to monitor the situation closely and engage in dialogue with Russia to safeguard its national interests, highlighting the need for a strategic approach to defense procurement that considers diverse suppliers and operational readiness.
