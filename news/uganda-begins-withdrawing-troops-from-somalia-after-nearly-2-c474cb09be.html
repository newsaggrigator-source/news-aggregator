Uganda begins withdrawing troops from Somalia after nearly 20 years - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 14:52:28 GMT

Uganda has initiated the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia after nearly 20 years of military presence, as announced by Gen. Muhoozi Kainerugaba, the chief of Uganda’s defense forces. This move marks the beginning of a security transition to Somali forces amid increasing financial and political pressures on the African Union mission, known as AUSSOM. Although the exact number of troops being withdrawn and the timeline for completion remain undisclosed, Uganda has been the largest contributor to AUSSOM, which was established to support Somali forces against the militant group al-Shabab. The mission aims to gradually transfer security responsibilities to Somali institutions, with a planned phased drawdown by December 2029, contingent on security conditions. Uganda's involvement has been significant, with troops engaged in key battles and suffering casualties. The withdrawal raises questions about the future of security in Somalia, especially given the ongoing threat from al-Shabab and the mission's funding challenges.
