Türkiye Rejects Reports of Plans to Deploy 15,000 Troops to Somalia - Horseed Media

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 15:48:30 GMT

Türkiye has officially denied reports suggesting plans to deploy 15,000 troops to Somalia, asserting that such claims do not reflect reality. The Turkish Ministry of National Defence clarified that there has been no request from the Somali government for this deployment. This statement followed the emergence of reports from Somali media, which indicated that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud had sought international military support amid increasing security challenges in Somalia. While Türkiye maintains an active military presence in Somalia, including training and advisory roles, the ministry emphasized that the existing military cooperation continues without any new troop deployments. The backdrop of these discussions includes the withdrawal of Ugandan forces from Somalia, raising concerns about the future of international military support in the region. Türkiye has been involved in military training and assistance in Somalia for over a decade, with significant investments in building Somali security capabilities.
