Somalia joins 17 countries in condemning Israeli strike on Gaza hospital - Hiiraan Online

Sun, 15 Nov 2026 19:56:22 GMT

Somalia has joined 17 other Arab and Islamic nations in condemning an Israeli airstrike on the Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis, Gaza, which resulted in casualties among patients and medical staff. In a joint statement released on November 14, the foreign ministers of these countries called for Israel to uphold the ceasefire, protect civilians, and allow unrestricted humanitarian aid into Gaza. They emphasized that the airstrike violated international humanitarian law, which safeguards healthcare facilities. The ministers also condemned ongoing Israeli attacks on Palestinian civilians and highlighted the worsening humanitarian crisis due to restrictions on essential supplies as winter approaches. They urged Israel to comply with international agreements and ensure the safe delivery of humanitarian assistance. The statement reflects Somalia's consistent support for Palestinian rights and its commitment to collaborating with regional and international partners to enhance humanitarian efforts and restore stability in the region.
