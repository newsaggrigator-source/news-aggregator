Northeastern State: Somalia’s Cornered Frontline Region in the Red Sea Proxy Contest. - pan african visions

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 15:23:38 GMT

Somalia's newly established Northeastern State has emerged as a focal point in the escalating Red Sea proxy contest, particularly highlighted by a map presented by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. This map categorizes the Northeastern State, inaugurated in January 2026, as part of a “Sunni Diamond” alliance alongside Türkiye, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and Pakistan, contrasting with the UAE and Israel-aligned regions of Somaliland and Puntland. The Northeastern State, which was formed from territories previously claimed by its neighbors, faces hostility from both regions, which have received significant support from the UAE and Israel. While the Northeastern State has gained local support following military victories, its legitimacy remains contested. The strategic implications of the map underscore the need for robust backing from its allies to ensure its survival against established adversaries. The recent Mecca Joint Defense Agreement among its supporters aims to bolster security, yet the Northeastern State requires more than diplomatic frameworks to navigate its precarious position.
