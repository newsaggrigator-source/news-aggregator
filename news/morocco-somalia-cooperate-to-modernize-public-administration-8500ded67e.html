Morocco, Somalia Cooperate to Modernize Public Administration - Mirage News

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 09:50:00 GMT

Morocco and Somalia have signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) to enhance public administration modernization, facilitated by the United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia (ESCWA). The agreement, signed on October 13 in Athens during the Rethinking Governance for a Changing World conference, aims to strengthen institutional cooperation between Morocco's École Nationale de l'Administration (ENA) and Somalia's National University (SNU). The MoU focuses on exchanging expertise and best practices in public administration, leadership, and management competency development. This initiative is part of broader efforts to improve governance and administrative reform in both countries, ultimately enhancing the efficiency and responsiveness of public institutions to citizens' needs.
