U.S. Reaffirms Commitment to Ethiopian Stability and Horn of Africa Security - Borkena

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 19:15:35 GMT

The U.S. has reaffirmed its commitment to Ethiopia's stability and security in the Horn of Africa during a meeting between U.S. Ambassador Ervin Massinga and Ethiopian Defense Minister Mohamed Idris. The discussions, held in Nairobi, focused on addressing regional security threats and promoting domestic peace initiatives. Ambassador Massinga highlighted the importance of ongoing defense cooperation and structured information sharing as essential components for fostering economic growth and national stability in Ethiopia. He reiterated the U.S. intention to maintain robust diplomatic and defense engagement to support peace efforts, counter regional instability, and encourage sustainable development throughout East Africa. This commitment underscores the U.S. role in enhancing security and stability in a region facing various challenges.
