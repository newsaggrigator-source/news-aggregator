Somalia army chief meets EU training mission commander - Horseed Media

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 13:50:00 GMT

Somalia's army chief, Major General Ibrahim Mohamed Mahmoud, recently met with Brigadier General Ivan Falasca, the commander of the European Union Training Mission in Somalia (EUTM-Somalia). Their discussions centered on enhancing military training and bolstering the capabilities of the Somali National Army (SNA). The officials emphasized the importance of expanding cooperation in military training and improving the skills of SNA personnel. They also addressed the need to enhance the quality of training programs for soldiers and officers, aiming to develop a professional and combat-ready national army capable of fulfilling its security responsibilities throughout Somalia. The meeting highlights ongoing efforts to strengthen Somalia's national forces and increase their role in security operations. The EUTM-Somalia is part of the EU's broader initiative to support Somalia's security sector, focusing on military training and capacity-building to develop the country's security forces and institutions. Continued collaboration is deemed essential for enhancing the operational capacity of the SNA.
