Eritrea Tells Us There Is No 'Crisis' Between Ethiopia and Eritrea

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 11:42:15 GMT

Eritrea's Ambassador to the UN, Sophia Tesfamariam, has asserted that there is no "crisis" between Eritrea and Ethiopia, despite recent tensions and accusations exchanged at the United Nations. In discussions with US diplomats, she emphasized the need for international attention to threats against Eritrea's sovereignty and territorial integrity. Ethiopia recently accused Eritrean forces of invading parts of Tigray, while Eritrea countered by alleging that Ethiopia is threatening war over access to the Red Sea. Sophia urged the international community to act responsibly to prevent further conflict and called for respect for Eritrea's sovereignty. She highlighted the importance of addressing underlying threats and upholding international law to avoid escalation in the region.
