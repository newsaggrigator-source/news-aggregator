Somalia offers condolences to Egypt after train collision kills seven, injures 125 - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 08:40:17 GMT

The Somali federal government has extended its condolences to Egypt following a tragic train collision in Giza province that resulted in seven fatalities and 125 injuries. The incident occurred on November 8 when a passenger train traveling from Giza to Fayoum collided with another train en route from Cairo to Aswan, leading to several carriages derailing. In a statement released on November 10, Somalia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs expressed solidarity with Egypt during this challenging time, offering prayers for the deceased and wishing a swift recovery for the injured. Emergency responders deployed 72 ambulances to assist victims, while local residents aided in rescue efforts. Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sissi has ordered an urgent investigation into the accident, emphasizing accountability for any negligence. Following the collision, railway services on the affected route were restored after crews cleared the wreckage and repaired the tracks.
