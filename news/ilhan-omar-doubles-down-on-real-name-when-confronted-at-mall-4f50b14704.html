Ilhan Omar doubles down on ‘real name’ when confronted at Mall of Somalia - Fox News

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 20:56:08 GMT

Rep. Ilhan Omar, D-Minn., reaffirmed that her "real name" is "Ilhan Abdullahi Omar" during an encounter at the Mall of Somalia, following a federal court filing that identified her as "Ilhan Nur Said Elmi," a name linked to her ex-husband, Ahmed Nur Said Elmi, who is alleged to be her brother. Omar's statement came amidst an ongoing immigration fraud investigation, where federal prosecutors referenced her in court documents using the disputed name. Omar's spokesperson has vehemently denied claims regarding her name and the allegations of her marriage to her brother, labeling them as "disgusting conspiracy theories." The controversy intensified after a raid on her Washington, D.C. residence by Homeland Security Investigations, part of a probe into potential immigration fraud involving her and her father. The investigation has led to subpoenas issued to her family members and the seizure of her cellphone. Omar's situation continues to draw significant media attention and public scrutiny.
