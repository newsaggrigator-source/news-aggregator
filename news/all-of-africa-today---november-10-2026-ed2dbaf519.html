All of Africa Today - November 10, 2026

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 07:04:57 GMT

On November 10, 2026, several significant events across Africa were reported. In South Africa, nine Zimbabweans were arrested for allegedly attempting to join the Russia-Ukraine war, facing charges under the Regulation of Foreign Military Assistance Act. Meanwhile, Kenyan authorities seized 454 kilograms of methamphetamine valued at $27 million, arresting three suspects linked to an international drug trafficking network. In Rwanda, the U.S. deported genocide suspect Jean Léonard Teganya, who is accused of participating in the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi. A tragic road accident in Tanzania resulted in 21 fatalities, prompting condolences from President Samia Suluhu Hassan. Additionally, around 50,000 Ethiopian refugees fleeing violence have crossed into Kenya, facing severe shortages of food and water. Lastly, Malawi announced a new strategy to combat organized crime, focusing on human trafficking and cybercrime, aiming to enhance cooperation among law enforcement agencies. These incidents highlight ongoing challenges and responses within the region. (159 words)
