UN Condemns Drone Strikes in Khartoum Suspected to Have Come From Ethiopia

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 12:22:51 GMT

The United Nations has condemned recent drone strikes in Khartoum, Sudan, believed to have originated from Ethiopia. The attacks, which occurred early on a Sunday morning, damaged several structures, including a UN building, although no casualties have been reported. A Sudanese military source indicated that the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) launched the drones from Ethiopian territory, amidst ongoing accusations from Sudan against Ethiopia for supporting the RSF in their conflict with the Sudanese army. The UN's humanitarian office expressed concern over the risks to humanitarian workers, labeling the situation as "unacceptable." The conflict has resulted in over 200,000 deaths and has triggered severe humanitarian crises, including widespread hunger and displacement. The UN has highlighted the increasing use of drones by both the RSF and the Sudanese army, which have led to significant civilian casualties in recent months.
