Somali Media Officials Begin 21-Day AI and Counter-Disinformation Training in China - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 12:40:20 GMT

A 21-day training program for 27 officials from Somalia’s Ministry of Information, Culture, and Tourism has commenced in Jinhua, China, focusing on artificial intelligence (AI) and counter-disinformation strategies. Conducted by experts from Zhejiang Normal University, the program aims to enhance the skills of Somali media personnel in modern media strategies, AI applications, and misinformation management. This initiative is part of Somalia's broader efforts to modernize its public communication systems amidst a rapidly evolving digital landscape. The training follows extensive engagements between Somalia and China on media cooperation throughout 2026, including discussions on media modernization led by Somalia's Information Minister. While the program is a significant step towards improving Somalia's media capabilities, it also highlights existing challenges, such as limited budgets and political interference that hinder effective communication. The effectiveness of the training will depend on how officials apply the knowledge gained in a context marked by skepticism towards state media and ongoing issues with digital censorship.
