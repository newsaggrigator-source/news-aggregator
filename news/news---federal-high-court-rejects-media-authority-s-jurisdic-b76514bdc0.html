News - Federal High Court Rejects Media Authority's Jurisdictional Objection, Decrees Hearing of Addis Standard's Case

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 15:12:14 GMT

The Federal High Court of Ethiopia has ruled against the Ethiopian Media Authority's jurisdictional objections, allowing a lawsuit from JAKENN Publishing PLC to proceed regarding the revocation of the Addis Standard's online media registration certificate. The court determined that the Media Authority, despite its independent status, is subject to judicial review under the Federal Administrative Procedure Proclamation No. 1183/2020. The Authority's claims that its regulatory decisions should be governed solely by the Media Proclamation were dismissed. The court highlighted that the Authority's failure to respond to JAKENN's grievance within the required timeframe constituted a rejection of the request, thus enabling the lawsuit to challenge the revocation. The court scheduled a hearing for December 9, 2026, to address the substantive issues of the case, which includes allegations that the revocation was made without proper warning or opportunity for response. This ruling marks a significant development in the ongoing regulatory dispute between the media outlet and the Authority.
