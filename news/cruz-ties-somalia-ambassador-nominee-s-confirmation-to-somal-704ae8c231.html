Cruz Ties Somalia Ambassador Nominee's Confirmation to Somaliland - Legis1

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 16:47:41 GMT

Senator Ted Cruz (R-TX) has linked the confirmation of Devin Kennington as the U.S. ambassador to Somalia to the State Department's stance on Somaliland. During a Senate Foreign Relations Committee hearing on November 10, Cruz emphasized that Kennington's confirmation hinges on the department adhering to President Trump's guidance regarding Somaliland's recognition. Kennington, when questioned about the barriers to U.S. recognition of Somaliland, stated that current policy upholds Somalia's sovereignty, which includes Somaliland. Cruz insisted that the State Department should align with the President's directives. The committee, chaired by Senator Jim Risch (R-ID), is reviewing multiple nominees, including those for ambassadorships in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and Ecuador. With a looming deadline for confirmations before the new Congress, Republicans are eager to finalize these nominations, as Democrats may pose challenges to controversial candidates in the future. No follow-up hearings or votes have been scheduled yet for the ten nominees under consideration.
