Ethiopia’s Deepening Human Rights and Humanitarian Crisis: - Borkena

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 04:54:02 GMT

Ethiopia is facing a severe human rights and humanitarian crisis, highlighted by an upcoming Congressional conference on December 11, 2026, aimed at addressing mass killings, religious persecution, and widespread displacement. Reports indicate that over 2.9 million people are internally displaced, with additional violence in regions like Amhara and Oromia exacerbating the situation. The conference will provide a platform for human rights advocates and policymakers to discuss the urgent need for international action and independent investigations into human rights violations, particularly against Ethiopian Orthodox Christians. Critics of Ethiopia's ethnic federalism argue it has deepened divisions and contributed to violence, necessitating a political framework that promotes equality and protects diversity. The Ethiopian government is urged to fulfill its constitutional duty to protect all citizens, while the international community is called upon to support accountability and civilian protection efforts. The U.S. can play a crucial role in promoting human rights and stability in Ethiopia through diplomatic engagement and legislative initiatives.
