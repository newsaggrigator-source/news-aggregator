Somalia: Ali Gudlawe Hussein re-elected as Hirshabelle leader - Horseed Media

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 15:39:29 GMT

Ali Gudlawe Hussein has been re-elected as the president of Hirshabelle State in Somalia, following a vote by 81 lawmakers in Jowhar, the state capital. His re-election was met with congratulations from Somalia’s President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, who also welcomed the newly elected vice-president, Abdifatah Hassan Afrah. President Mohamud emphasized the need for the new leadership to focus on peace, reconciliation, state-building, and enhancing public services in Hirshabelle. He praised the electoral process, underscoring the importance of strengthening democratic institutions and increasing public participation in Somalia's political landscape. However, the election has sparked controversy, particularly among segments of the Hiiraan community, who argue that the electoral process did not adequately represent their interests. This dispute occurs against a backdrop of broader tensions regarding Somalia's electoral system, with some opposition figures accusing the federal government of attempting to extend President Mohamud’s term beyond its scheduled end in May. The government has dismissed these allegations, asserting its commitment to democratic development.
