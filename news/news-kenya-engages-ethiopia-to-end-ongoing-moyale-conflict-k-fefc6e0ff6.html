Kenya's Inspector General of Police, Douglas Kanja, announced on November 5 that the country is actively engaging with Ethiopia to resolve the ongoing conflict in Moyale. During a high-level security visit to the region, Kanja emphasized the importance of collaboration between the two nations to restore peace and stability. The Moyale conflict has raised concerns over security and the well-being of residents in the border area. Kanja's delegation included various security officials who assessed the situation and discussed strategies to mitigate tensions. The Kenyan government is committed to fostering dialogue and cooperation with Ethiopian authorities to address the underlying issues contributing to the unrest. Both countries recognize the need for a coordinated approach to ensure lasting peace and prevent further escalation of violence in the region. This initiative reflects Kenya's broader commitment to regional stability and security cooperation with its neighbors.
