Trump says Ilhan Omar 'could go to jail' amid federal probe, calls Somalia a 's***house' - WION

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 01:14:00 GMT

Former President Donald Trump has intensified his attacks on U.S. Representative Ilhan Omar, suggesting she "could go to jail" amid a federal investigation into alleged immigration document irregularities. This scrutiny follows a reported search of Omar's Washington, D.C. residence by Homeland Security and ICE agents, which has raised questions about her citizenship status. Trump also made derogatory comments about Somalia, Omar's country of birth, calling it a "s***house" and criticizing her election to Congress. Omar has condemned the investigation as political intimidation and accused Trump of conducting smear campaigns against her. While she has largely remained out of public view, her family has been seen at her residence, further fueling speculation about the ongoing inquiry.
