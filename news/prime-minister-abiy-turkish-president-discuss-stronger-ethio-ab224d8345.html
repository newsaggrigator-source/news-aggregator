Prime Minister Abiy, Turkish President Discuss Stronger Ethiopia-T&#xFC;rkiye Ties

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 05:24:36 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed met with Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan in Antalya, Türkiye, prior to the COP31 Leaders' Summit. The discussions focused on enhancing bilateral relations, climate action cooperation, and addressing regional issues of mutual concern. PM Abiy praised Erdoğan for Türkiye's preparations for COP31 and emphasized the importance of their partnership, particularly as Ethiopia gears up to host COP32 in 2027. Both leaders expressed their commitment to translating shared priorities into concrete progress, highlighting the significance of Ethiopia-Türkiye cooperation in the context of their upcoming COP presidencies. The meeting underscores the growing collaboration between the two nations as they navigate climate challenges and regional developments together.
