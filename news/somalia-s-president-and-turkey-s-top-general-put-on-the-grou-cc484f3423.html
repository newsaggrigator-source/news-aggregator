Somalia’s president and Turkey’s top general laid the first stone for a new Somali Navy facility in Mogadishu, marking a significant step in a 2024 defense and economic agreement between the two nations. This initiative aims to bolster Somalia’s naval capabilities and enhance maritime security. Turkey, which already maintains a substantial military training base in Mogadishu and has warships operating in the region, is committed to supporting the reconstruction of Somalia’s navy. The establishment of this new facility is expected to strengthen Turkey's military presence along the Indian Ocean coast, further solidifying its role in regional security efforts. This collaboration reflects a growing partnership between Somalia and Turkey, focusing on defense and economic development to address security challenges in the waters surrounding Somalia.
