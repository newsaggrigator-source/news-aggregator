Somalia, Oman Discuss Expanding Trade and Investment Cooperation - Somali National News Agency

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 12:23:16 GMT

Somalia and Oman are actively working to enhance their trade and investment cooperation, as discussed in a recent meeting in Mogadishu. The Somali Minister of Commerce and Industry, H.E. Gamal Mohamed Hassan, along with other officials, met with the Omani Ambassador to Somalia, H.E. Salim bin Habib bin Salam Al Amiri. The primary focus of their discussions was on strengthening economic ties and exploring new business opportunities for entrepreneurs in both nations. This initiative aims to bolster bilateral trade relations and create a more favorable environment for investment. The meeting signifies a commitment from both countries to collaborate more closely in economic matters, potentially leading to significant advancements in trade and investment activities between Somalia and Oman. This cooperation is expected to benefit both economies and foster a stronger partnership moving forward.
