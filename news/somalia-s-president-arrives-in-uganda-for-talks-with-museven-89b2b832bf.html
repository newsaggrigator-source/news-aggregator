Somalia’s President Arrives in Uganda for Talks with Museveni on Regional Security, AUSSOM Transition - UG Diplomat

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 20:25:43 GMT

Somalia’s President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has arrived in Uganda for a one-day working visit to enhance bilateral relations and discuss regional security issues with President Yoweri Kaguta Museveni. The talks focus on the future of the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) and the transition of security responsibilities to Somalia’s National Armed Forces. This transition is crucial as Somalia aims to strengthen its capacity to secure its territory against threats from Al-Shabaab militants. Uganda has been a significant contributor of troops to AUSSOM, making these discussions vital for both nations. Additionally, the leaders are expected to address broader regional security concerns and explore economic cooperation opportunities. This visit highlights the strategic importance of Uganda-Somalia relations, particularly as Somalia navigates a critical phase in its security transition and institutional consolidation.
