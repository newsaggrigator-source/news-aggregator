The age of pirates isn't over: Modern-day Somali raiders are hijacking oil tankers, holding sailors hostag - The Economic Times

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 17:12:39 GMT

Modern piracy off the coast of Somalia has resurfaced, with armed Somali raiders hijacking oil tankers and holding crews hostage for ransom. A recent incident involved the Honour 25, a diesel tanker seized on April 21, 2026, while transporting fuel to Mogadishu. The crew of 17 was held captive for several months, leading to a tragic rescue attempt in September that resulted in the deaths of five crew members. Despite international efforts to combat piracy, economic instability in Somalia and shifting military focus due to the Iranian conflict have created new opportunities for pirates. The resurgence of piracy not only threatens the safety of seafarers but also disrupts global shipping routes, leading to increased costs for shipping and insurance that could ultimately impact consumers. The Honour 25 incident highlights the ongoing vulnerabilities in maritime security and the complex factors contributing to the return of piracy in the region.
