Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has unveiled a regional security map that categorizes Ethiopia and Sudan's Rapid Support Forces (RSF) as "friendly" to Israel. This development highlights Israel's strategic interests in the Horn of Africa, particularly in relation to security partnerships. The map suggests a shift in regional dynamics and reflects Israel's efforts to strengthen ties with countries in the area, including the United Arab Emirates (UAE). The portrayal of the RSF as friendly indicates a potential alignment of interests amid ongoing conflicts and geopolitical changes in Sudan. This move is part of Netanyahu's broader strategy to enhance Israel's influence and security in the region.
