Obituary - Prominent Kenyan Oromo Scholar, Anthropologist Professor Abdullahi Shongolo Passes Away

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 05:14:50 GMT

Professor Abdullahi Shongolo, a distinguished Kenyan Oromo scholar and anthropologist, passed away on October 20, 2026, at the age of 68 in Nanyuki Hospital, Kenya, after a prolonged illness. Born in 1958 in Moyale, he dedicated his life to the study of Borana Oromo culture, history, and customary law, significantly enhancing the community's global recognition. Shongolo's academic journey included roles in Kenya's Ministry of Education, where he became Assistant Director, and extensive research at Germany's Max Planck Institute for Social Anthropology from 1999 to 2019. He also contributed to the University of Gothenburg in Sweden as an Associate Research Fellow. His notable works, including collaborations with Professor Günther Schlee, explored themes of pastoralism, politics, and the cultural context of Borana proverbs. His death has deeply affected the Borana Oromo community and the broader Oromo population, as well as scholars worldwide. His funeral took place on October 21 in Nanyuki, marking a significant loss for many.
