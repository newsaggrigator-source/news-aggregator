Drone Strikes Kill Schoolchildren in Amhara, Civilians in Tigray Regions As Conflicts Show No Sign of Abating

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 15:25:12 GMT

On October 20, 2026, drone strikes in Ethiopia's Amhara and Tigray regions resulted in the deaths of schoolchildren and civilians amid ongoing conflicts. In Amhara, a drone strike targeted Sana Elementary School in Dera Woreda, killing at least six schoolchildren and injuring over 20 others. Eyewitnesses reported chaos as parents rushed to the school to help their injured children. A teacher disputed claims that the school was a target for armed groups, emphasizing the presence of students and the absence of conflict nearby. In Tigray, a separate drone strike in Finaruo village killed four people, including three relatives, while they were loading livestock. The strikes highlight the increasing impact of aerial warfare on civilian areas, raising concerns about safety and humanitarian conditions. The renewed fighting follows the collapse of a previous peace agreement, leading to significant civilian casualties and displacements across both regions. Humanitarian organizations estimate that at least 150,000 people have been displaced due to the ongoing violence.
