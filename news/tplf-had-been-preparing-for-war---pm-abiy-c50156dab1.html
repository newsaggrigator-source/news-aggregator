TPLF Had Been Preparing for War - PM Abiy

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 13:37:05 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed accused the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) of preparing for war and violating the Pretoria Agreement, which was intended to end hostilities and restore services in the region. In an interview, he stated that while the federal government had complied with the agreement, the TPLF had been procuring weapons and recruiting young people instead of disarming. Abiy highlighted the TPLF's use of displaced individuals as hostages and its destruction of infrastructure. He urged Tigrayan youth to abandon violence and focus on development, emphasizing that war brings no benefits and inflicts significant psychological and economic damage. The Prime Minister criticized the TPLF's leadership for avoiding personal risk while promoting conflict, asserting that their actions contradict their claims of being a popular movement.
