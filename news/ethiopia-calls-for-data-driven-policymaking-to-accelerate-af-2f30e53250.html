Ethiopia Calls for Data-Driven Policymaking to Accelerate Africa's Progress

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 12:16:54 GMT

Ethiopia is advocating for data-driven policymaking to enhance development across Africa, as emphasized by President Taye Atske Selassie during the 10th Session of the Statistical Commission for Africa. The country has reformed its statistical legislation and established an independent statistical service to improve the production and coordination of official data. President Taye highlighted the necessity of reliable statistics for effective development planning and accountability. He also discussed Ethiopia's advancements in financial and digital sectors, including the National Digital Payment Strategy and the Fayda digital identification program, which have significantly expanded access to essential services. The President urged African governments to protect the independence of statistical institutions and invest in national statistical systems, stressing that poor-quality data hinders evidence-based policymaking. The joint meetings aim to strengthen data systems and improve monitoring of development progress across the continent.
