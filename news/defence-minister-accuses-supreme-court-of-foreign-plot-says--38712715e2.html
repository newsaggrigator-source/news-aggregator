Defence Minister Accuses Supreme Court of Foreign Plot, Says Ruling Was an Abu Dhabi Agenda - FTL Somalia

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 19:24:05 GMT

Somalia's Defence Minister, Ahmed Moallim Fiqi, has publicly accused the Supreme Court and its dismissed Chief Justice of being part of a foreign conspiracy, specifically alleging an agenda orchestrated by Abu Dhabi. Following the Supreme Court's ruling on November 5, which declared the mandates of the President and Parliament expired and called for elections within 90 days, Fiqi framed the decision as the result of a plot involving "mercenaries" within the judiciary. He claimed that the Chief Justice's dismissal was lawful and criticized his subsequent refuge with former President Sharif Sheikh Ahmed, suggesting complicity in the alleged foreign agenda. This marks a significant escalation in the government's response to the court's ruling, as the Attorney General has already rejected it as lacking legal basis. Fiqi's statements aim to delegitimize the judiciary while framing the opposition as foreign agents, raising concerns about the integrity of both the government and judiciary amidst a deepening constitutional crisis.
