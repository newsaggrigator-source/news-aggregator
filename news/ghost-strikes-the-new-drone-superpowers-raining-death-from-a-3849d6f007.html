Ghost strikes: the new drone superpowers raining death from above - The Guardian

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 10:33:00 GMT

The rise of drone warfare, particularly in Somalia, has led to a phenomenon known as "ghost strikes," where victims are killed from the sky without warning or accountability. Various nations, including the US, UAE, Turkey, and Somalia, operate drones in the region, targeting suspected militants but often resulting in civilian casualties. The lack of transparency surrounding these strikes complicates efforts to identify responsible parties, as many countries do not disclose their drone operations. The proliferation of affordable drones has enabled both state and non-state actors to engage in remote warfare, increasing civilian deaths and making accountability difficult. In Somalia, a recent incident highlighted this issue when a family was killed in a drone strike, with no clear indication of who was responsible. As drone technology becomes more accessible, the landscape of warfare is shifting, raising significant concerns about civilian protection and the ethical implications of remote combat.
