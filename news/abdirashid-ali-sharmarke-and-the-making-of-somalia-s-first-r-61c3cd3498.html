Abdirashid Ali Sharmarke and the Making of Somalia’s First Republic - www.geeska.com

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 10:39:10 GMT

Abdirashid Ali Sharmarke played a pivotal role in the establishment of Somalia’s first republic, advocating for early independence and unifying the nation’s institutions. Born in 1919, Sharmarke's political journey began with the Somali Youth League, leading to his election to the Legislative Assembly in 1959. He was instrumental in pushing for Somalia's independence, which was achieved on July 1, 1960, ahead of schedule. As Prime Minister from 1960 to 1964, he focused on integrating the former British Protectorate of Somaliland and the UN Trust Territory of Somalia, successfully unifying their judicial and civil systems. Sharmarke later became President in 1967 but was assassinated in 1969, raising numerous unanswered questions about his death. His legacy remains significant, as he is remembered for his vision of Somali unity and the challenges he faced, particularly regarding the Greater Somalia Policy, which sought to unify all Somali-inhabited territories in the Horn of Africa.
