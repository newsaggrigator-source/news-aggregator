From Military Victory to Political Settlement - Why Ethiopia's Real Test Lies Beyond Controlling Mekelle

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 04:52:17 GMT

Ethiopia's recent military control of Mekelle, the capital of Tigray, marks a significant shift in the ongoing conflict, which has seen the city change hands multiple times since 2020. Federal forces, alongside the Tigray Peace Force, re-entered Mekelle on October 3, 2026, following the TPLF's withdrawal. While the mood in Addis Ababa is celebratory, the situation remains complex. The war's roots lie in unresolved political grievances, with multiple authorities claiming governance over Tigray. The federal government faces challenges in establishing legitimacy and authority, as many Tigrayans view the TPLF as their representative. The humanitarian crisis in the region is dire, with millions needing assistance. The future stability of Ethiopia hinges on whether the federal government can gain the consent of Tigrayans and address the underlying sociopolitical issues rather than merely exert military control.
