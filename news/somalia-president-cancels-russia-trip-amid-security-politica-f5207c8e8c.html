Somalia president cancels Russia trip amid security, political pressures - Somali Guardian

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 04:31:50 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has canceled a planned trip to Russia due to escalating security and political pressures at home. This decision follows a deadly ambush by Al Shabaab in the Lower Shabelle region, which resulted in the deaths of numerous Somali soldiers. Additionally, Uganda, a key contributor to the African Union forces in Somalia, has urged Mohamud to initiate talks with the opposition to avoid troop withdrawal. During a recent visit to Uganda, Mohamud sought to secure continued military support but faced resistance from President Yoweri Museveni, who cited economic constraints. In a shift towards dialogue, Mohamud has expressed a willingness to compromise on contentious issues, aiming to ease political tensions and stabilize the country. His recent actions reflect a strategic move to address both internal security challenges and external diplomatic pressures.
