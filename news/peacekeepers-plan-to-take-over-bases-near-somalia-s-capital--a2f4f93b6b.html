Peacekeepers plan to take over bases near Somalia’s capital as Ugandan troops withdraw - Ottumwa Courier

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 09:12:46 GMT

Peacekeepers are set to take over four military bases near Mogadishu, Somalia, as Ugandan troops begin their withdrawal, which is expected to be completed by the end of 2027. This transition aims to prevent al-Shabab militants from exploiting the security vacuum left by the departing Ugandan forces, who have been stationed in Somalia for two decades under a bilateral agreement with the Somali government. The African Union mission has communicated this plan to the U.N. support office in Somalia, emphasizing the need for a seamless handover to maintain stability in the region. Uganda's withdrawal marks a significant shift in the security landscape, as the country has been a key contributor to the African Union peacekeeping efforts in Somalia. The situation remains critical as peacekeepers prepare to fill the gap and counter potential threats from militant groups.
