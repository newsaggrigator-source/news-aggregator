Somalia, U.S. discuss security transition and fight against al-Shabab, ISIS - Hiiraan Online

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 09:30:57 GMT

Somali Defense Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi met with U.S. Embassy Chargé d’Affaires Ruth Anne Stevens-Klitz in Mogadishu to discuss military cooperation and counterterrorism efforts against al-Shabab and ISIS. The meeting focused on the transition of security responsibilities from African Union forces to the Somali National Army, emphasizing the need for adequate training and resources for Somali troops. Fiqi updated Stevens-Klitz on military operations against al-Shabab and reiterated the federal government's commitment to developing a capable national army. They also discussed the recent handover of a former AUSSOM military base to Somali forces, part of a broader strategy to enhance Somalia's control over its security. The U.S. remains a crucial partner, providing training and operational support, although no new military assistance package was announced. The discussions highlight the ongoing efforts to ensure a successful security transition while maintaining collaboration with international partners. (152 words)
