Indian Navy Frees 20 Crew After Somalia Boat Hijacking - JournalArta

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 23:54:13 GMT

The Indian Navy successfully rescued 20 crew members from an Iranian fishing boat, the Azini, that had been hijacked off the coast of Somalia. The operation, which lasted over 48 hours, concluded in the Arabian Sea, more than 3,000 kilometers from Mumbai. India’s Defense Minister Rajnath Singh confirmed the crew's safety following the rescue, which involved disarming 12 suspected Somali pirates. These suspects were subsequently released by Indian forces, along with their mobile phones, and later reached Bandar Bayla in northeastern Somalia. While the Indian Ministry reported no casualties during the operation, a Puntland Maritime Police official mentioned that three individuals who came ashore had minor injuries, though it was unclear whether they were pirates or security guards. The details surrounding the hijacking and the tactics used by the Indian Navy remain undisclosed, highlighting the complexities of maritime security in the region.
