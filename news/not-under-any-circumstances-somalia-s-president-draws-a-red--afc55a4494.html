“Not Under Any Circumstances”: Somalia’s President Draws a Red Line on Foreign Bases and Israeli Presence - FTL Somalia

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 10:50:05 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has firmly stated that his government will not allow foreign military bases on Somali soil, emphasizing national sovereignty amid rising geopolitical tensions in the Red Sea and Horn of Africa. In a recent interview, he highlighted concerns over the activities of countries like Russia, Israel, and the UAE, particularly regarding Israel's alleged plans to establish a naval base in Berbera. Mohamud also confirmed the cancellation of defense agreements with the UAE, citing actions that undermine Somalia's sovereignty. He downplayed the significance of Somaliland's diplomatic ties with Israel, asserting that Somalia's unity remains intact. The president's stance reflects a desire to prevent Somalia from becoming a battleground for foreign interests, although the reality of Somalia's security dependence on foreign partners complicates this position. The government's ability to navigate these relationships will be crucial in maintaining its declared policy against foreign military bases.
