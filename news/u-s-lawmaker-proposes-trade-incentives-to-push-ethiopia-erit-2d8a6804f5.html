U.S. Lawmaker Proposes Trade Incentives to Push Ethiopia, Eritrea Off Conflict Cliff, Toward Peace

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 10:35:00 GMT

U.S. Republican Representative Jim Baird has introduced two legislative proposals aimed at fostering peace between Ethiopia and Eritrea amid rising tensions that threaten renewed conflict. The Ethiopia SPARK Act and Eritrea PILLAR Act seek to link expanded trade and economic cooperation with progress towards lasting peace. These initiatives would offer U.S. agricultural exports, technical expertise, and cooperation in mineral development as incentives for both nations to pursue peaceful relations. The proposals also aim to enhance U.S. economic influence in the Horn of Africa while countering China's presence in the region. With the backdrop of deteriorating diplomatic relations and military buildups along their shared border, Baird emphasizes the potential for economic partnerships to promote regional stability. The full legislative texts are still in draft form, and their success will depend on congressional support. Neither government has publicly commented on the proposals yet.
