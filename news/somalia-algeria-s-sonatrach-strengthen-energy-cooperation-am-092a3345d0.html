Somalia, Algeria's Sonatrach Strengthen Energy Cooperation Amid Growing Interest in Oil and Gas Sector - Horseed Media

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 06:51:59 GMT

Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud recently met with a delegation from Algeria's state-owned oil and gas company, Sonatrach, to enhance cooperation in oil exploration and gas development. This meeting follows previous engagements between Sonatrach and Somali petroleum officials, including visits to Mogadishu's national oil depot. The partnership, established during President Mohamud's 2025 visit to Algeria, focuses on knowledge transfer, professional training, and building Somalia's technical capacity rather than direct commercial investment. Sonatrach has committed to sharing expertise in oil exploration and refining, as well as providing 500 scholarships for Somali students in petroleum engineering. Despite the high-level discussions, no formal agreement for oil exploration or investment has been announced yet. The collaboration signifies a strategic partnership that not only aims to develop Somalia's hydrocarbons sector but also strengthens diplomatic ties, as Algeria supports Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity. This engagement reflects Somalia's ongoing efforts to attract international expertise for its natural resource management.
