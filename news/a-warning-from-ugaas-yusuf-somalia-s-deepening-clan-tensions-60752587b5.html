A Warning from Ugaas Yusuf: Somalia’s Deepening Clan Tensions and the Risk of Renewed Civil Conflict - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 20:13:32 GMT

Ugaas Yusuf, the traditional chief elder of the Hawadle community, has issued a grave warning regarding escalating clan tensions in Somalia, particularly targeting the Hiraab political establishment linked to President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud. His remarks highlight a troubling trend where political disputes are increasingly framed through clan identities, undermining the federal government’s efforts to promote national unity and constitutional governance. Yusuf's appeal to Hawadle soldiers to align with their community underscores a significant breakdown in trust towards state institutions. The risk of renewed civil conflict looms as political exclusion and competing claims to authority intensify. The international community is urged to facilitate dialogue, ensure the neutrality of national security forces, and support an inclusive political settlement. Without addressing these grievances, Somalia risks descending into further violence, necessitating immediate diplomatic intervention to foster peace and accountable leadership. The situation calls for a government that unites rather than divides its citizens.
