Uganda Begins Withdrawal of Troops From Somalia After Nearly 20 Years - Garowe Online

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:19:42 GMT

Uganda has commenced the withdrawal of its troops from Somalia after nearly 20 years of military involvement, a significant shift in the African Union's security mission. The announcement was made by Uganda's Chief of Defence Forces, Gen. Muhoozi Kainerugaba, who stated that the country is transferring security responsibilities to Somali forces. This decision comes amid financial strains and a 25% reduction in funding from the United Nations Support Office in Somalia, raising concerns about the sustainability of the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM). Uganda has been a key player in the fight against al-Shabab, deploying troops under the African Union Mission in Somalia (AMISOM) since 2007. The withdrawal raises questions about Somalia's capacity to manage its security independently as international support diminishes. The African Union has pledged continued assistance to Somalia despite these challenges, emphasizing the need for political and financial support during this transition.
