Somalia’s Aid Paradox: Saving lives, sustaining dependency - Addis Standard

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 05:33:10 GMT

The article discusses the complex situation in Somalia regarding international aid, highlighting a paradox where humanitarian assistance saves lives but may also perpetuate dependency. While aid is crucial for addressing immediate needs, such as food insecurity and health crises, it can create a cycle of reliance that undermines local development and self-sufficiency. Critics argue that without a shift towards sustainable solutions, including investment in local economies and infrastructure, the country may remain trapped in a cycle of aid dependency. The article emphasizes the need for a balanced approach that combines immediate relief efforts with long-term strategies to empower communities and foster resilience.
