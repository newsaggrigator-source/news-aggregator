Somalia Supreme Court declares president’s term extension unlawful - Tribune Online

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 21:59:34 GMT

The Somalia Supreme Court has ruled that President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's term has expired, mandating elections within 90 days. This decision escalates the ongoing political turmoil in the country, particularly following Mohamud's dismissal of the chief justice just a day prior. The ruling raises questions about the government's compliance and the future of the electoral process, as tensions between the presidency and judiciary intensify. The political crisis is compounded by the recent closure of the United Nations political mission, which had facilitated negotiations among rival leaders. With no clear mediator in place, the prospects for a peaceful resolution appear bleak. The Supreme Court's intervention places significant pressure on Mohamud's administration, as the deadline for elections looms. The situation in Mogadishu remains precarious, with the potential for further unrest if political disagreements are not resolved. This development underscores the challenges Somalia faces in achieving political stability amid ongoing disputes over the electoral process.
