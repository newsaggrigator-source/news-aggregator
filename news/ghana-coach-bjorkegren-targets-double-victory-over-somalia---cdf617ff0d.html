Ghana coach Bjorkegren targets double victory over Somalia - TribalFootball

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 19:58:33 GMT

Ghana's interim coach, Kim Lars Bjorkegren, is aiming for a double victory against Somalia in their upcoming matches as part of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifying campaign. Ghana, having lost their first two qualifiers against Ivory Coast and Gambia, is under pressure to secure maximum points from these encounters to improve their standing in Group C. The first match will take place at the Essipong Stadium, followed by a return fixture in Somalia. Bjorkegren emphasized the importance of respecting their opponents while expressing confidence in Ghana's ability to perform well and create scoring opportunities. He highlighted the need for an attacking approach to ensure success. Forward Brandon Thomas-Asante noted the positive atmosphere generated by local supporters, which he believes will aid the team's performance. The Black Stars are determined to turn their fortunes around and regain momentum in the qualifying process.
