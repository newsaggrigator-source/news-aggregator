President Samia Arrives in Ethiopia for African Public Health Conference

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 05:18:07 GMT

Tanzanian President Samia Suluhu Hassan has arrived in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, for a two-day visit to participate in the Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026). Her arrival at Bole International Airport on November 22, 2026, marks her engagement in a high-level continental health conference aimed at discussing strategies to enhance public health systems across Africa. During her visit, President Samia is set to launch the African Maternal and Child Health Improvement Roadmap, an initiative focused on improving healthcare services for mothers and children throughout the continent. This participation provides Tanzania with an opportunity to collaborate with other African nations and stakeholders to tackle public health challenges and advance health priorities. The visit highlights Tanzania's commitment to regional cooperation in strengthening healthcare systems and enhancing the well-being of its citizens and those across Africa.
