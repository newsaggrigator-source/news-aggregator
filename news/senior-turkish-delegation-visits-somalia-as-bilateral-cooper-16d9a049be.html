Senior Turkish Delegation Visits Somalia as Bilateral Cooperation Expands - Horseed Media

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 14:11:00 GMT

A senior Turkish delegation, led by Fatih Dulkan, vice-president of the Turkish Space Agency, has visited Somalia to enhance bilateral cooperation in various sectors, including space technology, industry, and defense. The delegation, which includes officials from Türkiye’s Ministry of Industry and Technology and the defense company Roketsan, engaged in discussions with Somali officials and inspected projects in Mogadishu and Warsheikh. This visit underscores the expanding partnership between Somalia and Turkey, moving beyond traditional cooperation to encompass advanced technological and industrial collaboration. As both nations work to strengthen their ties, this visit marks a significant step in fostering strategic relationships in technology and defense industries. The ongoing collaboration reflects a commitment to mutual development and innovation, highlighting the importance of international partnerships in addressing contemporary challenges.
