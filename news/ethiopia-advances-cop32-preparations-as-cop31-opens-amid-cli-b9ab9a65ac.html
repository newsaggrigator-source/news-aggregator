Ethiopia Advances COP32 Preparations As COP31 Opens Amid Climate Finance Disputes

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 15:25:37 GMT

Ethiopia is leveraging its participation in the 31st United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Türkiye, to prepare for hosting COP32 in Addis Abeba in November 2027. Foreign Affairs Minister Gedion Timothewos, who is the president-designate of COP32, emphasized that Ethiopia's involvement at COP31 aims to lay the groundwork for a successful summit. The country has opened an official pavilion to showcase its climate mitigation efforts and engage with international partners. However, COP31 has revealed ongoing disputes over climate finance, particularly as developing nations advocate for stronger financial commitments from wealthier countries. UN Secretary-General António Guterres has called for urgent action on emissions reductions and financing for vulnerable nations. The challenges surrounding climate finance will be critical for Ethiopia as it prepares to host COP32, which aims to focus on implementation and international cooperation in climate action. Ethiopia's participation at COP31 is seen as a vital opportunity to build necessary partnerships and negotiating experience.
