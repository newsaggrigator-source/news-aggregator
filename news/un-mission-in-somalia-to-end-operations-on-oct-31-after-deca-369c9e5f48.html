UN mission in Somalia to end operations on Oct. 31 after decades of support - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 19:43:11 GMT

The United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) will conclude its operations on October 31, marking the end of over three decades of UN involvement in the country. This decision follows a two-year transition period and aligns with requests from Somalia's federal government and Security Council resolutions. UN spokesman Stephane Dujarric emphasized that this milestone signifies the transfer of UNTMIS responsibilities to Somali institutions and various UN agencies. Despite the mission's closure, UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres assured continued support for Somalia through resident agencies, focusing on peace, stability, human rights, and sustainable development. He highlighted the ongoing political, security, and humanitarian challenges in the country, calling for sustained international support to foster political dialogue, reconciliation, and institutional strengthening. Guterres reaffirmed the UN's commitment to aiding Somalia in its efforts to achieve a peaceful and prosperous future.
