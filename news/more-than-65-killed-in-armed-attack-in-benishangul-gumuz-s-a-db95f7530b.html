More Than 65 Killed in Armed Attack in Benishangul-Gumuz's Assosa Zone, Residents Say

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 15:23:28 GMT

More than 65 people were killed in a violent armed attack in the Dalati locality of Buldiglu Woreda, Assosa Zone, in Ethiopia's Benishangul-Gumuz region. The assault, which occurred on November 5 and 6, targeted regional anti-riot police, local militias, and civilians, resulting in significant casualties, including at least 27 civilians. Eyewitnesses reported that the attackers were heavily armed and some wore former security uniforms. Local reports suggest the death toll may exceed 100, although official figures from the Benishangul-Gumuz Regional Police Commission indicate 52 deaths. The attack is attributed to the armed group OLF-Shene, amidst ongoing security tensions and violence in the region, which has seen recurrent armed conflicts and counterinsurgency operations. Despite previous peace agreements with local armed groups, sporadic violence persists, raising concerns about the stability and security of the area. The federal government's response during the attack has been criticized for its lack of intervention. (159 words)
