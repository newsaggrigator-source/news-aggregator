Museveni Urges Somalia to Unite Against Al-Shabaab, Opens Door for Ugandan Coffee Exports - SoftPower News

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 14:08:26 GMT

During a recent meeting in Entebbe, Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni urged Somali leaders and citizens to unite against the militant group Al-Shabaab, emphasizing that ideological divisions have hindered peace efforts in Somalia. Museveni highlighted the need for a collective approach to address security challenges, warning that fragmentation allows a minority to maintain influence over the majority. The discussions also included enhancing bilateral trade, particularly through Ugandan coffee exports to Somalia, with plans for a Ugandan warehouse in Somalia to facilitate direct access to the market. Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud acknowledged Uganda's military support in stabilizing Somalia and expressed gratitude for the welcoming environment for Somali nationals in Uganda. The meeting underscored the importance of combining security cooperation with economic partnerships to strengthen relations between the two nations and open new markets for Ugandan products.
