Ethiopia's Su-35 fighter jets Arrive Amid Regional Tensions - https://kulu-media.com/

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 14:58:30 GMT

Ethiopia has received 12 advanced Russian Su-35 fighter jets, significantly enhancing its military capabilities amid escalating regional tensions, particularly concerning the ongoing conflict in Tigray and deteriorating relations with Eritrea. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announced the delivery on October 28, 2026, highlighting that the jets were provided under a defense agreement with Russia, which also includes plans for four S-400 air-defense systems. The acquisition aims to bolster Ethiopia's national security and sovereignty, especially as fighting intensifies in northern Ethiopia. Abiy's visit to Russia for the third Russia–Africa Summit coincided with the announcement, emphasizing the strengthening military ties between the two nations. The Su-35 jets are designed for both air-to-air and air-to-ground combat, potentially expanding Ethiopia's operational capabilities. However, the immediate deployment and operational readiness of the jets remain unclear, and international concerns about a broader conflict in the region are growing, with the U.S. urging restraint.
