Somali Airlines sets Dec. 10 relaunch date after 35 years of inactivity - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 08:59:25 GMT

Somali Airlines is set to relaunch on December 10, 2026, after 35 years of inactivity, as announced by Transport and Civil Aviation Minister Mohamed Farah Nuh. The airline, which ceased operations in 1991 due to the civil war, has faced multiple delays in its revival despite government promises. Preparations for the relaunch are reportedly underway, with an official ceremony planned for next month. The government previously announced plans to acquire two Airbus A320 aircraft and has now arranged to lease a Dash 8-400 turboprop from Daallo Airlines. However, details regarding initial destinations, ticket prices, and the start of commercial flights remain unclear. The relaunch is part of broader efforts by the federal government to rebuild national infrastructure and enhance air transport connections. Despite the optimism surrounding the announcement, skepticism persists due to past postponements of the airline's return to service.
