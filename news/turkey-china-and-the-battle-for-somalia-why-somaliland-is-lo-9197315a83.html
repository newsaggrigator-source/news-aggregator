Turkey, China and the Battle for Somalia: Why Somaliland Is Looking Towards India - Goa Chronicle

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 17:38:14 GMT

As Turkey and China expand their influence in Somalia, Somaliland is increasingly looking towards India as a strategic partner. Turkey has established a significant military presence in Somalia, including a military training facility and agreements on petroleum exploration and maritime security. Meanwhile, China focuses on protecting its diplomatic interests and limiting Taiwan's influence, particularly in light of Somaliland's growing ties with Taiwan. Somaliland, which declared independence in 1991, seeks to diversify its international relationships and is interested in economic partnerships with India, particularly in sectors like healthcare, technology, and infrastructure. This shift reflects Somaliland's desire for meaningful cooperation rather than mere recognition. India's involvement in Somaliland could enhance its strategic position in the western Indian Ocean, crucial for trade and energy security. However, India must navigate its existing diplomatic relations with Somalia while exploring opportunities in Somaliland, which holds significant potential for development and regional stability.
