‘She winked at me’: Ilhan Omar refused to walk comments back pledging allegiance to Somalia - News24

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 00:53:54 GMT

Ilhan Omar, the U.S. Congresswoman, has faced backlash for comments she made regarding her allegiance to Somalia. During a recent event, she stated, "I will always have a deep allegiance to my homeland," which has sparked controversy among critics who interpret her remarks as disloyal to the United States. Despite the criticism, Omar has refused to retract her statement, emphasizing her connection to her roots and the importance of her heritage. She described a moment where a woman in the audience winked at her, suggesting a shared understanding of her sentiments. Omar's comments have reignited discussions about the complexities of identity and loyalty among immigrant communities in the U.S.
