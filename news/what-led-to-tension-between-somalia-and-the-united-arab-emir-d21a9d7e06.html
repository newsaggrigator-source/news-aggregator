What led to tension between Somalia and the United Arab Emirates? - ACLED

Mon, 23 Nov 2026 19:50:58 GMT

Tensions between Somalia and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) escalated after Somalia's federal government accused the UAE of interfering in its internal affairs and undermining national sovereignty. On November 17, 2026, Somalia called for the expulsion of the UAE ambassador and insisted that all dealings with the UAE be conducted through the federal government. The UAE denied these accusations, asserting that Somalia's government was prioritizing personal interests over national ones. The UAE maintains relationships with various regional administrations in Somalia, which often conflict with the federal government, and has been involved in military operations against insurgent groups like Al Shabaab. The deterioration of relations coincided with failed national election talks, further complicating the political landscape. The Somali government’s actions could strain its relations with regional entities and deepen political instability, while the opposition alliance views the government's rhetoric as an attempt to consolidate power ahead of elections.
