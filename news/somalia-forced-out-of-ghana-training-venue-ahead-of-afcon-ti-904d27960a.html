Somalia forced out of Ghana training venue ahead of AFCON ti - Punch Newspapers

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 23:17:51 GMT

Somalia's national football team faced a setback in their preparations for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying matches against Ghana after being forced out of the University of Ghana Stadium during a training session. On November 11, 2026, security officers intervened shortly after the team began their practice, directing them to leave the facility despite having received prior permission to use it. Consequently, the Somali players resumed their training outside the stadium, as they had done the previous day. This disruption comes as Somalia gears up for two crucial matches against Ghana, who are under pressure to improve their qualifying campaign after losing their first two Group C matches. The Black Stars, currently at the bottom of the group, will host Somalia at the Essipong Sports Stadium on November 12, followed by a rematch on November 16. The matches present Ghana with an opportunity to secure their first points in the qualifiers.
