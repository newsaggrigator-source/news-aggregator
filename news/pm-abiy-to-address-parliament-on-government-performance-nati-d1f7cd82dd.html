PM Abiy to Address Parliament On Government Performance, National Priorities Thursday

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 05:16:53 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed is scheduled to address the House of Peoples' Representatives on Thursday, focusing on the federal government's performance during the first quarter of the 2026/27 fiscal year. This address will occur during the 4th regular session of the 1st year of the 7th parliamentary term. The Prime Minister will provide insights into the implementation of the government's 2019 development plan and respond to lawmakers' inquiries regarding national priorities and policy execution. The session will also include attendance from ambassadors, international organization representatives, religious leaders, and opposition party leaders. Following the Prime Minister's presentation, the House is expected to deliberate and potentially approve a motion of support for the government's initiatives. The proceedings will be broadcast live across various media platforms, ensuring transparency and public engagement in the discussions surrounding Ethiopia's governance and development strategies.
