Mass Displacement Persists in Merti Woreda Following Security Breakdown in Arsi Zone - Borkena

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 19:54:03 GMT

Mass displacement continues in Merti Woreda, Oromia region, due to a severe security breakdown in the Arsi Zone. Hundreds of families have been forced to flee their homes amid ongoing violence, leaving them without permanent shelter or adequate humanitarian aid. Reports indicate that armed groups have conducted systematic attacks on rural communities, leading to mass casualties and the destruction of agricultural livelihoods. As a result, 21 out of 23 administrative kebeles in Merti Woreda are now largely uninhabited, with only two maintaining active populations. Many displaced individuals have been living in temporary conditions for up to three years, facing critical shortages of food, clothing, and medical care. Despite the acknowledgment of ongoing relief efforts by local authorities, the aid provided is insufficient to meet the overwhelming needs of the displaced population. Community leaders are calling for increased support from charitable organizations and international relief agencies to address the humanitarian crisis.
