El Ni&#xF1;o, Iran War Shocks Could Push Nearly One Million More Ethiopians Into Poverty, New Report Warns

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 10:24:57 GMT

A new report from the International Food Policy Research Institute (IFPRI) warns that nearly 905,000 additional Ethiopians could fall into poverty and 2.8 million could face undernourishment in 2027 due to the combined impacts of a potentially strong El Niño and rising global fuel prices linked to the Iran war. The analysis predicts a GDP decline of 0.4% to 1.1%, with agriculture suffering the most, particularly in drought-prone areas. The report highlights that agricultural productivity could drop by 2% under moderate El Niño conditions, exacerbating food insecurity and household income pressures. Urban households are expected to experience a more significant decline in real expenditure compared to rural ones. The researchers emphasize the need for anticipatory action and additional assistance to mitigate these impacts, as existing social protection mechanisms may not be sufficient. The findings underscore the broader vulnerabilities facing Ethiopia amid ongoing conflicts and climate-related risks.
