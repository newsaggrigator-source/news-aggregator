Suspected Al-Shabaab militants kill 8 in northeastern Kenya ambush - Anadolu Ajansı

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 18:10:59 GMT

Suspected Al-Shabaab militants ambushed a vehicle in northeastern Kenya's Mandera County on November 10, killing at least eight individuals, including five road construction workers, two police reservists, and the driver. The attack occurred near the Somalia border as the vehicle was escorting workers along the Arabia-Lafey road. Mandera Governor Mohamed Adan Khalif expressed deep sorrow over the loss of lives dedicated to infrastructure development and called for an urgent investigation to ensure the perpetrators are brought to justice. In response to the ambush, police have deployed a multi-agency security team to track down the attackers. This incident follows recent assaults by Al-Shabaab militants in neighboring Garissa County, highlighting the ongoing threat posed by the group, which has been active in the region for nearly two decades. Kenya has been combating Al-Shabaab since deploying troops to Somalia in 2011, facing numerous retaliatory attacks since then.
