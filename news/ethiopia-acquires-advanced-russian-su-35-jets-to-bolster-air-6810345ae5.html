Ethiopia Acquires Advanced Russian Su-35 Jets to Bolster Air Defense - Fidel Post

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 16:02:47 GMT

Ethiopia has officially received its first batch of twelve advanced Su-35 fighter jets from Russia, marking a significant step in the country's military modernization efforts. Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announced that the delivery occurred one year ahead of schedule, as part of a strategic defense agreement with Moscow. The Su-35s, classified as 4++ generation fighters, enhance the Ethiopian Air Force's operational capabilities, including radar tracking and strike power. Additionally, Ethiopia is set to receive four S-400 surface-to-air missile defense system batteries. While Abiy emphasized that these acquisitions are defensive and aimed at ensuring national security, analysts express mixed views. Some warn that such military upgrades could escalate arms races in the Horn of Africa, while others argue that a stronger Ethiopian military is crucial for regional peacekeeping and security. This acquisition positions Ethiopia as the first nation in Sub-Saharan Africa to operate the Su-35, reflecting strengthened ties with Russia. (159 words)
