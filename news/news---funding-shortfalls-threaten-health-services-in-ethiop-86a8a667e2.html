News - Funding Shortfalls Threaten Health Services in Ethiopia's Conflict-Hit Regions, UN Warns

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 11:43:14 GMT

Funding shortages are severely impacting health and nutrition services in Ethiopia's conflict-affected regions, particularly in Amhara, where the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) reports that 10 of the 16 operational mobile health teams are set to shut down by the end of October. Currently, only 16 out of the required 57 teams are functioning. Despite ongoing insecurity, humanitarian partners are striving to deliver essential aid, including medical supplies in Tigray and food assistance for around 100,000 people in Amhara. However, the overall humanitarian needs in Ethiopia surpass available resources, with funding constraints also affecting food assistance and refugee protection. The situation is exacerbated by continued displacement, with over 660,000 people living in IDP sites across the region.
