IOM Warns 874,000 Could Be Displaced as Floods Threaten Somalia - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 09:57:23 GMT

The International Organization for Migration (IOM) has issued a stark warning that nearly 874,000 people in Somalia could be displaced due to impending floods over the next three months. This alarming projection, driven by heavy rainfall and rising river levels, comes as Somalia grapples with ongoing drought and previous displacements. The IOM, alongside the Danish Refugee Council, estimates that flooding will account for 92% of the anticipated displacements, particularly as the country enters a rainy season influenced by strengthening El Niño conditions. Manuel Marques Pereira, IOM Somalia Chief of Mission, emphasized the urgency of the situation, stating that many families are already vulnerable and lack resources. Despite the critical need for assistance, the IOM has only secured 14.4% of the $16.62 million required to address the crisis, severely limiting their capacity to provide essential services such as shelter, clean water, and healthcare. The warning highlights the dire humanitarian situation and the need for immediate international support.
