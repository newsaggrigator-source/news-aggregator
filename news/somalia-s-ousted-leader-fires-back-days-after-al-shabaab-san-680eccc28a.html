Somalia’s ousted leader fires back days after Al-Shabaab sanctions - People Daily

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:35:50 GMT

Somalia's ousted leader, Mohamed Abdullahi Farmajo, has responded to recent sanctions imposed by the U.S. Treasury on Al-Shabaab, the militant group responsible for ongoing violence in the country. Farmajo criticized the sanctions, arguing that they do not address the root causes of extremism and fail to support the Somali government's efforts to combat terrorism effectively. He emphasized the need for a comprehensive strategy that includes political solutions and community engagement to counter the influence of Al-Shabaab. Farmajo's remarks come amid heightened tensions in Somalia, where the government is grappling with security challenges and the need for international support to stabilize the region.
