Supreme Audit Institutions Deliberating On Strengthening Collaboration, Accountability in Africa

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 13:37:06 GMT

A workshop focused on enhancing collaboration and accountability among Supreme Audit Institutions (SAIs) in Africa is currently taking place in Addis Ababa. Ethiopia's Auditor General, Meseret Damtie, emphasized the importance of strengthening SAIs to promote accountable governance and sustainable development. The three-day event aims to improve audit quality and institutional capacity through peer support and collaboration, aligning with the ARISE (African Regional Initiative for Strengthening Effectiveness) program. INTOSAI Development Initiative representative, Muhammed Dabo, highlighted that sustainable improvements require strong institutional systems and effective leadership. The workshop encourages participants to share experiences and develop practical solutions to enhance public-sector accountability across the continent. The ARISE program, supported by the Norwegian Agency for Development Cooperation, aims to provide long-term assistance to SAIs in challenging environments from 2025 to 2028.
