Ethiopia Steps Up Regional, Global Partnerships Ahead of COP32

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 05:20:22 GMT

Ethiopia is enhancing its diplomatic efforts with regional and global partners in preparation for the 32nd United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP32), set to take place in 2027. Foreign Affairs Minister Gedion Timothewos recently met with Rwanda's Environment Minister, Bernadette Arakwiye, to discuss strengthening cooperation on climate priorities and ensuring a successful COP32. Additionally, during COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, Minister of Finance Fitsum Assefa engaged with World Bank Group's Paschal Donohoe to explore deeper collaboration on issues such as e-mobility, climate finance, and carbon credits. These discussions highlight Ethiopia's commitment to mobilizing regional support and fostering international partnerships to build consensus on key climate issues. As the host of this significant climate conference, Ethiopia aims to deliver meaningful outcomes for all participating nations, reflecting its growing role in global climate diplomacy.
