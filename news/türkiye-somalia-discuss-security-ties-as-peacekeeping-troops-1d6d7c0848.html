Türkiye, Somalia discuss security ties as peacekeeping troops begin withdrawal - TRT World

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 09:11:44 GMT

Türkiye and Somalia have engaged in discussions regarding military and security cooperation amid the withdrawal of peacekeeping troops from the African Union mission in Somalia. The meeting, held in Mogadishu on November 9, 2026, involved Turkish Ambassador Ferhat Alkan and Somalia’s National Security Adviser Awes Hagi Yusuf Ahmed. Key topics included counterterrorism efforts against the Al Shabab terror group and the importance of ongoing military collaboration. The Turkish Embassy emphasized Türkiye's commitment to supporting Somalia's peace and stability. Somalia's National Security Adviser expressed gratitude for Türkiye's longstanding friendship and support, highlighting the necessity for continued coordination to ensure security in the region. Additionally, Türkiye's military training mission, known as TURKSOM, plays a vital role in enhancing the capabilities of Somali forces in their fight against terrorism. The discussions reflect a mutual commitment to strengthening security ties as Somalia navigates a complex security environment. (159 words)
