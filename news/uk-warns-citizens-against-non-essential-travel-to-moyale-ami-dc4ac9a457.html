UK warns citizens against non-essential travel to Moyale amid Ethiopia clashes - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 20:02:08 GMT

The UK government has issued a travel advisory warning its citizens against non-essential travel to Moyale, Kenya, due to escalating violence linked to cross-border clashes with Ethiopia. The advisory, released on November 6 by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), highlights the deteriorating security situation, including reports of incidents affecting the Kenyan side of the border. The advisory specifically targets Moyale and a five-kilometer radius around it. In response to the violence, which began on October 22, Kenyan authorities have intensified security measures, deploying additional officers to the area. Tragically, stray bullets have resulted in casualties, including the death of a woman in Kenya. Approximately 10,000 people have been displaced and are currently in temporary shelters. The FCDO's advisory also maintains travel restrictions in other parts of northern and eastern Kenya due to terrorism threats, particularly from the al Shabaab militant group. The situation reflects broader instability in the Horn of Africa.
