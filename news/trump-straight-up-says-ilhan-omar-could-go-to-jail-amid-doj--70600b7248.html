Trump Straight Up Says Ilhan Omar ‘Could Go to Jail’ Amid DOJ Probe, Calls Somalia a ‘Sh*thouse’ - Yahoo

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 19:28:09 GMT

In a recent statement, former President Donald Trump suggested that Congresswoman Ilhan Omar could face jail time amid an ongoing Department of Justice (DOJ) investigation. Trump criticized Omar's background, referring to Somalia as a "sh*thouse" and implying that her origins are linked to her alleged legal troubles. His comments have sparked controversy and renewed discussions about Omar's role in Congress and her past. The DOJ probe reportedly focuses on various issues, although specific details have not been disclosed. Trump's remarks reflect his continued focus on Omar, who has been a frequent target of his criticism.
