Contract Farming Modestly Improves Smallholder Food Security in Northwest Ethiopia - Bioengineer.org

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 00:47:54 GMT

A recent study published in Discover Food highlights the modest improvements in food security among smallholder farmers in northwest Ethiopia participating in contract farming for oilseed and pulse production. Researchers Marta Mohammed and Mengistie Mossie found that these farmers experienced statistically significant gains in dietary diversity and overall food consumption compared to non-participants. The study, which surveyed 329 rural households, revealed that 96% of participating farmers viewed their contracts positively, citing benefits such as access to farm inputs and guaranteed markets. However, only 20% of non-participants shared this sentiment, often expressing skepticism. The study identified key factors influencing participation, including education, farm size, and access to extension services. While the improvements in food security were noted, they were described as incremental rather than transformative. The authors advocate for enhanced public policies and awareness programs to address barriers and maximize the potential benefits of contract farming for smallholders in the region.
