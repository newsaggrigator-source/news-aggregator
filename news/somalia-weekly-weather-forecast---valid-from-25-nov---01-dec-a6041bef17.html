Somalia Weekly Weather Forecast - Valid From 25 Nov - 01 Dec 2026 - ReliefWeb

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 03:31:50 GMT

The Somalia Weekly Weather Forecast for the period of November 25 to December 1, 2026, indicates a very dry week across the country. During the previous week (November 17-23), only four weather stations reported rainfall, with Banderbeyla recording the highest at 28.3 mm. Satellite estimates showed minimal precipitation in other areas, particularly in the river catchments of Ethiopia. Seasonal rainfall totals remain largely unchanged, with 37 stations having met their Deyr normal, including Baidoa and Galkacyo, which exceeded their averages significantly. However, Mogadishu and several other locations remain below their normal levels. River levels are decreasing, with major rivers well below flood-risk thresholds, particularly in Belet Weyne, where levels have dropped significantly. Flood waters in Jowhar district are receding, with satellite imagery showing a reduction in flooded areas. Reports from local callers indicate mixed reactions to the rainfall, with some areas experiencing flooding while others welcomed the moisture.
