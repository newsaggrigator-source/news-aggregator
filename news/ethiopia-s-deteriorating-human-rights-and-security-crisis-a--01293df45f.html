Ethiopia’s Deteriorating Human Rights and Security Crisis: A Call for Stronger U.S. Diplomatic Leadership - Borkena

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 23:00:44 GMT

Ethiopia is facing a severe human rights and security crisis, particularly in the Oromia region, where ongoing violence, including killings and abductions of civilians, has raised alarm. Reports of targeted attacks against Ethiopian Orthodox Christians, including the destruction of their homes and places of worship, have intensified concerns among Ethiopian Americans and the global community. The article criticizes U.S. Ambassador Massinga for not adequately condemning these atrocities and calls for stronger U.S. diplomatic leadership. It urges the appointment of a Senior Special Envoy for the Horn of Africa to facilitate regional peace efforts and emphasizes the need for the U.S. to prioritize civilian protection, defend religious freedom, and support accountability for human rights violations. The authors stress that the U.S. must actively engage to prevent further escalation of violence and instability, which could have dire consequences for Ethiopia and the broader Horn of Africa region.
