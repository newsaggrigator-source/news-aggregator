South West Somalia President Commends UPDF’s Contribution To Peace And Security - UPDF

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 09:28:48 GMT

Sheikh Adan Mohamed Nur, the President of South West State of Somalia, praised the Uganda Peoples’ Defence Forces (UPDF) for their significant role in promoting peace and security in Somalia during a meeting with Col Dr Justus Musenene, the commander of Battle Group 45. He highlighted UPDF's exceptional contributions compared to troops from other nations and expressed gratitude to Ugandan President Yoweri Kaguta Museveni for his commitment to African unity and stability in Somalia. The meeting also acknowledged the sacrifices of soldiers who lost their lives in the mission. Lower Shabelle Governor Abdifatah Said Ahmed commended the UPDF for fostering good relations with local civilians, which has built community trust. Somalia National Army Chief of Staff Maj Gen Mohamed Ali Bariise recognized the organizational skills and military tactics of UPDF, viewing them as a model for the Somali forces. Col Musenene emphasized the importance of patriotism for sustaining stability and pledged ongoing support for peace efforts in the region.
