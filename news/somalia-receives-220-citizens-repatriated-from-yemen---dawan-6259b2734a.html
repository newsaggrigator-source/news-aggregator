Somalia Receives 220 Citizens Repatriated From Yemen - Dawan Africa

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 17:17:13 GMT

Somalia has welcomed back 220 of its citizens who were repatriated from Yemen. The returnees arrived at Mogadishu's Aden Adde International Airport, where they were received by government officials and representatives from the International Organization for Migration (IOM). Many of the repatriated individuals had faced difficult conditions in Yemen, prompting their return to Somalia. The government expressed its commitment to supporting the reintegration of these citizens into society, providing them with necessary assistance and resources. This repatriation effort is part of a broader initiative to ensure the safety and well-being of Somali nationals abroad.
