Somalia Snubbed by Ankara, Kampala pulls some of its troops from Somalia and courts Abu Dhabi - Africa Intelligence

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 05:40:47 GMT

Uganda's military chief, Muhoozi Kainerugaba, has initiated the withdrawal of Ugandan troops from Somalia due to frustrations with Turkey's failure to provide necessary guarantees for their safety and operational support. This decision comes amid rising tensions in Somalia and dissatisfaction with the Somali government's handling of the political crisis. In a strategic shift, Uganda is now seeking closer ties with Abu Dhabi, potentially looking for new partnerships to bolster its military and diplomatic efforts in the region. The withdrawal reflects a broader trend of discontent among foreign troops in Somalia, as the Ugandan government reassesses its role in the African Union peacekeeping mission amid ongoing instability in Mogadishu. This move could have significant implications for the security landscape in Somalia, which continues to grapple with challenges from militant groups like Al-Shabaab.
