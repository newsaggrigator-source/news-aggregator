Securing Shared Interests in the Horn of Africa: Ethiopia’s Partnership Approach to Somalia’s Security Transition Beyond 2026 - IFA - Institute of Foreign Affairs

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:36:26 GMT

Ethiopia's strategic approach to Somalia's security transition beyond 2026 emphasizes collaboration with the Somali government, the African Union (AU), and the Ankara process, while enhancing its border security and evaluating maritime risks. The urgency stems from the impending expiration of the AU Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) on December 31, 2026, after which the U.S. will cease funding. Somalia's security has deteriorated due to internal disputes and setbacks against Al-Shabaab, which has regained territory despite previous gains by Somali forces. Ethiopia faces threats primarily from Al-Shabaab's incursions and piracy along the Puntland coast. To address these challenges, Ethiopia is advised to prioritize a post-2026 AUSSOM agreement, bolster border defenses, and support inclusive political dialogue in Somalia. Additionally, it should assess the implications of piracy on trade routes and maintain a balanced approach towards both the federal government and regional authorities to ensure stability in the Horn of Africa.
