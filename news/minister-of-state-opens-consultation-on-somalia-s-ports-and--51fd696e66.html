Minister of State Opens Consultation on Somalia's Ports and Maritime Transport Policy - Somali National News Agency

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 12:04:43 GMT

The State Minister of the Ministry of Ports and Maritime Transport of Somalia, Mr. Mohamed Abdulkadir, has initiated a consultation meeting aimed at reviewing and approving a draft national policy for the country's ports and maritime transport sector. This meeting, held in Mogadishu, brought together various government institutions and the management of the Port of Mogadishu to discuss the policy framework that will guide the development and management of maritime transport in Somalia. The consultation is part of broader efforts by the Ministry to enhance policies and regulations that govern the maritime sector, with the goal of improving port services and bolstering their contribution to the nation's economic growth. This initiative reflects Somalia's commitment to developing its maritime infrastructure and ensuring effective management of its ports, which are vital for trade and economic development.
