Ethiopia receives 12 Russian Su-35 jets, plans S-400 acquisition - Hiiraan Online

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 11:11:18 GMT

Ethiopia has received 12 Russian Su-35 fighter jets and plans to acquire four S-400 air defense systems, as announced by Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed during his visit to Moscow for the Russia-Africa Summit. This military acquisition, which arrived a year ahead of schedule, aims to enhance Ethiopia's defense capabilities amid ongoing conflicts with rebels in the Tigray and Amhara regions. Abiy expressed gratitude to Russian President Vladimir Putin for expediting the delivery, emphasizing the jets' role in defending Ethiopian airspace and deterring aggression. The military cooperation between Ethiopia and Russia has strengthened in recent years, with previous agreements focused on training and technology. The announcement comes as fighting in Tigray escalates, undermining a peace agreement from November 2022. The Russia-Africa Summit seeks to foster closer security and economic ties between Russia and African nations, amidst Moscow's efforts to counter Western sanctions related to its actions in Ukraine.
