Ethiopia Becomes Africa's Fifth-Largest Economy After Rebasing GDP

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 03:45:00 GMT

Ethiopia has officially become Africa's fifth-largest economy following a significant revision of its gross domestic product (GDP) figures, which now account for sectors like digital finance, services, and manufacturing. The Finance Minister announced that the economy was valued at $210.7 billion for the fiscal year ending July 2026, marking a 59.2% increase from previous estimates. This revision places Ethiopia behind South Africa, Egypt, Nigeria, and Algeria, while surpassing Morocco and Kenya. The update involved changing the base year for GDP calculations from 2015-16 to 2024-25, reflecting a more accurate economic structure. Services now contribute nearly 36% to the GDP, with agriculture and industry following closely. While the revised figures may enhance Ethiopia's debt indicators, they do not imply an actual increase in goods and services produced. The government aims for a 10.2% economic growth rate in the upcoming fiscal year, supported by reforms to attract foreign investment and improve economic conditions.
