Africa Urged to Strengthen Regional Power Integration for Energy Security

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 11:06:40 GMT

At the 36th African Hydro Symposium in Addis Ababa, UNECA Executive Secretary Claver Gatete emphasized the need for stronger regional electricity networks to enhance energy security across Africa. He highlighted that coordinated infrastructure planning and collaboration among governments, utilities, and regulators are essential for effective energy integration. Gatete pointed out that climate-resilient hydropower development is crucial, advocating for climate risk assessments and sustainable water management among countries sharing river basins. Ethiopian Water and Energy State Minister Dinku Seyum echoed these sentiments, noting that Ethiopia's Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD) could serve as a catalyst for regional energy integration and cross-border power trade. He urged African nations to cooperate in developing hydropower resources while addressing financing and technical capacity challenges. The symposium also launched the African Union's Continental Energy Security Policy Framework, aimed at advancing energy security at both national and regional levels, underscoring the importance of sustainable hydropower development for the continent's energy future.
