Somalia’s President Replaces Chief Justice, Citing Complaints From Judges - Dawan Africa

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 21:41:36 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has replaced Chief Justice Bashe Yusuf Ahmed amid complaints from judges and legal professionals regarding the need for a fair and impartial justice system. The president appointed lawyer Ibrahim Idle Suleiman as the new chief justice, emphasizing the necessity for judicial reforms to ensure that Somali citizens have access to a trustworthy legal framework. This decision follows a recent ruling by the Supreme Court under Ahmed, which annulled the results of parliamentary and local council elections in Galmudug due to serious violations affecting the election's integrity. Suleiman previously served as chief justice from 2016 until his dismissal in 2018 and has been acting as a special legal adviser since Hassan Sheikh's return to power in 2022. The presidency's statement highlighted the importance of judicial independence, the rule of law, and equal justice for all, although it did not address the specifics of the complaints or the recent election ruling.
