Defence Minister Meets Swedish Ambassador as Security Cooperation Continues Amid Deportation Dispute - FTL Somalia

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 15:09:55 GMT

Somalia's Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi met with Swedish Ambassador Hans Henric Lundquist in Mogadishu to discuss enhancing defence and security cooperation amid ongoing tensions over Sweden's deportation of Somali nationals. The meeting highlighted the importance of bilateral relations, with Fiqi expressing gratitude for Sweden's support in Somalia's fight against terrorism and efforts to bolster national security. However, the backdrop of the deportation dispute, which has raised concerns about financial arrangements linked to the returns, complicates the relationship. Sweden has redirected development aid towards projects associated with the Somali Prime Minister's office, leading to allegations of misconduct. Despite these tensions, the ambassador reaffirmed Sweden's commitment to supporting Somalia's security sector, which is crucial as the country faces a security transition and reduced international support. The meeting served as a reminder of the need to balance security partnerships with accountability for treatment of Somali citizens, a challenge for both nations moving forward.
