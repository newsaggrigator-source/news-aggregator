Ethiopia as a Focal Point Between the Quad and Quintet Coalition on Sudan - horn review

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 16:54:15 GMT

Ethiopia is emerging as a crucial mediator between the Quad (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, and the US) and the Quintet (African Union, IGAD, Arab League, EU, and UN) in addressing the ongoing conflict in Sudan. While the Quad focuses on humanitarian efforts and ceasefire, the Quintet aims for an inclusive political process. The need for coordinated efforts was highlighted in a joint meeting on September 23, 2026. Egypt has expressed interest in leading this coordination, but its close ties with Sudan's military raise concerns about impartiality. In contrast, Ethiopia, with its established diplomatic connections and experience in Sudanese political processes, is positioned to facilitate communication and align priorities between the two coalitions. Ethiopia's involvement is essential not only for regional stability but also for managing the humanitarian and security implications of Sudan's conflict, as it directly affects Ethiopia's national interests. A proposed coordination office in Addis Ababa could enhance collaboration under AU and UN oversight. (159 words)
