CS Aden Duale Jokes Somalia Has Many Gachaguas At GSEF 2026 Event in Nairobi - Dawan Africa

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 16:15:42 GMT

During the Global Somali Entrepreneurship Forum 2026 in Nairobi, Kenya’s Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale humorously remarked that Somalia has many individuals akin to former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua, whom he criticized for allegedly causing discord in Kenya. Duale, the event's Chief Guest, emphasized the importance of electing capable leaders in Somalia, urging the audience to learn from Kenya’s political experiences. He praised President William Ruto's administration for achieving significant economic progress, claiming that Kenya is now the seventh largest economy in Africa, with a GDP of $151 billion. Duale highlighted advancements in Kenya's agricultural sector and digital services, such as the eCitizen platform, which enhances access to government services. He also encouraged Somali entrepreneurs to focus on building generational wealth, citing examples of successful communities. The forum featured various businesses and aimed to foster collaboration between Kenya and Somalia, emphasizing the need for political stability to spur economic growth. The event concluded on October 25, 2026.
