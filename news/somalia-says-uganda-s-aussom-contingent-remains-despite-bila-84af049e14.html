Somalia Says Uganda’s AUSSOM Contingent Remains Despite Bilateral Troop Withdrawal - UG Diplomat

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 20:08:08 GMT

Somalia has clarified that the ongoing withdrawal of Ugandan troops does not mean a complete exit of all Ugandan military personnel from the country. Ambassador Awes Hagi Yusuf Ahmed, Somalia’s National Security Advisor, stated that the troops currently redeploying to Uganda are part of bilateral forces operating under separate agreements between Somalia and Uganda. He emphasized that the Ugandan contingent serving under the African Union Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) remains active and continues its mission alongside the Somali National Army (SNA). This clarification follows the Uganda People’s Defence Forces (UPDF) announcement of troop withdrawals, which are expected to be completed soon, with all Ugandan forces, including those under AUSSOM, set to leave by the end of 2027. Ahmed highlighted the importance of continued cooperation among Somalia, its international partners, and security institutions to ensure sustainable security and stability in the region. Uganda has been involved in peacekeeping efforts in Somalia for two decades. (158 words)
