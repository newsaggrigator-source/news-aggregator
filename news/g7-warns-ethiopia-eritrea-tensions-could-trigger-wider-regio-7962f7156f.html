G7 Warns Ethiopia–Eritrea Tensions Could Trigger Wider Regional Escalation - www.nationpathindia.com

Wed, 11 Nov 2026 20:21:59 GMT

The G7 foreign ministers have expressed serious concern regarding escalating tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea, warning that further conflict could destabilize the Horn of Africa and disrupt international trade, particularly shipping through the Red Sea. In a joint declaration issued on November 11, 2026, following their meeting in Paris, the ministers urged all parties to exercise maximum restraint and seek diplomatic solutions. The declaration highlights the historical context of conflict between the two nations, which previously engaged in a border war from 1998 to 2000, although relations improved after a peace agreement in 2018. The G7 emphasized the importance of humanitarian access and protecting civilians, particularly vulnerable groups. This warning aligns with calls from the African Union and the European Union for de-escalation and dialogue, underscoring the international community's concern over the potential for a wider security crisis in the region. The G7's statement does not propose military intervention or new sanctions but advocates for diplomatic engagement. (159 words)
