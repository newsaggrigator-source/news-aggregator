Somalia parliament speaker accuses Kenya of harbouring terrorists - Somali Guardian

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 17:59:32 GMT

Somalia's parliament speaker has accused Kenya of harboring terrorists, claiming that the country is providing a safe haven for individuals involved in militant activities against Somalia. This accusation comes amid ongoing tensions between the two nations, particularly concerning security and border issues. The speaker emphasized the need for Kenya to take responsibility for its actions and to cooperate with Somalia in combating terrorism. The remarks reflect a broader concern within Somalia regarding the influence of external actors on its security situation. The Somali government has previously expressed similar concerns, urging regional cooperation to address the threats posed by militant groups. The situation remains delicate as both countries navigate their complex relationship.
