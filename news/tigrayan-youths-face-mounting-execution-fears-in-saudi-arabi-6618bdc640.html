Tigrayan Youths Face Mounting Execution Fears in Saudi Arabia - Caretaker Admin Pleads With Fed. Govt to Intervene

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 10:24:40 GMT

Hundreds of Ethiopian migrants, primarily young Tigrayans, face execution in Saudi Arabia due to escalating drug-related offenses. Haish Subagadis, head of the Tigray Caretaker Administration Youth Bureau, reports that approximately 520 Ethiopians have been sentenced to death, with many detained in Khamis Mushait and Abha. Families are increasingly anxious as recent executions raise fears for their loved ones. Many of these youths fled conflict in Tigray, seeking better opportunities but often falling prey to drug trafficking charges, sometimes unknowingly. They struggle with language barriers and inadequate legal representation, leading to unfair trials. Human rights organizations have documented a surge in executions, with at least 17 Ethiopians executed in 2026 alone. The Ethiopian government is urged to enhance diplomatic efforts to protect its nationals, as families and rights groups call for intervention. The situation highlights the broader migration crisis stemming from Ethiopia's ongoing conflicts and economic challenges.
