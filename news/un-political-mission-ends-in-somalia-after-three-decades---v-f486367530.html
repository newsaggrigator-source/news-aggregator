UN Political Mission Ends in Somalia After Three Decades - Voice of Nigeria

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 12:36:54 GMT

The United Nations' political mission in Somalia is set to conclude after over 30 years of involvement aimed at fostering reconciliation and political stability. This decision follows a request from the Somali Government in 2024 and a UN Security Council-approved transition plan to hand over responsibilities to Somali institutions. The Somali Government views this closure as a significant step towards self-management, although opposition figures express concerns regarding ongoing political disputes, militant threats, and humanitarian crises exacerbated by drought. While the UN political mission is ending, the African Union will continue to support Somali security forces, and UN humanitarian agencies will remain active in the country. The UN's involvement in Somalia has evolved since the collapse of the central government in 1991, with various missions supporting elections and institutional development. Despite progress, challenges persist in extending government control and resolving political disagreements. The transition marks a new phase in international engagement with Somalia.
