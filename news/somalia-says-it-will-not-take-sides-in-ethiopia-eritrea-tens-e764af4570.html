Somalia says it will not take sides in Ethiopia-Eritrea tensions - Horseed Media

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 14:19:34 GMT

Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has declared that the country will remain neutral amid rising tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea. In an interview with Sky News on October 30, 2026, he emphasized that Somalia maintains diplomatic relations with both nations and does not intend to intervene in their disputes. Mohamud stated, "Whatever occurs between the two countries is a matter for them alone," highlighting Somalia's commitment to peaceful coexistence and cooperation with its neighbors. The president's remarks come as diplomatic strains between Ethiopia and Eritrea have resurfaced, raising concerns about regional stability in the Horn of Africa. Somalia aims to prioritize internal stability and constructive relations with both Ethiopia and Eritrea, advocating for dialogue as a means to resolve conflicts. This stance reinforces Somalia's broader foreign policy of fostering peace both domestically and internationally, while avoiding entanglement in the disputes of other states.
