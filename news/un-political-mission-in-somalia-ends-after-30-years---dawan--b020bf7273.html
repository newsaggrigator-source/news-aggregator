UN Political Mission in Somalia Ends After 30 Years - Dawan Africa

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 18:28:59 GMT

The United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) officially concluded its operations on October 31, 2026, marking the end of over 30 years of UN political involvement in the country. This closure follows a two-year transition process initiated by the Federal Government of Somalia, during which UNTMIS responsibilities were gradually handed over to Somali institutions and various UN agencies. The UN's engagement in Somalia began with the establishment of the United Nations Political Office for Somalia (UNPOS) in 1995, evolving through several phases, including the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNSOM) in 2013. UNTMIS Head Raisedon Zenenga emphasized that this closure does not signify the end of the partnership between Somalia and the UN but rather the transition to a new phase where Somalia's national institutions will assume greater responsibility for political management and reconciliation efforts. The UN has pledged to continue supporting Somalia despite the mission's conclusion.
