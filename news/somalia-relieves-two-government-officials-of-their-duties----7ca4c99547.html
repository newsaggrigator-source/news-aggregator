Somalia Relieves Two Government Officials of Their Duties - Somali National News Agency

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 16:55:04 GMT

The Federal Government of Somalia has relieved two officials from their positions, effective November 4, 2026. The dismissed officials are Mr. Said Mohamed Mahmoud, who served as the State Minister at the Ministry of Public Works, Reconstruction and Housing, and Ms. Fartun Abdikadir Farah, the Deputy Minister at the Ministry of Ports and Marine Transport. The announcement was made by the Somali National News Agency (SONNA), although specific reasons for their dismissal were not disclosed. This decision comes amid ongoing developments in the Somali government, including recent meetings on regional trade agreements and security discussions in the Gulf of Aden. The changes in leadership reflect the government's efforts to address various national issues and enhance its administrative effectiveness. The situation remains dynamic as the government continues to navigate challenges in governance and public service.
