OIC reaffirms support for Somalia’s territorial integrity during talks in Baghdad - Hiiraan Online

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 16:02:42 GMT

The Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) has reiterated its commitment to Somalia's territorial integrity during discussions held in Baghdad. The meeting focused on various issues affecting the region, with member states expressing solidarity with Somalia amid ongoing challenges. The OIC emphasized the importance of unity and cooperation among member nations to support Somalia's stability and development. Participants highlighted the need for a collective approach to address security concerns and promote peace in the country. The reaffirmation of support comes as Somalia continues to navigate complex political and security landscapes, with the OIC pledging to assist in efforts aimed at strengthening the nation's sovereignty and territorial claims.
