UK tightens Kenya travel warning over Moyale border unrest - https://www.thetraveler.org/

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 19:40:53 GMT

The UK has revised its travel advisory for Kenya, specifically highlighting security concerns in the northern border town of Moyale due to unrest in neighboring Ethiopia. The updated guidance, published by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) on October 30, 2026, warns against all travel to certain areas near the Kenya-Ethiopia border, citing increased military presence and cross-border violence. Reports indicate that clashes in Ethiopian Moyale have displaced thousands into Kenya, prompting local authorities to enhance security measures. The advisory restricts movement to essential travel along the Addis Ababa-Moyale road and within Moyale itself, significantly limiting options for overland travel between Kenya and Ethiopia. Travelers are urged to consult local operators and adhere to official advisories to ensure safety and maintain insurance coverage. The situation reflects broader regional security concerns, with similar warnings issued for other East African border areas, emphasizing the need for vigilance among travelers.
