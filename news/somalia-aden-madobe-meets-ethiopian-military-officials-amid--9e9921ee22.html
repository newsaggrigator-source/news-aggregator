Somalia: Aden Madobe meets Ethiopian military officials amid rising tensions in South West State - Garowe Online

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 05:43:40 GMT

Aden Madobe, the leader of Somalia's South West State, recently met with senior Ethiopian military officials in Baidoa to address escalating security concerns amid rising tensions with forces loyal to former regional president Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen. The discussions focused on security arrangements in Baidoa and strategies to prevent attacks from Laftagareen's supporters, who have reportedly gained ground in the Bay region. Madobe's administration is under pressure to assert its authority following his takeover in March, which ousted Laftagareen amid a political dispute. Ethiopian troops, part of international security efforts in Somalia, have remained neutral in the conflict, complicating Madobe's efforts to stabilize the region. Additionally, Madobe met with the commander of the African Union peacekeeping force to discuss cooperation against al-Shabab, highlighting the challenges faced by his administration in consolidating power while dealing with armed opposition.
