News: Ethiopia warns Somaliland recognition could strengthen Al-Shabaab, Somaliland officials push back - Addis Standard

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 12:42:31 GMT

Ethiopia has issued a warning that recognizing Somaliland as an independent state could bolster the influence of Al-Shabaab, the militant group operating in the region. Ethiopian officials argue that such recognition might destabilize the Horn of Africa and exacerbate security challenges. In response, Somaliland officials have pushed back against this assertion, emphasizing their desire for international recognition and asserting that their independence would not contribute to the strengthening of Al-Shabaab. They argue that Somaliland has maintained relative peace and stability compared to the rest of Somalia. The ongoing debate highlights the complex geopolitical dynamics in the region, as various stakeholders weigh the implications of Somaliland's quest for recognition against the backdrop of security concerns.
