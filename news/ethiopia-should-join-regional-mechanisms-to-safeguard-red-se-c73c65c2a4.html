Ethiopia Should Join Regional Mechanisms to Safeguard Red Sea Security: French, Danish Defense Attachés - ENA English

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 08:32:43 GMT

French and Danish Defense Attachés have emphasized Ethiopia's legitimate interest in accessing the Red Sea and its potential role in enhancing maritime security in the Horn of Africa. They highlighted that Ethiopia's pursuit of permanent access to the Red Sea is crucial for its development and stability, given its geographical and historical ties to the region. French Defense Attaché Colonel Damien Cypryk noted the strategic significance of the Red Sea and the Bab al-Mandab Strait, asserting that Ethiopia should actively engage in regional security mechanisms. He advocated for dialogue and negotiations to address Ethiopia's sea access aspirations. Danish Defense Attaché Commander Anders Lakjer underscored Ethiopia's capacity to combat terrorism and maritime threats, urging collaboration among nations to bolster regional security. Both attachés stressed the importance of Ethiopia's involvement in safeguarding peace and stability in this vital corridor, which has implications beyond the region.
