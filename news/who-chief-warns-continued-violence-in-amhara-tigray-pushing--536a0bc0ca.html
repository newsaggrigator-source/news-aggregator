WHO Chief Warns Continued Violence in Amhara, Tigray Pushing Ethiopia's Fragile Health System Toward Collapse

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 06:36:41 GMT

World Health Organization (WHO) Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus has expressed grave concerns over the escalating violence in Ethiopia's Amhara and Tigray regions, which is exacerbating the country's already fragile health system. Recent drone strikes have resulted in civilian casualties, including children, with reports indicating at least 16 deaths at a displacement camp in Amhara and 12 fatalities in Tigray. Tedros described these incidents as "deeply disturbing" and highlighted the dire shortages of healthcare workers, medicines, and medical equipment, alongside the insecurity hindering the delivery of essential supplies. He emphasized the urgent need for protection of civilians and access for health workers and humanitarian aid. The WHO, in collaboration with UN partners, is striving to provide additional medical supplies, but continued violence threatens to further deprive communities of critical healthcare services. Tedros underscored that "the best medicine is peace," urging for an end to hostilities to safeguard public health. (159 words)
