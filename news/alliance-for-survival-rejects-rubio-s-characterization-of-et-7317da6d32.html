Alliance for Survival Rejects Rubio's Characterization of Ethiopia's Conflict As Separatist, Terrorist

Mon, 19 Oct 2026 11:50:43 GMT

The Ethiopian Peoples' Forces Alliance for Survival has rejected U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio's characterization of the ongoing conflict in Ethiopia as separatist and terrorist. In a statement on October 18, the alliance, which includes various armed and political groups opposing the federal government, asserted that its military actions are acts of self-defense rather than attempts to fragment the country. They emphasized their goal of facilitating an inclusive democratic transition and restoring peace, while criticizing the government's military tactics against civilians. The alliance urged the U.S. to adopt a balanced approach to the conflict, warning that mischaracterizations could hinder peaceful resolutions. The statement follows Rubio's comments during a C-SPAN interview amid ongoing clashes between federal forces and the alliance's members across northern Ethiopia.
