Sweden Joins AU, EU, and IGAD Partners in Mogadishu Talks on Somalia’s Political Transition and Security - www.ftlsomalia.com

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 12:38:44 GMT

Sweden recently participated in a three-day diplomatic consultation in Mogadishu, focusing on Somalia's political transition and security, alongside representatives from the African Union, European Union, and the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD). The discussions, which concluded on November 18, 2026, emphasized the necessity of a broad domestic political agreement for stability in Somalia. Sweden's Embassy highlighted that inclusive political dialogue is crucial for sustaining security and institutional progress amid ongoing tensions over constitutional amendments and electoral frameworks. The meetings also addressed security threats from Al-Shabaab and the evolving roles of regional peacekeeping forces. Despite recent diplomatic strains over migration policies, Sweden reaffirmed its commitment to Somalia's state-building and community resilience efforts. However, the lack of substantive consensus among Somali political elites raises concerns about the effectiveness of international mediation efforts, as domestic actors often view these dialogues as temporary rather than as frameworks for lasting power-sharing.
