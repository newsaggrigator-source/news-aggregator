Pre-match press conference for Somalia Vs Ghana set for Sunday - Ghana Football Association

Sat, 14 Nov 2026 22:44:29 GMT

The Ghana Football Association has announced that a pre-match press conference for the upcoming Africa Cup of Nations qualifier between Somalia and Ghana will take place on Sunday, November 15, 2026. The event will be held at the Sekondi Stadium Conference Room, with Ghana's coach, Kim Lars Bjorkegren, addressing the media at 2:45 PM. Following him, Somalia's coach, Yusuf Ali, will speak at 3:30 PM. This press conference is set to preview the return fixture scheduled for Monday, where Ghana aims to capitalize on their recent 2-0 victory over Somalia in Sekondi. The match is crucial for both teams as they vie for qualification in the prestigious tournament. The GFA is keen to keep fans and media informed as the teams prepare for this important clash.
