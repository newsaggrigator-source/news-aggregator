IOM warns Somalia floods could displace 874,000 people - India Tribune - Chicago

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 22:08:44 GMT

The International Organisation for Migration (IOM) has issued a warning that imminent flooding in Somalia could displace nearly 874,000 people within the next three months. This alarming forecast comes as the country grapples with severe drought conditions, which have already affected 6.2 million Somalis in need of humanitarian assistance. The IOM emphasized the urgency for increased international support to mitigate the impending crisis, as heavy rainfall and rising river levels threaten communities already vulnerable to displacement. Projections indicate that 92% of the anticipated displacements will be due to flooding, exacerbated by the ongoing rainy season linked to strengthening El Niño conditions. The IOM has only secured 14.4% of the $16.62 million needed for emergency response efforts, which are critical for providing shelter, clean water, and healthcare. Without immediate funding, plans to establish safe relocation sites and deliver essential aid remain severely constrained, risking further humanitarian disaster in the region.
