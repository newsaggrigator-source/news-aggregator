Afcon 2027 qualifying: Ghana register first win against Somalia - BBC

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 17:02:10 GMT

Ghana secured their first victory in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (Afcon) qualifying campaign with a 2-0 win against Somalia in Sekondi-Takoradi. The Black Stars had previously lost their first two matches against Ivory Coast and The Gambia, which led to the departure of coach Carlos Queiroz. Interim coach Kim Bjorkegren took charge for this crucial match. Ghana missed an early opportunity to score when Jordan Ayew's penalty was saved by Somalia's goalkeeper Ibrahim Hussein. However, they took the lead just before halftime with a goal from Jonas Adjetey, who capitalized on a corner kick. Felix Afena-Gyan extended the lead in the second half with a shot that slipped past Hussein. This win puts Ghana three points behind group leaders Ivory Coast and The Gambia, who are set to face each other later. The victory is vital for Ghana as they aim to qualify for the Afcon finals after missing the previous edition. (158 words)
