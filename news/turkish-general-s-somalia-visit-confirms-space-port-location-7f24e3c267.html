Turkish general's Somalia visit confirms space port location - Janes Defence Intelligence

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 15:55:31 GMT

Turkish Chief of the General Staff, General Selçuk Bayraktaroğlu, recently visited Turkish troops stationed at the Space Base Complex under construction in Somalia, confirming the location of Türkiye's planned space port. A video released by the Turkish Armed Forces (TSK) on November 22 showcased his visit, which included inspections of various facilities, including barracks and defensive positions equipped with M48 Patton tanks. The site, identified through satellite imagery, is situated approximately 78 km northeast of Mogadishu and features a helicopter landing pad and protective measures against unmanned aircraft. Additionally, the compound has been prepared with positions for armored vehicles. Speculation arose earlier this year regarding the transfer of older Turkish tanks to the Somali National Armed Forces, although no official confirmation has been provided. The construction of the space port was first noted in January, with the perimeter established to enclose a significant area.
