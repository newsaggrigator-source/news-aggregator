Egypt's Conspiracy Against Ethiopia Over Abay River, Red Sea No Longer Workable, Says Water Minister

Mon, 09 Nov 2026 05:17:25 GMT

Ethiopia's Water and Energy Minister, Habtamu Itefa, declared that Egypt's efforts to obstruct Ethiopia's development projects on the Abay River and isolate it from the Red Sea are no longer effective. In an interview, he emphasized that Egypt should reconsider its historical stance towards Ethiopia, particularly regarding the Nile River. He pointed out that Ethiopia has successfully built the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD), which has transformed the dynamics of water usage in the region. Contrary to Egypt's claims, the dam has not reduced downstream water availability. Habtamu also highlighted Ethiopia's commitment to regional cooperation, noting that neighboring countries are already benefiting from electricity generated by the GERD. He criticized Egypt's past attempts to isolate Ethiopia and urged for a collaborative approach to regional development, asserting that Ethiopia's demand for access to the sea is justified given its population and economic growth. He concluded by calling for Egypt to support arrangements that promote regional stability. (159 words)
