Somalia: Key Laftagaren ally survives alleged Turkish drone strike - Somali Guardian

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 12:37:12 GMT

Hassan Abdulkadir, a significant ally of Laftagaren and former internal security minister of Southwest State, survived an alleged Turkish drone strike on Wednesday night. The attack occurred as Laftagaren's forces continued to strengthen their control over the Bay region, which they have taken from the federal government. Reports indicate that two members of Abdulkadir's forces were killed when their motorcycle was hit, but both Abdulkadir and military figure Mustafa Qoomaal escaped unharmed. The motive behind the drone strike remains uncertain, particularly whether it was aimed at assassinating Abdulkadir, who is seen as a key strategist in the ongoing guerrilla campaign following the ousting of the former regional president in March. This incident marks the second airstrike targeting a Somali opposition figure, following an earlier attack on General Mahad Abdirahman in September. The situation highlights the escalating tensions and violence in the region as Laftagaren's forces continue to challenge the federal government's authority.
