Somalia’s Misattribution of Security Failure and the fonts of Federal Overreach - horn review

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:46:05 GMT

The article discusses the legitimacy crisis faced by Somalia's federal government under Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, particularly in the South West State. The government's militarization of a political dispute and externalization of blame onto neighboring countries like Kenya and Ethiopia are highlighted as misattributions of security failures. The crisis, which escalated from March to October 2026, stems from internal constitutional issues rather than foreign interference. The federal government's intervention to remove South West State President Abdiaziz Laftagareen led to a lack of local support for the new administration, resulting in rapid territorial losses. The government's response has involved framing these setbacks as counterterrorism efforts, further complicating the political landscape. The article argues that the core issue lies in the federal government's approach to governance and federalism, which relies on coercion rather than negotiation, perpetuating a cycle of failure and external blame.
