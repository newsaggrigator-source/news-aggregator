Somalia U-15 Cubs Crush DR Congo 4-0 to Reach Tier D Semi-Final in Baku - FTL Somalia

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 10:49:59 GMT

Somalia's U-15 national football team achieved a remarkable 4-0 victory over DR Congo in the Tier D knockout stage of the FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival in Baku, securing their place in the semi-finals. This win follows a previous 3-1 triumph against Tanzania, marking a significant turnaround for the team after a challenging group stage where they finished last in Group T. Despite early defeats, including a historic win against Puerto Rico, the team has demonstrated resilience and skill in the knockout rounds. They will now face Canada in the semi-finals, a match that poses a new challenge against a well-established youth football system. The Somali Football Federation's focus on youth development is evident, as the U-15 and U-17 teams show promise for the future of Somali football. The team's performance in Baku has garnered government support, highlighting the potential for growth in the sport within the country.
