Regional forces capture key towns in southern Somalia - Hiiraan Online

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 21:45:21 GMT

Forces loyal to ousted South West State leader Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed Laftagareen have captured several key towns in southern Somalia, significantly undermining the federally-backed local government. Following President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's military intervention to remove Laftagareen in March, the latter has been attempting to regain control over the region, particularly the city of Baidoa. On Saturday, military officials reported that Laftagareen's forces took control of Berdale, Dinsoor, Buurhakaba, Qansaxdheere, and Awdiinle, which is located about 30 kilometers from Baidoa. This rapid territorial gain has left the local government struggling to maintain authority. While the takeover was mostly conflict-free, some clashes were reported, raising concerns among residents. Local officials have fled to bases, including those housing African Union troops, who did not intervene. Laftagareen praised his forces for their swift actions, while residents expressed confusion over the shifting power dynamics in the region. The situation remains tense as Laftagareen's forces focus on surrounding towns.
