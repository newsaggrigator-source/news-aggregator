Trump says Ilhan Omar could face jail as he attacks her Somali roots - The National

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 03:00:00 GMT

During an event in the Oval Office, President Donald Trump criticized Democratic Representative Ilhan Omar, suggesting she could face jail time amid a federal investigation into her immigration paperwork. He labeled her as "a sick and very dishonest person" who "hates our country," and disparaged her Somali heritage, calling Somalia a "real [expletive]" with no government. Trump's remarks come as he continues to attack Omar, one of the first Muslim women elected to Congress, and reflect a broader trend of Islamophobic rhetoric directed at Muslim politicians. Omar, who fled civil war in Somalia as a child and represents Minnesota's 5th District, has denied any wrongdoing, claiming the allegations against her are politically motivated.
