Somali Ministers Call for Dialogue as Somaliland Elders Attend Mogadishu Event - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 19:57:31 GMT

Somali federal ministers have called for national dialogue during a welcoming event for Somaliland elders in Mogadishu, emphasizing the importance of unity over separation. Defence Minister Ahmed Moalim Fiqi highlighted the need to address historical grievances stemming from the military government era, suggesting that discussions about the 1960 union agreement's flaws are necessary for healing. Information Minister Abdulfatah Kasim Mohamud reinforced this message, stating that separation cannot achieve what unity has failed to accomplish. The ministers' remarks come amid heightened political sensitivity regarding Somaliland's status, particularly following Israel's recognition of Somaliland as a sovereign state, which Mogadishu vehemently opposes. The presence of Somaliland elders at the event signals a willingness for cross-regional collaboration, despite the Hargeisa leadership's rejection of unity. However, the ministers' calls for dialogue lack a concrete framework, raising concerns about the effectiveness of such outreach. For meaningful dialogue to occur, it must transition from ceremonial events to structured negotiations addressing power-sharing and recognition issues that have long defined the Somalia-Somaliland impasse.
