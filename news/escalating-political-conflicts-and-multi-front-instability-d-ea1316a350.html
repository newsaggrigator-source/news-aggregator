Escalating Political Conflicts and Multi-Front Instability Deepen Crisis Across Ethiopia - Borkena

Mon, 16 Nov 2026 15:23:27 GMT

Ethiopia is facing a severe crisis marked by escalating political conflicts and multi-front instability, particularly in the Amhara, Tigray, and Oromia regions. Over the past two months, armed violence and inter-communal clashes have resulted in more than 1,500 deaths, with October alone accounting for 863 fatalities, including around 300 civilians. Analysts attribute this surge in violence to a "conflict-based political economy" and systemic governance failures, where armed conflict has become a tool for political enforcement by both state and insurgent forces. The resurgence of military hostilities between federal forces and Tigray units, along with the formation of coalitions among various armed groups, has further strained state security. Humanitarian conditions are deteriorating, with significant displacement and urgent needs arising from territorial disputes and violence. Critics argue that the government has failed to protect its citizens, highlighting the necessity for renewed political dialogue and comprehensive security reforms to address the ongoing instability.
