Russia Offers to Mediate Ethiopia-Eritrea Talks at Moscow Summit - Borkena

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 17:10:51 GMT

Russia has offered to mediate talks between Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed and Eritrean President Isaias Afwerki during the third Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow. This proposal comes amid escalating tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea, particularly following a deterioration in their diplomatic relations since September 2026. Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov emphasized the importance of resolving differences through diplomatic channels, highlighting the risks posed by prolonged estrangement between the two nations. The summit marks the first time both leaders have attended the same international forum since the recent conflicts in northern Ethiopia. While no official confirmation of a bilateral meeting has been made, the summit presents a potential opportunity for direct engagement. Lavrov also addressed broader regional stability concerns and discussed cooperation on nuclear energy and maritime security with Ethiopia. The international community is closely monitoring developments in the Horn of Africa, where military tensions have raised alarms about regional instability.
