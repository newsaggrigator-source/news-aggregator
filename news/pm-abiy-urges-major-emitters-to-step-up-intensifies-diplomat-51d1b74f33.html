PM Abiy Urges Major Emitters to Step Up, Intensifies Diplomatic Outreach Ahead of Ethiopia's COP32

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 07:40:35 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has called on major greenhouse gas emitters to enhance their emissions reductions and expedite climate financing as Ethiopia prepares to host the 32nd United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP32) in November 2027. During COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, Abiy emphasized the need for countries with significant historical emissions to take the lead in cutting emissions, highlighting the diversion of resources from essential development areas like education and healthcare due to climate-related damages. His diplomatic outreach included meetings with leaders from Somalia, Barbados, Finland, Norway, Qatar, Iraq, and Botswana, focusing on bilateral cooperation and climate diplomacy. Abiy also discussed Ethiopia's initiatives, such as restrictions on petrol and diesel vehicle imports, aimed at transitioning to electric mobility. As Ethiopia gears up for COP32, the challenge remains to secure concrete commitments on climate financing and adaptation, particularly for vulnerable nations facing severe climate impacts.
