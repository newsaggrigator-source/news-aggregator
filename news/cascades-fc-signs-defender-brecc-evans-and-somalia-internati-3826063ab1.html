Cascades FC signs defender Brecc Evans and Somalia international Mo Mohamed - KCBY

Tue, 17 Nov 2026 21:11:40 GMT

Cascades FC has announced the signing of defender Brecc Evans and Somalia international Mo Mohamed, enhancing their roster with experienced talent. The club made the announcement on November 17, 2026, in Eugene, Oregon, with the signings pending league and federation approval. Evans, a veteran center back, has been a standout in USL League One since turning professional in 2019, known for his defensive skills and leadership qualities. He previously played for Portland Hearts of Pine, where he made 32 appearances in the 2026 season. Mohamed, an attacking fullback from Portland, recently debuted for the Somalia national team and has shown promise in his performances with Portland Hearts of Pine. Both players expressed excitement about joining Cascades FC, with Evans emphasizing his commitment to bringing success to the club and its loyal fanbase. The signings reflect Cascades FC's strategy to strengthen their defense while adding dynamic attacking options.
