UN Staff Evacuated From Somalia to Kenya After UNTMIS Closure - Garowe Online

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 11:34:39 GMT

United Nations staff were evacuated from the Halane compound in Mogadishu, Somalia, to Nairobi, Kenya, following the official closure of the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) on October 31, 2026. This marked the end of a significant political mission that had been active for decades, transitioning responsibilities to Somali institutions as part of a two-year process aimed at enhancing national ownership of political and institutional affairs. While the closure of UNTMIS signifies a shift in the UN's engagement in Somalia, other UN agencies will continue to operate in the country, focusing on humanitarian assistance, development, and peacebuilding efforts. The evacuation raised questions about future arrangements for international staff in the region. The Halane compound has been a key base for international operations, and the UN's ongoing presence in Somalia will adapt to the new phase following the mission's conclusion.
