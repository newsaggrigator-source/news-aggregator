UN political mission in Somalia closes after more than three decades - TRT Afrika

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 20:12:06 GMT

The UN Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) officially concluded its operations on Saturday, marking the end of over three decades of UN political engagement in the country. This closure followed a request from the Somali government, which sought to transition away from the longstanding mission. Throughout its tenure, UN political missions have played a crucial role in supporting Somalia's peacebuilding and state-building efforts, aiding in governance restoration and national reconciliation. Raisedon Zenenga, the mission's officer-in-charge, emphasized that this closure signifies not an end to the partnership between the UN and Somalia, but rather a new chapter. UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres acknowledged the resilience of the Somali people and called for continued international support to address ongoing political, security, and humanitarian challenges. Despite the mission's end, the UN remains committed to fostering peace and development in Somalia through its resident agencies and broader initiatives.
