PM Abiy Welcomes Global Health Leaders to Cphia 2026 in Addis Ababa

Fri, 20 Nov 2026 17:26:51 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has invited African leaders, health experts, and global partners to the Fifth International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026), scheduled for November 23-27 in Addis Ababa. In a video message, he emphasized the importance of collective action to enhance health resilience across the continent. The conference, co-hosted by Ethiopia and the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention, aims to unite heads of state, ministers, researchers, and development partners to address public health challenges in Africa. Under the theme "Africa's Health Security and Sovereignty," CPHIA 2026 will focus on reducing external dependencies in healthcare, strengthening resilient systems, and promoting African ownership of health priorities. PM Abiy expressed pride in hosting the event and highlighted the opportunity to foster partnerships and advance solutions tailored to Africa's health needs. He called for unity in transforming shared aspirations into actionable outcomes for a healthier Africa. (159 words)
