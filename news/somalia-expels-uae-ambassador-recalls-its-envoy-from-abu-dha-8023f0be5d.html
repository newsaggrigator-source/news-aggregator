Somalia expels UAE ambassador, recalls its envoy from Abu Dhabi - Somalia Today

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 12:07:30 GMT

Somalia's cabinet has expelled the UAE ambassador, ordering him to leave within 72 hours, and recalled its own envoy from Abu Dhabi amid escalating diplomatic tensions. This decision, made during a cabinet meeting led by Prime Minister Hamza Abdi Barre, follows serious accusations against the UAE for interfering in Somalia's internal affairs and undermining its sovereignty. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud has specifically accused the UAE of establishing military bases and supporting armed factions without the federal government's consent. The foreign ministry's report highlighted concerns over Emirati actions that threaten Somalia's national integrity. The situation has worsened since January when Somalia canceled several agreements with the UAE related to security and defense, citing similar sovereignty concerns. The diplomatic rift has intensified further after a senior Emirati adviser publicly criticized the Somali president's claims, indicating a significant deterioration in relations between Mogadishu and Abu Dhabi.
