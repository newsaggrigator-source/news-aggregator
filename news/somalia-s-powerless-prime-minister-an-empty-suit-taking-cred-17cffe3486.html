Somalia’s Powerless Prime Minister: An Empty Suit Taking Credit for Decisions He Did Not Make. A Government of Political Theatre, Misleading Statements, and Weak Leadership - Hiiraan Online

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 01:00:29 GMT

Somalia's Prime Minister is criticized for lacking genuine leadership and taking credit for decisions made by international bodies, particularly regarding the United Nations Support Office in Somalia (UNSOS). The article highlights the Prime Minister's misleading public statements, which confuse the separate mandates of UNSOS and the now-concluded United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS). It argues that the Prime Minister should clarify whether the Somali government officially requested the cancellation of UNSOS or if it simply expired. The piece emphasizes the need for a Prime Minister who can independently lead, challenge ineffective policies, and communicate transparently with the public. As Somalia grapples with significant political and security challenges, the call for authentic leadership is urgent. The article concludes that the country deserves a Prime Minister who prioritizes accountability and national interests over political theatrics and empty rhetoric. Somalia requires a leader capable of genuine governance, not just a figurehead. (160 words)
