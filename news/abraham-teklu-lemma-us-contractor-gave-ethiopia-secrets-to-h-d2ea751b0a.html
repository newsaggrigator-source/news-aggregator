Abraham Teklu Lemma: US contractor gave Ethiopia secrets to help kill Tigray fighters - BBC

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 10:35:03 GMT

Abraham Teklu Lemma, an Ethiopian-born American citizen and former US contractor, has pleaded guilty to sharing classified intelligence with Ethiopian officials during the country's civil war from 2020 to 2022. He admitted to passing sensitive documents and photographs, including satellite imagery and military activity details, which he hoped would assist in targeting Tigray fighters. Lemma, who worked for various US government agencies, used his position to extract classified materials and communicate with Ethiopian officials through encrypted messages. His actions were described as a severe betrayal of his country and colleagues, jeopardizing national security. Facing up to 22 years in prison, Lemma's sentencing is scheduled for April 2027. The case highlights the significant risks associated with the unauthorized transfer of sensitive intelligence, particularly during a conflict that resulted in widespread atrocities and loss of life. The FBI noted that the intelligence he provided was utilized in military operations against Tigray fighters.
