Puntland President Calls on Somalia’s President to Resign - Horseed Media

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 15:31:00 GMT

Puntland President Said Abdullahi Deni has publicly called for the resignation of Somalia’s President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, citing ongoing political disputes between the federal government and Puntland. In statements made to media outlets linked to his administration, Deni criticized President Mohamud for failing to unify the Somali people and for not organizing elections as per a political agreement. He emphasized Puntland's significant contributions to the development of Somalia, asserting that his call for the president's resignation is in the best interest of the nation and its citizens. Deni's remarks highlight the tensions within Somalia's political landscape, as Puntland seeks to address grievances and push for a more effective governance structure. His appeal reflects broader frustrations regarding the federal government's performance and the need for political accountability in the country.
