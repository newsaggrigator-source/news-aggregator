Danish Diplomat Hails Ethiopia's Energy Advances As Catalyst for Strategic Partnership

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 21:33:45 GMT

A Danish diplomat has praised Ethiopia's advancements in renewable energy as a foundation for enhancing strategic partnerships between Denmark and Ethiopia. During an interview at the Third Ethiopian Water and Energy Week in Addis Ababa, Thomas Fleurine Sorensen, Counsellor at the Danish Embassy, emphasized the potential for cooperation in clean energy, water management, and climate resilience. He highlighted the Assela Wind Farm as a key example of successful collaboration. Sorensen noted that Ethiopia's commitment to sustainable development and its expanding electricity grid could serve as a model for other African nations. He reaffirmed Denmark's support for Ethiopia's development priorities and stressed the need for evidence-based policymaking in the energy sector. With Ethiopia set to host COP32 in 2027, he underscored the importance of strategic planning in achieving climate goals and commended Ethiopia's bold policy decisions in renewable energy and green growth. Denmark is ready to assist Ethiopia in its climate ambitions. (157 words)
