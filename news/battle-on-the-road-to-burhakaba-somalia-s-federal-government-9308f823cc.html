Battle on the Road to Burhakaba: Somalia’s Federal Government Forces Suffer Heavy Losses - Garowe Online

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 21:30:41 GMT

Somalia's federal government forces have faced significant losses during a military operation aimed at recapturing Buurhakaba, a town currently held by forces loyal to exiled South West State President Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed, known as Laftagareen. The elite Gorgor and Danab units suffered heavy casualties and lost 36 military vehicles, including six Turkish-made Kirpi armored vehicles, during intense fighting along the Mogadishu-Baidoa highway. The operation, which has been criticized for its timing and execution, coincides with ongoing political instability in Somalia, as President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud seeks to consolidate authority amid disputes over the legitimacy of rival administrations. Concerns have also been raised about the vulnerability of federal troops to Al-Shabaab attacks, which have historically plagued the region. The situation is further complicated by Uganda's warning of potential troop withdrawal unless Somalia's political crisis is resolved, highlighting the precarious security landscape in the country.
