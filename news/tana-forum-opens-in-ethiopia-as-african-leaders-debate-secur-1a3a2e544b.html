Tana Forum Opens in Ethiopia As African Leaders Debate Security and Economic Sovereignty

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 18:25:56 GMT

The 12th Tana High-Level Forum on Security in Africa commenced in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, from October 23 to 25, 2026, focusing on peace, security challenges, and economic sovereignty. Under the theme "Pathways to Peace in Africa: Economic Sovereignty and Geopolitical Transformation," the forum gathered African leaders, policymakers, and security experts to discuss strategies for enhancing economic resilience and governance. Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed emphasized the need for African ownership of initiatives and sustainable financing. Key discussions included the importance of local value addition, reducing dependency on external markets, and strengthening regional cooperation. Uganda's Defence Minister, Kiryowa Kiwanuka, advocated for prioritizing domestic processing of raw materials to retain economic value. The forum also addressed the relationship between economic policies and peace, highlighting the necessity for effective resource management and regional integration. Participants explored how to leverage Africa's resource wealth for collective prosperity while navigating geopolitical rivalries and external dependencies.
