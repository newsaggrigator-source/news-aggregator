Somalia Says Beledweyne Airport Not Permanently Closed, Demands Troop Withdrawal - FTL Somalia

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 12:01:06 GMT

Somalia's Minister of Transport, Mohamed Farah Nuux, clarified that Beledweyne's Ugaas Khaliif Airport is not permanently closed, despite a recent Notice to Airmen (NOTAM) that suspended flights until December 1 due to security concerns. The government is reviewing the security situation and has called for the withdrawal of troops from the airport to ensure a stable environment for operations. The airport's closure follows a clash between Djiboutian troops and local militia, highlighting ongoing tensions in the region. Nuux emphasized the need for a peaceful atmosphere for airlines and travelers, while acknowledging that armed groups remain a threat. The minister's remarks indicate a shift in the government's approach, framing the closure as temporary and contingent on security improvements. However, the underlying political disputes in Hiiraan remain unresolved, and without a political settlement, the airport's status may not change, leaving it effectively closed.
