ICRC Reaffirms Commitment to Partnership With Ethiopia

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 05:05:21 GMT

The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) has reaffirmed its commitment to enhancing its partnership with Ethiopia, recognizing the vital humanitarian efforts of the Ethiopian Red Cross Society (ERCS). In a meeting with President Taye Atske Selassie, ICRC Regional Director for Africa Mamadou Sow praised the ERCS for its extensive work throughout the country and underscored the significance of international humanitarian law and effective leadership in delivering essential aid. Sow commended President Taye for fostering collaboration among various Ethiopian government ministries, including Health, Justice, and Defense. He expressed gratitude for the access granted to the ICRC, which facilitates its humanitarian operations. Highlighting the ERCS as a crucial partner, Sow emphasized its role in programs addressing water, health, and food security, while also acknowledging the invaluable contributions of its volunteer network, which he described as the backbone of the organization and the broader Red Cross movement.
