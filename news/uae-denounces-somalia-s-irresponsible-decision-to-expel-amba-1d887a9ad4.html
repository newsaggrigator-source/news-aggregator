UAE denounces Somalia’s ‘irresponsible’ decision to expel ambassador - Arab News

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 20:38:31 GMT

The UAE has condemned Somalia's recent decision to expel its ambassador, Ahmad Juma Al-Rumaithi, labeling it as "escalatory and hostile." Somalia ordered the UAE diplomat to leave within 72 hours and recalled its own ambassador from Abu Dhabi, citing actions by the UAE that allegedly threaten Somalia's sovereignty and national integrity. The UAE's foreign ministry criticized the move, stating it reflects "irresponsible positions" that damage bilateral relations and cooperation. The ministry emphasized that diplomatic decisions should be based on shared interests rather than internal political conflicts. This diplomatic rift follows a previous period of tension, during which the UAE closed its embassy in Mogadishu and withdrew staff, accusing Somalia of hostile policies, including the cancellation of bilateral agreements. The situation highlights ongoing strains in the relationship between the two nations, which have historically been linked.
