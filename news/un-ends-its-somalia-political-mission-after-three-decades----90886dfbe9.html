UN ends its Somalia political mission after three decades - Oman Observer

Sat, 31 Oct 2026 16:30:00 GMT

The United Nations officially concluded its political mission in Somalia on October 31, 2026, marking the end of over three decades of peacebuilding efforts in the country. The UN Support Mission in Somalia (UNSM) was established in 2013 to assist Somali authorities in their transition to democracy following years of conflict. Initially launched as the UN Political Office for Somalia (UNPOS) in 1995, the mission has evolved through various phases. Raisedon Zenenga, the UN's deputy special representative for Somalia, emphasized that this closure signifies not an end to the partnership between the UN and Somalia, but a transition to a new chapter. Despite the progress made, significant challenges remain, particularly with the militant group Al Shabaab, which continues to exert control over large areas of central Somalia. The responsibilities of the mission have now been transferred to Somali institutions and the UN country team.
