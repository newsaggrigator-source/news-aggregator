Algerian Delegates Hail Ethiopia's Dev't Model, Call for Stronger Pan-African Political Cooperation

Fri, 13 Nov 2026 05:20:17 GMT

Algerian delegates attending the Second African Political Parties Summit in Ethiopia praised the country's development model and called for enhanced political cooperation across Africa. Jarrallah Abdelmonem, a member of Algeria's National Building Movement party, highlighted the potential for Algeria to learn from Ethiopia's experiences, particularly its impressive economic growth of over 9 percent last year. He emphasized that fostering collaboration among political parties could significantly contribute to economic development and the well-being of African citizens. Another party member, Ali Hashani, commended Ethiopia's infrastructure advancements and expressed hope that other African nations would adopt similar strategies for progress. The Ethiopian Prime Minister's focus on youth leadership during the summit was also noted, with delegates recognizing the importance of empowering a new generation of leaders to drive the continent's future development. Overall, the summit served as a platform for exchanging ideas and strengthening ties among African political entities. (151 words)
