Sudanese Govt Rejects Ethiopia's Initiative to Host Sudanese Dialogue, Calls for Its Exclusion From Facilitation Efforts

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 05:38:34 GMT

The Sudanese government has firmly rejected Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's proposal for Ethiopia to host a dialogue aimed at resolving Sudan's ongoing conflicts. The Sudanese authorities emphasized that any host must demonstrate neutrality and respect Sudan's sovereignty, which they argue Ethiopia has failed to do due to allegations of its support for the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) militia and the use of Ethiopian territory for drone attacks against Sudan. In a statement, the government called for an independent investigation into these accusations and asserted its right to take necessary diplomatic and legal actions to protect its sovereignty. Additionally, Sudan urged the African Union and other regional and international stakeholders to respect its stance and exclude Ethiopia from any facilitation efforts. The government reiterated that the decision regarding the dialogue's venue and agenda should be determined by the Sudanese people through consensus and free will, underscoring that Sudan's sovereignty is non-negotiable.
