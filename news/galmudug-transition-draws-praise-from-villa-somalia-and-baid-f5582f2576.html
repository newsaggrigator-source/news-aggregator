Galmudug Transition Draws Praise from Villa Somalia and Baidoa as New Leadership Urged to Prioritize Security - FTL Somalia

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 17:15:00 GMT

Galmudug's recent leadership transition has garnered praise from both Villa Somalia and Baidoa, following the parliamentary vote held in Dhusamareb. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud and South West State President Abdiaziz Hassan Mohamed congratulated the newly elected President Liban Ahmed Hassan and Vice President Abdiweli Ibrahim Adan (Garyare), emphasizing the importance of prioritizing security, resolving local conflicts, and enhancing economic infrastructure. The election, which saw Hassan secure 72 out of 89 votes, was described as historic, involving significant public participation despite concerns over its legitimacy due to the exclusion of the Towfiiq party. Notably, the Sincad party's candidate accepted the results, indicating a positive political culture. Laftagareen's supportive message reflects a desire for regional unity amid his own political challenges. However, the new leadership faces significant hurdles, including persistent security issues and clan grievances, necessitating effective governance to translate the goodwill from Mogadishu and Baidoa into tangible progress for Galmudug.
