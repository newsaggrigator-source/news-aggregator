‘Not Even Somalia Wants You’: Ilhan Omar Gets Brutal Reality Check; Trump Goes On Unhinged Rant - The Times of India

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 21:55:10 GMT

In a recent exchange, Donald Trump launched a vehement attack on Congresswoman Ilhan Omar, targeting her Somali heritage. During his remarks, Trump referred to Somalia as a “shithouse country” and questioned why the U.S. should accept immigrants from nations he deems undesirable. He provocatively stated, “Not even Somalia wants you,” intensifying the ongoing political discourse surrounding Omar and immigration issues. This confrontation highlights the contentious atmosphere in U.S. politics, particularly regarding immigration and the treatment of representatives with immigrant backgrounds. Omar's roots and her political stance have made her a frequent target for criticism from Trump, reflecting broader tensions within American political dialogue. The incident underscores the divisive nature of contemporary political rhetoric and the challenges faced by lawmakers like Omar in navigating these hostile exchanges.
