UN agency warns nearly 874,000 could be displaced by floods in Somalia - A News

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:16:01 GMT

The UN migration agency has warned that nearly 874,000 people in Somalia could be displaced within the next three months due to severe flooding caused by anticipated heavy rainfall and rising river levels. This situation is exacerbating an already critical humanitarian crisis, as many communities are still recovering from drought and previous displacements. The International Organization for Migration (IOM) estimates that 92% of the potential displacements will be due to flooding, particularly affecting areas like Beletweyne and Gaalkacyo. The IOM has only secured 14.4% of the $16.62 million needed for flood response efforts, which aim to assist over 517,000 individuals. The agency is calling for immediate international funding to enhance early warning systems, prepare evacuation routes, and provide essential services such as emergency shelter and clean water.
