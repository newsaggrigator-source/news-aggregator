UAE Adviser Calls Mohamud’s Speech an “Attack,” But Doesn’t Address the Allegations - www.ftlsomalia.com

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 21:11:07 GMT

In response to Somali President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's recent speech, a senior adviser from the United Arab Emirates (UAE) labeled the remarks as an "attack." The adviser, however, did not directly address the specific allegations made by Mohamud regarding the UAE's involvement in Somalia. The speech has sparked controversy, as it accused foreign entities of meddling in Somalia's internal affairs, which the UAE adviser dismissed without providing a counterargument. This exchange highlights ongoing tensions between Somalia and the UAE, particularly concerning diplomatic relations and foreign influence in the region. The lack of a direct response to the allegations raises questions about the UAE's stance and intentions in Somalia, as both nations navigate complex political dynamics. The situation remains fluid, with potential implications for future interactions between the two countries as they seek to address their differences.
