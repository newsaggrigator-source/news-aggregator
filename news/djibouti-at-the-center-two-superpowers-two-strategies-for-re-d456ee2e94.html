Djibouti at the Center: Two Superpowers, Two Strategies for Red Sea Security - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 08:42:52 GMT

Djibouti is becoming a focal point for both China and the United States as they pursue distinct strategies to address security concerns in the Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Recently, Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi and Djiboutian counterpart Abdoulkader Houssein Omar issued a joint statement expressing worries over threats to shipping in the Red Sea, emphasizing the need for dialogue to ease tensions linked to the broader Middle East conflict. In contrast, the U.S. announced the opening of the Maritime Rule of Law Center of Excellence in Djibouti, aimed at enhancing maritime law enforcement capabilities in the region. Djibouti's strategic location has attracted multiple foreign military bases, including the U.S. Camp Lemonnier and China's military base, making it a hub for global military infrastructure. However, Somalia, which shares the same maritime security challenges, remains under-resourced and lacks the attention and support needed to address piracy and illegal activities along its extensive coastline, facing the repercussions of the geopolitical rivalry without adequate assistance.
