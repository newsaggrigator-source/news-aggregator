Ethiopia's Women, Youth Participation in National Development Impresses African Summit Participants

Tue, 10 Nov 2026 14:58:54 GMT

Ethiopia's significant engagement of women and youth in national development has garnered admiration at the Second African Political Parties Summit. Participants, including Ngako Patricia-Thelsy Moveni from Cameroon, highlighted Ethiopia's progress in various sectors such as infrastructure, renewable energy, and technology. Moveni praised the active roles of women and youth in government, suggesting that Ethiopia serves as a model for other African nations. She emphasized the importance of including these groups in discussions about Africa's future, fostering connections between them and governmental bodies like the African Union and UNDP. Limam Boussif from Morocco also commended Ethiopia's advancements, noting similarities between the two countries that could enhance bilateral relations. He stressed the necessity for increased youth involvement in political leadership, advocating for their active participation in decision-making processes. Overall, the summit underscored Ethiopia's pivotal role in shaping Africa's development and the potential of its youth to contribute meaningfully to the continent's future. (159 words)
