Somalia breaks ground on new naval facilities with Turkey's assistance - The Jerusalem Post

Sun, 22 Nov 2026 08:52:04 GMT

Somalia has commenced the construction of new naval facilities with the assistance of Turkey, marking a significant step in enhancing its maritime defense capabilities. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud laid the foundation for three facilities, including the Somali Navy Command Headquarters and a maritime operations coordination center. Defense Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi emphasized the partnership with Turkey as crucial for building national institutions and strengthening Somalia's defense. Turkey has been a key ally, providing military training, counterterrorism support, and economic investment in Somalia. The collaboration aims to bolster Somalia's sovereignty and protect its extensive coastline, which is vital for national security and economic stability. The groundbreaking ceremony featured Turkish flags, highlighting the deepening ties between the two nations. Turkey's military presence in Somalia has expanded significantly, reflecting its strategic interests in the Horn of Africa and the Red Sea region. This partnership underscores Somalia's reliance on Turkey for military and defense support.
