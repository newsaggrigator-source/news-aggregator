African Union Endorses Three-Phase AUSSOM Exit, Warns Against Funding-Driven Drawdown - FTL Somalia

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 20:47:49 GMT

The African Union’s Peace and Security Council has approved a three-phase exit strategy for the African Union Transition Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), emphasizing the need for a careful and coordinated withdrawal to avoid creating vulnerabilities that could be exploited by Al-Shabaab. The plan, which spans from November 2026 to post-2027, was endorsed following a joint operational planning meeting in Mogadishu. The Council warned against a funding-driven drawdown, which could reverse progress made in stabilizing the region. It also highlighted the importance of securing financial and logistical support from international partners, as the mission faces a projected funding shortfall of approximately $190 million. While acknowledging recent successes by Somali security forces, the communiqué reflects ongoing political instability in Somalia, with the government facing a crisis regarding its legitimacy. The Council's endorsement of the transition plan underscores the necessity for a political resolution to ensure a sustainable security framework moving forward.
