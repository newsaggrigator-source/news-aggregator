Court News: High-Level Ethiopian Ministry Officials and Associates Charged in Over 1.1 Billion Birr Corruption and Money Laundering Scheme - EthiopiaToday

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 14:35:46 GMT

High-level officials from the Ethiopian Ministry of Finance and their associates have been charged in connection with a corruption and money laundering scheme involving over 1.1 billion Birr. The charges stem from allegations of embezzlement and misappropriation of public funds, with the accused reportedly diverting significant amounts of money intended for government projects. The investigation, led by the Federal Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission, uncovered a network of fraudulent activities that exploited the ministry's financial systems. The case highlights ongoing concerns about corruption within the Ethiopian government and the efforts to hold accountable those involved in such illicit practices. The accused individuals face serious legal repercussions as the case progresses through the judicial system.
