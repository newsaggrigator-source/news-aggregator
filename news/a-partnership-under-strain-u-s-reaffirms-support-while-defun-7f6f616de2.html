A Partnership Under Strain: U.S. Reaffirms Support While Defunding the Mission Somalia Depends On - FTL Somalia

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 16:17:23 GMT

The U.S. reaffirmed its security partnership with Somalia during a meeting between Somali Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi and U.S. Chargé d’Affaires Ruth Anne Stevens-Klitz, despite simultaneously withdrawing funding for the AUSSOM mission, which is crucial for Somalia's security. The discussions included the handover of a former AU base in Barawe to the Somali National Army (SNA), highlighting the need for a well-coordinated security transition. However, this handover occurs amidst a backdrop of financial strain on the security architecture, as the U.S. has ceased funding for the UN Support Office in Somalia, which provides essential resources for AUSSOM personnel. The Somali army faces significant challenges, including limited logistics and command issues, raising concerns about its ability to maintain control over territories previously secured by foreign forces. The situation is further complicated by political instability, as the Somali government struggles to assert authority, leaving the SNA vulnerable to potential threats from Al-Shabaab.
