Somalia’s Danab Commandos Launch Operation Banooda, Kill Seven Al-Shabaab Militants - FTL Somalia

Sat, 24 Oct 2026 21:39:48 GMT

Somalia's elite Danab Commandos initiated "Operation Banooda" on October 25, 2026, targeting Al-Shabaab militants in the South West State. In the operation's first engagement near Leego, seven militants were killed, and several others were injured, with troops seizing various weapons. The Ministry of Defence praised the Danab Commandos for delivering a "stern lesson" to Al-Shabaab, emphasizing the operation's significant victory. The Danab Brigade, supported by U.S. training and resources, has been pivotal in counterterrorism efforts, previously conducting successful operations against Al-Shabaab and ISIS-affiliated groups. The Bay region, where Leego is located, has been a persistent battleground against Al-Shabaab, which continues to conduct attacks despite military efforts. The operation reflects a broader strategy by the Somali government to reclaim territory and combat insurgency, although challenges remain, including the group's ability to regroup and exploit political divisions. The effectiveness of Operation Banooda will ultimately depend on sustaining security and addressing local governance issues beyond military victories.
