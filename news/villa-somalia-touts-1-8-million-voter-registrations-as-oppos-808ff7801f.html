Villa Somalia Touts 1.8 Million Voter Registrations as Opposition Rejects Electoral Process - FTL Somalia

Mon, 26 Oct 2026 14:45:24 GMT

Villa Somalia has announced that 1.8 million citizens have registered to vote, with 1,435 officials elected across 1,102 polling stations in 50 of Somalia's 92 districts. President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud emphasized this achievement as a step towards restoring democracy after 57 years, claiming no armed incidents occurred during the voting process. However, the opposition, particularly the Somali Future Council, has rejected the electoral process, arguing that it lacks legitimacy due to the exclusion of major political parties and the absence of a consensus-driven approach. They demand a halt to what they view as unilateral actions by the government and are open to genuine dialogue to establish an electoral agreement. The timing of the government's announcement, ahead of a planned National Consultative Forum, raises concerns that it aims to solidify its position rather than engage in meaningful negotiations. The situation remains tense, with warnings from regional leaders about the potential risks to Somali statehood if consensus is not achieved.
