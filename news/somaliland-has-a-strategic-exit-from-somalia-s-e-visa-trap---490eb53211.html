Somaliland Has A Strategic Exit From Somalia’s e-VISA Trap - The Times of Israel

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 21:32:00 GMT

Somaliland has successfully established its own e-VISA system, allowing it to bypass the challenges posed by Somalia's centralized visa process. This move is seen as a strategic exit from the complexities and limitations of Somalia's e-VISA framework, which has often hindered travel and economic opportunities for Somaliland. By implementing its own system, Somaliland aims to enhance its international standing and facilitate easier access for travelers. The new e-VISA initiative is expected to boost tourism and trade, contributing to the region's economic development. Somaliland's decision underscores its ongoing efforts to assert its autonomy and improve its global engagement, despite the lack of international recognition as an independent state.
