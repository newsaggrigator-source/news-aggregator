Somalia’s Neighbor Signals Progress: Kenya Says Ebola Outbreak “Within Reach” of Ending - FTL Somalia

Fri, 23 Oct 2026 17:12:28 GMT

Kenya's Ministry of Health has reported significant progress in managing the Ebola outbreak, with no new cases confirmed for 17 days. The total confirmed cases remain at nine, all linked to a single cluster, and all 135 contacts have been traced, with the first group of quarantined individuals set to return home soon. The Ministry's Director General, Dr. Patrick Amoth, emphasized the effectiveness of Kenya's containment measures, which have prevented community transmission. This progress is crucial for Somalia, as Nairobi serves as a key transit hub for the region. Somalia's preparedness, however, remains a concern, with its airport screening rated at only 20% capacity and lacking dedicated isolation facilities. While Kenya has established a National Ebola Taskforce to coordinate its response, Somalia is still developing its capacity to manage potential outbreaks. The ongoing Ebola crisis in the Democratic Republic of Congo adds urgency to Somalia's efforts to strengthen its health infrastructure and response capabilities.
