Ethiopia, Pakistan Agree to Deepen Defence and Aviation Cooperation

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 12:42:58 GMT

Ethiopia and Pakistan have agreed to enhance their cooperation in defence and aviation, marking a significant step towards a strategic partnership. This agreement was established during a meeting in Rawalpindi between Ethiopia's Ambassador to Pakistan, Oumer Hussein, and Pakistan's Federal Secretary for Defence, Lt. Gen. (R) Muhammad Ali. The discussions focused on mutual interests, including regional peace and security, and the potential for expanding collaboration in various sectors such as trade, investment, tourism, and culture. Ambassador Oumer emphasized Ethiopia's commitment to strengthening bilateral relations, while Secretary Ali expressed support for advancing the partnership under Pakistan's "Look Africa" and "Engage Africa" initiatives. This initiative reflects both countries' desire to broaden their engagement and explore new areas of cooperation, as stated by Ethiopia's embassy in Islamabad. The agreement signifies a growing relationship between Ethiopia and Pakistan, aiming to foster stability and collaboration in the region. (160 words)
