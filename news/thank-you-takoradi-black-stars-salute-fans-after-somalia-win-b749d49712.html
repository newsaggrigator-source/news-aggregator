Thank You, Takoradi — Black Stars salute fans after Somalia win, calls for same Energy on Monday - Ghana Football Association

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 22:15:32 GMT

The Ghana Football Association (GFA) and the Black Stars expressed their heartfelt gratitude to the fans in Takoradi for their unwavering support during the recent Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Somalia. The match, held at Sekondi Sports Stadium, saw the Black Stars secure a 2-0 victory, marking their first win in the qualifying campaign. The enthusiastic crowd created an electrifying atmosphere that motivated the players throughout the game. The GFA acknowledged the significant impact of the fans' passion and energy, emphasizing that it felt like a true home advantage. As the team prepares for the return fixture against Somalia on November 16, 2026, they are calling on fans to replicate their support, urging them to fill the stadium once again with the same fervor. The Black Stars aim to build on their recent success and are hopeful for another victory with the fans' backing.
