Ethiopia ranks 30th in Governance Index as security decline offsets gains - CapitalEthiopia

Sat, 07 Nov 2026 22:00:00 GMT

Ethiopia has been ranked 30th in the latest Governance Index, reflecting a mixed performance in governance and security. While the country has made notable strides in areas such as political participation and civil liberties, a significant decline in security has overshadowed these advancements. The Governance Index evaluates various factors, including the rule of law, government effectiveness, and political stability. Analysts suggest that the deterioration in security, particularly in regions affected by ethnic conflicts and violence, has negatively impacted overall governance. This situation poses challenges for the Ethiopian government as it seeks to balance progress in governance with the pressing need to address security concerns. The findings highlight the importance of improving security measures to sustain governance improvements and foster a stable environment for citizens. As Ethiopia navigates these complex issues, the need for comprehensive strategies to enhance both governance and security remains critical for the nation's future stability and development.
