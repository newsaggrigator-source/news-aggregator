Salsay Weyane Tigray Warns of Famine-Driven Displacement in Tselemti, Calls for Halt to Revenge-Driven Actions

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 05:32:24 GMT

Salsay Weyane Tigray, an opposition political group in Ethiopia, has issued a grave warning regarding the escalating famine and displacement in Lailay Tselemti Woreda, Northwestern Tigray. The group reports that severe hunger has forced residents to flee their homes in search of survival amid worsening humanitarian conditions exacerbated by drought, extreme weather, and renewed conflict. They highlight that many are leaving areas under "invaders," and the situation is dire, with projections indicating that parts of Tigray could face Emergency food insecurity by early 2027. A recent assessment revealed widespread food insecurity and acute child malnutrition, with many families already struggling before the recent resurgence of violence. Salsay Weyane Tigray calls for an end to revenge-driven actions and urges authorities to prioritize humanitarian efforts and inclusive dialogue to address the region's challenges, emphasizing that political power should serve the public interest rather than personal agendas. They advocate for accountability through judicial means rather than unilateral retaliation.
