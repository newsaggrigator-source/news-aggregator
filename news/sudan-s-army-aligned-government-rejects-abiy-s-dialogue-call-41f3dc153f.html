Sudan's Army-Aligned Government Rejects Abiy's Dialogue Call - RSF-Backed Administration Welcomes It

Wed, 25 Nov 2026 15:20:43 GMT

Sudan's army-aligned government has rejected Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's call for an inclusive dialogue aimed at resolving the ongoing conflict in Sudan. In contrast, a rival administration aligned with the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) has welcomed the initiative, indicating a split in Sudanese factions regarding Ethiopia's role in peace efforts. Abiy's proposal, made on November 24, emphasized the need for a dialogue free from political agendas, aiming to foster national consensus and establish a unity government. However, the army-aligned government accused Ethiopia of bias, alleging it supports the RSF and has facilitated drone operations against Sudan. This rejection comes amid escalating diplomatic tensions, including mutual consulate closures and accusations of foreign drone strikes. The RSF-aligned Transitional Peace Government expressed readiness to engage with Ethiopia's initiative, viewing it as a chance to address the war's root causes and pursue sustainable peace. The situation remains complex, with ongoing allegations and deteriorating relations between Sudan and Ethiopia. (157 words)
