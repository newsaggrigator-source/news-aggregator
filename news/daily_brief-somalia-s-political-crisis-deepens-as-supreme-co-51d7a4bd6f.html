Somalia's political crisis intensified on Thursday when the Supreme Court rejected President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's attempt to extend his term. The court's ruling has raised concerns about the stability of the government and the potential for increased tensions among political factions. The president's term extension was seen by many as a move to consolidate power amid ongoing challenges, including security issues and humanitarian crises. The decision has sparked reactions from various political leaders and civil society groups, who are calling for adherence to constitutional processes and democratic norms. As the political landscape becomes more volatile, there are fears that this ruling could lead to further unrest and complicate efforts to achieve peace and stability in the country. The situation remains fluid, with stakeholders urging dialogue and cooperation to navigate the ongoing crisis.
