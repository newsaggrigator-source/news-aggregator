Somalia brings home 220 nationals from Yemen, raising annual returns to over 1,500 - Hiiraan Online

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 20:20:42 GMT

The Somali federal government repatriated 220 citizens from Yemen on October 21, 2026, as part of ongoing efforts to assist nationals facing difficult conditions abroad. This operation, conducted in collaboration with the International Organization for Migration (IOM) and the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), increased the total number of Somali returnees from Yemen this year to over 1,500. Many of the returnees had lived in Yemen for years, enduring conflict and economic hardship. Deputy Foreign Minister Hassan Mohamed Ali emphasized the government's commitment to facilitating safe returns and reuniting families. The repatriation program aims to help returning citizens rebuild their lives in Somalia, with support from various international and local agencies.
