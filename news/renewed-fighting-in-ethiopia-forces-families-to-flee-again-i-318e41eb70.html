Renewed Fighting in Ethiopia Forces Families to Flee Again in Tigray, Displaces Thousands in Amhara

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 15:08:29 GMT

Renewed fighting in northern Ethiopia has led to significant displacement, forcing families in Tigray to flee their homes once again. The United Nations reports that thousands are now displaced in the Amhara and Tigray regions, particularly in Debre Birhan and Shire town. Debre Birhan is already overwhelmed with internally displaced persons from other regions, facing severe shortages of food, shelter, and essential services. Many families had only recently returned to their homes in Tigray, particularly in the Tahtay Adiyabo district, before being forced to evacuate again due to escalating violence. The UN is preparing to enhance humanitarian assistance, but ongoing insecurity and movement restrictions are hampering aid delivery. This latest wave of displacement follows renewed hostilities that began in late September, disrupting recovery efforts from the previous conflict that lasted from 2020 to 2022, which had already devastated local communities and infrastructure.
