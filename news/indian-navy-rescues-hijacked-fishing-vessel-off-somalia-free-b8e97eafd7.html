Indian Navy rescues hijacked fishing vessel off Somalia, frees 20 crew - Greater Kashmir

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 02:02:27 GMT

The Indian Navy successfully rescued the hijacked fishing vessel Azini off the coast of Somalia, freeing all 20 crew members after an operation lasting over 48 hours. The vessel was seized by armed pirates last month, raising significant maritime security concerns as it could have been used to launch further attacks on merchant ships. In response, the Indian Navy deployed a warship and a maritime reconnaissance aircraft to track the vessel. Through coordinated efforts and non-kinetic measures, they restricted the pirates' movements. Marine Commandos were then deployed via a precision airdrop from an Indian Air Force C-17 aircraft. They executed a tactical operation that neutralized the pirate threat and secured the vessel without any casualties. This operation highlights the Indian Navy's readiness and capability to conduct complex maritime security missions, reaffirming India's role as a key security partner in the Indian Ocean Region and its commitment to safeguarding maritime trade routes.
