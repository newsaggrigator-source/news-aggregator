Foreign rivalries risk turning Somalia’s political crisis into a protracted war - Wired Gov

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 09:28:21 GMT

Foreign rivalries are exacerbating Somalia's ongoing political crisis, potentially leading to a prolonged conflict. The involvement of various international actors, each with their own interests, complicates the situation further. These rivalries not only hinder efforts for political stability but also fuel tensions among local factions. As external powers vie for influence, the risk of escalating violence increases, threatening the fragile peace in the region. The article highlights the urgent need for a coordinated international approach to address the underlying issues and support Somalia in achieving lasting stability. Without such intervention, the potential for a drawn-out war looms large, impacting both the nation and the broader Horn of Africa.
