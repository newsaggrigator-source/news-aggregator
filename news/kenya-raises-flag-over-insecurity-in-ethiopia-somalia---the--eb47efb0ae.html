Kenya raises flag over insecurity in Ethiopia, Somalia - The EastAfrican

Fri, 27 Nov 2026 12:05:38 GMT

Kenya has expressed growing concerns over the escalating insecurity in neighboring Ethiopia and Somalia, particularly due to the resurgence of militant groups and inter-communal violence. The Kenyan government has highlighted the potential spillover effects of this instability, which could threaten regional peace and security. In response, Kenya is enhancing its border security measures and collaborating with regional partners to address these challenges. The situation has been exacerbated by the ongoing conflict in Ethiopia's Tigray region and the persistent threat posed by Al-Shabaab in Somalia. Kenyan officials are advocating for a coordinated regional approach to tackle the root causes of insecurity, emphasizing the need for diplomatic engagement and support for peace initiatives. The Kenyan leadership is urging for increased international attention and assistance to stabilize the Horn of Africa, as the region grapples with complex security dynamics that could have far-reaching implications for its neighbors.
