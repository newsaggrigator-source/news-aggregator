Ethiopia-Eritrea Border Clashes Raise Fears of Regional War

Wed, 18 Nov 2026 06:54:50 GMT

Clashes along the Ethiopia-Eritrea border have intensified fears of a potential regional war, as Ethiopian troops advance against Tigrayan forces near Eritrea. Following a period of relative calm, renewed fighting in the contested border area has escalated tensions among Ethiopia, Eritrea, and Sudan. The Ethiopian National Defense Forces (ENDF), supported by pro-government militias, have been pushing the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF) out of Tigray since September. The strategic town of Badme, historically significant due to its role in the Eritrean-Ethiopian War, is now a focal point of conflict. Analysts suggest that Ethiopia may aim to reclaim Badme to stabilize its internal situation. Meanwhile, relations between Ethiopia and Sudan have deteriorated, particularly concerning the Grand Renaissance Dam, which has raised water security concerns for Sudan and Egypt. The ongoing civil war in Sudan complicates the regional dynamics, with fears of proxy conflicts rather than direct military engagement for now.
