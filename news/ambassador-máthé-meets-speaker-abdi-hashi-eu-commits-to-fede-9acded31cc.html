Ambassador Máthé Meets Speaker Abdi Hashi: EU Commits to Federal and State Development Projects in Somalia - FTL Somalia

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 09:13:40 GMT

European Union Ambassador to Somalia, László Eduárd Máthé, recently met with the Speakers of both houses of the Federal Parliament, Abdulkadir Mohamed Nur and Abdi Hashi Abdullahi, to discuss the EU's commitment to supporting federal and state development projects in Somalia. The meetings aimed to enhance cooperation between the Somali parliament and EU legislative bodies, focusing on capacity building and infrastructure development. The EU has been a significant partner for Somalia, providing financial support for security missions and engaging in political processes, including constitutional reforms. However, the legitimacy of the Somali parliament is currently contested, particularly following controversial constitutional amendments. The EU's challenge lies in balancing its support for Somali institutions while advocating for democratic principles and an inclusive electoral process.
