Ethiopia: Military action ordered against Tigray 'rogue' rulers - Yeni Safak English

Sat, 21 Nov 2026 03:27:43 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed has ordered military action against the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF), labeling them as "rogue" rulers following an attack on a military camp in Mekele, the regional capital of Tigray. The TPLF, which has distanced itself from the ruling party, had previously obstructed military movements in and out of the region. This escalation comes after a federal general was sent back to Addis Ababa upon arriving in Mekele, highlighting tensions following the TPLF's defiance of federal authority in August elections. In response to the TPLF's actions, Ahmed has called on the Ethiopian populace to support the military's operations against the TPLF, which reportedly has already begun attacking TPLF bases in Mekele. The situation underscores the ongoing conflict and instability in Ethiopia, particularly in the Tigray region, which has faced significant unrest and military confrontations in recent years.
