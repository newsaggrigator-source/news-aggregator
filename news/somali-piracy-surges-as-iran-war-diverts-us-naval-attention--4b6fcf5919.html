Somali piracy surges as Iran war diverts US naval attention - CNN - Iran International

Thu, 29 Oct 2026 17:05:08 GMT

Somali piracy has surged to its highest levels since 2013, driven by the diversion of U.S. naval resources to address threats from Iran and Houthi attacks on shipping. This summer, six vessels were held by pirates, with 90 crew members taken hostage, according to the European Union's Operation Atalanta. Major hijackings, including the MT Eureka and Honour 25 tankers, have highlighted the resurgence of piracy, exacerbated by rising fuel prices and economic hardship in Somalia. Experts attribute the increase in piracy to the focus on threats in the Persian Gulf, which has left commercial vessels vulnerable, particularly those without armed guards. The Honour 25's crew was held for over five months, with ransom demands fluctuating significantly. Analysts warn that as long as regional conflicts persist, piracy will continue to thrive, fueled by worsening poverty and drought in Somalia, making it a more lucrative venture for pirates.
