Ethiopia Expels Sudanese Diplomats As Regional Tensions Deepen

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 15:07:20 GMT

Ethiopia has expelled six Sudanese diplomats, ordering them to leave within 48 hours, amid escalating tensions between the two nations. This diplomatic move follows Sudan's formal complaints to the United Nations Security Council and the African Union, accusing Ethiopia of allowing the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) to launch drone attacks from its territory. Both countries have exchanged accusations of supporting armed groups, with Sudan alleging Ethiopia's facilitation of RSF operations, while Ethiopia claims Sudan backs Tigrayan rebels. The situation is further complicated by Ethiopia's deteriorating relations with Eritrea, which recently involved the expulsion of Eritrean diplomats. The ongoing conflict in Sudan, which has displaced over 14 million people since April 2023, raises concerns about the potential for a broader regional conflict. The expulsion of Sudanese diplomats highlights the increasing diplomatic strain and the risk of overlapping conflicts in the Horn of Africa.
