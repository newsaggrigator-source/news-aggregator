Ethiopia expels 6 Sudanese consular staff and orders consulate closed, officials say - WRAL

Wed, 04 Nov 2026 23:37:50 GMT

Ethiopia has expelled six staff members from Sudan's consulate in Assosa and ordered the consulate's closure, marking a further deterioration in relations between the two nations. This decision follows Sudan's allegations that drones attacking its infrastructure are being launched from Ethiopian territory. An Ethiopian intelligence official confirmed that the expelled staff were given 48 hours to leave the country, while a Sudanese official noted that the announcement was made after the Sudanese ambassador was summoned. This expulsion comes on the heels of Ethiopia's recent diplomatic actions, including the expulsion of ten Eritrean diplomats and an Egyptian diplomat, amid ongoing tensions over Ethiopia's dam on the Blue Nile. Sudan's Foreign Ministry has lodged complaints with the U.N. Security Council regarding the drone attacks, which have severely disrupted electricity and water supplies in Sudan. Both Ethiopia and the UAE have denied accusations of arming the paramilitary group responsible for the attacks.
