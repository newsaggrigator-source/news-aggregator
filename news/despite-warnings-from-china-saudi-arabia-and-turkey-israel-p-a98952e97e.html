Despite warnings from China, Saudi Arabia and Turkey, Israel presses ahead with embassy plans in breakaway African territory near Red Sea - Business Insider Africa

Mon, 02 Nov 2026 22:31:59 GMT

Israel is advancing its plans to establish an embassy in Somaliland, a self-declared independent territory near the Red Sea, despite strong opposition from China, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Turkey, and Somalia. These nations have condemned Israel's recognition of Somaliland, citing violations of Somalia's sovereignty and warning of potential regional destabilization. Israel's Foreign Ministry confirmed the appointment of its first ambassador to Somaliland, Michael Lotem, who presented his credentials to President Abdirahman Mohamed Abdullahi. This move follows Israel's recognition of Somaliland in December 2025, making it the first UN member state to do so. While the U.S. supports Israel's right to diplomatic relations, it has not recognized Somaliland's independence and maintains its support for Somalia's territorial integrity. The strategic location of Somaliland, particularly its proximity to vital shipping routes, has attracted international interest, further complicating the geopolitical landscape in the region.
