Forum Vows to Advocate Ethiopia’s Strategic Interests in Nile, Red Sea - ENA English

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 17:15:48 GMT

The newly established Nile and Red Sea Strategic Forum in Ethiopia aims to advocate for the country's strategic interests in these crucial waterways. CEO Solomon Tefera announced the forum's commitment to enhancing research, policy advocacy, and public engagement to support Ethiopia's economic development and geopolitical standing. The forum seeks to mobilize civil society, scholars, and the public to protect national interests, emphasizing the need for coordinated efforts among various stakeholders. Solomon highlighted that Ethiopia's interests extend beyond water resources to include political stability and regional peace. The forum plans to conduct research, organize conferences, and promote diplomatic advocacy on issues related to the Nile and Red Sea. It has outlined 12 objectives focused on enhancing Ethiopia's access to maritime routes and fostering regional cooperation. By bringing together experts and stakeholders, the forum aims to develop comprehensive perspectives on Ethiopia's interests, supporting diplomatic engagement and strategic partnerships to strengthen regional relations.
