Somalia’s U-15 Team Football Team Falls to Tier D After Group Stage, Faces Tanzania in Knockout Round - FTL Somalia

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 19:13:45 GMT

Somalia's U-15 football team has advanced to the Tier D knockout stage of the FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival after a challenging group stage. The team began their campaign with a historic 3-1 victory over Puerto Rico, highlighted by Ismail Mohamed's two goals and an assist. However, subsequent matches saw Somalia suffer three consecutive defeats: 5-1 against Venezuela, 3-2 against New Zealand, and 2-1 against Madagascar, leaving them at the bottom of Group T. Despite these setbacks, the knockout format provides a fresh opportunity for the team to compete for a trophy within their tier. They will face Tanzania in the Round of 32 on October 28. The Somali Football Federation emphasizes youth development, and while the team's performance reflects the challenges of competing against more established football nations, their participation in this tournament marks a significant step forward for Somali football. The focus now is on building a sustainable football program for the future.
