UN agency warns nearly 874,000 could be displaced by floods in Somalia - Anadolu Ajansı

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 14:52:13 GMT

The UN migration agency, IOM, has warned that nearly 874,000 people in Somalia could be displaced due to anticipated heavy rainfall and rising river levels over the next three months. This forecast, developed in collaboration with the Danish Refugee Council, indicates that flooding could account for 92% of the displacements, particularly affecting communities already suffering from drought. The rainy season is expected to bring above-normal rainfall, exacerbated by strengthening El Niño conditions. IOM has secured only 14.4% of the $16.62 million needed for flood preparedness, which aims to assist over 517,000 individuals. Areas like Beletweyne and Gaalkacyo are highlighted as facing the greatest threats. The agency is urging immediate international funding to enhance early warning systems and provide essential resources such as emergency shelter and clean water.
