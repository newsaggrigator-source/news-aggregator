Attorney General Ellison again leads court defense of Temporary Protected Status for immigrants from Somalia - Minnesota Attorney General's Office

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 21:27:52 GMT

Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison has once again led a coalition of 17 attorneys general in filing an amicus brief to support the Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for Somali immigrants, following a lawsuit against the Department of Homeland Security's (DHS) decision to terminate TPS. This program, established in the 1990s, protects immigrants from unsafe countries, allowing them to live and work in the U.S. Approximately 3,850 Somali immigrants are currently under TPS, with many residing in Minnesota. Ellison argues that ending TPS would harm communities and economies, as Somali immigrants contribute significantly to Minnesota's workforce and economy, generating an estimated $8.6 billion annually. The brief emphasizes the negative impacts on public health and education, particularly for U.S. citizen children of TPS holders, who may face anxiety and trauma from potential family separation. This is the second amicus brief filed by Ellison this year, highlighting ongoing concerns about the welfare of Somali immigrants in the U.S.
