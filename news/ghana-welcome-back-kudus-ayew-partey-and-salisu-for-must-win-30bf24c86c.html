Ghana welcome back Kudus, Ayew, Partey and Salisu for must-win Somalia qualifier - GhanaWeb

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:43:55 GMT

Ghana's national football team has welcomed back key players Mohammed Kudus, Jordan Ayew, Thomas Partey, and Mohammed Salisu for their crucial 2026 World Cup qualifier against Somalia. The match, scheduled for October 12, is vital for Ghana's hopes of advancing in the qualification process. Coach Chris Hughton expressed confidence in the squad's strength with the return of these experienced players, who have been instrumental in previous matches. Kudus, currently playing for West Ham United, has been in impressive form, while Ayew and Partey bring valuable leadership and experience. Salisu's inclusion adds depth to the defense. The team aims to secure a decisive victory to boost their standing in the group. Fans are optimistic about the players' return, hoping it will enhance Ghana's chances of qualifying for the World Cup. The match will take place at the Accra Sports Stadium, where a strong home crowd is expected to support the Black Stars. (151 words)
