Guterres Urges Climate Finance Equity for Developing Nations

Thu, 12 Nov 2026 05:29:18 GMT

At COP31, UN Secretary-General António Guterres emphasized the urgent need for equitable climate financing for developing nations, highlighting that nearly two-thirds of the global population in emerging economies outside China receive less than 20% of global clean energy investments. He described climate justice as a critical challenge of the decade and called for the international community to address the financial imbalance that hampers these countries' clean energy transitions. Guterres asserted that developing nations cannot be expected to meet climate commitments without adequate financial support, stressing the necessity of closing the financing gap to enable sustainable development. He warned that current national climate plans fall significantly short of the required emissions reductions, urging world leaders to translate commitments into actionable results. Guterres also pointed to the rapid growth of renewable energy as evidence that a transition away from fossil fuels is both feasible and economically viable, while cautioning against the influence of fossil fuel interests on climate action.
