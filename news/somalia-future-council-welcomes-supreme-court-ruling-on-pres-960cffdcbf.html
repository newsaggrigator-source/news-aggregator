Somalia Future Council welcomes supreme court ruling on president’s term extension - Somali Guardian

Sun, 08 Nov 2026 16:54:38 GMT

The Somalia Future Council has expressed strong support for the Supreme Court's ruling that declared the president's term extension illegal. The opposition coalition condemned the president's administration for allegedly attempting to seize control of the Supreme Court and pursuing its leadership following the ruling. The court determined that both the presidential and parliamentary mandates had expired, calling for consensus-based elections within 90 days. The Future Council described this ruling as a critical opportunity to address the country's political crisis and prevent further instability. They warned citizens about the severe threats to governance and national security posed by the president, whom they hold accountable for the ongoing turmoil. Additionally, the council declared all contracts and decisions made after the expiration of the mandates null and void, promising a comprehensive review once legal authority is restored. The situation underscores the urgent need for political resolution in Somalia.
