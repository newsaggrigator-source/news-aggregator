Somalia’s security transition faces major test as international support comes under pressure - People Daily

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 21:32:12 GMT

Somalia's security transition is facing significant challenges as international support wanes, particularly following the closure of the United Nations Transitional Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNTMIS) and Uganda's troop withdrawal after two decades. The African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) continues to assist Somali forces against Al-Shabaab, but concerns about its financing and logistical support have intensified. A report by Balqiis Insights warns that Somalia risks losing security gains if it assumes full responsibility without adequate resources and political coordination. The report highlights the critical role of the United Nations Support Office in Somalia (UNSOS) for logistical support, which is now under threat as the U.S. plans to wind down its support. Additionally, political divisions among Somali leaders complicate the transition, hindering cooperation and intelligence sharing necessary for effective security operations. The UN emphasizes the need for ongoing international engagement to address these challenges and sustain progress in Somalia.
