Somalia’s ex-spy chief does not rule out Al-Shabaab alliance to topple president - Somali Guardian

Sun, 25 Oct 2026 18:23:54 GMT

Somalia's political landscape is facing renewed instability as former intelligence chief Abdullahi Mohamed Ali Sanbalolshe announced his Hawadle clan's intention to establish a new regional administration in Hiran. This move follows their dissatisfaction with the federal government's handling of political representation, particularly regarding the presidency of Hirshabelle state. Sanbalolshe's comments suggest a potential shift towards armed resistance against President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud's government, raising fears of a major conflict. He did not dismiss the possibility of collaborating with Al-Shabaab, the militant group opposing the government, which could lead to a dangerous alliance. Additionally, Sanbalolshe has sought support from regional leaders in the UAE for an armed rebellion, although it remains unclear if any agreements were reached. Concurrently, Somali counterterrorism officials have warned of planned rebellions in Hiran and Middle Shabelle, allegedly financed by the UAE, further complicating the already fragile political situation in Somalia.
