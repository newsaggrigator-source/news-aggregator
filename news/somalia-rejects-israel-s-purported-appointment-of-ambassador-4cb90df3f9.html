Somalia Rejects Israel’s Purported Appointment of Ambassador in Hargeisa - Somali National News Agency

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 18:19:29 GMT

The Federal Government of Somalia has rejected Israel's alleged appointment of an ambassador in Hargeisa and the establishment of what Israel claims to be an embassy on Somali territory. The Somali Ministry of Foreign Affairs condemned these actions as violations of the country's sovereignty and territorial integrity, asserting that they were conducted without the consent of the Somali government and thus hold no legal validity. The statement emphasized that Hargeisa and the Somaliland region are integral parts of Somalia, and any diplomatic relations must be established with the approval of the sovereign state, in line with the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations. Somalia also highlighted that Israel's recognition of Somaliland, which occurred in December 2025, has been dismissed by various international organizations, including the African Union and the League of Arab States. The Somali government called for Israel to retract its actions and warned that such moves could escalate tensions in the region.
