Ethiopia conflict shifts focus on Kenya's security - Daily Nation

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 07:52:50 GMT

The ongoing conflict in Ethiopia has heightened security concerns in Kenya, particularly in the border town of Moyale. As clashes intensify, stray bullets and rocket-propelled grenades have posed a significant threat to local residents, prompting Kenyan authorities to identify internally displaced persons (IDPs) in the area. Thousands of Ethiopians have sought refuge in Kenya, while many Kenyans have also fled their homes due to safety fears. The Kenyan government, alongside the UNHCR, is actively monitoring the situation and assessing the needs of both Kenyan and Ethiopian IDPs. Humanitarian agencies are stepping up interventions, but challenges remain, including food insecurity and limited access to healthcare for displaced populations. Local officials emphasize the urgency of addressing the security threats and providing support to affected families, as the situation continues to evolve with ongoing violence across the border.
