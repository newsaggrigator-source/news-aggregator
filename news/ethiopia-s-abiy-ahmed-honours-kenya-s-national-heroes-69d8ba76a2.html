Ethiopia's Abiy Ahmed Honours Kenya's National Heroes

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 05:20:49 GMT

Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed honored Kenya's national heroes during the Mashujaa Day celebrations in Eldoret on October 20, 2026. His tribute emphasized the shared histories of struggle and sacrifice between Ethiopia and Kenya, particularly highlighting Kenya's fight for independence and the Ethiopian victory at the Battle of Adwa in 1896. Abiy praised the courage of Kenyans who defended their nation, stating, "These shared histories remind us that freedom was never given; it was won through courage, unity, and sacrifice." He called for enhanced economic cooperation between the two nations, focusing on clean energy, regional connectivity, telecommunications, and trade as vital areas for collaboration. Abiy's remarks underscored the concept of Medemer, which promotes unity and collective efforts for national development. The relationship between Ethiopia and Kenya is characterized by close diplomatic ties and joint initiatives in infrastructure and security. He concluded by saluting those honored during the celebrations, wishing them a happy Mashujaa Day.
