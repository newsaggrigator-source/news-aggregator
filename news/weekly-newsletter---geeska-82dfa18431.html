Weekly newsletter - Geeska

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 15:45:13 GMT

The latest issue of the Geeska newsletter highlights significant political and social issues in Somalia and the Horn of Africa. It discusses the ongoing governance crisis in Somalia, with Yusuf reflecting on the Arta Conference's impact on state-building. Ahmed Mohamed analyzes Hirshabelle's political dynamics, where leaders engage social media influencers amid escalating security and humanitarian challenges. The newsletter also features historical insights, including John Okello's revolutionary impact in East Africa. Cultural developments in Hargeisa are explored, alongside reports of land grabs in Mogadishu, revealing deep corruption. Additionally, workers at Mogadishu's airport protested against labor rights violations by the Turkish company Favori. In Djibouti, the ruling party has nominated President Guelleh for a sixth term, raising questions about the country's future. The newsletter encapsulates a region grappling with political strife, cultural evolution, and social unrest.
