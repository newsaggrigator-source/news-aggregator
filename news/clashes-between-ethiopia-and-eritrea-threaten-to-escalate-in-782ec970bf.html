Clashes between Ethiopia and Eritrea threaten to escalate into full-scale war - The Japan Times

Thu, 19 Nov 2026 02:38:00 GMT

Clashes between Ethiopian and Eritrean forces have intensified, raising concerns of a potential full-scale war in the region. The conflict, which has its roots in historical tensions and territorial disputes, has recently escalated with reports of heavy fighting along the border. Both nations have mobilized troops, and there are fears that the situation could spiral out of control, drawing in neighboring countries and destabilizing the Horn of Africa. International observers are urging both governments to engage in dialogue to de-escalate tensions. The humanitarian impact of the clashes is already being felt, with civilians caught in the crossfire and facing dire conditions. The international community is closely monitoring the situation, emphasizing the need for a peaceful resolution to prevent further violence and suffering. As the situation develops, the potential for a broader conflict remains a significant concern for regional stability and security.
