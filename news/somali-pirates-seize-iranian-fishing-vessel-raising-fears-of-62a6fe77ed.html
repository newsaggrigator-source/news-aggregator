Somali pirates seize Iranian fishing vessel, raising fears of attacks on cargo ships - ABC News - Breaking News, Latest News and Videos

Sun, 01 Nov 2026 17:51:34 GMT

Somali pirates have hijacked an Iranian fishing vessel, the Azini, which was carrying 23 individuals, including 21 Iranian crew members and two Somali security guards. The seizure occurred between Bandar Bayla and Hafun in northeastern Somalia, raising concerns that the vessel could be used as a "mother ship" for further attacks on commercial shipping in the region. The Maritime Security Center Indian Ocean issued a warning about the potential threat, as the ship was approaching a busy shipping lane. This incident follows a resurgence of piracy in the Gulf of Aden and western Indian Ocean, with six commercial vessels hijacked since April. The European Union's naval Operation Atalanta is investigating the situation, urging ships in the area to remain vigilant. The renewed piracy poses additional risks to maritime operations already complicated by geopolitical tensions, including threats from Iran-backed Houthi rebels and disruptions in oil shipping routes.
