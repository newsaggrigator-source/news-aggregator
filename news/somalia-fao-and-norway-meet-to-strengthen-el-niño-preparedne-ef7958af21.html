Somalia, FAO and Norway Meet to Strengthen El Niño Preparedness as Flood Risks Mount - FTL Somalia

Tue, 20 Oct 2026 06:26:29 GMT

Somalia, in collaboration with the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) and Norway, is taking proactive measures to enhance preparedness for potential El Niño-related flooding. As the risk of floods increases due to changing weather patterns, the meeting focused on strategies to mitigate the impact on vulnerable communities. The discussions emphasized the importance of early warning systems, improved agricultural practices, and community resilience initiatives. The FAO highlighted the need for coordinated efforts to ensure food security and protect livelihoods in the face of climate challenges. Norway's involvement underscores the international commitment to support Somalia in addressing these pressing environmental issues. The partnership aims to strengthen local capacities and enhance response mechanisms to safeguard affected populations.
