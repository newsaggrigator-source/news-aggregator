'Ethiopia's Return to a Sea Port Is Inevitable': Navy Commander Calls for Regional Maritime Cooperation

Fri, 30 Oct 2026 16:02:34 GMT

Ethiopian Navy Commander Vice Admiral Kindu Gezu has emphasized the necessity of regional maritime cooperation, asserting that Ethiopia's return to a sea port is inevitable. Speaking at a recent maritime security forum hosted by the Ethiopian Navy, he highlighted the importance of dialogue with neighboring countries to address maritime security challenges. The forum, the first of its kind in Ethiopia, included naval commanders from Yemen, Oman, Djibouti, Somaliland, and Puntland, focusing on joint maritime search and rescue operations. Kindu warned of growing security threats in the Strait of Hormuz that could impact the broader region, advocating for collaborative efforts among nations of varying sizes to enhance maritime security. Commodore Jemal Tufisa, Deputy Commander for Operations, noted that the forum laid the groundwork for cross-border information sharing and joint operational exercises. The discussions concluded with strategies for responding to maritime emergencies and threats, reinforcing the commitment to regional cooperation in maritime security. (158 words)
