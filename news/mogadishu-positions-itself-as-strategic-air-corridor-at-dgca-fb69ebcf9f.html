Mogadishu Positions Itself as Strategic Air Corridor at DGCA-MID/8 in Qatar - FTL Somalia

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 09:47:24 GMT

At the DGCA-MID/8 meeting in Doha, Somalia's Director General of Civil Aviation, Ahmed Moallin Hassan, emphasized the country's strategic role in regional aviation. He highlighted Somalia's advancements in civil aviation and its regained Class A airspace status, which allows for full air traffic control services. This reclassification is expected to increase daily flight numbers and significantly boost annual revenue. Amid ongoing disruptions in Middle Eastern aviation due to conflicts, Somalia's geographic position offers a viable alternative for airlines seeking safer routes. The meeting addressed various aviation issues, including safety and cybersecurity, where Somalia aims to demonstrate its capacity as a reliable corridor. However, the SCAA's progress remains fragile, as maintaining safety standards is crucial for sustaining confidence in Somali airspace. The meeting serves as a pivotal opportunity for Somalia to position itself as a preferred aviation route, rather than merely a refuge from regional instability.
