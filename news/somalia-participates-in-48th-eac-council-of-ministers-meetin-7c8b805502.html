Somalia Participates in 48th EAC Council of Ministers Meeting in Kampala - Horseed Media

Tue, 24 Nov 2026 15:45:00 GMT

Somalia is actively participating in the 48th Ordinary Meeting of the East African Community (EAC) Council of Ministers, held at Speke Resort Munyonyo in Kampala, Uganda. The meeting commenced with a session involving Senior Officials from EAC partner states, focusing on regional cooperation and economic integration. Somalia is represented by Ahmed Warsame and Abdirahman Abshir from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, alongside other officials participating virtually. The agenda includes reviewing previous decisions, addressing financial matters, and discussing priorities for regional integration. Somalia's involvement underscores its commitment to enhancing engagement within the EAC and fostering partnerships with other member states. The discussions aim to strengthen economic and political cooperation, providing Somalia a platform to advocate for trade and investment opportunities while addressing shared regional priorities. This meeting is anticipated to bolster dialogue among member states and advance the EAC's integration agenda.
