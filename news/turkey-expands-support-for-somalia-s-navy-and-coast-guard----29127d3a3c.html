Turkey Expands Support for Somalia’s Navy and Coast Guard - Garowe Online

Thu, 26 Nov 2026 17:47:00 GMT

Turkey is enhancing its support for Somalia's naval and coast guard capabilities through a defense cooperation agreement, as announced by Turkish Defense Ministry spokesman Rear Adm. Zeki Aktürk. This initiative aims to bolster maritime security along Somalia's coastline and inland waters, involving training and capacity-building for Somali personnel. Key projects include the construction of naval facilities, such as a headquarters and a maritime operations coordination center, which were recently inaugurated in Mogadishu. These developments are part of a broader 10-year agreement signed in February 2024, which focuses on equipping the Somali Navy and addressing maritime threats like piracy and illegal fishing. Turkey's involvement is seen as a crucial step in helping Somalia rebuild its military infrastructure and maintain stability in the region. The ongoing discussions between Turkish and Somali officials will further define the scope and responsibilities of this partnership.
