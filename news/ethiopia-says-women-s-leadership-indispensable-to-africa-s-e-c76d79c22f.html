Ethiopia Says Women's Leadership Indispensable to Africa's Economic Transformation

Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:20:21 GMT

Ethiopia's Minister of Women and Social Affairs, Shawit Shanka, emphasized the crucial role of women's leadership in Africa's economic transformation during the 2nd African Political Parties Summit 2026 in Addis Ababa. She urged African nations to ensure equal participation of women in political and economic decision-making, asserting that marginalizing half the population hinders the continent's development potential. Shawit highlighted that women should be viewed as producers, innovators, and leaders rather than mere beneficiaries of assistance. She called for proactive leadership and strong institutional support to enhance women's representation in political parties and decision-making processes. The Minister reiterated Ethiopia's commitment to women's empowerment under Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's leadership, advocating for a future where every girl can aspire to leadership roles without gender barriers. Shawit concluded by urging collective efforts to advance women's political leadership across Africa, stressing that today's decisions will shape future generations. (159 words)
