Boost security along Kenya-Ethiopia border - The Standard

Fri, 06 Nov 2026 03:00:00 GMT

Escalating violence along the Kenya-Ethiopia border has prompted urgent calls for enhanced security measures. The conflict, initially a localized territorial dispute, has evolved into a humanitarian crisis, resulting in multiple casualties on both sides. Recent incidents include the death of a 65-year-old Kenyan woman from stray gunfire and an attack by armed Ethiopian warriors on a Kenyan watering point, highlighting the severity of the situation. The ongoing clashes have disrupted local communities, with rocket-propelled grenades landing in Kenyan areas and over 50,000 Ethiopians seeking refuge in Marsabit County, straining resources. The Kenyan government is urged to deploy the Kenya Defence Forces to secure the border and protect civilians. Additionally, dialogue is essential for a lasting resolution, with calls for Ethiopian authorities and international organizations to facilitate ceasefire negotiations and address the root causes of the conflict. Immediate humanitarian support is also critical to assist displaced individuals and prevent further suffering.
