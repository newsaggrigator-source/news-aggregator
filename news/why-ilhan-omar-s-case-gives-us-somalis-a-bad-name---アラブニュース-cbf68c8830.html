Why Ilhan Omar’s case gives US Somalis a bad name - アラブニュース

Thu, 22 Oct 2026 13:34:36 GMT

Ilhan Omar, a Minnesota congresswoman, is under federal investigation regarding her citizenship acquisition, specifically whether she or her late father misrepresented her age on immigration documents. This inquiry could lead to criminal charges, denaturalization, and potential deportation. Federal agents conducted a search of her home, seizing various personal items, as part of a broader investigation into possible false statements on government documents. The focus includes allegations that her father misrepresented his status as a refugee and questions surrounding Omar's previous marriage, which some claim was arranged for immigration benefits. Omar has denied all accusations, and no formal charges have been filed against her. The investigation has sparked concerns within the Somali-American community, with advocates warning of possible backlash and discrimination. Omar has framed the investigation as politically motivated and has called for support from her constituents to help combat the legal challenges she faces. Legal experts note that denaturalization is rare and requires proving intentional misrepresentation.
