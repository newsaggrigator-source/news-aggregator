Liberation Without Domination - Ethiopia's Opposition Alliance and the Right to Decide

Tue, 27 Oct 2026 10:19:46 GMT

The Ethiopian Peoples' Forces Alliance for Survival (EPFAS), formed on September 20, 2026, includes the Oromo Liberation Front-Oromo Liberation Army (OLF-OLA), the Amhara Fano National Movement, and the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF). The alliance advocates for an inclusive temporary government and emphasizes the importance of political authorship, where the people retain the power to shape their governance. The article critiques the potential for domination within this alliance, arguing that authority must be derived from the people and that leaders should be accountable to their constituents. It highlights the need for ongoing public engagement and dialogue to ensure that diverse voices, including marginalized groups, are heard in decision-making processes. The article also stresses that any political arrangements must respect the rights of all ethnic groups and individuals, advocating for a lawful approach to self-determination and governance in Ethiopia. Ultimately, it calls for a political environment where power is answerable and non-domination is prioritized.
