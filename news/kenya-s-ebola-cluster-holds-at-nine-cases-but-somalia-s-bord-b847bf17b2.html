Kenya’s Ebola Cluster Holds at Nine Cases, but Somalia’s Border Defenses Remain Thin - FTL Somalia

Wed, 21 Oct 2026 10:28:26 GMT

Kenya's Ministry of Health has confirmed a total of nine Ebola cases linked to a single cluster, with the latest case being a family member of the index patient. All patients are stable and responding well to treatment, with extensive contact tracing successfully identifying 99.3% of contacts. While Kenya has effectively contained the outbreak, Somalia's border defenses remain inadequate, with a WHO assessment indicating only 20% capacity at airports for disease detection. Somalia has initiated a national Ebola preparedness committee and implemented monitoring at airports, but critical gaps in infrastructure and protocols persist. The ongoing outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo, which has surpassed 9,500 cases, poses a significant risk to both countries. Experts warn that without strengthening its health systems, Somalia remains vulnerable to a potential Ebola outbreak.
