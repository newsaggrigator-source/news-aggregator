Ethiopia Retakes Rebel Stronghold in Tigray While Seeking to Stabilize Security Situation - Black Agenda Report

Tue, 03 Nov 2026 18:30:04 GMT

Ethiopian forces have regained control of Mekelle, the capital of the Tigray region, as armed opposition groups struggle to mount a significant challenge against the government led by Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. The Ethiopian National Defense Forces (ENDF) took over Mekelle without resistance from the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF), which has since vowed to continue its fight outside the city. The TPLF's retreat has led to the emergence of the Tigray Peace Forces (TPF), which now manages local security. The recent developments have raised concerns about the viability of the 2022 Pretoria Peace Agreement, which aimed to resolve the conflict between the TPLF and the central government. Meanwhile, reports indicate internal strife within the TPLF, including defections of key military figures. The Ethiopian government has also received military support from Russia, further complicating the regional security landscape, as tensions with Eritrea persist.
