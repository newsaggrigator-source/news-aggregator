Somaliland Joins Ethiopian Navy's Red Sea Workshop

Wed, 28 Oct 2026 04:46:19 GMT

Somaliland is participating in a four-day regional maritime security workshop hosted by the Ethiopian Navy in Addis Ababa, which runs from October 26 to 29. The workshop aims to enhance cooperation among naval and maritime representatives from Yemen, Oman, Djibouti, Puntland, and Somaliland, focusing on maritime search and rescue, regional security challenges, and improving coordination among maritime authorities. Ethiopian Navy Commander Vice Admiral Kindu Gezu emphasized the importance of maritime cooperation during the opening ceremony. The event, part of the Crisis Response Project for the Red Sea and Western Indian Ocean, provides a platform for discussing search-and-rescue coordination and operational responses to maritime incidents. Somaliland's involvement is significant due to its strategic coastline along the Gulf of Aden and the development of the Port of Berbera, which serves as a key trade gateway. The workshop reflects growing regional attention to maritime security in the Gulf of Aden and aims to strengthen emergency response capabilities. (159 words)
